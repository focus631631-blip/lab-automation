## Beyond the Child: A Systematic Review of Parental Wellbeing and Family Functioning in Paediatric Colorectal Conditions.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 11:40
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: VIC · Clinical Sciences
- 通讯作者: Koo C (caryn.koo@mcri.edu.au)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163523
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163523) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42860148/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统综述接受手术治疗的肛门直肠畸形（ARM）或先天性巨结肠（HD）患儿父母的心理健康与家庭功能结局。
- 核心发现/数据：从1205条记录中纳入9项定量研究，因方法学异质性未进行荟萃分析。研究显示，家庭在心理 wellbeing、育儿压力、照护负担和家庭功能方面面临挑战，尤其在持续肠功能障碍和粪失禁背景下更为突出。同时，家庭在韧性和适应方面存在较大差异，提示ARM和HD对更广泛家庭单元具有显著心理社会影响。
- 临床启示：临床应将父母心理健康和家庭功能纳入评估与随访，提供以家庭为中心、纵向整合的生物心理社会支持。尤其对存在持续肠功能障碍或粪失禁的患儿家庭，应主动筛查照护负担并给予心理社会干预。

**原文摘要**：Families of children with anorectal malformation (ARM) and Hirschsprung disease (HD) often experience ongoing caregiving demands extending beyond the child's medical management. This systematic review aimed to synthesise quantitative literature examining parental wellbeing and family functioning outcomes among families of children with ARM or HD. Using the PRISMA protocol, a systematic search was conducted across Ovid Medline, Embase, Web of Science, PsycINFO, and CINAHL. Eligible studies included quantitative studies evaluating psychosocial outcomes relating to parents or families of children (aged <18 years) surgically treated for ARM or HD. Data extraction and quality appraisal using the Joanna Briggs Institute tool were completed independently by two reviewers. From 1205 records identified, nine studies met inclusion criteria and were synthesised without meta-analysis due to methodological heterogeneity. Across studies, families reported challenges relating to psychological wellbeing, parenting stress, caregiving burden, and family functioning, particularly in the context of ongoing bowel dysfunction and faecal incontinence. However, there was also considerable variability in resilience and adaptation among families. Current evidence suggested that ARM and HD may have significant psychosocial impacts on the broader family unit. Future research should prioritise longitudinal and family-centred approaches to better inform integrated models of biopsychosocial care.

## Clinical and Sonographic Features of Neonatal Ovarian Cysts With Autoamputation.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:40
- 期刊: J Clin Ultrasound
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Guangdong Women and Children Hospital
- 通讯作者: Shangjie X
- DOI: 10.1002/jcu.70389
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcu.70389) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42861479/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：回顾性比较进展为自截与未自截的新生儿卵巢囊肿的临床、超声及病理特征，以帮助临床医生在管理复杂性新生儿卵巢囊肿时识别自截。
- 核心发现/数据：19

**原文摘要**：Autoamputation is a rare but severe complication of neonatal ovarian cysts. This case series describes the clinical, sonographic, and pathological characteristics of neonatal ovarian cysts that progressed to autoamputation, compared with those that did not, based on a retrospective review of 19 cysts. We aim to help clinicians suspect autoamputation when managing complex neonatal ovarian cysts. Medical records of 18 neonates (19 ovarian cysts) treated at two medical centers between January 2012 and December 2023 were reviewed. All cysts appeared complex on postnatal ultrasonography. We documented prenatal and postnatal sonographic features (cyst size, solid components, septations, fluid-debris levels, calcification), timing of examinations, intraoperative findings (adhesions, autoamputation), and pathology results. Descriptive statistics and exploratory comparisons between autoamputation (n = 9 cysts) and non-autoamputation (n = 10 cysts) groups were performed, without claims of predictive modeling. Of the 19 complex ovarian cysts, 9 (47.3%) were confirmed autoamputated at surgery. The autoamputation group showed a longer median interval from first complex features to surgery (68 vs. 21.5 days) and an older age at last postnatal ultrasound (11 vs. 1 days). Intra-abdominal adhesions were observed in 66.7% of autoamputation cases versus 10% of non-autoamputation cases. Follicles were absent in all autoamputated cysts on pathology but present in 90% of non-autoamputated cysts. Sonographic findings evolved over time: fluid-debris levels became the predominant feature with increasing duration, while calcification appeared late (22.2% in the autoamputation group). Autoamputation of neonatal ovarian cysts was associated with a longer interval from first complex features to surgery, absence of follicles on pathology, and frequent intra-abdominal adhesions. Sonographic features evolved over time, with fluid-debris levels becoming common and calcification appearing late but with poor sonographic sensitivity. These observations may help clinicians better understand the evolution of complex neonatal ovarian cysts associated with autoamputation; however, the diagnostic and predictive value of these findings requires further validation.

## Peripheral venous access in the neonate: current evidence, persistent uncertainties, and proposal of a structured insertion bundle.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:40
- 期刊: Eur J Pediatr · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区) · IF 2.9 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 意大利
- 通讯作者: Pittiruti M (mauro.pittiruti@me.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00431-026-07497-y
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-026-07497-y) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42860492/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：本叙述性综述旨在评估新生儿外周静脉通路的适应证、装置选择、置管技术、固定、监测及并发症的现有证据，并提出结构化置管集束化方案SIPVeC。
- 核心发现/数据：外周静脉装置是NICU最常用的侵入性装置，但不到一半能完成既定治疗，平均留置时间仅32-36小时；证据支持聚氨酯优于聚四氟乙烯、26G作为默认规格（包括输血），以及氰基丙烯酸酯固定、结构化术前静脉评估和专职血管通路团队与更好结局相关。多数证据来自回顾性单中心研究，集成装置或长外周导管优势、管径-静脉关系及浸润客观检测等问题仍不确定。
- 临床启示：应推动新生儿外周静脉置管与维护标准化，采用SIPVeC七步集束化方案，优先考虑聚氨酯26G装置、结构化静脉评估和氰基丙烯酸酯固定。仍需高质量研究解决现有不确定性，以改善留置时间和并发症结局。

**原文摘要**：Peripheral venous access devices are the most frequently inserted invasive devices in neonatal intensive care, yet fewer than half of them complete the therapy for which they were placed, and their mean dwell time is between 32 and 36 h. Despite this, they have attracted far less attention than central venous access devices: insertion and maintenance practices remain heterogeneous, rarely neonate-specific, and almost never standardized. This narrative review examines the current evidence on indications, device choice, insertion technique, securement, surveillance, and complications of peripheral venous access in the newborn. Evidence suggests that polyurethane outperforms polytetrafluoroethylene; the largest available cohort study supports 26G as the default caliber, including for transfusion; cyanoacrylate securement, structured pre-procedural vein assessment, and dedicated vascular access teams are each associated with better outcomes. Much of this evidence, however, comes from retrospective single-center studies, and important questions-such as the potential advantages of integrated devices or of long peripheral catheters, the caliber-to-vein relationship, and objective detection of infiltration-remain open.  On this basis, we propose a protocol for Safe Insertion of Peripheral Venous Catheters (SIPVeC), a seven-step insertion bundle formatted the same way as the protocols already available for umbilical and central devices in the neonate. • Peripheral venous access devices are the most used vascular access device in neonates, yet most fail before therapy is completed. • Insertion and maintenance practices remain heterogeneous and are rarely neonate-specific. • Evidence now favors polyurethane, 26G devices, structured vein assessment, and cyanoacrylate securement. • The SIPVeC protocol translates these elements into a reproducible seven-step insertion bundle for peripheral devices in neonates.

## Early postnatal brain volumes and two-year cognitive outcomes in preterm infants: An exploratory machine learning study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:40
- 期刊: Brain Dev · 中科院医学4区(临床神经病学4区/儿科4区) · IF 1.7 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: Rotterdam · Erasmus MC Sophia Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Savvopoulos F (f.savvopoulos@erasmusmc.nl)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.braindev.2026.104602
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.braindev.2026.104602) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42860101/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨利用约30周校正胎龄的早期脑体积特征联合临床及人口学围产期特征，预测胎龄小于30周早产儿2岁时认知和运动结局，并比较逻辑回归、线性支持向量分类器和K近邻三种机器学习算法的性能。
- 核心发现/数据：在认知结局分析（N=63）中，仅使用脑体积特征时K近邻模型验证准确率为0.64，对认知结局具有中等偏低的预测能力；加入临床特征后准确率未显著提高。运动结局分析（N=41）中，各模型预测性能均未优于随机水平。
- 临床启示：早期脑体积MRI联合机器学习对早产儿2岁认知结局仅有有限预测价值，对运动结局预测不足，尚不足以直接指导临床决策。其临床可靠性取决于数据数量与质量、方法学标准化及预测模型泛化能力。

**原文摘要**：Premature birth is associated with an increased risk of cognitive and motor developmental problems. Early prediction of these outcomes is crucial for timely intervention and adequate follow-up. This study aimed to predict cognitive and motor outcomes in preterm-born infants using brain volumetric features in combination with clinical and demographic perinatal characteristics, and to compare the performance of three different machine learning algorithms. We acquired quantitative structural brain MRI at approximately 30 weeks postmenstrual age (PMA) and clinical and demographic birth characteristics including gestational age, sex, birthweight, and maternal education level. Machine learning techniques were applied to predict cognitive (N = 63) and motor (N = 41) outcomes at two years corrected age, assessed using the Bayley Scales of Infant and Toddler Development III, in infants born before 30 weeks gestation. We evaluated the performance of logistic regression, linear support vector classifier (linear SVC), and k-nearest neighbour (KNN). Employing volumetric features only, KNN achieved a validation accuracy of 0.64, showing modest predictive power for cognitive outcomes. Adding clinical characteristics did not significantly improve accuracy. The models did not perform better than chance when predicting motor outcomes. Volumetric features demonstrated modest predictive ability for cognitive outcomes, and the addition of clinical features did not significantly improve performance. The low to moderate overall validation accuracy underscores, however, the challenges faced in the clinical application of machine learning in neuroimaging. The reliability and clinical applicability of predictions of outcomes from advanced neuroimaging data will largely depend on the quantity and quality of the data, methodological standardisation, and the generalisability of the prediction models.
