## The Evolving Landscape of Neonatal Care and Research : Based on 14

- 期刊: Indian J Pediatr · 中科院医学4区(儿科4区) · IF 2.4 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Puducherry · Jawaharlal Institute of Postgraduate Medical Education and Research
- 通讯作者: Bethou A (adhisivam1975@yahoo.co.uk)
- DOI: 10.1007/s12098-026-06525-x
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12098-026-06525-x) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42932155/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：总结近60年新生儿照护与研究的演变，并勾勒未来十年改善新生儿结局的重点方向。
- 核心发现/数据：新生儿学已从以降低死亡为主转向关注生存质量、神经发育、家庭整合照护和长期健康；但2024年全球仍有约230万新生儿死亡，撒哈拉以南非洲和南亚负担最高。印度2015—2023年新生儿死亡率由28/1000活产降至17/1000活产，设施内新生儿照护快速扩展，但早产、低出生体重、产时并发症、感染、抗菌药物耐药、可及性不均、人力不足和随访不足仍是主要挑战。疾病谱随极早产儿存活增加而改变，BPD、NEC、ROP、脑损伤和发育障碍更受关注，研究正转向预测、预防、精准医学和实施科学。
- 临床启示：临床实践应将评价标准从“存活”扩展到质量生存和长期发展潜能，强化家庭整合照护、零分离、KMC及长期随访。未来应优先推进早产预防、精准脓毒症管理、呼吸与脑

**原文摘要**：Neonatology has undergone a remarkable transformation over the past six decades, evolving from a discipline focused predominantly on preventing neonatal death to one increasingly concerned with quality of survival, neurodevelopment, family-integrated care and long-term health. Despite substantial progress, neonatal mortality remains a major global health challenge. In 2024, approximately 2.3 million newborns died globally, with the highest mortality rates in sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia. India has achieved a substantial decline in neonatal mortality, from 28 to 17 per 1000 live births between 2015 and 2023, accompanied by rapid expansion of facility-based newborn care. However, prematurity, low birth weight, intrapartum complications, infection, antimicrobial resistance, inequitable access, workforce constraints and inadequate long-term follow-up remain major challenges. The epidemiology of neonatal disease is also changing, with increasing survival of extremely premature infants and greater recognition of bronchopulmonary dysplasia, necrotizing enterocolitis, retinopathy of prematurity, brain injury and developmental impairment. Neonatal research is similarly moving from descriptive epidemiology and rescue therapy towards prediction, prevention, precision medicine and implementation science. The next decade should prioritize prevention of prematurity, precision sepsis management, respiratory and brain protection, nutrition and gut health, Kangaroo Mother Care, zero separation, artificial intelligence and predictive analytics, long-term outcomes and affordable innovations. The ultimate measure of neonatal care should move beyond survival to enabling every newborn to develop and reach their full potential.

## Human amniotic fluid stem cell-derived extracellular vesicles enhance enteric neural crest cell extension following transplantation into aganglionic mouse gut.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:53
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 279-0021 · Juntendo University Urayasu Hospital
- 通讯作者: Nakazawa-Tanaka N (nana-n@juntendo.ac.jp)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06699-7
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06699-7) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42931968/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨人羊水干细胞来源细胞外囊泡（hAFSC-EVs）能否增强移植肠神经嵴衍生细胞（ENCC）在无神经节肠管中的延伸。
- 核心发现/数据：将E18.5 Sox10-Venus+ ENCC神经球移植至E12.5 Ednrb敲除小鼠无神经节肠管后，1% hAFSC-EVs处理组72小时最大延伸距离显著大于对照组（483.9±55.7 vs. 303.2±119.9 μm，P=0.049），延伸面积亦显著增加（45,546±18,623 vs. 15,049±11,510 μm²，P=0.039）。EV处理组更易见与Sox10-Venus和TUJ1阳性结构密切相关的Ki67阳性核，RNA测序显示氧化磷酸化及细胞周期相关基因上调。
- 临床启示：hAFSC-EVs可能作为增强ENCC移植再生策略的辅助手段，为先天性巨结肠（HSCR）细胞治疗提供新思路，但尚需进一步转化研究验证。

**原文摘要**：Enteric neural crest-derived cell (ENCC) transplantation is a potential regenerative strategy for Hirschsprung disease (HSCR), but approaches to enhance transplanted ENCC extension remain limited. We investigated whether human amniotic fluid stem cell-derived extracellular vesicles (hAFSC-EVs) enhance ENCC extension in aganglionic gut. Neurospheres (NS) generated from embryonic day 18.5 (E18.5) Sox10-Venus+ ENCCs were transplanted into aganglionic gut from E12.5 Ednrb knockout mice and cultured with (EV+, n = 4) or without (EV-, n = 4) 1% hAFSC-EVs. ENCC extension was assessed over 72 h, and explants were examined by immunofluorescence staining. RNA sequencing was performed on Sox10-Venus+ ENCC-derived NS cultured with or without 1% hAFSC-EVs. At 72 h, maximum ENCC extension was greater in EV+ than EV- explants (483.9 ± 55.7 vs. 303.2 ± 119.9 μm, P = 0.049), as was extension area (45,546 ± 18,623 vs. 15,049 ± 11,510 μm², P = 0.039). Ki67-positive nuclei in close association with Sox10-Venus- and TUJ1-positive structures were more readily observed in EV-treated explants. RNA sequencing demonstrated upregulation of oxidative phosphorylation- and cell-cycle-related genes. hAFSC-EVs enhanced transplanted ENCC extension in aganglionic gut, accompanied by transcriptional changes in bioenergetic and proliferative pathways. These findings suggest that hAFSC-EVs may provide a strategy to enhance ENCC-based regenerative approaches for HSCR.
