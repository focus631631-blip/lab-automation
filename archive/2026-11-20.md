## Vaping-Related Pneumomediastinum in Adolescents: Characterizing Work Up and Management.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:52
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: B232 · Children's Hospital Colorado
- 通讯作者: Vincent SA (Sage.Vincent@cuanschutz.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163600
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163600) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43057460/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：描述青少年电子烟相关纵隔气肿的人群特征及诊疗实践差异，以改善未来管理。
- 核心发现/数据：单中心回顾性研究纳入41例青少年，中位发病年龄16岁（14-18岁）；73.2%报告使用尼古丁电子烟、78.0%报告使用THC电子烟，超过50%为每日使用。68.3%需住院，中位住院2天（IQR 1-3天）；3例入住ICU，其中1例因低氧和意识迟钝需插管。住院患者胸片次数多于急诊离院者（中位2次 vs 1次，p=0.027）；19例接受食管造影均未发现损伤，无需干预且随访无复发。
- 临床启示：青少年电子烟相关纵隔气肿的影像学检查等管理差异较大；多数患者可能无需住院或转至专科儿童机构，除非存在低氧或呼吸窘迫。

**原文摘要**：Adolescent vaping, including nicotine and tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) products, carries numerous adverse effects. Case reports, primarily in young adults, describe an association between inhaled substance use and pneumomediastinum, but management of affected adolescents remains unclear. This study sought to characterize this population and identify variations in practice patterns to improve future care. A single center, retrospective descriptive study with 60-day follow-up was conducted. The hospital's radiology database was searched for imaging studies noting "pneumomediastinum" in patients >10 and 18 years from January 2014-May 2025. Patients with pneumomediastinum secondary to another etiology (ex: trauma, recent surgery) were excluded. Those reporting recent inhaled substance use were included. Forty-one adolescents met inclusion criteria. Median age at symptom onset was 16 years (range 14-18 years). Nicotine and THC vaping were reported in 73.2% and 78.0% of patients, respectively, with >50% using daily. Hospital admission occurred in 68.3% of cases with median length of stay of 2 days (IQR 1, 3). Three patients required intensive care unit admission, one of whom was hypoxic and obtunded at presentation necessitating intubation. Admitted patients underwent more chest radiographs than those discharged from the emergency department (median 2 vs 1, p=0.027). Nineteen patients underwent esophagram, none demonstrating injury. No interventions were required and no patient experienced recurrence during follow up. Management of vaping-related pneumomediastinum in adolescents, notably the use of imaging, varies considerably. Most patients may not require admission or transfer to a specialized pediatric facility unless presenting with hypoxia or respiratory distress.

## Endoscopic Ablation Techniques for Posterior Urethral Valves: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Failure and Stricture Rates.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:52
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Chennai · Sri Ramachandra Institute of Higher Education and Research
- 通讯作者: Babu R (drrameshbabu1@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163599
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163599) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43057459/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估冷刀、电灼（热刀）和激光消融及单点与三点消融治疗儿童后尿道瓣膜（PUV）的失败率与尿道狭窄风险。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入15项研究共953例患儿，冷刀、激光和电灼的合并失败率分别为3%（95%CI 0-7%）、9%（95%CI 3-17%）和10%（95%CI 4-18%）；单点（12点）消融复发率为7%，低于三点消融的11%。4项单中心比较研究显示，冷刀较电灼术后尿道狭窄风险更低（OR 0.31，95%CI 0.11-0.84）。但现有证据多来自非比较性观察研究，定义和随访异质性较大。
- 临床启示：冷刀消融在失败率和术后狭窄风险方面相对更优，激光可作为可行替代方案；单点前壁消融可能降低事件风险，但仍需前瞻性、标准化定义的比较研究进一步确认。

**原文摘要**：While several endoscopic modalities exist for posterior urethral valve (PUV) ablation, the optimal energy source and ablation technique remain debated. This systematic review evaluated the comparative efficacy of cold knife, diathermy (hot knife), and laser ablation, as well as the outcomes of single-point versus three-point ablations. A systematic literature search of PubMed and Embase was conducted in accordance with PRISMA 2020 guidelines for studies published during 2005-2026. Eligible studies included children undergoing primary endoscopic PUV ablation that reported follow-up outcomes confirmed via cystoscopy or voiding cystourethrography (VCUG). Fifteen studies comprising 953 patients met the inclusion criteria. The pooled failure rate (recurrence/residual) was lowest in cold knife cohort at 3% (95% CI: 0-7%), followed by laser ablation at 9% (95% CI: 3-17%) and diathermy fulguration at 10% (95% CI: 4-18%). Among the included heterogenous studies, single-point fulguration (12 o'clock only) demonstrated a lower recurrence rate of 7% (95% CI: 0-17%) compared to 11% (95% CI: 6-17%) for three-point fulguration. In four single-center comparative studies, cold knife ablation was associated with lower odds of postoperative urethral stricture compared with diathermy (OR: 0.31, 95% CI: 0.11-0.84). Cold knife ablation had the lowest pooled observed event proportion; however, these estimates derive predominantly from non-comparative observational studies with heterogeneous definitions and follow-up. Laser ablation appears to be a feasible alternative, with an intermediate pooled event rate. Single-point anterior fulguration may reduce event risk compared with three-point fulguration, but comparative evidence remains insufficient. Given the heterogeneity of the available literature, prospective comparative studies with standardized definitions and follow-up are warranted. CRD420261486567.

## Imaging Practices Before and After Transfer in Children Undergoing Appendectomy for Appendicitis.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:52
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2

- 核心发现/数据：在73,879例患儿中，转诊前影像以仅CT最常见（53.3%），其次为仅超声（32.9%）、CT+超声（12.9%）、含MRI（0.9%）。转诊后额外影像在仅超声后为35.7%，仅CT后为7.8%，CT+超声后为6.6%（P<0.001），重复CT仅1.5%。无超声记录的CT使用从2016年60.7%降至2024年51.8%（P<0.001），青少年（aOR 2.44）和较高年龄别体重z评分（每增加1个SD，aOR 1.19）与之相关；仅超声转诊与转诊后额外影像强相关（aOR 6.40）。

**原文摘要**：Ultrasound (US)-first imaging is recommended for children with suspected appendicitis, yet computed tomography (CT) remains common. We evaluated cross-institution imaging patterns among transferred children undergoing appendectomy. American College of Surgeons National Surgical Quality Improvement Program-Pediatric (ACS NSQIP-P) data from 2016-2024 were used to identify children undergoing appendectomy after transfer from a non-NSQIP-P hospital. Referring-facility imaging was classified as CT-only, US-only, CT+US, or MRI-containing. Additional imaging included CT, US, or MRI at the receiving hospital. Multivariable logistic regression identified factors associated with CT without documented US and additional imaging after transfer. Among 73,879 children, referring imaging was CT-only in 53.3%, US-only in 32.9%, CT+US in 12.9%, and MRI-containing in 0.9%. Additional imaging occurred in 7.8% after CT-only, 35.7% after US-only, and 6.6% after CT+US imaging (P<0.001); repeat CT was uncommon (1.5%). CT without documented US declined from 60.7% in 2016 to 51.8% in 2024 (overall P<0.001), with an early decline followed by fluctuation. Adolescent age (adjusted odds ratio [aOR], 2.44; 95% CI, 2.36-2.52) and higher weight-for-age z score (aOR per 1-SD increase, 1.19; 95% CI, 1.18-1.21) were associated with CT without documented US. US-only referring imaging was strongly associated with additional imaging (aOR, 6.40; 95% CI, 6.10-6.71). CT without documented US remained the predominant referring pattern, while repeat CT was uncommon. More than one-third of children transferred after US-only evaluation underwent additional imaging. These findings support further investigation of persistent CT use before transfer and the clinical value of additional imaging after transfer. Retrospective cohort study LEVEL OF EVIDENCE: III.

## State of The Art of The da Silva's Cone Operation for Treatment of Ebstein's Anomaly.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:52
- 期刊: Braz J Cardiovasc Surg · 中科院医学4区(心脏和心血管系统4区/外科4区) · IF 1.4 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University of Pittsburgh Medical Center Department of Cardiothoracic Surgery
- 通讯作者: Silva LDFD
- DOI: 10.21470/1678-9741-2026-0180
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.21470/1678-9741-2026-0180) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43056614/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：总结da Silva锥形修复术（CR）治疗Ebstein畸形（EA）的技术现状、适应证、预后及专家共识。
- 核心发现/数据：CR是最符合解剖的EA修复技术，适用于各型EA，多数患者效果最佳，具有低死亡率和低并发症、有效且持久、极少需三尖瓣置换的优点，并支持在右心室显著扩大或功能不全前手术。CR可用于任何年龄，但危重新生儿风险较高，可先行Starnes手术、后期再行CR的分期策略。2025年AATS专家共识涵盖术前评估、新生儿/儿童/成人手术适应证与禁忌证、EA相关心律失常处理及三尖瓣置换、双向腔肺分流等替代术式，并认为有手术指征时CR修复三尖瓣有益。
- 临床启示：对有手术指征的EA患者，CR应作为优先考虑的解剖修复策略；危重新生儿宜个体化采用分期手术，并依据2025年AATS共识综合管理适应证、心律失常及替代术式。

**原文摘要**：Ebstein's anomaly (EA) is a complex congenital heart disease caused by embryological failure of the tricuspid valve (TV) leaflet delamination, leading to displacement of the leaflets and right ventricular myopathy. In 1958, Hunter and Lillehei began developing surgical techniques for the repair of EA. Their technique was followed by many others. However, those techniques presented significant limitations due to the wide range of anatomical variations in the disease. The Da Silva Cone repair (CR) for EA emerged as the most anatomical repair technique for all forms of the disease, yielding the best results for most patients. Since CR can be performed with low mortality and morbidity, is effective and durable, and rarely requires TV replacement, it has been widely adopted. It allowed that surgical intervention can be indicated before the development of significant right ventricular dilatation or dysfunction. CR can be applied to any age, but it carries more risk in the neonate with critical EA. Therefore, neonates with critical EA may benefit from a staged approach, performing the Starnes procedure in the neonatal period and the CR later. The 2025 American Association for Thoracic Surgery expert consensus on managing EA covered preoperative evaluation, surgical indications, and contraindications in neonates, children, and adults. Additionally, the expert consensus discussed the approach to cardiac arrhythmias associated with EA and alternative procedures for select cases, including TV replacement and a bidirectional cavopulmonary shunt, depending on patient characteristics. They also agreed that the CR repair of the TV is beneficial when surgery is indicated.

## Maintaining integrity amidst turmoil: A qualitative exploration of parental coping process during hematopoietic stem cell transplantation for pediatric cancer.

**原文摘要**：Childhood cancer, particularly pediatric hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (HSCT), imposes profound psychosocial and socioeconomic challenges on parents. This study aimed to develop a grounded theory explaining the dynamic coping process experienced by parents during their child's HSCT journey. This qualitative study used Corbin and Strauss's (2015) grounded theory approach to develop an explanatory account of the parental coping process during pediatric HSCT. Outcomes included "relative adaptation" and "self-actualization". Tailored support systems that address systemic gaps and cultural beliefs are essential for fostering adaptive coping. Strengthening health-seeking and emotional-cognitive strategies can promote functional stability and personal growth among parents.

## Emerging Insights into the Dysregulated Monocyte-Macrophage Axis in Necrotizing Enterocolitis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:52
- 期刊: Am J Perinatol · 中科院医学4区(妇产科学4区/儿科4区) · IF 1.4 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: Morgantown · West Virginia University School of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Garg PM
- DOI: 10.1055/a-3009-0071
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1055/a-3009-0071) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43057319/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：综合人类相关性数据、描述性转录组证据和小鼠研究，采用结构化证据分层框架阐明单核-巨噬细胞失调在坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）发病中的作用。
- 核心发现/数据：早产儿肠道巨噬细胞TLR4表达 paradoxically 升高，并呈胎儿来源的未成熟表型、缺乏耐受性程序；外周单核细胞减少，尤其绝对单核细胞计数连续百分比下降，在3个独立队列中与NEC发生和严重程度相关，可作为零成本临床生物标志物。单细胞转录组在NEC组织中鉴定出4个功能不同的巨噬细胞亚群，以氧化应激相关、损伤放大亚型为主。小鼠研究显示选择性清除巨噬细胞可减轻输血相关NEC样损伤，抑制巨噬细胞铁死亡可限制上皮损伤；肠源性HMGB1激活小胶质细胞TLR4，将肠道损伤与幸存者神经发育损害联系起来。
- 临床启示：上述证据将NEC重新定义为髓系失衡性疾病，支持TLR4调控和铁死亡靶向等巨噬细胞靶向策略作为未来预防和治疗方向；动态监测外周单核细胞变化可能有助于NEC风险分层。

**原文摘要**：To synthesize correlative human, descriptive transcriptomic, and murine evidence on the role of monocyte-macrophage dysregulation in the pathogenesis of necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC), using a structured evidence-tier framework not previously applied to this literature. Narrative synthesis and critical appraisal of the peer-reviewed literature on monocyte and macrophage biology in NEC, organized by evidentiary tier: correlative human biomarker and tissue data, descriptive single-cell transcriptomic profiling, and interventional murine studies (macrophage depletion and ferroptosis inhibition). The preterm intestinal macrophage shows paradoxically elevated TLR4 expression and an immature, fetal-derived phenotype lacking tolerogenic programming. Peripheral monocytopenia, particularly serial percentage decline in absolute monocyte count, correlates with NEC onset and severity across three independent cohorts and offers a zero-cost clinical biomarker. Single-cell transcriptomics identifies four functionally distinct macrophage subpopulations in NEC tissue, dominated by an oxidative-stress-related, injury-amplifying subtype. Murine studies provide supportive evidence for a mechanistic contribution: selective macrophage depletion attenuates transfusion-associated NEC-like injury, and pharmacologic inhibition of macrophage ferroptosis limits epithelial damage. Separately, epithelium-derived HMGB1 activates microglial TLR4, a resident macrophage-lineage population, linking intestinal injury to the neurodevelopmental impairment observed in survivors. Dysregulation of monocyte-macrophage pathways plays a significant role in the pathogenesis of NEC, supported by convergent correlative, descriptive, and interventional evidence. These findings reframe NEC as a disease of myeloid imbalance and identify macrophage-targeted strategies, including TLR4 modulation and ferroptosis-targeted therapy, as rational directions for future prevention and treatment.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估健康社会决定因素对Hirschsprung相关小肠结肠炎（HAEC）发生风险的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：单中心回顾性研究纳入60例0-6岁原发性Hirschsprung拖出术后患者，53.3%发生HAEC。校正临床风险后，监护人婚姻状况为分居与HAEC显著相关（HR 3.10，95% CI 1.14-8.41，p=0.026）；低于中位收入（HR 1.56，p=0.3）和居住于机构所在城市（HR 1.36，p=0.4）呈非显著升高趋势，移民身份、社工参与和可见少数族裔状态呈非显著降低趋势。

Separated guardian status was the only social factor significantly associated with HAEC. These findings suggest that social determinants may influence outcomes in Hirschsprung's disease and warrant further multi-center studies.
