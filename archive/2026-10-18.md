## Natural history and perinatal outcomes of antenatally diagnosed CPAM in a resource-limited setting.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 08:32
- 期刊: Int J Gynaecol Obstet
- 国家/单位: 印度 · Birthright by Rainbow Hospitals
- 通讯作者: Thakur S
- DOI: 10.1002/ijgo.71459
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/ijgo.71459) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42891504/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估资源有限的三级胎儿医学中心中产前诊断的先天性肺气道畸形（CPAM）胎儿的产前自然史、预后因素及围产结局。
- 核心发现/数据：研究期间共12,058例畸形筛查中诊断22例CPAM，患病率0.18%；微囊型占68%，自发消退/平台期合计59.1%。CPAM体积比（CVR）>1.6者2例，其中1例发生胎儿水肿并宫内死亡；22例中19例活产，有出生后即时资料的17例中82.4%无症状，有出生后影像的17例中52.9%病灶持续、41.2%完全消退。5例新生儿行肺叶切除术，均预后良好。
- 临床启示：多数产前诊断的孤立性CPAM自然史良性且围产结局良好，支持对经选择的非水肿胎儿采取保守管理；但仍需更大队列及标准化出生后影像研究验证。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate the antenatal natural history, prognostic factors, and perinatal outcomes of fetuses diagnosed with congenital pulmonary airway malformation (CPAM) in a resource-limited tertiary fetal medicine setting. This single-center retrospective observational case series included 22 fetuses diagnosed with CPAM between 2018 and 2025 at a tertiary fetal medicine unit. Clinical, sonographic, and postnatal outcome data were obtained from electronic medical records and parental follow-up. Lesions were categorized based on sonographic morphology, and serial congenital pulmonary airway malformation volume ratio (CVR) measurements were assessed to evaluate antenatal progression and prognostic correlation. Among 12 058 anomaly scans conducted during the study period, 22 fetuses were diagnosed with CPAM, giving a prevalence of 0.18%. Microcystic CPAM was the predominant subtype (15/22, 68%). Spontaneous regression occurred in 5/22 (22.7%) cases, while 8/22 (36.4%) demonstrated lesion plateauing, resulting in an overall regression/plateau rate of 59.1%. A CVR >1.6 was observed in two fetuses; one developed hydrops followed by intrauterine demise, while the other resulted in a live birth. Among the 22 antenatally diagnosed fetuses, outcomes included two terminations of pregnancy, one intrauterine demise and 19 live births. Immediate postnatal outcome data were available for 17/19 neonates: 3/17 (17.6%) had respiratory distress, while 14/17 (82.4%) were asymptomatic at birth. Postnatal imaging was available in 17/19 (89.5%) liveborn neonates and demonstrated persistent lesions in 9/17 (52.9%) and apparent complete resolution in 7/17 (41.2%). Overall, five neonates underwent lobectomy, all with favorable outcomes. Most antenatally diagnosed isolated CPAMs demonstrated a benign natural history with favorable perinatal outcomes. These findings support conservative management in carefully selected non-hydropic fetuses; however, larger prospective studies with standardized postnatal imaging are required.

## Outcome after vacuum-assisted closure in patients with anorectal malformations-a multi-center comparative study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 15:16
- 期刊: Langenbecks Arch Surg · 中科院医学3区(外科3区) · IF 2.2 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Hannover · Hannover Medical School
- 通讯作者: Borsch LM (borsch.lisa-marie@mh-hannover.de)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00423-026-04334-6
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-026-04334-6) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42891717/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过多中心比较研究评估真空辅助闭合（VAC）对肛门直肠畸形（ARM）患者后矢状入路肛门直肠成形术（PSARP）术后结局尤其是会阴伤口并发症的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入48例患者，其中VAC组26例、非VAC组22例，两组基线特征及随访情况可比。术后会阴伤口裂开在VAC组为2例（8%）、非VAC组为1例（5%），差异无统计学意义（p>0.99）；粪便污染分别为0例和1例（p>0.99），两组均未观察到失禁。
- 临床启示：本研究中VAC治疗未显著降低PSARP术后会阴伤口裂开发生率，暂不支持基于现有证据常规推广VAC；仍需更大规模前瞻性研究加以验证。

**原文摘要**：In patients with anorectal malformations (ARM), perineal wound complications following posterior sagittal anorectoplasty (PSARP) remain a challenge with an occurrence of 22%-43%. In adults, vacuum-assisted closure (VAC) aims to support wound healing after colorectal procedures. In children evidence is limited, suggesting a potential benefit of VAC therapy after PSARP. We aimed to evaluate the impact of VAC therapy on outcomes after PSARP in a multicenter comparative study. All consecutive patients who underwent PSARP from 01/2018-12/2025 in two participating pediatric surgical departments were included. Complications including postoperative perineal wound dehiscence were analyzed. The study was approved by the local ethics committee (approval number: 12354-BO-K-2026). 48 patients were included: 26 with and 22 without VAC therapy. Baseline characteristics, follow-up duration and the interval until first follow-up were comparable between cohorts respectively. Postoperative outcomes did not differ significantly between groups. Stool smearing occurred in none of the patients in the VAC cohort and in one patient in the non-VAC cohort (p > 0.99). No incontinence was observed in either cohort (p > 0.99). Perineal wound dehiscence occurred in two patients (8%) in the VAC and in one (5%) in the non-VAC cohort (p > 0.99). In our study, VAC therapy was associated with comparable rates of postoperative wound dehiscence after PSARP compared to the non-VAC cohort and to previous reports. Larger, prospective studies are required to confirm our findings.
