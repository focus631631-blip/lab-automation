## Intestinal GPR128 deficiency protects against acetaminophen-induced liver injury through the gut-liver axis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:11
- 期刊: Cell Mol Gastroenterol Hepatol · 中科院医学1区(胃肠肝病学2区) · IF 8.2 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Henan Clinical Research Center of Childhood Diseases
- 通讯作者: Zhou F (zhoufang_78@126.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jcmgh.2026.101925
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcmgh.2026.101925) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42962887/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探究肠道GPR128缺失是否通过菌群依赖的肠肝轴机制调节对乙酰氨基酚诱导的肝损伤（AILI）易感性。
- 核心发现/数据：肠道GPR128缺陷小鼠在APAP攻击后肝损伤受保护，并伴随杯状细胞黏液层稳态保留及黏液相关*Akkermansia muciniphila*富集；该菌相关β-半乳糖苷酶活性促进膳食龙胆酸（GA）释放并升高全身GA水平。GA通过HSP90α减少其与CBFβ结合，抑制CBFβ核转位及RUNX1依赖的UPP1转录；抑制UPP1可降低尿苷来源乳酸生成并改善AILI。
- 临床启示：肠道GPR128

**原文摘要**：Acetaminophen (APAP)-induced liver injury (AILI) is the leading cause of acute liver failure, but the intestinal host gene-microbe interactions that regulate AILI through the gut-liver axis remain poorly defined. We investigated whether intestinal GPR128 regulates susceptibility to AILI and sought to elucidate the underlying microbiota-dependent molecular mechanisms. We used intestinal GPR128-deficient mice challenged with APAP to assess liver injury, goblet cell and mucus-layer integrity, gut microbiota composition, and microbial β-galactosidase activity. The roles of Akkermansia muciniphila, dietary gentisic acid (GA), and the HSP90α-CBFβ/RUNX1-UPP1 pathway were evaluated using mouse, cellular, biochemical, and metabolic assays. Intestinal GPR128 deficiency protected mice from AILI and was associated with preservation of goblet cell mucus-layer homeostasis and enrichment of mucus-associated A. muciniphila. A. muciniphila-associated β-galactosidase activity promoted dietary GA liberation and increased systemic GA levels. GA appeared to act through HSP90α and concomitantly reduced its interaction with CBFβ, thereby inhibiting CBFβ nuclear translocation and suppressing RUNX1-dependent UPP1 transcription. Inhibition of UPP1 reduced uridine-derived lactate production and ameliorated AILI. Intestinal GPR128 deficiency protects against AILI through a gut-liver axis involving goblet cell-associated A. muciniphila, microbial β-galactosidase, dietary GA, and the HSP90α-CBFβ/RUNX1-UPP1 pathway. These findings identify this cascade as a potential therapeutic target for APAP-induced liver injury.

## The psychosocial impact of living with cloacal malformations in young people: A qualitative study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:11
- 期刊: J Pediatr Adolesc Gynecol · 中科院医学4区(妇产科学4区/儿科4区) · IF 2.0 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 英国 · Leeds Institute of Health Sciences
- 通讯作者: Turgoose D (d.turgoose@leeds.ac.uk)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpag.2026.10.007
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpag.2026.10.007) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42962848/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨年轻泄殖腔畸形患者如何与疾病共同生活，以及其过去和持续治疗的心理社会影响与体验。
- 核心发现/数据：研究访谈了8名来自英国、中欧、北美和澳大利亚的16—38岁患者，提炼出“身体自我感”“医学复杂性”“与泄殖腔畸形共同成长”三大主题。治疗和症状管理会影响身体感知与性身份发展，患者易产生医疗焦虑和创伤，并需在身份整合、疾病披露及同伴连接中完成意义建构。成长过程中的疾病与治疗可显著影响长期心理福祉和身份形成。
- 临床启示：应将心理社会评估与支持纳入从首次接触到全生命周期的常规外科和临床护理中。

**原文摘要**：Cloacal malformations are rare congenital condition affecting females, characterised by the convergence of the urinary tract, reproductive tract, and rectum into a single channel. Complex medical interventions are often required from the neonatal period onwards, with potential lifelong implications for bowel, urinary, and gynaecological health. Few centres have substantial experience in treatment and long-term support. Research on the lived experience and psychosocial impact of cloacal malformations is limited, particularly during the transition from adolescence to adulthood. This qualitative study explored how young people born with cloacal malformations live with the condition and what their experiences are of past and ongoing treatment. One-to-one interviews were conducted and analysed using grounded theory and major themes identified. Eight participants (aged 16-38) from the UK, Central Europe, North America and Australia were interviewed. Three themes were developed. Sense of the bodily self highlights the impact of treatment and symptom management on body perception and the development of sexual identity. Medical complexity reflects awareness of the complexity of the condition and treatment, interactions with medical professionals, self-management and vulnerability to medical anxiety and trauma. Growing up with the impact of cloaca explores how cloacal malformations are integrated into identity, including decisions around disclosure, and how connecting with others with cloaca offers sense-making opportunities. Growing up with a cloacal malformation and the necessary treatments can significantly impact long term psychological wellbeing and identity formation. It is recommended that psychological aspects are considered as part of routine surgical and clinical care from first contact across the lifespan.

## Dual-mode colorimetric sensing of bilirubin using a PDDA@peroxomolybdate composite: A spectrophotometric platform and cost-effective cotton-swab sensor for point-of-care testing.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:11
- 期刊: Spectrochim Acta A Mol Biomol Spectrosc · 中科院化学2区(光谱学2区) · IF 4.8 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: Turkiye · Istanbul University-Cerrahpaşa
- 通讯作者: Filik H (filik@iuc.edu.tr)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.saa.2026.129041
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.saa.2026.129041) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42962526/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：开发基于PDDA@过氧钼酸盐复合物的双模式比色传感平台，用于胆红素床旁检测，并整合实验室分光光度法与智能手机辅助棉签传感器。
- 核心发现/数据：分光光度模式检测线性范围为0.1–4.5 mg/dL（1.72–77.0 μM），检出限为0.032 mg/dL（0.54 μM）；智能手机棉签模式基于归一化红通道强度，线性范围为0.1–9.0 mg/dL（1.72–154 μM），检出限为0.046 mg/dL（0.78 μM）。该平台具有良好选择性，并已在成人及新生儿血清样本中验证其实际适用性。
- 临床启示：该平台有望为新生儿黄疸提供低成本、可及且灵活的床旁胆红素筛查与监测工具，可根据临床需求选择实验室精确定量或现场宽范围筛查。

**原文摘要**：In this work, the synthesis and characterization of a poly(diallyldimethylammonium chloride) (PDDA)@peroxomolybdate composite are presented for the development of a dual-mode colorimetric sensing platform for point-of-care bilirubin (BLR) detection. The platform combines a laboratory-based spectrophotometric assay with a smartphone-assisted cotton-swab sensor, enabling versatile and accessible screening. The spectrophotometric mode exhibited a wide linear detection range of 0.1-4.5 mg/dL (1.72-77.0 μM) and a limit of detection (LOD) of 0.032 mg/dL (0.54 μM). In parallel, the smartphone-based assay, utilizing normalized red-channel intensity values, demonstrated a broader linear range of 0.1-9.0 mg/dL (1.72-154 μM) with an LOD of 0.046 mg/dL (0.78 μM). This dual-mode sensing approach offers flexibility by letting users choose between the high analytical accuracy of laboratory instrumentation and the convenience of wide-range, on-site screening, depending on clinical requirements. Furthermore, the selectivity of the platform was comprehensively evaluated, and its practical applicability was successfully validated using adult and neonatal blood serum samples. The results highlight the strong potential of the proposed platform for reliable clinical monitoring of BLR levels.

## Clinical effects of multi-strain probiotics in extremely preterm infants: a multicenter pre-post implementation study in the Netherlands.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:11
- 期刊: Eur J Pediatr · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区) · IF 2.9 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: Amsterdam · Emma Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: van Wesemael AJ (a.j.vanwesemael@amsterdamumc.nl)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00431-026-07560-8
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-026-07560-8) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42963493/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估多菌株益生菌作为标准治疗在极早产儿（胎龄<28周）中降低坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）、晚发型脓毒症（LOS）和死亡等临床结局的效果。
- 核心发现/数据：荷兰6家NICU的481例极早产儿（实施前212例、实施后269例）显示，实施后校正NEC风险显著降低（OR 0.49，95%CI 0.25-0.99，p=0.049）；LOS（OR 0.87，p=0.49）和全因死亡（OR 0.67，p=0.22）无统计学显著差异。达到全肠内喂养时间缩短2天（p<0.001），生长速度增加1.8 g/kg/day（p<0.001），益生菌相关脓毒症仅1例（0.4%）。
- 临床启示：多菌株益生菌与极早产儿NEC减少、喂养进程加快和早期生长改善相关，支持在该脆弱人群中继续使用，但仍需监测益生菌相关脓毒症及LOS/死亡率等安全结局。

**原文摘要**：Probiotics may reduce the risk of necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC), late-onset sepsis (LOS), and mortality in preterm infants. However, their efficacy in extremely preterm infants (gestational age < 28 weeks) is still regularly questioned due to limited data and safety concerns. In Dutch Neonatal Intensive Care Units (NICUs), a three-strain probiotic product (ProPrems®; Bifidobacterium infantis Bb-02, Bifidobacterium lactis BB-12, and Streptococcus thermophilus TH-4) was implemented as standard of care from 2021 onwards. This study evaluates the clinical effects of these probiotics in extremely preterm infants. Pre- and postimplementation cohorts of extremely preterm infants from 6 Dutch NICUs (2018-2023) were compared. Clinical data from the first 29 days of life were collected. Incidence of NEC (modified Bell's stage ≥ IIA), LOS, and mortality were compared with logistic regression; growth velocity with linear regression; and time to full enteral feeds (tFEF) with survival analyses. In total, 481 extremely preterm infants (212 pre-, 269 post-implementation) were included. After implementation, the adjusted odds ratio was 0.49 (0.25-0.99; p = 0.049) for NEC; 0.87 (0.59-1.28; p = 0.49) for LOS; and 0.67 (0.35-1.27; p = 0.22) for all-cause mortality. Furthermore, tFEF decreased by 2 days (p < 0.001) and probiotic use was associated with an increase in growth velocity of 1.8 g/kg/day (p < 0.001). One infant (0.4%) developed probiotic-associated sepsis.  This multicenter pre-postimplementation study showed that multi-strain probiotic administration was associated with lower NEC incidence, shorter tFEF, and greater growth velocity in extremely preterm infants during the first four weeks of life. Probiotic-associated sepsis was rare. These results support continued use of probiotics in this vulnerable population. • Certain probiotic strains effectively reduce incidence of necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) in preterm infants, but the efficacy and safety for extremely preterm infants (GA < 28 weeks) remain under debate. • This study shows that a multi-strain probiotic supplement was associated with lower NEC incidence, shorter time to full enteral feeds, and greater early postnatal growth in a Dutch multicenter cohort of extremely preterm infants. • The incidence of probiotic-associated sepsis in extremely preterm infants was very low in our cohort with just 1 case (0.4%), consistent with the rare occurrence reported previously.
