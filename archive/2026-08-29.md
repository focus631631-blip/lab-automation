## Comparative Outcomes of Laparoscopic Inguinal Hernia Repair in Children: With or Without Peritoneal Dissection.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:46
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 韩国 · Busan Paik Hospital
- 通讯作者: Nam SH (d011029@naver.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163399
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163399) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42665037/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估儿童腹腔镜腹股沟疝修补术中省略腹膜分离是否影响复发率、术后并发症或手术时间。
- 核心发现/数据：628例患儿中，未分离腹膜组（238例）与分离组（390例）的手术时间（33 vs 32分钟）、复发率（0.8% vs 0.5%）及总体并发症发生率（2.1% vs 5.4%）均无显著差异（p>0.05）。远端疝囊切除虽显著延长手术时间（75 vs 30分钟，p<0.001），但未增加并发症或复发风险。
- 临床启示：儿童腹腔镜腹股沟疝修补术无需常规进行腹膜分离，可获得不劣的短期结局；远端疝囊切除仅应选择性应用于特定病例。

**原文摘要**：The necessity of routine peritoneal dissection during pediatric laparoscopic inguinal hernia repair remains uncertain. This study evaluated whether omitting peritoneal dissection affects recurrence, postoperative complications, or operative time after laparoscopic intracorporeal inguinal hernia repair in children. In this multicenter retrospective cohort study, children younger than 8 years who underwent elective laparoscopic inguinal hernia repair at six tertiary centers in South Korea (January 2023-December 2024) were grouped according to whether peritoneal dissection was performed. Distal sac removal was analyzed as a subgroup procedure within the dissection group. All patients had 1-year follow-up, and propensity score matching was used to reduce baseline imbalance. Of 628 patients, 238 underwent repair without dissection and 390 with dissection. Operative time, recurrence, and overall complications did not differ significantly between groups (33 vs 32 minutes; 0.8% vs 0.5%; 2.1% vs 5.4%; all p>0.05), with similar results after matching. Distal sac removal was associated with longer operative time (75 vs 30 minutes, p<0.001) but not with higher complication or recurrence rates. Laparoscopic intracorporeal inguinal hernia repair without routine peritoneal dissection was not associated with worse outcomes. Distal sac removal prolonged operative time and should be reserved for selected cases. Prospective studies incorporating standardized anatomical measurements are warranted.

## Feasibility of Magnetic Compression-Mediated Transection of Rabbit Xiphoid Cartilage: A Simulation Study for Tracheobronchial Remnant-Type Congenital Esophageal Stenosis.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:46
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · The First Affiliated Hospital of Xi'an Jiaotong University
- 通讯作者: Liu S (liushiqi23@foxmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163430
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163430) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42665035/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估磁压缩装置能否有效离断兔剑突软骨，为气管支气管残留型先天性食管狭窄的软骨离断提供概念验证。
- 核心发现/数据：所有实验组兔均实现软骨完全离断，平均时间11.83±0.41天；组织学未见明显炎症或纤维化，电镜示软骨细胞严重损伤及胶原纤维紊乱；实验组MMP-1蛋白和mRNA显著高于假手术组（P<0.05），TNF-α在术后第11天升高（vs对照组P<0.001，vs假手术组P<0.01），BMP-2在4周和8周时显著高于对照组和假手术组（分别P<0.001和P<0.01）。
- 临床启示：持续磁压缩可有效离断软骨，为未来构建模拟气管支气管残留型先天性食管狭窄的动物模型及潜在临床治疗提供了生物力学依据。

**原文摘要**：Tracheobronchial remnant (TBR)-type congenital esophageal stenosis (CES) is a rare infantile anomaly characterized by ectopic tracheobronchial cartilage within the esophageal wall. Magnetic compression devices are effective for soft-tissue approximation, but their ability to transect cartilage remains unproven. This study assessed magnetic compression-mediated transection of rabbit xiphoid cartilage. Eighteen rabbits were divided into control, sham-operated, and experimental groups (n = 6 each). Experimental rabbits received magnetic compression devices to compress the xiphoid cartilage; sham-operated rabbits underwent surgery without device placement. Radiography monitored angular changes and time to transection. After transection in the experimental group, cartilage specimens were collected from both experimental and sham groups for histopathology, transmission electron microscopy (TEM), immunohistochemistry, qPCR, and ELISA (measuring serum IL-1β, TNF-α, and BMP-2). All experimental rabbits achieved cartilage transection (mean, 11.83 ± 0.41 days). No significant inflammation or fibrosis was observed. TEM revealed severe chondrocyte damage and disorganized collagen fibers in the experimental group, whereas the sham group showed near-normal ultrastructure. MMP-1 protein and mRNA were significantly elevated in the experimental group versus the sham group (P < 0.05), whereas TIMP-1 showed no differences. TNF-α was higher in the experimental group on day 11 (vs. control, P < 0.001; vs. sham, P < 0.01) and remained elevated versus controls at 2 weeks (P < 0.01). IL-1β showed no intergroup differences. BMP-2 was significantly higher in the experimental group than in both the control and sham groups at 4 and 8 weeks (P < 0.001 and P < 0.01, respectively), but showed no difference versus the sham group at other timepoints. Sustained magnetic compression effectively transects rabbit xiphoid cartilage, providing key proof-of-concept evidence for cartilage transectability and establishing a foundational biomechanical rationale for future in vivo animal models mimicking TBR-type CES. not applicable.

## Intravenous magnesium sulfate for analgesia in spinal surgery: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:46
- 期刊: J Clin Anesth · 中科院医学2区(麻醉学2区) · IF 6.0 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Second Affiliated Hospital of Army Medical University
- 通讯作者: Dong X (drxueshandong@tmmu.edu.cn)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jclinane.2026.112306
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclinane.2026.112306) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42664719/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估脊柱手术围术期静脉输注硫酸镁对术后疼痛、阿片类药物消耗、恢复及不良事件的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：硫酸镁可降低术后24小时疼痛评分（MD: -0.55, 95% CI: -1.01至-0.10）和阿片类药物消耗（MD: -4.95 mg, 95% CI: -8.74至-1.15 mg），负荷量加维持量方案效果更显著；同时减少术中阿片用量，降低术后恶心呕吐发生率，但增加低血压风险，并可能延长苏醒及定向力恢复时间。
- 临床启示：围术期硫酸镁可改善脊柱手术镇痛并减少阿片用量，尤其适用于负荷量联合维持量方案，但对高危患者需加强血流动力学监测并警惕苏醒延迟风险。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate the effects of perioperative intravenous magnesium sulfate on postoperative pain, opioid consumption, recovery, and adverse events in patients undergoing spinal surgery. Systematic review with meta-analysis. Perioperative setting. Twenty-one randomized controlled trials including 1271 adult patients. Perioperative intravenous magnesium sulfate. Two researchers independently evaluated the risk of bias of included randomized controlled trials using the Cochrane Risk-of-Bias Tool 2.0 (RoB 2). Disagreements were settled by discussion or consultation with a third reviewer. We used funnel plots to detect publication bias when at least 10 studies were available, and adopted the leave-one-out sensitivity analysis to verify the robustness of pooled results. The certainty of evidence for each outcome was rated via the GRADE framework from five dimensions, with evidence classified into four grades: high, moderate, low and very low. Perioperative intravenous administration of magnesium sulfate can reduce postoperative 24-h pain scores (MD: -0.55, 95% CI: -1.01 to - 0.10, P = 0.02) and opioid consumption (MD: -4.95 mg, 95% CI: -8.74 to - 1.15 mg, P = 0.01). Furthermore, effect was more pronounced in the subgroup receiving a loading dose plus a maintenance dose. Perioperative intravenous magnesium administration significantly reduced intraoperative opioid consumption. Compared to the control group, magnesium sulfate was associated with a lower incidence of postoperative nausea and vomiting, but a higher incidence of hypotension. Meanwhile, intraoperative intravenous administration of magnesium sulfate appears to prolong the time to follow commands and the time to recovery of orientation. Perioperative magnesium sulfate improves analgesia and reduces opioid consumption in spinal surgery, especially with a bolus-plus-infusion regimen. However, it may delay emergence and increase hypotension risk. Careful hemodynamic monitoring is recommended for high-risk patients.

## Adverse neonatal outcomes and associated factors among births through operative vaginal delivery at the University of Gondar Comprehensive Specialized Hospital, Northwest Ethiopia: A facility-based retrospective cross-sectional study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:46
- 期刊: PLoS One · 中科院综合性期刊3区(综合性期刊3区) · IF 2.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Ethiopia · Bedele General Hospital
- 通讯作者: Ayele HM
- DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0357043
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0357043) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42664221/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估埃塞俄比亚西北部贡达尔大学综合专科医院经产钳或真空吸引器助产分娩的新生儿不良结局发生率及其相关因素。
- 核心发现/数据：新生儿不良结局发生率为23.1%（95% CI: 18.68-28.21）。羊水I/II度粪染（AOR=4.13, 95% CI: 1.96-8.69）、III度粪染（AOR=24.9, 95% CI: 6.14-46.06）及初产妇（AOR=2.49, 95% CI: 1.13-5.16）与不良结局显著相关。
- 临床启示：对羊水粪染及初产妇应加强产时监护并及早进行产科干预，以降低器械助产相关的新生儿不良结局风险。

**原文摘要**：Obstetric interventions can improve maternal and neonatal outcomes when performed appropriately by skilled healthcare professionals. Appropriate use of forceps and vacuum extraction can reduce neonatal complications associated with operative vaginal deliveries. However, there is limited evidence on the prevalence of adverse neonatal outcomes and associated factors among operative vaginal deliveries in Northwest Ethiopia. This study aimed to assess the prevalence of adverse neonatal outcomes and associated factors among births through operative vaginal delivery at the University of Gondar Comprehensive Specialized Hospital in Northwest Ethiopia. A facility-based retrospective cross-sectional study was conducted among 303 mothers who underwent operative vaginal delivery at the University of Gondar Comprehensive Specialized Hospital from September 1, 2019, to August 30, 2021. Data was extracted using a structured questionnaire. The collected data was entered into Epi-Data version 3.1 statistical software and exported to IBM SPSS statistics version 24 for data analysis. Descriptive statistics were used to summarize the participants' characteristics and neonatal outcomes. Bivariable logistic regression analysis was performed to identify candidate variables. Variables with a p-value < 0.25 were entered into the multivariable logistic regression model. Adjusted odds ratio (AOR) with 95% confidence intervals (CIs) were calculated to assess the strength of associations. Statistical significance was declared at a p-value of < 0.05 in the multivariable logistic regression analysis. The prevalence of adverse neonatal outcomes among births delivered through operative vaginal delivery was 23.1% (95% CI: 18.68-28.21). The mean age and standard deviation of the study participants were 25.19 ± 4.7 years. All participants were married and 154 (50.8%) had obtained a college education or higher. The presence of grade ½ (AOR = 4.13, 95% CI: 1.96-8.69) and grade three meconium-stained amniotic fluid (AOR = 24.9, 95% CI: 6.14-46.06) and primigravidity (AOR = 2.49, 95% CI: (1.13-5.16) were significantly associated with adverse neonatal outcomes. Adverse neonatal outcomes among births through operative vaginal delivery at the University of Gondar Comprehensive Specialized Hospital were high. Close intrapartum monitoring and early obstetric intervention among women with meconium-stained amniotic fluid and primigravid mothers are essential to reduce adverse neonatal outcomes associated with operative vaginal delivery.

## Colorectal Centre of Excellence (COCOE) Registry: A protocol for a prospective longitudinal cohort of children with Hirschsprung's disease and anorectal malformations in Canada.

- 期刊: PLoS One · 中科院综合性期刊3区(综合性期刊3区) · IF 2.8 · JCR Q2

- 通讯作者: Wissanji H

- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0356813) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42664155/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：建立加拿大首个针对先天性巨结肠和肛门直肠畸形的儿童前瞻性纵向队列注册登记系统。
- 核心发现/数据：该注册系统在蒙特利尔儿童医院连续纳入0-17岁确诊患儿，随访至18岁，使用REDCap收集标准化临床数据，并采用6种验证量表评估患者报告结局。研究已获伦理批准（MUHC REB No. 2026-10997），并在ClinicalTrials.gov注册（NCT07603232）。
- 临床启示：该注册系统将提供长期纵向数据，有助于优化临床随访、促进医疗规划、支持向成人医疗的公平过渡，并为参与国际多中心研究奠定基础。

**原文摘要**：Hirschsprung's disease (HD) and anorectal malformations (ARM) are congenital colorectal conditions requiring lifelong multidisciplinary care, yet no prospective, longitudinal registry exists for affected children in Canada. We describe the protocol for the COCOE Registry, the first prospective cohort of paediatric patients with HD and ARM at the Colorectal Centre of Excellence (COCOE), Montreal Children's Hospital, McGill University Health Centre, Montréal, Québec, Canada. The registry enrolls children aged 0-17 years with a documented diagnosis of HD or ARM on a continuous basis, with follow-up until age 18. Clinical data are abstracted from a standardised multidisciplinary assessment form and entered into REDCap. Patient-reported outcomes are collected using six validated instruments spanning disease-specific quality of life, continence, general well-being, family functioning, parental adjustment, and bowel management experience. Statistical analyses will include descriptive summaries, group comparisons, survival analyses, and multivariable logistic regression to identify factors associated with key outcomes, reported in accordance with STROBE guidelines. Research ethics approval has been granted by the McGill University Health Centre Research Ethics Board (MUHC REB No. 2026-10997). This study is registered with ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT07603232). The COCOE Registry will generate longitudinal data to improve clinical follow-up, inform healthcare planning, support equitable transitions to adult care, and enable participation in international multicentre research.

## Three-dimensional tracheal morphologic changes after left pulmonary artery sling repair with and without tracheoplasty.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:46
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 650-0047 · Hyogo Prefectural Kobe Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Morita K (keimorita1209@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06591-4
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06591-4) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42663739/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨左肺动脉吊带（LPAS）修复术联合或不联合滑行气管成形术对气管三维形态学变化的影响，以期为手术指征提供参考。
- 核心发现/数据：气管成形组（10例）术后隆突上30mm段等效直径显著增大（3.3 [2.4-4.1]→5.2 [4.2-6.7] mm；P=0.002），估算气流阻力显著下降（2.30 [0.83-9.6]→0.39 [0.11-1.88] cmH2O/(L/s)；P=0.004）。仅行LPAS修复组（3例）中，2例术前阻力较低（0.92和1.52 cmH2O/(L/s)），与气管成形组术后水平相当。
- 临床启示：滑行气管成形术可显著改善气管形态和功能，但部分患者仅行LPAS修复即可获得可接受的气道阻力；计算流体阻力可能有助于术前评估和手术决策。

**原文摘要**：In patients with a left pulmonary artery sling (LPAS) and congenital tracheal stenosis, indications for simultaneous slide tracheoplasty remain controversial. This study quantified three-dimensional tracheal morphologic changes after LPAS repair with and without slide tracheoplasty to provide insights into establishing surgical indications. Airway geometries were reconstructed from pre- and postoperative computed tomography scans in 13 patients (10 with tracheoplasty, three with LPAS repair alone), measuring equivalent diameter and geometry-based flow resistance in a 30-mm segment proximal to the carina. All results are expressed as median [range]. The tracheoplasty group showed significant pericarinal enlargement (3.3 [2.4-4.1] to 5.2 [4.2-6.7] mm; P = 0.002) and a marked decrease in resistance estimates (2.30 [0.83-9.6] to 0.39 [0.11-1.88] cmH2O/(L/s); P = 0.004). In the LPAS repair alone group, two patients exhibited low preoperative resistance estimates (0.92 and 1.52 cmH2O/(L/s)), comparable to postoperative values in the tracheoplasty group. Slide tracheoplasty provides substantial morphologic and functional improvements, but certain patients achieve acceptable airway resistance with LPAS repair alone. Computational flow resistance may represent a useful tool for visualizing or quantifying the effects of surgical intervention.

## Robotic-assisted choledochal cyst surgery in infants: are we operating too early?

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:46
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Lyon
- 通讯作者: Kassir R (rani.kassir@outlook.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06611-3
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06611-3) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42663727/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨机器人辅助胆总管囊肿切除术在婴儿中是否过早实施，并评估其手术时机的合理性。
- 核心发现/数据：这是迄今最大单中心儿童机器人辅助胆总管囊肿切除经验，包含大量婴儿；但新生儿及小婴儿的手术指征尚不明确，尤其缺乏症状状态数据。早期手术存在技术挑战，且吻合口狭窄风险可能因随访有限而被低估；婴儿期恶变风险极低，现有证据支持对无症状患者延迟干预以促进生长和风险分层。
- 临床启示：机器人技术的可及性不应决定手术时机，缺乏明确临床指征时不宜对无症状婴儿过早手术，应权衡术后并发症风险与延迟手术的获益。

**原文摘要**：Zhang et al. report the largest single-center experience of robotic-assisted choledochal cyst resection in children, including a substantial proportion of infants. However, the indications for surgery in neonates and young infants remain unclear, particularly in the absence of data on symptomatic status. Early surgery involves technical challenges and may carry a higher risk of anastomotic stricture, potentially underestimated given limited follow-up. Emerging evidence supports delaying intervention in asymptomatic patients, allowing growth and risk stratification. Furthermore, the risk of malignant transformation is negligible in infancy. While robotic surgery is a valuable advancement, its availability should not determine timing of intervention without clear clinical justification.

## Multiple congenital dermal sinus tracts: a case-based review involving a unique triple-tract configuration that challenges current embryological concepts.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:46
- 期刊: Childs Nerv Syst
- 国家/单位: Aichi · Aichi Prefectural Children's Health and Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Kurimoto M (rythmista@me.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00381-026-07448-w
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00381-026-07448-w) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42665681/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告一例罕见的三重先天性皮窦道（DST）并综述文献，以明确侧方及多发性DST的临床和解剖特征，并探讨其胚胎学机制。
- 核心发现/数据：患儿女婴，1月龄，骶尾部同时存在左侧、右侧及正中三个独立DST，硬膜内汇合，合并脑膜炎和皮样囊肿；手术完全切除，无神经并发症，随访无复发。文献综述显示侧方DST罕见，多发性DST极罕见，其中双侧或硬膜内汇合报道极少，此前无三重构型报道。
- 临床启示：DST可表现为非中线及多发，传统局限性神经管不分离理论不足以解释双侧和正中DST共存及硬膜内连续性，需考虑尾侧发育过程（含次级神经管形成阶段）异常；认识非典型DST对早期诊断、全面影像评估和完整手术切除至关重要。

**原文摘要**：Congenital dermal sinus tract (DST), a rare spinal dysraphism, typically presents as a solitary midline lesion. Lateral and multiple DSTs are extremely rare, and their embryological basis remains unclear. Here, we report a case of triple DSTs and review the literature on lateral and multiple DSTs to better characterize their clinical and anatomical features. A unique case of a 1-month-old infant with triple DSTs (left and right laterals and a midline) at the sacrococcygeal level with intradural convergence, complicated by meningitis and an associated dermoid cyst, is reported. A literature review of lateral and multiple DST case reports was performed in PubMed. Three independent DSTs converged into the dural sac, all requiring surgical excision. Complete resection was achieved without neurological complications. At the latest follow-up, neurological function was preserved, with no signs of recurrent infection or lesion recurrence. The literature review revealed that lateral DSTs are rare and multiple DSTs are exceptionally uncommon. Very few reports described bilateral or intradural convergence patterns; none reported this specific triple-tract configuration. DSTs may present as multiple and non-midline lesions. Bilateral and midline DST coexistence with intradural continuity cannot be fully explained by the conventional focal nondisjunction theory. It is necessary to consider not only "simple localized failure of separation during the primary neural tube formation stage"-the conventional model-but also caudal developmental process abnormalities, including the secondary neural tube formation stage. Awareness of non-midline and multiple DSTs is essential for early diagnosis, comprehensive imaging, and complete surgical treatment.
