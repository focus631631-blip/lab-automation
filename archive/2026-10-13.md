## Biliary Complications After High-Grade Blunt Liver Trauma in Children: Impact of Early Recognition on Management Burden.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:32
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 意大利
- 通讯作者: de Ville de Goyet J (Jdeville@Ismett.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163539
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163539) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42863656/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估儿童高级别钝性肝创伤后胆道并发症的发生率、表现和管理，并探讨早期识别是否影响治疗负担。
- 核心发现/数据：共发现18例胆道并发症（15例胆瘘、3例胆汁瘤），发生率随肝损伤等级升高而增加（III级20%、IV级50%、V级67%，P=0.046）。合并胆道并发症者中位住院时间显著长于无并发症者（21天 vs 9.5天，P=0.007）。在肝周填塞后计划二次探查者中，早期识别胆汁漏并经胆囊引流可实现单步治愈，而延迟诊断与更复杂、分期管理（经皮引流、内镜或延迟干预）显著相关（P<0.01）。
- 临床启示：对高级别钝性肝损伤高危患儿应进行结构化监测，以便早期识别胆道并发症。早期识别可简化治疗并缩短住院，应纳入现代非手术治疗路径。

**原文摘要**：Biliary complications (BIC) are rare after high-grade blunt liver trauma in children, but a recognized source of morbidity. The main clinical issue is whether they are recognized in time to limit treatment burden. This study evaluated the incidence, presentation, and management of BIC after high-grade blunt liver trauma in a pediatric referral cohort. All consecutive children younger than 18 years referred to a quaternary pediatric hepatobiliary center between January 2006 and December 2025 for severe blunt liver injury were retrospectively reviewed. Demographic data, injury grade, timing of diagnosis, treatment, and clinical outcomes were analyzed. Length of hospital stay was used as a surrogate marker of management burden. Eighteen BIC events were identified in 18 children, including 15 biliary fistulas and 3 bilomas. BIC occurred more often with increasing injury severity, affecting 20% of grade III, 50% of grade IV, and 67% of grade V injuries (P= 0.046). Median hospital stay was longer in children with BIC than in those without BIC (21 days [IQR, 16.5-33.5] vs 9.5 days [IQR, 7-13]; P = .007). In children who underwent planned second-look laparotomy after peri-hepatic packing, bile leak was recognized early, with trans-cystic drainage allowing single step cure; delayed diagnosis on the other hand was significantly associated with more complex and staged management, including percutaneous drainage, endoscopic or delayed interventions (P< 0.01). Timely recognition was associated with simpler management and shorter hospitalization, suggesting that structured surveillance of selected high-risk injuries should be incorporated into contemporary nonoperative management pathways.

## Therapeutic effect of colonic spring expansion and interposition into the small intestine on short bowel model in rats.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:32
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Children's Hospital of Nanjing Medical University
- 通讯作者: Dunn JCY (wwjiang@njmu.edu.cn)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163526
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163526) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42863655/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨弹簧诱导结肠延长并间置入小肠对短肠综合征大鼠模型的治疗效果。
- 核心发现/数据：压缩弹簧植入的结肠段可延长至初始长度的300%±50%，间置1周后大鼠体重下降速度显著减缓（P<0.01）。延长结肠段环形肌层厚度较原结肠段增加（145±40 μm vs 127±45 μm，P<0.01），隐窝深度无显著变化（P>0.1）；间置结肠段PCNA表达高于原生结肠，以压缩弹簧组最高（166±10 a.u.）。
- 临床启示：弹簧介导的结肠延长并间置可改善短肠综合征大鼠的肠道吸收和生存，具有转化为短肠综合征治疗策略的潜力。

**原文摘要**：Short bowel syndrome (SBS) is a morbid disease with limited effective treatment modalities. Spring-mediated intestinal distraction has been successfully applied to lengthen the small intestine. This study investigates the therapeutic efficacy of spring-induced colonic lengthening followed by interposition into the small intestine in a rat SBS model. Twenty Sprague-Dawley rats matched for age and body weight were selected and randomly divided into four groups. Two groups underwent the implantation of nitinol spring-loaded capsules into the colon, while the other two groups underwent colonic resection and anastomosis only. After 3 weeks, 75% of the small intestine was resected, and three of the groups underwent colonic segment transplantation, while the other group did not. Four weeks later, tissue samples were collected and evaluated for histology and the expression of proliferation-related genes. Compressed spring-implanted colonic segments elongated to 300%±50% of their initial length. One week after colonic interposition, the rate of weight loss slowed significantly (P < 0.01). Compared with the original colonic segments (127±45 μm), the circular muscle layers thickness of the elongated colonic segments (145±40 μm) in the compressed spring group increased (P < 0.01), but there was no significant change in crypt depth (P > 0.1). The interposed colonic segments exhibited higher PCNA expression than the native colon segments, with the highest expression levels (166±10 a.u.) observed in the compressed spring group. Spring-mediated colonic lengthening and interposition can improve intestinal absorption and survival in SBS rats, showing translational potential for SBS treatment.

## Neonatal appendicitis with rectal bleeding: an unusual presentation.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:32
- 期刊: Ir J Med Sci · 中科院医学4区(医学：内科4区) · IF 2.0 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Istanbul · Istanbul Okan University
- 通讯作者: Kılıç S (dr.sinankilic@yahoo.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s11845-026-04678-4
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11845-026-04678-4) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42862451/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报道一例以直肠出血为不典型表现的新生儿阑尾炎，并强调其应纳入新生儿血便的鉴别诊断。
- 核心发现/数据：患儿为36周、2500 g男婴，生后第2天出现腹胀、易激惹、进食减少及鲜红色直肠出血；腹平片示多发气液平，超声示肠管扩张和腹腔游离液。剖腹探查见阑尾充血、水肿、硬结并局限性炎症，无穿孔或坏疽，阑尾切除后病理证实急性阑尾炎伴透壁炎症和纤维蛋白渗出，无坏死或坏死性小肠结肠炎证据。术后恢复顺利，未再出现直肠出血。
- 临床启示：新生儿出现血便时，尤其伴腹胀及肠梗阻或肠麻痹征象，应考虑阑尾炎可能。尽管该病罕见且表现非特异，识别不典型表现有助于及时进行外科评估和治疗。

**原文摘要**：Neonatal appendicitis is an exceptionally rare condition with a high risk of delayed diagnosis and perforation. Its nonspecific clinical presentation may mimic other causes of acute abdomen and gastrointestinal bleeding in newborns, particularly necrotizing enterocolitis. A 36-week, 2500-g male neonate developed abdominal distension, irritability, decreased oral intake, and bright red rectal bleeding on the second day of life. Abdominal radiography demonstrated multiple air-fluid levels, while ultrasonography revealed dilated bowel loops and free intraperitoneal fluid. Exploratory laparotomy revealed a hyperaemic, oedematous, and indurated appendix with localized inflammatory changes, without perforation or gangrenous changes. Appendectomy was performed, and histopathological examination confirmed acute appendicitis with transmural inflammation and fibrinous exudates, without necrosis or histopathological findings suggestive of necrotizing enterocolitis. The patient recovered uneventfully, and no further rectal bleeding was observed after appendectomy. Neonatal appendicitis should be considered in the differential diagnosis of newborns presenting with hematochezia, particularly when rectal bleeding is accompanied by abdominal distension and signs of intestinal obstruction or ileus. Although the condition is rare and its clinical presentation is nonspecific, recognition of atypical presentations may facilitate timely surgical evaluation and treatment.

## Stable Thalamic Topography Across Perinatal Development: a Diffusion MRI Study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:32
- 期刊: Brain Topogr · 中科院医学3区(神经科学3区/临床神经病学4区) · IF 5.3 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 瑞士 · Center for MR Research
- 通讯作者: Jakab A (andras.jakab@kispi.uzh.ch)
- DOI: 10.1007/s10548-026-01257-9
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10548-026-01257-9) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42862397/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：利用扩散MRI和GMM聚类描绘新生儿丘脑分区，并评估围产期丘脑拓扑发育轨迹及早产、性别差异。
- 核心发现/数据：丘脑被分为7个簇，GMM重测重叠为0.65-0.73，并与Morel数字图谱解剖对齐；各丘脑簇绝对体积随年龄显著增加（FDR校正p<0.001），但相对体积拓扑总体稳定，仅腹前核和丘脑枕群两簇呈轻中度年龄相关变化（FDR校正p<0.05，调整R²=0.039和0.101）。早产与足月新生儿协调体积分析无显著组间差异，亦无性别差异；但早产儿缺乏系统足月等效扫描，限制了对早产影响的结论。
- 临床启示：围产期丘脑拓扑大体已建立，提示晚孕期丘脑主要组织模式较稳定；临床评估早产儿脑发育时应关注绝对体积增长而非拓扑重构，并谨慎解释扩散MRI聚类结果。

**原文摘要**：The thalamus is critical relay hub of brain connectivity, yet its topographical organization during the early human brain development remains incompletely understood. Our study characterized the critical perinatal developmental period by optimizing and applying a clustering framework to parcellate the neonatal thalamus and delineate its developmental trajectory.Thalamus topography was studied using Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) based clustering applied to voxel-level fiber orientation distribution functions, obtained from diffusion MRI data. Three perinatal datasets were used: term-born infants from developing human connectome project (dHCP, n = 68, age at MRI: 37.4-44.7 weeks), dHCP preterm infant data (n = 48, 29.3-37.4 weeks) and a neonatal control dataset from Zürich (n = 42, 39.0-48.7 weeks). Clustering was optimized using an adult test-retest diffusion MRI dataset (n = 30).We subdivided the thalamus into seven clusters, where GMM showed moderate reproducibility (test-retest overlap ranged from 0.65 to 0.73), and comparable anatomical alignment with digital Morel atlas. Robust regression analysis revealed significant age-related volume increases in absolute volumes across thalamic clusters (FDR-corrected p < 0.001). Topography by means of relative volumes remained stable, suggesting preservation of thalamic topography during late gestation. Two clusters, closely corresponding to the ventral-anterior nucleus and pulvinar group, showed moderate age-related topographical changes (FDR-corrected p < 0.05, adjusted R²=0.039 and 0.101). Group-wise analysis of harmonized volumes revealed no significant group effects between preterm and term born neonates. No sex differences were observed.Our findings demonstrate that thalamic topography is largely established across the perinatal period; however, the lack of systematic term-equivalent scans in preterm infants limits our ability to draw conclusions about the effects of prematurity. The interpretation might be limited by the indirect nature of diffusion MRI features and the moderate reproducibility of the clustering techniques.

## Factors associated with lateral abdominal protrusion after congenital diaphragmatic hernia repair.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:32
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 594-1101 · Osaka Women's and Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Noguchi Y (noguchi@wch.opho.jp)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06674-2
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06674-2) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42862396/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：开发客观影像学指标并探索先天性膈疝（CDH）修复术后侧腹膨出（LAP）的相关因素。
- 核心发现/数据：73例2013—2023年新生儿CDH修复患者中，19例（26.0%）出现视觉识别的LAP，中位年龄35天；标准化LAP指数（SLAPI）开发队列AUC为1.00（95%CI 1.00-1.00），预设切点≥1.0时开发队列敏感性91.7%、特异性100%，验证队列均为100%。调整分析显示，产前胃疝入分级2-3（OR 6.67，95%CI 1.62-32.63）与LAP相关，而观察/预期肺面积-头围比每增加10个百分点与LAP风险降低相关（OR 0.54，95%CI 0.24-0.96）；观察者内可靠性良好（ICC 0.885）。
- 临床启示：SLAPI可客观记录CDH修复术后LAP，产前胃疝入较重及肺发育较差可能提示LAP风险，但其临床和功能意义仍需进一步研究。

**原文摘要**：Lateral abdominal protrusion (LAP) is observed after congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH) repair. We developed an objective radiographic index and explored factors associated with LAP. Seventy-three patients who underwent neonatal CDH repair between 2013 and 2023 were retrospectively evaluated. The standardized LAP index (SLAPI) was developed in the 2013-2018 cohort and validated in the 2019-2023 cohort. Candidate factors available before LAP detection were examined using univariable logistic regression, followed by exploratory Firth logistic regression. Intraobserver reliability was assessed by repeated measurement. Visually identified LAP occurred in 19 patients (26.0%) at a median age of 35 days. In the development cohort, the SLAPI area under the curve was 1.00 (95% confidence interval [CI], 1.00-1.00). At the prespecified cutoff of ≥ 1.0, sensitivity and specificity were 91.7% and 100% in the development cohort and 100% and 100% in the validation cohort, respectively. In the adjusted analysis, prenatal stomach herniation grade 2-3 (odds ratio [OR], 6.67; 95% CI, 1.62-32.63) and observed-to-expected lung area-to-head circumference ratio per 10-percentage-point increase (OR, 0.54; 95% CI, 0.24-0.96) were associated with LAP. Intraobserver reliability was good (intraclass correlation coefficient, 0.885). SLAPI objectively documented LAP after CDH repair. Its clinical and functional significance requires further study.

## CT-guided methylene blue staining to facilitate thoracoscopic resection of intermediate-to-deep pulmonary metastases in pediatric patients.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:32
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 德国 · Hannover Medical School
- 通讯作者: Kiblawi R (kiblawi.rim@mh-hannover.de)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06675-1
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06675-1) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42862376/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估CT引导亚甲蓝染色用于术前定位，以促进儿科患者中深部肺转移灶胸腔镜切除的可行性和临床结局。
-

**原文摘要**：Thoracoscopic resection of pulmonary metastases offers a minimally-invasive approach but requires preoperative localization due to absent tactile feedback. CT-guided methylene blue staining (MBS) was implemented to facilitate resection of pulmonary metastases, including intermediate-to-deep lesions. We retrospectively analyzed pediatric patients (0-18 years) undergoing resection of pulmonary metastases (2007-2024). Lesion depth was classified as superficial (≤ 10 mm), intermediate (11-20 mm), or deep (> 20 mm). Primary endpoint was completeness of resection; secondary endpoints included perioperative and oncological outcomes. Subgroup analysis included patients with ≤ 2 pulmonary metastases. Fifty-eight resections in 43 patients were included: 49 open, and 9 thoracoscopic following CT-guided MBS. Eleven lesions were stained prior to thoracoscopic resection: three superficial, seven intermediate, one deep lesion. All were resected with negative margins, without complications. Thoracoscopy following CT-guided MBS was associated with shorter operative times (65 vs. 157 min, p = 0.01), hospital stay (4.0 vs. 5.6 days, p = 0.01), and time-to-chemotherapy-resumption (2.25 vs. 18.6 days, p = 0.01). No differences were observed in pulmonary recurrence, disease-free interval, or six-month-survival. In pediatric patients with up to two pulmonary metastases, CT-guided methylene blue staining provided feasible preoperative localization, facilitating thoracoscopic wedge resection of superficial, intermediate and deep lesions without complications. Prospective multicenter studies should further evaluate efficacy and inclusion criteria.
