## Association of Red Blood Transfusion With Necrotizing Enterocolitis in Very Low Birth-Weight Neonates: A 7-Year Retrospective Cohort Study in Saudi Arabia.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:36
- 期刊: Adv Neonatal Care
- 国家/单位: 埃及 · College of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Abellatif M
- DOI: 10.1097/ANC.0000000000001418
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/ANC.0000000000001418) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42896071/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估极低出生体重（<1500 g）或胎龄<32周新生儿中浓缩红细胞输注与坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）发生风险的关联。
- 核心发现/数据：该7年回顾性队列纳入441例VLBW新生儿，NEC发生49例（11.1%），无NEC 392例（88.9%）；NEC组出生体重、胎龄及初始红细胞压积均显著更低（P<0.05）。输血记录在两组间存在显著差异（7.4% vs 32.1%，P<0.01），但校正其他危险因素后，仅脓毒症与NEC显著相关（OR 8.2，95%CI 3.893–17.259），PRBC输注未增加NEC风险。
- 临床启示：不应将PRBC输注视为VLBW新生儿NEC的独立危险因素，临床应重点加强脓毒症防治；贫血、输血时机及输血次数对NEC的影响仍需前瞻性队列研究证实。

**原文摘要**：Necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) is a multifactorial disorder of the gastrointestinal tract of very low birth-weight (VLBW) infants. Blood transfusions are suspected to be associated with NEC in this population. To examine the association between packed red blood cells (PRBCs) transfusion and NEC in VLBW neonates (less than 1500 gm) or less than 32 weeks. A retrospective cohort study of all VLBW neonates admitted to the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) at King Abdulaziz University Hospital (KAUH) from January 1, 2014, to December 31, 2020. Of 3018 newborns admitted to the NICU, 441 were VLBW neonates born at less than 32 weeks of gestational age (mean: 29 weeks ± 2.3; male: 220, 49.9%). The sample was divided into 2 groups: those who developed NEC 49 (11.1%) and those who did not develop NEC 392 (88.9%). Infants who developed NEC had significantly lower birth weight, gestational age (GA), and initial hematocrit (P < 0.05). Although blood transfusions were more often noted in those who developed NEC 7.4% versus 32.1% (P < 0.01), only sepsis showed significant associations with NEC, OR 8.2 (3.893-17.259) after adjusting for other studied risk factors. PRBC transfusion was not associated with an increased risk of NEC in VLBW neonates. Sepsis was the only variable found to be significantly associated with NEC. Future prospective cohort studies to assess the role of anemia, the timing of PRBC transfusion, and the number of transfusions on incidence of NEC are needed.

## Prenatal Fetoscopic Intervention for Gastroschisis: A Systematic Review of First Published Human Cases.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:36
- 期刊: Fetal Diagn Ther · 中科院医学3区(妇产科学3区) · IF 1.7 · JCR Q3
- 通讯作者: Acacio GL
- DOI: 10.1159/fdt/acuag026
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1159/fdt/acuag026) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42895927/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统识别并描述性总结已发表的人类证据，聚焦产前胎儿镜干预腹裂的手术特征、母胎事件、新生儿结局及现有文献局限性。
- 核心发现/数据：共检索827条记录，最终仅纳入3篇单病例报告，均来自高度专业化中心，干预时机、子宫入路、CO₂充气、羊水管理、闭合技术、胎儿监测及术后随访差异显著。3例均尝试宫内肠管复位，其中1例术后48小时内肠管再脱出并伴静脉多普勒异常，需分娩后行类EXIT修复；所有新生儿均存活。因缺乏对照、病例选择极端、围手术期管理异质且可能存在发表偏倚，无法推断疗效或新生儿获益。
- 临床启示：产前胎儿镜修复腹裂目前仍应视为研究性干预，不宜常规开展；仅在伦理批准的前瞻性方案中，以标准化选择标准、母胎安全监测、规范化手术报告和长期随访进行评估。

**原文摘要**：To systematically identify and descriptively summarize the available published human evidence on prenatal fetoscopic intervention for gastroschisis, focusing on reported procedural characteristics, maternal-fetal events, neonatal outcomes, and limitations of the current literature. A systematic review was conducted according to PRISMA guidance, with protocol registration in PROSPERO (CRD420261338353). MEDLINE/PubMed, Embase, CINAHL, CENTRAL, Scopus, and Web of Science were searched from inception to March 2026. Studies including fetuses with prenatally diagnosed gastroschisis undergoing intrauterine intervention were eligible. Two reviewers independently screened studies, extracted data using a standardized form, and assessed reporting quality using the Joanna Briggs Institute checklist for case reports. Given the nature of the available evidence, synthesis was descriptive only. Of 827 records identified, three single-patient case reports met the inclusion criteria. All described prenatal fetoscopic intervention for gastroschisis in highly specialized centers, with substantial heterogeneity in timing of intervention, uterine access, carbon dioxide insufflation, amniotic fluid management, closure technique, fetal monitoring, and postoperative surveillance. Initial intrauterine bowel reduction was reported in all three cases; however, one fetus developed postoperative bowel re-extrusion within 48 hours, associated with venous Doppler abnormalities, and required delivery followed by EXIT-like repair. All neonates survived, but the absence of comparators, extreme case selection, heterogeneous perioperative management, and likely publication bias preclude any inference regarding efficacy or neonatal benefit. Published human evidence on prenatal fetoscopic intervention for gastroschisis is limited to three isolated case reports. These reports describe procedural completion in highly selected cases managed at specialized fetal surgery centers, but they remain insufficient to determine the generalizable feasibility, safety profile, reproducibility, or clinical benefit of this approach compared with contemporary postnatal management. Prenatal repair should therefore remain investigational and should be evaluated within ethically approved prospective protocols with standardized selection criteria, maternal-fetal safety monitoring, operative reporting, and long-term follow-up.
