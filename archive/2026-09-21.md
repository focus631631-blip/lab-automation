## Favorable maternal and neonatal outcomes in probable Eisenmenger physiology with suspected DORV: a case report and literature review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:20
- 期刊: Egypt Heart J
- 国家/单位: Mogadishu · Mogadishu Somali Turkiye Training and Research Hospital
- 通讯作者: Mohamed MM (drmohamuqtar@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1186/s43044-026-00781-0
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1186/s43044-026-00781-0) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42763816/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过一例疑似DORV并可能Eisenmenger生理的妊娠病例及文献复习，总结其诊断、围产期管理和母儿结局。
- 核心发现/数据：25岁初产妇孕34周就诊，血氧饱和度80%（面罩吸氧）、估测肺动脉收缩压85 mmHg，超声示大房间隔/室间隔缺损、重度三尖瓣反流，疑诊DORV合并Eisenmenger生理。经心产科评估后引产，4小时后阴道分娩，活产儿体重2.7 kg、Apgar评分5分并入住NICU。母亲产后血红蛋白降至7 g/dL并输注2单位红细胞，ICU监护3天后第6天转心内科、第7天稳定出院。
- 临床启示：该病例虽实现母亲存活至产后第1周并活产，但血流动力学、解剖、麻醉及新生儿数据不完整，不能据此认定Eisenmenger综合征阴道分娩安全。此类复杂先心病妊娠需多学科协作、完善影像与肺高压管理，并加强新生儿监护。

**原文摘要**：Pregnancy in Eisenmenger syndrome is associated with very high maternal and fetal risk. Complex congenital anatomy further complicates diagnosis and peripartum management, particularly where advanced imaging and pulmonary hypertension therapies are limited. Published evidence specifically addressing pregnancy with uncorrected DORV and Eisenmenger physiology remains sparse. A 25-year-old primigravida at 34 weeks' gestation presented with progressive dyspnoea and generalized oedema. The available record documented a heart rate of 130 beats/min, respiratory rate of 25 breaths/min, oxygen saturation of 80% despite face-mask oxygen, a loud second heart sound, and a systolic murmur. Echocardiography showed large ventricular and atrial septal defects with colour Doppler flow, severe tricuspid regurgitation, and an estimated systolic pulmonary artery pressure of 85 mmHg. In the setting of marked hypoxaemia, these findings supported probable Eisenmenger physiology, although shunt direction and pulmonary vascular resistance were not documented. The aorta appeared to arise predominantly from the right ventricle, supporting suspected DORV, but the origin of both great arteries, VSD-great-artery relationship, and DORV subtype could not be defined. Following cardiology and obstetric assessment, labour was induced and vaginal delivery occurred four hours later. The liveborn neonate weighed 2.7 kg, had an initial Apgar score of 5, and was admitted to neonatal intensive care for stabilisation and monitoring. Postpartum haemoglobin fell to 7 g/dL and two units of packed red cells were transfused. The mother was monitored in intensive care for three days, transferred to cardiology on day 6, and discharged in stable condition on day 7. This report documents maternal survival through induced vaginal delivery and the first postpartum week, with a live birth, despite late recognition of probable Eisenmenger physiology and suspected complex congenital heart disease. The report does not establish the safety of vaginal delivery in Eisenmenger syndrome, and incomplete haemodynamic, anatomical, anaesthetic, and neonatal data limit causal interpretation.

## Acute Healthcare Utilization During the First Year After Hospital Discharge in Children With Trisomy 18.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:36
- 期刊: Am J Med Genet A · 中科院生物学3区(遗传学4区) · IF 1.7 · JCR Q4
- 国家/单位: 日本 · Hyogo Prefectural Kobe Children's Hospital Perinatal Center
- 通讯作者: Yoshimoto S
- DOI: 10.1002/ajmg.a.70309
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/ajmg.a.70309) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42764676/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估18三体（T18）患儿出院后第一年内的急性医疗资源使用情况，并分析其生存和出院存活趋势。
- 核心发现/数据：2008—2022年单中心87例T18婴儿中，总体生存率和出院存活率随时间改善。2018—2022年出院的16例存活患儿中，出院后1年内13例（81%）至少就诊急诊1次，11例（69%）需住院治疗；住院最常见原因为呼吸系统疾病，其次为尿路感染和胃肠道疾病。2例在出院后1年内死亡，探索性分析未发现气管切开状态与急诊就诊或住院显著相关。
- 临床启示：尽管T18患儿生存率改善，但出院后1年医疗负担仍很高，提示需建立结构化多学科随访体系和强有力的家庭照护支持，以管理呼吸、泌尿及消化系统并发症。

**原文摘要**：Survival of infants with trisomy 18 (T18) has improved with advances in neonatal intensive care and surgical management, yet postdischarge outcomes remain poorly characterized. This single-center retrospective cohort study included infants with T18 admitted to a Level III neonatal intensive care unit between 2008 and 2022. Survival and survival-to-discharge rates were analyzed by birth era. For patients discharged alive in the most recent era (2018-2022), emergency department (ED) visits and hospital admissions during the first year after discharge were evaluated. Among 87 infants with confirmed outcomes, overall survival and survival-to-discharge rates improved over time. Sixteen patients discharged alive in the late cohort were included in the postdischarge analysis. During the first year after discharge, 13 patients (81%) visited the ED at least once, and 11 (69%) required hospital admission. Respiratory disorders were the most frequent reason for admission, followed by urinary tract infections and gastrointestinal disorders. An exploratory analysis found no significant association between tracheostomy status and ED visits or hospital admissions. Two patients died within 1 year after discharge. Despite improving survival, infants with T18 experience substantial healthcare utilization after discharge, underscoring the need for structured multidisciplinary follow-up and robust home-based care support systems.

## Epidemiology, Early Therapy and Sequelae Predictors in Congenital CMV: A Nationwide Cohort Study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:36
- 期刊: Pediatr Infect Dis J · 中科院医学4区(儿科3区/免疫学4区/传染病学4区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 瑞士 · University of Geneva
- 通讯作者: L'Huillier AG
- DOI: 10.1097/INF.0000000000005388
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/INF.0000000000005388) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42764414/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：描述先天性巨细胞病毒（cCMV）的全国流行病学特征，识别后遗症早期预测因素，并评估早期抗病毒治疗对结局的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入209例确诊cCMV婴儿；出生常见表现为小头畸形35%、宫内生长受限34%、神经影像异常36%，36%接受抗病毒治疗，其中75%在生后1个月内启动。多因素分析显示，出生时肝脾肿大（OR 13.2，95%CI 2.3-75.2）和肌张力低下（OR 6.6，95%CI 1.1-41.1）是迟发性感音神经性听力损失（SNHL）的独立预测因素。1年随访时，早期治疗组神经发育障碍发生率显著低于延迟治疗组（23% vs 50%，P=0.004）。
- 临床启示：临床应关注出生时肝脾肿大和肌张力低下，以识别迟发性SNHL高风险患儿并长期听力监测；一旦有治疗指征，应尽早诊断并启动抗病毒治疗，以降低神经发育障碍风险。

**原文摘要**：Congenital cytomegalovirus (cCMV) is the most common congenital infection, with approximately 17%-20% of infected neonates developing permanent sequelae. Early risk stratification remains challenging yet crucial for therapeutic decision-making. We aimed to characterize national epidemiology, identify early predictors of sequelae, and assess the impact of early antiviral treatment on outcomes in infants with cCMV. We conducted a nationwide prospective observational study between 2017 and 2024, analyzing birth and 1-year follow-up data of infants with confirmed cCMV in Switzerland. Two hundred and nine cCMV-infected children were included. The most common findings at birth were microcephaly (35%), intrauterine growth restriction (34%) and neuroimaging abnormalities (36%). Antiviral treatment was administered to 36% (75/209) of patients, among whom 75% (56/75) had treatment initiation within the first month of life. Multivariate analysis identified hepatosplenomegaly (odds ratio 13.2, 95% confidence interval, 2.3-75.2) and hypotonia at birth (odds ratio 6.6, 95% confidence interval, 1.1-41.1) as independent predictors of late-onset sensorineural hearing loss (SNHL). At the 1-year follow-up assessment, early treatment initiation was associated with significantly lower rates of neurodevelopmental disorders compared with delayed treatment initiation (23% vs. 50%, P = 0.004). This Swiss nationwide study confirms the previously reported association between hepatosplenomegaly at birth and late-onset SNHL and identifies hypotonia as a new independent predictor of late-onset SNHL. The data also confirm the crucial role of early antiviral treatment, particularly for neurodevelopmental disorders, emphasizing the importance of prompt cCMV diagnosis and treatment when indicated.

## Implementing Congenital CMV Screening in a Large Hospital System.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:36
- 期刊: Pediatrics · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区) · IF 6.1 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: Ohio · Cleveland Clinic Lerner College of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Esper F
- DOI: 10.1542/peds.2026-076776
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2026-076776) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42764176/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估在大型医院系统内启动先天性巨细胞病毒（cCMV）筛查的实施结果，并提供运营层面的经验。
- 核心发现/数据：2022至2025年间，50438例活产中7491例（14.9%）接受cCMV筛查，年检测量从约150例增至超过2000例；34例（0.45%）确诊cCMV，病例检出增加约2.5倍，且目标筛查组检出率高于普遍筛查组（0.74% vs 0.33%，P=.01）。最常见临床发现为异常神经影像（32.3%）和小于胎龄（26.5%）。筛查流程可快速整合入工作流，但听力随访受家庭后勤、社会经济和教育障碍限制。
- 临床启示：混合式cCMV筛查在大型医疗系统中可行并能提高检出率，成功实施依赖多学科协作。临床实践中需改善听力学及其他随访服务的可及性，以最大化筛查的临床获益。

**原文摘要**：Congenital cytomegalovirus (cCMV) is the most common congenital infection and the leading nongenetic cause of sensorineural hearing loss. Despite expanding legislative screening requirements across the United States, there is no consensus guidance on the screening approach, resulting in wide variation in implementation. Our objective was to assess the outcomes and provide operational insights of initiating cCMV screening within a large hospital system. A multidisciplinary team representing key clinical and laboratory specialties developed a comprehensive cCMV screening and follow-up program. Infants born within the Cleveland Clinic Ohio enterprise were screened through a hybrid screening protocol (universal screening in neonatal intensive care units and targeted screening in newborn nurseries). Detection rates and implementation insights were compiled. Clinical outcomes were assessed through retrospective medical record review. Among 50 438 live births, 7491 infants (14.9%) underwent cCMV screening from 2022 to 2025, increasing annual testing from approximately 150 to over 2000 infants. Thirty-four infants (0.45%) were confirmed to have cCMV, increasing case detection approximately 2.5-fold, with higher detection rates in targeted vs universal groups (0.74% vs 0.33%; P = .01). The most common clinical findings were abnormal neuroimaging (32.3%) and small for gestational age (26.5%). Although screening protocols were rapidly integrated into workflows, audiology follow-up was limited by logistical, socioeconomic, and educational barriers of families. Hybrid cCMV screening is feasible across a large health care system and increases detection. Multidisciplinary collaboration is essential to successful implementation. Improved access to audiology and other follow-up care is needed to maximize clinical impact.

## Care of the Infant With Congenital Cytomegalovirus Infection: Clinical Report.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:36
- 期刊: Pediatrics · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区) · IF 6.1 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: New York · University of Rochester Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Council on Children With Disabilities
- DOI: 10.1542/peds.2026-078595
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2026-078595) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42764165/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：本临床报告旨在填补先天性巨细胞病毒（cCMV）感染婴儿诊疗中的已知知识缺口，并更新2024-2027年《Red Book》相关内容。
- 核心发现/数据：报告回顾了cCMV的流行病学、临床表现（cCMV疾病及孤立性感音神经性听力损失）、诊断和治疗；摘要未提供具体流行病学或疗效数值。筛查策略方面，比较不同方法利弊后推荐“听力靶向筛查+扩展检测”（HT plus expanded testing），并涵盖初始评估、预防进展及关键行动声明。
- 临床启示：临床应据此规范cCMV婴儿的诊断、治疗和初始评估，优先采用听力靶向联合扩展检测策略，以改善听力及神经发育结局。

**原文摘要**：This clinical report addresses recognized knowledge gaps regarding congenital CMV infection (cCMV) in infants and expands on information present in the 2024-2027 Red Book. The clinical report reviews the epidemiology of cCMV, clinical manifestations of cCMV disease and cCMV with isolated sensorineural hearing loss (SNHL), diagnosis of cCMV, and treatment of cCMV disease and cCMV with isolated SNHL. The current advantages and disadvantages of different approaches to screening for cCMV infection are reviewed with a recommendation for hearing-targeted (HT) plus expanded testing. Additionally, the initial evaluation for an infant with cCMV and current knowledge of prevention efforts are discussed. Key action statements are provided.
