## A Collaborative Model For Bladder Exstrophy Care: A Retrospective Review of Surgical Outcomes, Urinary Continence, and Renal Function.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：回顾性评估为期九年的协作手术模式管理经典膀胱外翻（BE）患者的手术结局、控尿功能及肾功能。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入55例患者（80%既往已行

**原文摘要**：We report the surgical outcomes of a nine-year collaborative model for managing a predominantly previously operated cohort of patients with bladder exstrophy (BE). We retrospectively reviewed patients with classic BE managed between 2016-2024 during annual collaborative surgical missions. Patients with ≥1 year follow-up were included. Surgical management was individualized and included bladder closure (BC), bladder neck reconstruction (BNR), bladder neck injection (BNI), bladder neck closure (BNC), bladder augmentation (BA), and continent urinary diversion (CUD). Follow-up combined in-person care with telemedicine. Outcomes included continence (volitional voiding ≥3 hours), dryness (≥3 hours with clean intermittent catheterization), complications, and renal function. Fifty-five patients were included, 80% of whom had undergone previous reconstruction. Initial procedures included primary BC (n=11), redo BC (n=11), BNR without CUD (n=15), BNR with CUD with/without BA (n=6), BNC with CUD and BA (n=7), and BNI (n=5). At last follow-up, 11 patients (20%) had undergone additional continence surgeries. All primary BCs performed by our team were successful. Following the last major continence procedure, BNR without CUD achieved 36.4% continence and 3% dryness, BNR with CUD (±BA) achieved 66.7% dryness, and BNC with CUD and BA achieved 90% dryness. Overall, 47.3% of patients achieved continence or dryness. Postoperative complications occurred in 30.9% of patients, and no patient developed renal failure. A structured collaborative model can deliver outcomes comparable to high-income settings, even in complex, previously operated patients. Individualized surgical planning combined with hybrid follow-up provides a practical and potentially scalable model for delivering specialized BE care.

## Utility of MRI as sole cross-sectional imaging for pediatric cervical spine clearance following radiography.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 08:58
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Utah · University of Utah
- 通讯作者: Kahan AM (Sasha.kahan@hsc.utah.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163548
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163548) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42925921/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估儿童钝性颈椎外伤后，在初始X线异常或X线正常但持续疼痛时，序贯CT和MRI是否比单独MRI发现更多有临床意义的颈椎损伤。
- 核心发现/数据：在X线异常且同时接受CT和MRI的106例患者中，CT阳性/MRI阴性不一致16例（15.1%），但无CT发现而MRI未发现的有临床意义损伤，也无仅CT发现的不稳定损伤。在X线正常但持续疼痛且同时接受CT和MRI的71例患者中，不一致8例（11.3%），但无仅CT发现的有临床意义损伤。直接MRI路径未显示需要补救性CT诊断的有临床意义损伤。
- 临床启示：在X线异常或X线正常但持续疼痛后需横断面影像时，当前数据不支持常规同时使用CT和MRI；MRI可及且临床可行时优先选择。对神经功能完整、持续疼痛但X线其余可靠者，短期颈托固定并门诊复查可作为立即横断面影像的合理替代。

**原文摘要**：In children evaluated for blunt cervical spine injury, abnormal screening radiographs (XR) and persistent pain after a normal XR commonly prompt additional evaluation. Whether conducting dual cross-sectional imaging adds additional value in injury detection after initial XR is unclear. We evaluated whether sequential computed tomography (CT) and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) identify clinically significant cervical spine injuries beyond those detected on MRI alone. We performed a secondary analysis of a multicenter, prospective pediatric cervical spine imaging cohort conducted across 72 trauma centers in the United States. All children with blunt trauma who underwent XR within 24 hours and had either abnormal XR or normal XR with persistent pain were included if they underwent subsequent MRI, either as the sole cross-sectional imaging modality or in combination with CT. CT-MRI discordance was assessed among patients who underwent both modalities. Clinically significant injury was defined as injury requiring surgery or halo, atlanto-occipital dislocation, or unstable ligamentous injury. Among patients with abnormal XR who underwent both CT and MRI (n=106), CT-positive/MRI-negative discordance occurred in 16 patients (15.1%); however, no clinically significant injury identified on CT was not also identified on MRI, and no unstable injuries were detected exclusively on CT. Among patients with normal XR and persistent pain who underwent both CT and MRI (n=71), CT-positive/MRI-negative discordance occurred in 8 patients (11.3%), but no clinically significant CT-only findings were identified. The XR to MRI cohort demonstrated that a direct MRI pathway was utilized across participating centers without evidence in the available dataset of a clinically significant injury requiring rescue CT-based diagnosis. When cross-sectional imaging is pursued after abnormal XR or normal XR with persistent pain, routine use of both CT and MRI is not supported by the current data. MRI is favored when available and clinically feasible. In neurologically intact children with persistent pain and otherwise reassuring XR, short-term collar immobilization with outpatient reassessment may also be a reasonable alternative to immediate cross-sectional imaging.

## Advances in preclinical tissue engineering strategies for bladder reconstruction: a systematic review.

- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2

- 通讯作者: Paraboschi I (irene.paraboschi@unimi.it)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163564

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：本综述旨在系统整合体外和体内证据，评估用于儿童膀胱重建的组织工程策略。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入73项研究，天然聚合物支架最常见（61.6%），其次为合成支架（20.5%）和混合支架（17.8%）；54项体内研究中尿路上皮和平滑肌再生均有报道，90.7%报道血管生成，34项评估膀胱功能容量（多用于无细胞支架）。细胞接种研究（n=44）以间充质干细胞为主；天然支架支持再生，合成支架常需天然成分，混合支架整合性不一；天然ECM支架免疫反应轻、混合支架中等、合成支架持久，30项研究报告并发症。持久功能再生似依赖整合机械支撑、ECM生物活性和生物信号的设计，复合/生物功能化支架结果较一致，但大动物研究仍是临床转化的关键。
- 临床启示：天然ECM或复合/生物功能化支架是更有前景的儿童膀胱重建候选，但需关注合成材料的免疫反应和并发症。当前证据仍以临床前为主，需大动物研究和标准化功能结局评估后再推进临床转化。

**原文摘要**：Bladder reconstruction remains challenging in pediatric patients needing augmentation cystoplasty for congenital anomalies, malignancies, or trauma. This review synthesizes in vitro and in vivo evidence on tissue-engineering strategies for pediatric bladder reconstruction. PRISMA guidelines were followed. PubMed, Scopus, and Embase were searched from inception to May 2025. Eligible studies investigated bioengineered constructs for pediatric bladder reconstruction and reported histological, molecular, or functional outcomes. Two reviewers independently performed study selection and data extraction, resolving disagreements by consensus. Seventy-three studies met inclusion criteria. Natural polymeric scaffolds predominated (61.6%), followed by synthetic (20.5%) and hybrid scaffolds (17.8%). Functionalization included VEGF, IGF-1, bFGF, surface patterning, and nanoparticles. Among cell-seeded studies (n=44), mesenchymal stem cells predominated, followed by urothelial cells, smooth muscle cells, and co-cultures. Among 54 in vivo studies, models included rodents (n=30), rabbits (n=10), pigs (n=9), dogs (n=4), baboons (n=1), chicken embryos (n=1). Categories overlapped, as one study included both rats and pigs. Urothelial and smooth muscle regeneration was reported in all in vivo studies, angiogenesis in 90.7%. Functional bladder capacity was assessed in 34 studies, mainly in acellular scaffolds. Natural scaffolds supported regeneration, whereas synthetic scaffolds often required natural components; hybrid scaffolds showed variable integration. Immune responses were mild with natural ECM-based scaffolds, moderate with hybrids, and prolonged with synthetics. Thirty studies reported complications. Durable functional regeneration appears dependent on integrated scaffold designs combining mechanical support, ECM-derived bioactivity, and biological cues. Composite and biofunctionalized scaffolds yielded consistent results, but large-animal studies remain essential for clinical translation.

## Effectiveness and safety of adjunct interventions for external cephalic version in breech presentation: a systematic review and meta-analysis.

- 期刊: Rev Bras Ginecol Obstet
- 国家/单位: 巴西
- 通讯作者: Fortunato VCR
- DOI: 10.61622/rbgo/2026rbgo40

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估臀位孕妇外倒转术（ECV）相关辅助及实施策略的有效性和安全性。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入10项随机对照试验共2745例孕妇，辅助及实施相关策略可提高ECV成功率（RR 1.53，95% CI 1.15-2.04；I²=87%）。其中药物宫缩抑制效果最大（RR 2.39，95% CI 1.39-4.11），其次为椎管内麻醉（RR 1.75，95% CI 1.07-2.85）和全身镇痛（RR 1.39，95% CI 1.15-1.69）；方案化策略未显著提高成功率。剖宫产率无显著降低（RR 0.88，95% CI 0.76-1.02），严重母婴并发症少见。
- 临床启示：在合适临床环境下，可考虑药物宫缩抑制或椎管内麻醉以提高ECV成功率，但剖宫产获益仍不确定，需结合个体情况并重视安全监测。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate the efficacy and safety of adjunct and implementation-related strategies associated with external cephalic version (ECV) in pregnant women with breech presentation. Randomized controlled trials comparing adjunct or protocol-based strategies related to ECV (including pharmacological tocolysis, neuraxial anesthesia or analgesia, systemic analgesia, or timing/procedural approaches) with standard care, placebo, or expectant management were included. MEDLINE (PubMed), Embase, Scopus, Cochrane CENTRAL, and LILACS were searched from inception to February 2026. Data extraction and risk of bias assessment using the Cochrane RoB 2 tool were performed independently by two reviewers. Dichotomous outcomes were pooled using risk ratios (RR) with 95% confidence intervals (CI) under random-effects models. Ten randomized controlled trials comprising 2,745 randomized women were included; 2,722 contributed complete outcome data to the primary meta-analysis. Adjunct and implementation-related strategies were associated with higher ECV success rates (RR 1.53; 95% CI 1.15-2.04; I² = 87%). Pharmacological tocolysis demonstrated the largest effect (RR 2.39; 95% CI 1.39-4.11), followed by neuraxial anesthesia (RR 1.75; 95% CI 1.07-2.85) and systemic analgesia (RR 1.39; 95% CI 1.15-1.69). Protocol-based strategies did not significantly improve success rates. No statistically significant reduction in cesarean delivery was observed (RR 0.88; 95% CI 0.76-1.02). Serious maternal and neonatal complications were uncommon. Adjunct and implementation-related strategies, particularly pharmacological tocolysis and neuraxial anesthesia, increase the likelihood of successful ECV. Although the effect on cesarean delivery remains inconclusive, ECV appears safe when performed in appropriate clinical settings.

## Environmentally relevant PFBS exposure triggers colonic inflammation through PPAR-mediated mitochondrial dysfunction and cGAS-STING activation.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 08:58
- 期刊: Ecotoxicol Environ Saf · 中科院环境科学与生态学2区(毒理学1区/环境科学2区) · IF 6.6 · JCR Q1

- 通讯作者: Wang Y (wy757311@cqmu.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.ecoenv.2026.120967
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecoenv.2026.120967) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42925865/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探究环境相关浓度PFBS暴露是否诱导结肠炎症及其分子机制与潜在干预策略。
- 核心发现/数据：0.05和0.5 mg/kg PFBS暴露可致小鼠结肠炎症和肠屏障破坏，表现为炎症细胞浸润、促炎因子升高、ZO1荧光强度及紧密连接蛋白表达显著降低。整合分析确定PPARα/γ为核心靶点，PFBS稳定结合并抑制二者，导致线粒体功能障碍、ROS积累及mtDNA胞质释放，进而激活cGAS-STING通路放大炎症；贝扎贝特激活PPAR通路可有效缓解。
- 临床启示：提示PFBS作为PFAS替代物仍存在肠道炎症和屏障损伤风险，临床应关注相关环境暴露。PPAR/线粒体/cGAS-STING轴及PPAR激动剂可能为干预PFBS相关结肠炎症提供候选策略，但需进一步临床转化验证。

**原文摘要**：Perfluorobutane sulfonate (PFBS), a widely used replacement for legacy per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), is increasingly detected in both environmental matrices and human serum. However, its intestinal toxicity remains poorly understood, and effective intervention strategies to mitigate these adverse effects are currently lacking. Here, we demonstrate that environmentally relevant PFBS exposure (0.05 and 0.5 mg/kg) induces colonic inflammation and intestinal barrier disruption in mice, characterized by inflammatory cell infiltration, elevated pro-inflammatory cytokine production, and significantly reduced ZO1 fluorescence intensity and tight junction protein expression. Integrated transcriptomic and network toxicology analyses identified peroxisome proliferator-activated receptors (PPARα/γ) as central molecular targets of PFBS-induced colonic inflammation. Molecular dynamics simulations predicted stable interactions between PFBS and PPARα/γ, while cellular thermal shift assays provided experimental evidence of PFBS target engagement with both receptors. Mechanistically, PFBS stably interacted with PPARα/γ and suppressed their expression, leading to mitochondrial dysfunction, increased ROS accumulation, and cytosolic release of mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA). The released mtDNA subsequently activated the cGAS-STING innate immune signaling pathway, thereby amplifying intestinal inflammatory responses. Importantly, pharmacological activation of the PPAR pathway with bezafibrate effectively alleviated PFBS-induced inflammation, supporting a critical role of PPAR dysregulation in PFBS intestinal toxicity. Collectively, our findings reveal a previously unrecognized PPAR/mitochondria/cGAS-STING signaling axis underlying PFBS-induced colonic inflammation and highlight the potential environmental health risks associated with PFBS as an emerging PFAS substitute.
