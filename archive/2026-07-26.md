## Safety and Efficacy of Ultrasound-Guided Percutaneous Biopsy with FAL Tract Sealing for Pediatric Hypervascular Malignant Solid Tumors: A Retrospective Cohort Study.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 06:35
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Beijing 100045 · Beijing Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Lv C (164376327@qq.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163326
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163326) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42501859/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估超声引导下经皮穿刺活检联合FAL组织胶封闭针道在儿童富血供恶性实体瘤中的安全性与有效性。
- 核心发现/数据：倾向评分匹配后，FAL组≥4次穿刺的比例显著高于对照组[84.6% vs. 60.5%，P=0.034]，但两组术后实验室指标变化、并发症发生率及诊断准确率无显著差异；FAL组中位随访140天未见针道种植或不良事件。
- 临床启示：FAL针道封闭可安全地允许更多次穿刺活检而不增加并发症风险，适用于需要充分组织标本的儿童富血供恶性肿瘤。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate the safety and efficacy of ultrasound (US)-guided percutaneous needle biopsy combined with FAL tissue adhesive needle tract sealing in pediatric hypervascular malignant solid tumors. A retrospective analysis was conducted on 149 pediatric patients with Adler grade ≥2 malignant solid tumors who underwent US-guided percutaneous needle biopsy between January 2021 and October 2025. Based on the application of FAL sealing, patients were divided into the FAL group (n=57) and control group (n=92). Using 1:1 propensity score matching (PSM) for baseline covariates (e.g., age, tumor size, Adler grade, biopsy needle size, and hematological metrics), we generated 39 matched pairs. The number of needle passes, postoperative changes in laboratory values, complication rates, and follow-up outcomes were compared between the two cohorts. Before matching, the FAL group presented with larger tumors, lower preoperative hemoglobin levels, a different pathological distribution, and imbalance in biopsy needle size than the control group. After PSM, baseline characteristics were balanced between the two groups. Patients in the FAL group more frequently underwent ≥4 needle passes than those in the control group [84.6% (33/39) vs. 60.5% (23/38), P=0.034]. Furthermore, there were no significant differences in postoperative laboratory changes, complication rates, or diagnostic accuracy among surgically validated cases between the two groups. No FAL-related adverse events or needle tract seeding were observed during a median follow-up of 140 days in the matched FAL group. In pediatric hypervascular malignant solid tumors, US-guided PNB combined with FAL tract sealing was associated with a higher number of biopsy passes without an observed increase in procedure-related complications, while diagnostic accuracy remained comparable. Further prospective multicenter and large-sample studies are needed for validation.

## Multiple Relaxing Short Incisions Significantly Reduce Urethral Fistula After Hypospadias Repair: A Retrospective Cohort Study.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 06:35
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Nanxishan Hospital (the Second People's Hospital) of Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region
- 通讯作者: Ye W (yeweijing011744@163.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163327
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163327) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42501858/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估多松弛短切口（MRSI）技术在尿道下裂修复术中的安全性、有效性和可重复性。  
- 核心发现/数据：MRSI组术后并发症率显著低于传统组（P=0.009），尿道瘘发生率分别为2.16%（3例）和8.47%（15例）（P=0.025）；多变量逻辑回归证实MRSI独立降低瘘风险（OR=0.081；95% CI: 0.010-0.372；P=0.005）。  
- 临床启示：MRSI技术可显著降低存在皮肤张力患者的尿道下裂术后尿道瘘并发症，提供安全、有效且可重复的手术方法，且美容效果良好。

**原文摘要**：The study aimed to assess the safety, efficacy, and reproducibility of the multiple relaxing short incisions (MRSI) technique in hypospadias repair. This retrospective cohort study included 316 patients with hypospadias who used the MRSI technique (group MRSI) or not (group traditional) in hypospadias surgery between September 2020 and September 2023. A comparison was made between the two groups regarding the penis skin cosmetic outcomes and the occurrence of complications such as urethral fistula, wound infection, glans dehiscence, urethral stricture, and urethral diverticulum. Among 316 patients, 139 were assigned into group MRSI while 177 were into group traditional. TIP was performed on 169 patients, inlay with TIP on 82 patients, and onlay island flap urethroplasty on 65 patients. The postoperative complication rate was significantly lower in group MRSI than in group traditional (P = 0.009). Group MRSI presented 3 cases of urethral fistula, with one concurrent wound infection, resulting in a ratio of 2.16%. In contrast, group traditional had 15 cases of urethral fistula, with 8 concurrent wound infections, yielding a ratio of 8.47%. The occurrence of urethral fistula in group MRSI was significantly lower than in group traditional (P = 0.025). Multivariable logistic regression confirmed that MRSI was independently associated with a reduced risk of fistula (OR = 0.081; 95% CI: 0.010-0.372; P = 0.005). No significant differences were observed between the two groups in terms of wound infection, glans dehiscence, urethral stricture, urethral diverticulum, and penile skin cosmetic score. MRSI technique decreases the occurrence of urethral fistula complications following hypospadias repair in patients with identifiable skin tension, providing a safe, effective, and reproducible method with favorable cosmetic results.

## Parental perspectives on neurodevelopmental risk communication and care coordination in congenital heart disease: a nationwide survey in France.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 06:35
- 期刊: Eur J Pediatr · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区) · IF 2.9 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 法国 · Nantes Université
- 通讯作者: Baruteau AE (albanelouen.baruteau@chu-nantes.fr)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00431-026-07277-8
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-026-07277-8) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42502114/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过法国全国性调查，了解先心病患儿家长对神经发育风险沟通、转诊、护理协调及未满足需求的看法。  
- 核心发现/数据：62.8%的患儿为产前诊断，但56.3%的家长认为未被告知神经发育风险；99%的家长希望获得相关信息；3岁以上患儿中57.1%存在神经发育迟缓或障碍，但仅9.2%纳入多学科围产期医疗网络，75%的家长表示缺乏发育护理协调员。  
- 临床启示：应从诊断时起建立结构化、以家庭为中心的神经发育随访路径，并延续至学龄期，以弥补当前在风险沟通、转诊和协调方面的严重不足。

**原文摘要**：Neurodevelopmental disorders are a major non-cardiac source of morbidity in congenital heart disease (CHD), but families' experiences of risk communication and developmental care remain insufficiently described. We aimed to characterize parental perspectives on neurodevelopmental risk communication, referral, care coordination, and unmet needs in France. We conducted a nationwide, anonymous, cross-sectional online survey of parents of children with CHD in France between May and June 2025. The questionnaire, co-developed with expert parents and a national patient organization, assessed timing and modalities of risk communication, access to follow-up pathways, coordination, and family impact. Of 667 invited families, 293 completed the survey (response rate 43.9%). Among 293 respondents, 184 (62.8%) reported a prenatal CHD diagnosis and 189 (64.5%) reported critical CHD. Overall, 165 parents (56.3%) felt they had not been informed about neurodevelopmental risk. Of 127 parents informed by health professionals, 83% received no written materials or practical guidance and 45% reported no referral to professionals or support services. Almost all respondents (290/293, 99.0%) wanted information about neurodevelopmental risk. Among children older than 3 years, parents reported neurodevelopmental delay or disorder in 120 of 210 cases (57.1%), yet only 11 of these children (9.2%) were enrolled in a multidisciplinary perinatal healthcare network. Three quarters of parents reported no identified coordinator for developmental care.  Parents described substantial gaps in neurodevelopmental risk communication, care coordination, and continuity of follow-up for children with CHD. Structured, family-centered pathways should begin at diagnosis and extend into school age. • Children with congenital heart disease are at increased neurodevelopmental risk, and expert guidance recommends surveillance, referral, and coordinated follow-up. • This nationwide parent survey identifies major gaps in risk communication, referral, and coordination, supporting staged counselling and longitudinal developmental pathways into school age.

## Incidence and risk factors for intraoperative hypotension in neonates undergoing non-cardiac surgery: a retrospective cohort study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 06:35
- 期刊: J Pediatr (Rio J) · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区) · IF 3.0 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 中国 · National Clinical Research Center for Children and Adolescent's Health
- 通讯作者: Jin Y (yue_jin@zju.edu.cn)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jped.2026.101592
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jped.2026.101592) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42501971/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探究新生儿非心脏手术中术中低血压的发生率、危险因素及其对预后的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：术中低血压发生率为59.8%（1024/1712）；独立危险因素包括ASA分级>Ⅲ（OR=1.357）、急诊手术（OR=1.323）、手术时长>60分钟（OR=1.457）、胸部手术（OR=1.727）、术前先天性心脏病（OR=1.379）及术前酸中毒（OR=1.349），而较高胎龄（OR=0.936）和神经外科手术（OR=0.566）为保护因素；术中低血压与术后恢复延长、急性肾损伤及脑室内出血增加显著相关。
- 临床启示：新生儿非心脏手术中低血压高发，识别危险与保护因素有助于指导围术期血流动力学优化，改善这一脆弱群体的短期预后。

**原文摘要**：Intraoperative hypotension is associated with adverse postoperative outcomes, yet evidence in the neonatal population is limited. This study aimed to investigate the incidence, risk factors, and prognostic significance of intraoperative hypotension in this population. This single-center retrospective study included 1712 neonates undergoing non-cardiac surgery from 2020 to 2025 at a tertiary children's hospital in China. Intraoperative hypotension was defined as a reduction in mean arterial pressure of more than 20% from the preoperative baseline and lasting for more than 5 min. Multivariate logistic regression identified independent risk factors. The incidence of intraoperative hypotension was 59.8% (1024/1712). Independent risk factors included American Society of Anesthesiologists Physical Status Classification > Ⅲ (OR = 1.357, 95% CI: 1.079-1.706, P = 0.009), emergency surgery (OR = 1.323, 95% CI: 1.054-1.660, P = 0.016), surgery duration > 60 min (OR = 1.457, 95% CI: 1.162-1.826, P = 0.001), thoracic surgery (OR = 1.727, 95% CI: 1.053-2.831, P = 0.030), preoperative congenital heart disease (OR = 1.379, 95% CI: 1.117-1.702, P = 0.003), and preoperative acidosis (OR = 1.349, 95% CI: 1.036-1.757, P = 0.026). Higher gestational age (OR = 0.936, 95% CI: 0.891-0.983, P = 0.008) and neurosurgery (OR = 0.566, 95% CI: 0.385-0.832, P = 0.004) were protective factors. Intraoperative hypotension was associated with prolonged postoperative recovery and increased acute kidney injury and intraventricular hemorrhage (all P < 0.05). Intraoperative hypotension is highly prevalent in neonatal non-cardiac surgery and was associated with short-term outcomes. Identified risk and protective factors may guide perioperative hemodynamic optimization in this vulnerable population.

## Medical Travel and Geographic Inequities in Access to Pediatric Congenital Heart Surgery: Evidence from a Nationwide Cohort in South Korea.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 06:35
- 期刊: Pediatr Cardiol · 中科院医学4区(儿科3区/心脏和心血管系统4区) · IF 1.3 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 韩国 · College of Pharmacy
- 通讯作者: Je NK (jenk@pusan.ac.kr)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00246-026-04393-y
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00246-026-04393-y) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42501126/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨韩国先天性心脏病手术的区域差异及其患者医疗旅行的决定因素。
- 核心发现/数据：非首尔大都市区居民中52.6%需前往首尔手术；至2022年，首尔手术量占全国总量超70%，高风险手术近90%；基础设施最差地区居民旅行概率是基准地区的29.9倍，但新生儿、急诊患者和医疗补助受助者等脆弱群体旅行概率显著更低。
- 临床启示：应推动从简单分散化向结构化区域化政策的转变，以保障脆弱群体公平获得优质手术资源。

**原文摘要**：Congenital heart disease (CHD) requires highly specialized surgical care. In South Korea, despite policies for balanced regional development, resources are increasingly concentrated in the Seoul Metropolitan Area (SMA), requiring non-SMA residents to undertake "medical travel" for surgery. We aimed to examine regional disparity and the determinants of medical travel. We analyzed nationwide claims data from the Health Insurance Review and Assessment Service (HIRA) (2009-2022), including patients aged 0-19 years who had undergone CHD surgery. Residence was defined using a proxy based on the most frequent outpatient visits. Factors associated with medical travel were evaluated using multivariable logistic regression. Of 12,101 non-SMA residents, 52.6% traveled to the SMA for surgery. By 2022, the proportion of surgeries performed in the SMA exceeded 70% overall and reached nearly 90% for high-risk procedures. Medical travel was driven by lack of local infrastructure; compared to Area 1 (baseline infrastructure), residents in Area 5 (virtually no infrastructure) were 29.9 times more likely to travel. High surgical risk increased the likelihood of travel. Conversely, vulnerable groups were less likely to travel, despite their greater clinical need for specialized care. Neonates, patients with emergency admissions, and Medical Aid recipients were all significantly less likely to travel than their respective comparators. This study confirmed the "de facto centralization" of pediatric CHD surgery in South Korea, driven by infrastructure insufficiency and expertise polarization. Although medical travel has become a common pathway for accessing specialized surgical care, access is restricted for the most vulnerable populations: neonates, emergency patients, and the socioeconomically disadvantaged. Policy interventions should shift from simple decentralization to structured regionalization to ensure equity and safety.

## Surgical treatment and outcomes of neural tube defects at the university of Gondar Comprehensive Specialized Hospital, Ethiopia: a two-year retrospective review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 06:35
- 期刊: Childs Nerv Syst
- 国家/单位: Gondar · College of Medicine and Health Sciences
- 通讯作者: Engidaw EA (eyobayenew@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00381-026-07411-9
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00381-026-07411-9) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42501095/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：分析埃塞俄比亚贡达尔大学综合专科医院神经管缺陷（尤其是脊柱裂谱系疾病）的临床表现、治疗模式及预后。
- 核心发现/数据：在176例确诊脊柱裂谱系疾病中，脊髓脊膜膨出最常见（77.8%），腰骶部（39.4%）好发；脑积水发生率78.3%（其中71%与脊柱裂相关），仅4.2%病例获得产前诊断；术后并发症率33.8%，死亡率4.6%，仅45.4%接受康复治疗。
- 临床启示：尽管手术可解决即时问题，但产前预防、多学科随访及康复体系严重不足，需加强叶酸补充、早期诊断和综合医疗策略以改善整体预后。

**原文摘要**：Spina bifida, particularly myelomeningocele (MMC), is one of the most common congenital central nervous system abnormalities compatible with life. Despite global efforts to reduce its incidence through folic acid fortification, the burden remains high in low-resource settings like Ethiopia. This study aimed to analyze the pattern of presentation, clinical characteristics, management, and outcomes of spina bifida spectrum disorders (SBSDs) at the University of Gondar Comprehensive Specialized Hospital. A retrospective cross-sectional study was conducted on 240 neonates and infants with neural tube defects, of whom 176 had confirmed spina bifida spectrum disorders (SBSDs) treated between December 2022 and November 2024 G.C at the University of Gondar Comprehensive Specialized Hospital. Data were extracted from medical records, including demographics, lesion characteristics, associated anomalies, surgical interventions, complications, and discharge outcomes. Descriptive statistics were used for analysis via SPSS version 27. Among the 176 confirmed SBSD cases (out of the total 240 patients), myelomeningocele was the most prevalent diagnosis, accounting for 77.8% of cases, followed by encephalocele (10.7%) and meningocele (7.9%). The most common lesion locations were lumbosacral (39.4%), lumbar (28.1%), and thoracolumbar (19.4%). Hydrocephalus was present in 78.3% of the total cohort and 71% of SBSD cases, with (66.4%) of those directly associated with SBSD, while sonographic Chiari malformation was identified in (31.3%). Prenatal diagnosis was made only in 10 (4.2%) patients and no counselling or intervention was employed. A total of 260 surgeries were performed for the cases, with the most common being neural tube defect repair/closure (58.5%), and followed by ventriculoperitoneal (VP) shunt insertion (25.0%). Endoscopic third ventriculostomy was done for (5.4%) of cases. Postoperative complications occurred in a considerable proportion of patients (33.8%), although most were minor and manageable, including wound infections (4.2%), seizures (5.0%), shunt malfunctions (0.4%), and mortality rate was (4.6%). Only 109 patients (45.4%) received physical rehabilitation, and developmental delays were noted in 107 (44.6%) cases. School attendance data were undocumented in (78.8%) of patients. The study highlights the higher prevalence of severe SBSDs in northwest Ethiopia, with significant associated morbidity. While surgical management addressed immediate needs, comprehensive care remained deficient. Major gaps persist in prenatal prevention, multidisciplinary follow-up, and rehabilitation. Enhancing public health strategies-including folic acid supplementation, early diagnosis, and integrated care-is essential to improve the overall outcomes.

## A novel topical nitroglycerin-lidocaine combination for procedural pain management and rapid cannulation in neonates: results from a randomized controlled clinical trial.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 06:35
- 期刊: Eur J Clin Pharmacol · 中科院医学4区(药学3区) · IF 3.5 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 伊朗 · Traditional Pharmacy and Pharmaceutical Sciences Research Center
- 通讯作者: Sahebnasagh A (masoomehsahebnasagh@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00228-026-04146-z
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00228-026-04146-z) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42501076/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估新型利多卡因-硝酸甘油局部制剂作为口服甜溶液辅助用药，用于新生儿外周静脉置管时镇痛的有效性和安全性。
- 核心发现/数据：与安慰剂加口服蔗糖相比，利多卡因-硝酸甘油组PIPP评分显著降低（7.0 vs 8.5，P=0.038），NFCS评分显著降低（5.0 vs 7.0，P<0.001），哭泣时间显著缩短（10秒 vs 25秒，P=0.045），且需要重复静脉穿刺的新生儿更少（6例 vs 14例，P=0.041），未观察到不良反应或高铁血红蛋白血症。
- 临床启示：该新型局部制剂可在操作前15分钟应用，快速提供镇痛并减少重复穿刺次数，为NICU程序性疼痛管理提供了一种实用且安全的策略。

**原文摘要**：Venipuncture is a common and painful procedure in neonates. Sweet solutions (sucrose and glucose) are recommended and widely used as standard care for reducing neonatal procedural pain. Topical local anesthetics may also reduce procedural pain but generally require longer application times to achieve adequate analgesia. This study investigated the efficacy and safety of a novel topical lidocaine-nitroglycerin preparation as an adjunct to standard oral sweet solutions in neonates undergoing peripheral venous catheterization. In this double-blind randomized clinical trial, 84 neonates (≥ 34 weeks gestation) undergoing peripheral venous catheterization were randomized to receive either topical lidocaine/nitroglycerin 2% or placebo ointment, applied 15 min before the procedure. All neonates in both groups received 0.5 mL of 24% oral sucrose two minutes before venipuncture as standard care. Pain was assessed using the Premature Infant Pain Profile (PIPP) and Neonatal Facial Coding System (NFCS) scales using standardized procedures and video verification. The duration of crying, time to cry onset, number of venipuncture attempts, and adverse events were recorded. Neonates treated with lidocaine-nitroglycerin topical formulation plus standard oral sucrose had significantly lower PIPP scores [7.0 vs. 8.5, P = 0.038] and NFCS scores [5.0 vs. 7.0, P < 0.001] compared with placebo ointment plus standard oral sucrose. Crying duration was significantly shorter [10 s vs. 25 s, P = 0.045], and fewer neonates required repeated venipuncture (6 vs. 14, P = 0.041). No adverse reactions or clinical signs of methemoglobinemia were observed. Topical lidocaine/nitroglycerin 2% provided rapid analgesia and reduced repeated venipuncture attempts in neonates, offering a practical strategy for pain management in the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU). Further large multicenter trials are warranted to confirm efficacy and long-term safety. The trial was registered as IRCT20190810044500N19 (22/08/2021) https://irct.behdasht.gov.ir/trial/47524 .

## Evolving role of mitochondrial dysfunction and mitophagy in necrotizing enterocolitis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 06:35
- 期刊: Pediatr Res · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区) · IF 3.3 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University at Buffalo
- 通讯作者: Garg PM (gargparvesh@hotmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1038/s41390-026-05350-4
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-026-05350-4) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42502112/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨线粒体功能障碍和线粒体自噬在坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）发病机制中的演变作用，以明确其作为连接微生物信号、免疫反应和上皮损伤的核心环节。  
- 核心发现/数据：线粒体功能障碍是NEC发病的核心，连接了菌群失调、免疫失调和上皮损伤，将NEC重新定义为免疫代谢性疾病；ACSL1被确定为有前景的生物标志物和治疗靶点，可指导早期诊断并调节炎症与氧化应激。  
- 临床启示：未来研究聚焦线粒体功能障碍的时机与线粒体自噬机制，有望在不可逆肠损伤发生前实现早期检测和靶向干预，从而改善NEC患儿的预后。

**原文摘要**：Necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) is a severe, multifactorial disease of prematurity characterized by a heterogeneous pathophysiology. A dysregulated cytokine-driven inflammatory response is central to its progression, underscoring the need to identify key molecular triggers and pathways. Emerging evidence suggests that mitochondrial dysfunction represents a central nidus linking microbial signals to immune responses, and ultimately to epithelial injury. Future studies defining the timing and mechanisms of mitochondrial dysfunction and the role of mitophagy to better understand the interplay of the microbiome, immune responses, and mitochondria in the pathogenesis of NEC are greatly needed. IMPACT: Mitochondrial dysfunction is central to NEC pathogenesis, linking dysbiosis, immune dysregulation, and epithelial injury, and redefining NEC as an immunometabolic disease. ACSL1 emerges as a promising biomarker and therapeutic target, with potential to guide early diagnosis and modulate inflammation and oxidative stress. Future focus on timing and mitophagy may enable early detection and targeted interventions before irreversible intestinal injury develops, improving outcomes in NEC.
