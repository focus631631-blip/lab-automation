## Surgical, Reproductive, and Child Developmental Outcomes After 10 Years of Uterus Transplantation in the United States.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:58

**原文摘要**：To review the surgical, reproductive, and child developmental outcomes after uterus transplants performed in the United States over the past decade. In this prospective cohort study, data on donors, recipients, and children born after uterus transplantation were collected at the four centers performing uterus transplants in the United States from February 2016 to December 2025. Interventions included donor hysterectomy, uterus transplantation, postoperative immunosuppression and monitoring, fertility and obstetric care, recipient graft hysterectomy, and neonatal and pediatric follow-up. Survival analyses evaluated trends and compared outcomes between living donor and deceased donor transplants. Among 60 uterus transplants, the most common indication was Mayer-Rokitansky-Küster-Hauser syndrome (n=52), followed by prior hysterectomy (n=6), dysfunctional uterus (n=1), and androgen insensitivity syndrome (n=1). Most transplants (42, 70.0%) involved living donors; 18 (30.0%) involved deceased donors. No life-threatening complications or mortality occurred. Complications necessitating reoperation occurred in 17 of 60 recipients (28.3%), including graft loss attributable to vascular thrombosis, and in 6 of 42 living donors (14.3%), including ureteral injury and vaginal cuff dehiscence. As of December 2025, 42 of 60 recipients (70.0%) had achieved at least one live birth or ongoing clinical pregnancy, and nine (15.0%) had two live births. Among the 48 neonates born, median gestational age at delivery was 36 5/7 weeks (IQR 34 3/7-37 1/7 weeks), with 58.3% born preterm. Neurodevelopment was normal at discharge and 6 months; at 3-year follow-up (n=29), two children had been diagnosed with autism, and no other diagnoses or growth disorders were observed. Uterus transplantation offers a viable path to childbearing for women with absolute uterine factor infertility. These aggregate data from active U.S. programs provide a contemporary reference for clinicians counseling patients on treatment options.

## Biophysical rationale and emerging clinical perspectives of surfactants in thoracic surgery: a translational review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:58
- 期刊: Langenbecks Arch Surg · 中科院医学3区(外科3区) · IF 2.2 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Aachen · University Clinic RWTH Aachen
- 通讯作者: Yibulayin X (xyibulayin@ukaachen.de)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00423-026-04265-2
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-026-04265-2) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42809060/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：综述肺表面活性剂在胸外科中的当前与新兴应用，并评估其在围术期肺保护中的潜在作用。
- 核心发现/数据：外源性表面活性剂可改善肺顺应性、预防肺不张并减轻术后呼吸并发症。其还可能作为药物递送载体或抗炎剂用于胸外科。摘要未提供具体定量数据，且临床应用仍有限，最佳剂量、给药时机和患者选择尚待明确。
- 临床启示：表面活性剂有望成为胸外科围术期呼吸管理的辅助手段，但当前证据不足以支持常规应用，需进一步临床研究验证后推广。

**原文摘要**：Pulmonary surfactants are essential for alveolar stability and gas exchange. While their clinical use is well established in neonatal care, their potential in thoracic surgery remains underexplored. To review the current and emerging applications of surfactants in thoracic surgical practice, and to assess their role in perioperative lung protection. We reviewed recent literature on surfactant biology, perioperative administration strategies, and novel formulations, including bio-derived and responsive surfactants. Exogenous surfactants may improve lung compliance, preventing atelectasis, and mitigating postoperative respiratory complications. In thoracic surgery, surfactants may also serve as drug delivery vehicles or anti-inflammatory agents. However, clinical use remains limited, and further studies are needed to define optimal dosing, timing, and patient selection. Surfactants represent a versatile and evolving tool in thoracic surgery. With continued innovation and clinical validation, they may become an effective adjunct for enhancing perioperative respiratory outcomes.

## Between technique and virtue: a bioethical reflection on the management of patients with anorectal malformations.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:58
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Bogotá · Department of Clinical Epidemiology and Biostatistics
- 通讯作者: Correa C (catalina.correa@javeriana.edu.co)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06649-3
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06649-3) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42809054/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：从生物伦理视角探讨肛门直肠畸形（ARM）患者从新生儿手术决策到长期功能康复管理中的临床与伦理挑战。
- 核心发现/数据：该综述聚焦三类生物伦理问题：Beauchamp与Childress生物医学伦理原则在ARM手术与康复中的应用、罕见病临床与伦理决策交叉的伦理框架，以及复杂小儿外科疾病专业能力获取中的伦理挑战。本文为综述，无原始定量数据；作者提出美德伦理与医学专业精神可补充原则主义，为小儿外科医生全程照护中的困难决策提供实践指导，并构建了相应生物伦理框架。
- 临床启示：在ARM全程管理中，除遵循原则主义外，应融入美德伦理和医学专业精神，以促进患者安全、专业问责和负责任的职业发展。该框架有助于临床团队在手术决策、康复及能力建设中更系统地处理伦理困境。

**原文摘要**：Anorectal malformations (ARM) present complex clinical and ethical challenges that extend from neonatal surgical decision-making to long-term functional rehabilitation. This review explores three bioethical issues that emerge in the care of patients with ARM: first, the application of Beauchamp and Childress's principles of biomedical ethics to surgical management and rehabilitation; second, the identification of an ethical framework to navigate the intersection between clinical and ethical decision-making in rare diseases; and third, the ethical challenges related to acquiring professional competence in the management of complex pediatric surgical conditions. We argue that virtue ethics and medical professionalism complement Beauchamp and Childress's principles by providing practical guidance for pediatric surgeons facing difficult decisions throughout the continuum of care. This review offers a bioethical framework for addressing clinical decision-making and questions regarding professional competence in ARM. It highlights the role of bioethics in promoting patient safety, professional accountability, and responsible professional development.
