## Outcomes of Acute Kidney Injury in Pediatric Liver Transplant: Retrospective Cohort Study, 2013-2023.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:11
- 期刊: Pediatr Crit Care Med
- 国家/单位: 英国 · King's College Hospital NHS Foundation Trust
- 通讯作者: Deep A
- DOI: 10.1097/PCC.0000000000004052
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/PCC.0000000000004052) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42987181/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：明确儿童肝移植术后急性肾损伤（AKI）的发生率及其相关术前和术中因素。
- 核心发现/数据：单中心2013—2023年283例肝病肝移植患儿中，术后第1周AKI发生率为64.7%（183/283），其中严重AKI（KDIGO 2—3期或需CRRT）占30.4%（86/283）；50.2%（142/283）的AKI峰值出现在术后24小时内。多因素分析显示，移植前胆红素水平、基线肾功能及较长手术时间与严重AKI相关；严重AKI与机械通气延长、PICU及住院时间延长和死亡率增高相关。90天随访时1.9%（5/260）患儿eGFR<60 mL/min/1.73 m²。
- 临床启示：儿童肝移植术后早期AKI负担高，应加强术后24小时至1周内肾功能监测，并关注术前胆红素、基线肾功能和手术时长等风险因素以优化围术期管理。

**原文摘要**：We aimed to determine the prevalence of acute kidney injury (AKI) after pediatric liver transplantation (LT) and the associated preoperative and intraoperative factors. Single-center retrospective cohort study. Sixteen-bed PICU in a university-affiliated tertiary care hospital. PICU admissions with liver disease who underwent LT between January 2013 and December 2023. None. We analyzed data from the first week, 90 days, and at 1-year follow-up. Patients with stage 2-3 AKI according to the Kidney Disease: Improving Global Outcomes (KDIGO) definitions or those receiving continuous renal replacement therapy for renal or mixed hepatorenal indications were defined as having severe AKI. There were 283 children with liver disease who underwent LT. Over the first postoperative week, 64.7% (183/283) developed AKI, including 30.4% (86/283) with severe AKI. Peak AKI occurred within the first 24 hours after surgery in 50.2% of LT patients (142/283). In a multivariable logistic regression model, pretransplant bilirubin levels, baseline renal function, and longer operative duration were associated with severe AKI. Severe AKI was associated with prolonged mechanical ventilation, longer PICU and hospital stays, and greater mortality in posttransplant patients. At 90-day follow-up, 1.9% of patients (5/260) had an estimated glomerular filtration rate less than 60 mL/min/1.73 m2. In our 2013-2023 retrospective cohort, a high proportion of children developed severe AKI within 1 week of LT. Preoperative factors such as bilirubin level, renal function, and intraoperative factors like procedure duration were associated with severe AKI. Severe AKI was associated with prolonged mechanical ventilation, length of PICU and hospital stay, and greater mortality. Further studies are needed to explore risk models.

## False-Positive Prenatal Suspicion of Partial Anomalous Pulmonary Venous Connection: A Benign Confluence Variant Clarified by Cardiac CT.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:11
- 期刊: Echocardiography · 中科院医学4区(心脏和心血管系统4区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Wuhan Asia General Hospital affiliated to Wuhan University of Science and Technology
- 通讯作者: Deng W
- DOI: 10.1111/echo.70664
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1111/echo.70664) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42986780/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过一例产前超声假阳性怀疑部分型肺静脉异位连接的病例，说明心脏CT对澄清良性肺静脉汇合变异的决定性作用。
- 核心发现/数据：产前超声曾假阳性怀疑PAPVC，受初始报告及声窗限制，产后超声又误报肺静脉引流至右心房。心脏CT血管造影最终证实为良性肺静脉汇合变异并全部引流至左心房，同时发现纵隔肺疝、支气管狭窄及叶内型肺隔离症，提示Scimitar样变异；患者拒绝手术并接受保守管理。
- 临床启示：超声对肺静脉回流的判断可能出现序贯性假阳性，临床需警惕；心脏CT可用于明确复杂肺静脉及合并畸形的解剖诊断。

**原文摘要**：Prenatal ultrasound raised a false-positive suspicion of partial anomalous pulmonary venous connection (PAPVC). Influenced by this initial report and limited by acoustic windows, postnatal echocardiography erroneously reported drainage into the right atrium. Cardiac CT angiography definitively confirmed a benign pulmonary venous confluence variant draining entirely into the left atrium, and revealed coexisting mediastinal lung herniation, bronchial stenosis, and an intralobar pulmonary sequestration, suggestive of a Scimitar-like variant. Surgery was declined, and conservative management was pursued. This case highlights sequential false-positive echocardiographic interpretations and the decisive role of CT.

## Risk factors for adverse perinatal outcomes in pregnancies with early- and late-onset extreme fetal growth restriction, estimated fetal weight ≤ 1st centile: A multicenter retrospective study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:11
- 期刊: Taiwan J Obstet Gynecol · 中科院医学4区(妇产科学4区) · IF 2.7 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Incheon · Inha University Hospital
- 通讯作者: Choi SR (csran@inha.ac.kr)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.tjog.2025.08.012
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tjog.2025.08.012) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42951457/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨早发（<32周）和晚发（≥32周）极重度胎儿生长受限（EFW≤第1百分位）妊娠不良围产结局的相关危险因素。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入261例（早发135例、晚发126例），早发与晚发组围产死亡率分别为7.4%和1.6%，复合不良围产结局发生率分别为54.1%和12.7%。早发组危险因素包括分娩孕周（aOR 0.52，95%CI 0.36-0.74）、胎心监护无反应（aOR 0.34，95%CI 0.12-0.95）及分娩前1周EFW（aOR 1.003，95%CI 1.000-1.005）；晚发组危险因素包括分娩孕周（aOR 0.79，95%CI 0.64-0.98）和妊娠期糖尿病（aOR 1.87，95%CI 1.47-9.58）。摘要结论称两组复合不良围产结局具有可比性，分娩孕周可能是重要危险因素。
- 临床启示：应重视分娩孕周作为早发和晚发极重度FGR复合不良围产结局的关键危险因素，个体化优化分娩时机；晚发者需关注妊娠期糖尿病并加强围产期监测。

**原文摘要**：To investigate the risk factors associated with adverse perinatal outcome in early-onset (<32 weeks) and late-onset (≥32 weeks) extreme fetal growth restriction (FGR), defined as estimated fetal weight (EFW) ≤ 1st centile. A multicenter, retrospective cohort study was performed on pregnancies complicated by early- and late-onset extreme FGR within a week of delivery between 2018 and 2021. Doppler indices were converted as percentile adjusted for gestational age (GA). Composite adverse perinatal outcome (APO) was defined as fetal or neonatal death or at least one of the after severe perinatal morbidities: respiratory distress syndrome, bronchopulmonary dysplasia, intraventricular hemorrhage, proven sepsis or necrotizing enterocolitis. Odds ratios (OR) with 95% confidence intervals (CI) were estimated using multivariable logistic regression. Overall, 261 cases were included: 135 cases of early- and 126 cases of late-onset extreme FGR. The perinatal mortality rate was 7.4% in early- and 1.6% in late-onset extreme FGR. Composite APO was 54.1% and 12.7% in the early- and late-onset extreme FGR groups, respectively. The risk factors for the composite APO were GA at delivery (aOR 0.52, 95% CI 0.36-0.74), the presence of non-reassuring cardiotocography (aOR 0.34, 95% CI 0.12-0.95), and EFW within 1 week before delivery (aOR 1.003, 95% CI 1.000-1.005) in early-, and GA at delivery (aOR 0.79, 95% CI 0.64-0.98) and diabetes during pregnancy (aOR 1.87, 95% CI 1.47-9.58) in late-onset extreme FGR. Composite APO in early- and late-onset extreme FGR were comparable. GA at delivery may be the significant risk factor for the composite APO in early- and late-onset extreme FGR.

## Biliary Atresia: Advances in Screening, Diagnosis, and Therapeutic Approaches.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:11
- 期刊: Clin Liver Dis · 中科院医学4区(胃肠肝病学4区) · IF 2.9 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Rabbani TA (Tebyan.Rabbani@UTSouthwestern.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.cld.2026.08.001
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cld.2026.08.001) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42946518/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：综述胆道闭锁在筛查、诊断及治疗方面的最新进展。
- 核心发现/数据：胆道闭锁以肝外胆管梗阻为特征，是儿童肝移植的主要指征。初始胆道损伤可能始于宫内，涉及遗传、病毒、免疫、毒性和血管等机制。生后30至45天内行Kasai肝门肠吻合术时结局最佳，早期识别至关重要。
- 临床启示：应重视胆道闭锁的早期识别与及时转诊，争取在生后30至45天窗口内完成Kasai手术以改善预后。

**原文摘要**：Biliary atresia is characterized by obstruction of the extrahepatic bile ducts and is the leading indication for pediatric liver transplantation. This review organizes recent advances across 3 intervals of the disease's natural history: before birth, from birth to the Kasai hepatic portoenterostomy, and after surgery. The initial biliary insult appears to begin in utero, with proposed etiologies spanning genetic, viral, immune, toxic, and vascular mechanisms. Because outcomes are best when surgery occurs within the first 30 to 45 days of life, early identification is essential.

## Prenatal Prediction of Delayed Abdominal Closure in Isolated Congenital Diaphragmatic Hernia Using Observed-to-Expected Lung-to-Head Ratio.

- 来源: medRxiv (preprint) · 推送 04:11
- 期刊: medRxiv (preprint)
- 国家/单位: Hospital Universitario La Paz
- 通讯作者: Jose Luis Encinas
- DOI: 10.64898/2026.11.04.26367256
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.04.26367256)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估观察值与期望值肺头比（O/E LHR）对孤立性先天性膈疝（CDH）新生儿产后需延迟腹壁关闭（DAC）的产前预测能力。
- 核心发现/数据：回顾性纳入45例孤立性左侧CDH新生儿，28例一期关闭、17例需DAC；DAC组胎龄更低、肝疝出率更高、ECMO需求更高且死亡率更高（均p<0.01）。O/E LHR预测DAC的AUC为0.939，最佳截断值34.1%，敏感性82.35%、特异性82.14%、阳性预测值73.68%、阴性预测值88.46%。多因素调整肝疝出后，O/E LHR仍与DAC独立相关（OR 0.821，95%CI 0.668-0.939，p=0.002）。
- 临床启示：O/E LHR可用于产前识别DAC高风险患儿，其预测价值独立于肝疝出。该初步发现有望支持产前风险分层，但仍需外部验证。

**原文摘要**：Objective: O/E LHR is a well-established prenatal severity marker in congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH), but its capacity to predict the postnatal need for delayed abdominal wall closure (DAC) remains undefined. We aimed to determine its predictive capacity. Method: Retrospective analysis of neonates with isolated left-sided CDH (2015-2026), comparing primary closure with DAC. Discriminative capacity was assessed by ROC analysis and the optimal cut-off was determined by bootstrap-aggregated approach. Logistic regression estimated univariate and liver herniation adjusted multivariate odds ratios (OR). Result: 45 neonates were included, 28 underwent primary closure and 17 required DAC. DAC was associated with lower gestational age, higher rates of liver herniation, greater ECMO requirement, and higher mortality than primary closure (all p < 0.01). O/E LHR showed excellent discriminative ability for DAC (AUC 0.939), with an optimal cut-off of 34.1% (sensitivity 82.35%, specificity 82.14%, positive predictive value 73.68%, negative predictive value 88.46%). O/E LHR was independently associated with DAC after multivariate analysis (OR 0.821, 95% CI 0.668-0.939, p = 0.002). Conclusions: O/E LHR was independently associated with DAC risk beyond liver herniation. These preliminary findings suggest a potential role for O/E LHR in prenatal risk stratification for DAC, pending external validation.

## Cardiac Magnetic Resonance Before Minimally Invasive Repair of Pectus Excavatum and After Bar Removal.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 11:48
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Buenos Aires · Clinica Mi Pectus
- 通讯作者: Toselli L (dra.luzia.toselli@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163589
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163589) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42988107/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨微创漏斗胸修复术（MIRPE）取出支撑条后，心脏磁共振（CMR）所示心脏受压及相关结构和运动异常是否改善。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入60例孤立性漏斗胸患者，术前平均Haller指数5.9±3.0，全部存在心脏受压，40%报告运动不耐受；取条后无运动不耐受，心脏受压比例由100%降至33%（平均差-66.7%，95%CI -78.6至-54.7），心包积液由25%降至5%，异常室间隔运动由60.0%降至33.3%，吸气性室间隔变平由22%降至10%。CMR还显示三尖瓣/二尖瓣环比值增加（0.86±0.16 vs 0.92±0.06），左室偏心指数降低，尤其吸气时（1.61±0.2 vs 1.45±0.1）。
- 临床启示：MIRPE取条后多数患者可获得右心机械减压和室间隔运动改善，但约1/3仍有残余心脏受压，提示术后需结合症状与CMR进行随访评估。

**原文摘要**：Although minimally invasive repair of pectus excavatum (MIRPE) is associated with favorable outcomes, it remains generally unknown whether cardiac compression persists after bar removal. We therefore explored cardiac magnetic resonance (CMR) outcomes after bar removal. We evaluated a retrospective cohort of patients with diagnosis of isolated pectus excavatum (PEX) who underwent CMR before MIRPE and after bar removal. We evaluated the effect of MIRPE on CMR outcomes including the presence of cardiac compression, pericardial effusion, abnormal septal motion, inspiratory septal flattening, and left ventricular eccentricity. We included 60 patients who underwent CMR before repair and after bar removal. Before surgery, the mean Haller index was 5.9±3.0. All patients had baseline cardiac compression and 24 (40%) patients referred exercise intolerance. After bar removal, none of the patients reported exercise intolerance and we identified a significant decrease in the presence of cardiac compression [from 100% to 33%, mean difference -66.7 (95%CI -78.6; -54.7)]. With regard to RV inflow and septal wall kinetics, we identified a significant increase in the tricuspid to mitral annulus ratio [0.86±0.16 vs. 0.92±0.06, mean difference 0.06 (95%CI 0.02; 0.10)], and significantly reduced rates of abnormal septal motion [60.0%, vs 33.3%, mean difference -26.7 (95%CI -43.2; -10.1)], and a change in the rates of inspiratory septal flattening from 22% to 10% (mean difference -11.9 (95%CI -22.5; -1.3). Furthermore, we found a significant reduction in left ventricular eccentricity indexes after bar removal, particularly during inspiration [before MIRPE 1.61±0.2 vs. after removal 1.45±0.1, mean difference -0.16 (95%CI -0.23; -0.09)]. Finally, we also found a significant decline in the rates of pericardial effusion [25% vs. 5%, mean difference -20.0 (95%CI -30.1; -9.9)]. CMR assessment before MIRPE and after bar removal demonstrated persistent anatomical decompression, with a significant decrease in the presence and severity of cardiac compression, enhanced right ventricular inflow geometry, and favorable changes of septal wall kinetics. These structural modifications reflect mechanical relief of the right ventricle following bar removal. Retrospective before and after study LEVEL OF EVIDENCE: III.

## Negative-pressure wound therapy for postoperative wound dehiscence after pediatric spinal dysraphism surgery: neonatal portable SNaP® case report and PRISMA-guided review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:48
- 期刊: Neurochirurgie · 中科院医学4区(临床神经病学4区/外科4区) · IF 1.5 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 67200 Strasbourg · Hôpitaux Universitaires de Strasbourg (HUS)
- 通讯作者: Padonou X (xerxes.padonou@chru-strasbourg.fr)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.neuchi.2026.101897
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuchi.2026.101897) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42988330/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告便携式负压伤口治疗（SNaP®）用于新生儿脊髓闭合不全术后伤口裂开的病例，并通过PRISMA导向综述评估相关儿科证据。
- 核心发现/数据：足月女婴因复杂开放性腰骶部脊髓闭合不全于生后第2天手术，术后第12天出现5.5×4.0 cm伤口裂开且无硬膜暴露；术后第21天启用SNaP®（-125 mmHg持续，每3-4天换药）治疗22天，术后第43天完全上皮化，无脑脊液漏、感染或再手术，1年随访神经功能及瘢痕满意，MRI无假性脊膜膨出或复发。PRISMA综述仅纳入4篇文献共6例儿童脊髓闭合不全相关脊柱手术后NPWT病例，均使用传统VAC®系统，尚无SNaP®在此类情境的报道。
- 临床启示：在确保硬膜水密闭合、做好界面保护和优化营养支持的前提下，便携式SNaP®负压伤口治疗可作为新生儿腰骶部椎管内手术后伤口裂开的可行救援选择；但现有证据有限，需密切监测并个体化应用。

**原文摘要**：Wound complications after neonatal spinal dysraphism surgery carry a high risk of infection and, when dural opening is required, potential cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) leakage. While negative-pressure wound therapy (NPWT) is well established in adults, evidence in neonatal neurosurgery-especially with portable systems-remains scarce. A term female neonate underwent surgery on day 2 for complex open lumbosacral spinal dysraphism with an exophytic mass first identified at birth. Imaging and surgery showed split cord malformation, low tethered cord, and mature teratoma with intradural extension. En bloc resection, filum terminale transection, and watertight dural closure were performed. On postoperative day (POD) 12, wound dehiscence developed, resulting in a 5.5 × 4.0 cm skin defect without dural exposure. After directed wound care, portable NPWT (SNaP®) was initiated on POD21 (-125 mmHg, continuous) for 22 days, with dressing changes every 3-4 days. Complete epithelialization occurred by POD43 without CSF leak, infection, or reoperation. At one year, neurological and scar outcomes were satisfactory, with no pseudomeningocele or recurrence on MRI. In a PRISMA-guided search, we identified 4 publications reporting 6 pediatric cases of NPWT after dysraphism-related spinal surgery with dural opening and/or exposed dura. Published cases exclusively used conventional VAC® systems, and data in intradural settings remain limited; no prior report described SNaP® use in this context. After secure watertight dural closure, appropriate interface protection, and optimized nutritional support, portable NPWT (SNaP®) may represent a feasible rescue option for neonatal lumbosacral wound dehiscence following intradural surgery.
