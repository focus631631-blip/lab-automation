## Outcomes of Acute Kidney Injury in Pediatric Liver Transplant: Retrospective Cohort Study, 2013-2023.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:11
- 期刊: Pediatr Crit Care Med
- 国家/单位: 英国 · King's College Hospital NHS Foundation Trust
- 通讯作者: Deep A
- DOI: 10.1097/PCC.0000000000004052
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/PCC.0000000000004052) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42987181/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：明确儿童肝移植术后急性肾损伤（AKI）的发生率及其相关术前和术中因素。
- 核心发现/数据：单中心2013—2023年283例肝病肝移植患儿中，术后第1周AKI发生率为64.7%（183/283），其中严重AKI（KDIGO 2—3期或需CRRT）占30.4%（86/283）；50.2%（142/283）的AKI峰值出现在术后24小时内。多因素分析显示，移植前胆红素水平、基线肾功能及较长手术时间与严重AKI相关；严重AKI与机械通气延长、PICU及住院时间延长和死亡率增高相关。90天随访时1.9%（5/260）患儿eGFR<60 mL/min/1.73 m²。
- 临床启示：儿童肝移植术后早期AKI负担高，应加强术后24小时至1周内肾功能监测，并关注术前胆红素、基线肾功能和手术时长等风险因素以优化围术期管理。

**原文摘要**：We aimed to determine the prevalence of acute kidney injury (AKI) after pediatric liver transplantation (LT) and the associated preoperative and intraoperative factors. Single-center retrospective cohort study. Sixteen-bed PICU in a university-affiliated tertiary care hospital. PICU admissions with liver disease who underwent LT between January 2013 and December 2023. None. We analyzed data from the first week, 90 days, and at 1-year follow-up. Patients with stage 2-3 AKI according to the Kidney Disease: Improving Global Outcomes (KDIGO) definitions or those receiving continuous renal replacement therapy for renal or mixed hepatorenal indications were defined as having severe AKI. There were 283 children with liver disease who underwent LT. Over the first postoperative week, 64.7% (183/283) developed AKI, including 30.4% (86/283) with severe AKI. Peak AKI occurred within the first 24 hours after surgery in 50.2% of LT patients (142/283). In a multivariable logistic regression model, pretransplant bilirubin levels, baseline renal function, and longer operative duration were associated with severe AKI. Severe AKI was associated with prolonged mechanical ventilation, longer PICU and hospital stays, and greater mortality in posttransplant patients. At 90-day follow-up, 1.9% of patients (5/260) had an estimated glomerular filtration rate less than 60 mL/min/1.73 m2. In our 2013-2023 retrospective cohort, a high proportion of children developed severe AKI within 1 week of LT. Preoperative factors such as bilirubin level, renal function, and intraoperative factors like procedure duration were associated with severe AKI. Severe AKI was associated with prolonged mechanical ventilation, length of PICU and hospital stay, and greater mortality. Further studies are needed to explore risk models.

## False-Positive Prenatal Suspicion of Partial Anomalous Pulmonary Venous Connection: A Benign Confluence Variant Clarified by Cardiac CT.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:11
- 期刊: Echocardiography · 中科院医学4区(心脏和心血管系统4区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Wuhan Asia General Hospital affiliated to Wuhan University of Science and Technology
- 通讯作者: Deng W
- DOI: 10.1111/echo.70664
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1111/echo.70664) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42986780/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过一例产前超声假阳性怀疑部分型肺静脉异位连接的病例，说明心脏CT对澄清良性肺静脉汇合变异的决定性作用。
- 核心发现/数据：产前超声曾假阳性怀疑PAPVC，受初始报告及声窗限制，产后超声又误报肺静脉引流至右心房。心脏CT血管造影最终证实为良性肺静脉汇合变异并全部引流至左心房，同时发现纵隔肺疝、支气管狭窄及叶内型肺隔离症，提示Scimitar样变异；患者拒绝手术并接受保守管理。
- 临床启示：超声对肺静脉回流的判断可能出现序贯性假阳性，临床需警惕；心脏CT可用于明确复杂肺静脉及合并畸形的解剖诊断。

**原文摘要**：Prenatal ultrasound raised a false-positive suspicion of partial anomalous pulmonary venous connection (PAPVC). Influenced by this initial report and limited by acoustic windows, postnatal echocardiography erroneously reported drainage into the right atrium. Cardiac CT angiography definitively confirmed a benign pulmonary venous confluence variant draining entirely into the left atrium, and revealed coexisting mediastinal lung herniation, bronchial stenosis, and an intralobar pulmonary sequestration, suggestive of a Scimitar-like variant. Surgery was declined, and conservative management was pursued. This case highlights sequential false-positive echocardiographic interpretations and the decisive role of CT.

## Risk factors for adverse perinatal outcomes in pregnancies with early- and late-onset extreme fetal growth restriction, estimated fetal weight ≤ 1st centile: A multicenter retrospective study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:11
- 期刊: Taiwan J Obstet Gynecol · 中科院医学4区(妇产科学4区) · IF 2.7 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Incheon · Inha University Hospital
- 通讯作者: Choi SR (csran@inha.ac.kr)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.tjog.2025.08.012
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tjog.2025.08.012) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42951457/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨早发（<32周）和晚发（≥32周）极重度胎儿生长受限（EFW≤第1百分位）妊娠不良围产结局的相关危险因素。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入261例（早发135例、晚发126例），早发与晚发组围产死亡率分别为7.4%和1.6%，复合不良围产结局发生率分别为54.1%和12.7%。早发组危险因素包括分娩孕周（aOR 0.52，95%CI 0.36-0.74）、胎心监护无反应（aOR 0.34，95%CI 0.12-0.95）及分娩前1周EFW（aOR 1.003，95%CI 1.000-1.005）；晚发组危险因素包括分娩孕周（aOR 0.79，95%CI 0.64-0.98）和妊娠期糖尿病（aOR 1.87，95%CI 1.47-9.58）。摘要结论称两组复合不良围产结局具有可比性，分娩孕周可能是重要危险因素。
- 临床启示：应重视分娩孕周作为早发和晚发极重度FGR复合不良围产结局的关键危险因素，个体化优化分娩时机；晚发者需关注妊娠期糖尿病并加强围产期监测。

**原文摘要**：To investigate the risk factors associated with adverse perinatal outcome in early-onset (<32 weeks) and late-onset (≥32 weeks) extreme fetal growth restriction (FGR), defined as estimated fetal weight (EFW) ≤ 1st centile. A multicenter, retrospective cohort study was performed on pregnancies complicated by early- and late-onset extreme FGR within a week of delivery between 2018 and 2021. Doppler indices were converted as percentile adjusted for gestational age (GA). Composite adverse perinatal outcome (APO) was defined as fetal or neonatal death or at least one of the after severe perinatal morbidities: respiratory distress syndrome, bronchopulmonary dysplasia, intraventricular hemorrhage, proven sepsis or necrotizing enterocolitis. Odds ratios (OR) with 95% confidence intervals (CI) were estimated using multivariable logistic regression. Overall, 261 cases were included: 135 cases of early- and 126 cases of late-onset extreme FGR. The perinatal mortality rate was 7.4% in early- and 1.6% in late-onset extreme FGR. Composite APO was 54.1% and 12.7% in the early- and late-onset extreme FGR groups, respectively. The risk factors for the composite APO were GA at delivery (aOR 0.52, 95% CI 0.36-0.74), the presence of non-reassuring cardiotocography (aOR 0.34, 95% CI 0.12-0.95), and EFW within 1 week before delivery (aOR 1.003, 95% CI 1.000-1.005) in early-, and GA at delivery (aOR 0.79, 95% CI 0.64-0.98) and diabetes during pregnancy (aOR 1.87, 95% CI 1.47-9.58) in late-onset extreme FGR. Composite APO in early- and late-onset extreme FGR were comparable. GA at delivery may be the significant risk factor for the composite APO in early- and late-onset extreme FGR.

- DOI: 10.1016/j.cld.2026.08.001
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cld.2026.08.001) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42946518/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：综述胆道闭锁在筛查、诊断及治疗方面的最新进展。
- 核心发现/数据：胆道闭锁以肝外胆管梗阻为特征，是儿童肝移植的主要指征。初始胆道损伤可能始于宫内，涉及遗传、病毒、免疫、毒性和血管等机制。生后30至45天内行Kasai肝门肠吻合术时结局最佳，早期识别至关重要。
- 临床启示：应重视胆道闭锁的早期识别与及时转诊，争取在生后30至45天窗口内完成Kasai手术以改善预后。

**原文摘要**：Biliary atresia is characterized by obstruction of the extrahepatic bile ducts and is the leading indication for pediatric liver transplantation. This review organizes recent advances across 3 intervals of the disease's natural history: before birth, from birth to the Kasai hepatic portoenterostomy, and after surgery. The initial biliary insult appears to begin in utero, with proposed etiologies spanning genetic, viral, immune, toxic, and vascular mechanisms. Because outcomes are best when surgery occurs within the first 30 to 45 days of life, early identification is essential.

## Prenatal Prediction of Delayed Abdominal Closure in Isolated Congenital Diaphragmatic Hernia Using Observed-to-Expected Lung-to-Head Ratio.

- 通讯作者: Jose Luis Encinas
- DOI: 10.64898/2026.11.04.26367256
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.04.26367256)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估观察值与期望值肺头比（O/E LHR）对孤立性先天性膈疝（CDH）新生儿产后需延迟腹壁关闭（DAC）的产前预测能力。
- 核心发现/数据：回顾性纳入45例孤立性左侧CDH新生儿，28例一期关闭、17例需DAC；DAC组胎龄更低、肝疝出率更高、ECMO需求更高且死亡率更高（均p<0.01）。O/E LHR预测DAC的AUC为0.939，最佳截断值34.1%，敏感性82.35%、特异性82.14%、阳性预测值73.68%、阴性预测值88.46%。多因素调整肝疝出后，O/E LHR仍与DAC独立相关（OR 0.821，95%CI 0.668-0.939，p=0.002）。
- 临床启示：O/E LHR可用于产前识别DAC高风险患儿，其预测价值独立于肝疝出。该初步发现有望支持产前风险分层，但仍需外部验证。

**原文摘要**：Objective: O/E LHR is a well-established prenatal severity marker in congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH), but its capacity to predict the postnatal need for delayed abdominal wall closure (DAC) remains undefined. We aimed to determine its predictive capacity. Method: Retrospective analysis of neonates with isolated left-sided CDH (2015-2026), comparing primary closure with DAC. Discriminative capacity was assessed by ROC analysis and the optimal cut-off was determined by bootstrap-aggregated approach. Logistic regression estimated univariate and liver herniation adjusted multivariate odds ratios (OR). Result: 45 neonates were included, 28 underwent primary closure and 17 required DAC. DAC was associated with lower gestational age, higher rates of liver herniation, greater ECMO requirement, and higher mortality than primary closure (all p < 0.01). O/E LHR was independently associated with DAC after multivariate analysis (OR 0.821, 95% CI 0.668-0.939, p = 0.002). Conclusions: O/E LHR was independently associated with DAC risk beyond liver herniation. These preliminary findings suggest a potential role for O/E LHR in prenatal risk stratification for DAC, pending external validation.

## Cardiac Magnetic Resonance Before Minimally Invasive Repair of Pectus Excavatum and After Bar Removal.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 11:48
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Buenos Aires · Clinica Mi Pectus
- 通讯作者: Toselli L (dra.luzia.toselli@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163589
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163589) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42988107/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨微创漏斗胸修复术（MIRPE）取出支撑条后，心脏磁共振（CMR）所示心脏受压及相关结构和运动异常是否改善。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入60例孤立性漏斗胸患者，术前平均Haller指数5.9±3.0，全部存在心脏受压，40%报告运动不耐受；取条后无运动不耐受，心脏受压比例由100%降至33%（平均差-66.7%，95%CI -78.6至-54.7），心包积液由25%降至5%，异常室间隔运动由60.0%降至33.3%，吸气性室间隔变平由22%降至10%。CMR还显示三尖瓣/二尖瓣环比值增加（0.86±0.16 vs 0.92±0.06），左室偏心指数降低，尤其吸气时（1.61±0.2 vs 1.45±0.1）。

**原文摘要**：Although minimally invasive repair of pectus excavatum (MIRPE) is associated with favorable outcomes, it remains generally unknown whether cardiac compression persists after bar removal. We therefore explored cardiac magnetic resonance (CMR) outcomes after bar removal. We evaluated a retrospective cohort of patients with diagnosis of isolated pectus excavatum (PEX) who underwent CMR before MIRPE and after bar removal. We evaluated the effect of MIRPE on CMR outcomes including the presence of cardiac compression, pericardial effusion, abnormal septal motion, inspiratory septal flattening, and left ventricular eccentricity. All patients had baseline cardiac compression and 24 (40%) patients referred exercise intolerance. After bar removal, none of the patients reported exercise intolerance and we identified a significant decrease in the presence of cardiac compression [from 100% to 33%, mean difference -66.7 (95%CI -78.6; -54.7)]. With regard to RV inflow and septal wall kinetics, we identified a significant increase in the tricuspid to mitral annulus ratio [0.86±0.16 vs. 0.92±0.06, mean difference 0.06 (95%CI 0.02; 0.10)], and significantly reduced rates of abnormal septal motion [60.0%, vs 33.3%, mean difference -26.7 (95%CI -43.2; -10.1)], and a change in the rates of inspiratory septal flattening from 22% to 10% (mean difference -11.9 (95%CI -22.5; -1.3). Furthermore, we found a significant reduction in left ventricular eccentricity indexes after bar removal, particularly during inspiration [before MIRPE 1.61±0.2 vs. after removal 1.45±0.1, mean difference -0.16 (95%CI -0.23; -0.09)]. Finally, we also found a significant decline in the rates of pericardial effusion [25% vs. 5%, mean difference -20.0 (95%CI -30.1; -9.9)]. CMR assessment before MIRPE and after bar removal demonstrated persistent anatomical decompression, with a significant decrease in the presence and severity of cardiac compression, enhanced right ventricular inflow geometry, and favorable changes of septal wall kinetics. These structural modifications reflect mechanical relief of the right ventricle following bar removal. Retrospective before and after study LEVEL OF EVIDENCE: III.

## Negative-pressure wound therapy for postoperative wound dehiscence after pediatric spinal dysraphism surgery: neonatal portable SNaP® case report and PRISMA-guided review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:48
- 期刊: Neurochirurgie · 中科院医学4区(临床神经病学4区/外科4区) · IF 1.5 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 67200 Strasbourg · Hôpitaux Universitaires de Strasbourg (HUS)
- 通讯作者: Padonou X (xerxes.padonou@chru-strasbourg.fr)

- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuchi.2026.101897) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42988330/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告便携式负压伤口治疗（SNaP®）用于新生儿脊髓闭合不全术后伤口裂开的病例，并通过PRISMA导向综述评估相关儿科证据。
- 核心发现/数据：足月女婴因复杂开放性腰骶部脊髓闭合不全于生后第2天手术，术后第12天出现5.5×4.0 cm伤口裂开且无硬膜暴露；术后第21天启用SNaP®（-125 mmHg持续，每3-4天换药）治疗22天，术后第43天完全上皮化，无脑脊液漏、感染或再手术，1年随访神经功能及瘢痕满意，MRI无假性脊膜膨出或复发。PRISMA综述仅纳入4篇文献共6例儿童脊髓闭合不全相关脊柱手术后NPWT病例，均使用传统VAC®系统，尚无SNaP®在此类情境的报道。

**原文摘要**：Wound complications after neonatal spinal dysraphism surgery carry a high risk of infection and, when dural opening is required, potential cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) leakage. While negative-pressure wound therapy (NPWT) is well established in adults, evidence in neonatal neurosurgery-especially with portable systems-remains scarce. A term female neonate underwent surgery on day 2 for complex open lumbosacral spinal dysraphism with an exophytic mass first identified at birth. Imaging and surgery showed split cord malformation, low tethered cord, and mature teratoma with intradural extension. En bloc resection, filum terminale transection, and watertight dural closure were performed. On postoperative day (POD) 12, wound dehiscence developed, resulting in a 5.5 × 4.0 cm skin defect without dural exposure. After directed wound care, portable NPWT (SNaP®) was initiated on POD21 (-125 mmHg, continuous) for 22 days, with dressing changes every 3-4 days. Complete epithelialization occurred by POD43 without CSF leak, infection, or reoperation. At one year, neurological and scar outcomes were satisfactory, with no pseudomeningocele or recurrence on MRI. In a PRISMA-guided search, we identified 4 publications reporting 6 pediatric cases of NPWT after dysraphism-related spinal surgery with dural opening and/or exposed dura. Published cases exclusively used conventional VAC® systems, and data in intradural settings remain limited; no prior report described SNaP® use in this context. After secure watertight dural closure, appropriate interface protection, and optimized nutritional support, portable NPWT (SNaP®) may represent a feasible rescue option for neonatal lumbosacral wound dehiscence following intradural surgery.

## Successful ablation of lower loop re-entry after Starnes procedure in an infant with severe tricuspid valve dysplasia: a case report.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 18:48
- 期刊: Eur Heart J Case Rep · 中科院医学4区(心脏和心血管系统4区) · IF 0.7
- 国家/单位: 日本 · Osaka Women's and Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Tsumura S
- DOI: 10.1093/ehjcr/ytag777
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1093/ehjcr/ytag777) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42991632/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：总结一例严重三尖瓣发育不良合并肺动脉闭锁婴儿在Starnes术后发生药物难治性非典型心房扑动的导管消融及后续外科消融经验。
- 核心发现/数据：2个月女婴Starnes术后出现药物难治性非典型房扑，三维电解剖标测证实为累及下腔静脉-三尖瓣峡部（CTI）的低位环折返；经CTI射频消融成功终止心动过速，但1周后复发。后续体肺分流术中以原导管消融灶为解剖标志行外科消融，剥离三尖瓣补片后将CTI消融线延伸至三尖瓣环，最终完全控制心律失常。作者认为胎儿期严重三尖瓣反流所致容量负荷可能促进早期心房重构和致心律失常基质形成。
- 临床启示：小婴儿Starnes术后CTI导管消融可行，但补片相关解剖屏障可能导致消融损伤不完整、房扑复发；既往导管消融部位可作为术中标志，必要时补充外科消融以实现完全控制。

**原文摘要**：Tricuspid valve dysplasia (TVD) is a rare right-sided congenital heart disease characterized by severe tricuspid regurgitation and right atrial enlargement, which may predispose to atrial arrhythmias. In severe neonatal cases, the Starnes procedure can be life-saving, but postoperative anatomical alterations may complicate subsequent arrhythmia management. A 2-month-old female infant with TVD and pulmonary atresia developed drug-refractory atypical atrial flutter after the Starnes procedure. Three-dimensional electroanatomical mapping identified lower loop re-entry involving the cavotricuspid isthmus (CTI). Radiofrequency catheter ablation across the CTI successfully terminated the tachycardia. However, atypical flutter recurred 1 week later. During subsequent systemic pulmonary shunt surgery, additional surgical ablation was performed using the prior catheter ablation site as an anatomical landmark. After detaching the tricuspid valve patch, the CTI line was extended to the tricuspid annulus, resulting in complete arrhythmia control. The outcome of this case suggests that foetal-onset volume overload from severe tricuspid regurgitation may promote early atrial remodelling and arrhythmogenic substrate formation in TVD. Although CTI ablation after the Starnes procedure may be feasible in a small infant, patch-related anatomic barriers may prevent complete lesion formation. Prior catheter ablation sites may be useful intraoperative landmarks for successful surgical ablation.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估单绒毛膜双胎妊娠中单胎死亡后初始存活儿脑损伤的患病率、类型、严重程度及相关因素。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入395例单绒毛膜双胎妊娠，单胎死亡后初始存活儿严重脑损伤发生率为18%（72/395），最常见类型为多囊性脑软化（32%，23/72），82%（59/72）的严重脑损伤推测发生于产前。多因素分析显示，单胎死亡时孕周较晚（aOR 1.09/周，95%CI 1.03-1.14；p=0.001）和胎儿贫血（aOR 2.29，95%CI 1.24-4.25；p=0.009）与严重脑损伤相关；活产儿中较早出生孕周亦与严重脑损伤相关（p=0.009）。
- 临床启示：单胎死亡后应常规进行系列产前和产后神经影像学评估，以识别严重脑损伤；对于临床稳定的妊娠，不应仅因单胎死亡而提前分娩，影像时机、宫内输血及分娩时机仍需前瞻性研究确定。

**原文摘要**：Single fetal demise (sFD) in monochorionic (MC) twins may lead to acute exsanguination through placental anastomoses and result in brain injury in initially surviving cotwins. We aimed to evaluate the prevalence, type, severity, and factors associated with brain injury in initially surviving cotwins after sFD. In this international multicentre retrospective cohort study, all MC twin pregnancies complicated by sFD between 1 January 2000 and 1 January 2025 at 13 fetal therapy centres in Argentina, Belgium, Canada, Germany, Israel, Italy, the Netherlands, Spain, Sweden, and the United Kingdom were included. Pregnancies with sFD following laser surgery, selective feticide or twin anaemia-polycythaemia sequence were excluded. The primary outcome was severe brain injury in the initially surviving cotwin, assessed using all available fetal and/or neonatal neuroimaging. Severe brain injury was defined as cystic periventricular leukomalacia, multicystic encephalomalacia, migration disorders, severe ventriculomegaly or volume loss, acute hypoxic-ischaemic injury, arterial ischaemic infarction, high-grade intraventricular haemorrhage, non-punctate intraparenchymal or cerebellar haemorrhage. A total of 395 MC pregnancies with sFD and available neuroimaging were included. Median gestational age at diagnosis of sFD was 22.7 (interquartile range [IQR] 19.3-27.1) weeks. Severe brain injury was detected in 18% (72/395) of initially surviving cotwins. The most common type was multicystic encephalomalacia in 32% (23/72). The presumed timing of severe brain injury was antenatal in 82% (59/72), perinatal in 5% (4/72), postnatal in 10% (7/72) and indeterminate in 3% (2/72) of cases. In multivariable analysis, later gestational age at sFD (adjusted odds ratio [aOR] 1.09 per week, 95% confidence interval [CI] 1.03-1.14; p = 0.001) and fetal anaemia (aOR 2.29, 95% CI 1.24-4.25; p = 0.009) were associated with severe brain injury. In liveborn infants, earlier gestational age at birth was associated with severe brain injury (p = 0.009). Severe brain injury was diagnosed in one fifth of initially surviving cotwins in MC twin pregnancies after sFD and occurred predominantly antenatally, with imaging patterns consistent with hypoxic-ischaemic injury following acute exsanguination. Our findings support routine serial antenatal and postnatal neuroimaging after sFD. Earlier birth was associated with brain injury, but causality cannot be inferred because of confounding by indication. sFD alone should not prompt early delivery in clinically stable pregnancies. Prospective studies are needed to determine the optimal timing and modality of neuroimaging after sFD and evaluate management strategies including the use of intrauterine transfusion and optimal timing of delivery. ## Preoperative intestinal transit scintigraphy for targeted surgery in pediatric intestinal pseudo-obstruction.

- 期刊: J Pediatr Gastroenterol Nutr · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/胃肠肝病学4区/营养学4区) · IF 3.1 · JCR Q1

- 通讯作者: Fusaro F
- DOI: 10.1002/jpn3.70643
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/jpn3.70643) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42989767/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告首次使用肠道传输闪烁显像联合SPECT/CT（ITS-SPECT/CT）指导儿童假性肠梗阻（PIPO）肠造口定位的病例系列，以评估其用于靶向手术规划的价值。
- 核心发现/数据：5例确诊PIPO患儿接受^67Ga-枸橼酸盐ITS-SPECT/CT，3D断层成像消除组织重叠并精准定位功能性受损及示踪剂稳定滞留区域，直接指导手术规划。所有患儿均接受靶向干预且无重大并发症；随访12–24个月，造口输出和肠内喂养耐受性均改善，肠外营养停用或显著减少。
- 临床启示：ITS-SPECT/CT是安全、客观的术前工具，可支持PIPO个体化、功能导向的手术规划，帮助优化肠造口部位选择。

**原文摘要**：Pediatric intestinal pseudo-obstruction (PIPO) is a severe gastrointestinal motility disorder for which surgical enterostomy is often required to achieve effective bowel decompression and optimize nutrition, although the optimal site of diversion remains uncertain. We report the first pediatric case series utilizing hybrid Intestinal Transit Scintigraphy with Single Photon Emission Computed Tomography/Computed Tomography (ITS-SPECT/CT) to guide enterostomy placement. Five children with confirmed PIPO underwent ITS-SPECT/CT with ^67Ga-citrate to map functionally impaired segments. Unlike conventional planar scintigraphy, this 3D tomographic approach eliminated tissue overlap, precisely localizing stable regions of tracer retention that directly informed surgical planning. All patients underwent targeted interventions without major complications. At 12-24 months of follow-up, all patients showed improved stoma output and enteral feeding tolerance, with discontinuation or substantial reduction of parenteral nutrition. These findings suggest that ITS-SPECT/CT is a safe and objective preoperative tool for individualized surgical planning in PIPO, enabling precise, functionally guided interventions.

## Effects of Concomitant Ovariohysterectomy During Elective Caesarean Section in Bitches: A Prospective Clinical Study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 18:48
- 期刊: Reprod Domest Anim
- 国家/单位: 巴西 · School of Veterinary Medicine and Animal Science
- 通讯作者: da Veiga GAL
- DOI: 10.1111/rda.70334
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1111/rda.70334) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42989734/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估母犬择期剖宫产同期行卵巢子宫切除术对术中情况、术后恢复及产褥期结局的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：25只择期剖宫产母犬非随机分为剖宫产组（CG，n=15）与剖宫产+卵巢子宫切除组（COHG，n=10）；两组麻醉时间、手术时间、血液污染手术废弃物总重量、术后恢复、泌乳、幼犬活力及伤口愈合均无统计学显著差异。产后30天心率组间有显著差异，但两组产褥期生命体征均在生理范围内；母性排斥发生率CG为13.3%（2/15）、COHG为55.6%（5/9），差异未达统计学显著（p=0.061），研究期间无重大母体或幼犬并发症。样本量小（尤其COHG组）限制排除临床相关差异的能力。
- 临床启示：择期剖宫产同期OVH

**原文摘要**：The association of ovariohysterectomy with caesarean section in bitches remains controversial due to concerns regarding surgical time, haemorrhage, maternal recovery, lactation, and neonatal care. This prospective study evaluated intraoperative findings, postoperative recovery, and puerperal outcomes in bitches undergoing elective caesarean section with or without concomitant ovariohysterectomy. Twenty-five pregnant bitches undergoing elective caesarean section were prospectively enrolled and classified according to the surgical procedure performed into a Caesarean Group (CG, n = 15) and a Caesarean plus Ovariohysterectomy Group (COHG, n = 10). Group assignment was not randomized and was determined by owner consent and clinical and reproductive management considerations. Clinical, surgical, and postpartum variables were assessed from the preoperative period until 30 days postpartum. No statistically significant differences were detected between groups regarding anaesthetic time, surgical time, total weight of blood-contaminated surgical waste, postoperative recovery, lactation, neonatal viability, or wound healing. Vital parameters remained within physiological limits throughout the puerperal period in both groups, although heart rate differed significantly between groups at 30 days postpartum. One bitch in the COHG group was not available for behavioural and lactation assessments. Maternal rejection was observed in 13.3% (2/15) of bitches in the CG and 55.6% (5/9) in the COHG group, although the difference did not reach statistical significance (p = 0.061). No major maternal or neonatal complications were observed during the study period. However, the limited sample size, particularly in the COHG group, warrants cautious interpretation of these findings and limits the ability to exclude clinically relevant differences between groups. The possible influence of concomitant ovariohysterectomy on maternal behaviour warrants further investigation.
