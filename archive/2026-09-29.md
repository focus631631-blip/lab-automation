## Nine-year ocular developmental outcomes after ranibizumab or laser treatment for zone II retinopathy of prematurity: follow-up of a randomized trial cohort.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:16
- 期刊: Eur J Pediatr · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区) · IF 2.9 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Shenzhen Eye Hospital
- 通讯作者: Ying G (gsying@pennmedicine.upenn.edu)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00431-026-07452-x
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-026-07452-x) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42803828/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：描述曾随机接受玻璃体腔注射雷珠单抗（IVR）或激光光凝（LP）治疗II区需治疗性早产儿视网膜病变（ROP）的儿童在9年后的眼部发育结局。
- 核心发现/数据：本随访纳入29名儿童（LP 10名，IVR 19名），两组等效球镜无显著差异（-1.22 vs -1.90 D，p=0.65），最佳矫正视力也无显著差异（0.15 vs 0.21 logMAR，p=0.51）。与LP组相比，IVR组晶状体更薄（3.52 vs 3.75 mm，p<0.001）、最小角膜曲率更陡（43.8 vs 42.6 D，p=0.048）、中央内界膜-视网膜色素上皮厚度更薄（249.2 vs 286.9 μm，p=0.005），且部分全视野视网膜电图异常存在组间差异。
- 临床启示：9年随访中IVR与LP的视力和屈光结局总体相似，但部分结构和电生理指标存在差异。由于样本量小且失访不均衡，这些探索性发现需谨慎解读，尚不足以仅凭长期眼部结局推荐一种治疗优于另一种。

**原文摘要**：The purpose of this study is to describe 9-year ocular developmental outcomes in children previously randomized to intravitreal ranibizumab (IVR) or laser photocoagulation (LP) for zone II treatment-requiring retinopathy of prematurity (ROP). In this follow-up of a single-center randomized trial, infants with stage 2 or 3 ROP with plus disease in zone II were randomized in 2014 to bilateral LP (n = 25) or IVR (n = 25). At school age, 29 children (10 LP, 19 IVR) underwent assessment of best-corrected visual acuity, cycloplegic refraction, ocular biometry, anterior segment parameters, spectral-domain optical coherence tomography, optical coherence tomography angiography, and full-field electroretinography. Eye-level comparisons used generalized estimating equations. Mean spherical equivalent did not differ significantly between LP and IVR groups (- 1.22 vs. - 1.90 D; mean difference 0.68 D, 95% CI - 2.26 to 3.62; p = 0.65), nor did best-corrected visual acuity (0.15 vs. 0.21 logMAR; mean difference - 0.07, 95% CI - 0.27 to 0.13; p = 0.51). Compared with the LP group, the IVR group showed thinner lens thickness (3.52 vs. 3.75 mm, p < 0.001), steeper minimum keratometry (43.8 vs. 42.6 D, p = 0.048), and thinner central internal limiting membrane-retinal pigment epithelium thickness (249.2 vs. 286.9 μm, p = 0.005). Selected electroretinography abnormalities also differed between groups.  In this small 9-year follow-up cohort with differential loss to follow-up, visual acuity and refractive outcomes were broadly similar after IVR and LP, while selected ocular structural and electrophysiological differences were observed. These preliminary exploratory findings should be interpreted cautiously and do not provide sufficient evidence to recommend one treatment approach over the other on the basis of long-term ocular outcomes alone. • Laser photocoagulation and intravitreal anti-vascular endothelial growth factor therapy are established treatments for severe retinopathy of prematurity. • Long-term ocular developmental and functional outcomes after anti-vascular endothelial growth factor treatment remain incompletely defined, particularly beyond early childhood. • This follow-up of a randomized trial cohort provides 9-year multimodal ocular outcome data in children treated with ranibizumab or laser for zone II retinopathy of prematurity. • Visual acuity and refractive outcomes were broadly similar between treatment groups, while selected anterior segment, retinal structural, and electroretinography differences were observed and should be interpreted as exploratory.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过患者水平系统评价已发表的确诊畸胎样肾母细胞瘤（TWT）病例，阐明其临床特征、治疗反应及预后。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入44篇文献、57例确诊TWT患者，中位诊断年龄3.0岁，87.7%为儿童，82.5%的肿瘤位于肾脏；组织学常见鳞状/角化上皮、脂肪组织及骨骼/横纹肌母细胞样肌肉等异源成分。在21例可评估新辅助化疗反应者中，81.0%表现为反应轻微、差或无反应或疾病进展；末次随访时，83.3%（35/42）可评估疾病状态者无病证据，91.1%（41/45）可评估生命状态者存活。
- 临床启示：TWT对术前化疗常不敏感但并非普遍，完整手术切除仍是治疗核心；广泛畸胎样分化的生物学意义尚需进一步研究。

**原文摘要**：Teratoid Wilms tumor (TWT) is a rare morphological variant of Wilms tumor characterized by extensive heterologous differentiation. Its clinical significance remains uncertain because the available evidence consists predominantly of isolated case reports and small case series. We present a patient-level systematic review of published definite TWT cases. A systematic review of original reports was performed using PubMed/MEDLINE, Google Scholar, citation tracking, and manual reference screening. Patient-level data were extracted exclusively from original publications and harmonized using predefined coding rules. Forty-four publications contributed 57 unique patients with definite TWT. Median age at diagnosis was 3.0 years, and 50 patients (87.7%) were pediatric; 47 tumors (82.5%) were renal. Histopathological findings demonstrated marked heterogeneity, with squamous/keratinizing epithelium, adipose tissue, and skeletal/rhabdomyoblastic muscle among the most frequently documented heterologous components. Among 21 patients with evaluable response to neoadjuvant chemotherapy, 17 (81.0%) showed minimal response, poor or no response, or progressive disease. At last follow-up, 35 of 42 patients (83.3%) with evaluable disease status had no evidence of disease, and 41 of 45 patients (91.1%) with evaluable vital status were alive. TWT is a morphologically heterogeneous tumor in which limited response to neoadjuvant chemotherapy is common but not universal. Complete surgical resection remains central to management, although the biological significance of extensive teratoid differentiation requires further investigation.

## Critical Shortages in Pediatric Surgical Care Across West Africa: A Workforce Density Projection.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估13个西非外科医师学院成员国的小儿外科医生队伍密度，并预测达到推荐目标所需的小儿外科医生数量。
- 核心发现/数据：区域小儿外科医生密度仅为0.16/10万儿童，212百万儿童仅有341名小儿外科医生，无国家达到1:10万基准，多数低于0.2/10万。塞内加尔密度最高（0.63/10万），冈比亚和利比里亚为零；法语国家平均0.25/10万高于英语国家0.05/10万，尼日利亚绝对缺口最大，需新增970名，区域总缺口为1,776名。若5年内弥合缺口，需每年培训并留任406名新小儿外科医生。
- 临床启示：该短缺严重限制西非儿童外科可及性，需通过区域协作大幅扩大 fellowship 培训并实施有效留任策略，以建立可持续的小儿外科服务能力。

**原文摘要**：West Africa faces severe pediatric surgeon (PS) shortages amidst a rapidly growing youth population and high surgical disease burden. The recommended benchmark is one PS per 100,000 children. However, persistent workforce gaps, driven by migration, limited training, and infrastructure constraints, hinder care. To guide regional capacity building, this study assessed the pediatric surgeon workforce density (PSWD) across 13 West African College of Surgeons (WACS) member nations and modeled projected workforce requirements. Using 2023 UNICEF population data and 2024 workforce records, we conducted a cross-sectional analysis of PSWD. Population-based modeling was utilized to project the number of PS needed to achieve target densities over 5, 10, and 20 years, comparing Anglophone and Francophone countries. The overall regional PSWD was 0.16 per 100,000 children, representing only 341 surgeons for 212 million children. Senegal recorded the highest density (0.63 per 100,000), while Gambia and Liberia had none. Francophone countries averaged a higher density (0.25 per 100,000) than Anglophone nations (0.05 per 100,000). Nigeria faces the largest absolute deficit, requiring 970 additional surgeons. Regionally, there is a total shortfall of 1,776 pediatric surgeons. Closing this gap within five years would require training and retaining 406 new surgeons annually. West Africa suffers from critical PSWD deficits, leaving millions without adequate surgical care. Coordinated regional efforts to dramatically expand fellowship training and implement robust retention strategies are urgently required to build sustainable pediatric surgical capacity.

**原文摘要**：To describe current practices among neonatologists and cardiologists regarding the diagnosis and management of hemodynamically significant patent ductus arteriosus (hsPDA) in premature infants, with particular focus on the valuation of echocardiographic parameters, use of scoring systems, and availability of treatment modalities. Similarly, subgroup analyses according to therapy type, including tocolytics alone, antibiotics alone, and combination therapy, similarly showed no significant effect. In the UIC subgroup, no association between adjunctive therapy and PTB <37 weeks was observed (OR, 1.14; 95% CI, 0.71-1.85). In the PEIC subgroup, adjunctive therapy was associated with a nonsignificant trend toward reduced PTB <37 weeks (OR, 0.72; 95% CI, 0.43-1.21), PTB <28 weeks (OR, 0.70; 95% CI, 0.37-1.34), and PTB <24 weeks (OR, 0.44; 95% CI, 0.18-1.08), as well a longer (by >1 week) latency. Adjunctive pericerclage therapy with tocolysis and/or antibiotics was not associated with reduced PTB or improved maternal and neonatal outcomes. Indication-specific analyses did not demonstrate a significant benefit in either UIC or PEIC. Point estimates for several outcomes favored adjunctive therapy in the PEIC subgroup; however, these findings are exploratory and should be interpreted cautiously. Further adequately powered, indication-specific studies are needed.
