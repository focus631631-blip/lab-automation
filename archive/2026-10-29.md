## Improving Hand Hygiene to Reduce Healthcare Associated Infections in Neonatal Wards: WHO Multimodal Strategy, Rwanda.

- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/ANC.0000000000001439) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42950071/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估采用WHO多模式手卫生改善策略（MMHHIS）能否提高卢旺达新生儿病房医护人员手卫生依从性并降低医疗保健相关感染（HAIs）发生率。
- 核心发现/数据：18周前后质量改进项目后，医护人员手卫生知识由64.07%升至86.40%，13项基础设施要素均达到并维持100%合规，手卫生依从性由31.9%升至88.0%。新生儿/新生儿重症监护病房HAI发生率由10.1%降至3.9%（相对下降61.4%），新生儿外科病房由42.6%降至24.4%（下降42.7%）。
- 临床启示：WHO MMHHIS在新生儿病房可行、有效，可改善手卫生依从性并显著降低HAIs，适合在CHUK及类似环境中推广。后续需进一步研究其长期可持续性和成本效果。

**原文摘要**：Healthcare-associated infections (HAIs) are major causes of preventable neonatal morbidity and mortality. Suboptimal hand hygiene (HH) compliance among healthcare workers (HCWs) remains a key, modifiable driver of infection transmission. At the University Teaching Hospital of Kigali (CHUK), neonatal HAI incidence increased from 16% in 2023-2024 to 24% in July 2024-January 2025, whereas HH compliance was 39.7% without neonatal ward specific improvement program. Improve HH compliance among HCWs by ≥50% and reduce neonatal HAI incidence by ≥10% through implementation of the WHO Multimodal HH Improvement Strategy (MMHHIS). An 18-week pre-post quality-improvement project guided by the Plan-Do-Study-Act model (October 2025-January 2026) in neonatal wards (neonatology/neonatal intensive care unit [Neo/NICU], Neonatal Surgery). Intervention included system change, staff training (N = 60), contextual workplace reminders, weekly audits with structured feedback (3494 opportunities), and leadership engagement. Process measures included knowledge scores and infrastructure compliance, outcomes included HH compliance and HAI rates. HCWs' HH knowledge improved from 64.07% to 86.40%. All 13 infrastructure elements were achieved and sustained with 100% compliance. HH compliance increased from 31.9% to 88.0%. Neonatal HAI rates decreased from 10.1% to 3.9% (61.4% relative reduction) in Neo/NICU and from 42.6% to 24.4% (42.7% reduction) in Neonatal Surgery. WHO MMHHIS is feasible, effective in improving compliance and reducing HAIs and scalable approach within CHUK and similar settings. Additional research on long-term sustainability and cost-effectiveness across settings.

## MRI BRAIN lesions and growth after antenatal steroids in preterm infants: Does timing matter?

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:28
- 期刊: Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand · 中科院医学2区(妇产科学2区) · IF 3.5 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 意大利
- 通讯作者: Ramenghi LA
- DOI: 10.1111/aogs.70342
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1111/aogs.70342) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42949830/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估完整产前糖皮质激素疗程（ccACS）尤其是给药时机对极低出生体重儿MRI脑损伤、脑生长成熟及神经发育的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入189例极低出生体重儿，139例（73.5%）接受ccACS；ccACS组白质病变发生率更低（p=0.02），中/重度脑室内出血发生率更低（p=0.04）。在<28周超早产儿中，出生前7天内给予ccACS者脑室内出血显著更少（p=0.006）；脑生长、成熟及2岁神经发育结局无显著差异。
- 临床启示：完整ACS与极低出生体重儿脑损伤率降低相关，超早产儿出生前7天内给药可能获益更明显，且未见对脑生长、成熟或2岁神经发育的不良影响。

**原文摘要**：Antenatal corticosteroids (ACS) are widely used to improve outcomes in preterm infants, with known benefits on respiratory morbidity and intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH). However, the effect of ACS timing on brain injury and development remains unclear. We aimed to evaluate the impact of a complete course of ACS (ccACS), with particular attention to timing, on brain lesions, growth, and neurodevelopment in very low birth weight (VLBW) infants using magnetic resonance imaging (MRI). In this historical cohort study, all VLBW infants admitted to a tertiary neonatal intensive care unit between January 2019 and December 2021 were included. Clinical data, including timing of ACS administration, were collected. The primary outcome was the occurrence of brain injury on MRI at term-equivalent age, including IVH, white matter lesions (WML), and cerebellar hemorrhage (CBH). Secondary outcomes were brain growth and maturation on MRI and neurodevelopment at 2 years assessed using the Griffiths Mental Development Scales. Outcomes were compared between infants with and without ccACS and according to different administration-to-birth intervals. A total of 189 infants were included, of whom 139 (73.5%) received ccACS. Infants exposed to ccACS had a lower incidence of WML (p = 0.02) and moderate/severe IVH (p = 0.04). In extremely preterm infants (<28 weeks), IVH was significantly less frequent when ccACS was administered within 7 days before birth (p = 0.006). No significant differences were observed in brain growth, maturation, or neurodevelopmental outcomes at 2 years. A complete course of ACS was associated with unadjusted lower rates of brain injury in VLBW infants, particularly when administered within 7 days before birth in extremely preterm infants, without adverse effects on brain growth, maturation, or neurodevelopment at 2 years.

## Predictors and Risk Factors of Necrotizing Enterocolitis: An Integrative Review.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：整合极低出生体重儿（<1500 g）坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）的预测因素和危险因素证据，为机器学习预测模型、护理评估及早期临床决策提供依据。
- 核心发现/数据：从405条记录中筛选325篇摘要，最终纳入14项研究，共识别55个预测因素或危险因素；其中胎龄、出生体重和人乳暴露与NEC风险 consistently 相关，仅16个因素在多项研究中重复出现。多数研究为回顾性队列设计，仅1项纳入机器学习方法，其他母体、产前、分娩、营养、社会和临床变量差异较大。
- 临床启示：护士可通过促进人乳喂养、风险监测和床旁倡导在NEC预防中发挥核心作用；未来预测模型应整合动态情境变量，并采用标准化定义和多场景验证。

**原文摘要**：Necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) is associated with high morbidity and mortality among preterm infants. Early intervention is critical, but existing predictors and risk factors remain inconsistent. Integrating bedside nursing assessments with machine learning (ML) may enable prediction of NEC before deterioration. This integrative review synthesizes evidence on predictors and risk factors of NEC in very low birth weight (VLBW; <1500 g) infants to guide ML-based prediction models, support nursing assessment, and support early clinical decision making. MEDLINE, CINAHL, and Embase. We included quantitative studies published from 2015 to 2025, involving VLBW infants <32 weeks gestation and examining NEC predictors or risk factors. From 405 records, 325 abstracts were screened, and 14 studies met inclusion criteria. Two reviewers independently assessed full texts, resolving differences through consensus. Data were organized thematically using an inductive approach with a predetermined extraction table. Study quality was evaluated with a Joanna Briggs Institute tool. Across 14 studies, 55 predictors or risk factors were identified. Gestational age, birth weight, and human milk exposure were consistently associated with NEC risk. Other maternal, antenatal, delivery, nutritional, social, and clinical variables varied widely, with only 16 factors appearing in multiple studies. Most studies used retrospective cohort designs, and one incorporated ML methods. Nurses are central to NEC prevention through human milk promotion, risk surveillance, and bedside advocacy. Future NEC prediction should integrate dynamic, contextual variables with standardized definitions and validation across diverse settings.

## Comparison of Surgical Approaches for Hirschsprung's Disease: A Network Meta-Analysis.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 10:43
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国台湾 · Taipei Medical University Hospital
- 通讯作者: Huang FH (191009@h.tmu.edu.tw)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163568
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163568) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42951178/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过网状Meta分析比较Soave、Swenson、Duhamel等术式经开放、腹腔镜、经肛或机器人途径治疗先天性巨结肠的围手术期效率与远期排便功能。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入28项研究、1911例患者，比较9种技术。经肛Swenson手术时间最短，其次为经肛Soave和腹腔镜Swenson；腹腔镜Swenson住院时间排名最佳，机器人手术较多数开放术式住院更短且术中出血最少（统计学优于其他方式）。术后HAEC、便秘及大便失禁在各术式间无显著差异，排便功能结局相当。
- 临床启示：术式选择主要影响围手术期效率而非远期排便功能；微创/经肛途径在手术时间和住院方面有优势，机器人手术可减少出血，可结合围手术期管理进行个体化术式规划。

**原文摘要**：Hirschsprung's disease can be treated using multiple procedures, including Soave, Swenson, and Duhamel techniques, performed via open, laparoscopic, transanal, or robotic approaches. However, the optimal surgical approaches remain controversial, particularly regarding perioperative efficiency and long-term bowel function. We performed a network meta-analysis to compare these approaches. A comprehensive literature search was conducted according to PRISMA guidelines across electronic databases, including PubMed, Embase, and the Cochrane Library, from inception until the last update on October 31, 2025. Nine techniques (open, laparoscopic, transanal Soave and Swenson; open, laparoscopic Duhamel; and robotic-assisted procedures) were included. Primary outcomes were operative time, length of stay, and blood loss; secondary outcomes were postoperative Hirschsprung-associated enterocolitis (HAEC), constipation, and fecal incontinence. A random-effects frequentist network meta-analysis was conducted using MetaInsight to obtain the ranking probabilities. Twenty-eight studies, including 1,911 patients, were included. For perioperative outcomes, Transanal Swenson (TSw) had the shortest operative time, followed by Transanal Soave (TS) and Laparoscopic Swenson (LSw), whereas Open/ Laparoscopic Duhamel and robotic-assisted procedures required longer times. LSw ranked best for length of stay, and Robotic-assisted surgery was associated with shorter hospitalization compared with most open techniques. Robotic-assisted surgery showed the lowest intraoperative blood loss and was statistically superior to the other approaches. No significant differences were observed in postoperative HAEC, constipation, or incontinence; bowel function outcomes were comparable across techniques. This is the first network meta-analysis to comprehensively compare all major surgical approaches, suggesting that the choice of surgical approach for Hirschsprung's disease mainly affects perioperative efficiency rather than long-term bowel function. Minimally invasive and transanal procedures offer advantages in operative time and hospitalization, and robotic surgery minimizes blood loss. No significant differences were identified in postoperative HAEC, constipation, or incontinence. These findings may guide surgical planning in consideration of perioperative care.

## Platelet transfusion biology: from hemostatic support to inflammatory and immune signaling.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:43
- 期刊: Haematologica · 中科院医学1区(血液学2区) · IF 8.2 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: Saint-Etienne · Université Jean Monnet
- 通讯作者: Cognasse F (fabrice.cognasse@efs.sante.fr)
- DOI: 10.3324/haematol.2026.301566
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.3324/haematol.2026.301566) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42952460/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：基于供者、血小板制品和受者构成的生物学三联体，综述血小板输注从止血支持到炎症与免疫信号传导的生物学作用及其临床决定因素。
- 核心发现/数据：摘要未提供具体数值数据；该综述指出血小板浓缩物在采集、处理和储存中形成由供者生物学、残余血浆、添加液、去白细胞、病原体灭活、温度和储存时间塑造的产品特异性表型，影响输注后恢复、止血功能及炎症/免疫并发症。输注血小板具有代谢活性免疫细胞特征，可通过模式识别受体、细胞因子/趋化因子、脂质介质、细胞外囊泡、线粒体来源物质和DAMPs，并与白细胞、内皮及补体互作；受者的脓毒症、化疗、恶性肿瘤、新生儿免疫发育、内皮激活和既往同种免疫决定其恢复止血、放大炎症或触发无效输注。
- 临床启示：临床不应只追求单一“最优”血小板制品，而应依据供者、制品特征和受者状态，在合适时机为合适患者选择或工程化合适血小板制品，以兼顾止血、炎症免疫风险及输注有效性。

**原文摘要**：Platelet transfusion remains indispensable for the prevention and treatment of bleeding in hematology, oncology, transplantation, critical care, surgery and trauma. Yet the biological impact of a platelet concentrate extends well beyond numerical correction of thrombocytopenia. Platelets are anucleate but metabolically active immune cells that express pattern-recognition receptors, release cytokines, chemokines, lipid mediators, extracellular vesicles, mitochondria-derived material and damage-associated molecular patterns; and interact with leukocytes, endothelial cells and complement. During collection, processing and storage, platelet concentrates acquire a distinct productspecific phenotype shaped by donor biology, residual plasma, additive solutions, leukoreduction, pathogen reduction, temperature and storage time. These variables affect not only post-transfusion recovery and hemostatic function, but also inflammatory signaling and immune related complications. The recipient is equally important: sepsis, chemotherapy, malignancy, neonatal immune development, endothelial activation and prior alloimmunization can determine whether transfused platelets restore hemostasis, amplify inflammation, or trigger refractoriness. This review explores platelet transfusion through the lens of a biological triad formed by the donor, the platelet product, and the recipient. We focus on how platelet immune receptors, storage-induced inflammatory remodeling, soluble biological response modifiers, extracellular vesicles and complement shape the interactions of transfused platelets with recipient leukocytes and endothelium. Alloimmunization and refractoriness are considered more briefly as recipient-dependent modifiers of platelet recovery and transfusion efficacy. The central challenge is no longer to define a single optimal platelet product, but to engineer and select the right platelet product for the right patient, at the right moment.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：总结益生菌在新生儿护理中从分子机制到临床实践的作用，评估其预防早产儿并发症的潜力及全球安全指南。
- 核心发现/数据：早产儿（<37周）因器官系统和肠道菌群发育不成熟，易发生坏死性小肠结肠炎、败血症和喂养不耐受；乳杆菌、双歧杆菌和酵母菌等益生菌可通过竞争性排除病原体、增强肠上皮屏障和免疫调节恢复肠道稳态。尽管证据有前景，但安全性、菌株特异性、剂量及缺乏标准化给药指南导致研究到实践存在差距，且危重新生儿使用活菌存在固有风险。

**原文摘要**：Premature births remain a significant global health concern, contributing to high mortality rates among children under five. Preterm newborns (<37 weeks' gestation) encounter considerable health issues due to their underdeveloped organ systems and gut microbiota. This review aims to provide a thorough and even-handed approach on the role of probiotics in preventing preterm complications and evaluate the evolving global safety guidelines, emphasizing the urgent need for standardized, strain-specific clinical protocols to ensure their safe and effective integration in neonatal intensive care.
