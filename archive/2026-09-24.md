## Placento-cerebral adhesion: a morphology-based classification derived from a case report and literature review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:11
- 期刊: Childs Nerv Syst
- 国家/单位: Bydgoszcz
- 通讯作者: Kroczek K (kacper.kroczek@cm.umk.pl)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00381-026-07478-4
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00381-026-07478-4) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42776247/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告1例新生儿血管化胎盘-脑粘连，并结合文献

**原文摘要**：Placento-cerebral adhesion is an exceptionally rare congenital cranial anomaly with heterogeneous anatomy and inconsistent terminology. We report a neonate with vascularized placento-cerebral adhesion, review the published morphology, and propose a descriptive anatomical framework. Published human cases were reviewed for the configuration of the placental attachment, tissue continuity, vascular communication, associated anomalies, operative findings, and pathological features. Recurring observable patterns informed the proposed classification. Three recognizable patterns were identified: Type I, a thin avascular fibrous ABS-like adhesion; Type II, direct placento-neural continuity without demonstrable vascular communication; and Type III, direct continuity with organized vessels. The present Type III lesion required neonatal microsurgical separation, vascular control, preservation of viable pia and venous drainage, and staged soft tissue reconstruction. The framework standardizes anatomical description and multidisciplinary communication. It is an expert-informed descriptive model, not a validated grading scale, and it should not be used to predict survival, cranial re-ossification, or long-term neurocognitive outcome. Additional cases and prolonged follow-up are required for validation.

## Association of surgery and risk of long-term comorbidities for congenital lung malformations in pediatrics.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 09:00
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国台湾 · School of Post Baccalaureate Medicine
- 通讯作者: Chou CM (cmchou@vghtc.gov.tw)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163472
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163472) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42777913/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估儿童先天性肺畸形手术及手术时机、入路和切除范围与长期并发症风险之间的关联。
- 核心发现/数据：多中心回顾性队列纳入20,101例6岁前诊断CLM患儿和20,089,085例对照，经倾向评分匹配后，主要哮喘结局未达统计学

**原文摘要**：Congenital lung malformations are increasingly recognized through prenatal and early postnatal imaging. Although many affected children are asymptomatic in infancy, CLMs are associated with long-term respiratory and structural complications. The influence of surgical timing, approach, and extent on these outcomes remains uncertain. Using the TriNetX platform, we conducted a multicenter retrospective cohort study that included 20,101 children with CLM diagnosed before age 6 and 20,089,085 controls. Propensity score matching was applied. Asthma was the primary outcome, while recurrent infections and structural abnormalities were secondary outcomes. Subgroup analyses assessed the impact of surgical age, approach, and resection extent. While the primary asthma analysis did not reach statistical significance (RR 0.79, 95% CI 0.54-1.14, p=0.191), but statistical significance existed in asthma with strict definition (RR 0.22, 95% CI 0.11-0.42, p<0.001), recurrent pneumonia (RR 0.29, 95% CI 0.16-0.51, p< 0.001) and structural deformities (RR 0.54, 95% CI 0.30-0.97, p=0.039). Thoracoscopic surgery was associated with fewer selected complications than thoracotomy, particularly musculoskeletal outcomes. CLMs confer increased risks of respiratory and structural morbidity. The observational associations suggest that the clinical window of benefit may span the first two years of life.

## The developing auditory system as a window into bilirubin neurotoxicity.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:00
- 期刊: Semin Perinatol · 中科院医学2区(妇产科学2区/儿科2区) · IF 3.2 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: California 94103 · School of Health Sciences
- 通讯作者: Musacchia G (gmusacchia@pacific.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.semperi.2026.152308
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.semperi.2026.152308) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42778479/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨发育中的听觉系统作为观察胆红素神经毒性及其可逆性损伤窗口的价值。
- 核心发现/数据：胆红素神经毒性可导致从重度听力损失到轻中度暴露下细微听觉处理障碍的连续谱损害；常规听阈检查可发现阈值相关异常，而频率跟随反应（FFR）等新兴方法可在常规指标正常的中度高胆红素血症新生儿中检出听觉处理缺陷，并随治疗使胆红素下降而恢复。细胞机制研究提示，在永久性损伤发生前可能存在生理功能障碍的可逆窗口。
- 临床启示：FFR等新兴听觉处理测量有望补充传统听力筛查，帮助更早识别胆红素暴露新生儿的细微听觉损伤并动态监测可逆性，从而指导及时干预和发育随访。

**原文摘要**：Neonatal hyperbilirubinemia is one of the most common conditions in infancy and a cause of potentially preventable neurodevelopmental injury worldwide. Auditory impairment is a well-known consequence of bilirubin neurotoxicity, with effects ranging from hearing loss in severe cases to more subtle processing problems with mild to moderate exposure. Several cellular mechanisms can shed light on how these impairments arise and when physiological dysfunction might be reversible before permanent injury. Current clinical tools provide excellent information about bilirubin-induced changes related to hearing thresholds. Emerging methods in this field, including the frequency-following response (FFR), are promising candidates to include more subtle, suprathreshold measures of auditory processing. Our work has shown that the FFR reflects deficits in neonates with moderate hyperbilirubinemia despite conventional measures within normal limits, and rebounds as bilirubin levels improve with treatment. Future studies are needed to better characterize the pattern of auditory deficits, determine when testing is most informative, and establish the extent to which emerging measures can predict later developmental outcomes.

## Umbilical and Middle Cerebral Artery Pulsatility Indices as Predictors of a Nuchal Cord: A Retrospective Cohort Study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:00
- 期刊: Z Geburtshilfe Neonatol · 中科院医学4区(妇产科学4区/儿科4区) · IF 0.9 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: Uberaba · Mario Palmério University Hospital - University of Uberaba (UNIUBE)
- 通讯作者: Peixoto AB
- DOI: 10.1055/a-2956-6022
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1055/a-2956-6022) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42778160/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估出生时脐带绕颈对分娩前7天内多普勒超声测得的脐动脉、大脑中动脉搏动指数及脑胎盘比率的影响，并分析其与不良新生儿结局、脐带绕颈圈数及脐血气的关联。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入203例单胎妊娠，均在分娩前7天内行多普勒超声。出生时脐带绕颈或紧绕颈均未影响上述多普勒参数，且脐带绕颈与不良围产结局无显著关联。脐带圈数与大脑中动脉搏动指数呈弱但统计学显著正相关，与脐动脉搏动指数、脑胎盘比率及脐血pH无显著相关。
- 临床启示：产前7天内多普勒参数正常并不能排除脐带绕颈，且脐带绕颈本身未必提示不良围产结局。临床应结合其他监测指标综合评估，不宜仅据此改变管理策略。

**原文摘要**：This retrospective cohort study aimed to evaluate the impact of a nuchal cord at birth on the pulsatility indices of the umbilical artery, the middle cerebral artery, and the cerebroplacental ratio, assessed by Doppler ultrasound within 7 days before delivery. Secondary objectives included assessing the associations between the presence of a nuchal cord and a tight nuchal cord with adverse neonatal outcomes, as well as the correlations between the number of cord loops and Doppler indices and umbilical cord blood gas values. Data from 203 singleton pregnancies with Doppler ultrasound performed within 7 days before delivery were analyzed. Participants were grouped according to the presence or absence of nuchal cord at birth. Doppler parameters (umbilical artery pulsatility index, middle cerebral artery pulsatility index, and cerebroplacental ratio) and clinical variables, including arterial and venous umbilical cord blood pH, Apgar scores, birth weight, and adverse perinatal events, were evaluated. The presence of a nuchal cord or a tight nuchal cord at birth did not affect the Doppler parameters in obstetric ultrasound examinations conducted up to 7 days prior to delivery. The presence of a nuchal cord was not significantly associated with adverse perinatal outcomes. A weak but statistically significant positive correlation was observed between the number of cord loops and the middle cerebral artery pulsatility index, with no significant correlation with the umbilical artery pulsatility index, cerebroplacental ratio, or umbilical cord blood pH values. These findings suggest that the presence of a nuchal cord does not impact the pulsatility index of the umbilical artery, middle cerebral artery, and cerebroplacental ratio, assessed by Doppler ultrasound within 7 days before delivery. Further research is needed to clarify the clinical implications.

## Modified Right Vertical Infra-Axillary Thoracotomy for Ventricular Septal Defect Repair Across Age Groups: A Single-Center Cohort Study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:00
- 期刊: J Thorac Cardiovasc Surg · 中科院医学2区(外科1区/心脏和心血管系统2区/呼吸系统2区) · IF 4.7 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Department of Children's Heart Center
- 通讯作者: Liang W (Lwj3781@126.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jtcvs.2026.09.012
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtcvs.2026.09.012) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42777943/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估改良右侧垂直腋下小切口（MRVIAT）在广泛年龄和体重范围内修补室间隔缺损（VSD）的可行性、围手术期结局及短期随访结果。
- 核心发现/数据：本研究回顾性纳入2022年1月至2024年12月单中心1212例行MRVIAT修补VSD的患者，均经单一2–5 cm切口在中心心肺转流下完成手术，无院内死亡、无中转胸骨正中切开。患者中位年龄0.6岁（18天–45岁），含16例新生儿（1.3%）和36例成人（3.0%）；中位体重7.0 kg（1.8–105 kg），含16例≤3 kg（1.3%）。中位手术时间100.0分钟（IQR 95.0–110.0），体外循环46.0分钟（IQR 40.0–52.0），主动脉阻断27.0分钟（IQR 23.0–33.0），术后住院6.0天（IQR 6.0–8.0）；中位随访1.3年（IQR 0.3–2.5）期间未见胸廓畸形、中度及以上瓣膜反流或手术相关再干预。
- 临床启示：对于经过筛选的、年龄和体重跨度较大的VSD患者，MRVIAT是一种可行且短期效果良好的微创修复方式，可作为胸骨正中切开的潜在替代选择。但其相对优势仍需开展对比研究加以证实。

**原文摘要**：To describe the feasibility, perioperative outcomes, and short-term follow-up of ventricular septal defect (VSD) repair using a modified right vertical infra-axillary thoracotomy (MRVIAT) across a broad age and weight range. This retrospective study included 1,212 patients with ventricular septal defect who underwent surgical repair via a MRVIAT at our institution between January 2022 and December 2024.All procedures were performed through a single 2-5 cm incision under central cardiopulmonary bypass. All procedures were completed successfully, with no in-hospital mortality and no conversion to median sternotomy. The median age was 0.6 years (range, 18 days to 45 years), including 16 neonates aged <28 days (1.3%) and 36 adults (3.0%). The median body weight was 7.0 kg (range, 1.8-105 kg), including 16 patients weighing ≤3 kg (1.3%). The median operative time was 100.0 minutes (IQR, 95.0-110.0), with a median cardiopulmonary bypass time of 46.0 minutes (IQR, 40.0-52.0) and a median aortic cross-clamp time of 27.0 minutes (IQR, 23.0-33.0). The median postoperative hospital stay was 6.0 days (IQR, 6.0-8.0). During a median follow-up of 1.3 years (IQR, 0.3-2.5 years), no thoracic deformity, moderate or severe valvular regurgitation, or surgery-related reintervention was observed. In this single-center retrospective cohort, VSD repair via MRVIAT was feasible in selected patients across a broad age and weight range and was associated with favorable short-term outcomes. Comparative studies are required to determine whether this approach offers advantages over median sternotomy.

## Retinopathy of Prematurity in the Anti-VEGF Era: Screening Systems and Practical Treatment Decision-Making Across Anti-VEGF, Laser, and Surgery.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:00
- 期刊: Prog Retin Eye Res · 中科院医学1区(眼科学1区) · IF 16.2 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Xinhua Hospital Affiliated to Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Xu Y (xuyu01@xinhuamed.com.cn)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.preteyeres.2026.101531
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.preteyeres.2026.101531) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42777939/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：总结抗VEGF时代早产儿视网膜病变（ROP）筛查、分类、治疗决策、手术及长期结局的证据。
- 核心发现/数据：抗VEGF可快速消退I区、后II区及侵袭性ROP，并可能允许周边视网膜继续血管化，但存在持续无血管视网膜、延迟复发、再治疗不确定和全身安全顾虑。抗VEGF最佳支持用于I区、后II区及侵袭性ROP，激光仍适用于前部病变、随访不可靠及残留无血管视网膜，晚期牵引性病变仍需手术。Ranibizumab和aflibercept全身安全性较bevacizumab更令人放心，但长期比较数据有限；faricimab和局部地塞米松仍属研究性。
- 临床启示：抗VEGF时代治疗成功应超越短期消退，追求稳定视网膜血管成熟、预防牵引性并发症和长期发育随访。临床决策应综合ROP分区/类型、随访可靠性和全身风险，选择抗VEGF、激光或手术。

**原文摘要**：Retinopathy of prematurity (ROP) is a vasoproliferative disease of the incompletely vascularized retina and remains a major preventable cause of childhood blindness. Its clinical profile varies by region: in high-income countries, severe ROP mainly affects extremely preterm infants, whereas in many low- and middle-income countries it may also occur in larger, more mature infants because neonatal survival has improved faster than screening capacity. The introduction of intravitreal anti-vascular endothelial growth factor (anti-VEGF) therapy has changed ROP management. Anti-VEGF agents can rapidly regress posterior and aggressive ROP and may allow continued peripheral vascularization, but they also introduce challenges including persistent avascular retina, delayed reactivation, retreatment uncertainty, and systemic safety concerns. This review summarizes evidence across the ROP care pathway, including screening, classification, treatment decision-making, surgery, and long-term outcomes. In the anti-VEGF era, treatment success should extend beyond short-term regression to stable retinal vascular maturation, prevention of tractional complications, and sustained developmental follow-up. Anti-VEGF therapy is best supported for Zone I, posterior Zone II, and aggressive ROP, whereas laser remains important for anterior disease, unreliable follow-up settings, and residual avascular retina. Surgery remains necessary for advanced tractional disease. Ranibizumab and aflibercept appear to have more reassuring systemic profiles than bevacizumab, although long-term comparative data remain limited. Faricimab and topical dexamethasone remain investigational. Future priorities include integrated screening networks, standardized retreatment algorithms, and longitudinal registries tracking ocular and developmental outcomes.

## Integrated plasma proteomic and metabolomic profiling demonstrated specific proteins and bile acids remodeling after liver transplantation of children with biliary atresia.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:00
- 期刊: Biochim Biophys Acta Proteins Proteom · 中科院生物学4区(生化与分子生物学4区/生物物理4区) · IF 2.2 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Second Affiliated Hospital
- 通讯作者: Zhou Y (15306321426@163.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.bbapap.2026.141181
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbapap.2026.141181) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42777789/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过整合血浆蛋白质组和代谢组分析，表征胆道闭锁患儿肝移植后血浆蛋白及胆汁酸的重塑特征。
- 核心发现/数据：对7例Kasai术后失败并接受肝移植的胆道闭锁患儿移植前后配对血浆进行无标记定量蛋白质组和15种胆汁酸靶向代谢组分析，发现113种蛋白显著改变，主要富集于炎症、凝血异常和细胞外基质重构通路。8种特征蛋白包括HGFAC、IGFBP5、IGFALS、F11上调及PICALM、S100A11、PIGR、CTHRC1下调；GCA、TCA、GCDCA、TCDCA四种结合型初级胆汁酸术后急剧下降并与临床肝损伤标志物强相关。HGFAC、PIGR联合上述四种胆汁酸诊断准确性高，AUC为0.86-1.00。
- 临床启示：这些蛋白和胆汁酸标志物有望作为无创生物标志物，用于监测胆道闭锁患儿肝移植后的移植物功能。

**原文摘要**：This study aimed to characterize plasma protein and bile acid remodeling after liver transplantation (LT) in children with biliary atresia (BA). Four conjugated primary bile acids, glycocholic acid (GCA), taurocholic acid (TCA), glycochenodeoxycholic acid (GCDCA), and taurochenodeoxycholic acid (TCDCA), showed precipitous postoperative declines and correlated strongly with clinical liver-injury markers. Two proteins, HGFAC and PIGR, together with these four conjugated bile acids demonstrated high diagnostic accuracy (area under the curve, AUC = 0.86-1.00), supporting their potential as non-invasive biomarkers for monitoring graft function after LT.

## Dietary modulation of intestinal microbes prolongs survival in a Hirschsprung disease mouse model.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:00
- 期刊: Gastroenterology · 中科院医学1区(胃肠肝病学1区) · IF 29.7 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Children's Hospital of Philadelphia
- 通讯作者: Heuckeroth RO (HeuckerothR@chop.edu)
- DOI: 10.1053/j.gastro.2026.09.007
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2026.09.007) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42777967/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探究饮食调节肠道微生物对Hirschsprung病（HSCR）小鼠模型（Piebald lethal, sl/sl）生存期的影响及其机制，以寻找HSCR相关小肠结肠炎的预防策略。
- 核心发现/数据：饮食显著影响sl/sl小鼠生存；保护性饮食促进结肠上皮缺氧、支持远端结肠线粒体代谢、增加近端结肠抗菌肽、改善上皮屏障并减少肠杆菌科，使中位生存期延长为3倍。有害饮食则升高结肠上皮氧、导致远端结肠线粒体异常和氧化磷酸化相关mRNA减少、增加肠杆菌科；粪便丁酸等代谢物可能介导保护作用，钨酸盐抑制肠杆菌科厌氧呼吸也可改善生存。
- 临床启示：除母乳喂养外的饮食干预可能通过调节肠道微生物和上皮代谢预防HSCR相关小肠结肠炎，提供潜在非抗生素策略，但尚需人体研究验证。

**原文摘要**：Enterocolitis is a common, potentially deadly complication of Hirschsprung disease (HSCR), a congenital anomaly defined by absence of enteric neurons from distal bowel. Unexpectedly, we discovered diet dramatically affects lifespan of a HSCR mouse model (Piebald lethal, sl/sl). We analyzed diet-dependent sl/sl survival, diet and stool composition by untargeted metabolomics, gut microbiome by 16S rDNA sequencing and culture on MacConkey agar, colon epithelial anatomy by light and electron microscopy, colon epithelial gene expression by bulk RNA sequencing, colon epithelial oxygen level by pimonidazole staining, intestinal permeability to 4kDa FITC-dextran, and the impact on sl/sl survival of antibiotic or tungstate treatment to broadly or selectively eliminate gut microbes. Detrimental diet-fed sl/sl mice had elevated colon epithelial oxygen, abnormal distal colon epithelial mitochondria, reduced distal colon epithelial mRNA encoding oxidative phosphorylation proteins, and increased Enterobacteriaceae. Protective diet promoted colon epithelial hypoxia, supported distal colon epithelial mitochondrial metabolism, elevated proximal colon anti-microbial peptides, improved epithelial barrier function, reduced Enterobacteriaceae, and tripled median sl/sl lifespan. Protective diet-fed sl/sl mice had higher fecal butyrate and other metabolites that may underlie these effects. Tungstate-mediated inhibition of Enterobacteriaceae anaerobic respiration also improved sl/sl survival. These observations suggest for the first time to our knowledge that dietary interventions (other than breastfeeding) might prevent Hirschsprung disease-associated enterocolitis.
