## Cesarean Myomectomy for Refractory Uterine Atony in an Eclamptic Woman with Multiple Fibroids: A Case Report from the Democratic Republic of Congo.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:45
- 期刊: Int J Womens Health · 中科院医学3区(妇产科学3区) · IF 2.5 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Democratic Republic of Congo · University of Lubumbashi
- 通讯作者: Kinenkinda Kalume X
- DOI: 10.2147/IJWH.S651824
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.2147/IJWH.S651824) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43034789/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告一例子痫合并多发性子宫肌瘤患者因难治性子宫收缩乏力行剖宫产子宫肌瘤剔除术的病例，探讨其作为保留子宫处理选择的可能性。
- 核心发现/数据：患者38岁、G3P0A2，因子痫急诊行剖宫产，娩出2030 g女婴但于出生后30分钟死亡；术中发现9个肌瘤，最大16×10×8 cm、约1050 g，按摩及缩宫素后宫缩乏力仍持续。剔除包括巨大下段肌瘤在内的5个主要肌瘤后子宫张力逐步恢复、止血满意，估计出血900 mL、手术90分钟，术后输血并恢复良好，术后第5天出院。
- 临床启示：在具备手术经验和输血能力的条件下，剖宫产子宫肌瘤剔除术可考虑作为广泛肌瘤相关难治性子宫收缩乏力患者的个体化保留子宫选项。但单例观察不能确立该术式的安全性和有效性。

**原文摘要**：Cesarean myomectomy remains a controversial procedure because of concerns regarding severe hemorrhage and peripartum hysterectomy. Recent reports suggest that it may be performed safely in carefully selected patients; however, the certainty of the available evidence remains very low, and reports describing its use for refractory uterine atony in women with eclampsia are scarce. We report the case of a 38-year-old Congolese woman, gravida 3 para 0 abortus 2, admitted to the University Clinics of Lubumbashi with generalized tonic-clonic seizures, severe hypertension (180/120 mmHg), and marked proteinuria, leading to a diagnosis of eclampsia. No ultrasound examination was performed before surgery because of the maternal emergency. After stabilization with magnesium sulfate and nicardipine, an emergency cesarean section was performed. A live female infant weighing 2030 g was delivered but died 30 minutes after birth in the neonatal intensive care unit. Extensive uterine fibromyomatosis was identified, including nine fibroids, the largest measuring 16 × 10 × 8 cm and weighing approximately 1050 g. After hysterorrhaphy, severe uterine atony persisted despite uterine massage and oxytocin administration. Considering the patient's nulliparity and the wish to preserve the uterus, a conservative cesarean myomectomy was undertaken. Five major fibroids were removed, including the giant lower-segment fibroid. Progressive restoration of uterine tone was observed after myomectomy, with satisfactory hemostasis. Estimated blood loss was 900 mL, and total operative time was 90 minutes. Postoperatively, the patient received blood transfusion and recovered without complications. She was discharged in stable condition on postoperative day five. In this patient, removal of the dominant fibroids was temporally associated with restoration of uterine tone and allowed emergency hysterectomy to be avoided. This single observation does not establish the safety or efficacy of the procedure, but suggests that cesarean myomectomy may be considered as a uterus-preserving option in carefully selected patients with refractory uterine atony related to extensive fibromyomatosis, provided that surgical expertise and blood transfusion capacity are available.

## Machine-Learning Derived Individual Risk Prediction in Congenital Heart Surgery: Development and internal validation of risk prediction models for in-hospital mortality after congenital heart surgery using data from the Pediatric Cardiac Critical Care Consortium (PC4) registry.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:45
- 期刊: Am Heart J · 中科院医学2区(心脏和心血管系统3区) · IF 3.4 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · The Children's Hospital of Philadelphia and Department of Pediatrics Perelman School of Medicine at the University of Pennsylvania
- 通讯作者: O'Byrne ML (obyrnem@chop.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.ahj.2026.107655
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ahj.2026.107655) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43033839/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：开发并内部验证基于机器学习的先天性心脏病手术后院内死亡个体风险预测模型，并评估其相较于传统逻辑回归风险调整模型的预测与风险重分类能力。
- 核心发现/数据：研究纳入52家PC4医院2014-09-01至2019-12-30期间≤18岁先心病手术患者共51,475例次（女性45%、新生儿20%、STAT 5级手术5%）。机器学习模型（随机森林、梯度提升）与传统逻辑回归模型在C统计量和Brier评分上无显著差异，整体模型拟合未改善。以仅含STAT分级模型为参照，绝对风险分类NRI：机器学习0.76（95%CI 0.68–0.83） vs 逻辑回归0.75（0.67–0.83）；风险排序NRI：机器学习0.26（0.19–0.32） vs 逻辑回归0.24（0.17–0.33），差异均无统计学意义，改善幅度至多有限。
- 临床启示：在当前PC4数据中，机器学习模型未显示优于传统风险调整模型的临床可用增益，暂不支持仅凭机器学习替代传统模型进行资源分配或患者监测。未来需整合更全面数据和先进计算技术并开展外部验证，再评估其临床价值。

**原文摘要**：Machine learning (ML) methods may improve prediction of individual risk of mortality risk after congenital heart surgery (CHD) by identifying nonlinear relationships/novel combinations of variables. Improved risk discrimination may improve outcomes by optimizing resource allocation and patient surveillance. Children and adolescents (age≤18 years) undergoing CHD surgery at Pediatric Cardiac Critical Care Consortium hospitals 9/1/2014-12/30/2019 were studied. Non-collinear potential predictors were identified empirically and used to create a logistic regression risk (LR) model (mimicking traditional risk-adjustment models) and ML (random forest and gradient boosting model) models. Model performance was evaluated using net reclassification index (NRI), the proportion of subjects in which either the absolute risk category or estimated risk relative to other subjects changed between each model and one composed only of STAT category as the primary outcome. This metric reflects how often a model changes how a clinician views the risk of a patient relative to a parsimonious model and provides some measure of the utility of these models. Secondary analyses compared overall model fit. In total 51,475 operative encounters from 52 hospitals were studied. Subjects were 45% female, 20% neonates, and 5% underwent STAT 5 operations. C-statistics and Brier scores did not differ significantly between ML and LR models. For absolute risk categorires, point estimates for NRI between ML-models and STAT category (0.76 95%CI: 0.68-0.83) suggested at best modest improvements compared to logistic regression model (0.75 95%CI 0.67-0.83), but the difference was not significant. Similarly, for rankings of risk within the cohort, NRI for ML models (0.26 95%CI: 0.19-0.32) was not significantly different to that for the LR model (0.24 95%CI: 0.17-0.33). ML derived models did not improve overall model fit. Magnitude of improvement in reclassification was at best modest and not statistically significant. Future research is necessary to evaluate the added values of incorporating more comprehensive data and/or advanced computational techniques and use of reclassification in risk prediction.
