## Developmental changes in hypoglossal canal morphometry and its anatomical relationships: a CT study in 180 pediatric patients.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:14
- 期刊: Surg Radiol Anat
- 国家/单位: Istanbul · University of Health Sciences Umraniye Training and Research Hospital
- 通讯作者: Topcuoglu ED (elifdilaratopcuoglu@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00276-026-04006-6
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00276-026-04006-6) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42758203/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：基于CT评估0–17岁儿科人群舌下神经管形态及其与颅底解剖标志关系的年龄和性别相关变化。
- 核心发现/数据：共分析180例儿童360个舌下神经管；13项测量中12项随年龄显著增大（FDR q<0.05），以舌下神经管至斜坡和枕髁距离最明显，年龄组间差异在最小与最大年龄组间最显著。12/13项测量存在性别差异，女性尺寸持续较小（回归系数−0.30至−0.93 mm，均FDR q<0.05），无年龄×性别交互；所有测量均无左右差异或年龄×侧别交互。
- 临床启示：本研究可为儿童颅底影像判读和手术规划提供年龄/性别相关形态学参考；尽管尺寸随发育和性别变化，双侧对称性可用于临床对照。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate age- and sex-related changes in hypoglossal canal (HC) morphology and its anatomical relationships with skull base landmarks in a pediatric population using computed tomography (CT). Children aged 0-17 years who underwent cranial CT were included. Each age group comprised five males and five females. The HC diameters were measured on transverse and multiplanar reformatted images at the midpoint, as well as at the intracranial and extracranial openings. The HC length was also recorded. The distances between the HC and the clivus cortex, the occipital condyle (OC) and the posterior condylar canal (if present) were evaluated. Inter-observer and intra-observer agreement was assessed in 20% of the sample (36 patients, 72 HCs). A total of 360 HCs from 180 pediatric patients were analyzed using linear mixed-effects models accounting for the non-independence of bilateral measurements. Twelve of 13 measurements demonstrated significant age-related enlargement (FDR q < 0.05), most pronounced in distances from the HC to the clivus and OC, a pattern confirmed by monotonic trend analysis. Age-group comparisons showed a significant omnibus effect for 12 of 13 measurements, with the most pronounced differences observed between the youngest and oldest groups. Twelve of 13 measurements also differed significantly by sex, with consistently smaller dimensions in females (regression coefficients ranging from - 0.30 to -0.93 mm, all FDR q < 0.05) and no significant age-by-sex interaction. In contrast, no measurement showed a significant right-left difference or age-by-side interaction. This CT-based study provides detailed morphometric data on the pediatric HC and its anatomical relationships. The findings demonstrate age- and sex-related developmental changes while preserving bilateral symmetry and may serve as a reference for pediatric skull base imaging and surgery.

## Combined conventional-to-sutureless repair of the supracardiac total anomalous pulmonary venous drainage.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:14
- 期刊: Multimed Man Cardiothorac Surg
- 国家/单位: 德国
- 通讯作者: Photiadis J
- DOI: 10.1510/mmcts.2026.071
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1510/mmcts.2026.071) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42757677/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报道一例新生儿心上型完全性肺静脉异位引流由常规修复转为无缝合修复的联合手术经验及结局。
- 核心发现/数据：患儿经超声心动图确诊心上型完全性肺静脉异位引流，症状轻微，解剖显示肺静脉汇合部发育良好，垂直静脉引流入无名静脉，并在房间隔水平存在梗阻。术中因左心房与肺静脉收集器不匹配，由原计划的常规修复改为无缝合技术。术后超声证实左心房与肺静脉汇合部吻合通畅，术后病程平稳，6个月随访良好且无肺动脉高压证据。
- 临床启示：当心上型完全性肺静脉异位引流常规修复中左心房与肺静脉汇合部尺寸不匹配时，转为无缝合技术可有助于获得通畅吻合。术后应通过超声随访评估吻合口通畅性及肺动脉高压情况，但本经验仅为单例病例。

**原文摘要**：This case describes a combined conventional-to-sutureless repair of the supracardiac form of total anomalous pulmonary venous drainage in a neonate. The diagnosis was established via echocardiography in a patient presenting with mild symptoms. Anatomically, a well-developed pulmonary venous confluence was identified, with the vertical vein draining into the innominate vein and obstruction detected at the interatrial level. Although the patient was initially scheduled for a semi-elective conventional repair, an intraoperative mismatch between the left atrium and the pulmonary venous collector necessitated a switch to a sutureless technique. Postoperative echocardiography confirmed an unobstructed anastomosis between the left atrium and the pulmonary venous confluence. The postoperative course was uneventful, and at the 6-month follow-up, the patient is doing well with no evidence of pulmonary hypertension.

## The relationship between prolonged travel and mortality among women with complicated deliveries in rural Kenya.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:14
- 期刊: J Glob Health · 中科院医学3区(公共卫生、环境卫生与职业卫生3区) · IF 5.5 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health
- 通讯作者: Croke K
- DOI: 10.7189/jogh.16.04300
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.7189/jogh.16.04300) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42757578/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：在肯尼亚农村复杂分娩女性中，评估产时长途转诊

**原文摘要**：In many low- and middle-income settings, deliveries often take place in primary health facilities that are not equipped to address life-threatening complications. Emergency referral systems seek to rapidly transport women and newborns facing complications but inevitably introduce delays. Estimating the mortality impact of these delays is challenging due to potential selection bias. As an alternative approach, we examine the relationship between delays and adverse outcomes among a sample that is less likely to exhibit selection effects: mothers who started at a low-level facility before transfer to a hospital equipped to provide definitive care (hospital-level). We leverage demographic, obstetric, and geodata collected through the parent study Service Delivery Reform for Maternal and Newborn Health in Kakamega County, Kenya to explore the relationship between intrapartum travel and whether there was a maternal death, neonatal death, or stillbirth (adverse health outcome). We conducted a series of logistic models estimating the relationship between longer travel distances and probability of an adverse health outcome, with analyses iteratively adjusted for sociodemographic and obstetric covariates. Of the 39,052 mothers with full geodata, 1,928 (4.9%) reported visiting at least one low-level facility before visiting a high-level facility. After restricting to those who experienced upward movement in facility level during delivery and adjusting for relevant covariates, we find that those in the fourth quartile of travel distances (42-436 km) had a 2.60 times higher odds of an adverse health outcome relative to those in the first quartile (2-16 km). Our findings show that Phase 2 delays in the form of intrapartum journeys have a pronounced association with maternal and child mortality when the total distance travelled is long. These findings align with the prior evidence that delays in receiving appropriate and timely delivery care are related to maternal and child mortality and morbidity.

## High rate of Healthcare Associated Infections in a Nigerian Tertiary Hospital: A Point Prevalence Survey Highlighting Neonatal and Paediatric Vulnerability.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:14
- 期刊: Niger Med J
- 国家/单位: Nigeria · Department of Medical Microbiology and Parasitology
- 通讯作者: Versporten A
- DOI: 10.71480/nmj.v67i5.1427
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.71480/nmj.v67i5.1427) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42756928/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估尼日利亚Usmanu Danfodiyo大学教学医院住院患者医疗保健相关感染（HAI）的患病率、类型及危险因素，重点关注新生儿和儿科人群的脆弱性。
- 核心发现/数据：在262例住院患者中，HAI患病率为14.5%（38/262），儿科/新生儿病房显著高于成人病房（31.0% vs 11.4%；p=0.001）。常见HAI包括其他感染（6.5%）、血流感染（5.3%）和手术部位感染（2.7%）；HAI与外周血管导管使用、手术及致命性McCabe评分相关。器械使用以外周血管导管最常见（78.2%），其次为导尿管（27.1%）和中心静脉导管（1.5%）。
- 临床启示：该院HAI负担高，尤其威胁新生儿和儿科患者，提示需加强感染预防与控制项目、监测体系、高危病房针对性干预及诊断能力，以指导HAI预防策略。

**原文摘要**：Healthcare-associated infections (HAIs) are a major patient safety threat globally, with high burdens in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs). In Nigeria, data on HAIs are limited, affecting effective infection prevention and control (IPC) strategies. This study evaluated the prevalence, types, and risk factors of HAIs at Usmanu Danfodiyo University Teaching Hospital (UDUTH) using the Global Point Prevalence Survey (GPPS) HAIs section. A cross-sectional point prevalence survey was conducted from 21-25 October 2024 across all inpatient wards using standardised GPPS protocols. All patients admitted before 08:00 on survey days were included. Data were collected on the presence of HAIs, infection types, invasive device use, prior hospitalisations, surgery during admission, and severity of underlying conditions using the McCabe score. Comparisons between patients with and without HAI were made using chi square. Among 262 inpatients, HAI prevalence was 14.5% (38/262), with significantly higher prevalence in paediatric/neonatal wards than adult wards (31.0% vs 11.4%; p = 0.001). Common HAIs included other infections (6.5%), bloodstream infections (5.3%), and surgical-site infections (2.7%). HAIs were associated with peripheral vascular catheter use, surgery, and fatal McCabe score. Peripheral vascular catheters were most frequently used (78.2%), followed by urinary (27.1%) and central venous catheters (1.5%). UDUTH revealed a high rate of HAIs, particularly affecting vulnerable children and neonatal populations, with bloodstream infections among the most reported types. These findings underscore an urgent need for improved IPC programmes, strengthened surveillance systems, targeted interventions in high risk wards, and enhanced diagnostic capacity to guide HAIs prevention strategies.

## Maternal and perinatal risk factors for necrotizing enterocolitis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:14
- 期刊: World J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学4区(儿科4区/外科4区) · IF 2.1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Washington University in St Louis School of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Martin C
- DOI: 10.1136/wjps-2026-001205
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1136/wjps-2026-001205) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42756918/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：在Developmental Origins of Health and Disease（DOHaD）框架下，将坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）重新置于母体-围产期系统背景中，综合产前、产后及健康社会决定因素，以补充传统发病机制模型并寻找更早预防靶点。
- 核心发现/数据：该叙述性综述未报告具体定量数据；其核心观点是NEC不能仅归因于早产儿肠道不成熟、菌群失调和免疫信号过度激活。母体心理社会压力可激活HPA轴、改变胎盘信号并干扰胎儿肠道发育和微生物群建立，父源应激表观遗传、母体营养不良、代谢不良及环境污染物进一步叠加，且对社会经济弱势社区影响更重；产后NICU、卫生系统及结构性健康决定因素继续影响NEC负担。
- 临床启示：临床实践应将NEC预防前移至孕前和围产期，关注母体心理、营养、代谢、环境暴露及健康公平，并采取多学科、系统化干预，同时不替代传统发病机制框架。

**原文摘要**：Necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) remains among the most devastating complications of prematurity, carrying significant mortality and lifelong morbidity despite decades of mechanistic and translational research. Although NEC is classically framed as an inflammatory disease of intestinal immaturity, driven by microbial dysbiosis and exaggerated immune signaling, this model does not fully explain the uneven distribution of disease burden across infants, hospitals and communities. In this review, we reframe NEC within a broader maternal-perinatal systems context grounded in the Developmental Origins of Health and Disease (DOHaD) framework. This review synthesizes evidence linking maternal psychosocial stress to activation of the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis. This alters placental signaling and disrupts fetal gut development and microbiome establishment. This is further compounded by paternal stress-related epigenetic mechanisms, suboptimal maternal nutrition, poor metabolic health and environmental pollutants that disproportionately affect socioeconomically disadvantaged neighborhoods. These prenatal influences intersect with postnatal modifiers within neonatal intensive care units and health systems. Social and structural determinants of health further affect prenatal exposures and the delivery of neonatal care. This framework does not replace traditional pathogenesis models of NEC but rather contextualizes them within broader developmental and health systems. This perspective may help identify earlier targets for prevention while supporting more equitable and multidisciplinary approaches to reducing the burden of NEC.

## Assessment of liver fibrosis in children with intestinal failure and prolonged parenteral nutrition.

- 国家/单位: 中国 · School of Clinical Medicine
- 通讯作者: Wong KK (kkywong@hku.hk)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06633-x

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估长期接受肠外营养（PN）的肠衰竭（IF）患儿中，应用瞬时弹性成像（TE）检测肝纤维化的患病率及严重程度。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入37例需长期PN的IF患儿，其中19例完成TE；中位PN时长为16个月（3-144个月），4例（21.1%）存在肝纤维化（FS≥6.8 kPa）。纤维化组与非纤维化组在基线特征、手术细节及PN特征上相似，但纤维化组死亡2例，死亡率显著高于非纤维化组（50% vs. 0%，p=0.035）。
- 临床启示：TE可发现长期PN的IF患儿中较高比例的肝纤维化，且肝纤维化与死亡风险升高相关；临床应重视TE监测，但传统IFALD危险因素可能不足以识别高风险患儿，仍需更大样本前瞻性研究验证。

**原文摘要**：Children with intestinal failure (IF) often necessitates prolonged parenteral nutrition (PN) use, which predisposes them to IF-associated liver disease (IFALD) and fibrosis. The aim of the study is to evaluate the prevalence and severity of liver fibrosis using transient elastography (TE) on children with IF who were on prolonged PN. Patients with IF requiring PN for more than 3 months between Jan 2000 to December 2024 were included in the study. Liver fibrosis was assessed by TE and defined by a fibrosis score (FS) > = 6.8 kPa. Baseline demographics, diagnosis, operation details, PN characteristics, and outcomes were compared between the fibrosis and non-fibrosis groups. 37 children suffered from IF requiring prolonged PN were identified, and TE was performed in 19. 17 patients (89.5%) had massive bowel resection, and 2 (10.5%) had visceral myopathies. Necrotizing enterocolitis was the commonest cause of IF in this cohort (14, 73.7%). 8 (42.1%) remained dependent on PN at the time of TE. Median PN duration was 16 months (3-144 months). 4 patients (21.1%) had liver fibrosis on TE. The fibrosis and non-fibrosis groups (15, 78.9%) were similar in terms of baseline demographics, operative details and PN characteristics. There were 2 deaths in the fibrosis group, one from IF related liver failure and the other from respiratory failure with known cholestasis. (50% vs. 0%, p = 0.035). With TE measurement, the incidence of liver fibrosis in this cohort was at 21%, which appears to be higher than previously reported. They are associated with higher risk of mortality but not with known risk factors of IFALD. Further prospective studies with larger sample size will be required for proper evaluation.

## Cirrhotic cardiomyopathy is independently associated with peri-transplant acute kidney injury in biliary atresia - a hepato-cardio-renal link.

**原文摘要**：Hepatorenal syndrome (HRS-AKI) confers significant morbidity and mortality in patients with cirrhosis. Cirrhotic cardiomyopathy (CCM), a prevalent co-morbidity in adults with growing recognition in children, leads to hemodynamic perturbations that may compound the maladaptive physiology resulting in HRS-AKI. We aimed to explore a hepato-cardio-renal link in in pediatric cirrhosis by examining peri-transplant AKI, with a focus on CCM and Major Adverse Kidney Events at 28 days (MAKE28) in liver transplant (LT) patients. Single-center, retrospective study of pediatric LT for biliary atresia (BA) from 2014-2020. Peri-LT AKI was defined as AKI within 7 days of LT. CCM was defined as left ventricular mass index to height (LMVI) of ≥95 g/m2.7, and/or relative wall thickness ≥0.42. Of 62 BA patients (age 1.1 years [IQR 0.8-2.4]), 29% had AKI, 61% had CCM, and 40% fulfilled MAKE28. AKI patients had higher blood product and vasoactive requirements, and lower ventilator free days, compared to no-AKI patients. Median length of stay was 133 (IQR 54-121) vs. 19.5 (IQR 8.5-41) days for AKI and no AKI patients, respectively (p<0.001). CCM was more prevalent in those with peri-LT AKI (OR 6.6, CI 1.7-23, p=0.005). CCM remained significant after adjusting for age, markers liver disease severity, and intraoperative events. BA patients face high morbidity peri-LT in the setting of prevalent AKI. The association between pediatric CCM with AKI suggests cardiac dysfunction may be an independent risk factor of HRS-AKI. While studies are needed to further define the relationship between CCM and renal injury, pre-LT echocardiogram has the potential to facilitate timely identification and management of children at risk for HRS-AKI, and thereby promote renal recovery.
