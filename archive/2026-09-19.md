## Developmental changes in hypoglossal canal morphometry and its anatomical relationships: a CT study in 180 pediatric patients.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:14
- 期刊: Surg Radiol Anat
- 国家/单位: Istanbul · University of Health Sciences Umraniye Training and Research Hospital
- 通讯作者: Topcuoglu ED (elifdilaratopcuoglu@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00276-026-04006-6
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00276-026-04006-6) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42758203/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：基于CT评估0–17岁儿科人群舌下神经管形态及其与颅底解剖标志关系的年龄和性别相关变化。
- 核心发现/数据：共分析180例儿童360个舌下神经管；13项测量中12项随年龄显著增大（FDR q<0.05），以舌下神经管至斜坡和枕髁距离最明显，年龄组间差异在最小与最大年龄组间最显著。12/13项测量存在性别差异，女性尺寸持续较小（回归系数−0.30至−0.93 mm，均FDR q<0.05），无年龄×性别交互；所有测量均无左右差异或年龄×侧别交互。
- 临床启示：本研究可为儿童颅底影像判读和手术规划提供年龄/性别相关形态学参考；尽管尺寸随发育和性别变化，双侧对称性可用于临床对照。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate age- and sex-related changes in hypoglossal canal (HC) morphology and its anatomical relationships with skull base landmarks in a pediatric population using computed tomography (CT). Children aged 0-17 years who underwent cranial CT were included. Each age group comprised five males and five females. The HC diameters were measured on transverse and multiplanar reformatted images at the midpoint, as well as at the intracranial and extracranial openings. The HC length was also recorded. The distances between the HC and the clivus cortex, the occipital condyle (OC) and the posterior condylar canal (if present) were evaluated. Inter-observer and intra-observer agreement was assessed in 20% of the sample (36 patients, 72 HCs). A total of 360 HCs from 180 pediatric patients were analyzed using linear mixed-effects models accounting for the non-independence of bilateral measurements. Twelve of 13 measurements demonstrated significant age-related enlargement (FDR q < 0.05), most pronounced in distances from the HC to the clivus and OC, a pattern confirmed by monotonic trend analysis. Age-group comparisons showed a significant omnibus effect for 12 of 13 measurements, with the most pronounced differences observed between the youngest and oldest groups. Twelve of 13 measurements also differed significantly by sex, with consistently smaller dimensions in females (regression coefficients ranging from - 0.30 to -0.93 mm, all FDR q < 0.05) and no significant age-by-sex interaction. In contrast, no measurement showed a significant right-left difference or age-by-side interaction. This CT-based study provides detailed morphometric data on the pediatric HC and its anatomical relationships. The findings demonstrate age- and sex-related developmental changes while preserving bilateral symmetry and may serve as a reference for pediatric skull base imaging and surgery.

## Combined conventional-to-sutureless repair of the supracardiac total anomalous pulmonary venous drainage.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报道一例新生儿心上型完全性肺静脉异位引流由常规修复转为无缝合修复的联合手术经验及结局。
- 核心发现/数据：患儿经超声心动图确诊心上型完全性肺静脉异位引流，症状轻微，解剖显示肺静脉汇合部发育良好，垂直静脉引流入无名静脉，并在房间隔水平存在梗阻。术中因左心房与肺静脉收集器不匹配，由原计划的常规修复改为无缝合技术。术后超声证实左心房与肺静脉汇合部吻合通畅，术后病程平稳，6个月随访良好且无肺动脉高压证据。

**原文摘要**：This case describes a combined conventional-to-sutureless repair of the supracardiac form of total anomalous pulmonary venous drainage in a neonate. The diagnosis was established via echocardiography in a patient presenting with mild symptoms. Anatomically, a well-developed pulmonary venous confluence was identified, with the vertical vein draining into the innominate vein and obstruction detected at the interatrial level. Although the patient was initially scheduled for a semi-elective conventional repair, an intraoperative mismatch between the left atrium and the pulmonary venous collector necessitated a switch to a sutureless technique. Postoperative echocardiography confirmed an unobstructed anastomosis between the left atrium and the pulmonary venous confluence. The postoperative course was uneventful, and at the 6-month follow-up, the patient is doing well with no evidence of pulmonary hypertension.

## The relationship between prolonged travel and mortality among women with complicated deliveries in rural Kenya.

- 期刊: J Glob Health · 中科院医学3区(公共卫生、环境卫生与职业卫生3区) · IF 5.5 · JCR Q1

- 通讯作者: Croke K
- DOI: 10.7189/jogh.16.04300
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.7189/jogh.16.04300) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42757578/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：在肯尼亚农村复杂分娩女性中，评估产时长途转诊

**原文摘要**：In many low- and middle-income settings, deliveries often take place in primary health facilities that are not equipped to address life-threatening complications. Emergency referral systems seek to rapidly transport women and newborns facing complications but inevitably introduce delays. Estimating the mortality impact of these delays is challenging due to potential selection bias. As an alternative approach, we examine the relationship between delays and adverse outcomes among a sample that is less likely to exhibit selection effects: mothers who started at a low-level facility before transfer to a hospital equipped to provide definitive care (hospital-level). We leverage demographic, obstetric, and geodata collected through the parent study Service Delivery Reform for Maternal and Newborn Health in Kakamega County, Kenya to explore the relationship between intrapartum travel and whether there was a maternal death, neonatal death, or stillbirth (adverse health outcome). We conducted a series of logistic models estimating the relationship between longer travel distances and probability of an adverse health outcome, with analyses iteratively adjusted for sociodemographic and obstetric covariates. Of the 39,052 mothers with full geodata, 1,928 (4.9%) reported visiting at least one low-level facility before visiting a high-level facility. After restricting to those who experienced upward movement in facility level during delivery and adjusting for relevant covariates, we find that those in the fourth quartile of travel distances (42-436 km) had a 2.60 times higher odds of an adverse health outcome relative to those in the first quartile (2-16 km). Our findings show that Phase 2 delays in the form of intrapartum journeys have a pronounced association with maternal and child mortality when the total distance travelled is long. These findings align with the prior evidence that delays in receiving appropriate and timely delivery care are related to maternal and child mortality and morbidity.

## High rate of Healthcare Associated Infections in a Nigerian Tertiary Hospital: A Point Prevalence Survey Highlighting Neonatal and Paediatric Vulnerability.

- 核心发现/数据：在262例住院患者中，HAI患病率为14.5%（38/262），儿科/新生儿病房显著高于成人病房（31.0% vs 11.4%；p=0.001）。常见HAI包括其他感染（6.5%）、血流感染（5.3%）和手术部位感染（2.7%）；HAI与外周血管导管使用、手术及致命性McCabe评分相关。器械使用以外周血管导管最常见（78.2%），其次为导尿管（27.1%）和中心静脉导管（1.5%）。

**原文摘要**：Healthcare-associated infections (HAIs) are a major patient safety threat globally, with high burdens in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs). In Nigeria, data on HAIs are limited, affecting effective infection prevention and control (IPC) strategies. This study evaluated the prevalence, types, and risk factors of HAIs at Usmanu Danfodiyo University Teaching Hospital (UDUTH) using the Global Point Prevalence Survey (GPPS) HAIs section. HAIs were associated with peripheral vascular catheter use, surgery, and fatal McCabe score. Peripheral vascular catheters were most frequently used (78.2%), followed by urinary (27.1%) and central venous catheters (1.5%). UDUTH revealed a high rate of HAIs, particularly affecting vulnerable children and neonatal populations, with bloodstream infections among the most reported types. These findings underscore an urgent need for improved IPC programmes, strengthened surveillance systems, targeted interventions in high risk wards, and enhanced diagnostic capacity to guide HAIs prevention strategies.

**原文摘要**：Necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) remains among the most devastating complications of prematurity, carrying significant mortality and lifelong morbidity despite decades of mechanistic and translational research. This alters placental signaling and disrupts fetal gut development and microbiome establishment. This is further compounded by paternal stress-related epigenetic mechanisms, suboptimal maternal nutrition, poor metabolic health and environmental pollutants that disproportionately affect socioeconomically disadvantaged neighborhoods. Social and structural determinants of health further affect prenatal exposures and the delivery of neonatal care. This framework does not replace traditional pathogenesis models of NEC but rather contextualizes them within broader developmental and health systems. This perspective may help identify earlier targets for prevention while supporting more equitable and multidisciplinary approaches to reducing the burden of NEC.
