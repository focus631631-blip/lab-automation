## Prognostic Utility of the Laboratory Risk Indicator for Necrotizing Fasciitis in Infants: A Prospective Observational Study.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 05:00
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 巴基斯坦 · The Children's Hospital and University of Child Health Sciences
- 通讯作者: Mahmood U (umarmahmud1992@live.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163596
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163596) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43051520/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估成人来源的LRINEC评分入院阈值≥6对婴儿坏死性筋膜炎不良临床结局的预测价值。
- 核心发现/数据：93例婴儿中复合不良结局发生77例（82.8%），死亡7例（7.5%），非计划再次清创43例（46.2%）。LRINEC≥6者19/20（95.0%）发生不良结局，<6者58/73（79.5%），但敏感性仅24.7%、特异性93.8%，阳性/阴性预测值为95.0%/20.5%，关联无统计学意义（RR 1.20，95% CI 0.95-1.40，P=0.178）。75.3%的不良结局发生于LRINEC<6的婴儿。
- 临床启示：传统LRINEC阈值≥6在婴儿坏死性筋膜炎中敏感性差，低评分不能提供预后 reassurance，也不能替代临床评估。仍需多中心研究验证儿童特异性预后工具。

**原文摘要**：The Laboratory Risk Indicator for Necrotizing Fasciitis (LRINEC) score was developed in adults, but its prognostic utility in children remains uncertain. This study evaluated the conventional admission LRINEC threshold of ≥6 for predicting adverse clinical outcomes in infants with necrotizing fasciitis. This prospective observational study included 93 infants with clinically diagnosed necrotizing fasciitis at a tertiary pediatric surgical center between February 2021 and August 2023. Admission LRINEC scores were categorized as <6 or ≥6. The primary endpoint was a composite of unplanned repeat surgical debridement for persistent or progressive infection, hospitalization exceeding 21 days, worsening sepsis, or in-hospital mortality. The mean age was 187.9 ± 96.6 days. Composite adverse outcomes occurred in 77 infants (82.8%), and seven (7.5%) died. Unplanned repeat debridement was required in 43 patients (46.2%). Among infants with LRINEC ≥6, 19/20 (95.0%) experienced an adverse outcome, compared with 58/73 (79.5%) with LRINEC <6. Sensitivity was 24.7% (95% CI, 16.4%-35.4%) and specificity was 93.8% (95% CI, 71.7%-98.9%). Positive and negative predictive values were 95.0% and 20.5%, respectively. The association was not statistically significant (risk ratio, 1.20; 95% CI, 0.95-1.40; P = 0.178). Overall, 75.3% of adverse outcomes occurred among infants with LRINEC <6. The conventional LRINEC threshold demonstrated poor sensitivity for adverse clinical outcomes in infants with necrotizing fasciitis. A low score should not provide prognostic reassurance or replace clinical assessment. Larger multicenter studies are needed to validate pediatric-specific prognostic tools. III - Prospective observational cohort study.

## Association of adverse perinatal outcomes of intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy with maternal serum bile acid profiles.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨妊娠期肝内胆汁淤积症（ICP）孕妇血清胆汁酸谱改变及其与不良围产结局的关系。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入149例孕妇（正常77例、ICP 72例）；ICP患者分娩孕周、Apgar评分及出生体重显著降低，剖宫产、低出生体重、新生儿重症监护室入住、羊水胎粪污染和早产率升高。ICP不良结局患者具有独特胆汁酸代谢特征，总胆汁酸及GCA、GCDCA、TCA、TCDCA、TUDCA等结合型胆汁酸升高与不良围产结局相关；上述指标联合预测效能优于单一指标。
- 临床启示：多胆汁酸联合指标有望用于识别高危ICP患者并辅助风险预测，但临床应用前需前瞻性外部验证。

**原文摘要**：Intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy (ICP) is associated with increased risk of adverse perinatal outcomes, yet current monitoring lacks predictive accuracy. This study aimed to explore alterations in serum bile acid profiles and their relationship with adverse perinatal outcomes in ICP. This study enrolled 149 pregnant women, including 77 normal pregnant women and 72 cases with ICP, subdivided into ICP with adverse perinatal outcomes and those without according to the perinatal outcomes. Serum concentrations of 15 bile acids were quantified using ultra-performance liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry and compared among the three groups. Elevated levels of total bile acids and conjugated bile acids, including GCA, GCDCA, TCA, TCDCA, and TUDCA, were associated with adverse perinatal outcomes and might serve as candidate biomarkers for risk prediction. The multi-bile acid combined indicator demonstrated superior exploratory predictive performance over individual markers, offering a potential tool for identifying high-risk ICP patients, which requires prospective external validation before clinical application. A combined indicator of GCA, GCDCA, TCA, TCDCA, TUDCA, and total bile acids demonstrated superior exploratory predictive efficacy for adverse perinatal outcomes compared to individual markers; prospective external validation is required before clinical use in high-risk ICP patients.

- 临床启示：临床可据此个体化、精准确定新生儿下肢PICC置入深度，减少反复调整和并发症
