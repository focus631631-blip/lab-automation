## Prognostic Utility of the Laboratory Risk Indicator for Necrotizing Fasciitis in Infants: A Prospective Observational Study.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 05:00
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2

- 通讯作者: Mahmood U (umarmahmud1992@live.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163596
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163596) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43051520/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估成人来源的LRINEC评分入院阈值≥6对婴儿坏死性筋膜炎不良临床结局的预测价值。
- 核心发现/数据：93例婴儿中复合不良结局发生77例（82.8%），死亡7例（7.5%），非计划再次清创43例（46.2%）。LRINEC≥6者19/20（95.0%）发生不良结局，<6者58/73（79.5%），但敏感性仅24.7%、特异性93.8%，阳性/阴性预测值为95.0%/20.5%，关联无统计学意义（RR 1.20，95% CI 0.95-1.40，P=0.178）。75.3%的不良结局发生于LRINEC<6的婴儿。
- 临床启示：传统LRINEC阈值≥6在婴儿坏死性筋膜炎中敏感性差，低评分不能提供预后 reassurance，也不能替代临床评估。仍需多中心研究验证儿童特异性预后工具。

**原文摘要**：The Laboratory Risk Indicator for Necrotizing Fasciitis (LRINEC) score was developed in adults, but its prognostic utility in children remains uncertain. This study evaluated the conventional admission LRINEC threshold of ≥6 for predicting adverse clinical outcomes in infants with necrotizing fasciitis. This prospective observational study included 93 infants with clinically diagnosed necrotizing fasciitis at a tertiary pediatric surgical center between February 2021 and August 2023. Admission LRINEC scores were categorized as <6 or ≥6. The primary endpoint was a composite of unplanned repeat surgical debridement for persistent or progressive infection, hospitalization exceeding 21 days, worsening sepsis, or in-hospital mortality. The mean age was 187.9 ± 96.6 days. Composite adverse outcomes occurred in 77 infants (82.8%), and seven (7.5%) died. Unplanned repeat debridement was required in 43 patients (46.2%). Among infants with LRINEC ≥6, 19/20 (95.0%) experienced an adverse outcome, compared with 58/73 (79.5%) with LRINEC <6. Sensitivity was 24.7% (95% CI, 16.4%-35.4%) and specificity was 93.8% (95% CI, 71.7%-98.9%). Positive and negative predictive values were 95.0% and 20.5%, respectively. The association was not statistically significant (risk ratio, 1.20; 95% CI, 0.95-1.40; P = 0.178). Overall, 75.3% of adverse outcomes occurred among infants with LRINEC <6. The conventional LRINEC threshold demonstrated poor sensitivity for adverse clinical outcomes in infants with necrotizing fasciitis. A low score should not provide prognostic reassurance or replace clinical assessment. Larger multicenter studies are needed to validate pediatric-specific prognostic tools. III - Prospective observational cohort study.

**原文摘要**：Unroofed coronary sinus (CS) is a rare cause of left-to-right shunting and right-sided volume overload. Transcatheter treatment is feasible only in selected anatomies. Reduction by manual manipulation under general anesthesia through laparotomy was unsuccessful and so the gangrenous bowel was resected and a primary end-to-end ileosigmoid anastomosis done. Histopathology did not reveal a lead point while the gross examination of the specimen confirmed the diagnosis of intussusception. The enteral feeds were started on postoperative day (POD) five and discharged on POD 10. He was asymptomatic and growing well at 1 month follow-up and still asymptomatic, achieving all his milestones at 6 months of follow-up. Idiopathic ileocolic intussusception should always be included in the differential diagnosis of all neonatal intestinal obstructions.

## Utility of Renal Replacement Therapy for Infants With Congenital Diaphragmatic Hernia Receiving Extracorporeal Life Support.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 19:54
- 期刊: Pediatr Crit Care Med
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University of Tennessee Health Science Center
- 通讯作者: Jancelewicz T
- DOI: 10.1097/PCC.0000000000004121
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/PCC.0000000000004121) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43055550/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估接受体外生命支持（ECLS）的先天性膈疝（CDH）婴儿中使用肾脏替代治疗（RRT）与生存结局之间的关联。
- 核心发现/数据：在2815例患儿中，749例（26.6%）接受RRT；匹配前RRT组出院生存率显著低于非RRT组（35.5% vs. 60.2%，p<0.0001）。倾向评分匹配形成413对后，RRT仍与更低的出院生存率相关（36.3% vs. 57.4%；OR 0.42，95%CI 0.32-0.56，p<0.0001），并伴更长ECLS中位时间（15 vs. 9.4天）和更高严重ECLS期间AKI发生率（10.9% vs. 3.6%）。分层分析显示，RRT仅在ECLS期间行CDH修补的患儿中与显著更差生存相关；RRT更可能是ECLS病程中显著临床恶化的标志。
- 临床启示：对ECLS期间需要RRT的CDH婴儿应视为高危人群，加强AKI及多器官功能监测并个体化评估预后与治疗策略。RRT本身不宜直接推断为导致死亡的原因，其使用可作为病情恶化和高死亡风险的警示信号。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate the association between renal replacement therapy (RRT) use and survival outcomes in infants with congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH) receiving extracorporeal life support (ECLS). Multicenter retrospective cohort study using the Extracorporeal Life Support Organization database (2011-2020). Propensity score (PS) matching was used to account for pre-ECLS disease severity. International multicenter ECLS centers. Infants with CDH requiring ECLS. None. Of 2815 infants, 749 infants (26.6%) received RRT. Before matching, +RRT patients had significantly lower survival to discharge (35.5% vs. 60.2%; p < 0.0001). PS matching generated 413 pairs with balanced pre-ECLS characteristics. After matching, +RRT remained associated with significantly lower survival to discharge (36.3% vs. 57.4%; survival odds ratio, 0.42; 95% CI, 0.32-0.56; p < 0.0001). +RRT patients also experienced longer median ECLS durations (15 vs. 9.4 d; p < 0.0001) and higher rates of severe on-ECLS AKI (10.9% vs. 3.6%; p < 0.0001). Stratified analysis showed that RRT was associated with significantly worse survival only in patients undergoing on-ECLS CDH repair. In infants with CDH on ECLS, RRT use is strongly associated with increased ECLS duration and decreased survival to discharge. Although PS matching suggests this association persists regardless of baseline severity, RRT likely serves as a marker for significant clinical deterioration during the ECLS course.
