## Relationship Between Timing of Surgery and Procedure-Specific Outcomes in Neonates and Infants for Congenital Heart Surgery.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:07
- 期刊: Ann Thorac Surg · 中科院医学2区(呼吸系统2区/外科2区/心脏和心血管系统3区) · IF 5.3 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: DC · Children's National Hospital
- 通讯作者: d'Udekem Y (yves.dudekem@childrensnational.org)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.athoracsur.2026.10.046
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.athoracsur.2026.10.046) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43044956/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：基于多中心数据库评估出生体重、手术时机与新生儿及婴儿先天性心脏病手术结局的关系，以识别最佳手术时机。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入35,315例患儿（新生儿26,367例，74.7%；婴儿8,948例，25.3%），低出生体重（<2500g）在新生儿中占15.8%、婴儿中占59.9%；0–1kg组手术死亡率最高（9%，p<0.001），连续出生体重每增加100g与死亡率降低相关（OR=0.45，95%CI：0.40–0.51，p<0.0001）。新生儿手术日龄、早产和遗传综合征均独立 associated with increased mortality；Norwood手术在<1.8kg和<5天时死亡率升高，ASO在1.8–3.5kg及>5天时风险最低。AVSD、VSD和CoA中出生体重及手术时机对死亡率无显著影响。
- 临床启示：除Norwood和ASO外，体重和手术时机对死亡率影响有限，支持个体化手术决策而非依赖历史体重阈值；对Norwood和ASO应特别关注低出生体重及过早手术的风险。

**原文摘要**：Determining the right timing for neonatal heart surgery remains difficult. In a large multicenter cohort, we studied the relationship between birthweight, timing of surgery, and outcomes to identify optimal surgical timing. Using the Society of Thoracic Surgeons Congenital Heart Surgery Database from 2010-2022, we retrospectively reviewed infants who underwent surgery within 90 days of birth. Procedures analyzed included coarctation of aorta (CoA), ventricular septal defect (VSD) repair, atrioventricular septal defect (AVSD) repair, tetralogy of Fallot (TOF) repair, Norwood procedure, and arterial switch operation (ASO). Birthweight, prematurity, and genetic syndromes were analyzed as predictors of operative mortality using categorical and continuous models, including nonlinear spline analysis. Patients were divided into five birthweight categories for risk comparison. The cohort included 35,315 patients: 26,367(74.7%) neonates and 8,948(25.3%) infants. Low birthweight (<2500g) was present in 15.8% of neonates and 59.9% of infants. Operative mortality was highest in the 0-1kg group (9%, p<0.001). Continuous birthweight was a predictor of mortality (OR=0.45 per 100g increase; 95% CI: 0.40-0.51; p<0.0001). Neonatal age at surgery, prematurity, and genetic syndromes were independently associated with increased mortality. For Norwood procedures, mortality increased below 1.8kg and before 5 days of age; for ASOs, lowest risk occurred between 1.8-3.5kg and after 5 days of age. In AVSD, VSD, and CoA, neither birthweight nor timing significantly affected mortality. Except for Norwood and ASO procedures, weight and timing had limited impact on mortality, supporting individualized surgical decision-making rather than reliance on historical weight thresholds.

## Mental health and behavioral difficulties in children with rare neonatal gastrointestinal surgical conditions: a systematic literature review and meta-analysis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:07
- 期刊: Eur J Pediatr Surg
- 国家/单位: Gothenburg · Sahlgrenska University Hospital Queen Silvia Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Dellenmark-Blom M
- DOI: 10.1055/a-3003-4580
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1055/a-3003-4580) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43044678/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估七种罕见新生儿胃肠道外科疾病患儿心理健康与行为困难的患病率及特征，并与常模比较、识别相关因素。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入47项研究、1786名患儿，心理行为困难较常模更常见（CDH 7/8项、EA 6/7项、ARM 4/6项研究）；Meta分析显示困难发生率分别为25.7%（CBCL，I²=59.6）和17.8%（SDQ，I²=65.3），孤独症为10–23%（CDH、AWD），ADHD为0–20%（CDH、AWD、NEC、ARM、HSCR）。研究质量多为“一般”至“较差”，临床及家庭相关因素与结局显著相关（p<0.05）。
- 临床启示：这类患儿存在较高的心理健康和行为困难风险，应纳入多学科随访并关注心理行为评估。现有研究质量仍需提高，且需评估多学科随访干预的效果。

**原文摘要**：Introduction This systematic review aimed to assess prevalence and characteristics of mental health and behavioral difficulties in children with seven neonatal gastrointestinal surgical conditions, to compare these to normative populations, and identify associated factors. Material and Methods Using a PRISMA compliant protocol (PROSPERO CRD42023443731), studies on congenital diaphragmatic hernia(CDH), esophageal atresia(EA), abdominal wall defects (AWD: gastroschisis/omphalocele), anorectal malformations (ARM), Hirschsprung's disease (HSCR) and surgical necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) were identified through searches in MEDLINE, Embase, PsycINFO, CINAHL, and Cochrane Library (inception-November 2025) A random-effects meta-analysis of prevalence was conducted, with heterogeneity assessed using I². Significance level was p<0.05. Results Forty-seven studies (published 1991-2025) with 1786 children (median sample size 28, range 10-157) were included: CDH (n=10), EA (n=11), AWD (n=9), NEC (n=2), ARM (n=17), and HSCR (n=5). Most studies originated from Europe (n=31). Mental health and behavioral difficulties were more frequently reported than in norms, in CDH (7/8 studies), EA (6/7 studies), and ARM (4/6 studies). Meta-analysis showed elevated difficulties in 25,7% (Child Behavior Check List; I²=59.6) and 17.8% (Strengths and Difficulties Questionnaire; I²=65.3). The prevalence of autism ranged from 10-23% (CDH, AWD) and ADHD from 0-20% (CDH, AWD, NEC, ARM, HSCR). Across studies, methodological quality was generally 'fair' to 'poor'. Clinical and family-related factors were significantly associated with outcomes (p<0.05). Conclusions Children with rare neonatal gastrointestinal surgical conditions risk mental health and behavioral difficulties. Current research leaves room for improvement of study quality. Multidisciplinary follow-up care is warranted, and its effects should be evaluated.

## Miscarriages and Perinatal Deaths in Sierra Leone: Data From A Nationwide Household Survey.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:07
- 期刊: Am J Trop Med Hyg · 中科院医学4区(热带医学3区/公共卫生、环境卫生与职业卫生4区) · IF 1.9 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 荷兰 · Center of Tropical Medicine and Travel Medicine
- 通讯作者: Grobusch MP
- DOI: 10.4269/ajtmh.26-0226
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.4269/ajtmh.26-0226) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43044121/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：基于2019—2020年塞拉利昂全国住户调查，从社区层面评估流产和围产期死亡的发生情况。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入3745名参与者，其中914名育龄女性；仅28.5%（216/914）使用避孕措施。回忆的2160次终生妊娠中，早期妊娠丢失104次（4.8%），其中自然流产79.8%、人工流产17.3%；死产55次（2.5%）。死产率为18.4/1000（95%CI：11.2—31.0），围产期死亡率为38.4/1000（95%CI：26.7—55.0），且流产可能存在漏报。
- 临床启示：应提高

**原文摘要**：Demographic data on adverse pregnancy outcomes in Sierra Leone are scarce. This study examined the prevalence of miscarriages and perinatal deaths from a community perspective by use of a population-based, cross-sectional household survey on surgical conditions conducted in Sierra Leone in 2019-2020. A weighted random cluster design was used to select the enumerator areas with a probability proportional to the population size. Seventy-five clusters were selected, with 25 households per cluster and two random participants per household. Women between 12 and 50 years of age were interviewed about family planning methods and their obstetrical history. A total of 3,745 participants were included; 914 were women of reproductive age. Family planning methods were used by 216/914 women (28.5%). In total, 2,160 lifetime pregnancies were recalled, of which 104/2,160 (4.8%) resulted in early pregnancy loss. Eighty-three (83/104; 79.8%) were reported as spontaneous abortion, 18/104 (17.3%) were intentional abortions. There were 55/2, 160 stillbirths reported (2.5%). The stillbirth rate was 18.4 per 1,000 births (95% CI: 11.2-31.0). The perinatal mortality rate, defined as stillbirth after a gestational age of 28 weeks or neonatal death within the first 7 days after birth per 1,000 births calculated over a 5-year period, was 38.4 per 1,000 births (95% CI: 26.7-55.0). This population-based survey demonstrates a high perinatal mortality rate and low use of family planning methods in Sierra Leone. Miscarriages were likely to be underreported. Improved access to affordable contraception and appropriate medical evaluation of pregnancy loss may help reduce the burden of adverse pregnancy outcomes.

## Management of Congenital Occipital Exostosis in the Pediatric Patient: An Illustrative Case Report.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:07
- 期刊: Pediatr Neurosurg · 中科院医学4区(临床神经病学4区/儿科4区/外科4区) · IF 1.3 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University of Mississippi Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Weaver K
- DOI: 10.1159/000551494
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1159/000551494) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43043999/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：描述一例儿童罕见先天性枕骨外生骨疣的病例特点及其神经外科管理经验。
- 核心发现/数据：患儿为2周龄足月顺产男婴，因枕部双侧骨性突起就诊，超声提示枕部异位骨化，CT和MRI证实对称性外生骨疣且无明显颅内受累，随访至15个月时骨性突起及覆盖脂肪垫明显增大。随后行手术切除外生骨疣并去除多余脂肪和皮肤，病理显示软骨、皮质骨和松质骨、造血组织及轻度髓内纤维化；手术成功且无并发症，术后无神经功能缺损并持续达到发育里程碑。
- 临床启示：对无症状先天性枕骨外生骨疣应结合个体化术前规划、全面影像学评估和持续临床监测，若病变进行性增大或影响外观轮廓，可考虑手术切除，整体预后良好。

**原文摘要**：The purpose of this article was to describe a case of an unusual congenital occipital bone defect in a pediatric patient and the corresponding neurosurgical management. The authors present a case of a 2-week-old male referred for neurosurgical evaluation after occipital bony prominences were detected on a postnatal exam. The patient was born full-term via uncomplicated vaginal delivery and underwent a head ultrasound, which disclosed an occipital heterotopic ossification. Physical exam revealed dimpling bilaterally over bony prominences at the occiput. Computed tomography and magnetic resonance image confirmed symmetric exostoses without clear intracranial involvement. However, fibrous adhesions were noted extending through the dermis, suggesting dermal involvement likely secondary to the bony changes, although no definitive sinus tracts were identified. The patient was monitored for the first year of life with no symptoms or developmental concerns. At follow-up at 15 months of age, the bony prominences and overlying fat pad were noticeably enlarged, which prompted corrective surgery. The exostosis was excised to restore normal occipital contour, and excess fat and skin were removed. Pathology revealed cartilage, cortical and trabecular bone, hematopoiesis, and mild intramedullary fibrosis. The surgery was successful with no complications, and the patient had an uneventful recovery. Postoperatively, the patient continues to meet developmental milestones without neurological deficits or complications. This case highlights the surgical management of an unusual occipital exostosis in an asymptomatic pediatric patient. It underscores the importance of individualized preoperative planning, comprehensive imaging, and ongoing clinical monitoring to assess changes over time.

In these cases that are diagnosed at a younger age, the incidence of type 1 SCM increases, while the frequency of occurrence in the lumbar region decreases and increases in the thoracic region. Furthermore, it has been determined that additional anomalies accompanying spina bifida aperta occur more frequently with complex SCMs.

## Dysregulated serotonin reuptake impairs intestinal serotonin availability in human necrotizing enterocolitis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:07
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 加拿大 · The Hospital for Sick Children
- 通讯作者: Pierro A (agostino.pierro@sickkids.ca)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06694-y
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06694-y) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43043471/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨5-HT信号失调是否通过影响肠道5-HT可利用性参与NEC相关胃肠动力障碍。
- 核心发现/数据：NEC患者远端回肠中EC细胞数量及5-HT合成（TPH1）、释放（TRPA1）、代谢（MAO-A）标志物未见改变，但5-HT再摄取相关SERT和VMAT1表达升高，患者来源EC细胞富集类器官中亦升高。NEC类器官细胞外5-HT水平降低，抑制SERT或VMAT1后可恢复。提示NEC存在5-HT再摄取增强，导致细胞外5-HT可利用性下降。
- 临床启示：5-HT再摄取失调可能解释NEC相关胃排空延迟和肠动力障碍，SERT/VMAT1或5-HT再摄取可作为潜在治疗靶点，尚需进一步临床验证。

**原文摘要**：Necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) is a devastating gastrointestinal disease in preterm infants associated with impaired gut motility. We previously demonstrated that delayed gastric emptying correlates with intestinal injury severity in experimental NEC. Serotonin (5-HT) from enterochromaffin (EC) cells regulates intestinal motility and gastric emptying. We hypothesized that dysregulated 5-HT signaling contributes to NEC-associated dysmotility. Distal ileal tissues from NEC and non-NEC patients were analyzed by immunofluorescence for EC cells (CHGA), 5-HT, synthesis (TPH1), release (TRPA1), metabolism (MAO-A), and reuptake proteins (SERT, VMAT1). EC cell-enriched human intestinal organoids were generated, and forskolin-induced 5-HT release was assessed following SERT or VMAT1 inhibition. EC cell number and expression of TPH1, TRPA1, and MAOA were unchanged in NEC ileum. In contrast, SERT and VMAT1 expression were increased in NEC tissues and patient-derived EC cell-enriched organoids. Reduced extracellular 5-HT levels in NEC organoids were restored following SERT or VMAT1 inhibition. NEC is associated with increased 5-HT reuptake despite an unchanged EC cell population, resulting in reduced extracellular 5-HT availability. Dysregulated serotonin signaling may contribute to impaired gastric emptying and gut dysmotility in NEC, identifying serotonin reuptake as a potential therapeutic target.

## Functional outcomes in pediatric colorectal surgery: a predictor-based analysis of Hirschsprung disease and anorectal malformation in a resource-limited setting.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:07
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2

- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06716-9) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43043531/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估资源有限地区中Hirschsprung病（HD）和肛门直肠畸形（ARM）患儿根治术后长期肠道功能结局，并识别有利结局的临床预测因素。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入136例患者（HD 94例、ARM 42例）。HD队列中，合并先天畸形和结肠切除至脾曲近端是肠道功能的不良预测因素，使有利结局概率分别从66%降至22%（p=0.007）和从75%降至28%（p=0.008）。ARM队列中，低严重度畸形患者有利结局达100%，而高/中度严重度患者为54.5%（p=0.004）。
- 临床启示：ARM的解剖严重度是影响肠道功能结局的重要因素，HD患儿则需关注相关系统畸形及结肠切除范围；应开展结构化长期术后随访，尤其关注功能结局高风险儿童。

**原文摘要**：Long-term fecal continence and bowel habit outcomes remain a significant challenge in pediatric colorectal surgery. This study evaluated long-term functional bowel outcomes in patients treated for Hirschsprung disease (HD) and Anorectal Malformations (ARM) using the standardized Rintala Bowel Function Score, identifying key clinical predictors of favorable outcomes. A retrospective cross-sectional study was conducted at St. Paul's Hospital Millennium Medical College, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, among children who underwent definitive surgical repair for HD and ARM between January 2018 and December 2022. Functional bowel outcomes were evaluated using the Rintala Bowel Function Score (0-20 scale), with a score ≥ 17 defined as a clinically favorable outcome. In the ARM cohort, 100% of patients with low-severity malformations achieved favorable outcomes compared with 54.5% of those with high/intermediate-severity malformations (p = 0.004). While anatomical severity appears to be an important factor influencing bowel function outcomes in ARM, associated systemic anomalies and the extent of colonic excision were also associated with poorer bowel function in children with HD. These findings highlight the potential importance of structured, long-term postoperative follow-up, with particular attention to children at higher risk of functional bowel problems.

## Limited Ladd's Procedure as a Mobilization Strategy in Duhamel Pull-Through for Long-Segment Hirschsprung Disease: A Single-Center Experience.

## Exploring the potential of bile fluid dynamics analysis to inform clinical strategies for choledochal cysts: Experimental and numerical studies.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过实验与数值模拟分析不同Todani分型胆总管囊肿的胆汁流体动力学特征，探讨其能否为临床治疗策略提供生物力学依据。
- 核心发现/数据：基于1例健康成人MRI重建胆道系统并生成8种胆总管囊肿模型，PIV与CFD显示囊肿扩张区流速持续偏低，提示胆汁淤积，尤以1a、2、4a和4b型明显；健康胆道壁面剪切应力（WSS）变化较小，而胆总管囊肿可出现高达10,000倍的极端WSS梯度，尤以1a、2和4a型突出；胆汁流动特征主要受局部解剖异常决定，与针对扩张区域的手术干预目标一致。
- 临床启示：CFD可补充揭示不同胆总管囊肿分型的胆汁流动异常和生物力学风险，为手术规划及靶向扩张区域治疗提供参考；其临床价值仍需未来研究验证。

**原文摘要**：Surgical intervention is essential for choledochal cysts (CCs) due to their high lifetime risk of progressing to cholangiocarcinoma, yet management strategies vary worldwide. Given bile fluid's vital role in digestion, investigating bile flow dynamics may provide surgeons with additional insights for treatment planning. In this study, one MRI-based healthy adult scan was used to reconstruct biliary system, which was then modified according to Todani classifications to generate eight CC types. Particle Image Velocimetry was used to first visualize local velocity field, followed by computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations of bile emptying to analyze pathological non-Newtonian bile flow. Wall shear stress (WSS), a key biomechanical parameter, was visualized through 2D surface mapping for comparative evaluation. Results showed consistently low velocity in dilated regions, suggesting bile accumulation in CCs, particularly in Types 1a, 2, 4a, and 4b. While WSS changes were minimal in the healthy biliary system, extreme gradients, up to 10,000-fold emerged in CCs, especially Types 1a, 2, and 4a. These findings highlight that bile flow characteristics are primarily dictated by localized anatomical abnormalities, aligning with surgical interventions that target dilated regions. These findings demonstrate the potential of CFD to provide additional biomechanical insights into bile flow abnormalities associated with different CC types, which may serve as a reference for future clinical investigations.
