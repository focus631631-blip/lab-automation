## Treatment of classic bladder exstrophy in German hospitals: Analysis of Nationwide Hospital Discharge Data from 2016 to 2024.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 15:55
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Munich · School of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Wilms M (m.wilms@soma-ev.de)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163427
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163427) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42660385/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估德国经典膀胱外翻（BE）患者的全国诊疗结构、治疗模式及围手术期结局，并探讨中心手术量与治疗策略的关系。
- 核心发现/数据：2016至2024年间，德国120家医院共收治177名BE新生儿，34.5%合并其他先天畸形，4.0%出生体重低于1500g；期间34家医院完成221例矫正手术，中位年手术量仅1例。按年手术量三分位分组，低、中、高容量中心分别为1例、3例和8例，高容量中心更常采用出生28天后的延迟一期关闭（85.1% vs. 40.7% vs. 39.3%），但机构病例量不足以支撑关于早期术后并发症的稳健“量-效”统计分析。
- 临床启示：德国BE诊疗高度分散，不同容量中心间治疗策略差异提示护理质量可能不一致；将BE矫正手术集中化有助于推动治疗标准化。

**原文摘要**：Classic bladder exstrophy (BE) is a rare and complex congenital anomaly requiring highly specialized multidisciplinary care. Although corrective surgery represents the central component of treatment, the national care structure for patients with BE in Germany has yet not been comprehensively evaluated. Nationwide hospital discharge data (Diagnosis-Related Group statistics) from 2016 to 2024 were analyzed to identify patients with BE at (1) birth hospitalization and (2) hospitalization for corrective surgery. Patient characteristics, associated anomalies, treatment patterns, hospital caseload, and perioperative outcomes were assessed. Between 2016 and 2024, 177 newborns with BE were treated across 120 hospitals at the time of birth. At least one additional congenital anomaly was documented in 34.5% of patients, and 4.0% had a birthweight below 1,500 g. During the same period, 221 corrective procedures were performed in 34 hospitals, with a median annual caseload of one case per institution. Hospitals were stratified into caseload terciles with median annual volumes of 1, 3, and 8 procedures in the low-, medium-, and high-volume groups, respectively. Delayed primary closure beyond 28 days of life was performed more frequently in high-volume centers compared with medium- and low-volume hospitals (85.1% vs. 40.7% vs. 39.3%). Institutional caseloads were insufficient to support a statistically robust volume-outcome analysis regarding early postoperative complications. Care for patients with BE in Germany is highly decentralized. Differences in treatment strategies between higher- and lower-volume centers suggest potential variability in care delivery. Centralization of corrective BE surgery may support greater standardization of treatment.

## Segmental dilatation of the intestine in preterm infants: absence of interstitial cells of Cajal in a paired histopathological study.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 15:55
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Czech Republic · Charles University in Prague
- 通讯作者: Matejek T (xmatt01@seznam.cz)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163426
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163426) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42660383/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨早产儿肠节段性扩张（SDI）的组织病理学特征、出生患病率及临床病理关联，并明确Cajal间质细胞（ICC）在其中的作用。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入35例早产儿SDI，所有标本肠神经节细胞保留，而所有SDI节段ICC缺失或显著减少、边缘正常；多数节段存在肌层萎缩。极低出生体重儿（<1500g）中中心出生患病率为17.2/1000，X线气腹、较低出生体重及黏膜下炎症与术后并发症相关。
- 临床启示：SDI在早产儿中表现为节段限制性神经肌肉发育异常（ICC耗竭、肌层萎缩），提示产前因素（如胎盘功能不全）可能参与发病；术前影像和出生体重评估有助于预测术后并发症风险。

**原文摘要**：Segmental dilatation of the intestine (SDI) is a rare congenital disorder with unclear pathogenesis. The role of interstitial cells of Cajal (ICC) in preterm infants remains insufficiently defined. We aimed to characterise the histopathological phenotype of SDI, determine its birth prevalence, and explore associations between clinical and histological features and postoperative complications. This retrospective single-centre study included preterm infants with clinically suspected SDI who underwent surgery. Resected SDI segments were compared with macroscopically normal resection margins. Histopathological assessment focused on ICC, enteric ganglion cells, and muscular layer alterations. Birth prevalence was calculated for very low birth weight infants below 1500 g. Postoperative complications were defined as mechanical ileus, necrotising enterocolitis, intestinal perforation, or death after resection. Thirty-five infants with SDI were included. Enteric ganglion cells were preserved in all specimens. ICC were absent or markedly reduced in all SDI segments and preserved in all margins. Muscle layer atrophy was present in the majority of SDI segments. The centre-specific birth prevalence was 17.2 per 1,000 very low birth weight infants. Pneumoperitoneum on X-ray, lower birth weight, and submucosal inflammation contributed most in exploratory modelling. SDI in preterm infants shows a consistent segment-restricted neuromuscular pattern with depletion of ICC, preserved enteric ganglion cells, and muscular layer atrophy. These findings are compatible with a segment-restricted developmental disturbance of the intestinal wall, but the causal role of ICC depletion remains uncertain and requires further investigation in relation to antenatal factors, including placental insufficiency.

## Outcomes of delayed Norwood operation in high-risk patients-time to reconsider the dogma of a primary Norwood operation?

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 15:55
- 期刊: Interdiscip Cardiovasc Thorac Surg · 中科院医学4区(心脏和心血管系统4区/呼吸系统4区/外科4区) · IF 1.4 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Children's National Hospital
- 通讯作者: Yerebakan C
- DOI: 10.1093/icvts/ivag218
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1093/icvts/ivag218) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42663575/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估高危新生儿采用先行双侧肺动脉束带、延迟行Norwood手术的策略是否可改善预后，并挑战“首选一期Norwood手术”的传统观念。
- 核心发现/数据：2019-2024年间20例高危左心发育不全综合征/变异型患儿，双侧肺动脉束带中位年龄2.5天，延迟Norwood手术中位年龄36天，手术存活率75%；束带后4周内行Norwood者无需肺动脉再干预，术后48小时血管活性药物评分下降47%，无ECMO支持者脑氧饱和度从53%升至58%和63%。
- 临床启示：对高危患儿先行双侧肺动脉束带、约4-6周后行延迟Norwood手术，可获得与低危患儿行一期Norwood相当的生存率，并减少手术或导管再干预，可作为高危群体的可行策略。

**原文摘要**：The delayed Norwood approach, preceded by bilateral pulmonary artery banding, has emerged as a strategy to improve outcomes in high-risk infants by addressing neonatal hemodynamic instability and organ immaturity. This single-center, retrospective study analyzed 20 consecutive patients with hypoplastic left heart syndrome/variants and multiple risk factors who underwent a delayed Norwood operation following bilateral pulmonary artery banding between 2019 and 2024. Bilateral pulmonary artery banding was performed at a median age of 2.5 days (IQR: 2-4). Preoperative risk factors included prematurity (35%), low birth weight <2.5 kg (25%), non-cardiac comorbidities (25%), genetic syndromes (50%), preoperative cardiogenic shock (35%), and restrictive atrial septum (25%). The median age at delayed Norwood was 36 days (IQR: 29-79), with an operative survival rate of 75%. Patients who underwent the delayed Norwood within 4 weeks of bilateral pulmonary artery banding did not require any pulmonary artery reinterventions. Median lactate clearance time was 8 hours (IQR: 5-18), and the median vasoactive inotropic score decreased by 47% within 48 hours postoperatively. Cerebral near-infrared spectroscopy values improved in patients without postoperative ECMO support from 53% to 58% and 63% at 0, 12, and 36 hours, respectively. The delayed Norwood approach, preceded by initial bilateral pulmonary artery banding in a high-risk patient cohort, achieved surgical outcomes comparable to those of lower-risk patients undergoing primary Norwood operation. A delayed Norwood procedure around 4-6 weeks of following bilateral pulmonary artery banding may improve survival rates in high-risk patients with reduction of surgical or catheter-based reinterventions.

## Endoarch Repair of Ruptured Extra-Anatomic Ascending to Descending Bypass Graft in Hypoplastic Arch With Coarctation.

- 期刊: JACC Case Rep
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Oregon Health and Science University
- 通讯作者: Bhamidipati CM (bhamidip@ohsu.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jaccas.2026.110034
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jaccas.2026.110034) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42663349/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨一例新生儿期升主动脉-降主动脉旁路移植术后，因胸部钝性创伤导致移植物破裂合并菌血症的复杂成人先天性心脏病患者的腔内修复治疗策略。
- 核心发现/数据：36岁女性患者，因胸部钝性创伤导致非移位性骨折刺破主动脉移植物，发生局限性破裂和菌血症；在延迟诊断后出现失代偿，经左胸切口行复杂胸主动脉腔内修复术，联合6周抗生素及保守管理获得安全结局。
- 临床启示：对于多次胸骨切开、需广泛清创及需终身抗生素抑制的破裂移植物患者，初始腔内治疗是合理选择；成人先天性心脏病患者需高度警惕早期识别，应考虑将腔内修复用于弓部病变。

**原文摘要**：Hypoplastic arch and residual coarctation are treated with ascending to descending extra-anatomic bypasses and are challenging to manage. A 36-year-old woman with several sternotomies had undergone ascending to descending extra-anatomic bypass as a neonate. She experienced blunt force trauma to her chest, and significant delay in diagnosis led to decompensation. A nondisplaced fracture lacerated the aortic graft and led to a contained rupture and bacteremia. She underwent left thoracotomy and complex thoracic endovascular aortic repair. Six weeks of antibiotics and careful conservative management led to a safe outcome. Management of a ruptured extra-anatomic bypass in the setting of bacteremia with thoracic endovascular aortic repair is controversial. Given multiple prior sternotomies, the degree of required tissue debridement, and anticipated lifelong antibiotic suppression, an initial endovascular approach seemed warranted. Unconventional approaches to complex adult congenital heart disease (ACHD) lesions are necessary to increase longevity and mitigate disease. Patients with ACHD require early recognition with a high index of suspicion. Endovascular repair in ACHD arch pathology should be considered.

## Accelerated Conservative Management of Severe Congenital Knee Hyperextension in a Low-Resource Setting: A Case Report.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 15:55
- 期刊: Int Med Case Rep J · 中科院医学4区(医学：内科4区) · IF 0.7
- 国家/单位: Somaliland · School of Medicine and Surgery

- DOI: 10.2147/IMCRJ.S608712
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.2147/IMCRJ.S608712) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42662841/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告在资源有限环境下，对严重先天性膝关节过伸（Leveuf I/II型）新生儿采用加速保守治疗的成功经验。
- 核心发现/数据：一名1日龄女性新生儿接受为期14天的两阶段连续石膏固定联合针对性物理治疗，3周内畸形完全矫正、功能完全恢复；3个月随访显示关节稳定性持久且运动发育达年龄相应水平。
- 临床启示：早期、个体化的加速保守治疗可作为手术或长期矫形器治疗的廉价有效替代方案，尤其适用于骨科资源匮乏地区。

**原文摘要**：Congenital knee hyperextension (CKH), or congenital genu recurvatum, is a rare orthopedic anomaly in neonates that requires prompt intervention to prevent long-term disability. This case report presents the successful non-surgical management of a one-day-old female neonate presenting with severe unilateral CKH (Leveuf Type I/II) in a resource-limited setting. The novelty of this case lies in the application of an accelerated, two-stage serial casting protocol spanning just 14 days, followed by targeted physiotherapy. This approach resulted in the complete resolution of the deformity and full functional recovery within three weeks. Clinical follow-up at 3 months confirmed durable joint stability and age-appropriate motor development. This case highlights the efficacy of early, tailored conservative intervention, offering a cost-effective alternative to surgical management or prolonged orthotic therapy in settings where advanced orthopedic resources are scarce.

## Farnesoid X receptor activates STAT3 to promote fatty acid oxidation and suppress inflammatory macrophage responses in cholestasis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 15:55
- 期刊: J Biol Chem · 中科院生物学2区(生化与分子生物学2区) · IF 4.1 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Aurora · University of Colorado School of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Ghosh S (swati.ghosh@cuanschutz.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jbc.2026.113508
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbc.2026.113508) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42660454/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探究法尼醇X受体（FXR）是否通过STAT3依赖性机制调节胆汁淤积性肝病中的肝脏免疫代谢与炎症反应。
- 核心发现/数据：在DDC诱导的慢性胆管病小鼠模型中，FXR激动剂显著减轻生化及组织学胆汁淤积性肝损伤并抑制巨噬细胞浸润，同时恢复脂肪酸氧化（FAO）相关基因表达；机制上，FXR激活增加肝脏STAT3磷酸化、乙酰化及线粒体易位，增强STAT3与FAO基因启动子结合并诱导其mRNA表达。STAT3激动剂colivelin可模拟GW4064的保护效应，而STAT3抑制剂C-188-9部分逆转GW4064对炎症/纤维化基因的抑制及FAO基因的诱导；花生四烯酸诱导的巨噬细胞活化和肝细胞凋亡可被GW4064减轻，STAT3 siRNA敲低则削弱FXR激动剂的转录应答。
- 临床启示：FXR激动剂驱动的STAT3免疫代谢轴有望成为胆汁淤积性肝病（如原发性硬化性胆管炎）的新型治疗靶点，通过同时促进脂肪酸氧化和抑制炎症性巨噬细胞反应来减轻肝损伤与纤维化。

**原文摘要**：Inflammatory cholangiopathies such as primary sclerosing cholangitis involve bile duct injury, macrophage-driven inflammation, and progressive fibrosis. While farnesoid X receptor (FXR) regulates bile acid metabolism and immune responses, its role in macrophage immunometabolic reprogramming during cholestasis remains unclear. Here, we investigated whether FXR regulates liver immunometabolism through STAT3-dependent mechanisms in cholestatic liver disease. Using transcriptomic, oxylipin lipidomic, histological, and metabolomic analyses in a chronic murine model of DDC-induced cholangiopathy, we explored the role of FXR-induced STAT3 signaling in regulating inflammatory and metabolic responses. FXR agonists significantly reduced biochemical and histologic cholestatic liver injury, as well as suppression of macrophage infiltration. Liver RNA-seq revealed that cholestatic injury activated inflammatory, TGF-β, and arachidonic acid (AA) metabolic pathways and reduced fatty acid oxidation (FAO) programs, while FXR restored pathways linked to FAO. Mechanistically, FXR activation in the liver increased STAT3 phosphorylation and acetylation and mitochondrial translocation, boosted STAT3 binding to promoters of genes involving FAO pathways, and triggered the mRNA expression of FAO genes. Pharmacologic STAT3 activation with colivelin reproduced protective effects of GW4064-mediated FXR activation. Conversely, co-administration of the STAT3 inhibitor C-188-9 partially reversed GW4064-mediated suppression of inflammatory and fibrogenic gene programs and reduced induction of FAO genes. Arachidonic acid injection of mice induced macrophage activation and hepatocyte apoptosis, both of which were attenuated by GW4064. STAT3 siRNA knockdown in hepatocyte and macrophage cell systems also blunted FXR agonist-induced transcriptional responses. These findings identify a novel FXR agonist-driven STAT3 immunometabolic axis that integrates bile acid signaling, FAO, and macrophage reprogramming to regulate inflammation during cholestatic liver injury.
