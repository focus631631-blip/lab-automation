## Pull-Through, Diversion, or Irrigation? Management of Neonatal Hirschsprung Disease in U.S. Children's Hospitals.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:34
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Rady Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Hyman SC (sihyman@health.ucsd.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163410
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163410) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42632412/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估美国儿童医院新生儿期先天性巨结肠（HD）的管理策略差异，并比较新生儿期拖出术与分期拖出术的临床结局。
- 核心发现/数据：662例患者中，44%接受了新生儿期拖出术，各医院新生儿期拖出术比例在0-79%之间。无造口的分期拖出术与新生儿期拖出术的HAEC发生率及抗生素使用时长相似；初始行造口转流的患者HAEC更频繁且更严重。
- 临床启示：鉴于既往研究显示分期拖出术可能改善控便功能，且不增加肠炎风险，对于短段型HD新生儿，分期拖出术（无需转流）可能是更优策略。

**原文摘要**：Both neonatal pull-through and staged repair after rectal irrigations have become accepted strategies for Hirschsprung disease (HD) in newborns. Staged pull-through may be associated with better continence, although its impact on the incidence of Hirschsprung associated enterocolitis (HAEC) is not clear. We assessed variations in the management of neonatal HD and evaluated outcomes after neonatal versus staged pull-through. The Pediatric Health Information Systems (PHIS) database was used to identify patients diagnosed with HD at less than 30 days of age at U.S. children's hospitals between 2017 and 2024 who underwent pull-through by 180 days. Operations performed within 10 days of diagnostic rectal biopsy were classified as primary pull-through procedures. Patients who underwent pull-through beyond that and had an enterostomy or not comprised the comparison groups. The incidence and severity of HAEC and incontinence was determined. Inclusion criteria were met in 662 patients, 44% of whom had a neonatal pull-through. Individual hospitals reported neonatal pull-throughs in 0-79% of cases. Patients managed with a staged pull-through without diversion had a similar incidence of HAEC and antibiotic duration as those who had a neonatal pull-through. Patients initially managed with diversion had more frequent and severe HAEC. There was substantial variation among hospitals in the frequency of neonatal pull-through and diversion for HD. Staged repair without diversion was associated with a similar overall incidence of enterocolitis as neonatal pull-through. Given the continence benefits established in prior studies, staged pull-through may represent a preferable strategy for neonates with short-segment HD. III.

## A Six-Day Window: Tracheostomy Timing and Short-Term Outcomes in Severe Pediatric Traumatic Brain Injury.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:34
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Texas Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Mina AS (alexander.mina@bcm.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163376
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163376) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42632411/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨儿童重型创伤性脑损伤（TBI）中气管切开时机与短期预后的关联，并确定最佳时间阈值。
- 核心发现/数据：基于TQIP数据库（2019-2023年）纳入1181例患儿，LOWESS和ZINB分析识别出入院后第6天为拐点；倾向评分匹配后（147对），早期气管切开组（≤6天）中位呼吸机-free天数显著更高（16.0 vs 9.0天，p<0.001），ICU-free天数亦显著更高（11.0 vs 4.0天，p<0.001），且机械通气时间更短、住院时间更短、居家出院率更高。
- 临床启示：对于儿童重型TBI，入院6天内行气管切开可能改善短期预后，临床医生可考虑将6天作为优化气管切开时机的参考窗口。

**原文摘要**：Early tracheostomy in pediatric patients with severe traumatic brain injury (TBI) is associated with reduced ventilator dependence and complications. However, the optimal timing of tracheostomy remains poorly defined. This study utilized a national trauma database to examine how the timing of tracheostomy is associated with critical care outcomes in children with severe TBI. We performed a retrospective cohort study using the Trauma Quality Improvement Program (TQIP) database (2019-2023). Inclusion criteria were age <18 years, severe TBI (GCS ≤8 and AIS Head ≥3 or ICD-10 code for intracranial injury), and tracheostomy placement. We first used LOWESS curves and a series of zero-inflated negative binomial (ZINB) models across candidate cut-offs to empirically identify a timing threshold, then applied propensity score matching (PSM) to compare Early Tracheostomy (≤6 days from admission) and Late Tracheostomy (>6 days). Primary outcomes were ventilator-free and ICU-free days. Of 15,934 children with severe TBI identified, 1,181 met the inclusion criteria and underwent tracheostomy (191 Early, 990 Late). LOWESS and ZINB analyses across candidate cut-offs identified Day 6 as an exploratory inflection point beyond which the incremental benefit of earlier tracheostomy diminished. Applying this threshold, PSM yielded a balanced cohort of 294 patients (147 pairs). The Early cohort demonstrated significantly higher median ventilator-free days (16.0 vs. 9.0, p<0.001) and ICU-free days (11.0 vs. 4.0, p<0.001). ZINB regression confirmed a higher rate of accumulating ventilator- and ICU-free days (IRR 1.47, 95% CI 1.31-1.66; IRR 1.46, 95% CI 1.27-1.67) compared with the Late cohort. Early tracheostomy was associated with significantly reduced mechanical ventilation duration, shorter hospital stays, and a higher likelihood of home discharge. These findings suggest that tracheostomy within the first 6 days of admission is associated with improved short-term outcomes in pediatric severe TBI.

## Clinical utility of serum Mac-2 binding protein glycosylation isomer as a noninvasive marker for liver fibrosis in biliary atresia.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:34
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 韩国 · Severance Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Ihn K (pedsik@yuhs.ac)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163409
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163409) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42632410/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估血清Mac-2结合蛋白糖基化异构体（M2BPGi）作为胆道闭锁（BA）患儿肝纤维化无创生物标志物的诊断价值。
- 核心发现/数据：M2BPGi水平随纤维化分期递增，诊断晚期纤维化（≥F3）的AUC为0.953，诊断肝硬化（F4）的AUC为0.986；最佳截断值3.02时，对≥F3的敏感性和特异性分别为87.8%和96.5%，对F4分别为97.6%和93.6%。排除阈值0.81对≥F3的敏感性和阴性预测值均达100%，且其诊断效能优于APRI、FIB-4和S-index。
- 临床启示：M2BPGi可作为BA患儿肝纤维化的准确无创检测指标，有助于临床进行纤维化监测及肝移植时机决策。

**原文摘要**：Hepatic fibrosis influences treatment decisions and clinical outcomes in biliary atresia (BA). As liver biopsy is invasive and unsuitable for serial assessments, noninvasive biomarkers are required. This study aimed to evaluate serum Mac-2 binding protein glycosylation isomer (M2BPGi) as a noninvasive biomarker by assessing its correlation with histopathologic fibrosis in children with BA. This prospective, exploratory diagnostic accuracy study enrolled 135 children who were assigned to one of the following three groups: BA with liver transplantation, BA with Kasai portoenterostomy, and choledochal cysts (control group). Intraoperative tissue was staged using METAVIR (stages F0-F4). The preoperative M2BPGi level and aspartate aminotransferase-to-platelet ratio index, Fibrosis-4 index, and S-index were compared across stages using receiver-operating characteristic (ROC) analysis with Youden-derived cutoffs. M2BPGi increased progressively with fibrosis stage and outperformed all comparators. M2BPGi discriminated advanced fibrosis (stage ≥F3) (area under the ROC curve [AUC], 0.953) and cirrhosis (stage F4) (AUC, 0.986). The optimal Youden-derived cutoff index (COI) of 3.02 showed strong performance (stage ≥F3: 87.8% sensitivity and 96.5% specificity; F4: 97.6% sensitivity and 93.6% specificity). A rule-out COI threshold of 0.81 achieved 100% sensitivity and a negative predictive value for stage ≥F3. M2BPGi is an accurate noninvasive biomarker for hepatic fibrosis in BA that may support clinical decision-making regarding fibrosis surveillance and transplantation timing.

## A historical journey through developmental dysplasia of the hip (DDH): from prehistoric evidence to the rise of modern diagnostic and therapeutic concepts.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:34
- 期刊: Int Orthop · 中科院医学3区(骨科3区) · IF 2.4 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Padova · University of Padova
- 通讯作者: Biz C (carlo.biz@unipd.it)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00264-026-06996-9
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00264-026-06996-9) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42631757/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统回顾发育性髋关节发育不良（DDH）从史前证据到当代诊疗概念的历史演变过程。
- 核心发现/数据：DDH的历史是概念持续演进的过程，而非孤立发现；史前病理证据证实其古老性，《希波克拉底文集》最早区分先天性与创伤性髋脱位，Pare、Dupuytren、Vrolik等确立解剖病理基础，19世纪Roser与Trendelenburg将其转化为功能性临床疾病，放射学建立临床-影像学特征，20世纪Codivilla、Putti、Hilgenreiner、Ortolani、Barlow推动新生儿不稳定与早期诊断预防，Graf超声分类和Pavlik吊带、Chiari与Salter截骨术奠定现代管理基础。
- 临床启示：理解DDH的历史演进有助于认识现代新生儿筛查、早期诊断和预防性治疗原则的形成逻辑，强调将解剖、临床与影像学方法整合为循证预防体系的重要性。

**原文摘要**：To review the historical evolution of developmental dysplasia of the hip (DDH), tracing the progressive development of its anatomical, clinical, diagnostic, and therapeutic concepts from prehistoric evidence to contemporary practice. An extensive electronic search of the medical and historical literature was performed and complemented by manual review of archaeological, artistic, literary, and scientific sources. Historical reports describing congenital hip dislocation, neonatal instability, diagnostic methods, and treatment strategies were critically analysed within their scientific, cultural, and chronological contexts. The history of DDH represents a continuous process of conceptual evolution rather than a succession of isolated discoveries. Palaeopathological evidence demonstrates the antiquity of the disorder, while the Hippocratic Corpus first distinguished congenital from traumatic hip dislocation. This knowledge was preserved through late antiquity and the medieval period before anatomical investigations by Ambroise Paré, Theodor Kerckring, Giovanni Battista Palletta, Guillame Dupuytren, and Willem Vrolik established its structural and pathological basis. During the nineteenth century, Wilhelm Roser and Friedrich Trendelenburg transformed DDH into a functional clinical disorder, whereas the introduction of radiography established its clinic-radiological identity. In the twentieth century, pioneering contributions by Codivilla, Putti, Hilgenreiner, Ortolani, and Barlow shifted attention towards neonatal instability, early diagnosis, and preventive treatment. Subsequently, Graf's ultrasonographic classification revolutionised imaging, while the Pavlik harness, and modern reconstructive surgery, such as the Chiari and Salter osteotomies, established the foundations of contemporary DDH management. The historical evolution of DDH illustrates the progressive integration of empirical observation, anatomy, clinical examination, imaging, and surgery into a preventive, evidence-based discipline. Understanding this historical pathway provides valuable perspective on the principles that continue to guide modern diagnosis, treatment, and neonatal screening.
