**原文摘要**：Congenital lung malformations (CLMs) are often identified on antenatal scanning and malignant transformation is cited as an indication for early resection. Long-term follow-up of asymptomatic lesions managed nonoperatively is lacking. Herein, we prospectively follow up children with antenatally identified CLMs and reviewed adult patients diagnosed with adenocarcinoma to look for background CLM changes. This is a single-center cohort study of pediatric patients with antenatal or early postnatal diagnosis of CLMs and a retrospective review of adult patients with lung adenocarcinoma. In children, data collected included type or location of CLMs and subsequent follow-up. Homozygosity mapping and logarithm of odds (LOD) score calculations were performed using single nucleotide polymorphism (SNP) array data. Whole-exome and whole-genome sequencing data were analyzed to identify candidate variants. Various analysis and interpretation tools were applied to evaluate and describe potential disease-associated variants. Homozygosity mapping identified a 2 Mb candidate locus on chromosome 21q22.3. Multipoint linkage analysis identified an overlapping locus on chromosome 21 (maximal LOD score: 3.168). A missense variant in PCNT (Gln1951Pro), along with several other segregating candidates, was evaluated. Our findings demonstrate a novel candidate locus at chromosome 21q22.3 associated with isolated IM, with PCNT emerging as a potential candidate gene. Further studies involving additional affected families and functional validation are required to establish the contribution of this locus and the identified variant to the pathogenesis of isolated IM.
