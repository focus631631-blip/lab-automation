## Esophageal atresia and co-occurring major anomalies in Denmark 1994-2021: A nationwide register-based study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:42
- 期刊: Eur J Pediatr Surg
- 国家/单位: Copenhagen · Copenhagen University Hospital Rigshospitalet
- 通讯作者: Christiansen M
- DOI: 10.1055/a-3003-6057
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1055/a-3003-6057) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43039353/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估丹麦1994-2021年食管闭锁（EA）出生患病率趋势、共存重大先天畸形谱及异常定义对注册估计的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：在1,696,357名筛查婴儿中诊断442例EA，出生患病率为2.61/10,000筛查活产（95%CI 2.37-2.86），年增长约1.6%（95%CI 0.4-2.9%，p=0.0073）。18.3%为综合征型；非综合征型中47.6%至少合并1种重大畸形，最常见为心血管畸形。排除房/室间隔缺损后，心脏畸形总体记录为8.8%（综合征型28.4%、非综合征型4.4%）。
- 临床启示：非综合征型EA近半数合并其他重大畸形，支持系统化多系统评估；房/室间隔缺损是否纳入会显著影响心脏畸形估计，注册研究比较时需注意。

**原文摘要**：Esophageal atresia (EA) frequently co-occurs with additional major congenital anomalies. Long-term national trends and the influence of anomaly definitions on registry estimates remain incompletely characterized. We conducted a nationwide, register-based cohort study of infants born in Den-mark during 1994-2021, who participated in the neonatal screening program, which has near-complete coverage. Infants were identified using ICD-10-DK codes in linked national registries. Associated major congenital anomalies were classified with reference to EURO-CAT guidance. Temporal trends were assessed using Poisson regression models. Pheno-types were classified as syndromic or non-syndromic; non-syndromic cases were further classified as isolated or with co-occurring major anomalies. Among 1,696,357 screened infants, 442 were diagnosed with EA, corresponding to a birth prevalence of 2.61 per 10,000 screened live births (95% CI 2.37 - 2.86). EA preva-lence increased by an estimated 1.6% annually (95% CI 0.4-2.9%; p=0.0073). Syndromic EA accounted for 18.3% of cases, with relatively stable proportions over time. Among non-syndromic cases, 47.6% had at least one co-occurring major anomaly, most commonly car-diovascular anomalies. After excluding atrial and ventricular septal defects, cardiac anoma-lies were recorded in 8.8% overall (28.4% of syndromic and 4.4% of non-syndromic cases). EA prevalence among screened live-born infants was consistent with international estimates and showed a modest model-based increase over time. Nearly half of non-syndromic infants had an additional major anomaly, supporting systematic multisystem assessment. Cardiac-anomaly estimates were strongly influenced by inclusion of atrial and ventricular septal de-fects, which should be considered when comparing registry studies.

## Antimicrobial Management of Surgical Necrotizing Enterocolitis: A Multi-Institutional Analysis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:42
- 期刊: J Surg Res · 中科院医学3区(外科4区) · IF 1.6 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: Pennsylvania · University of Pittsburgh Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Waltz P (waltzpk2@upmc.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jss.2026.10.043
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jss.2026.10.043) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43038844/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估外科坏死性小肠结肠炎患儿抗菌药物管理实践与临床结局之间的关联。
- 核心发现/数据：这项10家儿童医院2010—2019年的回顾性队列纳入306例新生儿（中位胎龄26周、出生体重850 g、诊断年龄8.5周），初始和术后分别使用94种和107种方案、涉及15种抗菌药物，67%的患儿围术期方案不同；最常用为氨苄西林、克林霉素、庆大霉素和万古霉素，34%于本次住院期间死亡。没有任何初始抗菌药物与死亡率相关；术后抗生素疗程>7天与<7天相比未显著改善院内生存（OR 0.86，P=0.93），术后抗菌药物选择与肠内自主、造口并发症、神经发育损害或胆汁淤积均无显著关联；抗真菌药使用与晚期死亡风险升高相关（OR 5.22，P=0.023），可能反映基础疾病严重程度。
- 临床启示：外科坏死性小肠结肠炎的抗菌药物管理差异很大，尚无单一药物或更长疗程被证实改善生存，临床应避免无依据延长疗程并加强抗菌药物规范化管理。仍需前瞻性随机研究明确最佳抗感染方案。

**原文摘要**：Evidence-based antimicrobial regimens are lacking for surgical necrotizing enterocolitis. We evaluated the association between antimicrobial practices and outcomes. We conducted a retrospective cohort analysis of infants with surgical necrotizing enterocolitis (2010-2019) at ten children's hospitals. Demographics, antimicrobial choice, and postoperative outcomes were assessed using multivariable regression. Among 306 neonates (median gestational age 26 wk [interquartile range 25-29], birth weight 850 g [680-1100], age at diagnosis 8.5 wk [3-21]), 15 antimicrobials were used in 94 initial regimens. Postoperatively, 15 were administered in 107 regimens. Pre- and postoperative regimens differed in 67%. The most frequently used antibiotics were ampicillin, clindamycin, gentamicin, and vancomycin. Antifungals were often given to those with lower weight at diagnosis. 34% died during the index admission. No initial antimicrobial agent was associated with mortality. Excluding early deaths, patients received a median of 14 d of postoperative antibiotics. The duration of postoperative antibiotics (>7 d versus < 7 d) was not significantly associated with in-hospital mortality (odds ratio 0.86, P = 0.93). Adjusted regression analyses demonstrated no associations between postoperative antimicrobial choice and enteral autonomy, stoma complications, neurodevelopmental impairment, or cholestasis. Antifungal use was associated with higher odds of late mortality (odds ratio 5.22, P = 0.023), likely secondary to underlying illness severity. Antimicrobial management varied widely. No individual antimicrobial agent improved survival; however, binary analysis of antimicrobial exposure limited conclusions. Longer antimicrobial duration was not associated with improved survival. Prospective randomized studies may help define optimal antibiotic management.
