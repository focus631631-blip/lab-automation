## Comparison of outcomes after transcatheter patent ductus arteriosus closure in infants weighing ≤ 1.5 kg and > 1.5-4 kg.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:30
- 期刊: Eur J Pediatr · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区) · IF 2.9 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 英国 · Royal Brompton & Harefield NHS Foundation Trust
- 通讯作者: Fraisse A (alain.fraisse@nhs.net)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00431-026-07581-3
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-026-07581-3) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42955400/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估≤4 kg婴儿经导管PDA封堵的结局，并比较体重≤1.5 kg与>1.5–4 kg两组的安全性和有效性。
- 核心发现/数据：这项2016—2024年国际多中心回顾性研究纳入358例婴儿（≤1.5 kg组191例，>1.5–4 kg组167例；中位体重1.48 kg，93.6%为早产儿）。≤1.5 kg组手术成功率更高（98.4% vs 94.0%，p=0.044），而>1.5–4 kg组主要不良事件更多（9.6% vs 3.1%，p=0.0144；OR=3.27）。装置栓塞仅见于>1.5–4 kg组（5.4% vs 0%，p<0.001；OR=22.96），且该组Amplatzer Piccolo封堵器使用率较低（69.5% vs 97.3%）。
- 临床启示：经导管PDA封堵在≤4 kg婴儿中总体安全有效，但>1.5–4 kg婴儿主要不良事件及装置栓塞风险更高，需更谨慎评估解剖条件并合理选择器械。专用器械的进一步应用可能改善该人群安全性。

**原文摘要**：Transcatheter closure of patent ductus arteriosus (PDA) with the Amplatzer Piccolo Occluder is safe and effective in infants ≤ 2 kg. In larger infants, off-label devices are often required, but outcome data are limited. We evaluated outcomes in infants ≤ 4 kg, comparing the ≤ 1.5 and > 1.5-4 kg groups. This retrospective, international, multicenter study included 358 infants who underwent transcatheter PDA closure at 5 tertiary centers between 2016 and 2024: 191 weighed ≤ 1.5 kg (group 1) and 167 > 1.5-4 kg (group 2). Median age was 39 days (interquartile range 25-61) and median weight was 1.48 (1.1-2.2) kg; 93.6% were preterm. Procedural success was higher in group 1 (98.4% vs 94.0%; p = 0.044). Major adverse events were more frequent in group 2 (9.6% vs 3.1%; p = 0.0144; odds ratio = 3.27; 95% confidence interval, 1.25-8.55). Device embolization occurred only in group 2 (5.4% vs 0%; p < 0.001; odds ratio = 22.96; 95% confidence interval, 1.33-397.51). The Amplatzer Piccolo Occluder was used in 97.3% of group 1 vs 69.5% of group 2. Transcatheter PDA closure is safe and effective in infants ≤ 4 kg, but the > 1.5-4 kg group has a higher risk of major adverse events, particularly device embolization. Better patient selection and dedicated devices may improve safety. • Transcatheter PDA closure with the Amplatzer Piccolo Occluder is safe and effective in very small infants. • In larger infants, alternative devices are often used off-label, but evidence on procedural outcomes remains limited. • Infants weighing >1.5-4 kg had lower procedural success and more major adverse events than those weighing ≤1.5 kg. • Device embolization occurred exclusively in the >1.5-4 kg group, supporting careful anatomical assessment and the development of dedicated devices.

## Early echocardiographic hemodynamic phenotypes and clinical outcomes in congenital diaphragmatic hernia.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:30
- 期刊: Eur J Pediatr · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区) · IF 2.9 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 韩国 · Asan Medical Center Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Kim SH (soohyun@amc.seoul.kr)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00431-026-07559-1
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-026-07559-1) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42955372/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估先天性膈疝（CDH）患儿出生24小时内超声心动图定义的血流动力学表型是否与心肺严重程度及ECMO或死亡相关。
- 核心发现/数据：66例患儿中，无/轻度肺高压21例、右室为主型肺高压22例、左室受累型肺高压23例，ECMO或死亡发生率分别为0%、27.3%、65.2%（P<0.001）。左室受累型肺高压较右室为主型肺高压呼吸严重程度更高、左室功能障碍更常见，但两组O/E LHR无显著差异。校正O/E LHR后，表型严重程度分级仍与ECMO或死亡相关（OR 5.98，95%CI 1.76-20.31，P=0.004），但左室受累型与右室为主型肺高压比较不再有统计学意义（OR 3.42，95%CI 0.87-15.10）。
- 临床启示：出生早期超声心动图血流动力学表型可用于识别CDH高危患儿，左室受累型肺高压提示高风险，可能补充产前解剖学风险评估。但其超越产前肺大小的独立预后价值仍需进一步验证。

**原文摘要**：Early postnatal hemodynamic adaptation may contribute to outcome heterogeneity in congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH). We evaluated whether echocardiography-defined hemodynamic phenotypes within 24 h of birth were associated with cardiorespiratory severity and extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO) or death. This retrospective cohort included infants with CDH admitted to a tertiary neonatal intensive care unit from 2021 to 2025 who underwent echocardiography within 24 h. Phenotypes were classified as no/mild pulmonary hypertension (PH), right ventricular (RV)-predominant PH, or left ventricular (LV)-involved PH based on ventricular function and ductal/atrial shunt direction. Associations with prenatal severity, respiratory indices, and ECMO or death were assessed. Among 66 infants, 21 had no/mild PH, 22 RV-predominant PH, and 23 LV-involved PH. ECMO or death occurred in 0%, 27.3%, and 65.2%, respectively (P < 0.001). Compared with RV-predominant PH, LV-involved PH was associated with greater respiratory severity and more frequent LV dysfunction; observed-to-expected lung-to-head ratio (O/E LHR) did not differ significantly between these two groups. Ordinal phenotype severity remained associated with ECMO or death after adjustment for O/E LHR (odds ratio (OR), 5.98; 95% confidence interval (CI), 1.76-20.31; P = 0.004). However, the comparison between LV-involved and RV-predominant PH was no longer statistically significant after adjustment for O/E LHR (OR, 3.42; 95% CI, 0.87-15.10).  Early hemodynamic phenotype was associated with cardiorespiratory severity and ECMO or death in CDH. LV-involved PH identified a high-risk phenotype and may complement prenatal anatomic risk stratification, although its independent prognostic value beyond prenatal lung size requires validation. • Prenatal lung size and liver herniation are established markers of disease severity and outcome in congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH). • Pulmonary hypertension and ventricular dysfunction contribute substantially to early cardiorespiratory instability and adverse outcomes in infants with CDH. • Early echocardiographic phenotypes were associated with cardiorespiratory severity and ECMO or death. • LV-involved PH identified a high-risk phenotype, although its prognostic value beyond prenatal severity remains uncertain.

## Kangaroo mother care in neonates after surgery: a scoping review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:30
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Beijing · Beijing Tsinghua Changgung Hospital
- 通讯作者: Wang H (wanghaoyue1211@163.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06696-w
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06696-w) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42955355/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：梳理新生儿术后应用袋鼠式母亲护理的现有证据，总结其干预组成、实施方法、结局指标、疗效及安全性，为临床实践和未来研究提供参考。
- 核心发现/数据：从4050篇文献中最终纳入12篇，包括6项随机对照试验、2项准实验研究、1项病例报告、1项质量改进项目、1项横断面研究和1项定性文献综述。研究提示，新生儿术后袋鼠式母亲护理有助于稳定生命体征、减轻疼痛、促进肠功能更早恢复并缩短住院时间；干预期间需持续监测心率、血氧饱和度和呼吸频率以确保安全。现有证据仍处于探索阶段。
- 临床启示：在新生儿外科病房开展袋鼠式母亲护理时，应标准化干预方案、优化结局测量并加强安全监测，以支持其结构化实施。

**原文摘要**：To map the available evidence on the application of kangaroo mother care in neonates after surgery, and to summarize its intervention components, implementation methods, outcome measures, efficacy, and safety, thereby informing clinical practice and future research. A systematic search was conducted in PubMed, CINAHL, CNKI, and Wanfang Database from inception to 7 November 2025. Empirical studies reporting the use of kangaroo mother care in postoperative neonatal populations were included, without restriction by study design. Data on intervention protocols, implementation methods, outcome measures, and safety-related events were extracted and collated. In total, 12 articles were selected from a pool of 4050 documents.They are six randomized controlled trials, two quasi-experimental studies, one case report, one quality improvement project, one cross-sectional study, and one qualitative literature review. The findings indicated that kangaroo mother care in neonates after surgery contributed to stabilized vital signs, reduced pain, earlier recovery of bowel function, and shorter hospital stays. Continuous monitoring of heart rate, oxygen saturation, and respiratory rate was identified as essential for safety during the intervention. Kangaroo mother care appears to offer several clinical benefits for neonates following surgery, although the evidence remains in an exploratory phase. Future research should prioritize standardizing intervention protocols, optimizing outcome measurement, and strengthening safety monitoring to support the structured implementation of this care practice in surgical neonatal units.

## Sequential therapy with lateral ventricular continuous external drainage combined with contralateral Ommaya reservoir for severe neonatal intraventricular hemorrhage and posthemorrhagic hydrocephalus: a retrospective controlled study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:30
- 期刊: Childs Nerv Syst
- 国家/单位: Nanchang · Jiangxi Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Zhang XH (31813791@qq.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00381-026-07514-3
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00381-026-07514-3) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42955347/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：比较侧脑室持续外引流联合对侧Ommaya储液囊植入的序贯双通道疗法与单纯侧脑室外引流治疗新生儿重度（III-IV级）脑室内出血合并出血后脑积水的临床疗效与安全性。
- 核心发现/数据：观察组外引流持续时间显著短于对照组（3.8±1.2 d vs. 5.5±1.5 d，P=0.001），脑脊液白细胞、蛋白和葡萄糖恢复正常时间均显著缩短（均P<0.001）。术后10-30天影像显示脑室明显缩小率观察组为92.3%（24/26）、对照组为70.0%（7/10）；随访12-18个月观察组无永久性脑室腹腔分流，对照组为1/10（10.0%，P=0.28），总并发症发生率分别为3.8%（1/26）和20.0%（2/10，P=0.15）。
- 临床启示：该序贯双通道疗法对重度新生儿脑室内出血合并出血后脑积水安全有效，可缩短外引流及脑脊液恢复时间，并呈现降低并发症和永久分流需求的趋势；但样本量较小且部分差异未达统计学显著，仍需更大样本研究验证。

**原文摘要**：To compare the clinical efficacy and safety of sequential dual-channel therapy (lateral ventricular continuous external drainage combined with contralateral Ommaya reservoir implantation) versus single lateral ventricular external drainage in neonates with severe (grade III-IV) intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH) complicated by posthemorrhagic hydrocephalus (PHH). This retrospective historically controlled study included 36 neonates with grade III-IV IVH and progressive hydrocephalus treated at the Department of Neurosurgery, Jiangxi Children's Hospital, between January 2016 and December 2024. Patients were divided into a control group (January 2016-December 2018, single lateral ventricular continuous external drainage, n = 10) and an observation group (January 2019-December 2024, external drainage combined with contralateral Ommaya reservoir implantation, n = 26). The duration of external drainage, time to normalization of cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) parameters, ventricular size reduction on imaging, complication rates, and long-term need for permanent ventriculoperitoneal shunt (VPS) were compared. The duration of external drainage was significantly shorter in the observation group than in the control group (3.8 ± 1.2 d vs. 5.5 ± 1.5 d, t = 3.55, P = 0.001). Times to normalization of CSF white blood cell count, protein, and glucose were all significantly shorter in the observation group (t = 5.30, 7.42, and 6.47, respectively; all P < 0.001). Cranial imaging at 10-30 days postoperatively showed a substantial ventricular reduction rate of 92.3% (24/26) in the observation group versus 70.0% (7/10) in the control group. After 12-18 months of follow-up, none of the 26 patients in the observation group required permanent VPS, whereas 1 of 10 (10.0%) in the control group underwent shunt placement (Fisher's exact P = 0.28). The overall complication rate was 3.8% (1/26) in the observation group versus 20.0% (2/10) in the control group (Fisher's exact P = 0.15). Sequential therapy with lateral ventricular continuous external drainage combined with contralateral Ommaya reservoir implantation is safe and effective for neonates with severe IVH complicated by PHH. This strategy significantly shortens the duration of external drainage and CSF recovery time, with a trend toward lower complication rates and a reduced need for permanent shunt surgery, suggesting clinical advantages compared with external drainage alone.

## Application of transanal intestinal tube placement in ileostomy closure for post-NEC enterostomy patients: complication spectrum shift from severe to manageable minor complications.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:30
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Children's Hospital Affiliated to Zhengzhou University
- 通讯作者: Wang X (wangxianl2009@163.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06703-0
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06703-0) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42955092/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估既往坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）肠造口患儿行回肠造口关闭术时，辅助经肛肠管置入（TITP）是否改善术后结局。
- 核心发现/数据：TITP组住院时间更短（9.6±2.2 vs. 12.5±3.1 d；β=-3.37，P<0.001），肠内营养启动及达到全肠内营养更早（P<0.001），手术时间约延长7分钟（P=0.05）。总体并发症发生率相似，但Clavien-Madadi并发症谱发生转移（P=0.004）：IIIb级事件占并发症事件的比例由47%降至19%（OR=0.27），I级肛周并发症由8%增至35%；吻合口漏无显著差异（P=0.38；把握度0.14）。
- 临床启示：在NEC肠造口关闭术中辅以TITP可能加速肠内营养恢复、缩短住院并将并发症谱转向更轻微且可管理的事件；但受回顾性时间分期设计限制且吻合口漏分析把握度不足，尚不能作因果推断，需前瞻性研究验证。

**原文摘要**：To assess whether adjunctive transanal intestinal tube placement (TITP) during ileostomy closure improves postoperative outcomes in infants with a prior necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) enterostomy. We retrospectively analyzed 187 infants undergoing elective NEC ileostomy closure (2020-2025). Using December 2022 as the cut-off, 92 received closure alone and 95 closure plus TITP. Length of stay, enteral nutrition (EN) recovery, operative time, and complications were compared; propensity score matching (PSM) and a near-time-window subgroup analysis addressed confounding. The TITP group had shorter stay (9.6 ± 2.2 vs. 12.5 ± 3.1 d; β = -3.37, P < 0.001), earlier EN initiation and full EN (P < 0.001), and ~ 7 min longer operative time (P = 0.05). Complication rates were similar; the Clavien-Madadi spectrum shifted (P = 0.004): grade IIIb events accounted for 19% vs. 47% of complication events (OR = 0.27) and grade I perianal complications increased (35% vs. 8%). Anastomotic leakage did not differ (P = 0.38; power 0.14). TITP was associated with a shift of the complication spectrum toward milder events-from grade IIIb to grade I manageable skin complications-and faster EN recovery and shorter stay, but the anastomotic-leakage finding is underpowered and the time-period design precludes causal inference.

## Treatment of Delayed Meconium Passage in Preterm Infants: A Systematic Review of Timing and Effect.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:30
- 期刊: Acta Paediatr · 中科院医学4区(儿科4区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 荷兰 · Amalia Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Hogeveen M
- DOI: 10.1111/apa.70804
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1111/apa.70804) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42954135/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统评价早产儿胎粪排出延迟时预防性与治疗性干预的时机及不同泻药的有效性和安全性，并重点关注治疗相关副作用。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入6项中至高偏倚风险研究，共956名早产儿；各研究在胎粪排出时间及治疗副作用方面结果不一致；目前无法就治疗时机（预防性 vs 治疗性）或泻药类型作出循证推荐。
- 临床启示：现有证据不足以支持对早产儿胎粪排出延迟常规采用预防性泻药或特定治疗时机/药物选择；临床应个体化评估并警惕治疗相关副作用，等待更高质量研究。

**原文摘要**：Meconium passage is often delayed in preterm infants and assumed to be related to delayed adaptation to enteral nutrition, meconium-related ileus, necrotizing enterocolitis, intestinal perforation and sepsis. While a definition for indication of treatment is lacking, strategies can be divided between a prophylactic approach: treatment is initiated within the first 48 h after birth irrespective of clinical symptoms, or a therapeutic approach: treatment is not initiated for at least 48 h after birth, and only indicated if clinical symptoms develop. This study systematically reviewed the efficacy and safety of prophylactic versus therapeutic treatments and compared various laxatives, with a focus on treatment-related side effects. Literature search was conducted in four databases up to 19 July 2025. Participants included preterm infants; studies were limited to rectal strategies and excluded pathological intestinal obstructions. The review was conducted according to standards for systematic reviews and registered prior to commencement. Six studies with moderate to high risk of bias were included (956 infants). The studies showed inconsistent findings regarding the timing of meconium passage and varying side effects of the treatments. No evidence-based recommendations can be made regarding timing of treatment or laxative type.

## Expanding the molecular and phenotypic spectrum of MMP2-related multicentric osteolysis, nodulosis, and arthropathy: two brothers with marked intrafamilial variability and a review of the literature.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:30
- 期刊: Mol Biol Rep · 中科院生物学4区(生化与分子生物学4区) · IF 3.2 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 伊朗 · Genomic Research Center
- 通讯作者: Sayad A (ar.sayad@sbmu.ac.ir)
- DOI: 10.1007/s11033-026-13024-x
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11033-026-13024-x) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42955438/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：描述一对分子确诊为MMP2相关多中心性骨溶解、结节病和关节病（MONA）的兄弟并复习MMP2变异谱，以扩展其分子和表型谱。
- 核心发现/数据：两兄弟（14岁和8岁，近亲婚配家系）均携带同一纯合MMP2致病性变异c.1648 C>T（p.Arg550Ter），但存在显著家族内表型差异：哥哥有严重足踝畸形、广泛足底结节和行走明显受限，弟弟骨骼表型较轻但出现视神经受累及颅内压升高；两人均有新生儿肝胆异常，哥哥确诊胆道闭锁并出现提示非经典先天性肾上腺增生的内分泌表型，但未检出CYP21A2致病性变异。间歇性唑来膦酸治疗改善了两人的骨密度，但已形成的骨骼畸形持续存在；文献复习显示变异类型或蛋白结构域位置不能单独预测骨骼外表型。
- 临床启示：MONA患者需进行包括肝胆、内分泌、眼科及颅内压在内的多系统评估，并警惕同一家系内显著表型异质性；双膦酸盐可改善骨密度但难以逆转已形成的畸形，仍需更大规模纵向分子确诊队列明确基因型-表型相关性。

**原文摘要**：Multicentric osteolysis, nodulosis, and arthropathy (MONA) is a rare autosomal recessive skeletal dysplasia caused by biallelic MMP2 pathogenic variants. We describe two brothers with molecularly confirmed MONA and review the MMP2 variant spectrum. Clinical, radiological, biochemical, and genetic findings were evaluated in two brothers, aged 14 and 8 years, from a consanguineous family. Whole-exome sequencing, Sanger sequencing and segregation analysis established the diagnosis. Published MMP2 variants were reviewed for phenotypic comparison. Both brothers carried the same homozygous pathogenic MMP2 variant, c.1648 C > T (p.Arg550Ter), confirming MONA. Both demonstrated skeletal manifestations, including osteopenia, progressive distal osteolysis, hand and foot deformities, and joint contractures, with marked intrafamilial variability: the elder brother developed severe foot and ankle deformities, extensive plantar nodulosis, and substantial limitation of ambulation, whereas the younger brother had a milder skeletal course but developed optic nerve involvement with raised intracranial pressure. Both had neonatal hepatobiliary abnormalities, although biliary atresia was confirmed only in the elder brother, who also developed an endocrine phenotype suggestive of non-classic congenital adrenal hyperplasia despite no pathogenic CYP21A2 variant on re-analysis. Intermittent zoledronic acid therapy was associated with improvement in bone mineral density in both brothers, although established skeletal deformities persisted. Review of variants showed clinical heterogeneity, with variant type or protein-domain location alone unable to predict extraskeletal manifestations. These brothers illustrate intrafamilial phenotypic variability in MMP2-related MONA, extending beyond the skeletal system to hepatobiliary, endocrine, and ophthalmologic findings whose relationship to MMP2 deficiency remains uncertain. Larger, longitudinal, molecularly confirmed cohorts are needed to clarify genotype-phenotype correlations.

**原文摘要**：Early diagnosis of Biliary atresia (BA) improves native liver and overall survival. Pale stools are a characteristic symptom and might be detected by deep learning image recognition. We tested convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to classify pictures of pale and healthy stools and compare the results with stool color card (SCC) assessment of health care professionals (HCP) and non-health care professionals (NHCP). One thousand six hundred and sixteen stool photos with diagnostic labels including BA and healthy controls were graded as pale or normal according to the SCC. A subset of photos (33 BA and 241 healthy) was presented to HCP and NHCP for SCC assessment to evaluate accuracy and interobserver reliability. Different visualization strategies and secondary data augmentation were used for explainability and estimating robustness. In SCC assessment, sensitivity by NHCP trended lower than HCP (86.4% vs. 97.3%), while there were no differences in specificity. Intra-cohort agreement on classification of BA stools (pale vs. normal) was also lower for NHCP than for HCP (36.4% vs. 78.8%), while no significant difference was observed in healthy stools. The selected CNN (ResNet18) correctly classified 97.5% of pale and 98.5% of normal labeled stools, also showing robust results in explainability methods. The high accuracy of CNN shows the potential for a digital stool color screening. In contrast, NHCP assessment of pale stools tends to be inconsistent, and there is high interobserver variability among HCP and NHCP.

## Trends in adoption of laparoscopy for the Ladd's procedure: a multicenter analysis.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估10家儿童医院2014—2020年儿童肠旋转不良Ladd手术中腹腔镜使用趋势。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入638例，182例（28.5%）尝试腹腔镜，456例（71.5%）开腹；2014—2020年腹腔镜尝试率由19.6%升至40.3%（p=0.007），中转开腹率为27.3%—41.9%。无术前肠扭转的504例中159例（31.5%）尝试腹腔镜、108例（67.9%）完成，中转率随时间稳定（p=0.895）；术前肠扭转的131例中22例（16.8%）尝试、7例（31.8%）完成，中转率有波动（p=0.159）。
- 临床启示：腹腔镜在Ladd手术中的应用呈上升趋势，但中转开腹率仍较高且未随时间改善；临床应结合术前是否合并肠扭转等因素谨慎选择腹腔镜入路并做好中转开腹准备。

**原文摘要**：We sought to evaluate contemporary trends in utilization of laparoscopy for the Ladd's procedure across 10 children's hospitals. Pediatric patients ≤ 15 years of age with malrotation who underwent a Ladd's procedure across 10 children's hospitals between 1/2014 and 12/2020 were retrospectively reviewed. Patient demographics, preoperative imaging, and operative details were collected. The primary outcome was temporal change in the utilization of laparoscopy. A total of 638 patients were included: laparoscopy was attempted in 182 (28.5%) and the remaining 456 (71.5%) were performed open. The median age of all patients was 3.0 months [IQR 0.3, 12.0] and median weight was 5.1 kg [IQR 3.3, 10.9]. From 2014 to 2020, attempted laparoscopic Ladd's increased from 19.6% to 40.3% (p = 0.007); conversion to open rates ranged from 27.3% to 41.9%. Among 504 patients without preoperatively diagnosed volvulus, laparoscopic Ladd's was attempted in 159 cases (31.5%) and completed laparoscopic in 108 (67.9%); conversion rates were stable over time (p = 0.895). Of 131 patients with preoperatively diagnosed volvulus, laparoscopic Ladd's was attempted in 22 cases (16.8%) and completed laparoscopically in 7 (31.8%); rates of conversion varied (p = 0.159). Utilization of laparoscopy for the Ladd procedure is increasing for malrotation; however, conversion rates remain high and unchanged.
