## Associations of personalized prenatal care with delivery and perinatal outcomes in women with high-risk pregnancies.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:46
- 期刊: Medicine (Baltimore) · 中科院医学4区(医学：内科4区) · IF 2.0 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Shijiazhuang Maternal and Child Health Hospital
- 通讯作者: Zhang L
- DOI: 10.1097/MD.0000000000051549
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000051549) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43063094/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估高危妊娠女性接受个性化产前保健与分娩方式及围产结局之间的关联。
- 核心发现/数据：倾向性匹配后每组各50例，个性化产前保健组阴道分娩率更高（62.0% vs 40.0%，OR 2.45，95%CI 1.10-5.47，P=0.028），剖宫产率更低（28.0% vs 48.0%，OR 0.42）；早产率更低（10.0% vs 26.0%，OR 0.32，P=0.037），1分钟和5分钟Apgar评分更高（P=0.026和P=0.006），复合不良围产结局发生率更低（16.0% vs 36.0%，OR 0.34，95%CI 0.13-0.88，P=0.023）。该研究为回顾性观察性研究，结果代表关联而非因果，且样本量小、存在残余混杂及部分结局统计效能有限。
- 临床启示：个性化产前保健可能与本组高危妊娠女性更高的阴道分娩率、更低的早产及不良围产结局相关，可作为优化产前管理策略的参考；但需更大样本和因果设计研究验证后再推广。

**原文摘要**：This study examines whether the receipt of personalized prenatal care was associated with delivery and perinatal outcomes among women with high-risk pregnancies in a retrospective observational cohort. This single-center retrospective cohort study included 100 women with high-risk pregnancies who delivered between January 2020 and December 2023. Participants were classified according to documented receipt of personalized prenatal care or routine prenatal care. Propensity scores were estimated using maternal age, gravidity, parity, pre pregnancy or early-pregnancy body mass index, gestational age at registration, gestational diabetes mellitus, hypertensive disorders of pregnancy, thyroid dysfunction, uterine scar, and history of adverse pregnancy outcomes. One-to-one nearest-neighbor matching without replacement was performed using a caliper of 0.2 standard deviations of the logit of the propensity score. All 100 women were retained after matching. Outcomes were analyzed using unadjusted independent-group comparisons (50 women per group). Vaginal delivery was more frequent in the personalized-care group than in the routine-care group (62.0% vs 40.0%), corresponding to an absolute difference of +22.0 percentage points (95% confidence interval [CI], +2.4- + 39.3) and an odds ratio (OR) of 2.45 (95% CI, 1.10-5.47; P = .028). Cesarean delivery occurred in 28.0% and 48.0% of women, respectively, corresponding to an absolute difference of -20.0 percentage points (95% CI, -37.1-- 1.0) and an OR of 0.42 (95% CI, 0.18-0.97). Preterm birth occurred in 10.0% versus 26.0% of women (absolute difference, -16.0 percentage points; 95% CI, -30.7- - 0.8; OR, 0.32; 95% CI, 0.10-0.97; P = .037). One and 5-minute Apgar scores were higher in the personalized-care group (P = .026 and P = .006, respectively). The composite adverse perinatal outcome, defined as the occurrence of at least one of preterm birth, low birth weight, neonatal asphyxia, or neonatal intensive care unit admission, occurred in 16.0% versus 36.0% of women (absolute difference, -20.0 percentage points; 95% CI, -35.8- - 2.8; OR, 0.34; 95% CI, 0.13-0.88; P = .023). In this study, the receipt of personalized prenatal care was associated with differences in delivery patterns, selected neonatal outcomes, nursing-compliance measures, and end-of-care psychological scores. The findings represent associations rather than causal effects and should be interpreted in light of the small sample size, residual confounding, and limited statistical power for several individual outcomes.

## Lung ultrasound in congenital heart disease: from physiology to precision medicine.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:46
- 期刊: Eur J Pediatr · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区) · IF 2.9 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: Madrid · Hospital General Universitario Gregorio Marañón
- 通讯作者: Gregorio-Hernández R (rebeca.gregorio@salud.madrid.org)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00431-026-07615-w
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-026-07615-w) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43062502/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：总结肺超声在先天性心脏病（CHD）患儿术前、围术期及术后呼吸并发症评估与管理中的现有证据，并从病理生理角度探讨其与心脏生理的联系。
- 核心发现/数据：CHD约影响8/1000活产儿，其中约25%为危重CHD，需早期诊断和新生儿干预。LUS是一种床旁、无辐射的实时成像技术，可评估肺通气、间质水肿、胸腔积液、肺不张和气胸，且CHD相关肺部异常随血流动力学状态在围术期动态变化。现有证据支持LUS用于围术期和术后，但其临床应用仍不统一、尚未常规整合，需前瞻性研究建立标准化方案并验证LUS指导管理能否改善临床结局。
- 临床启示：LUS有望作为CHD患儿床旁动态监测工具，结合临床和超声心动图评估实现肺表型分型，辅助精准呼吸管理。临床应用中需关注其解读陷阱，并推动标准化协议和结局验证。

**原文摘要**：Congenital heart disease (CHD) affects approximately 8 per 1000 births worldwide. Approximately 25% are classified as critical congenital heart disease (CCHD), requiring early diagnosis and neonatal intervention. Management of these patients is complex, dynamic and multiparametric. Respiratory complications are common during the hospitalization of patients with CHD. Lung ultrasound (LUS) has emerged as a valuable bedside tool to accurately assess lung aeration, particularly in critically ill patients in whom conventional imaging techniques may be difficult to obtain, impractical, or challenging to interpret. Although growing evidence supports the use of LUS throughout the perioperative and postoperative period, its application remains insufficiently characterized. LUS is evolving beyond its traditional role as a diagnostic imaging modality toward a dynamic bedside monitoring tool, with the potential to guide and optimize respiratory management before, during, and after cardiac surgery. This review aims to summarize the current evidence regarding the use of LUS in this population and to highlight its potential role in the assessment and management of preoperative and postoperative respiratory complications. • Lung ultrasound (LUS) is a bedside, radiation-free imaging technique that provides real-time information on pulmonary aeration, interstitial edema, pleural effusion, atelectasis, and pneumothorax in children with congenital heart disease (CHD). • Pulmonary abnormalities in CHD are closely related to the underlying hemodynamic physiology and may change dynamically throughout the preoperative, perioperative, and postoperative periods. However, the clinical application of LUS in this population remains heterogeneous and is not yet routinely integrated into CHD management. • This review summarizes the current evidence on LUS in CHD from a pathophysiological perspective, linking ultrasound findings to the underlying cardiac physiology and highlighting potential pitfalls in their interpretation. • LUS may contribute to a precision-medicine approach in children with CHD by integrating bedside pulmonary phenotyping with clinical and echocardiographic assessment, while identifying key areas where prospective studies are needed to establish standardized protocols and determine whether LUS-guided management improves clinically relevant outcomes.

## Hepatic Macrophages Depletion by Clodronate Liposomes Alleviates RRV-Induced Biliary Injury via Targeting the Notch3/PPAR Signalling Pathway.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 12:07
- 期刊: Immunology · 中科院医学3区(免疫学3区) · IF 5.4 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Guangzhou Women and Children's Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Wen Z
- DOI: 10.1111/imm.70227
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1111/imm.70227) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43065273/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨肝巨噬细胞在RRV诱导的胆道闭锁/胆道损伤中的调控机制及Notch3/PPAR信号通路的作用。
- 核心发现/数据：巨噬细胞上调DLL4，抗DLL4抗体REGN1035可抑制DLL4和Notch3表达；氯膦酸盐脂质体耗竭巨噬细胞可减轻RRV感染小鼠胆道损伤，抑制Notch3信号并下调HIF-1α、CD68和CD163。转录组分析鉴定715个上调、585个下调基因，证实PPAR信号通路为Notch3下游；Notch3敲除可减轻RRV诱导的胆道损伤和异常巨噬细胞极化

**原文摘要**：Biliary atresia is a rare obliterative cholangiopathy in neonates, and hepatic macrophages participate in biliary atresia pathogenesis. The study aimed to reveal the regulatory mechanism of macrophages in the progression of biliary atresia. Hepatic macrophages were co-cultured with vascular smooth muscle cells (VSMCs). Delta like canonical notch ligand 4 (DLL4) and neurogenic locus notch homologue 3 (Notch3) were quantified in rhesus rotavirus (RRV)-treated cells following anti-DLL4 antibody REGN1035. In RRV-challenged mice, Notch3 was activated by Ade-NICD3, while macrophages were depleted by clodronate liposomes (Clo-Lip) to compare intervention effects on RRV-induced biliary injury, hypoxia inducible factor-1α (HIF-1α), Notch3, CD68 and CD163. Notch3 knockout mice were used to verify the effect of Notch3 on RRV-induced injury. Transcriptome sequencing screened Notch3-related differentially expressed genes, and downstream pathway regulation was further verified. Macrophages upregulated DLL4, while REGN1035 treatment inhibited DLL4 and Notch3 expression. In RRV-infected mice, Clo-Lip-mediated macrophage depletion alleviated biliary injury, repressed Notch3 signalling and downregulated HIF-1α, CD68 and CD163. In RRV mice with NICD3 overexpression, macrophage depletion alleviated Notch3 activation-triggered biliary lesions, inhibited inflammation and downregulated HIF-1α, CD68 and CD163. Transcriptomic analysis identified 715 upregulated and 585 downregulated genes, confirming the peroxisome proliferators-activated receptor (PPAR) signalling pathway as a downstream pathway of Notch3. Compared with RRV-infected wild-type mice, Notch3 knockout alleviated RRV-induced biliary injury and abnormal macrophage polarization, downregulated HIF-1α and CYP7A1, but upregulated PPARα. This protective effect was abolished by the PPARα inhibitor GW6471. Macrophage depletion ameliorates RRV-induced biliary injury via regulating the Notch3/PPAR signalling pathway.

## Placental abnormalities in congenital diaphragmatic hernia: A systematic review of experimental and clinical studies.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 12:07
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Washington University School of Medicine in St. Louis
- 通讯作者: Zani A (augusto.zani@wustl.edu)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06723-w
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06723-w) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43064861/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：明确先天性膈疝（CDH）中胎盘异常的发生率，并刻画其大体形态、组织学、分子通路及血管功能。
- 核心发现/数据：从2820条记录中筛选并纳入14项人体和动物研究。人体研究未发现一致的胎盘大体表型，但报告了脐带过度螺旋、异常插入、单脐动脉，以及组织学上的胎儿血管灌注不良、血栓病变和慢性炎症；离体CDH胎儿胎盘动脉表现为过度收缩和松弛不足。动物研究显示胎盘重量降低、滋养细胞损伤和凋亡及维甲酸代谢改变。
- 临床启示：CDH可能与胎儿胎盘血管病理异常相关，临床应关注胎盘和脐带血管异常。尚需标准化评估以明确胎盘状态与CDH严重程度及新生儿结局的关系。

**原文摘要**：The placenta is essential for fetal organ development, and its dysfunction is implicated in several congenital anomalies. However, little is known about the status and role of the placenta in congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH). Herein, we aimed to define the incidence of placental abnormalities in CDH and characterize gross morphology, histology, molecular pathways, and vascular function. Using a predefined search strategy, we performed a systematic review following PRISMA guidelines. Human and animal studies reporting on placental and/or umbilical cord abnormalities in CDH were analyzed. Of 2,820 records identified, 14 articles were included. Human studies reported no consistent macroscopic placental phenotype. Cord abnormalities included hypercoiling, abnormal insertion, and single umbilical artery. Fetal vascular malperfusion, thrombotic lesions, and chronic inflammation were reported at histology. Ex-vivo CDH fetoplacental arteries showed impaired function with excessive constriction and inadequate relaxation. Animal studies reported reduced placental weight, trophoblast injury and apoptosis, and altered retinoid metabolism. Despite extensive literature on CDH, the status of the placenta remains understudied. Available evidence indicates that CDH is associated with abnormalities involving fetal placental vascular pathology. Studies using standardized assessment are needed to define the role of the placenta in CDH and correlate its status with disease severity and neonatal outcomes.

## Long-term (≥ 10-year) outcomes after laparoscopic choledochal cyst excision: a retrospective cohort study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 12:07
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 113-8421 · Juntendo University School of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Cazares J (joel@juntendo.ac.jp)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06731-w
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06731-w) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43064822/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估腹腔镜胆总管囊肿切除术（LCCE）后随访至少10年的长期结局。
- 核心发现/数据：本研究纳入47例连续患儿，均随访≥10年，中位随访12.85年；早期并发症发生率为17.0%，第一年并发症发生率为8.5%。2例（4.3%）发生晚期胆道事件（1例胆管炎经药物治疗，1例肝内结石行再次重建），10年和15年无晚期胆道事件生存率均为95.7%。另有1例于13.79年因粘连性肠梗阻手术，使15年总体无晚期事件生存率为90.4%；未发生晚期胰腺炎、临床相关残留胆管病变、内疝或胆道恶性肿瘤。
- 临床启示：标准化LCCE可获得持久的长期胆道结局，但仍可能发生晚期胆道及肠道并发症，支持对患者持续随访至成年。

**原文摘要**：To the best of our knowledge, no previous dedicated study has evaluated laparoscopic choledochal cyst excision (LCCE) in a cohort in which all patients had at least 10 years of follow-up. We therefore evaluated long-term outcomes after LCCE in this population. This retrospective two-center cohort study included all 47 consecutive eligible children who underwent LCCE between June 2009 and September 2016. All patients had at least 10 years of confirmed follow-up. The standardized technique included cyst excision with tailored intrapancreatic dissection, intraoperative endoscopy of the common channel and/or intrahepatic ducts, and mucosa-to-mucosa Roux-en-Y hepaticojejunostomy (HJ). The primary endpoint was late biliary event-free survival; overall late event-free survival including intestinal events was a secondary endpoint. Ten patients (21.3%) had at least 15 years of follow-up. Median follow-up was 12.85 years (IQR 10.89-14.64; range 10.00-17.26). Early and first-year complications occurred in 8 (17.0%) and 4 patients (8.5%), respectively. Two patients (4.3%) developed qualifying late biliary events: one developed cholangitis at 1.02 years and was treated medically, and the other developed an intrahepatic stone at 5.14 years and underwent redo reconstruction. Late biliary event-free survival was 95.7% at both 10 and 15 years. One additional patient underwent surgery for adhesive intestinal obstruction at 13.79 years, resulting in an overall late event-free survival of 90.4% at 15 years. No late pancreatitis, clinically relevant remnant duct pathology, internal hernia, or biliary malignancy occurred. Standardized LCCE was associated with durable long-term biliary outcomes. However, late biliary and intestinal complications remained possible, supporting continued surveillance into adulthood.
