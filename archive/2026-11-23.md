## Perinatal outcomes of minimally invasive fetal procedures for the treatment of sacrococcygeal teratoma: a systematic review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:43
- 期刊: Case Rep Perinat Med · 中科院医学4区(妇产科学4区) · IF 0.2
- 国家/单位: 巴西 · Paulista School of Medicine - Federal University of São Paulo (EPM-UNIFESP)
- 通讯作者: Araujo Júnior E
- DOI: 10.1515/crpm-2026-0030
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1515/crpm-2026-0030) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43066269/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估微创胎儿手术治疗骶尾部畸胎瘤的围产结局。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入5项研究（3项回顾性队列和2项病例系列，样本量4-22例，涉及6个国家），术式包括射频消融、经皮及胎儿镜激光消融、囊肿抽吸和分流，主要干预指征为胎儿水肿和心脏受损。有对照组的研究中，干预组生存率为40.0-75.0%，期待管理组为12.5-100%；两项研究新生儿死亡率达50.0%，五项研究中干预病例<32周早产发生率为25.0-100%。现有证据仅来自小样本回顾性研究，存在基于病情严重程度的选择偏倚，尚无法判断其对生存率的影响。
- 临床启示：对合并胎儿水肿或心脏受损的骶尾部畸胎瘤胎儿，微创胎儿手术是可考虑的选择，但应充分告知证据不确定性和高早产风险，并进行个体化多学科决策。

**原文摘要**：Sacrococcygeal teratomas are the most common fetal teratoma. When ultrasonographic parameters deteriorate at early gestational ages at which preterm delivery is not feasible, intrauterine treatment methods have been indicated. This study aimed to assess the perinatal outcomes of minimally invasive fetal procedures for the treatment of sacrococcygeal teratoma. This systematic review followed PRISMA guidelines and was registered in PROSPERO (CRD420251238039). We searched PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, and CENTRAL for studies published from January 2000 through November 2025, with four or more participants reporting minimally invasive intrauterine interventions for sacrococcygeal teratoma. The primary outcome was fetal death; secondary outcomes included perinatal mortality and preterm birth. Risk of bias was assessed using Downs and Black. Five studies met inclusion criteria (three retrospective cohorts and two case series; n=4-22 participants) across six countries. Procedures included radiofrequency ablation, percutaneous and fetoscopic laser ablation, cyst aspiration, and shunting. Fetal hydrops and cardiac compromise were the main indications for intervention. Among studies with a comparator group, survival ranged from 40.0-75.0 % in intervention groups vs. 12.5-100 % in expectant management. Neonatal mortality reached 50.0 % in two studies, and preterm birth before 32 weeks occurred in 25.0-100 % of intervention cases across the five studies. Minimally invasive fetal procedures are a feasible option for fetuses with sacrococcygeal teratoma complicated by hydrops or cardiac compromise, but the available evidence is limited to small retrospective studies subject to severity-based selection bias and does not allow conclusions regarding their effect on survival. Preterm birth was frequent after intervention.

## Evolution of POCUS in the NICU: From a diagnostic tool to an extension of clinical examination.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:43
- 期刊: Semin Fetal Neonatal Med · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区) · IF 2.9 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Saint Joseph's Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Singh Y (ygsingh@health.ucdavis.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.siny.2026.101780
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.siny.2026.101780) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43068926/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：总结POCUS在新生儿重症监护中从独立诊断工具向床旁临床检查延伸的演变，并综述其诊断与操作应用、急诊复苏方案及未来发展方向。
- 核心发现/数据：本叙述性综述未提供具体数值数据；现有证据和国际共识支持POCUS用于呼吸窘迫与气胸的肺超声、目标心脏评估、坏死性小肠结肠炎肠道超声、血管通路引导及急性失代偿评估。SAFE-R、Crashing Neonate Protocol、STEP、NeoSONAR等结构化方案促进其融入新生儿急诊评估与复苏，欧洲儿科与新生儿重症监护学会、美国儿科学会、美国超声心动图学会及国家新生儿POCUS协作组等推动了标准化培训、认证和质量保证框架。
- 临床启示：将POCUS作为床旁查体的延伸可提升危重新生儿诊断精确性、操作安全性和个体化临床决策；临床推广需依托标准化培训、认证和质量保证体系。

**原文摘要**：Point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) is increasingly transforming neonatal intensive care practice from a discrete diagnostic modality into an extension of the bedside clinical examination. Compared to traditional monitoring tools, POCUS provides rapid, real-time physiologic and anatomic information that may improve diagnostic precision, procedural safety, and individualized clinical decision-making in critically ill neonates. Over the past decade, expanding evidence and international consensus guidelines have supported a growing range of neonatal POCUS applications, including lung ultrasound for respiratory distress and pneumothorax, targeted cardiac assessment, bowel ultrasound for necrotizing enterocolitis, vascular access guidance, and evaluation of the acutely decompensating infant. Structured protocols such as SAFE-R, the Crashing Neonate Protocol, STEP, and NeoSONAR have further advanced the integration of ultrasound into neonatal emergency assessment and resuscitation. In parallel, formal recommendations from organizations including the European Society of Pediatric and Neonatal Intensive Care, the American Academy of Pediatrics, the American Society of Echocardiography, and the National Neonatal POCUS Collaborative have accelerated the development of standardized training, credentialing, and quality assurance frameworks. This narrative review summarizes the evolving role of POCUS in neonatology, reviews current evidence supporting diagnostic and procedural applications, discusses emerging protocols for emergency and resuscitative care, and explores future directions including advanced training pathways, artificial intelligence integration, research interests, competency-based education and standardized training.
