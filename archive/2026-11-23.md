## Perinatal outcomes of minimally invasive fetal procedures for the treatment of sacrococcygeal teratoma: a systematic review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:43
- 期刊: Case Rep Perinat Med · 中科院医学4区(妇产科学4区) · IF 0.2
- 国家/单位: 巴西 · Paulista School of Medicine - Federal University of São Paulo (EPM-UNIFESP)
- 通讯作者: Araujo Júnior E
- DOI: 10.1515/crpm-2026-0030
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1515/crpm-2026-0030) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43066269/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估微创胎儿手术治疗骶尾部畸胎瘤的围产结局。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入5项研究（3项回顾性队列和2项病例系列，样本量4-22例，涉及6个国家），术式包括射频消融、经皮及胎儿镜激光消融、囊肿抽吸和分流，主要干预指征为胎儿水肿和心脏受损。有对照组的研究中，干预组生存率为40.0-75.0%，期待管理组为12.5-100%；两项研究新生儿死亡率达50.0%，五项研究中干预病例<32周早产发生率为25.0-100%。现有证据仅来自小样本回顾性研究，存在基于病情严重程度的选择偏倚，尚无法判断其对生存率的影响。
- 临床启示：对合并胎儿水肿或心脏受损的骶尾部畸胎瘤胎儿，微创胎儿手术是可考虑的选择，但应充分告知证据不确定性和高早产风险，并进行个体化多学科决策。

**原文摘要**：Sacrococcygeal teratomas are the most common fetal teratoma. When ultrasonographic parameters deteriorate at early gestational ages at which preterm delivery is not feasible, intrauterine treatment methods have been indicated. This study aimed to assess the perinatal outcomes of minimally invasive fetal procedures for the treatment of sacrococcygeal teratoma. This systematic review followed PRISMA guidelines and was registered in PROSPERO (CRD420251238039). We searched PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, and CENTRAL for studies published from January 2000 through November 2025, with four or more participants reporting minimally invasive intrauterine interventions for sacrococcygeal teratoma. The primary outcome was fetal death; secondary outcomes included perinatal mortality and preterm birth. Risk of bias was assessed using Downs and Black. Five studies met inclusion criteria (three retrospective cohorts and two case series; n=4-22 participants) across six countries. Procedures included radiofrequency ablation, percutaneous and fetoscopic laser ablation, cyst aspiration, and shunting. Fetal hydrops and cardiac compromise were the main indications for intervention. Among studies with a comparator group, survival ranged from 40.0-75.0 % in intervention groups vs. 12.5-100 % in expectant management. Neonatal mortality reached 50.0 % in two studies, and preterm birth before 32 weeks occurred in 25.0-100 % of intervention cases across the five studies. Minimally invasive fetal procedures are a feasible option for fetuses with sacrococcygeal teratoma complicated by hydrops or cardiac compromise, but the available evidence is limited to small retrospective studies subject to severity-based selection bias and does not allow conclusions regarding their effect on survival. Preterm birth was frequent after intervention.

## Evolution of POCUS in the NICU: From a diagnostic tool to an extension of clinical examination.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:43
- 期刊: Semin Fetal Neonatal Med · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区) · IF 2.9 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Saint Joseph's Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Singh Y (ygsingh@health.ucdavis.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.siny.2026.101780
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.siny.2026.101780) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43068926/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：总结POCUS在新生儿重症监护中从独立诊断工具向床旁临床检查延伸的演变，并综述其诊断与操作应用、急诊复苏方案及未来发展方向。
- 核心发现/数据：本叙述性综述未提供具体数值数据；现有证据和国际共识支持POCUS用于呼吸窘迫与气胸的肺超声、目标心脏评估、坏死性小肠结肠炎肠道超声、血管通路引导及急性失代偿评估。SAFE-R、Crashing Neonate Protocol、STEP、NeoSONAR等结构化方案促进其融入新生儿急诊评估与复苏，欧洲儿科与新生儿重症监护学会、美国儿科学会、美国超声心动图学会及国家新生儿POCUS协作组等推动了标准化培训、认证和质量保证框架。
- 临床启示：将POCUS作为床旁查体的延伸可提升危重新生儿诊断精确性、操作安全性和个体化临床决策；临床推广需依托标准化培训、认证和质量保证体系。

**原文摘要**：Point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) is increasingly transforming neonatal intensive care practice from a discrete diagnostic modality into an extension of the bedside clinical examination. Compared to traditional monitoring tools, POCUS provides rapid, real-time physiologic and anatomic information that may improve diagnostic precision, procedural safety, and individualized clinical decision-making in critically ill neonates. Over the past decade, expanding evidence and international consensus guidelines have supported a growing range of neonatal POCUS applications, including lung ultrasound for respiratory distress and pneumothorax, targeted cardiac assessment, bowel ultrasound for necrotizing enterocolitis, vascular access guidance, and evaluation of the acutely decompensating infant. Structured protocols such as SAFE-R, the Crashing Neonate Protocol, STEP, and NeoSONAR have further advanced the integration of ultrasound into neonatal emergency assessment and resuscitation. In parallel, formal recommendations from organizations including the European Society of Pediatric and Neonatal Intensive Care, the American Academy of Pediatrics, the American Society of Echocardiography, and the National Neonatal POCUS Collaborative have accelerated the development of standardized training, credentialing, and quality assurance frameworks. This narrative review summarizes the evolving role of POCUS in neonatology, reviews current evidence supporting diagnostic and procedural applications, discusses emerging protocols for emergency and resuscitative care, and explores future directions including advanced training pathways, artificial intelligence integration, research interests, competency-based education and standardized training.

## The burden of disease for children diagnosed with Klinefelter syndrome - a European cohort study.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：调查欧洲产前或婴儿期诊断Klinefelter综合征儿童的疾病负担，以改善诊断后对父母的咨询。
- 核心发现/数据：在13个登记区约580万活产儿中，278例诊断为Klinefelter综合征，婴儿死亡率为3.2%（95% CI 1.5–6.1）。出生后第一年约2/3患儿住院，中位住院3.9天（参考儿童3.0天，任何重大先天畸形儿童7.9天）；1–4岁中位住院降至每年0.7天（参考儿童0.4天）。第一年内12.3%接受手术，1–4岁增至19.7%。
- 临床启示：Klinefelter综合征患儿出生后第一年疾病负担较高，但1–4岁明显下降，童年期总体预后良好，可据此安抚父母并加强早期随访与对症管理。

**原文摘要**：To investigate the burden of disease for European children diagnosed prenatally or in infancy with Klinefelter syndrome for better counselling of parents after the diagnosis. Thirteen registries in ten countries from the EUROCAT network participated. Data for children born between 1995 and 2014 and diagnosed with Klinefelter syndrome were linked to mortality and hospital records. Data for children born with any major congenital anomaly and children without a congenital anomaly (reference children) were included for comparisons. Out of 5.8 million liveborn children, 278 were diagnosed with Klinefelter syndrome in the 13 registry areas and with an infant mortality of 3.2% (95% CI 1.5;6.1). Two out of three were admitted to hospital during the first year with a median length of stay of 3.9 (95% CI 3.0;4.7) days compared to 3.0 days for reference children and 7.9 days for children with any congenital anomaly. In the age group 1-4 years, the median length of stay for children with Klinefelter syndrome decreased to 0.7 (95% CI 0.3;1.1) days per year compared to 0.4 days per year for reference children. Within the first year, 12.3% (95% CI 7.1;18.9) of the children with Klinefelter syndrome underwent surgery increasing to 19.7% (95% CI 10.2;31.4) at age 1-4 years. There is a high burden of disease for children diagnosed prenatally or in infancy with Klinefelter syndrome in the first year after birth but this falls during age 1-4 years. Parents can be reassured that the prognosis in childhood is good. • Mortality and morbidity are increased among adults with Klinefelter syndrome and studies indicate that this start from the early childhood. • This multicenter population-based study including boys diagnosed prenatally and in infancy with Klinefelter syndrome showed a higher morbidity within the first five years of life compared to children without congenital anomalies. One out of eight underwent surgery within the first year after birth and one in five underwent surgery at age 1-4 years.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：描述需手术胰腺疾病患者的诊断谱、手术管理、围手术期结局及疾病特异性随访。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入112例，包括慢性胰腺炎62例、急性坏死性胰腺炎21例、胰腺肿瘤18例和先天性异常11例；主要术后并发症发生率为17.9%（20/112），其中急性坏死性胰腺炎组达61.9%（13/21），且全部3例死亡（2.7%）均发生于该组（14.3%）。慢性胰腺炎术后83.9%（52/62）获得持续疼痛缓解（中位随访32个月）；胰腺肿瘤均实现R0切除且中位随访36个月未见复发；11例环状胰腺梗阻症状均缓解。
- 临床启示：急性坏死性胰腺炎是主要术后并发症和死亡的高危人群，应加强围手术期风险管理；慢性胰腺炎手术、肿瘤R0切除及环状胰腺手术在可获得随访中显示出较好的疾病特异性结局，但需结合单中心、亚组样本量及随访局限解读。

**原文摘要**：Pancreatic disorders requiring surgery differ in their presentation, operative management, and relevant outcomes. We describe the diagnostic spectrum, surgical management, perioperative outcomes, and disease-specific follow-up in a five-year, single-centre cohort. We conducted a retrospective cohort study of patients who underwent surgery for a pancreatic disorder at a tertiary-care centre in Karnataka, India, from January 2021 through December 2025. The primary outcome was major postoperative morbidity, defined as a Clavien-Dindo grade III-V complication within 30 days. Mortality was assessed within 30 days or during the index admission. Longer-term outcomes were summarized by disease group using available follow-up. Of 138 records screened, 112 patients were included: 62 with chronic pancreatitis, 21 with acute necrotizing pancreatitis, 18 with pancreatic neoplasms, and 11 with congenital anomalies. Major morbidity occurred in 20/112 patients (17.9%), including 13/21 (61.9%) with acute necrotizing pancreatitis. Three patients died within 30 days or during the index admission (3/112, 2.7%); all were in the acute necrotizing pancreatitis group (3/21, 14.3%). In the chronic pancreatitis group, documented durable pain relief was reported in 52/62 patients (83.9%), with a median follow-up of 32 months. All 18 patients with neoplasms had microscopically negative resection margins (R0); no recurrence was documented during a median follow-up of 36 months. Obstructive symptoms resolved in all 11 patients with annular pancreas. In this cohort, major morbidity and all reported deaths occurred in the acute necrotizing pancreatitis group. Disease-specific outcomes were favourable among patients with documented follow-up, including pain relief after chronic pancreatitis surgery, negative margins after neoplasm resection, and resolution of obstruction after annular pancreas surgery. These findings are descriptive and should be interpreted in light of the single-centre design, subgroup sizes, and available follow-up.

## Angiogenic factors and fetal Doppler for predicting adverse perinatal outcome in late-onset small fetuses with and those without pre-eclampsia.

- 期刊: Ultrasound Obstet Gynecol
- 国家/单位: 西班牙 · Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona
- 通讯作者: Mendoza M
- DOI: 10.1002/uog.70386
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/uog.70386) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43071506/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估血管生成因子与胎儿多普勒单独及联合预测晚发型SGA/FGR（伴或不伴子痫前期）复合不良围产结局（CAPO）及后续子痫前期的效能。

The study population comprised singleton pregnancies with an estimated fetal weight < 10 Overall, 754 pregnancies with late-onset fetal smallness were included. PE was present at diagnosis in 21/754 (2.8%) cases and developed later in 23 (3.1%) cases. CAPO occurred in 84 (11.1%) cases. In the overall cohort, the sFlt-1/PlGF ratio showed the highest predictive performance for CAPO (AUC, 0.791 (95% CI, 0.721-0.861)), outperforming fetal Doppler alone (AUC, 0.649 (95% CI, 0.566-0.732); P = 0.005). This was not significantly different from the performance of PlGF alone (AUC, 0.784 (95% CI, 0.711-0.856); P = 0.810). In pregnancies without PE, the predictive performance of individual angiogenic markers alone was lower compared with that in the overall cohort, and performance was similar across markers, with no significant differences between the sFlt-1/PlGF ratio, PlGF and fetal Doppler, alone or in combination. In pregnancies with PE at any time, the predictive performance of all markers was not significantly different from that observed in the overall cohort, and all markers showed comparable predictive performance for CAPO, with no clear superiority. In the prespecified secondary analysis for prediction of subsequent PE, the sFlt-1/PlGF ratio alone was the single marker that achieved the highest predictive performance (AUC, 0.884 (95% CI, 0.807-0.960)), outperforming both PlGF alone and fetal Doppler alone. Combining fetal Doppler with angiogenic factors reduced false-positive rates but did not improve sensitivity. This study confirms the predictive value of angiogenic factors, particularly the sFlt-1/PlGF ratio, for identifying CAPO and subsequent PE in late-onset SGA/FGR. Compared with the overall cohort, the predictive performance of PlGF and the sFlt-1/PlGF ratio was significantly lower in pregnancies without PE, whereas no significant difference was observed for fetal Doppler. Nevertheless, in pregnancies without PE, angiogenic markers showed predictive performance comparable to that of fetal Doppler. These findings suggest that angiogenic factors reflect underlying placental insufficiency rather than PE alone, supporting their integration into the clinical assessment of late-onset SGA/FGR in both pregnancies with and those without PE. © 2026 International Society of Ultrasound in Obstetrics and Gynecology.
