## Preventing pediatric backover trauma: The impact of a federally mandated car safety feature.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 03:07
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · McGovern Medical School at UTHealth Houston and Children's Memorial Hermann Hospital
- 通讯作者: Drucker NA (Natalie.a.drucker@uth.tmc.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163303
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163303) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42468686/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估美国联邦强制新车后视能见度标准对5岁以下儿童倒车碾压创伤发生率、严重程度及死亡的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：创伤登记中倒车碾压损伤年发生率由强制前11.6例/年降至强制后5.3例/年（RR 0.46，95%CI 0.28-0.75），每10万儿童发病率由17.3降至7.1（IRR 0.41，95%CI 0.27-0.60），严重伤由12例降至5例。KACS公开报告中年发生率由3.0例/年降至0.9例/年（RR 0.30，95%CI 0.12-0.75），死亡由18例（82%）降至4例（67%，p=0.58），公开报告死亡减少78%。
- 临床启示：该法规显著降低了儿童倒车碾压创伤的发生率和严重程度，提示临床及公共卫生应支持车辆后视/盲区安全法规，并加强家庭倒车安全宣教。

**原文摘要**：Backover trauma occurs when a reversing motorized vehicle strikes a pedestrian, often a small child. Injuries carry significant morbidity and mortality. A federal mandate required newly manufactured motor vehicles to meet a rear visibility standard by May 2018. This study characterizes the mandate's impact on pediatric backover trauma. A retrospective pre-post observational study was conducted of children (<5 years) with backover trauma (01/2011-11/2024). Two datasets were utilized: trauma registries from two Level I pediatric trauma centers serving a single catchment area, and case lists curated by Kids and Car Safety (KACS) from publicly available injury reports (same region). Univariate analyses and Poisson/quasi-Poisson models compared backover rates and incidences, pre-mandate versus post-mandate. From trauma registry data, 120 patients were identified: 85 pre-mandate, 35 post-mandate. Patients were median 2.1 years old (IQR: 1.6-2.8) and 65% male. Median ISS was 10.0 (IQR: 5.0-17.3) with no difference between groups (p = 0.68), but fewer severe injuries occurred post-mandate (12 pre-mandate, 5 post-mandate). Backover injury rate decreased from 11.6/year pre-mandate to 5.3/year post-mandate (RR: 0.46; 95% CI: 0.28-0.75), and incidence decreased from 17.3 to 7.1 per 100,000 (Incidence RR: 0.41; 95% CI: 0.27-0.60). From KACS data, 28 victims were identified: 22 pre-mandate, 6 post-mandate. Backover injury rate was higher pre-mandate (3.0/year) and decreased post-mandate to 0.9/year (RR: 0.30; 95% CI: 0.12-0.75). Fatalities decreased from 18 (82%) pre-mandate to 4 (67%) post-mandate (p = 0.58). Pediatric backover rates decreased following full compliance with the federal mandate. Fewer severe injuries occurred, and publicly reported fatalities decreased by 78%. Reduced rate, incidence, and severity of backover injuries justify public health-driven regulatory requirements.

## Textbook outcome after hepatoblastoma resection: Definition, failure modes, and risk-adjusted benchmarking in a tertiary European center.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 03:07
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: University of Paris-Saclay · Bicêtre Hospital
- 通讯作者: Fuchs J (juri.fuchs@aphp.fr)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163308
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163308) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42463005/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：在儿童肝母细胞瘤根治性肝切除人群中定义术后教科书结局（TO-HB），识别未达标模式并进行风险调整基准比较。
- 核心发现/数据：108例患儿中60例（55.6%）达到TO-HB；未达标主要由术后并发症（38/48，79%）和延长住院（18/48，38%）驱动，且TO-HB率随PRETEXT分期升高和肝切除范围扩大而下降。风险调整后平均预测概率为0.556，观察/预期比为1.00，风险标准化TO-HB率为55.6%（95%CI 42.4–71.5%）。
- 临床启示：TO-HB是可行且严格的围术期质量评价基准；改善肝母细胞瘤切除术质量应重点针对术后并发症，而非死亡或再干预。

**原文摘要**：Textbook Outcome (TO) has emerged as a composite quality metric for benchmarking perioperative performance in adult hepatobiliary surgery. Its role in pediatric oncologic liver surgery remains insufficiently explored and TO has never been defined for hepatoblastoma resection. Single-center cohort study including children undergoing curative-intent liver resection for hepatoblastoma. Textbook Outcome after hepatoblastoma resection (TO-HB) was defined as an all-or-none composite comprising: (i) no postoperative complications (CCI = 0), (ii) no prolonged length of stay (≤procedure-specific 75th-percentile), (iii) no reoperation within 90 days, (iv) no unplanned readmission within 90 days, and (v) no 90-day mortality. Case-mix adjustment was performed using ridge-penalized logistic regression with pre-specified covariates. Model performance was evaluated in terms of overall accuracy and calibration, including the Brier score and calibration-in-the-large, with bootstrap internal validation, to support risk-adjusted benchmarking analyses. TO-HB was achieved in 60/108 patients (55.6%). Failure to achieve TO-HB was predominantly driven by postoperative complications (38/48; 79%) and prolonged length of stay (18/48; 38%). TO-HB rates decreased with increasing surgical complexity, including higher PRETEXT stage and more extensive hepatectomy types. Risk-adjusted predictions were narrowly distributed around the overall TO rate (mean predicted probability 0.556), with preserved population-level calibration. The observed-to-expected ratio for TO-HB was 1.00, corresponding to a risk-standardized TO-HB rate of 55.6% (95% CI 42.4-71.5%). TO-HB is a feasible and stringent benchmark for perioperative quality in hepatoblastoma surgery. Failure to achieve TO-HB is predominantly driven by postoperative morbidity rather than mortality or reintervention, highlighting postoperative complications as target for quality improvement.

## Ductular Reaction Underlies Development of Portal Hypertension in Biliary Atresia.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:07
- 期刊: Liver Int
- 国家/单位: 芬兰 · New Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Pakarinen MP
- DOI: 10.1111/liv.70863
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1111/liv.70863) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42703703/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨胆道闭锁（BA）患儿肝组织中胆管反应（DR）和肝纤维化对门静脉高压（PH）的预测价值。
- 核心发现/数据：60例BA患者中70%在Kasai术后中位1.4年出现PH；Kasai时DR%≥6.9%和PIH%≥1.6%可预测PH，而纤维化除肝硬化外预测作用有限；术后1年DR%≥3.5%、PIH%≥0.7%、BE%≥3.2%及Metavir 3-4、SRP%≥28.4%均与PH相关，DR组分与PH的关联比纤维化更一致。
- 临床启示：胆管反应是胆道闭锁患儿发生门静脉高压的重要组织学预测指标，术后肝活检评估DR有助于早期识别高危患者并指导更主动的监测与管理。

**原文摘要**：As better understanding of pathophysiology and reliable predictors for portal hypertension (PH) are needed, we investigated how histological ductular reaction (DR) and liver fibrosis predict PH in biliary atresia (BA). 60 BA patients treated between 1987 and 2024 at Helsinki University Hospital were enrolled. Patients underwent liver biopsies at Kasai portoenterostomy and 1 year postoperatively. PH was defined as endoscopic detection of oesophageal varices or thrombocytopenia (< 150*109) and splenomegaly. A neural network model was used to quantify proportional DR (DR%) relative to entire liver tissue, composed of cytokeratin-7-positive portal biliary epithelium (BE%) and parenchymal intermediate hepatocytes (PIH%). Fibrosis was measured by Metavir staging and computerized quantification of Sirius-Red-positive area (SRP%). Predictors of PH were studied with AUROC and survival analyses, and Cox regression models. After portoenterostomy, PH was detected in 70% (n = 42) of patients at median age 1.4 years (IQR 0.7-2.6). At portoenterostomy, DR% ≥ 6.9% (p = 0.016) and PIH% ≥ 1.6% (p < 0.001), but not liver fibrosis apart from cirrhosis (n = 5) predicted PH (p = 0.032). At 1 year, DR% ≥ 3.5% (p < 0.001), PIH% ≥ 0.7% (p < 0.001), BE% ≥ 3.2% (p = 0.006) as well as Metavir 3-4 vs. 1-2 (p = 0.037) and SRP% ≥ 28.4% (p = 0.006) predicted PH. Patients with PH showed progression of PIH% (p < 0.001) from portoenterostomy to follow-up biopsy, accompanied by more rapid progression of fibrosis. In univariable regression models, different DR components, but not fibrosis, were associated with PH both at portoenterostomy and follow-up also after successful portoenterostomy. DR predicted development of PH at and one year after portoenterostomy, associating with PH more consistently than fibrosis. Biliary atresia is a rare childhood liver disease, in which the factors predicting the development of complications, such as portal hypertension, and the potential benefits of active surveillance remain unclear. This study advances our understanding of portal hypertension development in biliary atresia by highlighting a key role for ductular reaction—a histological process characterized by the proliferation of reactive cholangiocytes and transdifferentiating hepatocytes.

## Evolution of Surgical Management in Pilonidal Disease: A 25-Year Scoping Review and Bibliometric Analysis of Pediatric and Adult Literature.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 10:19
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Stanford University
- 通讯作者: Chiu B (bhsc@stanford.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163513
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163513) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42822663/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过25年范围综述与文献计量分析，评估2000—2025年藏毛疾病手术治疗趋势，并比较儿科与成人文献的演变差异。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入383项研究，其中成人327项（85%）、儿科56项（15%）；发文量持续增长，儿科文献在2015年后增加，但来源国仅12个且无低/中收入国家研究，成人来自36个国家。成人以单纯手术为主的比例高于儿科（80% vs 61%，p<0.001），儿科更多评估微创和多模式策略；关键词分析显示研究重点从切除手术和皮瓣重建转向微创及辅助治疗。
- 临床启示：藏毛疾病手术证据仍高度异质，儿科证据有限但正转向微创策略；临床实践需在现有证据基础上个体化选择术式，并推动标准化和国际协作以形成一致循证管理。

**原文摘要**：Pilonidal disease is a chronic inflammatory condition of the sacrococcygeal region that disproportionately affects adolescents and young adults. Although numerous treatment approaches exist, optimal management remains debated and practice patterns vary widely. How pediatric treatment strategies and research priorities have evolved relative to adult literature remains incompletely understood. A scoping review and bibliometric analysis were conducted to evaluate trends in surgical management of pilonidal disease from 2000 to 2025. A systematic PubMed search identified studies reporting clinical data on pilonidal disease treatment. Extracted data included publication year, origin country, patient population, treatment modality, recurrence outcomes, and follow-up duration. Bibliometric analysis assessed publication trends, geographic distribution, and thematic evolution using keyword co-occurrence mapping. A total of 383 studies were included - 327 adult (85%) and 56 pediatric (15%) studies. Publication volume increased steadily over time, with pediatric literature expanding after 2015. Adult studies originated from 36 countries compared with 12 countries in pediatric literature, with no pediatric studies from low- or middle-income countries identified. Operative-only approaches predominated in adult literature (80% vs 61%, p<0.001), while pediatric studies more frequently evaluated minimally invasive and multimodal strategies. Keyword analysis demonstrated temporal evolution from excisional surgery and flap reconstruction toward minimally invasive and adjunctive therapies. Research on pilonidal disease surgery has increased but remains heterogeneous in treatment strategies and research focus. Pediatric literature represents a small but evolving portion of the evidence base and increasingly explores minimally invasive approaches. Greater standardization and broader international collaboration are needed to develop consistent evidence-based management strategies.

## Parenteral nutrition-associated cholestasis and postnatal growth trajectories in preterm infants: A longitudinal cohort study.

**原文摘要**：To evaluate the association between parenteral nutrition-associated cholestasis and postnatal weight and linear growth trajectories to 12 months corrected age, and to assess whether cholestasis mediates the relationship between parenteral nutrition duration and 12-month linear growth in preterm infants. Retrospective cohort study of a standardized neonatal nutrition and follow-up database including infants born at <32 weeks' gestation. Cholestasis was defined as peak direct bilirubin ≥34.2 μmol/L. Longitudinal weight and length z-scores were analysed using generalized estimating equations with exchangeable correlation, including time, cholestasis, and time × cholestasis interaction, adjusted for gestational age, cumulative parenteral nutrition duration, length of stay, and surgical necrotizing enterocolitis. Mediation analysis demonstrated no significant indirect effect via cholestasis (adjusted β = -0.013, 95% CI -0.044 to 0.014). ## Epigastric typhoid ileal perforation unmasking an incidental adult midgut malrotation: a rare clinical co-existence.

**原文摘要**：Peritonitis due to typhoid ileal perforation is a life-threatening surgical emergency that remains common in endemic regions but is rarely encountered in developed countries.
