## Thymectomy Extent, Immune Recovery, and Infectious Burden After Neonatal Cardiac Surgery.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:40
- 期刊: J Am Heart Assoc · 中科院医学1区(心脏和心血管系统2区) · IF 6.0 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University of Cincinnati College of Medicine Cincinnati OH
- 通讯作者: Moore C
- DOI: 10.1161/JAHA.126.055490
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.126.055490) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42999988/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估新生儿心脏手术后部分与完全胸腺切除对感染负担及6个月淋巴细胞恢复的影响，并探索相关生物标志物和转录组变化。
- 核心发现/数据：105例新生儿中27例部分切除、78例完全切除；多次感染发生率分别为7%（2/27）和30%（23/78，P=0.02），但校正体外循环时长和手术年代后差异不显著（Firth校正OR 2.99，95%CI 0.82-15.85，P=0.10）。完全胸腺切除后6个月中位淋巴细胞计数下降，而部分切除后上升；生物标志物和RNA测序结果仅为探索性。
- 临床启示：结果提示完全胸腺切除可能与淋巴细胞恢复较差及未校正的较高感染负担相关，支持临床尽量保留胸腺并开展前瞻性研究评估胸腺保留和自体再植入；但校正后感染关联不显著且事件数少，不能据此确定因果关系。

**原文摘要**：Neonatal cardiac surgery frequently requires thymectomy, but the consequences of extensive thymic removal remain uncertain. We retrospectively studied 105 neonates undergoing sternotomy (2015-2024), comparing infections and 6-month lymphocyte recovery after partial versus total thymectomy, with Firth regression adjusted for bypass duration and surgical era. Separate cohorts underwent paired plasma biomarker (n=8) and whole-blood RNA sequencing (n=4) analysis before and after thymectomy. Among 105 neonates, 27 underwent partial and 78 total thymectomy. Multiple infections occurred in 2 of 27 (7%) after partial and 23 of 78 (30%) after total thymectomy (P=0.02); after adjustment for bypass duration and surgical era, the association was not statistically significant (Firth-adjusted odds ratio, 2.99 [95% CI, 0.82-15.85]; P=0.10). Median lymphocyte count decreased after total but increased after partial thymectomy (change, -0.8 versus +0.9×10 Total thymectomy was associated with reduced 6-month lymphocyte recovery and higher unadjusted odds of multiple infections. The infection association was not statistically significant after adjustment, and few events (2 of 27 after partial thymectomy) limit precision and cannot exclude a clinically important effect; these findings are hypothesis-generating. Biomarker and transcriptomic findings are exploratory and cannot distinguish thymectomy from surgery, maturation, or postoperative recovery. Prospective studies are underway to evaluate thymic preservation and autologous reimplantation.
