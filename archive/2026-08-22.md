## Authorship Analysis of Studies on Simulation-based Education in Low and Middle-income Countries: Results From a Systematic Review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 00:39
- 期刊: Simul Healthc · 中科院医学4区(卫生保健与服务4区) · IF 2.9 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Pacific Community (SPC) (L.H.) · School of Clinical Sciences
- 通讯作者: Nataraja RM
- DOI: 10.1097/SIH.0000000000000956
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/SIH.0000000000000956) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42627795/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：调查中低收入国家（LMICs）模拟医学教育研究中本地作者的比例及变化趋势。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入97项研究（来自50个LMICs），95%的研究包含本地作者，但本地作者仅占第一、第二或末位作者（FSF）位置的49%（145/291）。随时间推移，本地FSF作者比例无显著变化（2002-2006年44%、2007-2011年63%、2012-2016年47%、2017-2022年49%，P=0.45）。
- 临床启示：模拟医学教育研究者应主动支持并纳入本地作者，以确保培训项目贴合当地情境并产生实际影响，同时需着力提升LMICs的本地研究能力。

**原文摘要**：Growing emphasis has been placed on the importance of local authorship in research from low and middle-income countries (LMICs). This study aimed to investigate local authorship of simulation-based education research conducted in LMICs through an in-depth authorship analysis of publications retrieved from a systematic review. After retrieving 27,738 records, 97 studies from 50 LMICs were included. The median number of authors was 8, ranging was from 2 to 18. Ninety-three studies (95%) included a local author, and local authors composed 145/291 (49%) of the total first, second, or final (FSF) authorship positions. Although there was a substantial increase in the number of published studies, there were no significant changes to the proportion of local FSF authors over time: 44% in 2002-2006; 63% in 2007-2011; 47% in 2012-2016; and 49% in 2017-2022 (P = 0.45). The representation of local FSF authors is lower compared with other fields, though this may be reflective of including only studies that conducted high-level evaluation, or the fact that simulation is a relatively new field. Simulation researchers should actively support and include local authors to ensure programs are appropriate, contextualized and impactful, while making concerted efforts to develop local research capacity in LMICs.

## Effectiveness of ultrasound-guided hydrostatic versus fluoroscopy-guided air reduction for pediatric intussusception: a severity-adjusted cohort analysis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 00:39
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Cairo · Al-Azhar University
- 通讯作者: Abdelmohsen A (a.abdelmohsen@azhar.edu.eg)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06585-2
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06585-2) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42627511/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：比较超声引导下生理盐水灌肠（UGHR）与透视引导下空气灌肠（FGAR）治疗小儿肠套叠的疗效和安全性，并调整疾病严重程度后评估独立预测失败的因素。
- 核心发现/数据：调整严重程度后，两种技术失败风险无显著差异（aOR 1.22, 95% CI 0.64-2.28; p=0.54）；初始成功率FGAR为86.3%，UGHR为77.7%（p=0.12），但UGHR组基线病情更重（症状持续时间34 vs 22小时，p=0.01； trapped fluid 30.4% vs 16.3%，p=0.02）。独立预测失败因素包括症状持续时间（每12小时aOR 1.27）、肛门突出肿块（aOR 3.58）、套入长度（每cm aOR 1.41）和 trapped fluid（aOR 3.47），模型AUC为0.85；穿孔率均低（1.3% vs 0.9%）。
- 临床启示：在调整疾病严重程度后，UGHR与FGAR疗效和安全性相当；症状持续时间、套入长度及 trapped fluid 等严重程度标志物应指导临床决策和治疗方式选择。

**原文摘要**：The comparative effectiveness of fluoroscopy-guided air reduction (FGAR) versus ultrasound-guided hydrostatic reduction (UGHR) for pediatric intussusception remains uncertain, especially when clinical and sonographic severity are considered. To compare the effectiveness and safety of FGAR and UGHR after adjusting for disease severity and to identify independent predictors of non-operative reduction failure. A retrospective cohort of 192 children (0-24 months) with ileocolic intussusception treated between 2018 and 2024 was analyzed. Baseline clinical variables and ultrasound markers were collected. Multivariable logistic regression, adjusting for clinical and sonographic severity markers (symptom duration, anal protruding mass, intussusceptum length, and trapped fluid), identified predictors of failure, with model performance assessed using the area under the ROC curve. Children undergoing UGHR presented with more severe disease, including longer symptom duration (34 vs. 22 h; p = 0.01), higher delayed presentation > 48 h (32.1% vs. 15.0%; p = 0.004), and more trapped fluid (30.4% vs. 16.3%; p = 0.02). Initial success was 86.3% for FGAR and 77.7% for UGHR (p = 0.12); however, the UGHR group had significantly more trapped fluid and longer symptom duration at baseline. After adjustment for these and other severity markers, technique was not associated with failure (aOR 1.22, 95% CI 0.64-2.28; p = 0.54). Independent predictors included longer symptom duration (aOR 1.27 per 12 h; p < 0.001), anal protruding mass (aOR 3.58; p = 0.01), greater intussusceptum length (aOR 1.41 per cm; p < 0.001), and trapped fluid (aOR 3.47; p < 0.001). Model discrimination was excellent (AUC 0.85). Perforation rates were similarly low (1.3% FGAR vs. 0.9% UGHR). After adjusting for disease severity, UGHR and FGAR show equivalent effectiveness and safety. Crude differences reflect more severe presentations in the UGHR group. Severity markers-including symptom duration, trapped fluid, and intussusceptum length-are strong predictors of failure and should guide clinical decision-making.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨妊娠合并严重肺动脉高压（PAH）患者的多学科管理策略，并评估避免使用催产素对围产期血流动力学稳定的作用。  
- 核心发现/数据：一例未矫正房间隔缺损所致严重PAH孕妇在孕晚期确诊，经多学科团队启动肠外前列环素和磷酸二酯酶-5抑制剂治疗，并在区域麻醉及体外膜肺氧合备用下实施剖宫产，同时刻意避免使用催产素，最终母儿结局良好。产后加强PAH靶向治疗并严密监测，且强调哺乳期内源性催产素激增可能诱发血流动力学不稳定，需抑制泌乳。  
- 临床启示：对严重PAH合并妊娠者，应避免外源性催产素、进行前瞻性多学科规划并备好机械循环支持，同时产后需强化监测和泌乳抑制以降低风险。

**原文摘要**：Pregnancy in patients with pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) carries substantial maternal and fetal risk, necessitating meticulous hemodynamic control and multidisciplinary management. A pregnant woman with severe PAH secondary to an uncorrected atrial septal defect was diagnosed in the third trimester. A multidisciplinary team initiated parenteral prostacyclin therapy and phosphodiesterase-5 inhibition to reduce pulmonary vascular resistance. Delivery was planned under regional anesthesia with extracorporeal membrane oxygenation on standby. Oxytocin was deliberately avoided to minimize systemic vasodilation and the risk of pulmonary hypertensive crisis. Cesarean delivery was successfully performed, resulting in favorable maternal and neonatal outcomes. Postpartum management included escalation of PAH-targeted therapy and intensive monitoring. Carefully coordinated multidisciplinary management and avoidance of oxytocin may help mitigate peripartum hemodynamic instability in pregnancies complicated by severe PAH. Avoidance of exogenous oxytocin, proactive multidisciplinary planning, and preparedness for mechanical circulatory support are critical in managing high-risk pregnancies complicated by severe PAH. Endogenous oxytocin surges during lactation may induce hemodynamic instability in patients with PAH, highlighting the critical need for intensive postpartum monitoring and lactation suppression.

## Decreasing Treatment for Postoperative Clostridioides difficile Infection in Pediatric Surgery Patients.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:31

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过质量改进（QI）措施优化艰难梭菌检测流程，降低小儿外科患者术后艰难梭菌感染（CDI）的治疗率。
- 核心发现/数据：在16,926例NSQIP-Pediatric纳入患者中，55例接受CDI治疗，其中10例（18%）在确认试验阴性后仍被治疗。教育干预后确认检测率从82%降至62%，但CDI治疗率在最初18个月内从0.58%降至0.26%；实施院内自动EIA确认检测后，随后12个月CDI治疗率进一步降至0.07%。

**原文摘要**：Our hospital was an outlier for postoperative Clostridioides difficile infection (CDI) on the 01/2022 NSQIP-Pediatric Semiannual Report. Surgical patients eligible for NSQIP-Pediatric sampling from 01/2022-07/2025 were analyzed to identify patients treated for CDI within 30 days of surgery. Reducing inappropriate treatment of colonized patients lowered the NSQIP-P-defined CDI rate without an increase in missed or delayed diagnoses, fulminant CDI, or CDI requiring surgery.
