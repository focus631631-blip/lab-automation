## Prevention and treatment of tetanus in China: a comparative review against international standards.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 00:42
- 期刊: Infection · 中科院医学3区(传染病学3区) · IF 3.9 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Department of Trauma Center and Emergency Surgery
- 通讯作者: Lin J (jun@fzu.edu.cn)
- DOI: 10.1007/s15010-026-02891-y
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s15010-026-02891-y) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42622933/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：对比中国破伤风防治现状与国际标准（尤其WHO指南），识别差距并优化策略。
- 核心发现/数据：中国已消除新生儿破伤风，但非新生儿破伤风（尤其老年人）负担持续存在，农村为主，病死率达10-40%；诊断方面出现量子点侧向层析等灵敏抗体检测法；治疗指南推荐甲硝唑替代青霉素，并规范人破伤风免疫球蛋白剂量与自主神经管理。
- 临床启示：需针对老年人群强化免疫接种策略，并参照WHO指南统一破伤风诊疗规范以降低病死率。

**原文摘要**：Although China has successfully eliminated neonatal tetanus, non-neonatal tetanus, particularly among older adults, remains a persistent clinical and public health challenge. This narrative review synthesizes the current landscape of tetanus in China-covering epidemiology, pathophysiology, prevention, diagnosis, and treatment-and compares it with World Health Organization (WHO) guidance and practices in other countries to identify gaps and optimization strategies. A comprehensive narrative review was conducted across five core domains: epidemiology, pathogen and pathophysiology, prevention strategies, diagnostic advances, and therapeutic interventions. Data were integrated from the WHO, the Chinese Center for Disease Control and Prevention, national guidelines, and relevant clinical studies, with comparative analysis against global best practices. Epidemiological trends show a clear shift in disease burden from neonates to older adults, predominantly in rural areas, with high case fatality rates (10-40%) despite low incidence. Advances in antibody detection include sensitive assays (e.g., quantum dot-based lateral flow tests), while treatment guidelines prioritize metronidazole over penicillin, with specific protocols for human tetanus immunoglobulin dosing and autonomic management. Adult tetanus highlights critical immunity gaps in China's aging population. Refining immunization policies with WHO guidance and standardizing management may help improve outcomes. China's experience offers a valuable reference for other low- and middle-income countries undergoing similar epidemiological transitions.

## Prematurity Predicts Neurodevelopmental Outcomes in Congenital Heart Disease.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 00:42
- 期刊: J Am Heart Assoc · 中科院医学1区(心脏和心血管系统2区) · IF 6.0 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Division of Newborn Medicine Boston Children's Hospital Boston MA USA
- 通讯作者: Newburger JW
- DOI: 10.1161/JAHA.125.047668
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.125.047668) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42622214/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探究早产是否对先天性心脏病（CHD）婴儿心脏术后的神经发育结局产生额外不良影响。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入1350例CHD手术婴儿，513例完成Bayley评估，其中65例（13%）为早产。校正混杂因素后， gestational age仍独立预测认知和运动评分；出生≤33周者认知和运动复合分分别比足月儿低11分（95%CI 5-17）和15分（95%CI 8-22），且该模型对运动域变异解释度最高（26%）。
- 临床启示：对CHD患儿，尤其出生≤33周的早产儿，应加强神经发育监测并提供针对性早期干预，以改善其认知和运动结局。

**原文摘要**：Neurodevelopmental impairment is common in both prematurity and congenital heart disease (CHD). Preterm infants with CHD may have a "double hit," with worse neurodevelopmental impairments than seen in prematurity or CHD alone. This single-center retrospective cohort study included children with CHD who underwent cardiac surgery in infancy (2007-2020) and completed neurodevelopmental testing. Using the Bayley Scales of Infant and Toddler Development, Third Edition, we assessed cognitive, language, and motor outcomes. Patients were stratified by gestational age: extremely/very/moderately preterm (≤33 weeks), late-preterm (34-36 weeks), early-term (37-38 weeks), or term/postterm (≥39 weeks). Multivariable linear regression was used to model Bayley scores, adjusting for demographic, medical, and surgical covariates. Among 1350 infants who underwent cardiac surgery, 513 (38%) completed Bayley scale assessment at median age 28 months (interquartile range, 19-34). 448 (87%) were full term and 65 (13%) preterm. After multivariable adjustment, gestational age remained a significant independent predictor of cognitive and motor scores. Children born ≤33 weeks had the worst outcomes, with age-adjusted cognitive and motor composite scores 11 (95% CI, 5-17) and 15 (95% CI, 8-22) points lower, respectively, than those born at term. Other significant predictors included lower caregiver education, Black race, longer hospitalization, and genetic diagnosis. These models explained 17% to 26% of the variance in Bayley scores. The highest explained variance was in the motor domain. Prematurity is a significant risk factor for adverse cognitive and motor outcomes in children with CHD, especially in those born ≤33 weeks. Enhanced neurodevelopmental surveillance and targeted early intervention strategies are warranted for this vulnerable population.

## Methods of Assessing Postoperative Pain in Infants (0-12 Months): A Scoping Review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 00:42
- 期刊: Paediatr Neonatal Pain
- 国家/单位: 英国 · Faculty of Medicine and Health University of Sydney Sydney New South Wales Australia
- 通讯作者: Popat H
- DOI: 10.1002/pne2.70043
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/pne2.70043) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42621970/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统梳理0-12个月婴儿术后疼痛的评估方法及其心理测量学属性，并描述临床医生和父母使用的评估工具现状。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入35篇研究（4篇系统评价、8项试验、23项原始研究），识别出20种临床医生使用的疼痛评估方法；术后疼痛分类不一致且缺乏明确区分，疼痛评分时长、频率和记录存在差异，部分工具未经术后疼痛或该年龄段验证，且未发现父母使用的正式疼痛评估方法研究。
- 临床启示：现有评估方法存在主观性与标准化不足，需采用多维度方法并结合持续监测，同时应鼓励家庭参与婴儿术后疼痛评估。

**原文摘要**：Infants undergoing surgery experience postoperative pain beyond acute short-lasting procedural pain and have variable analgesic needs. Assessing pain in this nonverbal population presents challenges for clinicians and parents. Given the short- and long-term effects of pain and its treatment, timely and accurate assessment of pain is vital. The aim of this scoping review was to map pain assessment methods used by clinicians and parents to assess postoperative pain in infants aged 0 to 12 months and describe their psychometric properties. The review was guided by Arksey and O'Malley's (2005) five-stage framework. We searched Embase, Medline, PsycINFO, Scopus, Cochrane, CINAHL from January 2014 to March 2026 to retrieve eligible studies. Thirty-five studies (four systematic reviews, eight trials, and 23 original research studies) were included. We identified 20 different pain assessment methods used by clinicians. Postoperative pain categorization was inconsistent and lacked clear differentiation. There were inconsistencies in the duration, frequency, and documentation of pain scores. Several studies highlighted a discordance between pain scores, clinical judgment, and treatment decisions. Some tools had been used without being validated for postoperative pain or the age group studied. We found no studies of formal pain assessment methods used by parents. Observational subjectivity, the lack of current validity testing and standardization in methods, together with the multi-faceted nature of pain reflects the need for a multidimensional approach to assess pain. Research is warranted on continuous monitoring of postoperative pain in infants and family involvement in pain assessment.

- 核心发现/数据：可预防性死亡源于家庭、社区、转诊路径、初级卫生保健、医院、筹资、治理和问责等多环节的相互关联失灵；孤立的设施项目及短期人道主义响应无法解决问题。摘要未提供具体统计数据，但强调需建立涵盖社区卫生工作者、初级设施、紧急转运、转诊医院、公私机构和可持续筹资的问责性持续照护链。

**原文摘要**：This policy-oriented commentary aims to interpret the drivers of maternal and neonatal mortality in Somalia through the Three Delays Model and the World Health Organization health-system building-block framework and to propose a Somalia-specific policy framework for strengthening the continuum of care. It draws on purposively selected peer-reviewed literature, national and international reports, and health-system evidence. Its distinctive contribution is to frame maternal and neonatal mortality as both a women's health and reproductive-health equity crisis and a priority for health-system reform. The commentary argues that preventable deaths result from interconnected failures across households, communities, referral pathways, primary health care, hospitals, financing, governance, and accountability, and that isolated facility-based projects and short-term humanitarian responses are insufficient. Progress requires an accountable continuum of care linking community health workers, primary health-care facilities, emergency transport, referral hospitals, public and private providers, health-information systems, and sustainable financing. Priority actions include strengthening district referral networks, round-the-clock emergency obstetric and newborn care, workforce capacity and equitable deployment, financial and transport protection, government-led partner coordination, regulation of public and private providers, and maternal and perinatal death surveillance and response. Implementation should be monitored through disaggregated indicators, routine performance and accountability reviews, clearly assigned responsibilities, time-bound corrective actions, and implementation research.

## Incidence Trends and Disease Burden of Vascular Intestinal Disorders: A Population-Based Study in China and Worldwide from 1990 to 2021.

- 期刊: Vasc Health Risk Manag · 中科院医学3区(外周血管病4区) · IF 3.2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Affiliated Hospital of Jinggangshan University
- 通讯作者: Huang L
- DOI: 10.2147/VHRM.S609512
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.2147/VHRM.S609512) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42621617/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：基于GBD 2021数据，评估1990至2021年中国及全球血管性肠道疾病（VID）的发病率趋势与疾病负担。
- 核心发现/数据：全球VID年龄标准化发病率从每10万人18.81降至15.98（AAPC：-0.52%），中国从5.92降至5.03（AAPC：-0.50%），但中国绝对病例数因人口结构变化近翻倍；年轻男性ASIR和ASMR较高，而75岁及以上女性发病率和死亡率更高。
- 临床启示：尽管年龄标准化率下降，全球及中国绝对疾病负担持续加重，需针对老年女性和年轻男性等高危人群加强早期诊断、改善医疗可及性，并将VID预防整合入非传染性疾病管理框架。

**原文摘要**：Vascular intestinal disorders (VID), characterized by inadequate intestinal perfusion, lead to severe complications like tissue ischemia, infarction, and inflammation. This study evaluates global and Chinese VID trends and burden from 1990 to 2021 using Global Burden of Disease (GBD) 2021 data. Age-standardized incidence, prevalence, mortality, and disability-adjusted life years (DALYs) were extracted. Joinpoint regression analyzed temporal trends, computing average annual percentage changes (AAPC) and 95% confidence intervals. Subgroup analyses covered age, sex, and socio-demographic index. Globally, the age-standardized incidence rate (ASIR) declined from 18.81 to 15.98 per 100,000 (AAPC: -0.52%); in China, it fell from 5.92 to 5.03 (AAPC: -0.50%). However, China's absolute cases nearly doubled due to demographic shifts. Significant gender and age disparities emerged: younger males showed higher ASIR and ASMR, while women ≥75 years had elevated morbidity and mortality. Despite declining age-standardized rates, the absolute VID burden escalates globally and in China. Disparities disproportionately affect elderly women and younger males. Enhanced diagnostics, equitable access, and targeted prevention are urgent. Stakeholders must prioritize integrative VID prevention strategies within non-communicable disease frameworks to mitigate this growing concern.

## Biliary atresia incidence in Taiwan before, during, and after the COVID-19 pandemic: A nationwide population-based study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 00:42
- 期刊: J Pediatr Gastroenterol Nutr · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/胃肠肝病学4区/营养学4区) · IF 3.1 · JCR Q1

- 通讯作者: Tsai YW
- DOI: 10.1002/jpn3.70542

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估台湾COVID-19疫情及其非药物干预措施是否与胆道闭锁发生率及晚期Kasai手术比例变化相关。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入586例BA患儿，总体发生率1.55/万活产；与无NPI期相比，NPI期调整后IRR为1.08（95%CI 0.84-1.38），NPI放松期为0.58（95%CI 0.19-1.82），后NPI期为1.46（95%CI 0.98-2.19），发生率未见显著下降。晚期Kasai比例从无NPI期的35.3%降至后NPI期的16.0%（OR 0.38，95%CI 0.14-1.08）。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate whether COVID-19 nonpharmaceutical interventions (NPIs) were associated with changes in biliary atresia (BA) incidence and in late Kasai portoenterostomy in Taiwan. We conducted a nationwide population-based cohort study of all confirmed BA cases in Taiwan from January 2004 to December 2023. Monthly BA incidence was calculated per 10,000 live births. COVID-19 policy phases were defined as No NPI (Jan 2004-Dec 2019), NPI (Jan 2020-Nov 2022), NPI-relaxation (Dec 2022-Feb 2023), and Post-NPI (Mar-Dec 2023). Phase-specific incidence rate ratios (IRRs) were estimated using Poisson regression with live births as an offset and calendar-month fixed effects. The proportion of late Kasai decreased from 35.3% in No NPI to 16.0% (4/25) in Post-NPI (odds ratio 0.38, 95% CI 0.14-1.08). The incidence of BA in Taiwan did not decline during the COVID-19 NPI period, suggesting limited impact of reduced infectious exposure on BA occurrence. A trend toward fewer late Kasai procedures in the post-NPI phase may reflect improved early detection or care delivery.

## Operative Planning in Esophageal Atresia With Right Arch-Based Vascular Rings: A Referral-Center Experience.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:36
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Boston Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Zendejas B (Benjamin.Zendejas@childrens.harvard.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163401
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163401) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42624202/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：总结先天性食管闭锁合并右位主动脉弓相关血管环的临床特征、再手术模式及手术规划经验，并提出“ trapped esophagus ”这一新概念。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入24例患者，83%为C型食管闭锁；血管环亚型以右位主动脉弓+迷走左锁骨下动脉（71%）最常见，双主动脉弓占17%，右位主动脉弓+左侧动脉韧带占13%。超声心动图与术中发现的符合率为70%，错失血管环占20%。84%为外院转诊患者，其中75%需再手术；再手术原因包括气道/血管压迫（n=10）、食管闭锁相关并发症（n=8）及初次修复不完整（n=4）。新描述的“trapped esophagus”（n=3）指吻合口被未修复的血管环外压而无内在狭窄，导致吞咽困难并需再手术。
- 临床启示：对食管闭锁合并右位主动脉弓的患儿，术前应行横断面影像学检查和多学科规划，考虑一期联合修复食管闭锁与血管环，以减少再手术率和并发症。

**原文摘要**：Esophageal atresia (EA) with complete vascular ring (VR) presents complex airway, esophageal, and vascular challenges. We reviewed our referral-center experience to characterize morbidity, patterns of reoperation, and operative planning considerations. We reviewed children with EA and VR managed between 2017-2025. Patients were categorized as primary or referred (prior management elsewhere). Data included anatomy, imaging, operative approach, and outcomes. Twenty-four patients were identified (83% type C EA). VR subtypes included right aortic arch with aberrant left subclavian artery (RAA+ALSCA 71%), double aortic arch (17%), and right aortic arch with left ligamentum (13%). Echocardiography was concordant with operative findings in 70%, discordant in 10%, and missed a VR in 20%. Twenty patients (84%) were referred; 75% required reoperation. Referred patients had prior or active leak (42%), refractory stricture (60%), recurrent fistula (28%), dysphagia (90%), and vocal fold movement impairment (53%). Reoperation addressed airway pathology (e.g. tracheomalacia) or vascular compression (n=10), EA-related complications (n=8), and incomplete EA or VR repair (n=4). We identified and describe a new entity: "trapped esophagus" (n=3) characterized by extrinsic vascular compression of an EA anastomosis positioned within an unrepaired VR (RAA+ALSCA) without intrinsic stricture, leading to dysphagia and requiring reoperation. Four patients underwent multidisciplinary primary EA+VR concurrent repair at our institution, without complications. EA+VR can be associated with high morbidity and need for reoperation. Patients with EA + right arch on echocardiography could benefit from pre-operative cross-sectional imaging and multidisciplinary planning to consider primary EA+VR concurrent repair, taking into consideration reasons for reoperation. ## Posterior Urethral Valves In Children: Achieving Renal Preservation Through Integrated Multidisciplinary Care In A Resource-Limited Region.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:36
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: University of Peradeniya · Sirimavo Bandaranaike Specialized Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Hettiarachchi M (drmathula@yahoo.co.uk)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163392
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163392) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42624201/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估资源有限地区综合多学科管理下儿童后尿道瓣膜（PUV）的肾脏保留效果、手术结局及产前vs产后检出对预后的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：120例患儿中70.0%保留肾功能，64.2%肾功能正常；产前检出组（58例，48.3%）虽更早干预，但正常肾功能率（53.4% vs 74.2%）、肾功能保留率（58.6% vs 80.6%）均显著低于产后组，且手术次数更多（3.29 vs 2.13）、尿流改道需求更高（24.1% vs 4.8%）。多因素分析显示重度羊水过少、致密梗阻瓣膜、V级膀胱输尿管反流及需尿流改道是肾功能不良的独立预测因子。
- 临床启示：即使在资源受限地区，系统化多学科管理也能实现良好肾脏结局；产前检出组的较差预后反映疾病固有严重程度，应重视疾病严重度分层并早期积极手术干预。

**原文摘要**：Posterior urethral valves (PUV) remain the leading cause of congenital obstructive uropathy and chronic kidney disease (CKD) in male children. Outcomes vary widely across healthcare systems, with end-stage renal disease (ESRD) rates of 10-25% reported in international series. We present a single-centre PUV cohort, examining renal preservation, surgical outcomes, and the prognostic impact of detection timing. A retrospective review of 120 consecutive patients with PUV managed between 2018 and 2024 was conducted. Patients were stratified by antenatal versus postnatal detection. The primary outcome was renal functional status at last follow-up, classified according to CKD stage. Secondary outcomes included procedural burden, urinary diversion requirement, and predictors of poor renal outcome. Overall renal function was preserved (normal or mild impairment) in 84 of 120 patients (70.0%; 95% CI 61.0-77.9), with 77 patients (64.2%; 95% CI 55.3-72.3) achieving normal renal function. Mild impairment (CKD stage 1-2) was present in 7 patients (5.8%), moderate impairment (CKD stage 3) in 29 (24.2%), and severe impairment/ESRD in 7 (5.8%). ESRD occurred in 7 patients (5.8%; 95% CI 2.4-11.6). Antenatal detection was achieved in 58 patients (48.3%). Despite earlier diagnosis and intervention, the antenatal group demonstrated lower rates of normal renal function (53.4% vs 74.2%; absolute difference 20.8%; OR 0.40, 95% CI 0.19-0.84; p=0.008), lower rates of preserved renal function (58.6% vs 80.6%; OR 0.34, 95% CI 0.15-0.78; p=0.009), higher procedural burden (mean 3.29 vs 2.13 procedures; p<0.001), and greater requirement for urinary diversion (24.1% vs 4.8%; p<0.001). On multivariable analysis, severe oligohydramnios (OR 4.2, 95% CI 1.8-9.7), dense occluding valves (OR 3.1, 95% CI 1.4-6.8), Grade V VUR (OR 2.8, 95% CI 1.2-6.5), and urinary diversion requirement (OR 2.4, 95% CI 1.1-5.3) were independent predictors of poor renal outcome. A total of 323 procedures were performed. This cohort demonstrates that systematic, multidisciplinary PUV management is associated with favorable renal outcomes even in a resource-limited setting. The paradox of antenatal detection, where earlier identification is associated with worse outcomes, reflects inherent disease severity rather than treatment inadequacy. These findings highlight the importance of disease-severity stratification and early aggressive surgical intervention.

## HABP4/VIM-Mediated Focal Adhesion Assembly Regulates Enteric Neural Crest Cell Migration via the FAK/SRC Pathway.

- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163396) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42624200/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探究HABP4在肠神经系统发育中调控肠神经嵴细胞迁移的分子机制。
- 核心发现/数据：HABP4在ENCCs中高表达，敲低后显著损害hESC来源NCCs的迁移能力；机制上HABP4与VIM相互作用，促进黏着斑组装并激活FAK/SRC信号通路，而FAK激动剂可部分挽救迁移缺陷；HSCR患者中发现的HABP4变异（R379W）会损害ENCCs迁移。
- 临床启示：揭示HABP4/FAK/SRC轴在HSCR发病中的关键作用，为HSCR的分子诊断和靶向干预提供了新线索。

**原文摘要**：The enteric nervous system (ENS) arises from enteric neural crest cells (ENCCs), and defects in this process can lead to Hirschsprung's disease (HSCR), a congenital disorder characterized by the absence of enteric neurons in the distal colon. Our previous work has identified HABP4 as a potential susceptibility gene for HSCR; however, the mechanisms by which it regulates ENS development remain unclear. Single-cell RNA sequencing combined with immunofluorescence staining was used to characterize the spatiotemporal expression pattern of HABP4 in mouse intestinal tissues. HABP4-knockdown human embryonic stem cell-derived neural crest cells (hESC-derived NCCs) were generated to evaluate its role in ENS development, and cell migration was assessed using Transwell assays. Protein-protein interaction network analysis integrated with transcriptomic profiling was performed to identify HABP4-interacting partners and downstream signaling pathways, which were further validated by AlphaFold-based structural modeling and co-immunoprecipitation. In addition, pharmacological rescue experiments were conducted to examine the functional involvement of key downstream effectors, and the pathogenicity of HABP4 variants identified in HSCR patients was further evaluated. HABP4 was highly and specifically expressed in ENCCs. Knockdown of HABP4 significantly impaired the migratory capacity of hESC-derived NCCs. Transcriptomic analysis revealed significant enrichment of focal adhesion-related gene sets following HABP4 knockdown. Mechanistically, HABP4 interacted with VIM to promote focal adhesion assembly and activate the FAK/SRC signaling cascade, thereby facilitating ENCCs migration. Notably, treatment with a focal adhesion kinase (FAK) agonist partially rescued the migration defects induced by HABP4 knockdown, and the HABP4 variant (R379W) identified in HSCR patients was found to impair ENCCs migration. HABP4 is localized at the leading edge of migrating ENCCs, where it interacts with VIM to promote focal adhesion assembly and activate FAK/SRC signaling.

**原文摘要**：Extracorporeal life support (ECLS) is used as rescue therapy for refractory cardiopulmonary failure. Population-level evidence on prognosis and long-term sequelae in patients requiring ECLS during pregnancy or the peripartum period remains limited. We performed a nationwide retrospective dual-cohort study using linked Taiwanese databases (2001-22). In the delivery cohort, 247 ECLS pregnancies were compared with 3 970 948 non-ECLS pregnancies. In the ECLS cohort, 247 pregnancy-associated ECLS cases were compared with 2112 non-pregnant women of reproductive age receiving ECLS. Outcomes included 42-day maternal mortality and complications, neonatal outcomes, and long-term maternal outcomes beyond 42 days postpartum. The incidence of pregnancy-associated ECLS was 6.2 per 100 000 deliveries and increased over time (P trend <0.001). Leading indications were amniotic fluid embolism (26.7%), peripartum cardiomyopathy (18.6%), and respiratory failure (12.6%), with highest mortality in obstetric indications. Maternal mortality at 42 days was 41.3%, with renal failure (36.4%), major bleeding (11.3%) and stroke (10.9%) as major morbidities. Neonates had higher risks of preterm birth, low birth weight, and major morbidity. Beyond 42 days postpartum, ECLS-exposed pregnant patients had higher all-cause mortality (adjusted hazard ratio [aHR] 28.0, 95% confidence interval [CI] 16.8-46.8), cardiovascular mortality, permanent dialysis, and psychiatric disorders. In the ECLS cohort, pregnancy-associated ECLS had lower in-hospital mortality (43.7% vs 53.8%) and better long-term survival (aHR 0.42, 95% CI 0.23-0.78) than nonpregnant recipients. Pregnancy-associated ECLS is rare but is increasing in incidence, and identifies a clinically high-risk population with substantial early maternal and neonatal risk. Multidisciplinary follow-up is required for survivors of pregnancy-associated ECLS in view of their ongoing risk of long-term cardiovascular, renal, and psychiatric sequelae.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 12:36

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：明确极低出生体重儿中胎粪相关肠梗阻灌肠治疗的失败率及潜在非胎粪相关肠梗阻外科病理的临床警示指标。
- 核心发现/数据：31例接受灌肠治疗的极低出生体重儿中，20例成功、11例失败；仅失败组检出非胎粪相关肠梗阻外科病理（36% [4/11] vs 0% [0/20]，p=0.010），包括先天性巨结肠、坏死性小肠结肠炎、胎粪性腹膜炎和回肠狭窄。失败组总生存率显著低于成功组（55% vs 95%，p=0.013）。

**原文摘要**：Meconium-related ileus (MRI) often mimics other surgical pathologies in extremely low birth weight infants (ELBWIs), leading to diagnostic difficulties. This study aimed to clarify the clinical presentation and management outcomes of enema therapy in ELBWIs with a specific focus on the clinical indicators of underlying non-MRI surgical pathologies. A retrospective review was conducted of 31 ELBWIs (birth weight < 1000 g) who received enema therapy for suspected MRI at a single tertiary center between 2008 and 2024. Infants were categorized into Success (n = 20) and Failure (n = 11) groups. The clinical characteristics, timing of intervention, and underlying pathologies were compared. The overall survival was significantly lower in the Failure group than in the Success group (55% vs. 95%, p = 0.013). A descriptive review of these non-MRI patients highlighted that an atypically late initial enema therapy or a high frequency of repetitive procedures was critical clinical features that masked the underlying pathologies. In ELBWIs with suspected MRI, the failure of enema therapy was significantly associated with underlying non-MRI surgical pathologies. Although these findings require validation in larger cohorts, delayed initiation of therapeutic enema or repeated procedures without sustained improvement should prompt reassessment for non-MRI surgical pathology and early surgical consultation.

**原文摘要**：Endometriosis is a chronic inflammatory disorder that affects approximately 10% of reproductive-age women and is frequently associated with subfertility. These findings suggest that lactoferrin may represent a promising non-hormonal approach for mitigating endometriosis-associated subfertility.

**原文摘要**：Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) is a systemic rheumatic disease that often impacts women of childbearing age. Women and men with RA experience higher rates of subfertility compared to the general population.
