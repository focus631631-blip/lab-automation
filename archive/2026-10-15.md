**原文摘要**：Congenital cytomegalovirus (cCMV) is the most common congenital infection and a leading cause of sensorineural hearing loss as well as neurodevelopmental impairment. In recent years, awareness, screening, and management strategies for cCMV have evolved. We conducted a retrospective cohort analysis using data from the CCMVnet registry ( www.ccmvnet.org ), an international multicenter database collecting anonymized data on children with confirmed cCMV infection. A total of 1653 patients diagnosed between 2011 and 2024 were included and divided into two cohorts: 2011-2016 (n = 566) and 2017-2024 (n = 1087). The year 2017 was selected as a pragmatic cutoff, corresponding to the year of publication of the European expert consensus statement on congenital CMV. Children were identified through antenatal, clinical, hearing-related, and newborn screening pathways. Symptomatic cCMV at birth was defined by the presence of at least one predefined clinical, laboratory, hearing, or neuroimaging finding based on previously published criteria. A comparative analysis between two distinct time periods was conducted, encompassing neonatal clinical findings, neuroimaging results, treatment strategies, and long-term outcomes. Newborns classified as symptomatic at birth decreased in the more recent cohort, from 49.2% in P1 to 44.2% in P2 (p = 0.07). The use of intravenous ganciclovir decreased from 7.3% to 2.8% (p < 0.001), while the use of oral valganciclovir alone increased from 43.8% to 78.7% (p < 0.001). **中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估动态可视化疼痛评估工具在儿科疼痛管理中的实施效果，以推动护理从经验性判断转向数据驱动的精准评估。
- 核心发现/数据：在5个临床病房的阶梯楔形整群随机试验中，纳入15名婴儿并分析45个视频片段，系统同步高清面部视频与床旁生理监测，实时生成疼痛轨迹曲线和趋势警报。该工具使护理响应延迟从15.4分钟降至6.7分钟（P<0.001），评估一致性ICC为0.71-0.93。儿科满意度评分升高，父母焦虑水平下降。

**原文摘要**：Effective pediatric pain management relies on accurate assessment; however, current behavioral scales are limited by observer subjectivity and fail to capture dynamic fluctuations. The primary goal of this protocol is to detail the implementation and evaluation of a dynamic visualized pain assessment tool, providing a standardized methodology to transition nursing practice from experience-based estimation to data-driven precision. Using a stepped-wedge cluster randomized trial design across five clinical wards, the study enrolled 15 infants and analyzed 45 video segments across three clinical contexts: baseline, acute procedural pain, and postoperative recovery. Concurrently, pediatric satisfaction scores rose, and parental anxiety levels decreased. These findings suggest that dynamic visualization may improve clinical efficiency and patient outcomes through timely, objective decision support.

**原文摘要**：The enteric nervous system (ENS) is the main branch of the peripheral nervous system that innervates the gastrointestinal tract controlling vital functions. Together, these findings provide mechanistic insights into how Notch signalling disruption may contribute to the pathogenesis of human intestinal aganglionosis.

**原文摘要**：To estimate the birth prevalence and survival of infants with congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH) in a recent birth cohort in New South Wales (NSW) and the Australian Capital Territory (ACT), Australia, and to characterise the clinical factors that contribute to mortality. A retrospective population-based cohort study of infants with CDH. Data were obtained from 10 neonatal intensive care units (NICU) within NSW and the ACT between January 2007 and December 2023. The main outcome measure was NICU survival. The cohort consisted of 336 infants with CDH, establishing an estimated birth prevalence of CDH of 19.3 per 100 000 live births admitted to NICU. A total of 286 infants (85.1%) survived to surgery, with the majority (71.0%) undergoing an abdominal approach repair. The cohort’s overall survival rate was 78.6%, increasing to 85.3% in term infants with an isolated CDH defect. Survival rates varied significantly year to year. Infants who are antenatally diagnosed, have a low birth weight, low Apgar score, and severe metabolic acidosis have poorer outcomes. The use of base excess as an additional predictor of mortality requires further validation.
