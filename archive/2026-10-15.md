**原文摘要**：Ebstein anomaly is a rare congenital malformation of the tricuspid valve and right ventricle characterized by marked heterogeneity in anatomy, physiology, and clinical presentation. This broad phenotypic spectrum strongly influences the natural history of the disease, the timing and type of intervention, and ultimately long-term outcomes. Patients range from critically ill neonates with severe hemodynamic compromise and circular shunts to asymptomatic children and adults with progressive tricuspid regurgitation, right ventricular dysfunction, or arrhythmias, as well as survivors of previous surgical interventions. Accordingly, long-term outcomes are best understood within the context of the presenting phenotype and its associated management pathway rather than as a single aggregate measure of survival. Contemporary management therefore requires phenotype-specific decision-making. This review summarizes long-term outcomes across the major clinical and therapeutic subgroups, including critically ill neonates managed with the Starnes procedure, stable neonates and infants undergoing biventricular repair, patients treated with historical repair techniques, contemporary cone reconstruction, and tricuspid valve replacement. Particular emphasis is placed on transplant-free survival, valve durability, ventricular remodeling, arrhythmia burden, functional status, quality of life, and the need for reintervention.

**原文摘要**：Congenital cytomegalovirus (cCMV) is the most common congenital infection and a leading cause of sensorineural hearing loss as well as neurodevelopmental impairment. In recent years, awareness, screening, and management strategies for cCMV have evolved. This study aimed to assess temporal changes in the clinical presentation, treatment, and outcomes of children diagnosed with cCMV infection. We conducted a retrospective cohort analysis using data from the CCMVnet registry ( www.ccmvnet.org ), an international multicenter database collecting anonymized data on children with confirmed cCMV infection. A total of 1653 patients diagnosed between 2011 and 2024 were included and divided into two cohorts: 2011-2016 (n = 566) and 2017-2024 (n = 1087). The year 2017 was selected as a pragmatic cutoff, corresponding to the year of publication of the European expert consensus statement on congenital CMV. Children were identified through antenatal, clinical, hearing-related, and newborn screening pathways. Symptomatic cCMV at birth was defined by the presence of at least one predefined clinical, laboratory, hearing, or neuroimaging finding based on previously published criteria. A comparative analysis between two distinct time periods was conducted, encompassing neonatal clinical findings, neuroimaging results, treatment strategies, and long-term outcomes. Newborns classified as symptomatic at birth decreased in the more recent cohort, from 49.2% in P1 to 44.2% in P2 (p = 0.07). The use of intravenous ganciclovir decreased from 7.3% to 2.8% (p < 0.001), while the use of oral valganciclovir alone increased from 43.8% to 78.7% (p < 0.001). Differences in long-term outcomes were observed between the two periods, with motor impairment at 12 months decreasing from 16.5% to 6.2% (p < 0.001) and any long-term sequelae at 36 months decreasing from 40.0% to 31.0% (p = 0.05).  This study highlights temporal differences in the clinical spectrum, management, and outcomes of children with congenital CMV infection in Europe. The higher proportion of asymptomatic and mildly symptomatic children in the latter period may reflect changes in maternal and neonatal screening practices. The observed temporal differences provide insight into the evolving clinical landscape of cCMV diagnosis and management, while their underlying causes cannot be determined from the present data. • Congenital cytomegalovirus (cCMV) infection is the leading infectious cause of sensorineural hearing loss and neurodevelopmental impairment in children. • Clinical management of cCMV has evolved substantially over the last decade, particularly following the publication of the European expert consensus statement in 2017 and alongside expanding screening strategies. • In our cohort, children diagnosed with cCMV after 2017 presented with milder clinical manifestations and experienced fewer long-term sequelae than those diagnosed before 2017. • Temporal changes in diagnostic pathways, treatment practices, and clinical outcomes were observed between the two study periods.

## Impact of a Dynamic Visualized Pain Assessment Tool on Pediatric Pain Management: A Stepped-Wedge Cluster Randomized Trial.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:11
- 期刊: J Vis Exp
- 国家/单位: General Surgery and Neonatal Surgery Nursing Team · Capital Medical University Affiliated Capital Children's Medical Center; YueWangshouerkyb@sina.com
- 通讯作者: Wang Y (YueWangshouerkyb@sina.com)
- DOI: 10.3791/71023
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.3791/71023) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42872766/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估动态可视化疼痛评估工具在儿科疼痛管理中的实施效果，以推动护理从经验性判断转向数据驱动的精准评估。
- 核心发现/数据：在5个临床病房的阶梯楔形整群随机试验中，纳入15名婴儿并分析45个视频片段，系统同步高清面部视频与床旁生理监测，实时生成疼痛轨迹曲线和趋势警报。该工具使护理响应延迟从15.4分钟降至6.7分钟（P<0.001），评估一致性ICC为0.71-0.93。儿科满意度评分升高，父母焦虑水平下降。
- 临床启示：动态可视化疼痛评估可为临床提供及时、客观的决策支持，提高儿科疼痛管理效率与评估一致性，并改善患儿及家属体验。

**原文摘要**：Effective pediatric pain management relies on accurate assessment; however, current behavioral scales are limited by observer subjectivity and fail to capture dynamic fluctuations. The primary goal of this protocol is to detail the implementation and evaluation of a dynamic visualized pain assessment tool, providing a standardized methodology to transition nursing practice from experience-based estimation to data-driven precision. Using a stepped-wedge cluster randomized trial design across five clinical wards, the study enrolled 15 infants and analyzed 45 video segments across three clinical contexts: baseline, acute procedural pain, and postoperative recovery. The system captured high-definition facial video synchronized with bedside physiological monitoring to generate real-time pain trajectory curves and trend-based alerts. Generalized linear mixed models showed that the tool reduced nursing response latency from 15.4 to 6.7 min (P < 0.001) and increased assessment consistency (intraclass correlation coefficient [ICC]: 0.71-0.93). Concurrently, pediatric satisfaction scores rose, and parental anxiety levels decreased. These findings suggest that dynamic visualization may improve clinical efficiency and patient outcomes through timely, objective decision support.

## Notch signalling governs human enteric nervous system progenitor dynamics.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:11
- 期刊: Development · 中科院生物学2区(发育生物学2区) · IF 4.0 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 英国 · Centre for Stem Cell Biology
- 通讯作者: Tsakiridis A
- DOI: 10.1242/dev.205775
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.205775) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42872509/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：利用人多能干细胞模型明确Notch信号在人肠神经系统祖细胞命运决定与动态调控中的作用。
- 核心发现/数据：Notch信号是调控人肠神经系统祖细胞动态的关键通路；定量建模显示抑制Notch可加速祖细胞分化，但未显著改变神经元与胶质细胞谱系偏向。Notch还通过影响迁移速度和方向性调控人肠神经系统祖细胞迁移。
- 临床启示：Notch信号异常可能通过扰乱肠神经系统祖细胞分化与迁移，参与人类肠道无神经节细胞症的发病。该发现为Hirschsprung病等肠神经病的机制研究和潜在干预提供了依据。

**原文摘要**：The enteric nervous system (ENS) is the main branch of the peripheral nervous system that innervates the gastrointestinal tract controlling vital functions. It arises during embryogenesis via migration, proliferation and differentiation of neural crest-derived ENS progenitors. Perturbation of these processes, caused by mutations in key signalling pathway components and transcription factors, prevents progenitor colonisation of the distal gut causing aganglionic phenotypes and enteric neuropathies such as Hirschsprung (HSCR) disease. While animal models implicate Notch signalling in ENS specification, its role in human ENS progenitor cell fate decisions remains unclear. Here, we employ a human pluripotent stem cell-based model to show that Notch signalling is a key regulator of human ENS progenitor dynamics. Quantitative modelling of our in vitro data indicates that Notch inhibition accelerates progenitor differentiation without substantially altering neuronal versus glial lineage bias. Furthermore, we establish that Notch signalling controls human ENS progenitor migration by influencing migration velocity and directionality. Together, these findings provide mechanistic insights into how Notch signalling disruption may contribute to the pathogenesis of human intestinal aganglionosis.

**原文摘要**：Congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH) is a severe congenital anomaly characterized by impairment of both diaphragm and lung development in utero. CDH presents as a spectrum of forms and severities, with diaphragm defects arising in the dorsal/posterior region typically correlating with more severe pulmonary disease and higher risk of mortality than those appearing in ventral/anterior regions. The genetic etiology underlying CDH is complex, with many implicated genes showing variable expressivity and incomplete penetrance in both human patients and mouse models. Here we present in vivo validation of two genes previously unassociated with CDH: the CDC42-interacting kinase CDC42BPB; and CNOT1, a scaffolding protein of the CCR4-NOT protein complex, critical for mRNA regulation through modifications such as deadenylation. Each gene was found to have a damaging, de novo missense variant in a recent large-scale CDH patient sequencing screen. Loss of Cdc42bpb leads to ventral diaphragmatic hernias, heart septal defects, and minor lung epithelial differentiation defects in mouse embryos. Installation of the orthologous patient-specific missense variant through CRISPR/Cas9 editing leads to less severe ventral diaphragm defects. Mouse embryos with either one or two copies of the orthologous Cnot1 variant, c.1867C > T (p.R623W), develop dorsal diaphragmatic hernias with low (<50%) penetrance, and mutants showed alterations in mRNA isoform expression consistent with the molecular role of Cnot1 in RNA splicing. These results underscore the power of in vivo functional modeling to validate genes and patient-specific variants uncovered by patient sequencing, reveal two previously unrecognized genetic causes of CDH, and highlight the heterogeneity of different patient anatomic presentations.
