## Tapering enteroplasty versus primary anastomosis without tapering in neonatal small bowel atresia: a systematic review and meta-analysis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 00:39
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 印度 · Government Medical College
- 通讯作者: Pimpalwar A (ashwinpimpalwar@hotmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06600-6
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06600-6) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42635794/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统比较新生儿小肠闭锁术中行锥形肠成形术（TAP）与直接一期吻合（非TAP）的术后结局。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入6项回顾性队列研究（278例新生儿，TAP 72例，非TAP 206例）。TAP组与非TAP组在术后并发症（RR 0.91，95% CI 0.67-1.23）、再手术（RR 0.90，95% CI 0.38-2.15）、死亡率（RR 0.70，95% CI 0.20-2.39）、住院时间（MD 0.25天，95% CI -17.20至17.70）及达全肠内喂养时间（MD 0.21天，95% CI -5.01至5.43）上均无统计学显著差异，但置信区间宽、异质性高；各中心TAP使用率仅21%–33%，提示其被选择性用于解剖复杂病例。
- 临床启示：目前证据不支持对新生儿小肠闭锁常规行TAP，其潜在获益可能仅限于近端严重扩张、口径悬殊的复杂病例，而非普遍适用；需前瞻性多中心研究明确患者选择标准。

**原文摘要**：Small bowel atresia (SBA) affects approximately 1 in 1,500-4,500 neonates and represents one of the most common congenital causes of intestinal obstruction. Management of the characteristically dilated proximal bowel segment, whether by tapering enteroplasty (TAP) or primary anastomosis without caliber reduction (non-TAP), has remained controversial for five decades in the absence of randomized evidence. We conducted a systematic review and meta-analysis to systematically synthesize comparative evidence on postoperative outcomes following TAP versus non-TAP in neonatal SBA. A PRISMA 2020-compliant systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO: CRD420261329962) searched MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, and Cochrane from inception through April 2026. Pooled risk ratios (RR) and mean differences (MD) were computed using DerSimonian-Laird random-effects models. Leave-one-out sensitivity analyses were performed for all outcomes. Six retrospective cohort studies (n = 278 neonates; TAP: 72, non-TAP: 206) were included. No statistically significant differences were demonstrated for postoperative complications (RR 0.91; 95% CI 0.67-1.23; I²=0%), reoperation (RR 0.90; 95% CI 0.38-2.15; I²=0%), mortality (RR 0.70; 95% CI 0.20-2.39; I²=0%), length of hospital stay (MD 0.25 days; 95% CI - 17.20 to 17.70; I²=94%), time to full enteral feeding (MD 0.21 days; 95% CI - 5.01 to 5.43; I²=62%), or time to first enteral feeding (MD - 4.79 days; 95% CI - 11.50 to 1.92; I²=93%). Confidence intervals were wide for all outcomes. TAP was performed in only 21-33% of patients across all cohorts, a pattern consistent with selective reservation for anatomically complex cases. No statistically significant postoperative advantage was identified for routine TAP over primary anastomosis. However, equivalence cannot be concluded given wide confidence intervals and substantial heterogeneity. Critically, the consistent minority utilization of TAP across centers, combined with directionally opposing individual-study findings, points to patient selection rather than operative technique as a likely dominant outcome driver. We propose, as a hypothesis for prospective evaluation rather than an evidence-based recommendation, that any benefit of tapering is most likely to be realized in anatomically complex cases with severe proximal dilation and marked caliber mismatch, rather than through universal application. Prospective multicenter studies with standardized atresia phenotyping and objective TAP-selection criteria are required. CRD420261329962.

## In biliary atresia, cholangiography still remains the diagnostic gold standard.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 00:39
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Lyon
- 通讯作者: Kassir R (rani.kassir@outlook.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06603-3
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06603-3) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42635813/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨胆道闭锁（BA）的诊断标准，强调胆道造影仍应作为金标准。
- 核心发现/数据：尽管腹部超声联合血清GGT对BA诊断表现出良好性能，但该研究队列诊断时间较晚，不适用于更年幼婴儿。在BA早期，超声、GGT甚至肝活检均可能不可靠，增加延迟诊断风险。
- 临床启示：临床仍怀疑BA时，应优先保留Kasai手术的治疗窗口，不应因反复无创检查而延误胆道造影。

**原文摘要**：Early diagnosis of biliary atresia (BA) is essential to maximize the success of Kasai portoenterostomy. We read with interest the recent study reporting excellent diagnostic performance of abdominal ultrasonography combined with serum gamma-glutamyl transferase (GGT) for the diagnosis of BA. While these findings are encouraging, their applicability to younger infants deserves caution, as the study cohort was diagnosed relatively late. During the earliest stages of BA, ultrasonographic findings, serum GGT levels, and even liver biopsy may be less reliable, increasing the risk of delayed diagnosis. When BA remains clinically suspected, preserving the therapeutic window for Kasai portoenterostomy should take precedence, and cholangiography should remain the diagnostic gold standard rather than be delayed by repeated non-invasive investigations.

## Endoscopic vacuum-assisted closure for pediatric esophageal perforations: a systematic review and individual patient data meta-analysis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 00:39
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 巴西
- 通讯作者: Pimpalwar A (ashwinpimpalwar@hotmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06592-3
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06592-3) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42635807/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统评价负压辅助闭合技术（EVAC及相关真空疗法）治疗儿童食管瘘与穿孔的安全性和有效性。
- 核心发现/数据：8项观察性研究共52例患儿，其中35例提供个体患者数据，成功闭合率为94.3%（33/35）；汇总分析显示48/52成功，合并比例为99.81%（95% CI 92.11-100.00）。临床上相关并发症发生率为16/35，以狭窄最常见，6/35需挽救性手术。
- 临床启示：负压治疗对经过选择的、瘘口可及的儿童食管穿孔有较高闭合成功率，多数二次手术针对迟发狭窄而非漏口未闭；但仍需标准化前瞻性研究以制定明确推荐。

**原文摘要**：Esophageal leaks and perforations in children are uncommon, but potentially life-threatening conditions associated with substantial morbidity. Endoscopic vacuum-assisted closure (EVAC) and related vacuum-based techniques have emerged as minimally invasive alternatives to conventional management, but pediatric evidence remains limited to small observational studies. We performed a systematic review and meta-analysis to evaluate the safety and effectiveness of vacuum-based therapy for pediatric esophageal leaks and perforations. PubMed, Embase, and Cochrane Library were searched from inception to May 21, 2026. The primary outcome was a successful leak closure. Secondary outcomes included treatment escalation, clinically relevant complications, treatment duration, number of device revisions, and functional outcomes. IPD was synthesized using one-stage models accounting for clustering by study, with complementary aggregate-level analyses when compatible data were available. Eight observational studies, comprising 52 patients overall. Seven studies provided IPD for 35 patients, while one study contributed aggregate-level data only. In the IPD cohort, successful closure was achieved in 33 of 35 patients, corresponding to a crude proportion of 94.3% (95% CI, 81.4-98.4). Clinically relevant complications occurred in 16 of 35 patients, with stricture being the most frequent complication. Rescue surgery was required in 6 of 35 patients. In complementary aggregate-level analysis, successful closure was achieved in 48 of 52 patients, with a pooled proportion of 99.81% (95% CI, 92.11-100.00; I² = 0%). No evaluated predictor was significantly associated with successful closure or clinically relevant complications. Vacuum-based therapy for pediatric esophageal leaks and perforations was associated with high rates of defect closure in the available literature. Further operations were required in a minority of patients and generally addressed late complications, such as stricture, after leak closure. EVAC and related techniques appear promising for carefully selected children with accessible leaks or perforations, but standardized prospective data are needed before definitive practice recommendations can be made.

## Civil Medical Liability in Pediatric Surgery: A Systematic Review of the Literature.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:36
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 希腊 · School of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Sakelliadis EI (esakelliadis@med.uoa.gr)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163387
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163387) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42637089/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统综述儿科外科领域民事医疗责任的主要索赔原因及赔偿金额。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入6项研究，最常见索赔原因为诊断延迟或错误、治疗延迟、技术或术中错误、术后并发症或术后护理不当。现有经济数据有限，但部分案件带来显著经济负担，且法律风险不仅涉及复杂手术，也见于常规高频疾病与操作。
- 临床启示：儿科外科医生应重点防范诊断、时机、技术及术后环节的失误，以提升患者安全并减少可预防的医疗纠纷。

**原文摘要**：Civil medical liability is an important aspect of modern medical practice, especially in high-risk specialties such as pediatric surgery. The unique characteristics of pediatric surgical patients, diagnostic uncertainty, and the possibility of serious complications make the specialty vulnerable to legal claims. The purpose of this systematic review was to collect and synthesize available data on the civil medical liability of pediatric surgeons, with an emphasis on the most common causes of claims and compensation amounts, where reported. A systematic search was performed in the PubMed and Scopus databases in accordance with the PRISMA 2020 guidelines. After removing duplicates, studies were initially evaluated at the title and abstract level using the Rayyan platform, and then at the full-text level. Finally, 6 studies were included. The most common causes of claims were delayed or incorrect diagnosis, delayed treatment, technical or intraoperative errors, and postoperative complications or inadequate postoperative care. The available economic data were limited but showed that some cases were associated with a significant economic burden. Importantly, legal exposure was not confined to complex surgery but also involved routine, high-frequency conditions and procedures. Civil medical liability in pediatric surgery is mainly related to diagnostic, temporal, technical, and postoperative failures. Understanding these patterns can help improve patient safety and reduce preventable errors.

## Single-Stage Surgical Management Versus Conventional Two-Stage Management for Pediatric Choledocholithiasis: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:36
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 巴基斯坦 · Aga Khan University Hospital
- 通讯作者: Khan FA (fakhan1@stanford.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163412
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163412) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42637088/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：比较儿童胆总管结石单阶段手术（LC-LCBDE或LC-ERCP）与传统两阶段方案（ERCP后择期腹腔镜胆囊切除术）的安全性与有效性。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入8项回顾性队列研究、562例患儿。与两阶段方案相比，LC-LCBDE显著减少术后并发症（OR: 0.21, 95%CI: 0.08-0.60）和胰腺炎（OR: 0.27, 95%CI: 0.09-0.88），并缩短住院时间（MD: -1.46天, 95%CI: -2.35至-0.56），但额外操作需求更高（OR: 5.86, 95%CI: 1.05-32.7）；LC-ERCP仅缩短麻醉时间（MD: -25.5分钟, 95%CI: -49.0至-2.0），其余指标无显著差异。
- 临床启示：单阶段管理，尤其是经胆囊管LC-LCBDE联合胆囊切除术，是可行的替代方案，可减少并发症并缩短住院时间；治疗方案应由多学科团队结合机构经验个体化制定，以确保安全彻底清除结石。

**原文摘要**：The prevalence of pediatric choledocholithiasis is rising, yet the optimal management strategy remains uncertain. Pediatric practice has traditionally favored a two-stage strategy of endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography (ERCP) for ductal clearance followed by interval laparoscopic cholecystectomy. Single-stage management, combining laparoscopic cholecystectomy with either laparoscopic common bile duct exploration (LC-LCBDE) or intraoperative ERCP (LC-ERCP), offers a potential alternative. This meta-analysis aimed to compare the safety and efficacy of single-stage versus two-stage management in children. A PRISMA-compliant systematic review was registered with PROSPERO (CRD420251169007). PubMed, Embase, and Scopus were searched from inception through October 13, 2025. Primary outcomes were postoperative complication rate and length of hospital stay (LOS). Random-effects meta-analysis was performed. Eight retrospective cohort studies comprising 562 pediatric patients (single-stage: 278, conventional: 284) met inclusion criteria. Compared to conventional two-stage management, LC-LCBDE was associated with significantly fewer complications (OR: 0.21, [95% CI: 0.08, 0.60]), reduced pancreatitis (OR:0.27, [95% CI: 0.09, 0.88]), and a shorter LOS (MD: -1.46 days, [95% CI: -2.35, -0.56]), but a greater need for additional procedures (OR: 5.86, [95% CI: 1.05, 32.7]). LC-ERCP was associated with a significantly shorter anesthesia duration (MD: -25.5 minutes, [95% CI: -49.0, -2.0]) but otherwise did not differ significantly from conventional management. Readmission rates did not differ between groups. Single-stage management, particularly transcystic LC-LCBDE combined with cholecystectomy, represents a viable alternative associated with fewer complications and shorter length of stay. Treatment decisions should be guided by multidisciplinary evaluation and institutional expertise to ensure safe, definitive stone clearance.

## Recoarctation after neonatal hypoplastic aortic arch repair: impact of patch material.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:36
- 期刊: Thorac Cardiovasc Surg · 中科院医学4区(心脏和心血管系统4区/呼吸系统4区/外科4区) · IF 1.5 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: Munich · German Heart Centre Munich
- 通讯作者: Cleuziou J
- DOI: 10.1055/a-2942-3381
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1055/a-2942-3381) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42636875/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估新生儿主动脉弓发育不良修补术后再缩窄的危险因素，重点关注补片材料的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入597例新生儿，1年再缩窄累积发生率为23%。术前主动脉弓径更小（csHR 0.84/mm）、手术年龄更小（csHR 0.96/天）及补片材料与再缩窄独立相关；牛心包（csHR 2.94）和马心包（csHR 2.18）较戊二醛固定自体心包风险更高，而低温保存肺动脉同种异体补片风险更低（csHR 0.27）。
- 临床启示：选择低温保存肺动脉同种异体补片可能显著降低新生儿主动脉弓修复术后再缩窄风险，尤其对于术前弓发育不良和低龄手术患儿，应谨慎选择补片材料。

**原文摘要**：Recoarctation remains a frequent complication after neonatal aortic arch repair. This study aimed to identify risk factors for recoarctation, with particular emphasis on the patch material used for aortic enlargement. All neonates who underwent aortic arch repair with patch augmentation through median sternotomy at our institution between 2000 and 2024 were retrospectively reviewed. The primary endpoint was recoarctation, defined as any catheter-based or surgical reintervention on the reconstructed distal aortic arch. Risk factors were assessed using multivariable cause-specific Cox regression, and cumulative incidences of recoarctation and death were estimated using competing-risk methods. A total of 597 patients were included (38% female; 61% with single-ventricle physiology; median age and weight at surgery, 8 days and 3.2 kg, respectively). The cumulative incidence of recoarctation was 23% at 1 year. Smaller preoperative aortic arch dimensions (cause-specific hazard ratio [csHR], 0.84 per mm; 95% CI, 0.73-0.97), younger age at surgery (csHR, 0.96 per day; 95% CI, 0.93-0.99), and patch material were independently associated with recoarctation. Compared with glutaraldehyde-fixed autologous pericardium, bovine pericardium (csHR, 2.94; 95% CI, 2.01-4.65) and equine pericardium (csHR, 2.18; 95% CI, 1.36-3.24) were associated with higher recoarctation risk, whereas cryopreserved pulmonary homograft was associated with lower risk (csHR, 0.27; 95% CI, 0.16-0.48).
