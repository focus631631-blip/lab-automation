## Early hospital discharge following delivery during wartime is not associated with increased rates of postpartum hospital referrals.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:07
- 期刊: PLoS One · 中科院综合性期刊3区(综合性期刊3区) · IF 2.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 以色列 · Sheba Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Weissmann-Brenner A
- DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0335688
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0335688) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42886662/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：比较战时与上一年同期产后女性至妇科急诊室的就诊/转诊率，评估战时提前出院是否增加产后医院转诊。
- 核心发现/数据：研究纳入战时2023年首月分娩的801名女性和上一年同期913名女性；战时产后住院时间显著缩短（中位53.2小时 vs. 64.1小时，p<0.0001），且选择性剖宫产率显著降低（57% vs. 92%，p<0.0001）。产后女性至妇科急诊室的总转诊率无显著差异（8.7% vs. 8.8%，p>0.05），因感染或出血导致的转诊无差异，产后入院率亦无差异（17% vs. 14%，p=0.73）。
- 临床启示：在战争或资源紧张情况下，低危产后女性提前出院是可行的，未增加产后急诊转诊及因感染或出血的就诊；临床应配套做好出院评估与随访保障。

**原文摘要**：During war, significant adaptations take place in hospitals to improve the treatment of soldiers and civilians. For example, low-risk women were discharged earlier from hospitals following delivery. The aim of the study was to compare the rate of postpartum-visits to the gynecological-emergency-room (GER) between wartime to a similar period in the previous year. A retrospective study compared the visits of postpartum-women in the GER to a similar period in the previous year. The study included 801 women who delivered in the first month of the war in 2023 and 913 who delivered in the parallel period in the previous year. There were no significant changes in maternal age, gravidity, parity, mode of delivery, birthweight, meconium, low Apgar scores, and the rate of admissions to the neonatal intensive care unit. There were significantly less elective cesarean sections during wartime (57% vs. 92%; p < 0.0001). The duration of postpartum hospitalization was significantly shorter during wartime [median length of 53.2 [Interquartile-range (IQR] 42.4-72.4) hours vs. 64.1 (IQR 47.4-82.8) in the previous year (p < 0.0001)]. There were no significant differences in the rate of total referrals of postpartum-women to the GER (8.7% vs. 8.8%; p > 0.05), and no differences in referrals due to infections or bleeding. There was no difference in the rate of admission of postpartum-women (17% vs. 14%; p = 0.73). Shortening of hospitalization following delivery during war is feasible, does not cause increase postpartum visits to the GER, and does not increase GER visits due to infections or bleeding.

## The impact of pathological cyst wall changes on congenital biliary dilatation in infancy.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:07
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 日本 · Faculty of Medical Sciences
- 通讯作者: Matsuura T (matsuura.toshiharu.809@m.kyushu-u.ac.jp)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06685-z
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06685-z) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42886256/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估胆管囊肿病理改变对早期婴儿先天性胆管扩张症（CBD）临床及肝脏病理表现的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入14例≤4个月接受根治术的婴儿CBD，8例存在炎性细胞浸润（iCBD），其中4例合并胶原层形成；其余6例无炎性细胞浸润及胶原层形成（non-iCBD）。iCBD组术前肝脏生化水平更高、囊肿淀粉酶水平更低、肝纤维化程度更重，差异有统计学意义。iCBD组内有无胶原层者在术前肝脏生化、囊肿淀粉酶及肝纤维化严重程度上无差异。
- 临床启示：早期婴儿CBD中，胆管囊肿的炎性及纤维化改变与更差的术前肝脏生化指标和更重的肝纤维化相关，提示应关注梗阻性胆汁淤积所致的肝脏损伤。单纯胶原层形成似乎未进一步加重上述指标差异，可能不宜单独作为严重度分层依据。

**原文摘要**：This study aimed to evaluate the impact of pathological changes in bile duct cysts on the clinical and liver pathological findings in early infantile patients with congenital biliary dilatation (CBD). In patients with CBD who underwent definitive surgery ≤ 4 months of age, the clinical and liver pathological findings were analyzed according to the presence or absence of inflammatory and fibrotic changes on the luminal side of the bile duct cyst. Among the 14 patients with infantile CBD, 8 had inflammatory cell infiltration (iCBD), and 4 of these 8 patients showed the formation of a collagen layer together. In the remaining six patients, no infiltration of inflammatory cells or formation of collagen layers was observed (non-iCBD). In the iCBD group, the preoperative liver biochemistry levels were higher, the cyst amylase levels were lower, and the degree of liver fibrosis was more severe than those in the non-iCBD group, with significant differences. In comparisons of iCBD patients, there were no differences in the preoperative liver biochemistry levels, cyst amylase levels, and severity of liver fibrosis between the presence and absence of a collagen layer. Inflammatory and fibrotic changes in the bile duct cysts due to obstructive cholestasis may affect the clinical and liver histopathological differences in early infantile CBD after birth.

## Comparison of Feeding, Nutritional, and Procedural Outcomes in Infants with Ductal-Dependent Pulmonary Blood Flow Undergoing Surgical Systemic-to-Pulmonary Artery Shunt Versus Ductal Stenting.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:07
- 期刊: Pediatr Cardiol · 中科院医学4区(儿科3区/心脏和心血管系统4区) · IF 1.3 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Children's Hospital of Colorado
- 通讯作者: Tran P (phuong.tran@childrenscolorado.org)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00246-026-04519-2
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00246-026-04519-2) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42886361/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：比较依赖动脉导管的肺血流婴儿接受外科体肺分流术与动脉导管支架置入术后的喂养、营养及操作相关结局。
- 核心发现/数据：单中心回顾性研究纳入100例，支架组较外科分流组更早达到目标肠内喂养（3 vs 6天，p<0.001）、出院体重年龄Z评分更高（-1.30 vs -1.98，p=0.02）、住院期间生长下降更小（p=0.007），胃造瘘置入率更低（26% vs 51%，p=0.011）。但校正后支架策略与胃造瘘置入无独立关联（OR 0.43，95%CI 0.14-1.24，p=0.12）；术后5天内经口喂养或母乳喂养与较低胃造瘘置入风险独立相关（OR 0.24，95%CI 0.06-0.81，p=0.03）。支架组机械通气及ICU住院更短、血管活性正性肌力支持更少，总住院时长、再干预、NEC及死亡无显著差异。
- 临床启示：动脉导管支架置入可能改善术后早期恢复、喂养进展及生长保持；胃造瘘风险更多与早期喂养准备度和临床恢复相关，需综合评估。

**原文摘要**：Ductal stenting and surgical systemic-to-pulmonary artery shunting (surgical shunt) are alternative strategies to establish pulmonary blood flow in infants with ductal-dependent circulation. The impact of palliation strategy on feeding, nutrition, and neurodevelopment remains unclear. We conducted a single-center retrospective study of 100 infants with ductal-dependent pulmonary blood flow (2017-2024) undergoing surgical shunt (n = 57) or ductal stenting (n = 43). Outcomes included feeding, nutrition, hospital course, and neurodevelopment. Multivariable logistic regression evaluated factors associated with gastrostomy tube (GT) placement. Baseline demographics were similar except for cardiac anatomy. Ductal stent patients reached target enteral feeds post-procedurally sooner (3 vs. 6 days, p < 0.001), had higher discharge weight-for-age z-scores (- 1.30 vs. -1.98, p = 0.02), and smaller declines in growth during hospitalization (p = 0.007). GT placement was less frequent following ductal stenting (26% vs. 51%, p = 0.011), but palliation strategy was not independently associated with GT placement after adjustment (OR 0.43, 95% CI 0.14-1.24, p = 0.12). Oral feeding or breastfeeding within 5 days following palliation was independently associated with lower odds of GT placement (OR 0.24, 95% CI 0.06-0.81, p = 0.03). Ductal stent patients also had shorter mechanical ventilation and ICU stays and required less vasoactive-inotropic support. Total hospital length of stay, need for reintervention, suspected or diagnosed necrotizing enterocolitis, and mortality did not differ significantly between groups. Ductal stenting was associated with improved early post-procedural recovery, faster feeding progression, and better preservation of growth compared with surgical shunt palliation. Although GT placement was less frequent following ductal stenting in unadjusted analysis, palliation strategy was not independently associated with GT placement after adjustment. Early post-procedural oral feeding was independently associated with lower odds of GT placement but may also reflect feeding readiness and clinical recovery.
