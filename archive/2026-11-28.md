## Clinical characteristics and surgical outcomes of patients with bronchial atresia: a multicenter study from the East Asia Pediatric Surgery Research Consortium.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:29
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 日本 · Kobe University Graduate School of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Sugita K (ksugita@m.kufm.kagoshima-u.ac.jp)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06729-4
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06729-4) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43102646/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估支气管闭锁（BA）患者的临床特征和手术结局，以探讨最佳治疗策略。
- 核心发现/数据：本研究纳入24例病理确诊并接受手术切除的BA患者，其中胸腔镜手术（TS）19例（79.2%）、开胸手术（TC）5例（20.8%），无中转。TS较TC显著降低术中出血中位数（3.0 vs. 35.0 mL，p=0.02）并缩短术后住院时间（6 vs. 11天，p=0.03），围手术期并发症率相当。术前感染仅见于≥3岁患者；TS亚组分析显示，<2岁早期手术（n=12）与≥2岁晚期手术（n=7）的围手术期安全性和并发症率相当。
- 临床启示：TS是BA可行且微创的手术方式，可减少术中出血和住院时间；在2岁前择期行TS有助于预防年龄相关感染，同时保证手术安全。

**原文摘要**：Bronchial atresia (BA) is a rare airway malformation. We aimed to evaluate the clinical characteristics and surgical outcomes of BA to investigate optimal treatment strategies. This retrospective multicenter study included 24 patients pathologically diagnosed with BA who underwent surgical resection at four centers (2005-2024). Outcomes of patients treated with thoracoscopic surgery (TS) and thoracotomy (TC) were compared. Patients in the TS group were stratified by age at surgery (Early < 2 years, n = 12 vs. Late ≥ 2 years, n = 7). TS and TC were performed in 19 (79.2%) and 5 (20.8%) patients, respectively, with no conversions. TS was associated with significantly lower median intraoperative blood loss (3.0 vs. 35.0 mL, p = 0.02) and shorter postoperative hospital stay (6 vs. 11 days, p = 0.03). The perioperative complication rates were comparable. Preoperative infection occurred exclusively in patients ≥ 3 years of age. A subgroup analysis showed that Early TS achieved comparable perioperative safety and complication rates to TS. TS is a feasible, minimally invasive approach for BA that significantly reduces intraoperative blood loss and hospital stay relative to TC. Elective TS before 2 years of age is a reasonable strategy to prevent age-dependent infection while ensuring surgical safety.

## Robotic versus Open and Laparoscopic Surgery in Neonates and Infants: A Systematic Review.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 12:43
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 希腊
- 通讯作者: Zavras NJ (nzavras@med.uoa.gr)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163534
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163534) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43103696/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统评价机器人辅助手术在新生儿和婴儿中的安全性、有效性，并与开放、腹腔镜及胸腔镜手术进行比较。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入16项回顾性研究、共1,174例患儿（新生儿261例[22.2%]，婴儿913例[77.8%]），完成501例机器人手术（机器人胸腔镜93例[18.6%]，机器人腹腔镜408例[81.4%]）；常见术式为肾盂成形术（6项）和Kasai肝门空肠吻合术（3项）。与开放手术相比，机器人手术可减少估计失血量并缩短住院时间，但与腹腔镜相比优势不一致；机器人手术时间通常更长，重大并发症罕见，部分重建手术显示功能获益（如肛门直肠畸形控便评分、胆道闭锁黄疸清除率、食管闭锁吻合口并发症），泌尿手术成功率与传统方法相当。
- 临床启示：机器人辅助手术在新生儿和婴儿中具有应用前景，但当前证据主要为IV级回顾性研究，临床推广应个体化并谨慎，尚需更大样本前瞻性研究、标准化技术和专用儿科机器人平台来明确其角色。

**原文摘要**：Robotic-assisted surgery (RAS) has gained traction in pediatric populations, yet its safety and efficacy in neonates and infants remain underexplored. This systematic review aims to investigate the current evidence of RAS in neonates and infants, and compare it to laparoscopic, thoracoscopic, and open approaches. A systematic search of PubMed and Scopus was conducted from inception to September 30, 2025, using Boolean operators and MeSH terms related to robotic surgery, conventional approaches (open, laparoscopic, thoracoscopic), and the neonatal/infant population. Eligible studies were comparative (retrospective or prospective) reporting at least one primary outcome of interest, including operative time (OT), intraoperative complications, conversions, estimated blood loss (EBL), length of stay (LOS), readmissions, reoperations, or functional outcomes. Studies were excluded if they lacked direct comparative data between RAS and a conventional surgical approach, reported a mean patient age exceeding 12 months, or were published in languages other than English. Data synthesis was organized according to anatomical regions-thoracic, abdominal-pelvic, and urologic-to preserve clinically relevant distinctions. Sixteen retrospective studies involving 1.174 patients (261 neonates [22.2%] and 913 infants [77.8%]) were included. A total of 501 robotic procedures were performed, comprising 93 robot-assisted thoracoscopies (18.6% of robotic procedures) and 408 robot-assisted laparoscopies (81.4% of robotic procedures). The most commonly reported procedures were pyeloplasty for ureteropelvic junction obstruction (UPJO) (6 studies) and Kasai portoenterostomy for biliary atresia (BA) (3 studies). Other indications included anorectal malformation (ARM) repair (2 studies), as well as single-study reports on congenital choledochal cyst (CCC) excision, esophageal atresia (EA) repair, diaphragmatic eventration (DE) plication, and ureteral duplication anomaly ureteroureterostomies (UU). Compared to open surgery, RAS was associated with reduced estimated blood loss and a shorter length of hospital stay; this benefit was less consistent versus conventional laparoscopy. OT were generally longer with RAS. Major complications were rare. Notably, functional improvements were observed for RAS in certain reconstructive procedures, including superior continence scores for anorectal malformations, higher jaundice clearance rates for biliary atresia, and reduced anastomotic complications for esophageal atresia, while urological procedures demonstrated equivalent high success rates to conventional approaches. This systematic review confirms the promise of RAS in neonates and infants but underscores the critical need for larger prospective studies, standardized techniques, and dedicated pediatric robotic platforms to establish its definitive role. Systematic review of level IV studies LEVEL OF EVIDENCE: IV.

## Incidence and factors associated with surgical site infection after neonatal surgery: a nationwide registry study in Japan.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 12:43
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Osaka 594-1101 · National Hospital Organization Fukuyama Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Yamamichi T (yamamichi@pedsurg.med.osaka-u.ac.jp)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163608
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163608) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43103695/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：基于日本全国注册数据，估计新生儿首次符合条件手术后手术部位感染（SSI）的发生率，并分析新生儿、术前、围术期及手术相关因素。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入16,419例新生儿手术记录，术后30天总体SSI发生806例（4.9%），非吻合口SSI发生715例（4.4%）；两模型均纳入16,376例完整病例。污染或肮脏/感染伤口、术前SIRS或严重脓毒症/感染性休克、较长手术时间、手术日龄4-7天或8-14天、5分钟Apgar≥7或记录为未知，均与SSI风险升高相关；拟行/尝试微创手术与SSI风险降低相关。
- 临床启示：新生儿手术后约5%发生SSI，应重点关注上述可识别危险因素并加强围术期感染防控。该全国性数据可为后续新生儿SSI预防研究及质量改进提供基线。

**原文摘要**：We estimated surgical site infection (SSI) incidence after first recorded eligible neonatal operations in Japan and examined associated neonatal, preoperative, perioperative, and operative factors. This retrospective study used National Clinical Database-Pediatric Surgery Registry data for operations at postnatal age ≤30 days during 2015-2023. The earliest eligible record per within-institution patient identifier was selected, and records documenting surgery within the preceding 30 days were excluded. Outcomes were 30-day overall SSI and non-anastomotic SSI (recorded SSI without concomitant leak-associated organ/space SSI). Associations were assessed using multivariable logistic regression. Among 16,419 operation records, overall SSI occurred in 806 (4.9%) and non-anastomotic SSI in 715 (4.4%); both models included 16,376 complete cases. Contaminated or dirty/infected wounds, preoperative systemic inflammatory response syndrome (SIRS) or severe sepsis/septic shock, longer operative time, surgery at 4-7 or 8-14 days, and 5-minute Apgar ≥7 or recorded unknown were associated with higher odds in both models. Intended/attempted minimally invasive surgery (MIS) had lower odds in both. SSI affected approximately 5% of the neonatal operation records studied. These nationwide estimates and associated clinical and operative factors provide a basis for future neonatal SSI prevention studies. III.

## The Bumpy Road to Retirement: Current Practices, Perspectives, and the Need for Formal Transition Pathways in Pediatric Surgery.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 12:43
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国
- 通讯作者: McVay-Gillam MR (mmcvay.md@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163459
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163459) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43103693/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：调查小儿外科医生退休过渡的现行做法、认知障碍与偏好策略，以明确建立正式退休过渡路径的必要性。
- 核心发现/数据：共138名小儿外科医生参与调查，其中89%报告无强制退休年龄，75%无正式退休过渡政策，76%未曾被鼓励提前规划退休，仅8%已有过渡计划。主要障碍包括值班覆盖需求、补偿模式不足、继任者招聘延迟、继任规划有限以及资深外科医生非RVU生成贡献被低估，且团队规模影响实施。受访者多偏好结构化2-3年逐步退出路径、优先减少值班、灵活FTE、透明薪酬调整及有意识地转移临床、教学和领导职责。
- 临床启示：小儿外科应建立结构化、透明且灵活的退休过渡路径，以保留资深外科医生专业价值、促进工作量公平分配并加强继任规划。

**原文摘要**：Retirement transitions are rarely formalized in pediatric surgery. The American Pediatric Surgical Association Practice Committee surveyed pediatric surgeons to characterize current retirement practices, perceived assets and barriers, and preferred transition strategies. A voluntary, anonymous online survey was distributed to APSA members in late 2025. Questions addressed demographics, practice characteristics, organizational policies, transition options, compensation, succession planning, and ideal retirement pathways. Quantitative responses were analyzed descriptively, and free-text responses were reviewed for recurring themes. The survey was completed by 138 pediatric surgeons, including early-career (10%), mid-career (30%), late-career (46%), and retired (14%) respondents. Most reported no mandatory retirement age (89%), no formal transition-to-retirement policy (75%), and no prior encouragement to proactively plan for retirement (76%). Only 8% reported an existing transition plan. Major barriers included call coverage needs, inadequate compensation models, delayed recruitment of successors, limited succession planning, and undervaluation of senior surgeons' non-RVU-generating contributions. Group size influenced implementation. Respondents mostly favored a structured 2- to 3-year glidepath, a "reduce call first" strategy, flexible FTE models, transparent compensation adjustments, and intentional transfer of clinical, educational, and leadership responsibilities. Formal retirement transition pathways are uncommon in pediatric surgery. Structured, transparent, and flexible plans may preserve senior surgeon expertise, support equitable workload distribution, strengthen succession planning, and promote a more deliberate progression from full operative practice to retirement.

## Can We Measure Guideline Adherence for Rare Diseases in Pediatric Surgery? A review of Data Coverage from the European Pediatric Surgical Audit (EPSA).

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 12:43
- 期刊: Eur J Pediatr Surg
- 国家/单位: Rotterdam · Erasmus MC University Medical Center Rotterdam
- 通讯作者: Wijnen R
- DOI: 10.1055/a-3012-8692
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1055/a-3012-8692) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43103451/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估欧洲儿科外科审计（EPSA）数据可在多大程度上测量ERNICA罕见病指南/共识推荐的依从性。
- 核心发现/数据：从5份ERNICA指南/共识中提取212条推荐，并与EPSA数据字典逐条核对。仅27条（12.7%）可用当前EPSA数据集完整监测，76条（35.9%）需额外变量方可监测，109条（51.4%）不适合用EPSA数据测量。
- 临床启示：EPSA对部分ERNICA指南依从性监测具有应用潜力，但需进一步协调登记变量与指南内容，并对多数推荐辅以替代方法或外部数据源。

**原文摘要**：Introduction To evaluate the extent to which guideline recommendations are implemented, levels of adherence in clinical practice should be assessed. Adherence assessments may also be used to prompt targeted guideline revisions or encourage the need for new guidelines or research. This study aims to evaluate the extent to which guideline recommendations issued by the European Reference Network for rare Inherited and Congenital Anomalies (ERNICA) can be suitably measured using the European Pediatric Surgical Audit (EPSA). Materials and Methods Recommendations from five disease-specific ERNICA guidelines or consensus statements were extracted and cross-checked one by one with variables included in the current EPSA data dictionary. These guidelines or consensus statements concerned Hirschsprung's disease, Esophageal Atresia, Omphalocele, Gastroschisis and Necrotizing Enterocolitis. Recommendations were categorised as A) Measurable with current dataset, B) Requires additional variables for full assessment or C) Not suitable for measurement using EPSA data. Results A total of 212 recommendations were extracted from the five specified ERNICA guidelines and consensus statements. Overall, 27 (12.7%) of the recommendations can be fully monitored using current EPSA datasets (Category A). Another 76 (35.9%) are possible to monitor but require additional variables (Category B). The remaining 109 (51.4%) recommendations fall into Category C, requiring alternative approaches or external data sources for effective monitoring. Conclusions This study demonstrates the potential to monitor adherence to ERNICA guidelines and consensus statements using EPSA data. A substantial proportion of recommendations can be measured directly or with minor adaptations to the data sets, providing valuable insights into clinical practice. While some recommendations currently require additional approaches or external data sources for effective monitoring, findings suggest that there are opportunities to further harmonise EPSA registry variables with guideline content.

## SHORT-TERM OUTCOMES AFTER PULL-THROUGH FOR HIRSCHSPRUNG DISEASE IN PATIENTS WITH DOWN SYNDROME: A NATIONWIDE COHORT STUDY.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 12:43
- 期刊: Eur J Pediatr Surg
- 国家/单位: Stockholm · Karolinska Institutet
- 通讯作者: Wester T
- DOI: 10.1055/a-3013-1276
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1055/a-3013-1276) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43103452/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估合并唐氏综合征的先天性巨结肠患者行拖出术的诊断时间线

**原文摘要**：The association of Hirschsprung disease (HSCR) with Down Syndrome (DS) is well recognized. Patients with both HSCR and DS have higher rates of enterocolitis and postoperative complications, poorer functional outcome and increased mortality. Specific data concerning the early clinical course and diagnostic workup of coexisting HSCR and DS is limited. The aim of this study was to assess the diagnostic timeline, surgical management, and early postoperative outcomes in patients with HSCR and coexisting DS. This retrospective study collected data for all patients with HSCR that had undergone pull-through in Sweden from 1st of July 2013 to 30th of June 2023. Patients with DS were compared to patients without DS regarding diagnostics, surgical treatment, unplanned procedures under general anesthesia or readmissions within 90 days after pull-through, and complications (Clavien-Madadi grade ≥3) up to 30 days after pull-through. A total of 188 patients were included from four treating centers, of which 16 (8.5%) had DS. Comparing patients with DS to those with HSCR alone showed no significant differences in age at biopsy (median 9 vs. 4 days), need for re-biopsy (6.3 vs. 22.7%), age at diagnosis (median 22 vs. 28 days), preoperative stoma rates (6.3 vs 20.3%), or time from diagnosis to pull-through (median 25 vs. 32.5 days). Surgical method did not vary between groups. Patients with DS had significantly more unplanned readmissions within 90 days post-pull through (6/16 (37.5%) vs. 26/172 (15.1%); p=0.034), however, severe complications within 30 days (12.5 vs. 7.6%), HAEC rates (18.8 vs. 16.3%) and unplanned procedures within 90 days (18.8 vs. 14%) did not differ. The most common indication for readmission among DS patients was bowel or outlet obstruction (n=3), while among non-DS patients, HAEC and bowel or outlet obstruction were equally common (n=9 each). Within this retrospective, nationwide cohort, patients with DS undergoing pull-through for HSCR experienced significantly more unplanned readmissions within 90 days, but were otherwise comparable to non-DS patients in early postoperative outcomes. No evidence of delayed diagnosis or treatment was identified, although the small DS cohort warrants cautious interpretation.
