## Impact of an Ultrasound-First Clinical Care Guideline on Imaging Utilization and Diagnostic Performance in Suspected Midgut Volvulus.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 07:34
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Borst JM (johanna-borst@northwestern.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163604
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163604) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43072556/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估超声优先临床诊疗指南对疑似中肠扭转婴儿的影像学利用、诊断准确性及诊疗时效的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：单中心回顾性前后对照研究纳入≤1岁患儿151例（指南实施前96例、后55例），其中8例手术确诊中肠扭转；实施后超声优先使用率增加。超声明确诊断者灵敏度100%（4/4）、特异度100%（24/24），但不确定率为37.8%（17/45）；上消化道造影灵敏度83.3%（5/6，漏诊1例）、特异度100%（133/133），不确定率2.8%（4/143）。影像开具至明确判读中位时间：首选上消化道造影116分钟，首选超声92分钟；影像开具至手术室时间：仅超声102分钟（n=3）、仅上消化道造影178分钟（n=2）、两者均做128分钟（n=1）。
- 临床启示：超声优先指南可提高超声利用率，且明确判读时诊断准确性良好；对超声不确定者补充上消化道造影在本有限样本中未造成手术延迟，支持该流程，但仍需关注超声较高的不确定率及样本局限性。

**原文摘要**：Infants with suspected midgut volvulus are typically evaluated with an upper gastrointestinal (UGI) contrast series; we implemented an ultrasound-first clinical care guideline (CCG) and evaluated its impact on imaging utilization, diagnostic accuracy, and timeliness of care. Single-center retrospective pre-post study of infants ≤1 year evaluated for volvulus (January 2022-September 2025). Outcomes included ultrasound utilization, diagnostic performance, time to imaging interpretation, and time to operating room (OR). Among 151 patients (96 pre- and 55 post-CCG), 8 had operatively confirmed volvulus. Of the 6 taken immediately to surgery, volvulus was identified by ultrasound alone in 3, UGI alone in 2, and concordant ultrasound and UGI in 1. Ultrasound-first utilization increased after implementation. Ultrasound (n=45) demonstrated 100% sensitivity (4/4) and specificity (24/24) among definitive interpretations, with a 37.8% inconclusive rate (17/45); 1 inconclusive examination was confirmed as volvulus by UGI and operation. UGI (n=143) demonstrated 83.3% sensitivity (5/6; 1 volvulus missed) and 100% specificity (133/133), with a 2.8% inconclusive rate (4/143). Median time from imaging order to definitive interpretation was 116 minutes (IQR 90-164; n=107) for UGI first and 92 minutes (IQR 70-118; n=24) for US first. Median time from imaging order to OR was 102 minutes (n=3) for ultrasound-only, 178 minutes (n=2) for UGI-only, and 128 minutes (n=1) for both. An ultrasound-first CCG for volvulus increased ultrasound utilization with excellent accuracy among definitive reads. For patients with inconclusive ultrasounds (37.8%), obtaining subsequent UGI studies did not result in operative delays in this limited sample.

## Natural History of Asymptomatic Cholecystolithiasis in Children and Factors Associated with Gallstone Clearance.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 07:34
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Pediatric Institute of Soochow University
- 通讯作者: Huang S (drhuang01@163.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163603
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163603) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43072555/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨无症状儿童胆囊结石的自然病程、结局及相关因素，为随访和临床管理提供依据。
- 核心发现/数据：该回顾性研究纳入184例无症状胆囊结石患儿，中位年龄5.88岁、中位随访13.7个月；87例（47.3%）结石完全清除，中位清除时间25.7个月，其余稳定58例（31.5%）、部分缩小22例（12.0%）、进展17例（9.2%）。结石类型与清除显著相关：与单发结石相比，胆泥清除可能性更高（HR=3.70，95%CI 2.16–6.34，P<0.001），多发结石无显著差异；在95例单发结石患儿中，最大结石直径越大，清除可能性越低（HR=0.88，95%CI 0.80–0.96，P=0.005）。UDCA使用见于75例（40.8%），但多因素Cox回归未显示其与结石清除独立相关。
- 临床启示：多数无症状儿童胆囊结石在常规管理随访中可发生清除，提示对部分患儿可采取个体化随访观察而非立即手术干预。结石类型及单发结石大小有助于风险分层和随访决策。

**原文摘要**：The prevalence of cholecystolithiasis in pediatric populations has increased in recent years. Clinical evidence detailing the natural history and influencing factors of asymptomatic cases remains insufficient. This study aims to investigate the clinical course and outcome-associated factors of asymptomatic pediatric cholecystolithiasis, providing a basis for patient follow-up and clinical management. A retrospective analysis was conducted on the clinical and imaging follow-up data of pediatric patients diagnosed with asymptomatic cholecystolithiasis at our hospital between January 2016 and December 2025. The outcomes of asymptomatic cholecystolithiasis were evaluated over time, including complete clearance, stable persistence, progression, and reduction. Cox proportional hazards regression analysis was performed to identify factors associated with disease outcomes. A total of 184 children were included, with a median age of 5.88 years and a median follow-up of 13.7 months. Complete stone clearance was achieved in 87 patients (47.3%), with a median time to clearance of 25.7 months. Among the remaining patients, stones remained stable in 58 (31.5%), partially regressed in 22 (12.0%), and progressed in 17 (9.2%). Stone clearance differed significantly according to stone type (P<0.001). Compared with solitary stones, biliary sludge was associated with a substantially higher likelihood of clearance (HR=3.70, 95% CI: 2.16-6.34, P<0.001), whereas multiple stones showed no significant difference. Among the 95 children with solitary stones, a larger maximum stone diameter was independently associated with a lower likelihood of clearance (HR=0.88, 95% CI: 0.80-0.96, P=0.005). UDCA use was documented in 75 patients (40.8%). Although baseline characteristics differed between patients with and without UDCA use, UDCA use was not independently associated with stone clearance in multivariable Cox regression. Gallstone clearance was observed in a substantial proportion of children with asymptomatic cholecystolithiasis during follow-up under routine clinical management. Stone type and, among solitary stones, stone size were associated with clearance. These findings may inform individualized follow-up and clinical decision-making.

## Trimethylamine N-oxide aggravates lung injury in neonatal rats exposed to hyperoxia via NF-κB/NOX2 signaling.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 07:34
- 期刊: Biochim Biophys Acta Gen Subj · 中科院生物学3区(生物物理3区/生化与分子生物学4区) · IF 2.9 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Zibo Maternal and Child Health Hospital
- 通讯作者: Li B (binghuilowell@outlook.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.bbagen.2026.131024
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbagen.2026.131024) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43072648/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨气道菌群及血清代谢物（尤其TMAO）与早产儿支气管肺发育不良（BPD）相关肺损伤的关联及NF-κB/NOX2信号机制。
- 核心发现/数据：BPD早产儿与肺发育正常早产儿的气道菌群和血清代谢谱存在差异，BPD组Serratia富集，且与血清TMAO水平呈显著正相关。实验显示TMAO暴露与炎症反应、肺损伤及NF-κB/NOX2信号改变相关；高氧诱导BPD新生大鼠经Serratia或TMAO处理后进一步评估肺损伤。
- 临床启示：气道Serratia富集及血清TMAO升高可能提示BPD相关肺损伤风险，并可作为潜在干预靶点；NF-κB/NOX2信号通路可能为BPD防治提供新方向。

**原文摘要**：Bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD) is a common and severe respiratory complication in preterm infants. Previous research has highlighted the importance of the airway microbiota in normal lung function and disease processes. The role of the airway microbiota in BPD among preterm infants and its potential metabolic associations remain unclear. The sputum microbiome was characterized using 16S rRNA gene sequencing, and serum metabolites were profiled using untargeted metabolomics. In vitro, lung epithelial cells were treated with trimethylamine N-oxide (TMAO), with or without the NF-κB pathway inhibitor BAY 11-7082. A hyperoxia-induced BPD model was established in neonatal rats, which were treated with Serratia or TMAO. Histological examination, immunofluorescence staining, and RT-qPCR were performed to assess lung injury. The airway microbiota and serum metabolomic profiles differed between preterm infants with BPD and those with normal lung development. Serratia was enriched in the BPD group and showed a significant positive correlation with serum TMAO levels. Experimental studies further indicated that TMAO exposure was associated with inflammatory responses and lung injury, together with changes in NF-κB/NOX2 signaling. These findings suggest a potential association among airway microbial alterations, TMAO, and BPD-related lung injury. Airway Serratia enrichment was associated with elevated serum TMAO levels in preterm infants with BPD. Experimental findings suggest that TMAO-associated lung injury may involve NF-κB/NOX2 signaling.

## Retinopathy of prematurity in ethiopia: a prospective multicenter study on prevalence, risk factors, and treatment outcomes.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 14:32
- 期刊: BMC Ophthalmol · 中科院医学3区(眼科学3区) · IF 2.1 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: Addis Ababa · St. Paul's Hospital Millennium Medical College
- 通讯作者: Kassa MS (sintayehumandefro1@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1186/s12886-026-05574-2
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12886-026-05574-2) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43073820/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：确定埃塞俄比亚两家三级眼科中心早产儿视网膜病变（ROP）的患病率、危险因素及治疗结局。
- 核心发现/数据：共检查173名早产儿（346眼），任何期ROP为80眼（23.1%），其中1期43.75%、2期36.25%、3期10%、APROP 10%，需治疗的1型ROP为15眼（18.75%）；81.25%观察，15.0%单用玻璃体腔贝伐珠单抗，3.75%贝伐珠单抗后联合周边视网膜激光；81.25%自行消退，16.25%治疗后消退，2.5%进展为视网膜脱离。多因素分析显示输血、多胎、脑室内出血、胎膜早破、母体高血压、较低出生体重、较低胎龄及研究中心与ROP独立相关。该研究ROP患病率低于既往全国数据，但APROP比例较高。
- 临床启示：应加强早产儿ROP连续筛查与随访，尤其警惕APROP并及时治疗；同时针对输血、多胎、脑室内出血、胎膜早破、母体高血压等可干预因素优化围产期和NICU管理。

**原文摘要**：Retinopathy of prematurity (ROP) is an increasing cause of childhood blindness in Africa due to absent or underdeveloped screening and treatment programs. In Ethiopia, only a few centers provide continuous screening and treatment for preterm infants with ROP, and data on its incidence and treatment outcomes remain limited. This multicenter study aimed to determine the prevalence, risk factors, and treatment outcomes of ROP in two tertiary eye care centers in Ethiopia. A prospective longitudinal study was conducted in two university hospitals with level-three neonatal intensive care units (NICUs). All preterm infants with gestational age ≤ 34 weeks, birth weight ≤ 2000 g, or an unstable clinical course were included. Demographic, neonatal, and treatment data were collected and analyzed using SPSS version 26. A total of 173 preterm infants (346 eyes) were examined. The cohort showed a slight female predominance, with a male-to-female ratio of 0.83. Mean birth weight was 1299.6 g (range: 700-3400 g), and mean gestational age was 31.3 weeks (range: 27-40 weeks). ROP of any stage was diagnosed in 80 eyes (23.1%). The disease was bilateral in 35 infants (77.8%) and unilateral in 10 (22.2%). Stage 1 (35 eyes, 43.75%) was the most common presentation, followed by stage 2 (29 eyes, 36.25%), and Stage 3 (8 eyes, 10%). Aggressive posterior ROP (APROP) was diagnosed in 8 eyes (10%). Overall, type 1 ROP requiring treatment was seen in 15 eyes (18.75%). On multivariable logistic regression analysis, blood transfusion (AOR=2.71, p=0.028), multiple birth (AOR=4.29, p<0.001), intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH) (AOR=7.82, p<0.001), premature rupture of membranes (PROM) (AOR= 3.94, p=0.022), maternal hypertension(AOR=3.02, p=0.035), lower birth weight (AOR=2.67,p=0.004), lower gestational age (AOR=1.94,p=0.043) and study center (AOR = 5.08, p = 0.001) were independently associated with ROP. The majority of ROP cases (81.25%) were managed by observation, while 15.0% were treated with intravitreal bevacizumab alone and 3.75% underwent bevacizumab treatment followed by peripheral retinal laser ablation. Overall, 81.25% regressed spontaneously, 16.25% regressed after treatment, and 2.5% progressed to retinal detachment. The prevalence of ROP was lower than previously reported national data. However, a higher prevalence of APROP was observed, a finding not reported in earlier studies. In multivariable analysis, lower gestational age, lower birth weight, blood transfusion, multiple birth, IVH, PROM, maternal hypertension, and study center were independently associated with ROP.

## Seeing Beyond the Bowel: A Sentinel Loop as a Sonographic Clue to Pancreatobiliary Obstruction.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 14:32
- 期刊: Pediatr Emerg Care · 中科院医学4区(急救医学4区/儿科4区) · IF 1.1 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 加拿大 · Hospital for Sick Children
- 通讯作者: Holmes DAS
- DOI: 10.1097/PEC.0000000000003755
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/PEC.0000000000003755) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43074475/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报道一例儿童胰胆管梗阻病例中，点床超声（POCUS）识别哨兵肠袢作为诊断线索的价值与局限。
- 核心发现/数据：患儿为3岁女孩，因持续腹痛就诊，初期归因于便秘。POCUS发现哨兵肠袢，进一步检查提示其与胆总管囊肿并发胰胆管梗阻相关。该发现使诊断评估转向邻近胰胆系统病变，但哨兵肠袢本身非特异且诊断性能尚不确定。
- 临床启示：POCUS发现哨兵肠袢时，应警惕邻近炎症或梗阻，并将评估扩展至胰胆系统。鉴于其非特异性，需谨慎解释并结合进一步影像学检查。

**原文摘要**：The sentinel loop is a localized area of dilated bowel indicating localized abdominal inflammation, traditionally identified on plain films and computed tomography. Despite its diagnostic importance, sentinel loops have not been extensively characterized using point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) in pediatric populations. We report a case of a 3-year-old girl who presented with persistent abdominal pain, initially attributed to constipation. POCUS identified a sentinel loop associated with a choledochal cyst complicated by pancreatobiliary obstruction, an uncommon underlying pathology. Sonographic recognition of the sentinel loop redirected the diagnostic evaluation toward adjacent pathology in this case, illustrating its potential value as a contextual POCUS finding while underscoring the need for cautious interpretation given its nonspecificity and uncertain diagnostic performance.
