## Integrated Prenatal Cardiopulmonary Assessment Using Fetal Echocardiography in Isolated Left-Sided Congenital Diaphragmatic Hernia.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:02
- 期刊: Prenat Diagn · 中科院医学2区(遗传学2区/妇产科学3区) · IF 3.1 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 日本 · Osaka City General Hospital
- 通讯作者: Usui N
- DOI: 10.1002/pd.70298
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/pd.70298) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42998300/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨胎儿超声心动图参数对左侧孤立性先天性膈疝（CDH）预后的预测价值，并将其整合入产前预测模型。
- 核心发现/数据：本研究为全国多中心回顾性研究，共纳入363例产前诊断的左侧孤立性CDH胎儿，总生存率为74.9%；死亡胎儿的右肺动脉及二尖瓣尺寸显著小于存活胎儿。胎儿超声心动图参数中，二尖瓣/三尖瓣比值（MTR）预测生存表现最佳，AUC为0.77；在≤29周评估的胎儿中，MTR联合o/eLHR及肝脏上抬状态的探索性模型表观AUC达0.827。
- 临床启示：整合MTR的产前心肺综合评估可为左侧CDH的预后判断提供补充信息，但仍需独立外部验证后方可用于临床决策。

**原文摘要**：Prenatal severity assessment in congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH) primarily relies on lung-based parameters such as the observed-to-expected lung-to-head ratio (o/eLHR) and liver position; however, these markers do not fully reflect fetal cardiopulmonary adaptation. We investigated the prognostic significance of fetal echocardiographic parameters and integrated these in prenatal prediction models in left-sided CDH. This nationwide multicenter retrospective study included fetuses with prenatally diagnosed isolated left-sided CDH. Gestational age-adjusted Z-scores were generated for fetal cardiac dimensions. Receiver operating characteristic analyses were performed to evaluate prognostic performance for survival. A total of 363 fetuses were included, with an overall survival rate of 74.9%. Non-survivors demonstrated significantly smaller right pulmonary artery and mitral valve dimensions than survivors. Among fetal echocardiographic parameters, the mitral-to-tricuspid valve ratio (MTR) demonstrated the strongest predictive performance, with an area under the curve (AUC) of 0.77. In fetuses evaluated at ≤ 29 weeks' gestation, an exploratory model combining MTR with o/eLHR and liver-up status achieved an apparent AUC of 0.827. Fetal echocardiographic parameters were associated with survival in CDH. Integrated prenatal cardiopulmonary assessment incorporating MTR may provide complementary prognostic information although independent external validation is required.

## Pediatric Bow Hunter syndrome: clinical, vascular, and longitudinal findings from a single center and systematic review of the literature.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:02
- 期刊: Neuroradiology · 中科院医学3区(神经成像3区/核医学3区/临床神经病学4区) · IF 2.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 意大利
- 通讯作者: Rossi A (andrearossi@gaslini.org)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00234-026-04246-8
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00234-026-04246-8) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42998174/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：描述儿童Bow Hunter综合征的临床、血管影像及纵向转归特征，并结合系统综述归纳已发表儿科病例。
- 核心发现/数据：单中心纳入12例男孩（平均7.3岁，平均随访54.4个月），后循环AIS 10/12（83.3%），其中8例为不同时期多灶梗死、3例合并前循环栓塞性梗死。VAD双侧6/12（50%）且均累及V3，远端大脑后动脉和/或小脑上动脉狭窄或闭塞伴侧支7/12（58.3%），基底动脉受累4/12；动态影像显示9/12存在体位依赖椎动脉受压，颅颈交界变异10/12（83.3%）但无屈伸不稳。抗栓治疗下早期复发AIS 5/10，系列影像示血管病变早期演变后稳定；2例手术，其余保守治疗。
- 临床启示：儿童后循环AIS伴V3异常，尤其有被忽视的前驱症状或轻微外伤/运动触发时，应警惕头部旋转相关的机械性机制。多模态神经血管影像（尤其动态血管造影）对诊断、监测及个体化治疗以预防进一步血管损伤至关重要。

**原文摘要**：Bow Hunter syndrome (BHS) is a rare cause of pediatric vertebral artery dissection (VAD) and posterior circulation arterial ischemic stroke (AIS) due to dynamic compression during head rotation. Imaging features, vascular evolution, and outcomes remain poorly defined. We reviewed children with definite or probable BHS diagnosed at a tertiary pediatric center between 2010 and 2026. A systematic review identified published pediatric cases (0-18 years) of definite/probable BHS. Twelve boys were included (mean age, 7.3 years; mean follow-up, 54.4 months). Posterior circulation AIS occurred in 10/12 (83.3%), with multifocal infarcts of different ages in eight and additional anterior circulation embolic infarcts in three. VAD was bilateral in 6/12 (50%), consistently involving V3. Distal posterior cerebral and/or superior cerebellar artery stenosis or occlusion with collateral networks occurred in 7/12 (58.3%); basilar artery involvement in 4/12. Dynamic vascular imaging demonstrated position-dependent vertebral artery compromise in 9/12 patients (eight by rotational DSA and one by Doppler ultrasound). Craniocervical junction variants were present in 10/12 (83.3%), without flexion-extension instability. Early recurrent AIS occurred in 5/10 despite antithrombotic therapy. Serial imaging showed early vascular evolution followed by stabilization. Two patients underwent surgery; the others were treated conservatively. A rotational mechanical mechanism should be considered in children with posterior circulation AIS and V3 abnormalities, particularly when preceded by overlooked prodromal symptoms or minor trauma- and sport-related triggers. Multimodal neurovascular imaging, especially dynamic angiography, is essential for diagnosis, surveillance, and individualized treatment planning aimed at preventing further vascular injury.

## Unveiling the role of adipokine-related genes in biliary atresia through transcriptomic analysis and machine learning.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:02
- 期刊: Funct Integr Genomics · 中科院生物学2区(遗传学2区) · IF 4.0 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Qilu Hospital of Shandong University
- 通讯作者: Zhang Q (215382213@qq.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s10142-026-02093-7
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10142-026-02093-7) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42995284/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过转录组分析和机器学习揭示脂肪因子相关基因在胆道闭锁（BA）中的作用，并筛选关键诊疗靶点。
- 核心发现/数据：基于GSE46960数据鉴定出522个差异表达基因，并细化为30个脂肪因子相关基因集；采用101种机器学习算法优先筛选出LEPR、SPP1、LRG1、IL32、FBN1、IGF1和SAA1共7个关键基因，其区分BA与正常对照性能优异，并富集于细胞外基质组织和免疫通路。LRG1在BA中下调，在胆管毒素诱导BA小鼠模型中过表达LRG1可改善体重、降低胆管壁/腔比、减轻肝纤维化和肝脂肪变性。
- 临床启示：LRG1过表达可减轻胆汁淤积性损伤和纤维化进展，是有前景的治疗靶点；脂肪因子相关基因特征有望用于BA诊断评估和机制研究。

**原文摘要**：Biliary atresia (BA) is a devastating neonatal cholestatic disease characterized by progressive fibro-obliteration of the extrahepatic bile ducts. Although adipokine signaling is implicated in liver pathology, its key mediators in BA remain unclear. We analyzed BA transcriptomic data (GSE46960) to identify differentially expressed genes (DEGs) and derived an adipokine-associated gene set (AdipoBA-GeneSet). An ensemble of 101 machine learning algorithms was employed to prioritize key genes. The diagnostic value, associated biological pathways, and immune infiltration patterns of key genes were investigated. The role of Leucine-Rich Alpha-2-Glycoprotein 1 (LRG1) was validated in a biliatresone-induced BA mouse model by upregulating its expression. We identified 522 DEGs in BA livers, refined to a 30-gene AdipoBA-GeneSet. Machine learning prioritized seven key genes from this adipokine-related gene set (LEPR, SPP1, LRG1, IL32, FBN1, IGF1, and SAA1). These genes showed excellent performance in distinguishing BA patients from normal controls and were linked to extracellular matrix organization and immune pathways. Immune infiltration analysis revealed that key gene expression correlated with specific immune cell subsets. Notably, the adipokine LRG1 was downregulated in BA, and its overexpression alleviated the disease phenotype, as evidenced by improved body weight, decreased bile duct wall-to-lumen ratio, attenuated hepatic fibrosis, and ameliorated hepatic steatosis. This study identifies a novel adipokine-related gene signature in BA and highlights LRG1 as a key protector. LRG1 overexpression mitigates cholestatic injury and fibrotic progression in vivo, positioning it as a promising therapeutic target.

## Acute Kidney Injury in East Africa in an Era of Converging Renal Stressors: A Multiple-Hit Narrative Review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 17:31
- 期刊: Int J Nephrol · 中科院医学4区(泌尿学与肾脏学4区) · IF 1.9
- 国家/单位: Faculty of Medicine and Surgery Somali National University Mogadishu Somalia snu.edu.so · Faculty of Medicine and Surgery Somali National University Mogadishu Somalia snu.edu.so
- 通讯作者: Ulusow KH
- DOI: 10.1155/ijne/4263572
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1155/ijne/4263572) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42999537/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过多重打击框架综合东非急性肾损伤（AKI）区域证据，聚焦容量不足/血流动力学障碍、感染/炎症、肾毒性暴露三个可改变肾脏应激域。
- 核心发现/数据：共筛选902条记录，评估66篇全文，最终纳入45项研究，但证据仅来自埃塞俄比亚、乌干达、坦桑尼亚、肯尼亚、索马里/索马里兰和卢旺达6国。AKI与脱水、出血、低血压、休克、疟疾、脓毒症及其他感染、肾毒性药物、碘对比剂和传统/草药相关，最一致的模式是同一临床人群在队列层面聚集超过一个肾脏应激域。多数研究分别评估暴露，较少分析累积效应、暴露时序或统计交互作用，且证据地理集中，不能代表全部东非地区。
- 临床启示：东非AKI管理宜采用多重打击临床框架，主动识别并处理感染/炎症、血流动力学不稳定和肾毒性暴露的重叠。仍需前瞻性多国患者级时间数据，以明确应激因素的时序、重叠、持续时间和组合对AKI严重程度、恢复及长期结局的影响。

**原文摘要**：Acute kidney injury (AKI) remains an important cause of morbidity and mortality in East Africa, where infectious disease, volume depletion, hemodynamic instability, and nephrotoxic exposure frequently occur in resource-constrained clinical settings. This review aimed to synthesize regional evidence through a multiple-hit framework focused on three modifiable renal stressor domains. A structured narrative review of PubMed, Embase, and Scopus was conducted for peer-reviewed human studies published from January 2012 to 18 August 2026 across the 18 Eastern African countries defined by the United Nations M49 framework. Eligible studies examined AKI in relation to volume depletion or hemodynamic compromise, infection or inflammatory illness, and/or nephrotoxic exposure. Of 902 records identified, 420 duplicates were removed, 482 records were screened, 66 full-text reports were assessed, and 45 studies were included. Evidence originated from only six countries: Ethiopia, Uganda, Tanzania, Kenya, Somalia/Somaliland, and Rwanda. Across adult, pediatric, neonatal, obstetric, infectious-disease, emergency, cardiac, and critical-care populations, AKI was associated with dehydration, hemorrhage, hypotension, shock, malaria, sepsis and other infections, nephrotoxic medications, iodinated contrast, and traditional or herbal medicines. The most consistently documented pattern was cohort-level clustering of more than one renal stressor domain within the same clinical populations. Individual-level coexistence was documented in selected studies, whereas detailed temporal sequencing was uncommon. Most studies assessed exposures separately and rarely examined cumulative effects, exposure timing, or statistical interaction. The currently available evidence from East Africa supports a multiple-hit perspective as a clinical organizing framework rather than a proven biological or prognostic model. Because the evidence is geographically concentrated, particularly in Ethiopia, Uganda, and Tanzania, these findings should not be interpreted as representative of all East African settings. Recurrent cohort-level clustering of infectious or inflammatory, hemodynamic, and nephrotoxic stressors was well documented, but patient-level coexistence and temporal sequence remain incompletely characterized. Prospective multicountry studies with patient-level temporal data are needed to determine whether the timing, overlap, duration, or combination of these stressors influences AKI severity, recovery, and longer-term outcomes.

## Creatine metabolism is dysregulated in necrotizing enterocolitis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 17:31
- 期刊: Pediatr Res · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区) · IF 3.3 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University of Oklahoma Health College of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Burge KY (kathryn-burge@ou.edu)
- DOI: 10.1038/s41390-026-05627-8
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41390-026-05627-8) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42998992/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨肌酸代谢在坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）中是否失调，并评估肌酸补充作为防治策略的潜力。
- 核心发现/数据：在早产儿回肠肠类器官中，1–10 mM肌酸可促进肠上皮细胞划痕愈合、增加线粒体和糖酵解ATP生成，并保护其免受缺氧诱导的凋亡。NEC转录组分析显示，无论整体组织还是上皮细胞群，肌酸代谢相关基因均下调；靶向代谢组学显示，早产儿尿肌酸从基线至NEC诊断时显著下降，而相关代谢物未见同样下降。
- 临床启示：肌酸耗竭是NEC中此前未被认识且可干预的代谢脆弱点，补充肌酸可能成为安全、实用且有效的NEC预防策略。上述发现支持进一步开展临床研究验证肌酸补充在早产儿NEC预防中的作用。

**原文摘要**：Necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) remains a leading cause of morbidity and mortality in preterm infants, yet effective preventive or therapeutic strategies are lacking. Emerging evidence implicates metabolic dysfunction in NEC pathogenesis. We hypothesized metabolism of creatine (Cr), a key regulator of cellular energy homeostasis that supports adenosine triphosphate (ATP) buffering, oxidative phosphorylation, and protection against hypoxic injury, may be dysfunctional in NEC. Enterocyte scratch wound healing assays, Seahorse metabolic assays, and hypoxia-induced apoptosis assays in human preterm ileal enteroids were performed. Human NEC bulk and single-cell RNA-sequencing datasets were analyzed to characterize intestinal Cr metabolism, and targeted metabolomics was performed on longitudinal urine samples from preterm infants, before and at NEC diagnosis. Cr (1-10 mM) enhanced enterocyte wound healing, increased mitochondrial and glycolytic ATP production, and protected enteroids from hypoxia-induced apoptosis. NEC transcriptomic analyses demonstrated downregulation of Cr metabolism genes across bulk and epithelial cell populations. Targeted metabolomics indicated urinary Cr, but not related metabolites, declined significantly from baseline to NEC diagnosis, indicating a specific disruption in Cr homeostasis. Cr depletion represents a previously unrecognized metabolic vulnerability in NEC, supporting Cr supplementation as a safe, practical, and potentially effective strategy for NEC prevention. This study identifies dysregulated metabolism of creatine, an organic acid generated from glycine and arginine, in intestinal NEC tissue, with significant downregulation of key creatine transport and synthesis proteins compared with tissue of age-matched controls. This study is the first to demonstrate an abrupt decline in preterm urinary creatine at NEC diagnosis, establishing creatine depletion as a novel and modifiable metabolic vulnerability in NEC. Creatine supplementation accelerates intestinal epithelial wound healing, improves bioenergetic capacity, and protects against hypoxia-associated apoptosis, all physiological tenets of NEC. These findings support investigation of creatine repletion as a potential NEC preventative strategy in preterm infants.
