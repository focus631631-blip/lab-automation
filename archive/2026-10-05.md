## Management of Upper Limb Deformities in Arthrogryposis Multiplex Congenita: A Narrative Review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:11
- 期刊: Med Sci Monit
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Shijiazhuang People's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Chen L
- DOI: 10.12659/MSM.954073
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.12659/MSM.954073) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42830557/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：总结当前证据，为先天性多关节挛缩症（AMC）上肢复杂畸形的管理提供以建立最佳定位-伸展-抓握轴为核心的战略框架。
- 核心发现/数据：该叙述性综述于2026年4月通过MEDLINE进行选择性文献检索，重点关注2020—2025年发表的进展，关键词包括“arthrogryposis”“amyoplasia”和“upper limb deformities”。证据显示治疗理念转向功能性动力链，优先恢复自理自主性而非单纯解剖复位；管理以早期持续非手术活动为基础，手术采用分层框架，先通过关节松解或截骨解除被动约束、优化肢体空间定位，再考虑肌腱或肌肉转移重建主动运动。干预时机需依据儿童发育里程碑和代偿模式，重点保障手到口及个人卫生路径。
- 临床启示：临床应尽早并持续开展非手术干预，以功能独立性为目标个体化安排手术顺序和时机。未来应走向精准功能重建，结合基因组信息并采用标准化多中心结局评估。

**原文摘要**：Arthrogryposis multiplex congenita (AMC) presents complex therapeutic challenges in upper limb reconstruction, in which establishing an optimal positioning-reaching-grasping axis is critical for patient independence. However, clinical evidence is often limited by low-level data and significant phenotypic heterogeneity, leaving management strategies fragmented. This review synthesizes current evidence to provide a strategic framework for managing complex upper extremity deformities in AMC. A selective literature search was conducted via the MEDLINE database in April 2026, focusing on advancements published between 2020 and 2025. Key keywords included "arthrogryposis", "amyoplasia", and "upper limb deformities", analyzing the multi-joint therapeutic hierarchy, nonoperative timelines, and surgical sequencing. The evidence identifies a shift toward a functional kinetic chain approach, prioritizing the restoration of self-care autonomy over simple anatomical realignment. Effective management begins with early, nonoperative mobilization to leverage neonatal tissue plasticity. Surgical strategy is often conceptualized as a hierarchical framework, typically addressing passive constraints through joint release or osteotomy to optimize the limb's spatial positioning before considering active motion reconstruction via functional tendon or muscle transfers. The therapeutic logic emphasizes the critical hand-to-mouth and personal hygiene pathways, in which the timing of intervention is tailored to the child's developmental milestones and compensatory patterns, ensuring that each segmental correction supports the overall synergy of the limb. Early, persistent nonoperative intervention serves as the foundational pillar, while surgical strategies must be tailored to the child's adaptive patterns. Future care should move toward precision functional restoration, supported by genomic insights, and standardized multicenter outcome measures.

## Effect of Skin-To-Skin Contact During Caesarean Section on Pain and Stress in Newborns: A Randomized Controlled Trial.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:11
- 期刊: Nurs Open · 中科院医学4区(护理3区) · IF 3.1 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 土耳其 · Darica Farabi Training and Research Hospital
- 通讯作者: Aksoy SD
- DOI: 10.1002/nop2.70836
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/nop2.70836) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42830547/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估剖宫产术中皮肤接触对新生儿首次肌内注射期间疼痛和应激的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：该前瞻性随机对照试验纳入60例择期剖宫产（脊髓麻醉）健康足月新生儿，随机分为术中皮肤接触组和常规护理组（各30例），干预组接受约3分钟标准化皮肤接触。ALPS-Neo评分基线两组相似，但在首次肌内注射前、中、后皮肤接触组均更低；注射期间组间平均差为-2.633（95% CI：-3.135至-2.132，p<0.001），Hedges' g=-2.68（95% CI：-3.38至-1.99），效应量大。
- 临床启示：在确保临床和保温条件适宜时，剖宫产术中简短皮肤接触可作为简单的非药物策略，减轻新生儿常规致痛操作期间的疼痛和应激。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate the effect of skin-to-skin contact during caesarean section on neonatal pain and stress during the first intramuscular injection. Prospective randomized controlled trial. The study was conducted between January and July 2023 in an education and research hospital. Sixty healthy term newborns of women undergoing elective caesarean delivery under spinal anaesthesia were randomly allocated to skin-to-skin contact (n = 30) or routine care (n = 30). In the intervention group, standardized skin-to-skin contact was provided for approximately 3 min during caesarean section. Neonatal pain and stress were assessed using the Newborn Pain and Stress Assessment Scale (ALPS-Neo) at four predefined timepoints. ALPS-Neo scores were similar between groups at baseline but were lower in the skin-to-skin contact group immediately before, during, and after the first intramuscular injection. During the injection, the mean between-group difference was -2.633 (95% CI: -3.135 to -2.132, p < 0.001), with a large standardized effect (Hedges' g = -2.68, 95% CI: -3.38 to -1.99). Brief intraoperative skin-to-skin contact may reduce immediate neonatal pain and stress during the first intramuscular injection after caesarean birth. Skin-to-skin contact may provide a simple non-pharmacological strategy to support neonatal comfort during routine painful procedures after caesarean birth when appropriate clinical and thermal conditions are ensured. This study supports the analgesic and stress-reducing effects of skin-to-skin contact and extends existing evidence to brief intraoperative contact during caesarean delivery. Repeated assessment showed that the between-group difference was greatest during the painful procedure. The study was reported in accordance with the CONSORT Statement for Randomized Trials of Nonpharmacologic Treatments. There was no patient or public involvement in the design, management, interpretation, reporting, or dissemination of this study. Women and their newborns were involved solely as research participants, and written informed consent was obtained from the women before participation. ClinicalTrials.gov (registration number: NCT05700552).

## Eleven-year population-based evaluation of a regional newborn hearing screening programme.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:11
- 期刊: Eur Arch Otorhinolaryngol
- 国家/单位: Aarhus · Aarhus University Hospital
- 通讯作者: Lund A (andrealund@clin.au.dk)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00405-026-10651-9
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00405-026-10651-9) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42830358/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估丹麦中部地区新生儿普遍听力筛查项目11年期间的长期表现，重点关注筛查覆盖率、转诊率及不同筛查模式下的诊断结局。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入160,719名活产儿，157,849名（98.2%）接受筛查，初筛后转诊5,070名（3.2%）；单纯AABR路径初筛转诊率为0.2%，基于OAE路径约15%（p<0.001）。转诊婴儿中85.5%在听力复筛时双耳通过，仅732名（占筛查儿0.46%）进入诊断性ABR，其中417例确诊听力损失，诊断产出率为57.1%。该风险适应筛查模式长期表现稳定，覆盖率高且最终转诊率低。
- 临床启示：对无危险因素新生儿采用AABR初筛可显著降低初筛转诊率并维持诊断产出率，支持将AABR作为结构化新生儿听力筛查项目的主要筛查方式。

**原文摘要**：Universal newborn hearing screening (UNHS) is widely implemented, but long-term population-based evaluations of programme performance remain limited. This study aimed to evaluate long-term performance of a regional UNHS programme over an 11-year period, focusing on screening coverage, referral rates, and diagnostic outcomes across screening modalities. This retrospective population-based cohort study included all live-born infants in the Central Denmark Region from 2015 to 2025. Screening pathways were categorised in three groups: automated auditory brainstem response (AABR)-only screening for infants without recognised risk factors, combined AABR and otoacoustic emissions (OAE) screening for infants with recognised risk factors, and a group in which only OAE screening was recorded. Programme outcomes included screening coverage, primary referral rate, final referral rate to diagnostic auditory brainstem response (D-ABR), and diagnostic yield. Among 160,719 live-born infants, 157,849 (98.2%) underwent screening. Overall, 5,070 infants (3.2%) were referred after primary screening. Primary referral rates were 0.2% for AABR-only screening and approximately 15% for OAE-based screening pathways (p < 0.001). Of referred infants, 85.5% achieved bilateral pass at audiological rescreening, and only 732 infants (0.46% of screened infants) proceeded to D-ABR. Among these, 417 had confirmed hearing loss (diagnostic yield 57.1%). The programme demonstrated stable long-term performance with high screening coverage and low final referral rates within a structured, risk-adapted screening model. AABR-based screening in non-risk infants was associated with a very low primary referral rate while maintaining diagnostic yield. These findings support the use of AABR as a primary screening modality within structured UNHS programmes.
