## Clinical Consensus Guidelines on Antibiotic Prescribing Practices for Infants with Gastroschisis Utilizing Modified Delphi Methodology.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 05:13
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Cincinnati · Cincinnati Children's Hospital Medical Center and Department of Pediatrics
- 通讯作者: Riddle S (Stefanie.riddle@cchmc.org)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163573
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163573) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43003280/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：采用改良德尔菲法制定关于简单腹裂新生儿微生物筛查和抗生素使用实践的循证临床共识指南。
- 核心发现/数据：CHNC腹裂焦点小组经文献回顾和小组讨论起草了10条建议；第一轮调查有23名受访者，其中7/10条建议平均评分>80%达到共识标准，剩余建议经修改后第二轮调查全部达成共识。共识主题涵盖出生时通过血培养筛查感染，以及出生时和腹壁关闭时的抗生素使用实践。具体包括感染风险分层、抗生素治疗谱、疗程和给药时机。
- 临床启示：该共识为简单腹裂新生儿的抗生素使用和抗菌药物管理提供了循证框架，有助于减少不必要或过长的抗生素暴露。由于许多中心缺乏该人群标准化指南，该框架对规范临床实践具有重要参考价值。

**原文摘要**：Gastroschisis is the most common congenital abdominal wall defect in newborns and common surgical diagnosis for which antibiotics are prescribed in the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU). Multiple studies demonstrate early and prolonged antibiotic exposure, despite evidence of overuse resulting in delayed initiation of feedings, later full feedings, and blood stream infection. The purpose was to develop expert consensus on evidence-driven clinical guidelines for microbiologic screening and antibiotic usage in neonates with simple gastroschisis. The Children's Hospitals Neonatal Consortium (CHNC) Gastroschisis Focus Group utilized a modified Delphi method to develop clinical consensus guidelines (CCG). Committee leaders drafted 10 recommendations after literature review and group discussion. Each recommendation was sent to focus group members via REDCap survey tool and members scored responses on a 5-point Likert scale. In the first survey, there were 23 respondents and 7/10 recommendations received a mean score >80%, meeting the criteria for consensus. Following review of results and comments, the remaining statements were edited and sent out for the second round survey where all achieved consensus. Themes included the practice of screening for infection at birth by blood culture, and antibiotic use practices at the time of birth and abdominal wall closure. These included risk stratification for infection, spectrum of antibiotic therapies utilized, length of therapy, and timing of antibiotic administration. We present a consensus evidence-based framework for antibiotic use and stewardship practices in infants with simple gastroschisis, which will be important as many centers lack standardized guidelines in this population.

## A rare case of empyema caused by Streptococcus agalactiae serotype V.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:13
- 期刊: J Infect Chemother
- 国家/单位: Osaka 545-8586 · Osaka Metropolitan University Hospital
- 通讯作者: Mitani K (k.mitani@omu.ac.jp)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jiac.2026.103122
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jiac.2026.103122) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43003305/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告一例由无乳链球菌血清型V引起的成人脓胸罕见病例，并强调高危患者早期诊断及及时干预（包括手术）的重要性。
- 核心发现/数据：患者为80岁男性，有肺腺癌手术史，随访中出现痰量增多，胸部CT示左胸腔包裹性积液增多，诊断性胸腔穿刺抽出脓性胸水，胸水培养检出无乳链球菌，确诊为GBS脓胸。患者接受胸腔引流及抗菌治疗，虽一度临床恶化，但经手术干预后临床转归良好；分离株鉴定为血清型V。
- 临床启示：对高龄、合并恶性肿瘤等重症高危的GBS感染者，应警惕罕见脓胸可能，早期诊断并尽早采取包括手术在内的综合干预。

**原文摘要**：Group B Streptococcus (GBS) is a well-known causative pathogen of neonatal and maternal infections; however, empyema due to GBS in adults is rare. Invasive GBS infections are more likely to be severe, particularly in older patients or those with underlying diseases or immunocompromised status. We report a patient with empyema caused by Streptococcus agalactiae in an 80-year-old man with a history of lung malignancy. During routine follow-up after lung adenocarcinoma surgery, the patient showed increased sputum production, and chest computed tomography revealed an increase in encapsulated pleural effusion in the left thorax. Diagnostic thoracentesis yielded purulent pleural fluid, leading to the diagnosis of empyema. Pleural fluid culture revealed the presence of GBS. Therefore, the patient underwent chest drainage and received antimicrobial therapy. Although temporary clinical deterioration was observed, a favorable clinical course was achieved after surgical intervention. The isolated strain was identified as serotype V, which is one of the most frequently identified serotypes among invasive GBS isolates from nonpregnant adults. In patients at high risk of developing severe disease, early diagnosis and prompt intervention, including surgery, are essential.

## Pre- and post-kidney transplant outcomes of neonates with congenital kidney failure requiring fetal interventions.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:13
- 期刊: Pediatr Nephrol · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/泌尿学与肾脏学3区) · IF 2.5 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University of Cincinnati College of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Schuh MP (meredith.schuh@cchmc.org)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00467-026-07565-3
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00467-026-07565-3) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43002227/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：总结因先天性肾衰竭（膀胱出口梗阻[BOO]、无羊水多囊性发育不良肾[MCDKa]）接受胎儿干预并后续行肾移植（KTx）患儿移植前后的结局。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入25例（历史BOO 9例、当代BOO 9例、当代MCDKa 7例），所有当代BOO和MCDKa均需羊水灌注，MCDKa出生体重最低。当代队列住院时间更长、透析启动更早，许多患儿发生非计划透析方式改变且KTx时需血液透析；KTx中位年龄2.0–2.9岁，移植前住院时间占生命期最高达23.7%。移植后1年内生长恢复正常，总体移植结局优秀，包括7例既往无羊水灌注难以存活的MCDKa；1例历史BOO需二次KTx。
- 临床启示：该类复杂先天性肾衰竭患儿经胎儿干预后可获得良好肾移植结局，临床应重视移植前长期住院、透析通路与方式转换、营养生长及多学科随访管理。

**原文摘要**：Fetuses with congenital kidney failure, including bladder outlet obstruction (BOO) and multicystic dysplastic kidney with anhydramnios (MCDKa-includes bilateral MCDK/MCDK with kidney agenesis), require fetal intervention for pulmonary survivorship. However, data on their course to kidney transplant (KTx) and post-transplant outcomes are limited. This study reports pre- and post-KTx outcomes of patients with congenital kidney failure who required fetal interventions. We performed a retrospective chart review of patients who underwent both fetal interventions and KTx at Cincinnati Children's Fetal Care Center and Cincinnati Children's Hospital Medical Center before December 31, 2024. To evaluate evolving care practices, patients were stratified into three groups: historical BOO (born 2008-2014), contemporary BOO (born 2016-2022), and contemporary MCDKa (born 2019-2021). Outcomes included prenatal interventions, respiratory support, hospital length of stay (LOS), dialysis, growth, feeding, kidney function, hospital admissions, and graft status. Twenty-five patients were included (9 historical BOO, 9 contemporary BOO, 7 MCDKa). All contemporary BOO and MCDKa required amnioinfusions. MCDKa had the lowest birth weights. Contemporary cohorts had longer LOS and earlier dialysis initiation. Many patients had unplanned dialysis modality changes and required hemodialysis at the time of KTx. Median age at KTx ranged from 2.0 to 2.9 years. Patients spent up to 23.7% of life hospitalized pre-transplant. One historical BOO patient required a second KTx. Growth normalized within one-year post-KTx. Overall, transplant outcomes are excellent in this complex population, including 7 MCDKa, a diagnosis previously incompatible with life without amnioinfusions.

## Clinical outcomes and mortality predictors of peritoneal dialysis in critically ill neonates.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:13
- 期刊: Pediatr Nephrol · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/泌尿学与肾脏学3区) · IF 2.5 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 土耳其 · Şanlıurfa Training and Research Hospital
- 通讯作者: Okbay Güneş A (asliokbay@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00467-026-07616-9
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00467-026-07616-9) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43002225/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估危重新生儿急性肾损伤接受腹膜透析的临床结局、并发症及死亡预测因素。
- 核心发现/数据：共81例新生儿接受腹膜透析，58%为早产，中位胎龄36周，平均出生体重2449.8±1000.4 g；最常见指征为氮质血症（71.6%）和少尿/无尿（48.1%）。中位腹膜透析时长6天，总死亡率74.1%，置管后中位生存时间8天，透析液渗漏为最常见并发症（14.7%）。独立死亡预测因素包括胎儿水肿（HR 15.26，p<0.001）、先天性代谢病（HR 6.82，p<0.001）、液体超负荷（HR 4.43，p=0.039）和围产期窒息（HR 2.73，p=0.024）；腹膜透析可显著改善酸碱及电解质指标。
- 临床启示：对需要腹膜透析的急性肾损伤新生儿应警惕其高死亡率，早期识别胎儿水肿、代谢病、液体超负荷及围产期窒息等高危因素有助于风险分层和临床管理。腹膜透析虽可改善酸碱电解质紊乱，但仍需积极防治渗漏等并发症并加强综合救治。

**原文摘要**：Peritoneal dialysis (PD) is a commonly used kidney replacement therapy for neonatal acute kidney injury (AKI), yet data on outcomes and mortality predictors remain limited. This study evaluated the clinical outcomes, complications, and mortality predictors of PD in neonates with AKI. This retrospective cohort study included neonates who underwent PD in a tertiary neonatal intensive care unit between 2018 and 2023. Clinical characteristics, PD indications, complications, and outcomes were analysed. Survival was assessed using Kaplan-Meier analysis, and independent mortality predictors were identified using multivariable Cox regression. Eighty-one neonates underwent PD. Fifty-eight percent were preterm, with a median gestational age of 36 weeks and a mean birth weight of 2449.8 ± 1000.4 g. The most common indications for PD were azotemia (71.6%) and oligoanuria (48.1%). Median PD duration was 6 days, and overall mortality was 74.1%. Median survival time after catheter insertion was 8 days. Dialysate leakage was the most frequent complication (14.7%). Independent predictors of mortality were hydrops fetalis (HR 15.26, p < 0.001), inborn metabolic disorders (HR 6.82, p < 0.001), fluid overload (HR 4.43, p = 0.039), and perinatal asphyxia (HR 2.73, p = 0.024). PD was associated with significant improvements in acid-base and electrolyte parameters. Mortality remains high among neonates requiring PD. Hydrops fetalis, metabolic disorders, fluid overload, and perinatal asphyxia were independently associated with death. Early recognition of these high-risk features may improve risk stratification and clinical management.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨一例由ZEB2基因新发杂合移码变异所致Mowat-Wilson综合征胎儿的遗传病因。

**原文摘要**：To explore the genetic etiology of a fetus with Mowat-Wilson syndrome (MWS) due to a novel de novo heterozygous frameshift variant of ZEB2 gene. A pedigree presenting at the Affiliated Women and Children's Hospital of Ningbo University in February 2024 was enrolled. Clinical data were retrospectively analyzed. Amniotic fluid samples of the fetus and peripheral blood samples of the couple were collected for trio-whole exome sequencing (Trio-WES). Candidate variant was verified by Sanger sequencing. The pathogenicity of the candidate variant was assessed based on guidelines from the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics (ACMG). Postnatal follow-up was performed, and the pathogenicity of the variant was re-assessed by incorporating newly acquired phenotypic features. This study was approved by the Medical Ethics Committee of the hospital (Ethics No.: EC2023-094). Prenatal ultrasonography at 20 The newly identified ZEB2 c.3515del represents a likely pathogenic variant, which expanded the mutational spectrum of the ZEB2 gene. Longitudinal prenatal-to-postnatal phenotypic integration is critical for re-assessing VUS in MWS patients and providing evidence-based references for prenatal genetic diagnosis and genetic counseling of similar cases.
