## Effectiveness of prenatal predictors for congenital diaphragmatic hernia on survival to discharge and extracorporeal membrane oxygenation requirement: a retrospective cohort study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:30
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 英国 · Birmingham Medical School
- 通讯作者: Gurney L (leo.gurney1@nhs.net)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06677-z
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06677-z) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42900355/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估产前超声预测指标（o/e LHR、肝脏位置及其联合模型）与产前诊断先天性膈疝患儿存活出院及ECMO需求的关系。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入91例（左侧82例、右侧9例），总体存活出院率55%、ECMO需求率13%；左侧CDH分别为57%和15%，右侧CDH存活出院率为33%。在左侧CDH中，o/e LHR、肝脏位置及二者联合模型均可预测存活出院（AUC分别为0.769、0.703、0.823），联合模型预测能力最佳，尤其在孤立左侧CDH亚组；但这些指标均不能预测ECMO需求，右侧CDH中o/e LHR和肝脏位置也不能预测存活出院。
- 临床启示：产前o/e LHR与肝脏位置联合模型可用于左侧CDH的产前咨询和预后分层，但现有指标对ECMO需求及右侧/复杂左侧CDH的预测价值有限，需进一步研究更有效的预测工具。

**原文摘要**：Congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH) is associated with high mortality and morbidity. Antenatal counselling enables parents to prepare for the postnatal outcomes and relies on effective prognostic tools. Ultrasound (USS) predictors including observed/expected lung head ratio (o/e LHR) and liver position, are used independently, or combined into severity classifications. The aim was to evaluate the association between USS predictors, and severity classifications with survival to discharge and extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO) requirement in prenatally diagnosed CDH at Birmingham Women's and Children's Hospital (BWC). CDH patients from BWC with dates of birth between 1st December 2013-1st December 2023 were retrospectively collected. Study inclusion required o/e LHR measurement between 22- and 32-weeks gestational age (GA.) Comparisons between groups was assessed by Fisher exact test and Mann-Whitney U test. Logistic regression was performed to determine prognostic ability, with area under curve (AUC) calculated. 91 eligible patients were identified, which included 82 left-sided, and 9 right-sided CDH. Overall, 55% survived to discharge and 13% required ECMO. In the left CDH cohort, 57% of infants survived to discharge, and 15% required ECMO. O/e LHR, liver position and the combined predictive model including o/e LHR and liver position were predictive of survival to discharge with good prognostic ability (AUC 0.769, AUC 0.703, and AUC 0.823, respectively). O/e LHR, liver position, and the combined predictive model were not predictive of ECMO requirement. In the right CDH patients, 33% survived to discharge, O/e LHR and liver position were not predictive of survival to discharge. O/e LHR, liver position and the combination of these predictors, predicted survival to discharge in prenatally diagnosed left CDH with the combined model, the most predictive particularly in the isolated left CDH sub-group. Further analysis into effective predictors for ECMO requirement, and for right and complex left CDH is required.

## Neonatal gastric perforation: a systematic review and meta-analysis of clinical characteristics, risk factors, surgical management and outcomes.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:30
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Srinagar · Government Medical College
- 通讯作者: Najar FA (fayazgmc91@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06692-0
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06692-0) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42900289/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统评价新生儿胃穿孔（NGP）的临床特征、危险因素、外科管理及结局。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入20项研究、410例新生儿；合并早产率58.3%、低出生体重率63.5%、男性59.0%，腹胀（90.5%）和气腹（80.9%）最常见，手术患儿中78.4%行原发修补。合并院内死亡率为28.6%（95% CI 22.0–36.3%），就诊时脓毒症（OR 4.10）和手术延迟超过24小时（OR 3.55）是死亡的最强预测因素。
- 临床启示：NGP主要发生于早产、低出生体重儿，病死率仍约28%；早期识别、及时处理脓毒症并尽早手术是改善生存的关键。

**原文摘要**：Neonatal gastric perforation (NGP) is a rare but life-threatening surgical emergency of the newborn. We systematically reviewed its clinical characteristics, risk factors, surgical management and outcomes. Five databases were searched from inception to 31 December 2025 for observational studies reporting at least five neonates (≤ 28 days) with confirmed gastric perforation. Two reviewers independently screened records, extracted data and appraised quality. Proportions were pooled using random-effects models; predictors of mortality were summarised as odds ratios (OR). The review followed PRISMA 2020 and was registered in the International Prospective Register of Systematic Reviews (PROSPERO). Twenty studies (410 neonates) were included. Pooled prevalences were prematurity 58.3%, low birth weight 63.5% and male sex 59.0%; abdominal distension (90.5%) and pneumoperitoneum (80.9%) were the commonest features. Primary repair was performed in 78.4% of operated neonates. Pooled in-hospital mortality was 28.6% (95% CI 22.0-36.3%). Sepsis at presentation (OR 4.10) and delay to surgery beyond 24 h (OR 3.55) were the strongest predictors of death. NGP mainly affects premature, low-birth-weight neonates, and still carries approximately 28% mortality. Early recognition, prompt treatment of sepsis and timely surgery offer the clearest route to improved survival. CRD420261436438.

## Coagulation and fibrinolysis markers at birth as early predictors of surgical necrotizing enterocolitis in extremely low-birth-weight infants.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:30
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 日本 · Research Field in Medical and Health Sciences
- 通讯作者: Harumatsu T (harumatsu1040@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06684-0
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06684-0) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42900264/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估出生时凝血及纤溶标志物能否预测超低出生体重儿后续发生需手术的新生儿坏死性小肠结肠炎（S-NEC）。
- 核心发现/数据：回顾性分析250例出生体重<1000 g的超低出生体重儿，其中22例发生S-NEC；与对照组相比，S-NEC患儿出生时D-二聚体（11.7 vs. 5.5 µg/mL，p=0.003）、PT-INR（1.71 vs. 1.42，p=0.023）更高，PT延长（62.2 vs. 52.7 s，p=0.022），纤维蛋白原更低（107.3 vs. 160.7 mg/dL，p=0.037）。多变量模型AUC逐步提高至0.668、0.710、0.763；四项风险评分（D-二聚体>5 µg/mL、PT-INR>1.5、纤维蛋白原<100 mg/dL、出生体重<700 g）AUC为0.702，敏感度85.0%，阴性预测值96.3%。不同风险分层S-NEC发生率分别为3.7%、14.5%、19.6%（趋势p=0.013）。
- 临床启示：出生时止血功能异常与超低出生体重儿后续S-NEC相关，提示围产期或宫内起源；该风险评分阴性预测值高，可作为早期NICU分层和排除S-NEC的实用工具。

**原文摘要**：Early identification of surgical necrotizing enterocolitis (S-NEC) before clinical deterioration remains challenging in extremely low-birth-weight infants (ELBWIs). We evaluated whether coagulation and fibrinolysis markers at birth could predict subsequent S-NEC. We retrospectively analyzed 250 ELBWIs (< 1000 g) treated from 2013 to 2022; 22 developed S-NEC. Eleven coagulation and fibrinolysis markers were measured within 24 h of birth. Multivariable logistic regression models and a simple birth-time risk score were developed. Relative to controls, S-NEC infants had higher D-dimer (11.7 vs. 5.5 µg/mL, p = 0.003) and PT-INR (1.71 vs. 1.42, p = 0.023), prolonged PT (62.2 vs. 52.7 s, p = 0.022), and lower fibrinogen (107.3 vs. 160.7 mg/dL, p = 0.037). Multivariable models showed stepwise improvement in discrimination (AUC 0.668, 0.710, 0.763). A four-item risk score (D-dimer > 5 µg/mL, PT-INR > 1.5, fibrinogen < 100 mg/dL, and birth weight < 700 g) achieved an AUC of 0.702, with 85.0% sensitivity and 96.3% negative predictive value (NPV). S-NEC incidence increased across risk categories (3.7%, 14.5%, and 19.6%; p for trend = 0.013). Hemostatic abnormalities at birth were associated with subsequent S-NEC in ELBWIs, suggesting perinatal or intrauterine origins. The risk score showed a high NPV and may serve as a practical rule-out tool for early neonatal intensive care unit stratification.

**原文摘要**：Neonatal hemostatic immaturity complicates anticoagulation during cardiopulmonary bypass (CPB). This single-center randomized controlled trial evaluated the initial heparin dose of 600 U/kg to refine neonatal CPB anticoagulation. Larger studies are needed to establish clinical benefit and safety. ChiCTR2300069157.

## Nationwide outcomes of biliary atresia after Kasai portoenterostomy in Taiwan: the impact of postoperative cholangitis and corticosteroid use.

- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：提供台湾胆道闭锁患儿Kasai肝门空肠吻合术后全国性更新结局，重点分析术后胆管炎、肝移植、糖皮质激素使用及肾脏结局。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入334例患儿，急性胆管炎发生率76.3%，24.9%需住院≥5次；胆管炎发作次数越多与肝移植风险升高相关（OR 1.08，95%CI 1.00–1.16，p=0.044），30.8%发生胆汁性肝硬化，33.8%接受肝移植。96%患儿使用糖皮质激素，疗程较长与更高并发症率相关；急性肾损伤发生率1.8%，无进展至终末期肾病。

**原文摘要**：To provide updated nationwide outcomes of biliary atresia (BA) among patients in Taiwan, focusing on postoperative cholangitis, liver transplantation (LT), corticosteroid use, and renal outcomes. Infants with BA who underwent Kasai portoenterostomy were identified from the Taiwan National Health Insurance Research Database (2008-2019). Postoperative outcomes were evaluated, and the association between cholangitis episodes and LT was analyzed using exploratory logistic regression analysis. Approximately 30.8% of patients developed biliary cirrhosis, and 33.8% received LT. Corticosteroids were prescribed to 96% of patients, and a longer treatment duration was associated with higher complication rates. About 1.8% of patients developed acute kidney injury, with no progression to end-stage renal disease. Postoperative cholangitis remains an important clinical factor associated with LT in infants with BA. Corticosteroid treatment duration may reflect underlying disease severity rather than therapeutic benefit. Renal complications are uncommon. Optimizing postoperative care-particularly timely cholangitis management-is essential to improve outcomes.

## Three dimensional reconstruction guided anatomical regional resection versus segmentectomy for congenital pulmonary airway malformation in children.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:30
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Fujian Branch of Shanghai Children's Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Wang ZC (wzcfriendly@163.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06693-z
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06693-z) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42900268/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：比较CT三维重建引导的解剖性区域切除与肺段切除在儿童先天性肺气道畸形（CPAM）中的围手术期结局。
- 核心发现/数据：单中心回顾性纳入127例患儿，其中3D引导解剖性区域切除59例、肺段切除68例，两组病变类型、累及肺段数、累及肺叶分布及术前感染状态无显著差异。区域切除组胸管留置时间更短（2.2±1.4 vs. 4.1±1.3天，P<0.001），住院时间更短（5.0±1.3 vs. 5.5±1.4天，P=0.039）；手术时间、术后并发症率及CT示肺不张或过度充气无显著差异。119例术后1年CT均未发现残留CPAM。
- 临床启示：在经选择的CPAM患儿中，3D重建引导的解剖性区域切除可行，并可缩短胸管留置时间和住院时间。该技术有助于在切除解剖性病灶的同时实现保肺。

**原文摘要**：To compare CT-based three-dimensional (3D) reconstruction-guided anatomical regional resection with segmentectomy in children with congenital pulmonary airway malformation (CPAM). This single-center retrospective study included 127 children treated between July 2023 and March 2025: 59 underwent 3D-guided anatomical regional resection and 68 underwent segmentectomy. Perioperative outcomes were compared; 119 children underwent chest CT at 1 year. There were no significant differences between the two groups in the characteristics related to the disease such as the type of lesion, the number of involved lung segments, the distribution of involved lung lobes, and the preoperative infection status. Regional resection was associated with shorter chest tube duration (2.2 ± 1.4 vs. 4.1 ± 1.3 days; P < 0.001) and hospital stay (5.0 ± 1.3 vs. 5.5 ± 1.4 days; P = 0.039). Operative time, postoperative complication rates, and CT findings of atelectasis or hyperinflation did not differ significantly. No residual CPAM was detected on 1-year CT in either group among the 119 children imaged. In selected children with CPAM, 3D-guided anatomical regional resection was feasible and was associated with shorter chest tube duration and hospital stay than segmentectomy. Anatomical resection guided by ct three-dimensional reconstruction makes anatomical lesion resection and lung preservation possible in children with CPAM.

- 核心发现/数据：患儿4月龄，因呼吸困难、发热和喂养差1个月就诊，无产前筛查；胸片示右胸圆形阴影，非增强CT示多囊性病变伴病灶内高密度影，疑继发感染或胸膜肺母细胞瘤（PPB）。行右上、中肺双肺叶切除，病理证实CPAM并排除PPB，为叙利亚首例确诊并手术管理的CPAM。

**原文摘要**：Although congenital pulmonary airway malformation (CPAM) is usually diagnosed during prenatal screening, delayed presentation may increase diagnostic uncertainty and operative complexity. **原文摘要**：Head, face, and neck injuries impose a substantial burden for emergency departments (ED); the mechanisms driving them across the life course however remain poorly understood at the population level. **中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估极低出生体重儿喂养指南对中心静脉置管使用及肠内喂养实践的质量改进效果，并设定降低PICC置管和缩短达全肠内喂养时间且不增加NEC等不良结局的目标。

**原文摘要**：Feeding guidelines (FGs) reduce the incidence of necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) and improve growth and enteral nutrition (EN) practices in very low birth weight (VLBW) infants. Our unit's baseline data revealed delayed initiation of EN, prolonged time to full EN, prolonged days on parenteral nutrition (PN), and frequent use of peripherally inserted central catheters (PICC). After introduction of this FG, our specific, measurable, attainable, realistic, and time-bound (SMART) aims were to reduce PICC placements in infants 1000-1500 g by 30% and to reduce the time to achieve full EN in VLBW infants by 20% without adversely impacting NEC rate, growth, and mom's own milk (MOM) provision. We conducted a time-series quality improvement initiative over 48 months. Outcome and process measures included the percentage of PICC placements, nutrition-related central line days, day of life (DOL) EN initiated, and DOL on full EN. Balancing measures included NEC, growth, and MOM provision. Two-tailed t-tests, Mann-Whitney U tests, and chi-square tests were used to analyze demographic data, numerical variables, and categorical variables, respectively. Statistical process control charts and established rules for special cause variation were applied. Our results demonstrate a 54% reduction in PICC placements and a 38% reduction in time to achieving full EN. We saw no increase in NEC rate but observed a decrease in growth and MOM provision. The use of a standardized FG resulted in fewer PICC placements and decreased time to full EN without increasing the NEC rate. Future initiatives will focus on improving growth and MOM provision in VLBW infants.
