## The "POP-IT" ear deformity: early recognition and non-surgical management of a novel neonatal auricular deformation.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 06:57
- 期刊: Eur Arch Otorhinolaryngol
- 国家/单位: 以色列 · Gray Faculty of Medical and Health Sciences
- 通讯作者: Yaacobi DS (Dafna.yaacobi@icloud.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00405-026-10503-6
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00405-026-10503-6) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42543439/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：首次描述并评估一种新型新生儿耳廓畸形“POP-IT耳”的早期识别及非手术矫形治疗效果。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入7例新生儿（男6例，女1例），平均开始治疗年龄为12.0±0.3天；畸形单侧6例、双侧不对称1例，5例合并其他耳廓畸形。平均矫正时间7.0±0.3天，总治疗时长平均4.0±0.3周，无并发症；平均随访18.5±5个月，美学效果稳定。
- 临床启示：新生儿“POP-IT耳”可通过早期非手术耳模塑形快速、安全矫正，临床医生应提高识别意识，及时干预以避免未来手术需求。

**原文摘要**：Congenital auricular deformities are common in newborns, yet many remain underdiagnosed despite the availability of effective early non-surgical correction. We describe a previously unreported auricular deformation, termed the "POP-IT" ear deformity, and present our early clinical experience with non-invasive management METHODS: A retrospective case series of neonates diagnosed with the POP-IT ear deformity and treated with auricular molding between 2024 and 2025 was conducted.Demographic data, deformity characteristics, treatment timing, duration, outcomes, and follow-up were analyzed. Seven neonates (6 males, 1 female) were included. The mean age at treatment initiation was 12.0 ± 0.3 days. The deformity was unilateral in six patientsand bilaterally asymmetrical in one. Five patients had associated auricular deformities. The mean gestational age was 38.6 ± 0.2 weeks . Desired correction was achievedwithin a mean of 7.0 ± 0.3 days, followed by continued molding for maintenance, with a total treatment duration averaging 4.0 ± 0.3 weeks. No complications were observed.Mean follow-up was 18.5 ± 5 months, with stable aesthetic outcomes in all patients CONCLUSIONS: The POP-IT ear deformity represents a distinct neonatal auricular deformation that can be effectively corrected using early non-surgical molding. Prompt recognition and intervention lead to rapid correction and durable outcomes, potentially obviating the need for future surgical treatment.

## Cow's milk protein allergy masked by suspected necrotizing enterocolitis: diagnostic delay and serial eosinophil trends in a neonatal cohort.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 06:57
- 期刊: J Matern Fetal Neonatal Med
- 国家/单位: 土耳其 · Karadeniz Technical University
- 通讯作者: Güven B
- DOI: 10.1080/14767058.2026.2709750
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1080/14767058.2026.2709750) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42543220/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估连续监测外周血嗜酸性粒细胞百分比（PBEP）在新生儿中鉴别牛奶蛋白过敏（CMPA）与坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）的诊断价值，并量化先前NEC诊断导致CMPA诊断延迟的程度。
- 核心发现/数据：初始PBEP在三组间无显著差异，但随访PBEP显示出极佳的鉴别诊断准确性；先前NEC诊断与显著的CMPA诊断延迟相关。研究纳入112例新生儿，分为NEC+CMPA组、单纯CMPA组及单纯NEC组。
- 临床启示：对反复疑似NEC发作且经标准NEC治疗后胃肠道症状持续或复发的患儿，应考虑CMPA可能；连续PBEP监测可作为NICU中无创、易得的辅助鉴别工具，有助于减少诊断延迟。

**原文摘要**：This study aimed to investigate the diagnostic utility of serial peripheral blood eosinophil percentage (PBEP) monitoring in differentiating cow's milk protein allergy (CMPA) from necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) in neonates, and to quantify the diagnostic delay associated with a preceding NEC diagnosis in neonates subsequently diagnosed with CMPA. This retrospective cohort study was conducted at a tertiary neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) between 2015 and 2025. A total of 112 neonates were enrolled and divided into three groups: Group 1 (NEC + CMPA,  Initial PBEP was comparable across all three groups ( Serial PBEP monitoring may serve as a practical, noninvasive, and readily available biomarker for differentiating CMPA from NEC in the NICU. While initial PBEP lacks discriminative value, follow-up PBEP demonstrates excellent diagnostic accuracy. A preceding NEC diagnosis may be associated with a substantial diagnostic delay, highlighting the importance of considering CMPA in neonates with recurrent suspected NEC episodes, particularly when gastrointestinal symptoms persist or recur despite standard NEC treatment.
