## Navigating the unexpected: management of incidental severe Factor V deficiency in term pregnancy.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:51
- 期刊: BMJ Case Rep · 中科院医学4区(医学：内科4区) · IF 0.4
- 国家/单位: Tamilnadu · Sri Ramachandra Medical College and Research Institute
- 通讯作者: Challa SVS (vaishnavi750@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1136/bcr-2026-273327
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1136/bcr-2026-273327) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42833854/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告一例足月妊娠无症状初产妇在择期剖宫产术前常规评估中偶然发现严重凝血因子V缺乏的诊疗经过。
- 核心发现/数据：患者凝血因子V活性<1%，凝血酶原时间和活化部分凝血活酶时间显著延长，但无既往出血史；混合试验及正常凝血酶时间提示共同通路缺陷，并经因子测定确诊。围术期采用多学科管理、新鲜冰冻血浆输注并避免椎管内麻醉，在全身麻醉下剖宫产分娩健康新生儿，母儿均无并发症。该例提示血浆因子V水平与出血表型相关性较差。
- 临床启示：对无症状但凝血筛查显著异常者应警惕罕见凝血因子缺乏，围术期需个体化多学科管理；凝血酶时间有助于定位共同通路凝血缺陷并指导诊断。

**原文摘要**：Severe Factor V deficiency (parahaemophilia) is a rare autosomal recessive coagulation disorder with a highly variable clinical phenotype. We report the incidental detection of isolated severe Factor V deficiency (<1% activity) in a completely asymptomatic primigravida during routine preoperative evaluation prior to an elective caesarean section. Despite marked prolongation of prothrombin time and activated partial thromboplastin time, the patient had no prior bleeding history. Mixing studies and a normal thrombin time localised the defect to the common pathway, which was confirmed by factor assay. Management involved multidisciplinary planning, peri-operative fresh frozen plasma transfusion and avoidance of neuraxial anaesthesia. The patient underwent caesarean section under general anaesthesia and delivered a healthy neonate without maternal or neonatal complications. This case highlights the poor correlation between plasma Factor V levels and bleeding phenotype and underscores the diagnostic value of thrombin time in evaluating common pathway coagulation defects.

## Primary Surgical Resection of Localized Cervical Kaposiform Hemangioendothelioma in a Neonate: A Potentially Curative Strategy in Selected Patients.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:51
- 期刊: J Pediatr Hematol Oncol · 中科院医学4区(血液学4区/肿瘤学4区/儿科4区) · IF 0.9 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: VM Medical Park Samsun Hospital · VM Medical Park Samsun Hospital
- 通讯作者: Bernay F
- DOI: 10.1097/MPH.0000000000003297
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/MPH.0000000000003297) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42832746/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过一例新生儿局部颈部卡波西样血管内皮瘤（KHE）病例，探讨对解剖可切除的选择性患者行完整手术切除是否可作为潜在治愈策略。
- 核心发现/数据：足月女婴出生时即发现4×4 cm颈部肿块，影像示边界清楚、高度血管化、无重要结构浸润或压迫，且无凝血病，但影像表现不典型KHE且不能排除恶性。第10天行完整手术切除，术后病理证实KHE，术后过程平稳，1年随访无复发。
- 临床启示：对于解剖可切除、无Kasabach-Merritt现象或凝血障碍、且需排除恶性的选择性新生儿KHE，完整手术切除可能提供潜在治愈机会。临床决策应基于多学科评估和严格个体化选择。

**原文摘要**：Kaposiform hemangioendothelioma (KHE) is a rare, locally aggressive vascular tumor of infancy that may be associated with Kasabach-Merritt phenomenon (KMP). We report a term female neonate presenting with a 4×4 cm cervical mass at birth. Imaging demonstrated a well-circumscribed, highly vascular lesion without infiltration or compression of vital structures, and no coagulopathy was detected. Although these imaging features were atypical for KHE, the lesion was anatomically resectable, and malignancy could not be excluded from the differential diagnosis. Complete surgical excision was therefore performed on day 10. Histopathology confirmed KHE. The postoperative course was uneventful, with no recurrence at 1-year follow-up. This case highlights an uncommon radiologic presentation of KHE and suggests that complete surgical excision may represent a potentially curative option in carefully selected patients with anatomically resectable disease.

## An assessment of the health system's capacity for major structural birth defects care in Tanzania using anorectal malformations as a tracer condition: A multi-phase, mixed-methods study protocol.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:51
- 期刊: PLoS One · 中科院综合性期刊3区(综合性期刊3区) · IF 2.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Tanzania · Aga Khan University Medical College
- 通讯作者: Maswime S
- DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0359639
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0359639) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42832490/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：以肛门直肠畸形为示踪病种，采用多阶段混合方法评估坦桑尼亚卫生系统对重大结构性出生缺陷的预防、早期识别和诊疗能力。
- 核心发现/数据：该研究为方案性研究，尚无结果；背景数据显示，中低收入国家承担90%-95%的出生及相关死亡，结构性出生缺陷导致新生儿和5岁以下儿童疾病与死亡负担过高，而肛门直肠畸形占撒哈拉以南非洲儿科外科工作量的33%-40%。研究分四阶段：横断面调查母亲-新生儿预防利用及早期检测预测因素，家长现象学访谈，四家三级医院对照儿童外科最优资源清单评估，以及在Muhimbili国家医院测试术前肛门直肠畸形清单可行性。
- 临床启示：通过系统评估预防、检测、转诊和手术资源缺口，并验证术前清单，可促进肛门直肠畸形等重大出生缺陷的早期识别、规范转诊和围手术期管理。研究结果可为政策制定、资源分配和针对性干预提供依据。

**原文摘要**：Structural birth defects cause disproportionate neonatal and under-five morbidity and mortality in low- and middle-income countries, where 90-95% of births and associated deaths occur; however, health-system readiness for prevention uptake, early detection, and management remains poorly understood. Anorectal malformations account for 33-40% of the paediatric surgical workload in sub-Saharan Africa, making it a tracer condition for evaluating Tanzania's capacity across primary, secondary, and tertiary prevention levels. Although delayed detection, high postoperative morbidity, and referral dysfunction are documented, the upstream preconception, antenatal, delivery, and postnatal profile, parental lived experience, external benchmarking of paediatric surgical capacity, and feasibility of a context-specific preoperative checklist remain to be quantified within the Tanzanian clinical environment. This multi-phase mixed-methods protocol comprises four phases. In Phase I, all mother-newborn dyads where the infant has an anorectal malformation presenting to Muhimbili National Hospital will be enrolled in a cross-sectional study quantifying preconception, antenatal, delivery, and postnatal prevention uptake; multivariable logistic regression will identify predictors of early detection; the sample size will be derived via Cochran's formula using finite population correction. Phase II will use descriptive phenomenology through purposive in-depth interviews with parents exploring socio-cultural access determinants, analysed via the Stevick-Colaizzi-Keen method. Phase III will conduct a facility-level survey against the Optimal Resources for Children's Surgery checklist at four tertiary hospitals; domain-specific and total compliance scores will be calculated, gaps quantified against benchmarks, and centres compared using Friedman tests and chi-square or Fisher's exact tests. Phase IV will test feasibility of a preoperative anorectal malformation checklist at Muhimbili National Hospital, examining practicality and acceptability (non-parametric comparisons). This study will produce a system-level appraisal of Tanzania's capacity to prevent, detect, and manage major structural birth defects using anorectal malformation as a tracer condition. Findings will inform policy, guide resource allocation, and support targeted interventions.

## Pulmonary Valve Growth After Valve-Sparing Right Ventricle Outflow Tract Stenting in Tetralogy of Fallot: A Single-Centre Retrospective Cohort Study.

**原文摘要**：Right ventricular outflow tract (RVOT) stenting is an established initial palliation for neonates and infants with Tetralogy of Fallot (ToF). However, data on valve-sparing RVOT stenting and its impact on pulmonary valve (PV) growth are limited. Our aim was to evaluate pulmonary valve annular growth following valve-sparing RVOT stenting and to examine how this growth related to the type of definitive surgical repair performed. This retrospective single-centre study included 96 consecutive patients with palliated ToF over a 16-year period. Valve-sparing RVOT stenting was achieved in 46 patients, while 50 underwent valve-crossing stenting. PV growth was assessed using Z-scores in the valve-sparing group, and surgical strategies were compared between groups. In the valve-sparing group, PV annular Z-scores improved significantly from - 4.3 ± 1.3 at stenting to - 2.8 ± 1.0 before surgical repair (P < 0.001). In the valve-crossed group, final stent diameter Z-scores (-3.9 ± 1.9) showed minimal change from baseline (-4.5 ± 1.4). Time to surgical repair did not differ between groups. Definitive repair was achieved in 100% of valve-crossed cases and 95.6% of valve-spared cases, with similar weight between groups. A transannular patch (TAP) was performed in 65.9% of repaired valve-sparing patients and 64% of valve-crossed patients, while RV-PA conduit placement was performed in 22.7% and 36%, respectively. Valve-sparing repair was achieved in 11.4% of repaired patients in the valve-sparing cohort. Valve-sparing RVOT stenting is associated with significant pulmonary valve annular growth prior to definitive repair. Although only a minority of patients ultimately underwent valve-sparing surgery, the observed annular growth suggests that this strategy may increase future suitability for pulmonary valve preservation, subject to anatomical and surgical considerations.

**原文摘要**：Inguinal hernia (IH) occurs frequently in extremely- and very-low-birth-weight (ELBW/VLBW) infants. This study evaluates the feasibility of performing laparoscopic repair (LR) near neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) discharge, by comparing its perioperative outcomes with those of open repair (OR) in ELBW/VLBW infants. We retrospectively reviewed infants with birth weight < 1500 g who were diagnosed with IH during NICU hospitalization and underwent IH repair between 2015 and 2023. LR was performed using the laparoscopic percutaneous extraperitoneal closure (LPEC) technique. We compared the patient characteristics, operative variables, and postoperative outcomes between the groups. Among a total of 49 infants, 31 underwent OR and 18 underwent LR. The LR group had a higher body weight at surgery, a lower prevalence of chronic lung disease, and different sex distribution. Operative and anesthesia times were similar for unilateral repair but shorter with LR for bilateral repair. There were no significant differences in postoperative complications. Three infants from the OR group underwent subsequent umbilicoplasty, and one developed a metachronous contralateral hernia. LR appears technically feasible with acceptable short-term outcomes and may allow contralateral evaluation and concomitant umbilicoplasty. However, baseline imbalances, the small sample size, and limited follow-up preclude conclusions regarding superiority or equivalence to OR.
