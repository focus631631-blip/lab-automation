## Childbirth in an underground maternity ward during wartime: maternal birth experience and early bonding.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:54
- 期刊: Arch Womens Ment Health · 中科院医学3区(精神病学3区) · IF 3.2 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 以色列 · Galilee Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Frank Wolf M (mayaw@gmc.gov.il)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00737-026-01784-1
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00737-026-01784-1) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42975583/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估战时地下产房产妇的分娩体验、分娩支持偏好及母婴联结，并与战前数据比较。
- 核心发现/数据：共200名产妇参与，研究期间医院分娩量较去年同期下降25%；49%认为战争负面影响分娩体验，80%认为负面影响产后住院体验，30%认为战争负面影响与新生儿的联结。11%表示战争影响其分娩支持者选择，22%有家属正在服役；与战前队列相比，更多女性在器械分娩时希望有支持者在场（P=0.001）。
- 临床启示：战时地下产房应重点关注产妇分娩及产后心理体验，强化分娩支持与母婴联结促进措施，尤其应重视器械分娩时的陪伴支持。

**原文摘要**：Armed conflict may adversely affect childbirth and maternal well-being. Following the October 7, 2023 attacks and subsequent missile threats in northern Israel, maternity services at our tertiary medical center were relocated to a fortified underground facility. We evaluated childbirth experience, labor-support preferences, and maternal-newborn bonding during wartime. This prospective questionnaire-based study was conducted between October 2023 and February 2024 at a tertiary medical center in northern Israel. Participants completed anonymous questionnaires before childbirthand within 48 h postpartum. The survey assessed preferred labor support, the perceived impact of the war on childbirth and postpartum hospitalization, and maternal-newborn bonding. Findings were compared with data from a similar pre-war questionnaire study conducted between 2017 and 2018. A total of 200 women participated. During the study period, hospital delivery volume declined by 25% compared with the corresponding period in the previous year. Overall, 49% of participants reported that the war negatively affected their childbirth experience, 80% reported an adverse effect on their postpartum hospitalization experience, and 30% reported that the war negatively affected bonding with their newborn. 11% reported that the war influenced their choice of labor supporter, and 22% had a family member on active military duty. Compared with the pre-war cohort, more women preferred the presence of a support person during instrumental birth(P = 0.001). Women giving birth during the war in an underground maternity ward frequently reported adverse childbirth and postpartum experiences, including perceived negative effects on newborn bonding.

## Unformed Conus Medullaris in Children With Spinal Lipoma Is a Risk Factor Requiring Clean Intermittent Catheterization.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:54
- 期刊: Neurourol Urodyn · 中科院医学3区(泌尿学与肾脏学3区) · IF 2.0 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 日本 · Miyagi Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Ito A
- DOI: 10.1002/nau.70376
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/nau.70376) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42446243/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：识别脊髓脂肪瘤患儿需要清洁间歇导尿（CIC）的相关危险因素。
- 核心发现/数据：回顾性分析116例≤18岁患儿，47例（41%）需CIC，中位引入年龄43.7个月。多因素分析显示，脊髓圆锥未成形（HR 11.5，95%CI 3.53-37.7，p<0.0001）、初次视频尿动力学检查（VUDS）提示逼尿肌过度活动（DO）（HR 2.23，95%CI 1.07-4.62，p=0.0307）及逼尿肌-括约肌协同失调（DSD）（HR 2.34，95%CI 1.01-5.41，p=0.0465）是需CIC的显著危险因素。
- 临床启示：脊髓圆锥未成形患儿发生需CIC的神经源性下尿路功能障碍风险高，应密切监测；VUDS出现DO和DSD可能提示先天性神经组织发育不良，需及早进行泌尿系统评估和管理。

**原文摘要**：Spina bifida is the most common cause of neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction (NLUTD) in children. Spinal lipoma, a form of occult spina bifida, exhibits variable functional outcomes and unpredictable progression, which may delay timely urinary management. The requirement for clean intermittent catheterization (CIC) also imposes a substantial and lasting impact on the social lives of affected children and their caregivers. Consequently, identifying the predictors of NLUTD necessitating CIC is of significant clinical interest. This study aimed to identify the risk factors associated with the need for CIC in patients with spinal lipoma. A retrospective observational study was conducted on 116 patients aged ≤ 18 years who underwent videourodynamics (VUDS) and received treatment at Miyagi Children's Hospital between April 2007 and April 2022. We excluded patients with other complications, such as anorectal malformations. The study evaluated the necessity for CIC using magnetic resonance and computed tomography imaging, VUDS bladder function, and clinical data, including medication details, duration, and timing of untethering procedures. Among the 116 patients analyzed (50 boys and 66 girls), 47 (41%) required CIC at the time of assessment, with a median introduction age of 43.7 months. Patients were categorized into five subtypes of the Arai's classification: 33 with dorsal type, 21 with transitional type, 30 with lipomyelomeningocele (LMMC), 28 with caudal type, and four with filar type. CIC was required in 5 dorsal type, 14 transitional type, 18 LMMC, and 10 caudal type patients, whereas no filar type patients required CIC. Unformed conus medullaris was present in 73 patients (62.9%), and sacral agenesis in 50 patients (43.1%). Proportional hazard model analysis identified unformed conus medullaris (HR 11.5; 95% CI 3.53-37.7; p < 0.0001), detrusor overactivity (DO) (HR 2.23; 95% CI 1.07-4.62; p = 0.0307), and detrusor-sphincter dyssynergia (DSD) (HR 2.34; 95% CI 1.01-5.41; p = 0.0465) on initial VUDS as significant risk factors for the requirement of CIC. Patients with an unformed conus medullaris are at a high risk of NLUTD requiring CIC and warrant close monitoring. The presence of DO and DSD on VUDS likely reflects underlying congenital neural tissue dysgenesis, indicating that abnormalities during the junctional neurulation stage of spinal cord development are strongly associated with NLUTD.

## Closing the Evidence Gap in Paediatric Surgery Research in Low- and Middle-Income Countries: An Evidence Mapping Narrative Review.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 11:52
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University Hospital of Monastir
- 通讯作者: Kechiche N (kechichenahla@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163567
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163567) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42977022/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：映射低中收入国家小儿外科研究产出模式，识别研究设计、研究领导力和亚专科代表性方面的关键证据缺口，并提出强化证据生成的策略。
- 核心发现/数据：共识别1090条记录，筛选774篇，最终纳入50项核心研究；其中88.7%（44/50）为观察性研究，87.5%为单中心研究，多中心研究仅占16.0%（8/50）但方法学更严谨。研究产出集中于东南亚和东地中海地区，高收入国家作者常占据第一和资深作者位置。研究多聚焦普通小儿外科、神经外科和骨科，关键亚专科、长期生活质量指标及儿童自身决策视角明显不足。
- 临床启示：应优先建设多中心协作网络、公平研究伙伴关系、本地化注册登记和数字基础设施，以缩小全球小儿外科证据缺口。临床实践需依托情境适宜的高质量证据，并关注亚专科覆盖与患儿长期结局。

**原文摘要**：To map patterns in paediatric surgical research output from low- and middle-income countries (LMICs), identify critical gaps in study design, research leadership, and subspeciality representation, and highlight strategic opportunities to strengthen evidence generation. A narrative evidence-mapping review was conducted of paediatric surgery studies published between January 1, 2005, and December 31, 2025, across PubMed, Scopus, and Web of Science. Standardized data extraction captured study design, geographic distribution, authorship patterns, collaboration models, and core research focus areas. Of 1,090 records identified, 774 were screened for eligibility, and 50 core studies met all inclusion criteria for evidence mapping. The vast majority of studies were observational (88.7%, = 44/50) and single-centre (87.5%), frequently employing retrospective or cross-sectional designs with limited longitudinal follow-up. Research output was heavily concentrated within the Southeast Asia and Eastern Mediterranean regions, while authors from high-income countries (HICs) frequently occupied dominant first and senior authorship positions. Multicentre studies were less common (16.0%, n = 8/50) but demonstrated markedly superior methodological rigor. Research activity focused predominantly on general paediatric surgery, neurosurgery, and orthopaedics, whereas key subspecialties, long-term quality-of-life metrics, and children's own perspectives in surgical decision-making remained profoundly underrepresented. Paediatric surgery research in LMICs remains dominated by isolated, single-centre observational studies that do not adequately reflect the true scale, urgency, or diversity of children's surgical needs. Expanding multicentre collaborative networks, fostering equitable research partnerships, institutionalizing localized registries, and leveraging emerging digital infrastructures are critical pathways to closing the global paediatric surgery evidence gap and driving context-appropriate, evidence-based clinical care.

**原文摘要**：Meconium ileus (MI) affects up to 20% of newborns with cystic fibrosis (CF). Children with CF and more severe MI, characterized by g-tube placement, had decreased growth and pulmonary function. Gastrostomy tube placement may indicate a higher risk of poor health outcomes and could serve as a marker of MI severity, warranting attention to prevent subsequent adverse outcomes.

## Sleep position and sleep apnea in newborns.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:52
- 期刊: J Clin Sleep Med
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University of Michigan
- 通讯作者: Matlen LB (lmatlen@umich.edu)
- DOI: 10.1007/s44470-026-00218-0
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44470-026-00218-0) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42977878/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估需NICU护理新生儿及脊髓脊膜膨出修复术后新生儿中，睡眠体位与呼吸暂停低通气指数（AHI）的关联。
- 核心发现/数据：在261名新生儿中，NICU恢复组、产后及开放胎儿脊髓脊膜膨出修复组的仰卧与非仰卧AHI相似（调整分析均p>0.33）；胎儿镜修复组仰卧时AHI显著低于非仰卧（7.94 vs 22.1，p<0.001）。提示需NICU护理新生儿的睡眠呼吸暂停未必因仰卧而加重，特定情况下可能存在例外。
- 临床启示：支持出院后继续遵循仰卧睡眠（“Back to Sleep”）以预防SIDS，且该体位似乎不增加睡眠呼吸暂停风险；对特定修复类型或高危新生儿仍需个体化评估。

**原文摘要**：Public health measures recommend that babies sleep supine, but at all other times of life this can worsen sleep apnea. To examine associations between infant sleep position and the apnea-hypopnea index (AHI), we studied polysomnograms of neonates with repaired myelomeningocele, who are often positioned nonsupine during wound healing, and of neonates without congenital anomalies who were recovering in the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU). We evaluated sleep apnea severity, as reflected by the AHI, in different positions as recorded during polysomnograms from three cohort studies of infants in NICUs. Participants included 173 infants with myelomeningocele (multiple hospitals) and 88 NICU recovery infants across two single-center cohorts. The myelomeningocele cohort was subdivided by repair type (postnatal, open fetal, and fetoscopic surgery groups). We evaluated AHI in different sleep positions using generalized estimating equations to account for repeated measures. Among 261 neonates, AHI was similar between supine and nonsupine positions in the NICU recovery, postnatal myelomeningocele, and open fetal myelomeningocele groups (adjusted analyses, all p > 0.33). In contrast, the fetoscopic myelomeningocele group had substantially lower AHI when supine (7.94, 95% CI 4.95-12.75) compared to nonsupine (22.1, 95% CI 14.02-35.01; p < 0.001). Surprisingly, sleep apnea in neonates requiring NICU care may not be exacerbated by supine sleep, with possible exceptions in some specific conditions. Although we did not study healthy newborns, we speculate that obligate nasal breathing in neonates may protect against worsening of sleep apnea when supine. "Back to Sleep" remains life-saving and appears unlikely to increase sleep apnea risk. Public health measures to prevent SIDS recommend that babies sleep supine. The degree to which sleep position influences sleep apnea in infants is unclear. We had a unique opportunity to study the position dependence of sleep apnea for neonates in the NICU setting, who sometimes sleep in nonsupine positions. As studies at older ages widely show sleep apnea to be worse supine than nonsupine, we hypothesized that we would find the same in neonates. Study results suggest that sleep apnea severity in neonates who require NICU care is not exacerbated by supine sleep. "Back to Sleep" remains a life-saving intervention for infants upon discharge to home and, moreover, does not appear to increase risk for sleep apnea.

## Genetic subtyping of MASLD reveals a glucose-lowering axis linked to GCKR alternative splicing.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:52
- 期刊: Diabetologia · 中科院医学1区(内分泌学与代谢1区) · IF 10.4 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Human Phenome Institute
- 通讯作者: Wang J (jcwang@fudan.edu.cn)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00125-026-06916-x
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00125-026-06916-x) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42977848/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：确定代谢功能障碍相关脂肪性肝病（MASLD）是否存在血糖后果不同的遗传亚型，并阐明降糖型MASLD的遗传基础。
- 核心发现/数据：整合18个MASLD相关变异构建分区多基因风险评分，在英国生物银行发现双向血糖轴：一个PRS降低空腹血糖和2型糖尿病风险，其余PRS升高二者，但均与肝损伤标志物升高和不良肝脏结局相关，并在中国台州纵向队列中复制。共定位和精细定位将GCKR rs1260326（C>T）确定为MASLD-2型糖尿病共享信号及循环GKRP的主要顺式pQTL；功能实验显示T等位基因促进GCKR外显子15跳跃、减少全长GKRP并产生截短蛋白，结构模型提示截短

**原文摘要**：Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) is a global metabolic condition with a complex relationship to type 2 diabetes. Yet human genetic studies suggest the existence of a MASLD subtype with favourable glycaemic effects, raising a clinically important question: how can hepatic steatosis coexist with improved glycaemic outcomes? We aimed to determine whether MASLD comprises genetically distinct subtypes with divergent glycaemic consequences, and to define the genetic basis of a glucose-lowering MASLD subtype. We integrated genetic and triglyceride-related metabolomic profiles to cluster 18 established MASLD-associated variants and construct mutually adjusted partitioned polygenic risk scores (PRSs) in the UK Biobank. We tested associations with hepatic biomarkers, liver outcomes and type 2 diabetes, and replicated glycaemic findings in an independent Chinese cohort (the Taizhou Longitudinal Study [TZL]). We prioritised candidate loci using colocalisation and fine-mapping and performed allele-specific functional assays and structural modelling to investigate the underlying mechanism at the lead locus. Partitioned PRSs revealed bidirectional glycaemic axes: one PRS lowered fasting glucose and type 2 diabetes risk, whereas the remaining PRSs increased both, despite all associating with higher liver injury markers and adverse liver outcomes. The divergent glycaemic effects were replicated in the TZL. To pinpoint the genetic basis of the glucose-lowering axis, colocalisation and fine-mapping prioritised GCKR rs1260326 (C>T) as a shared MASLD-type 2 diabetes signal and the lead cis-pQTL for circulating glucokinase regulatory protein (GKRP). Functional experiments showed that the rs1260326 T allele promotes exon-15 skipping in GCKR, reduces the abundance of full-length GKRP and yields a truncated protein. In silico structural modelling suggested that the truncated GKRP isoform may have reduced binding affinity for glucokinase, although this predicted effect requires experimental validation. MASLD comprises genetically distinct subtypes with opposing glycaemic and type 2 diabetes risk profiles. Within the glucose-lowering subtype, rs1260326-mediated alternative splicing of GCKR provides a potential mechanistic link to reduced GKRP abundance and glycaemic protection. These findings support a more granular view of MASLD heterogeneity and may inform future subtype-specific risk stratification and prevention.
