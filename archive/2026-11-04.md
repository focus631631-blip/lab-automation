## Childbirth in an underground maternity ward during wartime: maternal birth experience and early bonding.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:54
- 期刊: Arch Womens Ment Health · 中科院医学3区(精神病学3区) · IF 3.2 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 以色列 · Galilee Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Frank Wolf M (mayaw@gmc.gov.il)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00737-026-01784-1
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00737-026-01784-1) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42975583/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估战时地下产房产妇的分娩体验、分娩支持偏好及母婴联结，并与战前数据比较。
- 核心发现/数据：共200名产妇参与，研究期间医院分娩量较去年同期下降25%；49%认为战争负面影响分娩体验，80%认为负面影响产后住院体验，30%认为战争负面影响与新生儿的联结。11%表示战争影响其分娩支持者选择，22%有家属正在服役；与战前队列相比，更多女性在器械分娩时希望有支持者在场（P=0.001）。
- 临床启示：战时地下产房应重点关注产妇分娩及产后心理体验，强化分娩支持与母婴联结促进措施，尤其应重视器械分娩时的陪伴支持。

**原文摘要**：Armed conflict may adversely affect childbirth and maternal well-being. Following the October 7, 2023 attacks and subsequent missile threats in northern Israel, maternity services at our tertiary medical center were relocated to a fortified underground facility. We evaluated childbirth experience, labor-support preferences, and maternal-newborn bonding during wartime. This prospective questionnaire-based study was conducted between October 2023 and February 2024 at a tertiary medical center in northern Israel. Participants completed anonymous questionnaires before childbirthand within 48 h postpartum. The survey assessed preferred labor support, the perceived impact of the war on childbirth and postpartum hospitalization, and maternal-newborn bonding. Findings were compared with data from a similar pre-war questionnaire study conducted between 2017 and 2018. A total of 200 women participated. During the study period, hospital delivery volume declined by 25% compared with the corresponding period in the previous year. Overall, 49% of participants reported that the war negatively affected their childbirth experience, 80% reported an adverse effect on their postpartum hospitalization experience, and 30% reported that the war negatively affected bonding with their newborn. 11% reported that the war influenced their choice of labor supporter, and 22% had a family member on active military duty. Compared with the pre-war cohort, more women preferred the presence of a support person during instrumental birth(P = 0.001). Women giving birth during the war in an underground maternity ward frequently reported adverse childbirth and postpartum experiences, including perceived negative effects on newborn bonding.

## Unformed Conus Medullaris in Children With Spinal Lipoma Is a Risk Factor Requiring Clean Intermittent Catheterization.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:54
- 期刊: Neurourol Urodyn · 中科院医学3区(泌尿学与肾脏学3区) · IF 2.0 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 日本 · Miyagi Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Ito A
- DOI: 10.1002/nau.70376
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/nau.70376) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42446243/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：识别脊髓脂肪瘤患儿需要清洁间歇导尿（CIC）的相关危险因素。
- 核心发现/数据：回顾性分析116例≤18岁患儿，47例（41%）需CIC，中位引入年龄43.7个月。多因素分析显示，脊髓圆锥未成形（HR 11.5，95%CI 3.53-37.7，p<0.0001）、初次视频尿动力学检查（VUDS）提示逼尿肌过度活动（DO）（HR 2.23，95%CI 1.07-4.62，p=0.0307）及逼尿肌-括约肌协同失调（DSD）（HR 2.34，95%CI 1.01-5.41，p=0.0465）是需CIC的显著危险因素。
- 临床启示：脊髓圆锥未成形患儿发生需CIC的神经源性下尿路功能障碍风险高，应密切监测；VUDS出现DO和DSD可能提示先天性神经组织发育不良，需及早进行泌尿系统评估和管理。

**原文摘要**：Spina bifida is the most common cause of neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction (NLUTD) in children. Spinal lipoma, a form of occult spina bifida, exhibits variable functional outcomes and unpredictable progression, which may delay timely urinary management. The requirement for clean intermittent catheterization (CIC) also imposes a substantial and lasting impact on the social lives of affected children and their caregivers. Consequently, identifying the predictors of NLUTD necessitating CIC is of significant clinical interest. This study aimed to identify the risk factors associated with the need for CIC in patients with spinal lipoma. A retrospective observational study was conducted on 116 patients aged ≤ 18 years who underwent videourodynamics (VUDS) and received treatment at Miyagi Children's Hospital between April 2007 and April 2022. We excluded patients with other complications, such as anorectal malformations. The study evaluated the necessity for CIC using magnetic resonance and computed tomography imaging, VUDS bladder function, and clinical data, including medication details, duration, and timing of untethering procedures. Among the 116 patients analyzed (50 boys and 66 girls), 47 (41%) required CIC at the time of assessment, with a median introduction age of 43.7 months. Patients were categorized into five subtypes of the Arai's classification: 33 with dorsal type, 21 with transitional type, 30 with lipomyelomeningocele (LMMC), 28 with caudal type, and four with filar type. CIC was required in 5 dorsal type, 14 transitional type, 18 LMMC, and 10 caudal type patients, whereas no filar type patients required CIC. Unformed conus medullaris was present in 73 patients (62.9%), and sacral agenesis in 50 patients (43.1%). Proportional hazard model analysis identified unformed conus medullaris (HR 11.5; 95% CI 3.53-37.7; p < 0.0001), detrusor overactivity (DO) (HR 2.23; 95% CI 1.07-4.62; p = 0.0307), and detrusor-sphincter dyssynergia (DSD) (HR 2.34; 95% CI 1.01-5.41; p = 0.0465) on initial VUDS as significant risk factors for the requirement of CIC. Patients with an unformed conus medullaris are at a high risk of NLUTD requiring CIC and warrant close monitoring. The presence of DO and DSD on VUDS likely reflects underlying congenital neural tissue dysgenesis, indicating that abnormalities during the junctional neurulation stage of spinal cord development are strongly associated with NLUTD.
