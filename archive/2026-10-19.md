## Ηip dysplasia in children with spina bifida after prenatal surgical closure.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:22
- 期刊: Dev Med Child Neurol · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/临床神经病学2区) · IF 3.7 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 瑞士 · University Children's Hospital Zurich
- 通讯作者: Dreher T
- DOI: 10.1111/dmcn.70546
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1111/dmcn.70546) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42893566/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：确定产前手术闭合后脊柱裂患儿新生儿髋发育不良的患病率，并探讨其与功能水平和早产的关系。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入168例有新生儿髋超声资料的患儿（336髋），其中82%（138/168）为早产；初次超声显示85%（286/336）髋成熟、9.8%（33/336）未成熟、5.1%（17/336）发育不良。髋状态较差与功能水平较高呈弱相关（r=-0.243，p<0.001），而髋发育不良与早产无显著关联（p=0.080）。该人群新生儿髋发育不良发生率处于普通人群报告范围内。
- 临床启示：产前手术闭合后的脊柱裂新生儿仍应按规范化路径在生后第1周（按矫正胎龄）行Graf髋超声

**原文摘要**：To determine the prevalence of neonatal hip dysplasia in children with spina bifida after prenatal surgical closure and examine its association with functional level and preterm birth. We conducted a retrospective analysis of prospectively collected standardized registry data from a tertiary specialized centre providing comprehensive spina bifida diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up. Standardized hip ultrasound according to the Graf method was performed as part of a protocolized clinical pathway after prenatal surgery, within the first week of life corrected for preterm birth. The primary outcome was the prevalence of hip dysplasia at the initial ultrasound. Associations between hip status, functional level, and gestational age were assessed using Spearman's rank correlation. A total of 212 children (101 males, 111 females) underwent prenatal surgical closure between 1st January 2011 and 30th August 2022; 168 children (336 hips) had available neonatal ultrasound data and were included in the analysis. Preterm birth was present in 138 (82%) of 168 children, including 99 (59%) born late preterm, 35 (21%) born very preterm, and four (2%) born extremely preterm. At the initial ultrasound, 286 (85%) of 336 hips were mature, 33 (9.8%) were immature, and 17 (5.1%) were dysplastic. Worse hip status was weakly associated with a higher functional level (r = -0.243; p < 0.001), whereas no significant association was found between hip dysplasia and preterm birth (p = 0.080). The prevalence of neonatal hip dysplasia in children with spina bifida after prenatal surgical closure was within the range reported for the general population.

## KSMFM factsheet on obstetric complications after myomectomy or adenomyomectomy: uterine rupture and placenta accreta spectrum.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:35
- 期刊: Obstet Gynecol Sci · 中科院医学4区(妇产科学4区) · IF 2.5
- 国家/单位: 韩国 · Kyung Hee University Hospital at Gangdong
- 通讯作者: Seol HJ (seolhj@korea.ac.kr)
- DOI: 10.5468/ogs.26214
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.5468/ogs.26214) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42893824/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：概述韩国育龄女性接受子宫肌瘤剔除术或子宫腺肌病病灶切除术后的产科并发症风险，重点为子宫破裂和胎盘植入谱系疾病（PAS）。
- 核心发现/数据：近20年韩国育龄女性孕前诊断子宫肌瘤和子宫腺肌病及接受保留生育功能手术的人数增加；肌瘤剔除术后子宫破裂发生率为0.5%–1.52%，腺肌病病灶切除术后更高，为3.2%–12.5%。子宫破裂可在产前发生且不受孕周限制，临床预测困难；两类手术后PAS风险高，可导致严重母儿并发症。
- 临床启示：育龄女性决定手术前应接受充分的产科风险咨询，术后妊娠需全程规范产前保健，并警惕子宫破裂和PAS。

**原文摘要**：Over the past 20 years, as the number of women of reproductive age in Korea diagnosed with uterine leiomyoma and adenomyosis prior to pregnancy has increased, fertility-preserving uterine surgeries such as myomectomy and adenomyomectomy have also increased. Although these surgeries can preserve fertility, they can cause serious obstetric complications, such as uterine rupture and placenta accreta spectrum (PAS). The incidence of uterine rupture after myomectomy is reported to be 0.5-1.52%, whereas the incidence after adenomyomectomy is considerably higher, ranging from 3.2 to 12.5%. A key clinical characteristic is that rupture can occur before labor regardless of gestational age, making clinical prediction difficult. Furthermore, myomectomy and adenomyomectomy carry a high risk of PAS, which can lead to severe maternal and neonatal morbidity. Therefore, women of childbearing age should receive sufficient counseling before deciding on surgery and undergo thorough prenatal care throughout pregnancy.

## Biomarkers of native liver survival and portal hypertension in biliary atresia.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:35
- 期刊: J Pediatr Gastroenterol Nutr · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/胃肠肝病学4区/营养学4区) · IF 3.1 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 巴西 · Department of Pediatrics/Faculty of Medical Sciences
- 通讯作者: Bellomo-Brandão MÂ
- DOI: 10.1002/jpn3.70618
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/jpn3.70618) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42893939/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估APRi、iBALF及Ishak纤维化评分对接受Kasai肝门空肠吻合术的胆道闭锁患儿自体肝生存和门脉高压的预测价值。
- 核心发现/数据：APRi、iBALF和Ishak纤维化评分升高均与自体肝生存降低相关（分别p<0.006、p<0.025、p<0.048）。APRi和iBALF对门脉高压具有良好预测价值（分别p<0.005、p<0.032）。三者均显示对KPE后胆道闭锁患儿自体肝生存的预后价值。
- 临床启示：上述无创生物标志物可用于胆道闭锁术后临床随访，协助风险分层和识别不良预后高风险患儿。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate the prognostic value of noninvasive markers Aspartate Aminotransferase to Platelet Ratio Index (APRi) and Infantile Biliary Atresia Liver Fibrosis score (iBALF), as well as the histological Ishak fibrosis score, as predictors of native liver survival (NLS) and portal hypertension (PH) in children with biliary atresia BA undergoing Kasai portoenterostomy (KPE). A single-center, retrospective observational study was conducted on children diagnosed with BA who underwent KPE. APRi and iBALF were calculated using laboratory data collected at hospital discharge after KPE. The degree of hepatic fibrosis was assessed and reviewed using the Ishak fibrosis score in liver biopsy specimens collected at KPE. NLS was analyzed using Kaplan-Meier survival curves and Cox proportional hazards regression. A p-value < 0.05 was considered statistically significant. Higher APRi, iBALF, and Ishak fibrosis score values were associated with lower NLS (p < 0.006, p < 0.025, and p < 0.048, respectively). APRi and iBALF emerged as good PH predictors (p < 0.005 and p < 0.032, respectively). APRi, iBALF, and the Ishak fibrosis score showed prognostic value in predicting NLS in children with BA after KPE. These findings reinforce the potential use of biomarkers in the clinical follow-up of these patients, supporting risk stratification strategies.
