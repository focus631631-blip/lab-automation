## Ηip dysplasia in children with spina bifida after prenatal surgical closure.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:22
- 期刊: Dev Med Child Neurol · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/临床神经病学2区) · IF 3.7 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 瑞士 · University Children's Hospital Zurich
- 通讯作者: Dreher T
- DOI: 10.1111/dmcn.70546
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1111/dmcn.70546) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42893566/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：确定产前手术闭合后脊柱裂患儿新生儿髋发育不良的患病率，并探讨其与功能水平和早产的关系。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入168例有新生儿髋超声资料的患儿（336髋），其中82%（138/168）为早产；初次超声显示85%（286/336）髋成熟、9.8%（33/336）未成熟、5.1%（17/336）发育不良。髋状态较差与功能水平较高呈弱相关（r=-0.243，p<0.001），而髋发育不良与早产无显著关联（p=0.080）。该人群新生儿髋发育不良发生率处于普通人群报告范围内。
- 临床启示：产前手术闭合后的脊柱裂新生儿仍应按规范化路径在生后第1周（按矫正胎龄）行Graf髋超声

**原文摘要**：To determine the prevalence of neonatal hip dysplasia in children with spina bifida after prenatal surgical closure and examine its association with functional level and preterm birth. We conducted a retrospective analysis of prospectively collected standardized registry data from a tertiary specialized centre providing comprehensive spina bifida diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up. Standardized hip ultrasound according to the Graf method was performed as part of a protocolized clinical pathway after prenatal surgery, within the first week of life corrected for preterm birth. The primary outcome was the prevalence of hip dysplasia at the initial ultrasound. Associations between hip status, functional level, and gestational age were assessed using Spearman's rank correlation. A total of 212 children (101 males, 111 females) underwent prenatal surgical closure between 1st January 2011 and 30th August 2022; 168 children (336 hips) had available neonatal ultrasound data and were included in the analysis. Preterm birth was present in 138 (82%) of 168 children, including 99 (59%) born late preterm, 35 (21%) born very preterm, and four (2%) born extremely preterm. At the initial ultrasound, 286 (85%) of 336 hips were mature, 33 (9.8%) were immature, and 17 (5.1%) were dysplastic. Worse hip status was weakly associated with a higher functional level (r = -0.243; p < 0.001), whereas no significant association was found between hip dysplasia and preterm birth (p = 0.080). The prevalence of neonatal hip dysplasia in children with spina bifida after prenatal surgical closure was within the range reported for the general population.
