## The relationship between hospital surgical volumes and patient outcomes in Hirschsprung's disease: A Canadian population-based study.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:34
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 加拿大 · University of Calgary
- 通讯作者: Romao R (rodrigo.romao@sickkids.ca)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163572
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163572) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42971504/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：基于加拿大人口数据探讨医院手术量与非计划性巨结肠病（HD）患儿手术结局之间的关系。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入563例在18家医院接受拖出术的HD患儿，医院年手术量中位数为4例，住院时间中位数为5天；医院手术量与住院时间无关（IRR 0.98，95%CI 0.96-1.01，p=0.18）。死亡2例（0.3%），1年内再入院222例（39%），再手术125例（22%）；限制性立方样条分析显示年手术量6例为输血和再入院风险的拐点，高于该阈值输血风险下降但再入院率升高。高容量中心占33%（6/18），其输血率较低但再入院率较高。
- 临床启示：加拿大各医院HD拖出术总体结局较均质，手术量单一指标可能不足以反映医疗质量。临床应关注高容量中心的再入院风险，并优化围手术期输血管理。

**原文摘要**：The association between operative volume and pediatric patient outcomes remains understudied. This retrospective cohort study investigated the relationship between hospital volume and outcomes in infants with Hirschsprung disease (HD) in Canada. Canadian Institute for Health Information data from April 2010 to March 2023. Patients aged ≤18 months who underwent pull-through surgery for HD were included. The association between hospital volume and length of stay (LOS) was evaluated using negative binomial regression. Restricted cubic spline (RCS) analysis assessed nonlinear associations between hospital volume and secondary outcomes (mortality, blood transfusion, readmission, and reoperation), with inflection points used to define high- and low-volume centres. A total of 563 infants with HD underwent pull-through surgery at 18 hospitals. Median age was 3 months (IQR 1-5), median annual hospital volume was 4 cases (IQR 2.5-8), and median LOS was 5 days (IQR 4-9). Hospital volume was not associated with LOS [IRR 0.98 (95% CI 0.96-1.01), p=0.18]. Two patients (0.3%) died, 222 (39%) were readmitted within one year, and 125 (22%) underwent reoperation. RCS analysis identified inflection points at 6 cases/year for blood transfusion, with decreasing risk above this threshold, and for readmission, with increasing rates beyond this threshold. Dichotomization classified 6 (33%) hospitals as high-volume centers CONCLUSIONS: Findings suggest that pediatric surgical outcomes for HD are relatively homogenous across Canadian hospitals. Higher-volume centers exhibited lower rates of blood transfusion but higher rates of readmission. Further research is needed to clarify volume-outcome relationships in pediatric surgery.

## Late rupture of a myelomeningocele sac devoid of neural placode associated with good functional outcome 2 years after postnatal repair: illustrative case.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:34
- 期刊: J Neurosurg Case Lessons · IF 0.7
- 国家/单位: Québec · McGill University
- 通讯作者: Farmer JP
- DOI: 10.3171/CASE26739
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.3171/CASE26739) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42970782/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨脊髓脊膜膨出囊的完整性和破裂时机如何影响其“三打击”病理生理过程，并报道一例囊内无神经基板、晚期破裂且出生后修复预后良好的病例。
- 核心发现/数据：患儿为S1脊髓脊膜膨出，囊直至约35周妊娠仍完整，于36+3周出生后修复；囊内无明确神经基板，可见功能性神经根呈发夹环经背侧缺损进出，出生后影像仅提示轻度Chiari II畸形。修复后2年，患儿下肢运动功能和尿括约肌功能均优，无后脑疝及脑室扩大。
- 临床启示：完整囊的持续静水压可能保护免受早期后脑疝和直接毒性，而囊内无神经基板可减少机械牵拉损伤；晚期破裂则可能减少神经组织的化学毒性暴露，提示脊髓脊膜膨出病理生理具有高度异质性。

**原文摘要**：Neurological deficits in myelomeningocele are driven by a three-hit paradigm: primary myelodysplasia, intrauterine chemotoxic amniotic fluid exposure, and mechanical traction on the neural placode. How sac integrity and rupture timing influence these progressive pathophysiological hits remains poorly understood. A neonate presented with a prominent S1 myelomeningocele sac that remained intact until approximately 35 weeks' gestation. Rather than a distinct neural placode, postnatal repair at 36+3 weeks revealed a hairpin loop of functional nerve roots within the myelomeningocele sac that entered and exited via the dorsal defect. A mild Chiari type II malformation was identified only on postnatal imaging. Following postnatal repair, the child demonstrated not only excellent lower extremity motor function as expected, but also excellent urinary sphincter function with no hindbrain herniation or ventriculomegaly 2 years postrepair. This case illustrates a possible "best of both worlds" clinical scenario in which the persistent hydrostatic pressure of the intact sac protected against early hindbrain herniation and direct toxicity, while the structural absence of a neural placode within the sac reduced mechanical stretch injury. Finally, rupture of the myelomeningocele sac only in late gestation minimized chemical toxicity to neural elements. Overall, this case highlights the multifaceted and varied nature of myelomeningocele pathophysiology. https://thejns.org/doi/abs/10.3171/CASE26739.

## Parental outcomes in congenital lung malformations: a transdisciplinary family-centered model.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:34
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 意大利 · "F. Del Ponte" Hospital
- 通讯作者: Antichi L (lorenzo.antichi@hotmail.it)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06691-1
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06691-1) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42970109/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估以家庭为中心的“Respiriamo, insieme”照护模型对先天性肺畸形（CLM）患儿家庭从产前诊断至术后随访的支持效果。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入41个家庭（干预组26个、标准照护组15个），术后1个月由母亲和父亲分别完成焦虑、创伤后应激及照护质量问卷。干预组母亲焦虑和创伤后应激显著更低、照护质量感知显著更高；父亲呈相同方向但证据较弱。干预组母亲所有照护质量维度均显著更高，父亲仅沟通和总体满意度显著差异；探索性母亲分析提示产前和产后支持均与较低痛苦相关。
- 临床启示：结构化的家庭中心照护模型可降低CLM患儿父母痛苦并提高照护质量感知。将心理社会支持整合入围手术期管理可能增强父母韧性，应作为标准照护。

**原文摘要**：The study aimed to evaluate a family-centered care model, the "Respiriamo, insieme" project, that supports families from prenatal diagnosis through postoperative follow-up. We conducted a posttest-only study comparing families of children with CLM. The intervention group received an integrated pathway combining multidisciplinary counseling, prenatal and postnatal psychological support, music therapy, minimally invasive surgery, locoregional anesthesia, and fast-track perioperative management. The standard care group included families managed at other centers where the integrated pathway was not implemented. One month after surgery, mothers and fathers separately completed validated questionnaires assessing anxiety, post-traumatic stress, and perceived quality of care. Forty-one families participated (26 in the intervention group, 15 in the standard care group). Mothers in the intervention group reported significantly lower anxiety and post-traumatic stress and higher perceived quality of care; fathers showed the same direction with weaker evidence. All quality-of-care domains were significantly higher among intervention mothers; among fathers, only communication and general satisfaction differed significantly. Exploratory maternal analyses suggested lower distress with both prenatal and postnatal support. A structured, family-centered care model reduced parental distress and improved perceived quality of care in CLM. Integrating psychosocial support into perioperative management may strengthen parental resilience and should be standard care.

## Long-term outcomes after thoracoscopic versus open repair of esophageal atresia: The role of surgical expertise.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:34
- 期刊: Semin Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学4区(儿科3区/外科4区) · IF 2.5 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: University of Catania · Department of Advanced Medical and Surgical Sciences
- 通讯作者: Fichera A (afichera2499@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.sempedsurg.2026.151701
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sempedsurg.2026.151701) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42971832/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：综述2020—2025年证据，比较胸腔镜与开放修补食管闭锁的长期结局，并评估外科经验与中心手术量的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：食管闭锁生存率已超过90%；在专科中心开展的注册研究和倾向评分匹配研究显示，两种术式在吻合口漏、狭窄及抗反流手术等长期结局上无显著差异。胸腔镜修补在多项当代研究中与较低的长期肌肉骨骼并发症（如脊柱侧弯和胸壁畸形）相关；低容量中心结局较差，提示外科经验和学习曲线的重要影响。
- 临床启示：应推动食管闭锁诊疗集中化，术式选择应依据机构技术专长和手术量，而非仅凭技术偏好。

**原文摘要**：Survival rates in esophageal atresia (EA) now exceed 90%, shifting attention toward long-term morbidity and quality of life. Thoracoscopic repair has been increasingly adopted, with evidence suggesting outcomes comparable to open surgery, with reduction of musculoskeletal sequelae. However, long-term results appear to be strongly influenced by surgical expertise and institutional volume. To review contemporary evidence comparing thoracoscopic and open repair of EA, focusing on long-term anastomotic, gastroesophageal reflux-related, respiratory, and musculoskeletal outcomes, and to evaluate the impact of surgical expertise and center volume. Comparative clinical studies, multicenter cohorts, national registry analyses, systematic reviews, and meta-analyses published between 2020 and 2025 were reviewed. Primary outcomes included anastomotic complications, respiratory morbidity, musculoskeletal deformities, reinterventions, and health-related quality of life. Recent registry-based and propensity-score matched studies show no significant differences in long-term outcomes between thoracoscopic and open repair when performed in specialized centers. Rates of anastomotic leak and stricture, and anti-reflux surgery were comparable after adjustment for baseline characteristics. Thoracoscopic repair was associated with a lower incidence of long-term musculoskeletal complications in several contemporary studies, including scoliosis and chest wall deformities. Outcomes were less favorable in low-volume centers, underscoring the importance of surgical experience and learning curve. Current data suggest that thoracoscopic and open repair of EA provide similar long-term outcomes in experienced centers. Compared with open repair, thoracoscopy offers an advantage in reducing musculoskeletal morbidity. These findings support centralization of care and emphasize that surgical approach should be guided by institutional expertise rather than technical preference alone.

## Stepwise direct reprogramming of adult human dermal fibroblasts into tendon-like cells via transcription factor optimization and 3D culture.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:34
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 602-8566 · Kyoto Prefectural University of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Konishi K (kai-k@koto.kpu-m.ac.jp)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06712-z
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06712-z) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42970166/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨通过直接重编程促进成人真皮成纤维细胞向肌腱样细胞分化，以构建患者来源的肌肉-肌腱移植物用于小儿外科重建。
- 核心发现/数据：六因子转导可诱导肌腱标志物TNMD和THBS4表达，并实现小鼠短期体内植入；优化后SCX、MKX、OCT3/4和MYCL四因子足以完成肌腱转化，其中OCT3/4促进TNMD、SCX影响THBS4表达。转化细胞呈细长肌腱样形态并强表达TNMD/THBS4，且从二维转至三维培养后TNMD表达进一步升高。
- 临床启示：该简化直接重编程策略有望利用自体皮肤成纤维细胞构建肌肉-肌腱移植物，为先天膈疝等小儿外科再生重建提供新方向。

**原文摘要**：Synthetic materials are widely used in pediatric surgery for congenital diaphragmatic hernia repair when primary closure is not feasible but remain associated with complications and lack growth potential. We investigated whether direct reprogramming could promote tenogenic differentiation of adult human dermal fibroblasts (aHDFs) toward patient-derived muscle-tendon constructs for pediatric reconstruction. aHDFs were transduced into directly converted tenoblasts (dTBs) using retroviral transduction of tendon-related transcription factors and Yamanaka factors. Different factor combinations and culture conditions were evaluated. Tenogenic induction was assessed using real-time RT-PCR and immunostaining for tendon markers. In vivo potential was examined by subcutaneous transplantation into NOG/SCID mice. Six-factor transduction induced the expression of tendon-related markers tenomodulin (TNMD) and thrombospondin-4 (THBS4), and enabled short-term engraftment in vivo. Optimization identified SCX, MKX, OCT3/4, and MYCL as a sufficient combination for tenogenic conversion. OCT3/4 promoted TNMD expression, whereas SCX affected THBS4 expression. dTBs exhibited elongated tendon-like morphology and prominent TNMD and THBS4 staining. TNMD expression increased following transfer from two-dimensional to three-dimensional culture. Direct reprogramming with SCX, MKX, MYCL, and OCT3/4 promoted tenogenic differentiation of aHDFs and induced tendon-like characteristics. This simplified strategy may facilitate development of autologous muscle-tendon constructs for regenerative reconstruction in pediatric surgery.

## Bronchopulmonary Dysplasia Starts in Utero: MicroRNA Dysregulation Across the Fetal-Neonatal Continuum in the Bronchopulmonary Dysplasia Development.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:37
- 期刊: J Cell Mol Med · 中科院医学3区(细胞生物学3区/医学：研究与实验3区) · IF 4.7 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 意大利 · University of Parma
- 通讯作者: Vento G
- DOI: 10.1111/jcmm.71427
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1111/jcmm.71427) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42972992/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：综述微小RNA（miRNA）在支气管肺发育不良（BPD）胎儿至新生儿连续过程中的失调及其作用，探讨BPD的产前起源与产后损伤交互机制。
- 核心发现/数据：本文为教育性综述，未提供原始研究数据；核心观点是miRNA失调贯穿BPD的胎儿-新生儿连续过程，胎盘功能不全、胎儿生长受限及C19MC等胎盘富集miRNA可影响产前易感性，而早产后miRNA网络紊乱参与肺发育异常。miRNA通过调控肺血管生成和VEGF信号、以及与非NRF2依赖性抗氧化反应形成双向关系，连接氧化应激与发育性肺损伤。miRNA在胎盘、脐血、外周血、呼吸道样本和细胞外囊泡中稳定可检测，具有纵向生物标志物潜力，miRNA模拟物和抑制剂亦提供治疗概念验证，但采样、检测方法、发育时点、BPD表型异质性及组织特异性递送和脱靶效应限制临床转化。
- 临床启示：临床研究不宜寻找单一“BPD miRNA”，而应关注整合产前编程与产后暴露的动态、多隔室miRNA网络，以改进BPD分子内型、早期风险分层和个体化预防治疗。

**原文摘要**：Bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD) is a heterogeneous developmental lung disorder resulting from the interaction between antenatal susceptibility and postnatal injury. Increasing evidence suggests that its biological origins may precede preterm birth, with placental dysfunction, fetal growth restriction, inflammation and oxidative stress influencing pulmonary developmental trajectories before exposure to the extrauterine environment. MicroRNAs (miRNAs), small non-coding RNAs that regulate gene expression at the post-transcriptional level, are emerging as potential molecular mediators linking these fetal and neonatal processes. This educational review examines miRNA dysregulation across the fetal-neonatal continuum of BPD. We first discuss the role of miRNAs in physiological lung development and their disruption following premature birth. We then explore how placental dysfunction and fetal growth restriction may influence miRNA networks, including placenta-enriched C19MC miRNAs, thereby contributing to antenatal susceptibility to BPD. Particular attention is given to miRNA-mediated regulation of pulmonary angiogenesis and VEGF signaling and to the bidirectional relationship between miRNAs and oxidative stress, including modulation of NRF2-dependent antioxidant responses. Beyond their mechanistic relevance, miRNAs offer potential translational opportunities. Their stability and detectability across different biological compartments-including placenta, cord blood, peripheral blood, respiratory samples and extracellular vesicles-make them attractive candidates for longitudinal biomarker profiling, while experimental modulation using miRNA mimics and inhibitors provides proof of concept for future therapeutic approaches. However, heterogeneity in biological sampling, analytical methods, developmental timing and BPD phenotypes, together with challenges in tissue-specific delivery and off-target effects, currently limit clinical translation. Rather than identifying a single 'BPD miRNA', future research should focus on dynamic, multi-compartment miRNA networks integrating antenatal programming with postnatal exposures. Such an approach may improve molecular endotyping, early risk stratification and ultimately the development of individualized preventive and therapeutic strategies for BPD.

## Pressure Injuries of Intrauterine Etiology in Neonates: A Scoping Review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:37
- 期刊: J Perinat Neonatal Nurs
- 国家/单位: 意大利 · Author Affiliations: Center of Excellence for Nursing Scholarship (CECRI)
- 通讯作者: Ciprandi G
- DOI: 10.1097/JPN.0000000000000986
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/JPN.0000000000000986) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42972642/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过JBI指导的范围综述，识别并整合新生儿宫内源性压力性损伤在病因、临床表现和伤口管理方面的现有证据。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入5项研究（4篇病例报告和1项病例系列），涉及7名新生儿；多数为剖宫产早产儿，常伴胎膜早破。主要病因为羊水减少或缺失及宫内受压，包括1例与宫内医疗器械相关；按EPUAP/NPIAP分类，病变从1期至深部组织损伤。所有病例均采用保守处理，愈合时间为1周至数月，结局良好。
- 临床启示：临床应提高对宫内源性压力性损伤的警觉并早期识别，避免与先天性皮肤病混淆而延误处理。需进一步建立标准化定义并制定新生儿特异性循证伤口护理方案。

**原文摘要**：Neonatal pressure injuries (PIs) are typically considered postnatal events, however, case evidence shows that some lesions originate in utero and are already present at birth. Their recognition is clinically relevant because they may mimic other congenital dermatological conditions and require specific neonatal wound care strategies. Misclassification can delay appropriate care and distort quality metrics. A clear framework distinguishing prenatal from postnatal lesions is needed. A JBI-guided scoping review was designed in order to identify and synthesize the available evidence on pressure injuries of intrauterine origin, focusing on etiology, clinical presentation, and wound care management. A three-step search strategy was adopted and the PRISMA 2020 flow diagram was used to document the study selection process. Five studies met the inclusion criteria (4 case reports and one case series), describing 7 neonates. Most cases involved preterm infants delivered by cesarean section, frequently associated with premature rupture of membranes. The main etiological factors were reduced or absent amniotic fluid and intrauterine compression, including one case related to intrauterine medical devices. According to the EPUAP/NPIAP classification, lesions ranged from Stage 1 to deep tissue injury. All cases were managed conservatively, with healing times ranging from 1 week to several months and favorable outcomes. Increased clinical awareness and early recognition of intrauterine PIs are essential. Further research is required to establish standardized definitions, improve diagnostic accuracy, and develop neonatal-specific, evidence-based wound care protocols.
