## The association of intrapartum oral labetalol for preeclampsia with severe features and risk of cesarean delivery: a retrospective cohort study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:44
- 期刊: J Matern Fetal Neonatal Med
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Mountain Area Health Education Center
- 通讯作者: de Los Reyes S
- DOI: 10.1080/14767058.2026.2758988
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1080/14767058.2026.2758988) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43009284/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估子痫前期伴严重特征患者产时使用口服拉贝洛尔以实现持续口服降压控制，是否较不加用口服降压药降低剖宫产风险。
- 核心发现/数据：回顾性队列共纳入642例≥34周、单胎、无畸形且尝试阴道分娩的子痫前期伴严重特征患者，其中134例（20.9%）产时启用口服拉贝洛尔，508例（79.1%）未启用口服降压药。启用拉贝洛尔者分娩孕周显著更低（35.0周 vs 37.7周），但产时口服拉贝洛尔使用与剖宫产率无显著差异。单因素分析提示复合不良新生儿结局 odds 更高及剖宫产指征不同，但很可能由指征混杂导致。
- 临床启示：产时口服拉贝洛尔用于子痫前期伴严重特征的血压控制不应被视为降低剖宫产风险的干预措施。临床决策仍需依据母胎指征，并注意用药患者本身孕周更低及指征混杂的影响。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate if the use of intrapartum oral labetalol vs. no antihypertensive medication additions for severe preeclampsia (PreE with SF) for sustained oral blood pressure control decreases the risk of cesarean. This was a retrospective cohort study of patients who underwent a trial of labor of a non-anomalous, singleton gestation at ≥ 34 weeks gestational age with preeclampsia with severe features. The exposure was intrapartum oral labetalol compared to no oral antihypertensive medication. The use of intrapartum labetalol was defined as the initiation of labetalol in labor for preeclampsia with severe features. The primary outcome was the need for cesarean. Univariable analyses were performed to evaluate the association of oral labetalol therapy and need for cesarean. Variables that had an independent association with the outcome were determined a priori and included in the multivariable regression analysis. Statistical significance was defined as  642 patients met the inclusion criteria. A total of 134 patients (20.9%) received intrapartum oral labetalol, whereas 508 (79.1%) were not started on antihypertensive medications. Patients who were initiated on oral labetalol in labor for blood pressure control were significantly more likely to have a lower gestational age at delivery (35.0 vs 37.7 weeks gestation,  Intrapartum oral labetalol use was not associated with a significant difference in cesarean rates. Univariable analysis suggests a higher odds of composite adverse neonatal outcomes as well as differing indications for cesarean sections, however, this is likely secondary to confounding by indication.

## Transcriptional dynamics of glutamate excitotoxicity in the mammalian cochlea.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:44
- 期刊: Brain Res · 中科院医学4区(神经科学4区) · IF 3.2 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 美国 · USA; Feinberg School of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Jung DH (David_Jung@meei.harvard.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.brainres.2026.150634
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brainres.2026.150634) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43008911/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：整合新生儿耳蜗外植体、成年噪声暴露模型及单细胞/空间转录组，解析哺乳动物耳蜗谷氨酸兴奋性毒性后AMPA受体亚基与神经营养因子受体信号的转录动态。
- 核心发现/数据：在新生儿CD1小鼠耳蜗外植体中，兴奋性毒性损伤后给予NT-3可激活修复许可性转录程序，主要在一类表达Type 1C样标志物的螺旋神经节神经元中上调Gria2、Gria3、Gria4及Ntrk3；而成年CBA/CaJ小鼠噪声暴露后，主要在Type 1A神经元亚型中协调性下调Gria2、Gria4和Ntrk3。空间RNA原位杂交显示该下调具有频率拓扑限制性，在高频区（突触变性最重区域）选择性丢失Gria2和Ntrk3。
- 临床启示：兴奋性毒性损伤后的分子反应具有年龄和情境依赖性；成人耳蜗突触再生可能可通过重新激活新生儿样转录程序、提高神经营养因子受体可用性来促进噪声性突触损伤修复。

**原文摘要**：Hearing loss affects approximately 1.3 billion people worldwide and is frequently accompanied by tinnitus, hyperacusis, and difficulty hearing in noise. Some of these symptoms may be linked to cochlear synaptopathy, the loss of synaptic connections between inner hair cells and spiral ganglion neurons. Glutamate excitotoxicity is a key driver of synaptic injury, and the molecular programs governing cochlear synapse vulnerability and repair are under active investigation. Here, we integrated neonatal cochlear explant models, adult noise-exposure models, and single-cell and spatially resolved transcriptomics to identify convergent and divergent patterns in transcriptional responses of AMPA-type glutamate receptor subunits and neurotrophin receptor signaling in acute response to excitotoxic injury across multiple contexts. In neonatal CD1 mouse cochlear explants, excitotoxic injury and subsequent neurotrophin-3 exposure activated a repair-permissive transcriptional program characterized by increased expression of AMPA receptor subunits (Gria2, Gria3, Gria4) and the neurotrophin receptor Ntrk3, predominantly within a spiral ganglion neuron population expressing Type 1C-like markers. In contrast, adult CBA/CaJ mice exposed to noise induced a coordinated downregulation of AMPA receptor subunits (Gria2, Gria4) and Ntrk3, primarily in Type 1A neuronal subtypes. Spatial RNA in situ hybridization further revealed that this downregulation is tonotopically restricted, with selective loss of Gria2 and Ntrk3 in high-frequency regions that sustain the greatest synaptic degeneration. Together, these findings reveal opposing, context-dependent molecular responses to excitotoxic injury, and may suggest that synaptic regeneration in the adult cochlea could be enhanced by reactivation of a neonatal-like transcriptional program to increase neurotrophin receptor availability for synapse recovery after noise-induced damage.

## Development and Validation of a Neonatal Mouse Brain Stereotaxic Injection Platform for Maintaining Perioperative Physiological Homeostasis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:44
- 期刊: J Neurosci Methods · 中科院医学4区(生化研究方法3区/神经科学4区) · IF 2.5 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 中国 · The First Affiliated Hospital of Shantou University
- 通讯作者: Li F (lifan@stu.edu.cn)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jneumeth.2026.110951
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jneumeth.2026.110951) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43008898/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：开发并验证一种整合数字控温、非侵入头固定、吸入麻醉和闭环热调节的新生小鼠脑立体定向注射平台，以维持围术期生理稳态。
- 核心发现/数据：摘要未提供具体统计学数值，但报告该平台可稳定维持围术期体温、显著提高术后存活率，并可靠支持脑损伤后二次脑内干预、超早期基因敲除及小胶质细胞病毒示踪。与依赖低温麻醉、室温手术或外部加温的现有方法相比，该平台将立体定向注射与主动闭环控温整合，简化操作并实现精确热管理。
- 临床启示：该平台通过将生理支持与立体定向手术整合，为新生儿神经科学研究和疾病建模提供标准化、可靠且可重复的脑内注射方法。其围术期主动控温理念对脆弱新生儿手术和干预中维持生理稳态具有借鉴意义。

**原文摘要**：Neonatal mouse brain stereotaxic injection is an essential experimental technique for investigating neurodevelopmental regulation and neonatal neurological disorders. However, immature thermoregulation and limited anesthetic tolerance make neonatal mice highly susceptible to uncontrolled perioperative hypothermia and physiological instability, which compromise postoperative survival, increase experimental variability, and may confound disease modeling and the interpretation of intervention outcomes. We developed an integrated digital temperature-controlled stereotaxic platform that combines non-invasive head fixation, inhalation anesthesia, and real-time closed-loop thermal regulation. The platform provides continuous perioperative thermal support to maintain physiological homeostasis and is compatible with a wide range of neonatal intracerebral injection applications. The platform maintained stable perioperative body temperature, significantly improved postoperative survival, and reliably supported secondary intracerebral intervention after brain injury, ultra-early gene knockout, and microglial viral tracing in neonatal mice. Existing neonatal stereotaxic procedures rely on hypothermia-induced anesthesia, room-temperature surgery, or external warming methods such as warm-water systems and heating pads, which may provide insufficiently stable temperature control and require additional operational steps. In contrast, the present platform integrates stereotaxic injection and active temperature regulation, simplifying surgical procedures while enabling precise thermal management and maintaining intraoperative body temperature in neonatal mice. By integrating physiological support with stereotaxic surgery, this platform provides a standardized, reliable, and reproducible approach for neonatal mouse intracerebral injection and facilitates advanced experimental manipulation in neonatal neuroscience research and disease modeling.

## Comparison of Pregnancy Outcomes from Fresh Transfer of High-Quality Single Blastocysts in Women of Different Ages: A Retrospective Study.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：比较不同母亲年龄组行新鲜高质量单囊胚移植后的妊娠结局和新生儿结局。
- 核心发现/数据：该回顾性队列纳入4352例，<30岁组活产率56.88%，显著高于35-37岁组的43.79%。30-34岁组的妊娠结局与<30岁组相当，而35-37岁组活产率显著降低、流产率显著升高。

**原文摘要**：To compare pregnancy and neonatal outcomes following fresh high-quality single blastocyst transfer (SBT) across different maternal age groups. This retrospective cohort study included 4352 women who underwent fresh high-quality SBT and were divided into three age groups: <30 years (Group A, n = 1939), 30-34 years (Group B, n = 1801), and 35-37 years (Group C, n = 612). Baseline characteristics, pregnancy outcomes, and neonatal outcomes were compared across the groups. Non-live birth was the prespecified primary outcome. For secondary and exploratory outcomes,  Women in Group A had a significantly higher live birth rate than those in Group C (56.88% vs 43.79%;  Among women undergoing fresh high-quality SBT, those aged 30-34 years achieved pregnancy outcomes comparable to those of women aged <30 years, whereas women aged 35-37 years had significantly lower live birth rates and higher miscarriage rates. These findings reflect age-related differences within the SBT population rather than a comparison between transfer strategies. Large-scale prospective multicenter randomized trials are warranted.

## Atypical Bilateral Congenital Ectropion in a 3-Day-Old Female: A Case Report.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 19:11
- 期刊: Case Rep Ophthalmol Med · 中科院医学4区(眼科学4区) · IF 0.8
- 国家/单位: Department of Surgery Ophthalmology Unit Tamale Teaching Hospital Tamale Ghana tamaleteachinghospital.org · Department of Surgery Ophthalmology Unit Tamale Teaching Hospital Tamale Ghana tamaleteachinghospital.org
- 通讯作者: Abdul-Samed FG
- DOI: 10.1155/crop/7578015
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1155/crop/7578015) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43011456/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告1例3日龄女婴双侧先天性上睑睑外翻，并强调在低资源环境下保守治疗可有效管理该罕见新生儿眼睑异常。
- 核心发现/数据：患儿为3日龄女性，出生即表现为双侧上眼睑先天性睑外翻，无相关先天畸形或全身并发症。治疗采用生理盐水清洁、眼部润滑、复位加压包扎及家长教育等完全保守措施，数天内逐渐改善，保守治疗第9天双侧睑外翻完全消退。完全消退后1周复查眼睑及睫毛位置正常、双侧眼表清晰、双眼对光有眨眼反应，后续随访无并发症。
- 临床启示：双侧先天性睑外翻可通过早期识别和及时保守治疗获得良好结局，尤其适用于手术干预不易获得的低资源环境。临床应重视眼表保护并预防暴露性角膜病变。

**原文摘要**：Bilateral congenital ectropion is an extremely rare neonatal eyelid anomaly. It is usually characterized by outward turning or eversion of the eyelid margins at birth, putting the affected neonates at a huge risk of exposure keratopathy. The global incidence remains unknown, with most literature limited to isolated case reports. In Ghana, published data are almost nonexistent on this condition. We report a 3-day-old female neonate who presented with bilateral congenital ectropion involving the upper eyelids. There were no associated congenital anomalies or systemic complications. Management consisted entirely of conservative treatment, including saline cleansing, ocular lubrication, reduction and pressure dressing, and parental education. Progressive improvement was noted within days, with complete resolution of the bilateral ectropion by Day 9 of conservative treatment. At review, 1 week following complete clinical resolution, the infant looked stable with normal lid and eyelash positions and a clear ocular surface bilaterally. She blinks to light in both eyes, and the mother reported no visual concerns, and there were no complications on subsequent follow-up visits. The aim of this study is to highlight that bilateral congenital ectropion can be effectively managed using conservative measures, particularly in low-resource settings where surgical intervention may not be readily accessible. Early recognition and timely conservative management are essential to achieving optimal outcomes.

## Real-world paediatric inflammatory bowel disease surgery: International variation in care and practice.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 19:11
- 期刊: J Pediatr Gastroenterol Nutr · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/胃肠肝病学4区/营养学4区) · IF 3.1 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: Czech Republic · Charles University and Motol and Homolka University Hospital
- 通讯作者: Bronsky J
- DOI: 10.1002/jpn3.70655
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/jpn3.70655) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43010702/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：描述当代国际儿童炎症性肠病（IBD）手术实践，评估护理组织、围术期管理和手术策略的国际差异。
- 核心发现/数据：26/36家中心（14国，72%）参与，96%为大学/教学医院，85%定期开展多学科团队会议；使用加速康复外科（ERAS）、结构化营养筛查、尽可能延迟择期手术以优化营养的中心分别占50%、54%、42%。药物停用差异显著（糖皮质激素69%、抗TNF 31%、新型生物/小分子15-19%），42%报告国家集中化被推荐或强制；克罗恩病肠腔手术中位年手术量2018年为2例、2022和2024年为2.5例，儿童溃疡性结肠炎IPAA中位年手术量为1例。围术期优化、药物管理、内外科治疗整合、集中化和手术策略均存在显著差异。
- 临床启示：该研究为儿童IBD手术提供了国际基准，提示需通过共识、标准化围

**原文摘要**：Paediatric inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) surgery is delivered across heterogeneous settings, with limited paediatric-specific evidence guiding organisation of care, perioperative management, and operative strategy. We characterised contemporary international practice. An international cross-sectional centre-level survey was conducted within the Paediatric IBD Surgery Network. A 28-item questionnaire addressing organisation of care, perioperative management, procedural volumes, and surgical practice was distributed electronically. Analyses were descriptive and performed per question. Twenty-six of 36 invited centres from 14 countries responded (72%). University or teaching hospitals represented 96% of centres, and paediatric surgeons accounted for 69% of respondents. Regular multidisciplinary team meetings were reported by 85%. Use of an enhanced recovery after surgery protocol, structured nutritional screening, and deferral of elective surgery for nutritional optimisation whenever feasible were reported by 50%, 54%, and 42% of centres, respectively. Medication withholding varied, from corticosteroids in 69% of centres to anti-tumour necrosis factor (anti-TNF) agents in 31% and individual newer biologic or small-molecule classes in 15-19%. Formal national centralisation was reported as recommended or mandatory by 42%. Median annual luminal Crohn's disease surgery volume per centre was two procedures (interquartile range [IQR] 1-5) in 2018, 2.5 (IQR 2-5) in 2022, and 2.5 (IQR 1-5) in 2024. Median annual ileal pouch-anal anastomosis volume in children with ulcerative colitis was 1 (IQR 0.1-3.5). Considerable variation was observed in perioperative optimisation and medication management, integration of medical and surgical therapy, centralisation, and operative strategy. These findings provide a baseline for international benchmarking, consensus development, and prospective collaborative research.

## Type IB Choledochal Cyst With a Giant Intracystic Choledocholithiasis: A Rare Presentation of a Rare Biliary Disorder.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 19:11
- 期刊: ACG Case Rep J · 中科院医学4区(胃肠肝病学4区) · IF 0.6
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Baylor Scott and White Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Das K
- DOI: 10.14309/crj.0000000000002369
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.14309/crj.0000000000002369) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43012058/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告一例罕见的IB型胆总管囊肿合并巨大囊内胆总管结石并梗阻性黄疸的成人病例及其诊疗经过。
- 核心发现/数据：患者为38岁女性，IB型胆总管囊肿内见直径5.46 cm的巨大结石，影像学证实远端胆总管囊状扩张并伴肝内、外胆管扩张。ERCP置入胆道支架实现减压，但并发ERCP后胰腺炎；最终行肝外胆道切除联合Roux-en-Y肝管空肠吻合术获得治愈。此为美国农村地区西班牙裔成人中首例报道的IB型胆总管囊肿伴巨大囊内结石。
- 临床启示：胆总管囊肿合并巨大囊内结石虽罕见，但可引起梗阻性黄疸，需结合影像和内镜处理；ERCP可临时减压但需警惕术后胰腺炎，确定性切除并Roux-en-Y肝管空肠吻合可获得治愈。

**原文摘要**：Choledochal cysts are biliary dilatations with malignant potential. Intracystic choledocholithiasis is a rare complication. This report describes a 38-year-old woman with a Type IB choledochal cyst containing a 5.46 cm intracystic calculus complicated by obstructive jaundice. Imaging confirmed a saccular cyst of the distal common bile duct with associated intrahepatic and extrahepatic biliary dilatation. Endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography with biliary stent placement achieved decompression, complicated by postendoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography pancreatitis. Extrahepatic biliary resection via a Roux-en-Y hepaticojejunostomy was curative. This represents the first reported Type IB choledochal cyst with a giant intracystic calculus in a Hispanic adult in the rural United States.
