## Cardiomyocyte-Specific LncRNA CSTPP Prolongs the Proliferation Window by Promoting CDKN1A Decay in Postinfarction Cardiac Regeneration.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:13
- 期刊: Arterioscler Thromb Vasc Biol · 中科院医学1区(血液学2区/外周血管病2区) · IF 8.8 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: Y.-y.C.) · Shenzhen University
- 通讯作者: Liu J
- DOI: 10.1161/ATVBAHA.126.325434
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1161/ATVBAHA.126.325434) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42877976/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探究心肌细胞特异性lncRNA CSTPP在心肌梗死后心肌细胞周期再停滞及心脏再生中的作用与机制。
- 核心发现/数据：鉴定出心肌损伤后下调的心肌细胞特异性lncRNA CSTPP；过表达CSTPP可促进新生大鼠心室肌细胞增殖并改善MI后心功能，而敲除CSTPP则减弱增殖、加重心功能障碍。机制上，CSTPP通过结合YBX1并增强YBX1-UPF1互作，促进UPF1募集至CDKN1A mRNA并加速其降解，从而延长心肌细胞周期激活窗口；人MI心脏样本中CSTPP下调，hiPSC-CM中过表达CSTPP可降低CDKN1A并促进增殖。
- 临床启示：CSTPP有望成为MI后促进心脏再生、延长心肌细胞增殖窗口的潜在治疗靶点，为改善梗死后心功能提供新策略。

**原文摘要**：Adult mammalian cardiomyocytes exhibit some capacity to reenter the cell cycle after ischemic injury. However, activated cardiomyocytes rapidly undergo cell cycle rearrest shortly after activation. Long noncoding RNAs (lncRNAs) have emerged as critical regulators of cardiac regeneration; however, their involvement in the process of cell cycle rearrest remains unexplored. Bulk RNA-sequencing was used to screen for lncRNAs differentially expressed during myocardial injury. Loss- and gain-of-function approaches were used to assess the role of lncRNA in cardiomyocyte proliferation and cardiac regeneration after myocardial infarction (MI). RNA pulldown and RNA immunoprecipitation were used to elucidate the underlying regulatory mechanisms. We identified a cardiomyocyte-specific lncRNA downregulated after myocardial injury and named it cardiomyocyte-specific transcript promoting proliferation (CSTPP). CSTPP overexpression enhanced neonatal rat ventricular myocyte proliferation, whereas its knockdown exerted the opposite effects. In vivo, CSTPP deletion attenuated cardiomyocyte proliferation, leading to exacerbated cardiac dysfunction post-MI. Conversely, CSTPP overexpression promoted cardiomyocyte proliferation and improved cardiac function. Furthermore, CDKN1A (cyclin-dependent kinase inhibitor 1A) was found to be upregulated in cardiomyocytes localized at the border zone after MI, which was aggravated by CSTPP knockout and abrogated by CSTPP overexpression. Mechanistically, we showed that CSTPP interacts with YBX1 (Y box-binding protein 1), reinforcing the interaction between YBX1 and UPF1 (upstream frameshift 1), thereby enhancing UPF1 recruitment to CDKN1A mRNA and accelerating its decay. Notably, CSTPP was downregulated in heart samples from patients with MI. CSTPP overexpression decreased CDKN1A expression and facilitated proliferation in human induced pluripotent stem cell-derived cardiomyocytes. Our findings identified CSTPP as a cardiomyocyte-specific lncRNA that functions to extend the activation window of the cardiomyocyte cell cycle after MI by stabilizing the YBX1-UPF1 interaction and thus facilitating the UPF1-mediated decay of CDKN1A mRNA. These results establish CSTPP as a promising therapeutic target for cardiac regeneration after MI.

## Persistent pneumothorax during pregnancy revealing histologically confirmed thoracic endometriosis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:13
- 期刊: J Surg Case Rep · 中科院医学4区(外科4区) · IF 0.5
- 国家/单位: 韩国 · Gangnam Severance Hospital
- 通讯作者: Lee S
- DOI: 10.1093/jscr/rjag922
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1093/jscr/rjag922) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42877470/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过一例妊娠期持续性气胸病例，探讨妊娠期胸膜子宫内膜异位症综合征（TES）的诊断与处理。
- 核心发现/数据：31岁初产妇孕27周出现右侧持续性气胸，胸管引流后仍持续，既往有复发性自发性气胸和盆腔子宫内膜异位症；VATS行右中叶楔形切除及部分膈肌切除修补，病理示子宫内膜腺体和间质累及肺实质、肺大疱和膈肌，ER及CD10阳性，确诊TES。术后恢复顺利，孕40周分娩3.2 kg健康女婴，母婴无并发症。
- 临床启示：妊娠不能排除TES，对反复或持续性右侧气胸的孕妇应保持警惕并尽早多学科手术干预，以明确诊断、治疗并优化母婴结局。

**原文摘要**：Thoracic endometriosis syndrome (TES) is the leading cause of catamenial pneumothorax but is rarely considered during pregnancy because ovulation and menstruation are physiologically suppressed. Consequently, persistent pneumothorax in pregnancy is commonly attributed to primary spontaneous pneumothorax, potentially delaying definitive diagnosis and treatment. We report a 31-year-old primigravida at 27 weeks' gestation who presented with persistent right-sided pneumothorax despite tube thoracostomy. She had a history of recurrent spontaneous pneumothorax and pelvic endometriosis. Following multidisciplinary evaluation, the patient underwent video-assisted thoracoscopic surgery with wedge resection of the right middle lobe and partial diaphragmatic resection with primary repair. Histopathological examination demonstrated endometrial glands and stroma involving the pulmonary parenchyma, pulmonary bulla, and diaphragm, with positive estrogen receptor and CD10 immunostaining, confirming TES. The postoperative course was uneventful, and the patient subsequently delivered a healthy 3.2-kg female infant at 40 weeks' gestation without maternal or neonatal complications. This case demonstrates that pregnancy does not exclude TES and highlights the importance of maintaining clinical suspicion in pregnant women with recurrent or persistent right-sided pneumothorax. Early multidisciplinary surgical management can safely achieve definitive diagnosis and treatment while optimizing both maternal and fetal outcomes.

## Severe constipation-associated postprandial abdominal pain in a pregnant native-liver biliary atresia survivor after Kasai portoenterostomy: A case report.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:13
- 期刊: JPGN Rep
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Department of Internal Medicine Western Michigan University Homer Stryker MD School of Medicine Kalamazoo Michigan USA
- 通讯作者: Vikash F
- DOI: 10.1002/jpr3.70253
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/jpr3.70253) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42877649/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过病例报告描述一例妊娠期自体肝胆道闭锁Kasai术后幸存者出现的严重便秘相关餐后腹痛，拓展该人群围产期表型。
- 核心发现/数据：患者18岁，先天性胆道闭锁Kasai术后并慢性肝病，孕32周因6周进行性餐后上腹/右上腹痛、恶心和进食差就诊；肝功能合成功能保留，无临床显著门脉高压，内镜、CT、CTA和胃排空检查分别排除静脉曲张、胰腺炎/胆道梗阻/急腹症、肠系膜缺血和胃轻瘫。因胎监异常于约33周4天提前引产，产后症状持续，影像复核发现严重便秘伴直肠嵌塞/粪便负荷是主要可治疗因素。
- 临床启示：对自体肝胆道闭

**原文摘要**：An 18-year-old woman with congenital biliary atresia status post Kasai portoenterostomy and magnetic resonance imaging-reported chronic liver disease presented at 32 weeks' gestation with 6 weeks of worsening postprandial epigastric/right upper quadrant pain, nausea, and poor oral intake. Evaluation showed preserved hepatic synthetic function and no demonstrated clinically significant portal hypertension. Endoscopy excluded esophageal and gastric varices, computed tomography showed no imaging evidence of pancreatitis, biliary obstruction, or other acute intra-abdominal pathology, computed tomography angiography excluded mesenteric ischemia, and gastric emptying scintigraphy excluded gastroparesis. Nonreassuring fetal surveillance prompted preterm induction at approximately 33 weeks 4 days. Postpartum symptoms persisted, and imaging review identified severe constipation with rectal impaction/fecalization as the dominant treatable contributor. This case broadens the peripartum phenotype reported in native-liver biliary atresia survivors by emphasizing constipation-associated postprandial abdominal pain, poor oral intake, nutritional risk, and medication-related bowel dysfunction rather than variceal bleeding or hepatic decompensation alone.
