## STEMI at Term Managed With IVUS-Guided Percutaneous Coronary Intervention and Cesarean Delivery.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:41
- 期刊: JACC Case Rep
- 国家/单位: Georgia · Piedmont Athens Regional Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Darko P (princedkwame1@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jaccas.2026.110661
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jaccas.2026.110661) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42867418/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告一例38周妊娠合并前壁STEMI患者采用IVUS引导PCI并随后剖宫产的多学科管理经验，探讨其可行性与决策要点。
- 核心发现/数据：妊娠合并急性心肌梗死母体病死率约4.5%；本例32岁、38周、无心血管危险因素，因左前降支完全闭塞发生前壁STEMI。急诊经桡动脉无镇静PCI，IVUS确认导丝穿过血栓性闭塞，重叠药物洗脱支架恢复TIMI 3级血流，胎儿辐射剂量低于阈值；持续双联抗血小板治疗下剖宫产，止血剂控制出血，母婴健康出院。
- 临床启示：足月妊娠STEMI可在多学科协作、辐射防护和IVUS辅助下实施急诊PCI，尤其当造影难以鉴别血栓闭塞与自发性夹层时。PCI后需个体化制定抗血小板策略，在剖宫产等手术中平衡血栓与出血风险。

**原文摘要**：Acute myocardial infarction complicating pregnancy is rare, with a maternal case-fatality rate of 4.5%. ST-elevation myocardial infarction in a near-term gravida requires revascularization, fetal radiation protection, and multidisciplinary coordination. A 32-year-old woman at 38 weeks of gestation with no cardiovascular risk factors presented with anterior STEMI from total left anterior descending artery occlusion. Emergent radial-access percutaneous coronary intervention was performed without sedation; intravascular ultrasound confirmed wire position through a thrombus-laden occlusion. Overlapping drug-eluting stents restored TIMI flow grade-3, with air kerma below fetal thresholds. Uninterrupted dual antiplatelet therapy continued through cesarean delivery, with blood loss controlled by hemostatic agents. Mother and neonate were discharged in good health. This case highlights the ambiguity between thrombotic occlusion and spontaneous dissection in pregnancy, the role of intravascular ultrasound when angiography is unreliable, and antiplatelet decision-making when surgery follows percutaneous coronary intervention. Emergent intravascular ultrasound-guided percutaneous coronary intervention is feasible at 38 weeks of gestation. Multidisciplinary planning, radiation mitigation, and individualized antiplatelet strategy determine outcomes.

## Robotic Parenchyma-Preserving Pancreatic Resection for Focal Congenital Hyperinsulinism in an Infant.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:41
- 期刊: CRSLS
- 国家/单位: 印度 · Manipal Hospital Whitefield
- 通讯作者: Nadiminti S
- DOI: 10.4293/CRSLS.2026.00102
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.4293/CRSLS.2026.00102) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42866314/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨机器人辅助保留胰腺实质的胰腺切除术在小于5 kg的局灶性先天性高胰岛素血症婴儿中的可行性与安全性。
- 核心发现/数据：患儿体重4.6 kg，采用达芬奇Xi机器人系统3臂、吲哚菁绿荧光、术中超声及缝线牵拉技术完成手术；术中冰冻证实局灶病变完整切除，最终病理为局灶性腺瘤样增生。切除后血糖短暂升至峰值23.1 mmol/L，随后停用静脉葡萄糖，术后第9天耐受全口喂养出院，8个月随访血糖正常且无需药物治疗、无低血糖发作。
- 临床启示：该病例提示机器人辅助保留胰腺实质切除术在4.6 kg婴儿中可行，但需精细手术规划并联合ICG荧光和术中超声等辅助手段。减少额外端口并使用缝线牵拉技术，有助于在高风险新生儿/婴儿中安全实施机器人手术。

**原文摘要**：Robot-assisted surgery has been increasingly adopted over traditional laparoscopic and open procedures. However, its application in neonates and infants remains limited. Restricted space, delicate tissues, and the need for special instruments complicate its use. This case report describes the application of robot-assisted surgery in an infant with focal congenital hyperinsulinism. We describe the management of focal congenital hyperinsulinism in an infant using robotic surgery with indocyanine green injection, intraoperative ultrasound, and a suture-based retraction technique. Herein, we present a case of an infant weighing less than 5 kg who underwent robotic surgery. The procedure was performed using the 3 arms of the da Vinci Xi robotic system and a suture-based intraoperative retraction technique. Intraoperative frozen sections confirmed the complete excision of the focal disease. Final histopathology revealed focal adenomatous hyperplasia. Intraoperatively, plasma glucose levels transiently increased after resection peaking at 23.1 mmol/L. Intravenous dextrose was discontinued. The postoperative course was uneventful, and the infant was discharged on postoperative day nine after tolerating full oral feeds. At follow-up in 8 months, the child remained euglycemic without any medical therapy or hypoglycemic episodes. This case demonstrates the feasibility of robotic-assisted parenchyma-preserving pancreatic resection in a 4.6-kg infant. Careful attention to operative planning and the use of adjuncts, such as indocyanine green fluorescence and intraoperative ultrasonography, is critical. Minimizing the number of additional ports and using suture-based retraction techniques can help achieve successful robotic surgery in this high-risk population.

## Extensive Aplasia Cutis Congenita with Calvarial Defect and Superior Sagittal Sinus Exposure in a Term Neonate: A Case Report of Successful Healing with Conservative Management.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:41
- 期刊: Int Med Case Rep J · 中科院医学4区(医学：内科4区) · IF 0.7
- 国家/单位: Somalia · Mogadishu Somali Turkey Recep Tayyip Erdogan Training and Research Hospital
- 通讯作者: Ali BO
- DOI: 10.2147/IMCRJ.S649235
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.2147/IMCRJ.S649235) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42866163/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告1例足月新生儿广泛性先天性皮肤发育不全伴颅盖缺损及上矢状窦暴露，经保守治疗成功愈合的病例，并探讨其个体化治疗策略。
- 核心发现/数据：患儿为足月男婴，中线额顶头皮缺损约4×4 cm，累及皮肤及颅盖并暴露上矢状窦，CT示大范围中线额顶颅盖缺损而无主要颅内异常。予静脉氨苄西林（50 mg/kg/次，每12小时1次，第一周）、局部呋喃西林和利福平及每日2次无菌生理盐水湿纱布换药，临床稳定，未见局部感染或全身脓毒症。持续8个月后创面近完全上皮化，形成成熟瘢痕、残余痂皮及瘢痕性脱发。
- 临床启示：对临床稳定的广泛ACC伴颅盖缺损及上矢状窦暴露患儿，保守换药可实现渐进性闭合，但不能据此认为保守治疗优于手术；应个体化评估出血、感染、组织缺损深度、硬脑膜/静脉窦暴露、神经状态及重建风险，并密切多学科监测、随时准备手术覆盖。

**原文摘要**：Aplasia cutis congenita (ACC) is a rare congenital disorder characterized by localized absence of skin, most commonly affecting the scalp. Extensive lesions involving the calvarium and dura, particularly when the superior sagittal sinus is exposed, are uncommon and may be complicated by hemorrhage, infection, thrombosis, neurological injury, and mortality. Management may be conservative or surgical and must be individualized according to defect size and depth, the structures exposed, the clinical condition of the neonate, and the resources and expertise available. We report a term male neonate with an approximately 4 × 4 cm midline frontoparietal scalp defect involving the skin and calvarium, with exposure of the superior sagittal sinus and underlying tissue. Brain computed tomography demonstrated a large congenital midline frontoparietal calvarial defect without a major intracranial abnormality. The neonate was clinically stable and was managed conservatively with intravenous ampicillin (50 mg/kg/dose every 12 hours during the first week), topical nitrofurazone and rifampicin, and twice-daily sterile saline-moistened gauze dressings. The available record did not document the concentration/formulation of the topical agents, specific nutritional supplementation, or a standardized parental home-care training protocol. Serial wound assessments demonstrated progressive granulation and epithelialization without documented local wound infection or systemic sepsis. After eight months of continued wound care, the lesion showed near-complete epithelialization with mature scar formation, residual crusting, and cicatricial alopecia. This case demonstrates that conservative wound care can result in progressive closure in a clinically stable neonate with extensive ACC and calvarial defect with superior sagittal sinus exposure. The result should not be interpreted as evidence that conservative management is preferable to surgery in general. Instead, treatment should be individualized after assessment of bleeding, infection, depth of tissue loss, dural and venous-sinus exposure, neurological status, and the feasibility and risks of reconstruction. Close multidisciplinary monitoring and readiness to escalate to surgical coverage remain essential.

## Social determinants of necrotizing enterocolitis-associated mortality in very low birth weight infants: a population based study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:41
- 期刊: J Perinat Med · 中科院医学4区(妇产科学4区/儿科4区) · IF 1.3 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 美国 · College of Nursing
- 通讯作者: Cousin L
- DOI: 10.1515/jpm-2026-0473
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1515/jpm-2026-0473) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42867162/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估母亲健康社会决定因素与极低出生体重儿坏死性小肠结肠炎相关死亡之间的关联。
- 核心发现/数据：2017至2023年CDC WONDER链接出生与死亡记录中，共纳入271,411例极低出生体重儿，其中1,725例发生NEC相关死亡，死亡率为6.36/1000活产极低出生体重儿。年轻母亲、未婚、Medicaid保险、居住美国南部及美洲印第安/阿拉斯加原住民母亲所生婴儿的NEC相关死亡风险升高；母亲年龄30-34岁或本科及以上学历者风险降低。
- 临床启示：NEC相关死亡存在明显母亲社会经济差异，临床与围产期管理应识别高危社会因素并加强公平资源配置和支持，以改善极低出生体重儿结局。

**原文摘要**：Necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) remains a leading cause of morbidity and mortality among very low birth weight (VLBW) infants. This study examined associations between maternal social determinants of health (SDoH) and NEC-related infant mortality among VLBW infants. This retrospective cross-sectional study used Centers for Disease Control and Prevention Wide-ranging Online Data for Epidemiological Research (CDC WONDER) linked birth and death records from 2017 to 2023. Associations between maternal SDoH and NEC-related mortality were assessed using logistic regression. Among 271,411 VLBW births, 1,725 NEC-associated deaths occurred, yielding a mortality rate of 6.36 per 1,000 live VLBW births. Younger maternal age, unmarried status, Medicaid insurance, and residence in the southern United States were associated with increased mortality risk. Infants of American Indian or Alaska Native (AIAN) mothers also had higher odds of NEC-related death, whereas infants of mothers aged 30-34 years or with a bachelor's degree or higher had reduced risk. Maternal socioeconomic disparities are associated with NEC-associated mortality in VLBW infants, underscoring the need to address these disparities to improve outcomes.

## LioNeo project: a phase 2, randomized, double-blind, non-inferiority trial of a lyophilized human milk-based fortifier for very low birth weight infants

- 来源: medRxiv (preprint) · 推送 02:41
- 期刊: medRxiv (preprint)
- 国家/单位: University of Sao Paulo Faculty of Medicine of Ribeirao Preto: Universidade de Sao Paulo Faculdade de Medicina de Ribeirao Preto
- 通讯作者: José  S. Camelo Jr.
- DOI: 10.64898/2025.12.10.25342013
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.64898/2025.12.10.25342013)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估冻干人乳来源强化剂LioNeo在极低出生体重儿生长方面是否非劣于牛乳强化剂。
- 核心发现/数据：129例患儿按意向治疗分析（LioNeo 67例，HMCA 62例）；第21天身长z评分调整后差异为0.07（95%CI -0.13至0.27），下限高于非劣效界值-0.27，达到非劣效。头围和体重无差异；迟发型新生儿败血症在HMCA组发生6例、LioNeo组0例（P=0.01）；两组血清锌均下降，LioNeo组第21天铜更高。
- 临床启示：对极低出生体重儿，LioNeo在线性生长上不劣于牛乳强化剂，且未增加不良事件，并可能减少迟发型新生儿败血症。仍需多中心3期试验进一步验证。

**原文摘要**：Background: Cow's milk protein-based fortifiers are the standard for human milk fortification in very low birth weight (VLBW) infants but have been associated with a higher risk of necrotizing enterocolitis. LioNeo is produced by lyophilization of donor human milk. This study assessed whether LioNeo is non-inferior to the bovine fortifier for growth in VLBW infants. Methods: This phase 2, randomized, double-blind, non-inferiority trial was conducted at a university hospital. Hemodynamically stable VLBW infants who were small or appropriate for gestational age and exclusively fed human milk ([&ge;]100 mL/kg/day) were randomized 1:1 to LioNeo or human milk with a bovine fortifier (HMCA) for 21 days. The primary outcomes were length and head circumference z-scores. Secondary outcomes were weight gain, adverse events, and serum copper and zinc concentrations. Results: In total, 129 infants were randomized and analyzed by intention to treat (LioNeo, 67; HMCA, 62). At day 21, the adjusted difference in length z-score was 0.07 (95% CI -0.13 to 0.27), with the lower limit above the non-inferiority margin (-0.27). There was no difference in head circumference or weight. Late-onset neonatal sepsis occurred in six HMCA participants and none in the LioNeo group (P = 0.01). Serum zinc decreased in both groups; copper was higher with LioNeo at day 21. Conclusions: LioNeo was non-inferior to the bovine fortifier for linear growth in VLBW infants, with no difference in head circumference or weight and no increase in adverse events. Multicenter phase 3 trials are needed. Trial registration: Brazilian Registry of Clinical Trials (ReBEC), RBR-8nnpfm (UTN U1111-1220-0550). Keywords: Freeze Drying; Infant, Premature; Milk Banks; Milk, Human; Randomized Controlled Trial.

## Lymphatic Dysplasia Shapes Liver Epithelial Outcomes in Biliary Atresia

- 期刊: medRxiv (preprint)
- 国家/单位: Charite Universitaetsmedizin Berlin
- 通讯作者: Milad Rezvani
- DOI: 10.64898/2026.10.12.26365253

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过多尺度图谱解析胆道闭锁（BA）肝组织，明确早期淋巴发育不良如何影响肝上皮状态及严重BA结局，并探索CCL21作为干预靶点。
- 核心发现/数据：严重BA婴儿早期即出现架构扭曲、未成熟且CCL21高表达的淋巴血管，并伴胆管细胞扩增及向免疫反应性MMP7/SOX9高表达状态转化。严重BA来源淋巴内皮与胆管细胞组装类器官后可诱导LGR5/S

**原文摘要**：Biliary Atresia (BA) is the leading cause of pediatric liver transplantation. Given the heterogeneity of the disease, outcomes after diagnosis remain unpredictable. A multiscale atlas of BA hepatic tissue established early lymphatic dysplasia in infants that later proceed toward liver failure and transplantation (severe BA). An architecturally distorted, immature and CCL21-high lymphatic vasculature marked severe BA, along with an expanding cholangiocyte compartment pivoting toward immune-reactive MMP7/SOX9-high states. Hepatocyte-to-biliary transdifferentiation emerged broadly across BA- and non-BA-cholestatic injuries, while dedifferentiation and acute-phase-reactions were specific to severe BA. Functionally, lymphatic endothelial tissue, isolated from severe BA livers, induced an analogous LGR5- and SOX9-positive epithelial progenitor response when assembled with cholangiocytes as organoids. Further experiments revealed that lymphatic endothelium signals ectopically on liver epithelium via CCL21-CCR7-axis after BA-specific inflammatory priming. CCL21-depletion strategies mitigated the BA-specific MMP7-program. In sum, a maladaptive lymphatic endothelial dysplasia perturbs intrahepatic epithelium in severe BA and offers CCL21 as a possible target for epithelial stability.
