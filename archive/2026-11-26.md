## Advances in the management of myasthenia gravis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:56
- 期刊: BMJ · 中科院医学1区(医学：内科1区) · IF 55.1 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Baylor College of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Anderson NC (ncanderson@bcm.edu)
- DOI: 10.1136/bmj-2026-085090
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2026-085090) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43085813/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统综述重症肌无力（MG）在流行病学、发病机制、分类、诊断及治疗方面的管理进展。
- 核心发现/数据：摘要未报告具体数值数据；综述指出已识别不同抗体亚型并拓展治疗选择，使MG从高致残致死疾病转变为日益可管理的疾病。已确立疗法包括乙酰胆碱酯酶抑制剂、糖皮质激素、传统口服免疫抑制剂、静脉免疫球蛋白、血浆置换、B细胞清除剂、补体抑制剂、FcRn抑制剂及胸腺切除，并强调新兴治疗、妊娠、肌无力危象和个体化治疗。医患沟通被视为优化依从性和结局的关键因素。
- 临床启示：临床应结合抗体亚型、安全性和患者特征个体化整合新旧疗法，并重视医患沟通与依从性。随着治疗复杂性增加，需持续优化治疗算法和循证决策工具。

**原文摘要**：Myasthenia gravis is an autoimmune disorder of the neuromuscular junction characterised by fluctuating fatigable weakness of skeletal muscles. Advances have led to the identification of distinct antibody subtypes and expanding therapeutic options, transforming myasthenia gravis from a disease of significant morbidity and mortality to one that is increasingly manageable. This review presents a comprehensive overview of myasthenia gravis, including its epidemiology, pathogenesis, classification, diagnostic strategies, and treatment modalities. Established therapies are discussed, including acetylcholinesterase inhibitors, corticosteroids, conventional oral immunosuppressants, intravenous immunoglobulin, plasma exchange, B cell depleting agents, complement inhibitors, neonatal fragment crystallisable receptor inhibitors, and thymectomy. A dedicated section highlights emerging treatments. Special considerations for pregnancy, myasthenic crisis, and individualised treatment approaches are emphasised. Physician-patient communication is highlighted as a critical factor in optimising adherence and outcomes. Effective integration of novel therapies into clinical practice depends on a clear understanding of their mechanisms, safety profiles, and applicability across diverse patient populations. As therapeutic complexity increases, improved clinical outcomes will require continued refinement of clinical tools, treatment algorithms, optimal communication strategies, and robust data to guide evidence based decision making.

A 37-year-old woman (gravida 9, para 9) with a twin pregnancy at 33-34 weeks of gestation was brought to Banadir Hospital, Mogadishu, on 10 This case demonstrates that, perimortem cesarean delivery done under resource-limited conditions following maternal cardiac arrest with a rapid timely intervention, coordinated multidisciplinary team, effective neonatal resuscitation, and access of tertiary neonatal intensive care unit, will ultimately achieve in a survival and good outcome of preterm twins.

## Preoperative azithromycin use in cesarean deliveries complicated by suspected intraamniotic infection.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:56
- 期刊: Am J Obstet Gynecol MFM · 中科院医学2区(妇产科学2区) · IF 3.6 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: Durham · Duke School of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Boissiere J (boissiere.jaye@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.ajogmf.2026.102211
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajogmf.2026.102211) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43085495/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估术前阿奇霉素使用对疑似羊膜腔内感染（sIAI）剖宫产患者产后感染并发症及新生儿发病率的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：在985例符合标准的剖宫产患者中，666例（67.6%）接受术前阿奇霉素。校正混杂因素后，阿奇霉素与母亲感染复合结局风险降低相关（aOR 0.58，95%CI 0.35–0.96），并与手术部位感染风险降低相关（aOR 0.48，95%CI 0.25–0.92）。阿奇霉素还与新生儿复合结局风险降低相关（aOR 0.36，95%CI 0.23–0.57），包括疑似脓毒症（aOR 0.29，95%CI 0.18–0.47）和NICU入住（aOR 0.49，95%CI 0.33–0.72）。
- 临床启示：在sIAI剖宫产中，术前阿奇霉素预防性使用可能与产后42天内感染并发症及新生儿住院期间发病率降低相关，支持临床考虑该策略，但仍需前瞻性研究验证。

**原文摘要**：A prior randomized clinical trial demonstrated the benefit of adjunctive azithromycin for labored cesareans in reducing postpartum infections and neonatal morbidity but excluded patients with intraamniotic infection (IAI). IAI is an independent risk factor for postpartum infectious morbidity and neonatal morbidity. The role of azithromycin in cesareans complicated by IAI remains unclear. This study evaluated the impact of preoperative azithromycin use on postpartum infectious complications and neonatal morbidity in patients who underwent cesarean deliveries complicated by suspected IAI (sIAI). This was a retrospective cohort study of patients undergoing cesarean deliveries complicated by sIAI, and their corresponding singletons, in a single healthcare system between April 1, 2013 and December 13, 2023. Patients were excluded if they were missing delivery admission data, antibiotic data, or lacked a clinical diagnosis of sIAI. All singleton neonates were included. Clinical diagnosis of sIAI was based on standard guidelines. Exposure of interest was preoperative azithromycin. The primary outcome in this study was a postpartum infectious morbidity composite including surgical site infection (SSI), endometritis, pelvic abscess, pneumonia, or sepsis within 42 days of delivery. Secondary maternal outcomes included components of the composite, readmission, and adverse reactions to azithromycin. A planned secondary analysis was performed assessing neonatal morbidity. The primary outcome for this analysis was a neonatal morbidity composite during the delivery admission, which included suspected or confirmed sepsis, respiratory distress syndrome, necrotizing enterocolitis, periventricular leukomalacia, intraventricular hemorrhage, bronchopulmonary dysplasia, and death. Secondary outcomes for the neonates included components of the composite, admission to the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU), and NICU length of stay. Data were analyzed with bivariate statistics and regression models to control for confounders and practice changes over the study period. Of the 985 delivering patients who met inclusion criteria, 666 (67.6%) received preoperative azithromycin. Differences in patient age, race, penicillin allergy frequency, and tobacco use were seen between patients who received azithromycin and those who did not. Preoperative azithromycin use was associated with different rates of intrapartum IAI treatment, vaginal preparation use, preoperative cefazolin prophylaxis, postpartum antibiotic receipt, and estimated blood loss at delivery. After controlling for confounding variables, azithromycin use was associated with markedly lower odds of the maternal composite outcome (aOR: 0.58; 95% CI:0.35, 0.96) and SSI (aOR: 0.48; 95% CI:0.25, 0.92). Preoperative azithromycin was also associated with reduced odds of the neonatal composite outcome (aOR: 0.36, 95% CI: 0.23, 0.57), suspected sepsis (aOR: 0.29, 95% CI: 0.18, 0.47) and NICU admission (aOR: 0.49, 95% CI: 0.33, 0.72). Azithromycin use as antibiotic prophylaxis in cesarean deliveries complicated by sIAI was associated with lower rates of postpartum infectious complications within 42 days postpartum and neonatal morbidity during the delivery admission.

## Single-test accuracy study to assess the diagnostic value of faecal calprotectin to diagnose necrotising enterocolitis in infants with cardiac defects: the SAFE NEC protocol.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估粪便钙卫蛋白在合并心脏缺陷婴儿中诊断坏死性小肠结肠炎的诊断准确性。
- 核心发现/数据：本研究为前瞻性、观察性、全国多中心诊断准确性研究，计划在英国4家专科儿童心脏中心纳入出生至3个月、因先天性心脏病需心脏手术或导管介入的婴儿，目标样本量为300例。将在心脏手术前后及临床怀疑坏死性小肠结肠炎时采集粪便检测钙卫蛋白，并采用ROC曲线评估其区分坏死性小肠结肠炎与非坏死性小肠结肠炎的诊断准确性；摘要未报告研究结果。

**原文摘要**：Necrotising enterocolitis is a gut disease that can develop in infants born with cardiac defects. In its early stages, the clinical presentation of necrotising enterocolitis is difficult to identify. An inaccurate diagnosis of necrotising enterocolitis results in the withdrawal of an infant's feed and unnecessary administration of antibiotics. These exacerbate parental stress and contribute to prolonged length of stay. Calprotectin is secreted into the lumen in the early stages of intestinal inflammation; thus, elevated faecal calprotectin concentrations are indicative of inflammatory diseases. The study aims to assess the diagnostic accuracy of faecal calprotectin in diagnosing necrotising enterocolitis in infants with cardiac defects. A diagnostic accuracy study design will be delivered as a prospective, observational, national multi-centre study. Infants between birth and three months of age who require cardiac surgery or catheterisation for a CHD. Four specialist paediatric cardiac units across England. Faecal samples will be collected from infants to measure calprotectin levels before and after cardiac surgery. An additional faecal sample will be collected if the medical team is suspicious that an infant is developing necrotising enterocolitis. REDCap will be used for data collection, including the daily necrotising enterocolitis checklist, anthropometrics ( The target sample size from all four sites is 300 infants. A receiver operating characteristic curve will be used to assess the diagnostic accuracy of faecal calprotectin to distinguish between necrotising enterocolitis and no necrotising enterocolitis. Study findings will be disseminated to recruited families, peer-reviewed journals, and international conferences.

## Prenatal Ultrasonographic Anal Canal Assessment for Detecting Anorectal Malformations: Insights From a Tertiary Referral Center.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 13:32
- 期刊: Prenat Diagn · 中科院医学2区(遗传学2区/妇产科学3区) · IF 3.1 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 荷兰 · Sophia Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Sloots CEJ
- DOI: 10.1002/pd.70281
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/pd.70281) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43087134/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估产前超声通过肛管“靶征”评估检测肛门直肠畸形的可行性及诊断性能。
- 核心发现/数据：本研究纳入1943例胎儿、共2238次专家胎儿异常筛查，19-21周靶征可评估率由35%升至70%，29-31周达97%。在1404例有产后数据的胎儿中，靶征不可评估439例（2例ARM）、正常960例（4例ARM）、异常2例（1例ARM）、缺失3例（3例ARM），阳性预测值为80.0%，阴性预测值为99.6%。
- 临床启示：产前超声靶征评估可行且预测价值强，即使在约20周妊娠时也具备评估潜力，有助于提高肛门直肠畸形的产前检出。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate the feasibility and diagnostic performance of prenatal ultrasound assessment of the anal canal, depicted as a target sign (TS), for detecting anorectal malformations (ARMs). This 1-year, single-center cohort study analyzed expert fetal anomaly scans (eFASs) in high-risk pregnancies for 18 weeks of gestation onwards. The assessability and appearance of the TS were prospectively documented. Feasibility was defined as the ratio of assessable to non-assessable TSs per gestational week. Diagnostic value was expressed as positive and negative predictive values, with a normal TS indicating a normal anus and an abnormal/absent TS indicating an ARM. The TS was scored in 1943 fetuses comprising 2238 eFASs. Throughout the study period, assessability rates at 19-21 weeks of gestation increased from 35% to 70%. At 29-31 weeks of gestation, 97% of TSs were assessable. Postnatal data were available for 1404 of 1943 fetuses, with 439 TSs nonassessable (two ARMs), 960 normal (four ARMs), two abnormal (one ARMs), and three absent (three ARMs), yielding positive and negative predictive values of 80.0% and 99.6%, respectively. This study highlights the feasibility and the strong predictive values of TS assessment on prenatal ultrasound, even around 20 weeks of gestation, emphasizing its importance in enhancing prenatal ARM detection.

## Outcomes of trial of labor after two cesarean deliveries: a single-center retrospective cohort study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 20:51
- 期刊: J Matern Fetal Neonatal Med
- 国家/单位: 德国 · Clinic St. Hedwig of the Order of St. John
- 通讯作者: Köninger A
- DOI: 10.1080/14767058.2026.2744005
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1080/14767058.2026.2744005) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43095791/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：比较两次剖宫产后试产（TOLA2C）与择期重复剖宫产（ERCD）的母婴结局，并描述TOLA2C成功与失败患者的母体和产科特征。
- 核心发现/数据：本研究纳入333名有≥2次剖宫产史孕妇，其中295名（88.6%）行ERCD，38名（11.4%）尝试TOLA2C；TOLA2C阴道分娩成功率为60.5%（23/38），39.5%（15/38）需非计划剖宫产。TOLA2C组子宫瘢痕裂开1例（2.6%），ERCD组10例（3.4%），ERCD组完全子宫破裂1例（0.3%），均无母儿死亡相关；两组母儿并发症无显著差异。但TOLA2C样本量小且存在选择偏倚，不能据此得出等效性或比较安全性结论。
- 临床启示：在设备齐全的产科中心，经个体化咨询并严格筛选后，可考虑对有两次剖宫产史的女性尝试TOLA2C。需充分告知子宫破裂等风险，并强调该研究不足以证明其与ERCD等效安全。

**原文摘要**：The global rise in cesarean delivery (CD) rates has led to an increasing number of women with multiple prior CDs. Despite growing evidence in support of trial of labor after two CD (TOLA2C), its adoption remains limited in many settings owing to concerns regarding potential complications, particularly uterine rupture. This study aimed to compare maternal and neonatal outcomes between trial of labor after two cesarean sections (TOLA2C) and elective repeat cesarean delivery (ERCD) within a single institution and to characterize maternal and obstetric features among women with successful and failed TOLA2C. This retrospective cohort study included 333 women with two or more previous CDs who gave birth between January 2021 and December 2023. Women with multiple pregnancies were excluded. Patients were categorized according to the intended mode of delivery into ERCD and TOLA2C groups. Maternal characteristics, birth outcomes, and complications were extracted from digital medical records. Continuous variables were summarized using means with standard deviations and medians with interquartile ranges, and categorical variables as frequencies and percentages. Group comparisons were performed using the Wilcoxon-Mann-Whitney test, Welch's two-sample t-test, chi-square test, or Fisher's exact test, as appropriate. A p value <0.05 was considered statistically significant. In total, 333 women met the eligibility criteria for the study. Among them, 295 (88.6%) underwent ERCD, whereas 38 (11.4%) attempted TOLA2C. The success rate of TOLA2C was 60.5% (23/38), whereas 39.5% (15/38) required an unplanned CD. Uterine scar dehiscence was identified in one woman undergoing TOLA2C (2.6%) and in 10 women undergoing ERCD (3.4%), whereas complete uterine rupture occurred in one woman with schedulded ERCD (0.3%). No uterine scar event was associated with maternal or neonatal death. No maternal deaths or embolic events were reported, one hysterectomy and one case of intrauterine infection occurred. Overall, there were no significant differences in maternal or neonatal complications between the two groups. Among women with two prior cesarean deliveries in our study population, TOLA2C resulted in vaginal birth in 60.5% of attempts. No statistically significant differences in the evaluated maternal and neonatal complications were detected between TOLA2C and ERCD; however, the small TOLA2C sample and potential selection bias preclude conclusions regarding equivalence or comparative safety. TOLA2C may be considered in appropriately selected women following individualized counseling in adequately equipped obstetric centers.
