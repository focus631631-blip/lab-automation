## Antisense oligonucleotide silencing rescues neurodevelopmental deficits in AMPA receptor gain-of-function mice.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:54
- 期刊: Neuron · 中科院医学1区(神经科学1区) · IF 16.9 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 中国台湾 · Institute of Cellular and Organismic Biology and Neuroscience Program of Academia Sinica
- 通讯作者: Huganir RL (rhuganir@jhmi.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.neuron.2026.10.013
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2026.10.013) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43014310/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探究孤独症谱系障碍/智力障碍相关的AMPA受体GluA1变异GRIA1 p.A636T的致病机制及等位基因特异性ASO的治疗潜力。
- 核心发现/数据：在Gria1-A636T敲入小鼠中，该变异足以产生ASD/ID核心行为表型，并导致选择性海马损伤，表现为进行性树突萎缩和神经元丢失。尽管含GluA1复合物减少，突触AMPAR仍呈过度兴奋，且发育过程中不能由钙通透型转换为钙不通透型，造成持续兴奋性毒性。新生期单剂等位基因特异性ASO可选择性沉默突变转录本，预防海马病理并挽救行为缺陷。
- 临床启示：该研究揭示GRIA1功能获得性通道病通过发育期兴奋性毒性致病的机制，支持对AMPAR相关神经发育障碍开展RNA靶向精准治疗。新生儿期单剂ASO在动物模型中显示预防性治疗潜力，但临床转化仍需进一步安全性和有效性验证。

**原文摘要**：AMPA receptors (AMPARs) mediate fast excitatory synaptic transmission and are essential for neuronal development and brain function. We investigated the role of a recurrent AMPAR GluA1 variant (GRIA1 p.A636T) identified in individuals with autism spectrum disorder (ASD) and intellectual disability (ID). Using a Gria1-A636T knock-in mouse model, we show that this variant is sufficient to produce core ASD/ID-like behaviors and cause selective hippocampal vulnerability characterized by progressive dendritic atrophy and neuronal loss. Despite a reduction in GluA1-containing complexes, synaptic AMPARs exhibit hyperexcitability and fail to transition from calcium-permeable to calcium-impermeable AMPARs during development, resulting in persistent excitotoxicity. To explore potential therapeutic interventions, we developed an allele-specific antisense oligonucleotide (ASO) to selectively silence the mutant transcript. A single neonatal ASO dose prevents hippocampal pathology and rescues behavioral deficits. These findings define a mechanism of developmental excitotoxicity driven by a GRIA1 gain-of-function channelopathy and support RNA-targeted precision therapy for AMPAR-related neurodevelopmental disorders.

## Physiology-adapted cardiac CT angiography during veno-arterial ECMO in neonatal and pediatric postoperative congenital heart disease: feasibility and clinical impact.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:54
- 期刊: Pediatr Radiol · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区/核医学3区) · IF 2.5 · JCR Q1

- DOI: 10.1007/s00247-026-06822-9
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00247-026-06822-9) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43013076/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估在新生儿及儿童先天性心脏病术后VA-ECMO支持期间，采用生理学适配的心脏CT血管造影（CCTA）方案的可行性、诊断价值及临床影响。
- 核心发现/数据：本研究为2013—2022年单中心回顾性队列，25例患儿共完成26次CCTA，年龄3天至14.8岁（中位133天），中位体重5.1 kg；对比剂经ECMO动脉支路给药15例，经静脉并在控制ECMO流量下给药11例。18/26次（69%）检出病理，22/26次（85%）提供超声心动图之外的解剖信息，14/26次（54%）直接指导手术或导管介入且均获操作确认。未发生转运相关或CT相关不良事件。
- 临床启示：当采集方案适应ECMO依赖的血流动力学时，CCTA在该危重人群中可行且具有重要诊断价值。生理学指导策略可稳定血管增强，并常发现超声未检出、可指导治疗决策的临床可干预病变。

**原文摘要**：Cardiac computed tomographic angiography (CCTA) provides high-resolution anatomical assessment in cases of congenital heart disease (CHD). This is crucial in patients supported by veno-arterial extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (VA-ECMO), since the fundamental alteration of systemic hemodynamics and contrast transit kinetics makes conventional CT acquisition strategies unreliable. We evaluated the feasibility, diagnostic yield, and clinical impact of a structured CCTA protocol adapted to ECMO-dependent physiology in postoperative neonatal and pediatric CHD patients. This retrospective, single-center cohort study (2013-2022) included all consecutive postoperative neonatal and pediatric patients with CHD who underwent clinically indicated CCTA while supported by VA-ECMO subsequent to cardiac surgery. Contrast administration route and scan timing were individualized according to ECMO circuit flow characteristics and clinical indication. Twenty-six CCTA examinations were performed in 25 patients ranging in age from 3 days to 14.8 years (median age 133 days; median weight 5.1 kg). Contrast was administered via the arterial ECMO limb in 15 cases and intravenously with controlled ECMO flow modulation in 11. In 18 of the 26 examinations (69%), the examination revealed a pathology. CCTA provided anatomical information beyond echocardiography in 22 of 26 cases (85%) and directly informed a surgical or catheter-based intervention in 14 cases (54%), with procedural confirmation in all. No transport-related or CT-related adverse events occurred. When acquisition protocols are adapted to ECMO-dependent hemodynamics, CCTA is feasible and diagnostically consequential in neonatal and pediatric patients supported by VA-ECMO after congenital cardiac surgery. A physiology-guided strategy enables consistent vascular enhancement and frequently identifies clinically actionable findings not detected by echocardiography which can directly inform therapeutic decision-making in this critically ill population.

## Bilateral polygnathia associated with oro-auricular fistulae and rudimentary lingual structures in a neonatal lamb: clinical, computed tomographic, surgical, and histopathological findings.

- 核心发现/数据：4 kg雄性Kivircik杂交羔羊于36小时龄就诊，因双侧耳下开口在吮乳时漏奶；CT显示下颌后腹侧、邻近舌骨装置存在双侧残余矿化及骨性复合体，三维重建明确其空间形态。全麻下切除异常结构并保留腮腺，组织病理见皮肤、舌型黏膜、横纹肌、唾液组织、成熟骨小梁及含成釉细胞、牙本质和成牙本质细胞的发育牙组织。术后管饲转瓶饲，第7天拆线示一期愈合，4个月电话随访示进食正常、持续增重、无明显并发症。
- 临床启示：早期手术切除该双侧多颌畸形可获得良好短期愈合和转归；因不能排除遗传因素，建议患病公羊不用于繁殖。

**原文摘要**：A 4-kg male Kivircik crossbred lamb was presented at 36 h of age because milk escaped from bilateral openings ventral to the auricles during suckling, raising concern about inadequate intake. Clinical examination revealed accessory jaw-like and lingual structures with elicitable suckling responses on both sides, while suckling through the normal oral cavity remained intact. Histopathology identified skin, lingual-type mucosa, striated muscle, salivary tissue, mature osseous trabeculae, and developing dental tissue containing ameloblasts, dentine, and odontoblasts. Tube feeding was followed by bottle feeding, and primary wound healing was confirmed when the skin sutures were removed on postoperative day 7. At a four-month telephone follow-up, the owner reported normal feeding, continued weight gain, and no apparent clinical complications. The combined clinical, imaging, surgical, and histopathological findings supported a diagnosis of bilateral polygnathia (accessory jaw formation) associated with oro-auricular fistulae and rudimentary lingual structures. Early excision was followed by favourable short-term healing and an owner-reported favourable outcome at four months. Exclusion of the affected ram from breeding is recommended as a precaution because a heritable contribution cannot be excluded.

## Age-specific histological features of severe alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency-associated liver disease (Pi*ZZ genotype) 

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:54
- 期刊: JHEP Rep
- 国家/单位: 德国 · Medical Clinic III
- 通讯作者: Strnad P (p.strnad@mul-ct.de)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jhepr.2026.102099
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhepr.2026.102099) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43014451/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：比较严重α-1抗胰蛋白酶缺乏症（Pi*ZZ基因型）相关肝病在儿童与成人中的组织学表型，并揭示年龄特异性特征。
- 核心发现/数据：研究纳入67例Pi*ZZ成人和48例Pi*ZZ儿童，儿童样本更多来自肝移植（64.6% vs 21.2%，p<0.001），且METAVIR纤维化分期更高（4.0 vs 2.5，p<0.001）。儿童胆栓（43.8% vs 10.4%）和胆管稀少（58.3% vs 1.5%）显著多于成人，校正纤维化分期后仍成立；儿童中6-17岁、1-5岁和<1岁组的肝硬化比例分别为64.7%、75.0%和30.8%。>70%儿童接受UDCA治疗，治疗者纤维化、胆汁淤积和胆管稀少更明显，多重染色证实儿童较成人成熟胆管丢失但肝细胞分区保留。
- 临床启示：Pi*ZZ相关肝病具有年龄特异性组织学模式，应据此支持患者咨询、疾病管理及临床试验患者选择。

**原文摘要**：Liver disease in severe alpha-1 antitrypsin (AAT) deficiency (Pi*ZZ genotype) displays a biphasic pattern with peaks in early childhood and later adulthood. The aim was to compare the histological liver phenotype in children and adults with Pi*ZZ genotype. Specimens from 67 Pi*ZZ adults and 48 Pi*ZZ children from six European countries were independently scored by two histopathologists in a blinded fashion. Sequential multiplex immunofluorescence staining (COMET) quantified histological features in a representative adult and pediatric sample. Pediatric Pi*ZZ samples originated more often from liver transplantation (64.6 vs. 21.2%, p < .001). Compared to adults, children presented a significantly higher fibrosis stage according to METAVIR (4.0 vs. 2.5, p < .001). Liver steatosis was somewhat more prevalent in Pi*ZZ adults, while bile plugs and duct paucity were significantly more common in Pi*ZZ children (bile plugs: 43.8 vs. 10.4%, p < .001; duct paucity 58.3 vs. 1.5%, p < .001) even when adjusted for fibrosis stage. Age-based assessment of pediatric cohort (6-17 years, 1-5 years, < 1 year) revealed that cirrhosis was more common in older age groups than in infants (64.7 vs. 75.0 vs. 30.8%). Youngest children displayed less AAT accumulation and less frequently had larger aggregates, while preschoolers had the highest liver injury markers. >70% of children received ursodeoxycholic acid (UDCA) and the treated children had a more pronounced fibrosis, cholestasis and duct paucity. Multiplex staining confirmed the loss of mature bile ducts while retaining hepatocyte zonation in pediatric vs adult. Our study reveals unique histological patterns in Pi*ZZ children and adults. It provides basis for further mechanistic studies and may guide selection into clinical trials. Alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency (AATD) is an inherited disorder that predisposes to liver disease and can manifest in early childhood as well as late adulthood. Our work systematically compares a large international set of pediatric and adult liver specimen and demonstrates age-specific histological features. Our results should support patient counseling (i.e. in case of late diagnosis of pediatric disease) and management including selection of patients for clinical trials.

## Eosinophilic esophagitis in esophageal atresia patients: current evidence and clinical implications.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:54
- 期刊: Eur J Pediatr Surg
- 国家/单位: Lille · Lille University
- 通讯作者: Gottrand F
- DOI: 10.1055/a-3001-2738
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1055/a-3001-2738) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43014411/)

**原文摘要**：Background Eosinophilic esophagitis (EoE) is increasingly recognized in children with esophageal atresia (EA), but its epidemiology, clinical phenotype, risk factors, and optimal management in this population remain incompletely defined. Studies reporting patients with both EA and EoE were included.ResultsTwenty-one studies were included, reporting 253 patients with EA-associated EoE. - 核心发现/数据：本例缺乏CHAOS典型超声标志（肺过度充气和水肿），产前诊断极为困难；气管食管瘘的“减压效应”使肺部表现直至晚孕期前均正常，随后出现严重羊水过多；死后MRI明确显示喉闭锁及复杂瘘管解剖，解释了常规复苏失败的原因。
- 临床启示：对VACTERL合并气管食管瘘的新生儿应警惕被掩盖的CHAOS，产前超声无典型征象不能排除诊断；死后MRI可作为无创确诊工具，并为家庭哀伤支持提供依据。

**原文摘要**：We report an atypical case of congenital high airway obstruction sequence (CHAOS) masked by a tracheo-esophageal fistula in a neonate with VACTERL association. The case was unique because classical sonographic markers of CHAOS, such as hyperinflated lungs and hydrops, were absent, making antenatal diagnosis exceptionally difficult. The venting effect of the tracheo-esophageal fistula allowed for normal lung appearances until late gestation when severe polyhydramnios developed. This report highlights the important role of postmortem magnetic resonance imaging (PMMR) as a non-invasive diagnostic tool. PMMR provided definitive evidence of laryngeal atresia and complex fistulous anatomy, offering essential insights into why standard resuscitation failed, supporting the family's grieving process.
