## Extracorporeal Membrane Oxygenation in Infants With Omphalocele: A Contemporary ELSO Registry Analysis.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 11:51
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Children's Hospital of Orange County
- 通讯作者: Yu PT
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163595
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163595) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43020322/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：基于ELSO注册数据库分析需ECMO治疗的脐膨出婴儿的结局及死亡预测因素。
- 核心发现/数据：2000—2025年共纳入88例需ECMO的脐膨出婴儿，总体出院生存率为41%；70%发生ECMO相关并发症，且任何ECMO相关并发症均独立与死亡相关（HR 2.33，95%CI 1.23–4.34，p=0.009）。双变量分析中，<28日龄置管（p=0.048）和女性（p=0.047）与死亡相关；与先天性肺畸形和先天性膈疝相比，脐膨出ECMO病例频率随时间增加更明显。
- 临床启示：该人群ECMO死亡率高，临床应加强ECMO相关并发症的预防与管理，并据此进行围产期多学科风险分层和病例选择。

**原文摘要**：Omphalocele is a rare congenital abdominal wall defect frequently associated with pulmonary hypoplasia, pulmonary hypertension, congenital heart disease, and severe respiratory failure. Infants with omphalocele requiring extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO) represent a high-risk population; however, contemporary multicenter data evaluating survival and predictors of mortality remain limited. We aimed to characterize outcomes and identify factors associated with mortality among infants with omphalocele requiring ECMO. We performed a retrospective cohort study using the Extracorporeal Life Support Organization Registry from 2000-2025. Demographic, clinical, ECMO-related, and complication variables were compared between survivors and non-survivors, and multivariable Cox proportional hazards regression was performed to identify independent predictors of mortality. 88 infants with omphalocele requiring ECMO were identified, with 41% overall survival to hospital discharge. On bivariate analysis, cannulation at <28 days of age (p=0.048) and female sex (p=0.047) were associated with mortality. ECMO-related complications occurred in 70% of patients and were associated with decreased survival probability. On multivariable analysis, the presence of any ECMO-related complication remained independently associated with mortality (HR 2.33, 95% CI 1.23-4.34, p=0.009). When compared to congenital lung malformations and congenital diaphragmatic hernia, omphalocele ECMO cases demonstrated a more pronounced increase in frequency over time. Infants with omphalocele requiring ECMO experience substantial mortality despite advances in neonatal and ECMO care. ECMO-related complications were independently associated with mortality. These findings add to risk stratification and multidisciplinary perinatal planning for this population. Further studies evaluating long-term outcomes and optimal patient selection are needed. Level III (Retrospective Cohort Study.

## Osteoarticular Infection in Immunocompromised Children and Neonates: A Retrospective Case Series Emphasising Diagnostic Challenges and Multimodal Management.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:51
- 期刊: J Pediatr Orthop
- 国家/单位: Department of Orthopaedic Surgery
- 通讯作者: Wong KPL
- DOI: 10.1097/BPO.0000000000003527
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/BPO.0000000000003527) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43021749/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：描述新生儿及免疫功能低下儿童骨关节感染（OAI）的临床表现、微生物学、治疗与结局，并强调其诊断挑战和多模式管理。
- 核心发现/数据：18例患者中6例（33.3%）为新生儿，所有患者均有患肢活动受限，14例（77.8%）有局部肿胀；12例（66.7%）发热，6例（33.3%）无发热且以新生儿为主，CRP在所有患者中均升高而白细胞反应不一。金黄色葡萄球菌最常见（6/18，33.3%），其他病原包括链球菌、分枝杆菌、热带念珠菌、铜绿假单胞菌、嗜麦芽窄食单胞菌和芽孢杆菌；15例（83.3%）接受手术，6例（33.3%）接受免疫导向治疗。末次随访时14例（77.8%）完全缓解，2例死于与OAI无关原因，1例失访，1例持续或复发感染，2例遗留慢性肌骨后遗症。
- 临床启示：新生儿和免疫低下儿童OAI可缺乏发热和白细胞升高，CRP升高可作为辅助评估指标，并应警惕非典型病原。多学科、多模式管理（抗菌治疗、外科干预及免疫导向治疗）可能改善预后。

**原文摘要**：Osteoarticular infections (OAIs) in neonates and immunocompromised children present distinct diagnostic and therapeutic challenges. Atypical clinical presentations, attenuated inflammatory responses, and a broader spectrum of pathogens complicate early recognition and management. This study characterizes the clinical presentation, microbiology, management, and outcomes of OAIs in this highly vulnerable population. We conducted a retrospective case series of patients younger than 16 years with osteomyelitis and/or septic arthritis managed at a tertiary pediatric center between February 2017 and February 2024. The cohort comprised neonates and children with primary or secondary immunodeficiency. Clinical presentation, inflammatory markers, microbiology, antimicrobial and surgical treatment, immune-directed therapy, and outcomes were analyzed descriptively. Ethical approval was obtained from the SingHealth Centralized Institutional Review Board (CIRB Ref: 2023-2559). Eighteen patients were included; 6 (33.3%) were neonates. All patients had reduced active limb mobility, and 14 (77.8%) had localized swelling. Twelve patients (66.7%) had fever, whereas 6 (33.3%), predominantly neonates, remained afebrile. CRP was elevated in all patients despite variable white blood cell responses. Staphylococcus aureus was the most frequently identified pathogen [6/18 (33.3%)]; other organisms included Streptococci, Mycobacteria, Candida tropicalis, Pseudomonas aeruginosa, Stenotrophomonas maltophilia, and Bacillus spp. Fifteen patients (83.3%) underwent surgical intervention, and 6 (33.3%) received immune-directed therapy. At last follow-up, 14 patients (77.8%) had complete resolution, 2 died from causes unrelated to the OAI, 1 was lost to follow-up, and 1 had persistent or recurrent infection. Two patients developed chronic musculoskeletal sequelae. OIAs in this heterogeneous cohort were frequently accompanied by attenuated systemic features and diverse microbiology. Fever and leucocytosis were not invariably present, whereas CRP was elevated in all cases and provided supplementary information alongside clinical assessment. Favorable outcomes were observed in the context of multidisciplinary, multimodal management, but the retrospective design, small sample, and population heterogeneity preclude causal or subgroup-specific conclusions. These findings are descriptive and hypothesis-generating and require prospective validation. Level IV-retrospective case series.

## Early-onset epilepsy and inborn errors of metabolism: A large retrospective pediatric cohort.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 11:51
- 期刊: Epilepsy Behav
- 国家/单位: 意大利 · "Cannizzaro Hospital"
- 通讯作者: Praticò AD (andrea.pratico@unikore.it)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.yebeh.2026.111368
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.yebeh.2026.111368) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43019943/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：明确儿童癫痫患者中遗传代谢病（IEM）的患病率、疾病谱、起病特征及诊疗延迟情况。
- 核心发现/数据：在21,072例因癫痫评估的儿童中，57例（0.27%）确诊癫痫合并IEM；最常见为溶酶体贮积病（31.6%）、氨基酸病（22.8%）、线粒体病（15.8%）、糖基化先天缺陷（12.3%）和有机酸血症（7.0%）。IEM相关癫痫83.3%在3岁前起病，显著高于非代谢性癫痫的27.6%（OR 12.3，95%CI 6.2–24.4，p<0.001）；半数患者首次发作后诊断延迟超过3.5年，约50%虽接受多药抗癫痫治疗仍为药物难治性癫痫。
- 临床启示：对任何癫痫儿童，尤其3岁前起病者，应系统考虑IEM，并采用结构化阶梯诊断流程，以减少诊断延迟并及时获得疾病特异性治疗。

**原文摘要**：Inborn errors of metabolism (IEM) are an under-recognised cause of childhood epilepsy. Early diagnosis is critical because specific treatments can substantially alter the disease course. We performed a retrospective study on 21,072 children evaluated for epilepsy in two Child Neurology referral centres in Eastern Sicily (i.e. Messina, Catania, and Enna) between January 2001 and December 2025. Medical records were reviewed to identify patients with a confirmed IEM diagnosis. Clinical, electrophysiological, neuroimaging, and treatment data were collected and analysed. Fifty-seven children (0.27%) had a co-diagnosis of epilepsy and IEM (28 males, 29 females). Lysosomal storage disorders were the most common category (31.6%), followed by aminoacidopathies (22.8%), mitochondrial disorders (15.8%), congenital disorders of glycosylation (CDG, 12.3%), and organic acidaemias (7.0%). Seizure onset occurred before the age of three years in 83.3% of IEM cases, compared with 27.6% of children with non-metabolic epilepsy (OR 12.3; 95% CI 6.2-24.4; p < 0.001). Diagnostic delay exceeded 3.5 years from first seizure in half the cohort. Approximately 50% of patients showed drug-resistant epilepsy despite multi-drug anti-seizure medication therapy. IEM should be systematically considered in any child presenting with epilepsy, particularly when onset occurs in the first three years of life. A structured stepwise diagnostic approach can substantially reduce diagnostic delay and facilitate access to disease-specific therapies.

## Incidence, predictors, and mortality of necrotizing enterocolitis among neonates admitted to two regional referral hospitals in Uganda: a prospective cohort study.

- 核心发现/数据：共纳入424名新生儿，其中61.1%为早产儿；30例发生NEC，发病率为7.1%，早产儿中显著更高（p<0.001）。独立危险因素包括母亲慢性病（尤其HIV）、子痫前期史、生后14天后就诊、极早产、极低出生体重、超低出生体重及新生儿败血症（均p<0.05）。NEC患儿死亡率显著高于无NEC患儿（26.8% vs. 2.3%，p<0.001）。

**原文摘要**：Necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) is a life-threatening gastrointestinal condition affecting neonates, characterized by abdominal distension, feeding intolerance, and bloody stools, with or without radiological signs such as pneumatosis intestinalis, portal venous gas, or pneumoperitoneum. Despite its significant contribution to neonatal morbidity and mortality, most epidemiological data originate from high-income countries, leaving a gap in evidence from low-resource settings. To determine the incidence, associated risk factors, and mortality of NEC among neonates aged ≤ 28 days in two regional referral hospitals in Uganda. A prospective cohort study was conducted among neonates admitted to Mubende and Fort Portal Regional Referral Hospitals between April 2025 and June 2025. NEC was diagnosed using modified Bell's criteria and participants were followed until discharge or 28 days of life. Neonates with major gastrointestinal malformations or referred to other facilities were excluded. NEC diagnosis was based on modified Bell's criteria. A total of 424 neonates were enrolled, comprising 38.9% term and 61.1% preterm infants. Thirty neonates developed NEC, yielding an incidence proportion of 7.1%, significantly higher among preterm neonates (p < 0.001). Mortality was markedly elevated in neonates with NEC compared to those without (26.8% vs. 2.3%, p < 0.001). NEC incidence in this low-resource setting is notably high, especially among preterm neonates. These findings underscore the urgent need for targeted interventions to reduce neonatal sepsis, prevent preterm births, address intrauterine growth restriction, and strengthen maternal HIV control measures to improve neonatal outcomes.

## Risk factors for surgery in neonatal necrotizing enterocolitis without pneumoperitoneum: Bell stage IIb or higher.

- 期刊: Biomol Biomed · 中科院医学4区(医学：研究与实验4区) · IF 2.8 · JCR Q3

These exploratory associations require confirmation in larger, multicenter studies.

## FGF13 Regulates Myocardial Ischemia-Reperfusion Injury via PGRN Glycosylation.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 19:06
- 期刊: Circ Res · 中科院医学1区(心脏和心血管系统1区/血液学1区/外周血管病1区) · IF 18.0 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: Y.Y.) · Hebei Medical University
- 通讯作者: Wang C
- DOI: 10.1161/CIRCRESAHA.126.329008
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCRESAHA.126.329008) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43023213/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：明确FGF13在心肌缺血再灌注损伤中的作用及其下游机制，并评估小分子FGF13抑制剂的治疗潜力。
- 核心发现/数据：心肌细胞特异性FGF13敲除可减轻、而过表达加重心肌I/R后的心功能障碍和心肌损伤；机制上FGF13通过其N121残基竞争占据PGRN的N263糖基化位点，空间阻断STT3A介导的PGRN糖基化并抑制其分泌。FGF13缺失恢复PGRN糖基化和分泌，旁分泌激活巨噬细胞TNFR2/PI3K/AKT信号，促进M2极化、抑制M1极化及补体激活和炎症；小分子FGF13抑制剂CWD8同样减轻心肌I/R损伤。
- 临床启示：FGF13-PGRN糖基化-PI3K/AKT轴是心肌I/R损伤的潜在

**原文摘要**：Macrophage polarization and complement activation play a pivotal role in the pathogenesis of myocardial ischemia-reperfusion (I/R) injury. FGF13 (fibroblast growth factor 13) is a critical regulator of cardiac homeostasis. However, the role and mechanism of FGF13 in myocardial I/R injury remain unclear. FGF13 expression was detected in cardiomyocytes after hypoxia/reoxygenation and in hearts subjected to myocardial I/R. Cardiomyocyte-specific FGF13 knockout and adeno-associated virus serotype 9-mediated overexpression mice were generated. Transcriptomic and proteomic analyses identified key downstream mechanisms. Immunoprecipitation-mass spectrometry, co-immunoprecipitation, and glycoproteomics identified PGRN (progranulin) as an FGF13-interacting protein and mapped its glycosylation site. Mechanistic studies were performed using Transwell cocultures of neonatal mouse cardiomyocytes and bone marrow-derived macrophages, together with molecular and biochemical approaches. Virtual screening and molecular dynamics simulations identified a small-molecule FGF13 inhibitor. FGF13 was upregulated in cardiomyocytes after myocardial I/R. Cardiomyocyte-specific FGF13 knockout alleviated, whereas overexpression exacerbated, cardiac dysfunction and myocardial injury after I/R. Mechanistically, FGF13, through its asparagine 121 residue, competitively occupied the asparagine 263 glycosylation site of PGRN, sterically preventing STT3A (STT3 oligosaccharyltransferase complex catalytic subunit A)-mediated glycosylation of asparagine 263 and suppressing PGRN secretion. FGF13 depletion restored PGRN glycosylation and secretion, which activated macrophage TNFR2/PI3K/AKT (tumor necrosis factor receptor 2/phosphoinositide 3-kinase/protein kinase B) signaling in a paracrine manner, promoted M2 polarization while suppressing M1 polarization, and inhibited complement activation and inflammation. The small-molecule FGF13 inhibitor CWD8 (1-[2-amino-2-methylpropyl]-5,5-dimethylpiperidin-3-ol) also attenuated myocardial I/R injury. Our findings highlight the critical role of FGF13 in myocardial I/R injury and reveal a competitive mechanism by which FGF13 hinders STT3A-mediated PGRN glycosylation. We identify the PGRN glycosylation-PI3K/AKT axis as the key downstream pathway mediating FGF13-regulated macrophage polarization and complement activation, suggesting that small-molecule FGF13 inhibitors may represent a promising therapeutic strategy for myocardial I/R injury.

- 核心发现/数据：基于PubMed、Web of Science、Embase和ClinicalTrials.gov截至2026年6月的结构化检索及叙述性综合，NAC在多种新生儿脑损伤模型中可能降低氧化应激和神经炎症、保护少突胶质细胞及白质完整性，并改善部分功能结局；部分实验中与亚低温或维生素D联用有额外获益。早期临床/转化研究证实胎盘转运、新生儿药代动力学表征可行性、短期耐受性及可测定的中枢氧化还原反应，但神经保护疗效证据仍有限。其转化受中枢神经系统暴露、中枢氧化还原反应、剂量与治疗时机、性别及与亚低温联用影响，且缺乏明确治疗窗、暴露-反应关系和验证过的药效生物标志物。
- 临床启示：NAC是有前景的候选神经保护剂，但临床疗效和最佳治疗方案尚未确立。后续需开展足够把握度的随机试验，采用PK/PD指导剂量，评估现实治疗窗、与亚低温及性别的交互作用以及长期结局。

**原文摘要**：Neonatal brain injury is a major cause of lifelong neurodevelopmental impairment and arises through overlapping pathogenic mechanisms, particularly oxidative stress and neuroinflammation. N-acetylcysteine (NAC), a cysteine donor and thiol-based redox modulator, has emerged as a promising neuroprotective candidate for the developing brain. We conducted a structured search of PubMed, Web of Science, Embase, and ClinicalTrials.gov for relevant preclinical, translational, and clinical studies published through June 2026. Reference-list screening and citation searching were used to identify additional eligible reports, and the evidence was synthesized narratively. Preclinical studies across models of white matter injury, hypoxic-ischemic injury, infection-sensitized hypoxic-ischemic injury, necrotizing enterocolitis-associated brain injury, and meningitis suggest that NAC may reduce oxidative stress and neuroinflammation, preserve oligodendrocytes and white matter integrity, and improve selected functional outcomes. In some experimental settings, NAC in combination with therapeutic hypothermia or vitamin D was associated with additional benefit. Early clinical and translational studies provide evidence of placental transfer, the feasibility of pharmacokinetic characterization in neonates, short-term tolerability, and measurable central redox responses; however, evidence of neuroprotective efficacy remains limited. The clinical translation of NAC is likely to be highly context-dependent and influenced by central nervous system (CNS) exposure and central redox response, dose and treatment timing, sex, and coadministration with therapeutic hypothermia. Key barriers include an undefined clinically relevant therapeutic window, uncertain exposure-response relationships, the absence of validated pharmacodynamic biomarkers, and limited evidence regarding optimal dosing, long-term efficacy, and safety. Mechanistic, preclinical, and early translational evidence supports further evaluation of NAC as a candidate neuroprotective therapy, but its clinical efficacy and optimal treatment regimen have not been established. Adequately powered randomized trials should evaluate pharmacokinetic/ pharmacodynamic (PK/PD)-informed dosing, clinically realistic therapeutic windows, potential interactions with therapeutic hypothermia and sex, and long-term outcomes.
