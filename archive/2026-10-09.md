## Comparative Analysis of Cytoskeletal Proteins and the Sarcoglycan Complex in the Cremaster Muscle of Patients with Retractile and Undescended Testis.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 03:39
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 意大利 · University of Messina
- 通讯作者: Arena S (salarena@unime.it)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163529
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163529) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42849787/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估并量化未降睾丸、回缩性睾丸及对照组儿童提睾肌中肌聚糖复合物及锚定蛋白 vinculin、talin 的表达差异。
- 核心发现/数据：免疫荧光显示 α、β、γ、δ、ε-sarcoglycan 及 talin、vinculin 表达趋势均为回缩性睾丸 > 对照组 > 未降睾丸，组间差异均有统计学意义

**原文摘要**：The cremaster muscle plays a crucial role in testicular mobility and thermoregulation. Structural and functional alterations of this muscle have been implicated in the pathophysiology of undescended testis. Previous studies have demonstrated a significant impairment of the cytoskeleton in cremasteric smooth muscle cells in patients with cryptorchidism. This alteration may explain the muscular dysfunction involved in testicular descent. The aim of this study was to assess and quantify the expression of sarcoglycans, as well as the anchoring proteins vinculin and talin, in the cremaster muscle of children with undescended testis. Cremaster muscle samples were obtained from pediatric patients undergoing surgery for undescended testis, retractile testis, or inguinal canal pathologies (control group). Immunofluorescence analysis was used to evaluate the expression of alpha-, beta-, gamma-, delta-, and epsilon-sarcoglycans, as well as talin and vinculin. Signal intensity was quantified and compared among the groups. All analyzed markers showed significantly higher expression in the retractile group, followed by the control group and, lastly, the undescended testis group. The differences among groups were statistically significant (p<0.05), with a consistent trend: retractile > control > undescended. The molecular profile of the cremaster muscle in patients with undescended testis shows poorer structural preservation compared to the other groups, with reduced expression of sarcolemmal and anchoring proteins relative to the retractile and control groups. This molecular pattern suggests a structural and functional impairment of the cremaster muscle, rendering it non-retractile. These findings support the concept that undescended testis is not merely an anatomical variant or a retractile testis awaiting descent, but rather a condition characterized by more profound structural alterations.

## Does it Fit? Testing a Novel Pediatric Surgical Risk Calculator in Africa.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 03:39
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University of California San Francisco
- 通讯作者: Ameh E
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163524
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163524) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42849786/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：在非洲独立队列中外部验证先前基于中低收入国家数据开发的儿科手术风险计算器，并评估其区分度、校准和风险分层临床适用性。
- 核心发现/数据：在埃塞俄比亚和尼日利亚4家三级医院前瞻性纳入1,670例儿科手术患者，住院死亡率为6.8%；模型总体区分度AUC为0.84，优于单独ASA分级（AUC=0.77），普通儿科手术AUC=0.83、急诊AUC=0.89，但新生儿AUC仅0.64。模型存在系统性校准偏差，经logistic重校准后改善；风险分层中低危和高危似然比分别为0.1和5.4，但66.5%患者落入中间风险组（LR=1.0），对决策帮助有限。
- 临床启示：该计算器可用于非洲儿科手术的风险沟通、临床决策和资源优先排序，但新生儿需纳入额外危险因素或开发单独模型，中间风险组也需进一步优化。

**原文摘要**：Low- and middle-income countries (LMICs), particularly Sub-Saharan Africa, experience a high burden of surgical disease and a disproportionately high postoperative morbidity and mortality. Although machine learning based pediatric surgical risk calculators (SRCs) have been developed to support clinical decision making, most are derived from high-income settings and may not generalize well to LMIC populations. We previously developed a pediatric surgical risk calculator using multinational LMIC data; however, its performance in independent African cohorts remains unknown. We conducted an external validation study of a previously developed pediatric surgical risk calculator using prospective data from children undergoing surgery at four tertiary referral hospitals in Ethiopia and Nigeria between July 2024 and February 2025. Model performance was assessed by evaluating discrimination (area under the receiver operating characteristic curve) and calibration (slope and intercept). Predicted risk was stratified into low, intermediate, and high-risk categories to enhance clinical applicability. Model recalibration was performed to optimize performance in the external cohort. A total of 1,670 pediatric surgical patients were included from the four hospitals. In-hospital mortality was 6.8%. Mortality varied significantly by site, age, weight, sex, prematurity, preoperative sepsis, transfusion, supplemental oxygen use, antibiotic use, ASA class, type of surgery (emergent vs elective), need of ICU postoperatively and reoperation. The risk prediction model had good discrimination overall with an area under the curve (AUC) of 0.84, outperforming ASA classification alone (AUC = 0.77). Discrimination remained high for general pediatric surgery cases (AUC=0.83) and was highest for emergency cases (AUC = 0.89), but was low in neonates (AUC = 0.64). The model demonstrated systematic miscalibration, which improved following logistic calibration. Patients were stratified into meaningful risk categories, which reliably distinguishes between low-and-hgih risk patients (LR=0.1 and LR=5.4 respectively). However, 66.5% fall in the intermediate risk group which is not useful in decision making (LR=1.0). The pediatric surgical risk calculator demonstrated good performance in the African context, with improved clinical utility following recalibration and risk stratification. Reduced performance in neonates suggests the need to incorporate additional risk factors and potentially separate models for this subgroup. Future efforts should focus on supporting the use of the SRC in risk communication, clinical decision making, and resource prioritization.

- 临床启示：机器人辅助手术对选择性儿科输卵管旁囊肿患者安全、可行，兼具精准与微创优势，并有利于保留附件解剖和潜在生育力保护。但长期生殖结局证据缺乏，仍需前瞻性研究验证。

**原文摘要**：Paratubal and paraovarian cysts are benign lesions located between the ovary and fallopian tube, often of mesothelial origin. Although typically asymptomatic, larger cysts may cause pelvic pain or complications requiring surgery. Management depends on cyst size, symptoms, and patient age, with fertility preservation essential in pediatric populations. This multicenter study evaluates robotic-assisted excision of paratubal cysts in children, alongside a literature review. We performed a retrospective analysis of pediatric patients undergoing robot-assisted paratubal cystectomy at five European pediatric surgery centers. Data included demographics, surgical details, hospitalization, and follow-up. A systematic review following PRISMA guidelines was conducted using Embase, Scopus, PubMed, and Web of Science. Median hospital stay was 2 days (range 1-4), with no complications at median follow-up of 19 months. The review identified 764 records; four studies were included, reporting nine pediatric cases treated robotically without complications. Robotic-assisted surgery appears safe and feasible for paratubal cyst excision in selected pediatric patients, offering precision and minimally invasive access, with favorable perioperative outcomes and preservation of adnexal anatomy. It may support fertility preservation, however evidence regarding long-term reproductive outcomes are lacking and further prospective studies are required.

- 临床启示：现有证据不能确立单期修复与伤口并发症的因果关系，粪便转流及分期决策应个体化，并等待前瞻性比较研究进一步验证。

**原文摘要**：The optimal repair strategy for rectovestibular fistula remains uncertain. We compared single-stage with staged repair and estimated complication and functional-outcome rates across operative techniques. PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus, and Web of Science were searched from inception through May 20, 2026. Studies of girls with congenital anorectal-malformation-associated rectovestibular fistula undergoing definitive repair were eligible. Both comparisons were unadjusted and nonrandomized. No clear differences were found for anal stenosis, constipation, soiling, or voluntary bowel movements. Sensitivity analyses gave consistent results. Single-stage repair was associated with a higher reported risk of wound infection, but this unadjusted, nonrandomized association is susceptible to confounding by indication and cannot establish causality; certainty was low. Evidence was insufficient to determine whether staging affects anal stenosis or age-eligible bowel outcomes. Decisions regarding fecal diversion should therefore remain individualized pending prospective comparative evidence. CRD420261436081.

This study examines the clinical and genetic spectrum of PCD in a South Asian cohort, focusing on genotype-phenotype correlations, protein interaction effects of pathogenic variants, and the role of bioinformatics in improving molecular diagnosis. We conducted a retrospective study of suspected PCD cases at a tertiary referral centre in India from 2017 to 2025, reviewing clinical, laboratory, imaging, and exome sequencing data. Protein-protein interaction (PPI) and Gene Ontology (GO) enrichment analyses were performed to assess functional convergence. Out of 41 individuals suspected of having PCD, 31 were molecularly confirmed. Most cases presented in childhood, while a subset presented in adulthood, often primarily with infertility. Pathogenic or likely pathogenic variants were identified in 18 genes associated with PCD, with the most common being DNAH11, DNAH9, DNAH5, and CCDC40; homozygous variants were predominant. Structural modelling indicated the preservation of global protein folds, with localised conformational deviations noted particularly in DNAH5 and DNAH11 . PPI network analysis revealed significant interaction enrichment ( P < 1 × 10 -16 ). GO analysis showed strong enrichment of cilia-related biological processes, including microtubule-based processes (-log 10 FDR = 22.96) and microtubule bundle formation (-log 10 FDR = 27.54). This study enhances understanding of PCD variants in the Indian population and highlights the value of combining clinical evaluations with genomic and structural analyses. PPI and GO analyses reveal disrupted ciliary pathways linked to PCD.
