## PGT-A, the theory versus the evidence: a systematic review on efficacy, safety, and cost-effectiveness.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:32
- 期刊: Hum Reprod Update · 中科院医学1区(妇产科学1区/生殖生物学1区) · IF 20.1 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 瑞典 · Karolinska Institutet
- 通讯作者: Johansson S
- DOI: 10.1093/humupd/dmag029
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1093/humupd/dmag029) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42789480/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统评价PGT-A在IVF/ICSI中是否提高总体或特定人群的活产率，并评估其安全性及成本效果。

**原文摘要**：Preimplantation genetic testing for aneuploidy (PGT-A) aims to increase live birth rate (LBR) after IVF/ICSI. While observational studies and most systematic reviews have indicated benefits with increased LBRs and reduced miscarriage rates, results from most randomised controlled trials (RCTs) have failed to show any increase in LBR when calculated per randomised patient. According to international, professional societies, PGT-A is currently not recommended for routine clinical use, still the use of PGT-A is increasing. The purpose of this systematic review was to evaluate if PGT-A results in increased LBR in general or in specific patient groups. Efficacy is summarised in meta-analyses, and certainty of evidence was assessed. It also includes systematic reviews on possible complications for children and mothers and a health economic analysis. A systematic search was conducted from inception to November 2024 in MEDLINE, Embase, CINAHL, Cochrane Library, PsycINFO, PubMed, and Scopus. Only RCTs where the PGT-A technique analysed all chromosomes were selected for efficacy. Concerning safety, observational studies were also included. Risk of bias (RoB) was assessed using Cochrane tools. Results from meta-analyses were presented as odds ratio (OR) and risk difference (RD) with 95% CI. Certainty of evidence was assessed using the Grading of Recommendations, Assessment, Development and Evaluation (GRADE) system. The search for efficacy and complications resulted in 9673 references. We included seven RCTs on efficacy, 55 studies on complications, and two studies on health economics. The primary outcome, live birth per randomised woman, assessed both for the first planned embryo transfer (ET) and cumulatively per oocyte retrieval, was comparable between IVF with PGT-A and IVF without PGT-A (OR 1.16; 95% CI 0.94-1.45 and OR 0.87; 95% CI 0.64-1.19) (low certainty of evidence), also for women over 35 years. The secondary outcome, live birth per ET, was higher after PGT-A (low certainty of evidence). It was not possible to evaluate if miscarriage rate is affected by PGT-A since the certainty of evidence was very low. Only two studies evaluated time to pregnancy. None of them showed a shorter time to live birth by use of PGT-A. There was no indication of an increase in complications for the child or the pregnant woman after PGT when comparing IVF with or without embryo biopsy using PGT-A, PGT-M, or PGT-SR. In a Swedish setting, adding PGT-A to IVF almost doubles the cost of an IVF treatment without any increase in live births. The strength of this report is that the main outcome is live birth per randomized woman. Many observational studies, earlier systematic reviews/meta-analyses, as well as two RCTs using all-chromosome PGT-A, were excluded from this systematic review; they only report live birth for those women who received an ET, which gives an incorrect and too positive view. No increase in complications for the child or the pregnant woman after PGT was observed; thus, embryo biopsy seems to be safe. Since our systematic review does not show any clear patient benefit, and PGT-A almost doubles the cost of an IVF treatment, PGT-A is currently not considered ethically justifiable. ## Cervical aortic arch with severe left ventricular dysfunction in a neonate: successful repair using whole-body perfusion.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:32
- 期刊: Multimed Man Cardiothorac Surg
- 通讯作者: Sivalingam S
- DOI: 10.1510/mmcts.2026.038
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1510/mmcts.2026.038) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42789258/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告1例右侧颈主动脉弓合并主动脉缩窄、对侧胸降主动脉及严重左心室功能减退新生儿，采用全身灌注成功修复的病例。
- 核心发现/数据：该患儿为罕见先天性主动脉弓异常，表现为右位颈主动脉弓、主动脉缩窄、对侧胸降主动脉和严重左心室功能减退。手术在全身灌注下完成主动脉弓修复及主动脉去交叉，通过同时插管主动脉弓和胸降主动脉实现，并成功修复。
- 临床启示：对复杂颈主动脉弓合并严重左心室功能不全的新生儿，全身灌注下同时插管主动脉弓和胸降主动脉的修复策略可行，可能为高危患儿提供有效手术选择。

**原文摘要**：The cervical aortic arch, first described by Reid, is a rare congenital anomaly of the aortic arch. We report the surgical management of a neonate with a right cervical arch with coarctation, a contralateral descending thoracic aorta, and severely reduced left ventricular function. Arch repair with aortic uncrossing was performed under whole-body perfusion, achieved by simultaneously cannulating the aortic arch and the descending thoracic aorta.

## Appendix tip biopsy for Total colonic aganglionosis: technical aspects and diagnostic accuracy in a prospective cohort of 107 pediatric patients.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:32
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Rostock · University Medicine Rostock
- 通讯作者: Lindert J (judith.lindert@med.uni-rostock.de)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06656-4
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06656-4) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42789101/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：前瞻性评估阑尾尖端活检在疑似全结肠无神经节细胞症（TCA）患儿中的诊断准确性与技术适用性。
- 核心发现/数据：在107例患儿中，TCA组19例阑尾尖端均未见神经节细胞；非全结肠型Hirschsprung病组24/25（96%）和对照组61/63（96.8%）可见神经节细胞。阑尾尖端活检诊断TCA的敏感性100%、特异性96.6%、阳性预测值86.4%、阴性预测值100%、总体准确率97.2%；仅3例因取样不足或严重炎症组织破坏而无法判读。
- 临床启示：阑尾尖端活检是一种准确且保留组织的辅助诊断方法，可快速诊断疑似TCA并保留近端阑尾以备未来重建手术，建议纳入疑似TCA患儿的诊断流程。

**原文摘要**：The diagnosis of Total colonic aganglionosis (TCA) remains challenging, frequently resulting in delayed treatment and multiple surgical procedures. Appendix biopsy has been proposed as a diagnostic technique. Concerns exist regarding reliability. This study evaluated the diagnostic accuracy and technical applicability of appendix tip biopsy in children with suspected TCA. A prospective observational study was conducted in 107 consecutive children (≤ 15 years) undergoing abdominal surgery with appendix tip biopsy between October 2022 and September 2024. Patients were allocated to three groups: Hirschsprung disease without TCA (n = 25), TCA (n = 19), and non-Hirschsprung controls (n = 63). Histopathological examination assessed the presence or absence of ganglion cells at the tips of the appendix. Ganglion cells were identified in 24/25 (96%) patients with Hirschsprung disease not involving the entire colon and in 61/63 (96.8%) controls. Ganglion cells were absent in all patients with TCA. Three inconclusive specimens resulted from inadequate sampling or very advanced inflammatory tissue destruction. Appendix tip biopsy achieved a sensitivity of 100%, specificity of 96.6%, positive predictive value of 86.4%, negative predictive value of 100%, and an overall diagnostic accuracy of 97.2%. Assessment was unaffected by patient age, including neonates, and generally possible in appendicitis when adequate full-thickness biopsy was obtained. Appendix tip biopsy is an accurate and tissue-sparing adjunct for diagnosing total colonic aganglionosis. When combined with appropriate histopathological assessment, it provides rapid diagnostic information while preserving the proximal appendix for potential future reconstructive procedures. These findings support its incorporation into the diagnostic pathway for children with suspected TCA.

## Early versus Late Initiation of Enteral Feeding in Premature Infants (28-34 Weeks GA) with Growth Restriction and Absent or Reversed End-Diastolic Flow on Umbilical Artery Doppler: A Randomised Controlled Trial.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:32
- 期刊: Indian J Pediatr · 中科院医学4区(儿科4区) · IF 2.4 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 印度 · All India Institute of Medical Sciences
- 通讯作者: Nangia S (drsnangia@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s12098-026-06416-1
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12098-026-06416-1) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42789183/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：在28–34周胎龄、生长受限且产前脐动脉多普勒显示舒张末期血流缺失或反向的早产儿中，比较生后6小时早期肠内喂养与48小时晚期启动喂养对达全肠内喂养时间及不良结局的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：87例随机分组（早期喂养44例，晚期喂养43例）。早期喂养组达全肠内喂养中位时间更短（6天 vs 8天），晚发型脓毒症发生率更低（6.8% vs 27.9%）。两组喂养不耐受（20.9% vs 25.6%）、NEC≥2b期（0 vs 4.6%）、死亡（6.8% vs 16.3%）及住院时间无统计学差异。
- 临床启示：对该高危早产儿人群，在严密监测下于生后6小时启动并逐步加量肠内喂养可更早达全肠内喂养，且不增加NEC等不良结局，或可优先考虑早期喂养策略。

**原文摘要**：The authors conducted an open-label RCT in growth-restricted preterm infants (28-34 wk) with Absent or Reversed end-diastolic flow on Antenatal Umbilical-Artery Doppler (AREDF) to compare early enteral feeding at 6 h (EIEF) with initiation at 48 h (LIEF). Eighty-seven infants were randomised (EIEF 44, LIEF 43). Feeds were advanced daily at 20, 40, 60, 90, 120, and 150 mL/kg/d, respectively, from the day of commencement of feeds. The primary outcome was time to full feeds; secondary outcomes included feed-intolerance (FI), NEC ≥ stage 2b, late-onset sepsis, mortality, and hospital stay. EIEF achieved full feeds earlier than LIEF (median 6 vs. 8 d). Rates of FI (20.9% vs. 25.6%), NEC (0 vs. 4.6%), mortality (6.8% vs. 16.3%), and hospital stay were not statistically different. Late-onset sepsis was lower with EIEF (6.8% vs. 27.9%). Early feeds from 6 h, with careful monitoring, enabled full feeds by day 6 without increasing adverse outcomes. The trial was registered prospectively at CTRI.org on 01/03/2019 (CTRI No. CTRI/2019/03/017899).
