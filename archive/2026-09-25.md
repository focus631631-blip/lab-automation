## Clinical Management and Outcomes of Neonatal Rhinitis Nasal Obstruction: A Scoping Review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:28
- 期刊: Otolaryngol Head Neck Surg
- 国家/单位: 加拿大 · Université de Sherbrooke
- 通讯作者: Bergeron M
- DOI: 10.1002/ohn.70458
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/ohn.70458) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42779522/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：综述新生儿及≤8月龄婴儿鼻炎所致鼻塞的病因、诊断、治疗和结局，并识别当前证据缺口。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入5项关键研究，涵盖回顾性病历审查、病例系列和叙述性报告，检索时间为1980年1月至2025年9月。新生儿鼻炎常表现为黏液性流涕、鼻塞、鼾声/喘鸣、喂养不良和呼吸窘迫，且多为无发热；诊断常用纤维内镜和CT排除后鼻孔闭锁、梨状孔狭窄等。多数病例采用生理盐水冲洗、皮质类固醇滴鼻（如地塞米松）和鼻咽气道等保守治疗有效，手术或侵入性干预仅用于严重或保守无效者。
- 临床启示：对无发热、以鼻塞和喂养/呼吸问题为主的新生儿，应警惕鼻炎，并在排除结构性病因后优先尝试保守治疗。现有证据有限，需进一步研究以制定标准化诊疗方案。

**原文摘要**：Neonatal rhinitis represents an underdiagnosed yet potentially serious condition in newborns. Standardized diagnostic and treatment protocols remain lacking. The objective is to review the current literature on the etiology, diagnosis, and management of neonatal rhinitis in infants ≤8 months of age. PubMed, Medline, Embase, Google Scholar and Sofia (January 1980-September 2025). A scoping review was conducted in accordance with PRISMA-ScR guidelines. The inclusion criteria targeted studies involving neonates with nasal obstruction caused by mucosal edema or inflammatory conditions. Data were extracted regarding clinical presentation, diagnostic approaches, therapeutic strategies, and outcomes. Five key studies were found, including retrospective chart reviews, case series, and narrative reports. Across the studies, neonatal rhinitis was consistently characterized by mucoid rhinorrhea, nasal congestion, stertor, poor feeding, and respiratory distress, often in afebrile newborns. Standard diagnostic tools included fibre-optic endoscopy and CT imaging to exclude choanal atresia, pyriform aperture stenosis, and other etiologies. Conservative treatment approaches, such as saline irrigation, corticosteroid nasal drops (eg, dexamethasone), and nasopharyngeal airway placement, were favoured and proved efficient in most cases. Surgical intervention and more invasive interventions were reserved for severe or nonresponsive cases. Neonatal rhinitis is a clinically significant yet undercharacterized condition. Although the available evidence is limited, it suggests that conservative management, including topical corticosteroids and mechanical airway support, is commonly used and associated with favourable outcomes. This scoping review identifies gaps and underscores the need for research to better define and guide the management of this condition.
