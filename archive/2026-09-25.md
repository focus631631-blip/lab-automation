**中文总结**：- 研究目的：综述新生儿及≤8月龄婴儿鼻炎所致鼻塞的病因、诊断、治疗和结局，并识别当前证据缺口。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入5项关键研究，涵盖回顾性病历审查、病例系列和叙述性报告，检索时间为1980年1月至2025年9月。新生儿鼻炎常表现为黏液性流涕、鼻塞、鼾声/喘鸣、喂养不良和呼吸窘迫，且多为无发热；诊断常用纤维内镜和CT排除后鼻孔闭锁、梨状孔狭窄等。多数病例采用生理盐水冲洗、皮质类固醇滴鼻（如地塞米松）和鼻咽气道等保守治疗有效，手术或侵入性干预仅用于严重或保守无效者。

**原文摘要**：Neonatal rhinitis represents an underdiagnosed yet potentially serious condition in newborns. Standardized diagnostic and treatment protocols remain lacking. The objective is to review the current literature on the etiology, diagnosis, and management of neonatal rhinitis in infants ≤8 months of age. PubMed, Medline, Embase, Google Scholar and Sofia (January 1980-September 2025). A scoping review was conducted in accordance with PRISMA-ScR guidelines. The inclusion criteria targeted studies involving neonates with nasal obstruction caused by mucosal edema or inflammatory conditions. Data were extracted regarding clinical presentation, diagnostic approaches, therapeutic strategies, and outcomes. Five key studies were found, including retrospective chart reviews, case series, and narrative reports. Across the studies, neonatal rhinitis was consistently characterized by mucoid rhinorrhea, nasal congestion, stertor, poor feeding, and respiratory distress, often in afebrile newborns. Surgical intervention and more invasive interventions were reserved for severe or nonresponsive cases. Neonatal rhinitis is a clinically significant yet undercharacterized condition. Although the available evidence is limited, it suggests that conservative management, including topical corticosteroids and mechanical airway support, is commonly used and associated with favourable outcomes. This scoping review identifies gaps and underscores the need for research to better define and guide the management of this condition.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估儿童腹腔镜腹股沟疝修补术中区域麻醉技术应用的相关文献。
- 核心发现/数据：该系统综述检索1993—2025年6个数据库，共3882篇文献，最终纳入8项研究、770例患者；涉及腹直肌鞘阻滞、局部浸润、腰方肌阻滞、腹横肌平面阻滞、鞘内阻滞、腹壁神经阻滞及骶管阻滞等技术。4项研究显示区域麻醉较对照可降低疼痛评分和镇痛药消耗，另4项比较两种区域麻醉方式的研究结果不一，且围术期疼痛评估指标异质性较大。
- 临床启示：区域麻醉较单纯全身麻醉可提供更优镇痛，但目前尚无单一技术可被推荐为最优方案；临床仍需进一步研究以建立标准化的区域麻醉方案。

**原文摘要**：Regional anaesthetic modalities are employed to mitigate intraoperative and postoperative pain in children undergoing laparoscopic inguinal hernia repair (LIHR), forming an important component of multimodal perioperative analgesia. This review aims to evaluate the literature on regional anaesthetic use in LIHR in children. A systematic review was conducted (1993-2025) using PRISMA guidelines. Selected studies included randomised controlled trials and cohort studies that evaluated regional anaesthetic techniques in LIHR in patients ≤16 years. The ROBINS-I (Risk of Bias in Non-randomised Studies of Interventions) and RoB 2 (Cochrane Risk-of-Bias tool for randomized trials) tools assessed the quality of included studies. The study was prospectively registered with PROSPERO (CRD420251072969). 6 databases were searched, identifying 3882 articles. 8 studies with 770 patients were included. Utilised regional anaesthetic techniques included: rectus sheath block (RSB), local infiltration (LAI), quadratus lumborum block, transversus abdominis plane block (TAPB), intrathecal block and abdominal wall nerve block (AWNB). Four studies compared regional anaesthesia with controls, finding that RSB, LAI, caudal block, intrathecal anaesthesia, TAPB, and AWNB, resulted in lower pain scores and analgesic consumption than controls. The remaining four studies compared two regional anaesthetic modalities, with mixed results. Several regional techniques have been described for LIHR, with considerable heterogeneity in the outcomes used to assess perioperative pain. Evidence from this review suggests that regional anaesthetic modalities provide superior analgesia compared to general anaesthesia alone. However, no single technique can presently be recommended as superior. Further research is required to establish standardised approaches to regional anaesthesia in LIHR.
