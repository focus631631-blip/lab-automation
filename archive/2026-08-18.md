## Conventional supine delivery position versus free-position delivery guidance intervention on delivery outcomes, pain, and maternal-neonatal outcomes in primiparous women.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 00:38
- 期刊: Afr J Reprod Health · 中科院医学4区(公共卫生、环境卫生与职业卫生4区) · IF 1.0
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Shanxi Provincial People's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Wang Z
- DOI: 10.29063/ajrh2026/v30i15.3
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.29063/ajrh2026/v30i15.3) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42605734/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估自由体位分娩指导联合产前干预对初产妇分娩结局、疼痛及母婴结局的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：研究组（n=40）的产程时间和总产程时间均显著短于对照组（P<0.05），疼痛评分显著更低（P<0.05）；研究组自然分娩率显著更高、剖宫产率显著更低（P<0.05），不良母婴结局发生率显著低于对照组（P<0.05）。
- 临床启示：自由体位分娩指导联合产前干预可有效改善初产妇的分娩结局，减轻产痛并降低母婴不良结局风险，值得临床推广。

**原文摘要**：This randomized comparative study investigated the effects of free-position delivery guidance combined with prenatal intervention on pain levels, delivery outcomes, and maternal-neonatal outcomes in primiparous women. A total of 80 primiparous women admitted to the hospital between April 2023 and October 2025 were included in the study. Participants were randomly divided into two groups of 40 each using a random number table. The control group received conventional supine delivery with basic guidance, while the study group received free position delivery guidance combined with prenatal intervention. Labor duration, pain scores, delivery outcomes, and the incidence of adverse maternal-neonatal outcomes were compared between the two groups. The study group showed significantly shorter labor duration and total labor time compared to the control group (P<0.05). The study group also demonstrated significantly lower pain scores (P<0.05). The study group exhibited a significantly higher rate of natural delivery and a significantly lower rate of cesarean section compared to the control group (P<0.05). The incidence of adverse maternal-infant outcomes in the study group was significantly lower than that in the control group (P<0.05). The combination of free-position delivery guidance and prenatal intervention can effectively improve delivery outcomes, alleviate labor pain, and reduce adverse maternal-neonatal outcomes in primiparous women. ## Does laparoscopic-assisted transanal endorectal pull-through improve outcomes compared with transanal endorectal pull-through in Hirschsprung disease? A systematic review and meta-analysis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 00:38
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Madhya Pradesh · Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose Medical College
- 通讯作者: Sisodiya RS (rssisodiyanscbpediasurgery@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06577-2
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06577-2) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42606588/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：比较经肛门内括约肌切除术（TERPT）与腹腔镜辅助经肛门内括约肌切除术（LA-TERPT）在先天性巨结肠患儿中的术后并发症及长期功能结局是否存在优劣差异。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入23项研究、1668例患儿（TERPT 980例，LA-TERPT 688例）。两组在便秘（log RR=0.11）、污便（log RR=0.05）、大便失禁（log RR=0.05）、HAEC（log RR=0.09）、BFS>17（log RR=-0.08）、吻合口狭窄（log RR=0.28）和吻合口漏（log RR=0.06）方面均无显著差异；TERPT的手术时间显著更短（MD=-43.25分钟），术后住院时间相当（MD=0.20天）。
- 临床启示：目前证据不支持两种术式在功能结局上孰优孰劣，手术方式选择应依据疾病特点、技术因素和外科医生经验，需谨慎解读现有低至中等确定性证据。

**原文摘要**：TERPT and LA-TERPT are widely used minimally invasive procedures for HD treatment. However, the comparative superiority of either technique in terms of postoperative complications and long-term functional outcomes remains uncertain. An SRMA was conducted in accordance with PRISMA 2020 guidelines and prospectively registered in PROSPERO (CRD420261378340). PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar, and trial registries were searched from inception to May 3, 2026. Comparative studies evaluating TERPT and LA-TERPT in children with HD were included. The primary outcomes were constipation, soiling, fecal incontinence, BFS, HAEC, anastomotic stricture, and anastomotic leak. Secondary outcomes included operative time and hospital stay. A random-effects meta-analysis was performed using log risk ratios (RRs) or mean differences (MDs) with 95% confidence intervals (CIs). Twenty-three studies involving 1,668 children with HD were included, comprising 980 TERPT and 688 LA-TERPT procedures. No significant differences were observed between TERPT and LA-TERPT for constipation (log RR = 0.11), soiling (log RR = 0.05), fecal incontinence (log RR = 0.05), HAEC (log RR = 0.09), BFS > 17 (log RR = - 0.08), anastomotic stricture (log RR = 0.28), or anastomotic leak (log RR = 0.06). TERPT was associated with a significantly shorter operative time (MD = -43.25 min). Post-op hospital stay was comparable between groups (MD = 0.20 days). Most outcomes showed low heterogeneity, whereas operative time and hospital stay showed substantial heterogeneity. This SRMA found no clear evidence that either TERPT or LA-TERPT yields superior postoperative or functional outcomes in children with RSHD. However, the available evidence is largely observational and has low to moderate certainty, so it should be interpreted cautiously. Until higher-quality evidence becomes available, the choice of surgical approach should be guided by disease characteristics, technical considerations, and surgeon expertise.

## Rates of post-operative complications in Hirschsprung Disease: A study by the Canadian Consortium for Research in Pediatric Surgery (CanCORPS) with suggested management-based stricture classification.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:33
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: N6A 5W9 · Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Bütter A (Andreana.Butter@lhsc.on.ca)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163389
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163389) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42607867/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：基于加拿大儿童医院多中心数据，评估先天性巨结肠术后并发症发生率，并提出一种基于管理策略的吻合口狭窄分类。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入227例患儿，中位诊断年龄1周，81.0%为男性，中位拖出术年龄2个月，最常见术式为腹腔镜（63.2%）。再手术率为31.3%，再入院率为41.4%（最常见原因为HAEC，占30.0%）；术后12周狭窄发生率为6.2%，吻合口漏为0.9%，HAEC为17.2%。手术在1月龄前或后实施，并发症发生率无显著差异。
- 临床启示：尽管总体并发症率较低，但再手术和再入院负担显著，提示需加强术后随访和HAEC管理；提出的管理导向性狭窄分类有助于风险分层和个体化治疗决策。

**原文摘要**：Morbidity and mortality following surgery for Hirschsprung disease (HD) vary widely in the literature and largely emanate from single center studies. We conducted a population-based study of postoperative complication rates in Canadian children. A multi-centre, retrospective review of children with HD from six Canadian children's hospitals was performed. Data was collected between 2010 and 2022. Patients were included if they had at least six months of follow-up. The study cohort included 227 patients. The median age at diagnosis was 1 week (interquartile range [IQR]:1-2) and 81.0% were male. The median age at time of pull-through was two months (IQR:1-7). The most common surgical approach was laparoscopic (63.2%), with the remaining procedures being transanal (27.3%), and open (9.5%). The rate of reoperation was 31.3%, of which 63.4% were minor procedures and 36.6% were major procedures. The rate of readmission was 41.4%, with the most common reason being Hirschsprung-associated enterocolitis (HAEC) (30.0%). Surgery-specific complications occurred in 15.0% of patients. These included stricture at 12-weeks (6.2%), anastomotic leak (0.9%), and HAEC (17.2%). Complication rates did not differ between patients who underwent surgery before or after one month of age. In this Canadian population-based study, significant rates of reoperation and readmission were found, despite overall low rates of complications (HAEC, stricture and leak). A new management-based classification of stricture is proposed to aid in stratification of patients. Prospective studies will aim to validate this approach and provide more meaningful rates of postoperative complications following pull-through for HD.

## Defining the force-injury profile for retrieval of esophageal button batteries: an ex vivo porcine model.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:33
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Medical University of South Carolina
- 通讯作者: Frederick AB (frederia@musc.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163391
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163391) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42607865/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过离体猪食管模型量化纽扣电池取出过程中的力-损伤关系。
- 核心发现/数据：黏膜损伤在接触21分钟内开始；离体狭窄模型中，30、60、120分钟时致伤力分别为8.99±3.30、4.99±0.53、1.18±0.51磅，均低于正常食管的11.68±1.48磅（p<.001）；而取出电池所需力仅为0.14-0.18磅。
- 临床启示：即使食管已发生损伤，取出电池所需力仍远低于致伤力，存在较大安全余量，支持及时内镜取出。

**原文摘要**：Treatment of button battery (BB) ingestion requires rapid removal to limit injury. Preliminary data demonstrate the rapidity with which injury occurs, but nevertheless rapid extraction is often delayed by transfer. Uncertainty regarding the concern for perforation and mechanical consequences of acute esophageal injury during retrieval remain poorly defined and contribute to low rates of early intervention. We developed a BB extraction model in cadaveric porcine esophagus to measure the force-injury relationship. Our model was developed to address 3 questions: 1) following BB ingestion, what is the time to injury? 2) what BB removal force is needed for esophageal injury? 3) in injured esophagus, what force is needed to remove a BB? Descriptive statistics were tabulated, and continuous variables were compared with t-tests. Mucosal injury began within 21 minutes of BB exposure. In an ex vivo stricture model, injury occurred with 8.99 ± 3.30 lbs of force at 30 minutes, 4.99 ± 0.53 lbs at 60 minutes and 1.18 ± 0.51 lbs at 120 minutes, which was less than the 11.68 ± 1.48 lbs required to injure native esophagus (p<.001). At matching time points, it took between 0.14 to 0.18 lbs of force for BB extraction. Using an ex vivo BB extraction model, we define the force-injury profile after esophageal BB exposure. Notably, force required to injure esophagus after 1 hour of exposure is half that required in native esophagus, but still 30 times more than the maximal force to extract a BB. This difference suggests a robust factor of safety present when removing esophageal foreign bodies.

## Non-Operative Management of Pediatric Empyema.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:33

- 国家/单位: 美国
- 通讯作者: Alturki N (nfalturki@cmh.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163372

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估进一步完善的儿童脓胸非手术方案（胸腔引流管+组织型纤溶酶原激活剂）的疗效，尤其是后续需要胸腔镜手术的比例。
- 核心发现/数据：共70例患儿纳入分析，中位年龄4.6岁，胸管留置中位时间5天，住院中位时间8天；所有患儿均接受胸膜腔内纤溶治疗，无一例后续需要VATS。21%的患儿需要再次纤溶治疗，多因素分析显示一疗程tPA后持续需氧是需再次治疗的独立预测因素（OR 5.819，95% CI 1.230-27.521，p=0.026）；30天内无再入院。
- 临床启示：标准化、以反复胸膜腔内纤溶为核心的方案可完全避免儿童脓胸的手术干预，持续需氧患儿需警惕需要重复纤溶治疗。

**原文摘要**：Primary fibrinolysis is the standard treatment for pediatric empyema at our institution. A prior randomized trial demonstrated no clinical benefit of video-assisted thoracoscopic surgery (VATS) compared with fibrinolysis. After garnering more experience, subsequent protocol refinement reduced progression to VATS to 4.5%. This study aims to report outcomes following further maturation of our protocol. A retrospective review was performed of patients ≤18 years treated for empyema between March 1, 2019 and July 1, 2025. The protocol includes placement of a ≤12Fr thoracostomy tube and administration of three daily doses of tissue plasminogen activator (tPA), 4 mg in 40 mL normal saline. Patients with immunocompromised status, alternative indications for thoracostomy, or contraindications to VATS at presentation were excluded. The primary outcome was progression to VATS. Secondary outcomes included examining potential factors associated with repeat fibrinolysis. 70 patients met inclusion criteria. Median age was 4.6 years [IQR 2.1-7.4]. Median chest tube duration was 5 days [IQR 4-7]. Median number of tPA doses was 3 [IQR 3-3]. Median length of stay (LOS) was 8 days [IQR 5-11]. All patients were treated with intrapleural fibrinolysis. No patients subsequently required VATS. Fifteen patients (21%) underwent repeat fibrinolysis. On multivariable analysis, persistent oxygen requirement after one course of tPA (OR 5.819, 95% CI 1.230-27.521; p=0.026) was independently predictive of repeat therapy. There were no 30-day readmissions. With a standardized protocol emphasizing primary and repeat intrapleural fibrinolysis, operative intervention was not required for pediatric empyema.

## Identifying Post-Discharge Needs in Pediatric Trauma Patients Through a Standardized Follow-up Telephone Program.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:33
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2

- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163398
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163398) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42607863/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估儿科创伤患者出院后未满足的医疗、社会及心理需求，并识别最可能需要额外支持的患者群体。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入419例患者，237例（57%）完成30天电话随访，其中66例（27%）请求进一步干预（如心理健康服务、转诊、交通援助或WIC社会服务）。随访完成率最高的人群包括外州居民（66%）、涉及儿童保护服务者（63%）、有私人保险者（65%）及非英语家庭（71%）；未满足需求最常见于公立/特许学校学生（37%）、动物攻击伤（25%）、烧伤/烟花伤（25%）及枪伤（20%）患者。
- 临床启示：标准化随访电话可有效识别儿科创伤患者出院后康复障碍，并帮助患者家庭获取必要的医疗、社会及心理资源。

**原文摘要**：Although advances in pediatric trauma care have improved survival, many patients experience unmet medical, social, and psychological needs after hospital discharge. Strategies to identify these needs remain limited. This study aimed to characterize unmet post-discharge needs among pediatric trauma patients and identify patient populations most likely to require additional support. A retrospective review was conducted of prospectively collected follow-up data from patients < 18 years who presented as Tier 1 or 2 trauma activations to an American College of Surgeons-verified Level II trauma center trauma between February 2023-December 2023 and October 2024-December 2024. All patients received a standardized telephone call 30 days after discharge assessing medical, social, and psychological needs. A total of 419 patients were included, and 237 (57%) completed the 30-day follow-up phone call. Follow-up rates were highest among patients residing out of state (66%), those with child protective services involvement (63%), those with private insurance (65%), and non-English-speaking families (71%). Among patients reached, 66 (27%) requested further intervention, including mental health services, appointment scheduling or referrals, transportation assistance, or Women, Infants, and Children (WIC) social services. Unmet needs were most common among patients who attend public or charter schools (37%) and among patients injured by animal attacks (25%), burn/fireworks (25%), and gunshot wounds (GSW) (20%). Many pediatric trauma patients continue to have unmet needs after discharge. Structured follow-up calls may help identify barriers to recovery and facilitate access to needed medical, social, and psychological resources for patients and families after traumatic injury. Retrospective LEVEL OF EVIDENCE: III.

## Does Age Matter? Comparing Post-Operative Outcomes Following Gastrostomy Tube Placement in Young Children: a NSQIP-Pediatric Study.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 04:33
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Nemours Surgical Outcomes Center and Department of Surgery
- 通讯作者: Turpin AG (agturpin12@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163393
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163393) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42607862/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估胃造口管置入时患儿年龄是否与术后并发症相关，尤其关注极年轻婴儿是否风险更高。  
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入33,973例患儿，<60天组30天并发症发生率最高（12.4%），60天至6个月组为9.3%，7至18个月组为6.6%（p<0.0001）；多因素回归显示年龄、早产、合并症、ASA分级和类固醇使用与术后并发症相关；<60天组和60天至6个月组术后住院时间均为6天，显著长于7至18个月组的2天（p<0.0001）。  
- 临床启示：极年轻婴儿（<60天）胃造口术后并发症风险最高，对这部分脆弱患儿延迟胃造口置入、先尝试延长鼻胃管喂养可能具有一定临床获益。

**原文摘要**：Young infants may be at higher risk of complications after gastrostomy tube (GT) placement and may benefit from a prolonged nasogastric tube (NGT) feeding trial. The purpose of this study was to determine whether age at time of GT placement is associated with post-operative complications. Using the American College of Surgeons' National Surgical Quality Improvement Program-Pediatric database, we identified children aged 0-18 months who underwent GT placement between 2014-2024. Patients who underwent major concurrent procedures were excluded. A multivariable logistic regression model was used to identify factors associated with 30-day complications. We compared composite 30-day complications across three age groups: 1) <60 days, 2) 60 days to 6 months, and 3) 7- to 18 months. We identified 33,973 patients, 25.0% were <60 days, 45.7% were 60 days to 6 months, and 29.3% were 7- to 18 months. Patients <60 days had the highest percentage of 30-day complications (12.4%), compared to patients 60 days to 6 months (9.3%) and 7- to 18 months (6.6%) (p<0.0001). On multivariable regression, age was associated with post-operative morbidity, as were prematurity, underlying comorbidities, ASA class, and steroid use. Post-operative length of stay (LOS) was longest in patients <60 days [6 days, interquartile range (IQR) 4-10] and patients 60 days to 6 months (6 days, IQR 2-10) compared to patients 7- to 18 months (2 days, IQR 1-4) (p<0.0001). Children aged <60 days have the highest proportion of 30-day complications following GT placement. Because of the known fragility of very young infants, delaying GT placement in this age group may confer some benefit.

## [Efficacy of fluconazole prophylaxis for late-onset invasive fungal infection in very preterm infants/very low birth weight infants: a retrospective cohort study based on propensity score matching].

- 通讯作者: Tang J

- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.7499/j.issn.1008-8830.2601077) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42608298/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估在晚发型侵袭性真菌感染发生率极低的NICU中，极早产儿/极低出生体重儿常规预防性使用氟康唑的必要性。
