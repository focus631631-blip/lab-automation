## Conventional supine delivery position versus free-position delivery guidance intervention on delivery outcomes, pain, and maternal-neonatal outcomes in primiparous women.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 00:38
- 期刊: Afr J Reprod Health · 中科院医学4区(公共卫生、环境卫生与职业卫生4区) · IF 1.0
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Shanxi Provincial People's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Wang Z
- DOI: 10.29063/ajrh2026/v30i15.3
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.29063/ajrh2026/v30i15.3) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42605734/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估自由体位分娩指导联合产前干预对初产妇分娩结局、疼痛及母婴结局的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：研究组（n=40）的产程时间和总产程时间均显著短于对照组（P<0.05），疼痛评分显著更低（P<0.05）；研究组自然分娩率显著更高、剖宫产率显著更低（P<0.05），不良母婴结局发生率显著低于对照组（P<0.05）。
- 临床启示：自由体位分娩指导联合产前干预可有效改善初产妇的分娩结局，减轻产痛并降低母婴不良结局风险，值得临床推广。

**原文摘要**：This randomized comparative study investigated the effects of free-position delivery guidance combined with prenatal intervention on pain levels, delivery outcomes, and maternal-neonatal outcomes in primiparous women. A total of 80 primiparous women admitted to the hospital between April 2023 and October 2025 were included in the study. Participants were randomly divided into two groups of 40 each using a random number table. The control group received conventional supine delivery with basic guidance, while the study group received free position delivery guidance combined with prenatal intervention. Labor duration, pain scores, delivery outcomes, and the incidence of adverse maternal-neonatal outcomes were compared between the two groups. The study group showed significantly shorter labor duration and total labor time compared to the control group (P<0.05). The study group also demonstrated significantly lower pain scores (P<0.05). The study group exhibited a significantly higher rate of natural delivery and a significantly lower rate of cesarean section compared to the control group (P<0.05). The incidence of adverse maternal-infant outcomes in the study group was significantly lower than that in the control group (P<0.05). The combination of free-position delivery guidance and prenatal intervention can effectively improve delivery outcomes, alleviate labor pain, and reduce adverse maternal-neonatal outcomes in primiparous women. ## Does laparoscopic-assisted transanal endorectal pull-through improve outcomes compared with transanal endorectal pull-through in Hirschsprung disease? A systematic review and meta-analysis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 00:38
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Madhya Pradesh · Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose Medical College
- 通讯作者: Sisodiya RS (rssisodiyanscbpediasurgery@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06577-2
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06577-2) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42606588/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：比较经肛门内括约肌切除术（TERPT）与腹腔镜辅助经肛门内括约肌切除术（LA-TERPT）在先天性巨结肠患儿中的术后并发症及长期功能结局是否存在优劣差异。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入23项研究、1668例患儿（TERPT 980例，LA-TERPT 688例）。两组在便秘（log RR=0.11）、污便（log RR=0.05）、大便失禁（log RR=0.05）、HAEC（log RR=0.09）、BFS>17（log RR=-0.08）、吻合口狭窄（log RR=0.28）和吻合口漏（log RR=0.06）方面均无显著差异；TERPT的手术时间显著更短（MD=-43.25分钟），术后住院时间相当（MD=0.20天）。
- 临床启示：目前证据不支持两种术式在功能结局上孰优孰劣，手术方式选择应依据疾病特点、技术因素和外科医生经验，需谨慎解读现有低至中等确定性证据。

**原文摘要**：TERPT and LA-TERPT are widely used minimally invasive procedures for HD treatment. However, the comparative superiority of either technique in terms of postoperative complications and long-term functional outcomes remains uncertain. An SRMA was conducted in accordance with PRISMA 2020 guidelines and prospectively registered in PROSPERO (CRD420261378340). PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar, and trial registries were searched from inception to May 3, 2026. Comparative studies evaluating TERPT and LA-TERPT in children with HD were included. The primary outcomes were constipation, soiling, fecal incontinence, BFS, HAEC, anastomotic stricture, and anastomotic leak. Secondary outcomes included operative time and hospital stay. A random-effects meta-analysis was performed using log risk ratios (RRs) or mean differences (MDs) with 95% confidence intervals (CIs). Twenty-three studies involving 1,668 children with HD were included, comprising 980 TERPT and 688 LA-TERPT procedures. No significant differences were observed between TERPT and LA-TERPT for constipation (log RR = 0.11), soiling (log RR = 0.05), fecal incontinence (log RR = 0.05), HAEC (log RR = 0.09), BFS > 17 (log RR = - 0.08), anastomotic stricture (log RR = 0.28), or anastomotic leak (log RR = 0.06). TERPT was associated with a significantly shorter operative time (MD = -43.25 min). Post-op hospital stay was comparable between groups (MD = 0.20 days). Most outcomes showed low heterogeneity, whereas operative time and hospital stay showed substantial heterogeneity. This SRMA found no clear evidence that either TERPT or LA-TERPT yields superior postoperative or functional outcomes in children with RSHD. However, the available evidence is largely observational and has low to moderate certainty, so it should be interpreted cautiously. Until higher-quality evidence becomes available, the choice of surgical approach should be guided by disease characteristics, technical considerations, and surgeon expertise.
