## Preterm Neonatal Nutrition: Evolution Over the Course of 50 Years and Contemporary Challenges.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:17
- 期刊: Pediatr Ann · 中科院医学4区(儿科4区) · IF 0.9 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: New Hampshire
- 通讯作者: Hartman TK
- DOI: 10.3928/19382359-20260730-06
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.3928/19382359-20260730-06) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42837583/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：总结过去50年早产儿营养的演变，并探讨当代在生长、神经发育及并发症防治方面的挑战。
- 核心发现/数据：50年来营养进步显著提高早产儿存活率，但实现最佳生长和神经发育仍具挑战；现代液体管理已从历史宽松策略转向基于胎龄和体重的个体化方案，以降低动脉导管未闭和坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）风险。母乳可显著降低NEC风险，但需强化才能安全满足高蛋白和矿物质目标；脂质和碳水化合物需精细滴定，以防高甘油三酯血症和高血糖。高钙磷摄入对预防代谢性骨病至关重要，未强化母乳不足，推荐以碱性磷酸酶监测指导目标强化。
- 临床启示：临床应采用个体化、体重指导的液体管理，推广母乳并实施目标强化，同时监测碱性磷酸酶及代谢指标，以优化早产儿营养、生长和长期神经发育结局。

**原文摘要**：Advances in neonatal nutrition have markedly improved preterm survival, but achieving optimal growth and neurodevelopment remain a challenge. Modern fluid guidelines favor individualized, weight-guided fluid management based on gestational age, shifting away from historic liberal strategies to avoid patent ductus arteriosus and necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC). Inadequate intake causes extrauterine growth restriction, negatively impacting neurodevelopment and long-term cardiometabolic risks. Human milk strongly reduces NEC risk but requires fortification to meet these high protein and mineral targets safely. Lipids supply critical fatty acids for brain development. Carbohydrates are the primary immediate energy substrate, and both macronutrients require careful titration to prevent complications, such as hypertriglyceridemia and hyperglycemia. High calcium and phosphorus intake are critical to prevent metabolic bone disease. Since unfortified milk is insufficient, targeted fortification guided by alkaline phosphatase monitoring is recommended.
