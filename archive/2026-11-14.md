## Establishment and characterisation of primary human oesophageal organoids as a versatile epithelial model.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:01
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 英国 · Great Ormond Street Institute of Child Health
- 通讯作者: De Coppi P (p.decoppi@ucl.ac.uk)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06704-z
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06704-z) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43024009/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：建立并表征来源于非病理人食管上皮的原代类器官，以获得可长期传代、可适配二维和三维培养的食管上皮模型平台。
- 核心发现/数据：从内镜活检和手术切除组织分离基底上皮细胞，在优化条件下成功建立并维持长期三维原代类器官，且可扩增为二维单层后再接种回三维培养。类器官维持上皮标志物表达，KRT14、involucrin、loricrin和filaggrin证实其食管表型；较大、多层的原代类器官较二维单层和再接种的小类器官表达更高水平的分化相关标志物，提示培养构型影响上皮分化但保持谱系身份。
- 临床启示：该平台提供可重复的二维与三维互补研究体系，支持健康与疾病来源食管组织的比较、患者特异性建模及机制研究，有望促进食管疾病和组织稳态相关研究及转化应用。

**原文摘要**：Human patient-derived oesophageal epithelial models are limited, and organoid cultures often have short passaging lifespans, restricting studies of tissue homeostasis and disease. We aimed to establish and characterise primary oesophageal organoids from non-pathologic epithelium as a renewable epithelial source and adaptable modelling platform. Primary oesophageal tissues were obtained from endoscopic biopsies and surgical resections. Basal epithelial cells were isolated and cultured under optimised conditions to establish long-term three-dimensional organoids. Organoid-derived cells were expanded as two-dimensional monolayers and re-seeded into 3D culture. Epithelial identity and differentiation were assessed by immunostaining and RT-qPCR (reverse transcription-quantitative polymerase chain reaction) analysis of markers. Primary organoids were established and maintained while preserving epithelial markers. Expression of KRT14, involucrin, loricrin and filaggrin confirmed the oesophageal phenotype. Larger, multilayered primary organoids showed increased expression of differentiation-associated markers relative to 2D monolayers and re-seeded small organoids, indicating culture configuration influences epithelial differentiation while maintaining lineage identity. We established a robust and reproducible protocol for generating and maintaining oesophageal organoids across culture formats. These models retained epithelial features while exhibiting culture-dependent differentiation. This platform enables complementary 2D and 3D investigation of oesophageal epithelial biology and supports future comparison of healthy and disease-derived tissues for patient-specific modelling and mechanistic studies.

## Post remote ischaemic conditioning and early pre remote ischaemic conditioning reduces the extent and severity of bowel injury in an animal model of necrotising enterocolitis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 04:01
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2

**原文摘要**：Remote ischaemic conditioning (RIC) has a protective effect in an experimental model of severe necrotising enterocolitis. We investigated the impact of delivering RIC at different time-points. We used an established intestinal ischaemia-reperfusion injury (IRI) model in rat pups. RIC was delivered by means of a lower limb ligature. Animals were randomly allocated to intestinal IRI only, RIC before IRI (preRIC), RIC after IRI (postRIC), RIC 48 h before IRI (earlyRIC), RIC 48 h and immediately before IRI (early + preRIC), or control. Macroscopic and microscopic intestinal injury are reported. Serum cytokine levels were assayed. Data are median (inter-quartile range). Animals receiving RIC had reduced severe macroscopic intestinal injury: pre-RIC 4% (0-21%, p = 0.0019), postRIC 4% (0-44%, p = 0.0021), earlyRIC 3% (0-42%, p = 0.0054) and early+preRIC 0% (0-39%, p = 0.0018) vs. IRI 78% (30-90). The preRIC and postRIC groups compared had reduced injury scores compared to IRI alone: 3.5 (1.25-5, p = 0.002) and 4.25 (4-5, p = 0.031) vs. 5.5 (4-6). RIC 48 h prior to IRI did not reduce injury but injury was lowest in early+preRIC group [2.375 (1.625-3) 0.4375, p = 0.0001]. IFN-γ, IL-4 and IL-5 were increased following RIC. RIC reduces the extent and severity of intestinal injury. Two doses of RIC delivered 48 h apart has the greatest effect. Cytokines IFN-γ, IL-4 and IL-5 may be important in the transmission of RIC.
