## Caught in a web: congenital anterior and posterior oral synechiae with concurrent diaphragmatic hernia and atypical cleft palate.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 01:04
- 期刊: J Surg Case Rep · 中科院医学4区(外科4区) · IF 0.5
- 国家/单位: 英国
- 通讯作者: Power A
- DOI: 10.1093/jscr/rjag681
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1093/jscr/rjag681) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42592335/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告一例罕见先天性口腔前、后黏连带合并左侧先天性膈疝及非典型腭裂的新生儿病例，并强调其临床识别与多学科管理的重要性。
- 核心发现/数据：男婴于出生后16天转诊，存在双侧前、后口腔黏连带、非典型黏膜下腭裂及左侧先天性膈疝；黏连带产前未检出，并导致产房新生儿插管失败。综合基因检测未发现致病突变；膈疝修补及黏连带松解术后可经口喂养，但2年随访显示喂养、言语、听力和反复感染问题持续存在，主要归因于未修复的非典型腭裂。
- 临床启示：新生儿插管意外失败时应警惕口腔黏连带的存在，此类复杂畸形需协调多学科团队进行序贯手术与长期综合管理。

**原文摘要**：Congenital oral synechiae are rare fibrous bands connecting the maxillary and mandibular structures. We report a male neonate referred to our oral and maxillofacial surgery service at 16 days of age with bilateral anterior and posterior oral synechiae, an atypical submucous cleft palate, and a concurrent left-sided congenital diaphragmatic hernia. The synechiae were undetected prenatally and obstructed neonatal intubation in the delivery suite, compounding the immediate airway demands of the diaphragmatic hernia. This triad has not been previously described, and comprehensive genetic testing identified no causative mutation. Following surgical repair of the hernia and release of the synechiae, oral feeding was established. Two years of follow-up demonstrate persistent challenges with feeding, speech, hearing, and recurrent infection, driven primarily by an unrepaired atypical palatal cleft. This case highlights the importance of recognizing oral synechiae when neonatal intubation fails unexpectedly, and the need for co-ordinated multidisciplinary care.

## Successful Transabdominal Cerclage for Recurrent Second-Trimester Loss in a Low-Resource Environment: A Case Report.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 01:04
- 期刊: Case Rep Obstet Gynecol · 中科院医学4区(妇产科学4区) · IF 0.8
- 国家/单位: knust.edu.gh · Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology
- 通讯作者: Ofori AA
- DOI: 10.1155/crog/5373643
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1155/crog/5373643) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42592125/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估经腹宫颈环扎术（TAC）在加纳低资源环境中治疗严重宫颈机能不全的可行性及初步结局。
- 核心发现/数据：3名32–33岁、既往共8次中孕期流产及多次经阴道环扎失败的严重宫颈机能不全女性，于孕12–13周接受开腹TAC（5-mm Mersilene带），最终全部活产，共娩出5名新生儿；分娩孕周分别为38周4天（单胎）、35周2天（单胎）和30周1天（三胎，合并子痫前期及围产期心肌病），无直接归因于手术的严重并发症。
- 临床启示：在加纳专科医院环境下，TAC对严重宫颈机能不全可行且结局令人鼓舞，但需更大样本研究验证其有效性和在西非地区的普适性。

**原文摘要**：Cervical insufficiency is a significant cause of recurrent midtrimester pregnancy loss. Although transabdominal cerclage (TAC) is an established option for selected patients with severe cervical insufficiency, its application and outcomes remain undocumented in West African clinical settings. This case report documents the first use of TAC in Ghana. Three Ghanaian women, aged 32-33, with severe cervical insufficiency and a cumulative history of eight prior midtrimester losses and multiple failed transvaginal cerclages, underwent open TAC using 5-mm Mersilene tape between 12 and 13 weeks of gestation at Oak Specialist Hospital in Kumasi, Ghana. All three reported pregnancies resulted in live births, yielding five neonates. Deliveries occurred at 38 weeks 4 days (singleton), 35 weeks 2 days (singleton) and 30 weeks 1 day (triplet gestation complicated by preeclampsia and peripartum cardiomyopathy). No major intraoperative or postoperative complications directly attributable to the surgery were observed. This case report demonstrates that TAC is a feasible intervention for severe cervical insufficiency in a Ghanaian specialist hospital setting. The preliminary outcomes are encouraging, but larger studies are needed to establish efficacy and generalizability across the West African region.

We established a clinically relevant immunocompetent neuroblastoma BM metastasis model and demonstrated MSC homing to metastatic BM lesions, providing a useful platform for evaluating therapies targeting metastatic neuroblastoma.

## Disentangling Feeding Practices from Socioeconomic Risk Factors in Pyloric Stenosis Risk.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 05:38
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Philadelphia · Children's Hospital of Philadelphia
- 通讯作者: Berger NF (Bergern2@chop.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163379
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163379) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42595045/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探究社会经济因素与已知肥厚性幽门狭窄（HPS）危险因素之间的关系，以厘清喂养方式与社会经济风险因素的独立作用。
- 核心发现/数据：单因素分析显示配方奶喂养、母亲吸烟、HPS家族史和公共医疗保险与HPS相关，而极高儿童机会指数（COI）与较低风险相关；多因素分析中仅配方奶喂养、母亲吸烟和家族史仍显著，公共保险和极高COI不再显著。COI与喂养类型显著相关（最终队列725例对照、655例病例）。
- 临床启示：社会经济因素并非HPS的独立预测因子，其风险效应主要由配方奶喂养等喂养方式介导，临床应重点关注喂养方式而非仅依赖社会经济指标来进行风险评估。

**原文摘要**：Formula feeding, tobacco use, and family history have been well-established as risk factors for hypertrophic pyloric stenosis (HPS). Several markers of socioeconomic status have also been associated with HPS, however, their relationship to other risk factors has not been delineated. The purpose of this study is to investigate the relationship between socioeconomic factors and known risk factors for HPS. We identified 735 patients who underwent pyloromyotomy at a high-volume children's hospital between 2012 and 2025. These patients were matched one-to-one by sex, gestational age, age, race, and ethnicity with 735 controls from the general pediatrics clinic at the same institution. A case-wise deletion was performed to address data missingness, which resulted in a final cohort of 725 controls and 655 cases. Data was retrospectively collected from patient charts and analyzed. Univariate logistic regressions revealed formula feeding, maternal tobacco use, family history of HPS, and public medical insurance were associated with HPS while very high Child Opportunity Index (COI) score was associated with a lower risk of HPS. On multivariable analysis, formula feeding, maternal tobacco use, and family history remained significant, however, public insurance and very high COI were no longer significant. COI and feeding type were significantly correlated on Chi-square analysis. Socioeconomic factors are associated with HPS on unadjusted analysis, however, they are not independent predictors. This is likely mediated by infant feeding practices, where formula feeding continues to be a primary driver of risk.

## A National survey of pediatric surgical care coverage models to outlying neonatal intensive care units.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 05:38
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Aurora · University of Colorado School of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Acker SN (Shannon.acker@childrenscolorado.org)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163381
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163381) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42595044/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：描述当前美国儿科外科对偏远新生儿重症监护病房（NICU）的覆盖模式，并评估儿科外科医生对在偏远NICU中治疗新生儿最佳方案的观点。
- 核心发现/数据：52位儿科外科主任中28.9%应答，其中26位（50%）报告覆盖一个或多个偏远NICU；这些NICU中76%为三级，51%与外科医生所在主要医院无隶属关系，仅15%的NICU年手术量超过25例。超过半数受访者认为，除胃造口管置入和中心静脉通路外，所有手术均应转运至主要医疗中心进行。
- 临床启示：当前实际提供手术服务的地点与外科医生认为的最佳诊疗地点存在明显脱节，提示需要采取区域化策略以优化偏远NICU新生儿的 surgical care。

**原文摘要**：The delivery of pediatric surgical care to newborns varies based on neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) coverage. We aimed to describe the current national coverage landscape and assess pediatric surgeons' perspectives on the optimal management of infants at outlying NICUs. The AAP Delivery of Surgical Care Committee administered a web-based survey to pediatric surgery division chiefs at Children's Hospital Association practices. Responses were analyzed using appropriate nonparametric and categorical statistical tests, with significance set at p<0.05. Fifty-two division chiefs responded (28.9%); 26 (50% reporting covering one or more outlying NICUs. Of these outlying NICUS, 76% were Level III. Just over half (51%) had no affiliation with the surgeons' primary hospital. Case volumes were low, with only 5 NICUs (15%) performing more than 25 operations annually. Factors influencing decisions to operate at outlying NICUs included rapport with neonatologists, NICU level designation, transfer capability, and availability of pediatric anesthesiology. The capacity of outlying NICUs to care for infants with index surgical diagnoses varied based on specific diagnosis. Over half of respondents endorsed transfer to the primary center as the optimal location for care for all procedures except gastrostomy tube placement and central venous access. Half of surveyed pediatric surgery groups provide coverage to one or more outlying NICUs despite low reported case volumes. A clear disconnect exists between pediatric surgeons' views on location for optimal care and sites of current care provision. These findings underscore the need for regionalized strategies to optimize surgical care of these infants.

## Fetus-in-fetu arising from the palate treated with ex-utero intrapartum treatment.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:38
- 期刊: BMJ Case Rep · 中科院医学4区(医学：内科4区) · IF 0.4
- 国家/单位: Amagasaki · Hyogo Prefectural Amagasaki General Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Suzui R (r_suzui@kuhp.kyoto-u.ac.jp)
- DOI: 10.1136/bcr-2026-273730
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1136/bcr-2026-273730) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42595387/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告一例产前诊断的腭部胎儿体内胎儿（fetus-in-fetu）经产时宫外治疗（EXIT）成功处理的罕见病例。
- 核心发现/数据：孕23周发现4 cm口腔肿物，导致羊水过多和胃缩小；孕34+5周行EXIT剖宫产，娩出14 cm肿瘤，行气管切开及分期切除。组织病理显示神经、胃肠道、肾上腺、皮肤、骨和牙齿等多种分化组织，符合Spencer标准。婴儿术后恢复良好，生后96天出院。
- 临床启示：EXIT对高危腭部畸胎瘤（epignathus）的气道管理至关重要；fetus-in-fetu较畸胎瘤恶性潜能更低，强调个体化产前规划与多学科协作。

**原文摘要**：Epignathus, a teratoma arising from the palate or pharynx, is extremely rare and palatal fetus-in-fetu represents an even rarer anomaly. We report a prenatally diagnosed palatal fetus-in-fetu successfully managed with ex utero intrapartum treatment (EXIT). A 4 cm oral mass detected at 23 weeks caused polyhydramnios and gastric shrinkage, suggesting impaired swallowing and potential airway obstruction. Multidisciplinary evaluation determined the need for EXIT to secure the airway under placental circulation. At 34+5 weeks of gestation, caesarean delivery with EXIT was performed; a 14 cm tumour was delivered and tracheostomy followed by staged resection was undertaken. Histopathology revealed multiple differentiated tissues-including nerve, gastrointestinal tract, adrenal gland, skin, bone and teeth-meeting Spencer's criteria for fetus-in-fetu. The infant recovered well and was discharged 96 days postnatally. EXIT proved invaluable for airway management in high-risk epignathus, and fetus-in-fetu differs from teratoma by its lower malignant potential, emphasising individualised prenatal planning and team collaboration.

## Bedside external ventricular drainage in the NICU: procedural efficiency and infection trends in a 15-year single-center cohort.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:38
- 期刊: Childs Nerv Syst
- 国家/单位: 韩国 · Seoul National University Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Phi JH (phijh@snu.ac.kr)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00381-026-07374-x
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00381-026-07374-x) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42593702/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：比较新生儿重症监护病房（NICU）床旁与手术室放置脑室外引流（EVD）的操作效率，并描述培养阳性的EVD相关感染情况。
- 核心发现/数据：床旁置管较手术室置管显著更快（24.5分钟 vs 57.4分钟，校正后差异32.5分钟，95%CI 16.7-48.4，p<0.001）。共发生7例医院获得性培养阳性EVD相关感染（2.37/1000导管日），均见于床旁置管，且感染发生率与NICU耐甲氧西林金黄色葡萄球菌（MRSA）获得率呈正相关（r=0.74，p=0.009）。
- 临床启示：床旁EVD置管效率更高，但感染数据仅具描述性，不能据此认为床旁操作在感染安全性上更优；需关注NICU中MRSA防控以降低EVD相关感染风险。

**原文摘要**：To compare the procedural efficiency of bedside versus operating-room external ventricular drainage (EVD) insertion in a neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) and to describe culture-positive EVD-associated infections. We performed a retrospective single-center cohort study of NICU infants who underwent EVD insertion between 2011 and 2025. Operative time and insertion characteristics were analyzed at the insertion level. Culture-positive EVD-associated infections acquired at the study hospital were described, and annual infection incidence was explored in relation to unit methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) acquisition rates. Seven study-hospital-acquired culture-positive EVD-associated infections occurred (2.37 per 1000 catheter-days at risk), all during bedside catheter episodes. Median time from catheter insertion to first positive culture was 2 days. In an exploratory annual ecological analysis, infection incidence correlated with NICU MRSA acquisition rates (Pearson r = 0.74; p = 0.009). Bedside NICU EVD insertion was substantially faster than operating-room insertion after adjustment for case-mix differences. Culture-positive infections occurred only during bedside catheter episodes; therefore, infection findings should be interpreted descriptively rather than as evidence of comparative infection safety.

## Effect of right- vs left-sided congenital diaphragmatic hernia and duration of tracheal occlusion on longitudinal lung-size changes.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:38
- 期刊: Ultrasound Obstet Gynecol
- 国家/单位: 比利时 · My FetUZ Fetal Research Center
- 通讯作者: Deprest J
- DOI: 10.1002/uog.70308
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/uog.70308) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42594402/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：比较右侧与左侧先天性膈疝胎儿行FETO术后肺容积增长的纵向变化模式，并探讨封堵时长对肺生长的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：FETO术后，右侧与左侧CDH的肺增长模式相似，均表现为封堵后早期急剧增大（与基线相比分别增加60%和50%），随后缓慢持续增长；6周内右侧CDH肺容积扩大140%，显著高于左侧的100%（P<0.001）。初始生长速率右侧低于左侧（13.11%/天 vs 15.67%/天，P<0.001），但6周内总体生长速率无显著差异（2.91%/天 vs 1.84%/天，P=0.081）。
- 临床启示：FETO术后肺生长呈持续增加趋势，提示封堵持续时间可能影响治疗反应；右侧与左侧CDH的肺生长速率存在差异，但总体模式相似，临床管理可参考这一动态变化规律。

**原文摘要**：To describe patterns of lung-size changes after fetoscopic endoluminal tracheal occlusion (FETO) in cases of right-sided congenital diaphragmatic hernia (RCDH) compared with in cases of left-sided congenital diaphragmatic hernia (LCDH), and the association between lung growth and the duration of occlusion. This was a longitudinal observational cohort study conducted at three fetal surgery centers (UZ Leuven, Leuven, Belgium; BCNatal, Barcelona, Spain; The Heart Hospital of São Paulo, São Paulo, Brazil) using similar prenatal management protocols. Inclusion criteria were pregnant women with a fetus with isolated congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH) that underwent FETO between July 2002 and October 2021 with a minimum of two measurements of the fetal observed-to-expected lung-to-head ratio (o/e-LHR) (one before and at least one after FETO) available. The o/e-LHR was used as a proxy for fetal lung size. Other variables were the side of the defect and gestational age at FETO and at balloon removal. Multilevel analysis was used to model longitudinal changes in fetal lung size. The changes in o/e-LHR during the occlusion period (∆ o/e-LHR (%), referred to as 'change in absolute lung size') were calculated and corrected for baseline measurement ('proportional' lung size change (%)) and expressed as a function of the duration of occlusion (referred to as proportional lung growth rate (%/day)). A structural break point was used to identify a significant change in proportional lung growth rate during the occlusion period. Patterns of proportional lung-size change and proportional lung growth rate were compared between RCDH and LCDH using Student's t-test. We reported 1026 o/e-LHR measurements in 338 fetuses (RCDH, n = 73; LCDH, n = 265). The pattern of lung growth after FETO was comparable in cases of RCDH and LCDH, with a sharp increase in size immediately after occlusion (60% vs 50%, respectively, compared with baseline measurements), followed by a slower increase over the remainder of the observation period. Overall, over the 6-week period, lung size in cases of RCDH expanded by 140% compared with baseline, while in cases of LCDH lung size expanded by 100% (P < 0.001). The pattern of proportional lung growth rate was also similar, starting with a high growth rate at day 2 after FETO, followed by slower but persistent growth. In cases of RCDH, lungs had a slower initial growth rate compared with LCDH (13.11%/day (95% CI, 3.72-22.52%/day) vs 15.67%/day (95% CI, 6.69-24.66%/day); P < 0.001). The proportional growth rates over the 6-week period were comparable between RCDH and LCDH (2.91%/day vs 1.84%/day; P = 0.081). The structural break point showed a significant change in growth rate at day 5 and day 31 after FETO for cases of LCDH and day 8 after FETO for cases of RCDH. There are measurable differences in proportional lung growth rates after FETO between cases of RCDH and LCDH. However, the pattern of lung-size changes parallel each other, with an initial fast growth rate that dropped to a lower but persisting rate later in the occlusion period. The continued increase in fetal lung size observed after FETO supports previous observations that the duration of occlusion may influence the treatment response in CDH. © 2026 International Society of Ultrasound in Obstetrics and Gynecology.
