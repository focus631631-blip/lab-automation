**原文摘要**：To evaluate the association between uterine synechiae identified on ultrasound and adverse pregnancy outcomes through a systematic review and meta-analysis of observational studies. A systematic search of PubMed, Cochrane Library, CINAHL and ScienceDirect was performed from database inception to July 2025 in accordance with MOOSE guidelines. Cohort and case-control studies reporting pregnancy outcomes among women with versus without uterine synechiae were eligible. Two reviewers independently screened studies, extracted data and assessed quality using the Newcastle-Ottawa Scale. Random-effects models were used to calculate pooled odds ratios and mean differences for maternal and neonatal outcomes. Four studies met inclusion criteria, comprising 628 pregnancies with uterine synechiae and 65,959 controls. Uterine synechiae were associated with an increased risk of preterm birth <37 weeks (odds ratio 1.80; 95% CI 1.35-2.39), preterm premature rupture of membranes (odds ratio 3.58; 95% CI 1.13-11.36), placental abruption (odds ratio 3.34; 95% CI 1.57-7.13) and placenta previa (odds ratio 3.19; 95% CI 1.07-9.57). A subgroup analysis demonstrated higher odds of cesarean delivery for fetal malpresentation in pregnancies with synechiae (odds ratio 1.97; 95% CI 1.23-3.13). Uterine synechiae identified on ultrasound are associated with increased risks of preterm birth, PPROM, placental abruption, placenta previa and fetal malpresentation, as well as lower birth weight. These findings indicate that synechiae are not incidental findings and support their association with adverse outcomes in pregnancy.

**原文摘要**：Ventilator-associated pneumonia (VAP) is a major healthcare-associated infection in critically ill children, primarily driven by microaspiration around the endotracheal tube cuff. Target-pressure compliance was higher with CACPM (P<0.001). CACPM significantly reduces the proportion of critically ill pediatric patients developing VAP compared with IMCPM. This preventive effect appears directly linked to improved target pressure compliance, suggesting that automated regulation could be a valuable addition to pediatric VAP prevention strategies. ## Bilateral upper-limb palsy of the newborn infant: Taxonomy, differential diagnosis, and clinical pathways.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:37
- 期刊: Dev Med Child Neurol · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/临床神经病学2区) · IF 3.7 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 英国 · Imperial College London
- 通讯作者: Jester A
- DOI: 10.1111/dmcn.70575
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1111/dmcn.70575) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42858240/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：针对新生儿双侧上肢活动减少或缺失这一罕见而危急的表型，提出统一的术语与分类框架（BULPN），并建立以定位为先的诊断流程和时间锚定、按严重度分层的管理路径，以区别于单侧臂丛出生损伤（BPBI）的固有范式。

- 核心发现/数据：双侧受累会显著扩大鉴别诊断范围，涵盖颈段脊髓损伤、神经肌肉疾病及非神经性活动受限，若默认归因于双侧BPBI可能漏诊严重的中枢性病变。本综述提出三类分型：真性BULPN（出生时双侧臂丛水平麻痹）、类似表现（mimics）与获得性（生后起病）麻痹；其中真性BULPN最常见于臀位分娩，且常呈不对称表现，已发表队列显示合并膈神经麻痹的比例较高。目前尚无针对该表型的标准化术语或检查框架。

- 临床启示：临床上遇到新生儿双侧上肢活动障碍时，应避免直接诊断为双侧臂丛损伤，而应按定位优先的算法系统排查中枢、脊髓及神经肌肉病因。采用该分类与分层路径有助于实现更安全、更有针对性的评估与管理，降低误诊风险。

**原文摘要**：Bilateral reduction or absence of upper-limb movement in a newborn infant is a rare but critical presentation that cannot be anchored on the unilateral paradigm of brachial plexus birth injury (BPBI). Bilaterality substantially widens the differential to include cervical spinal cord injury, neuromuscular disorders, and non-neurological movement limitation; thus, default attribution to bilateral BPBI risks missing a serious central pathology. No standardized terminology or investigation framework currently addresses this phenotype. This review introduces bilateral upper-limb palsy of the newborn infant (BULPN) and proposes a three-category taxonomy separating 'true BULPN' (bilateral plexus-level palsy at birth) from mimics and acquired (postnatal-onset) palsy. True BULPN is most consistently reported in breech delivery and it is frequently asymmetric; published cohorts describe a high burden of concurrent phrenic nerve palsy. A localization-first diagnostic algorithm and time-anchored, severity-stratified management pathways are presented to support safer and more targeted clinical assessment and management, and reduce the risk of misdiagnosis.
