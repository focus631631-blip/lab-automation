## PRDM9 Deficiency Drives Mosaic Promoter Deletions in Sporadic Hirschsprung Disease and Supports Blood-Based Molecular Stratification.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:21
- 期刊: Gastroenterology · 中科院医学1区(胃肠肝病学1区) · IF 29.7 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Guangdong Provincial Key Laboratory of Research in Structural Birth Defect Disease
- 通讯作者: Zhou W (zhouwenhao@gwcmc.org)
- DOI: 10.1053/j.gastro.2026.05.020
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2026.05.020) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42250889/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探究散发性先天性巨结肠（HSCR）的非经典分子机制及血液可及生物标志物，重点验证PRDM9缺陷与嵌合启动子缺失（MPD）的关系。
- 核心发现/数据：103例散发性HSCR配对结肠RNA-seq显示79.6%（82/103）存在神经发生程序协同抑制，PRDM9在无神经节组织中下调并伴启动子高甲基化；斑马鱼和小鼠prdm9扰动减少HuC/D阳性分化肠神经元并损害动力，PRDM9缺失使肠神经嵴细胞DNA双链断裂向启动子/增强子重分布、在神经发生位点产生MPD并抑制神经元分化。血液MPD评分区分HSCR与对照的AUROC在发现队列为0.78、验证队列为0.82，联合多基因风险评分分别升至0.89和0.91。
- 临床启示：血液MPD评分有望作为无创分子分层工具，补充HSCR诊断和风险分层；PRDM9缺陷相关亚组可能成为机制导向的干预靶点。

**原文摘要**：Hirschsprung disease (HSCR) is a congenital enteric neuropathy with distal aganglionosis and dysmotility. Germline mutations explain many familial cases, but most sporadic cases lack a molecular explanation. We sought noncanonical mechanisms and blood-accessible biomarkers. We performed RNA-sequencing (RNA-seq) on paired aganglionic and ganglionic colon biopsy specimens from 103 sporadic HSCR patients, with control colon samples (n = 22). Blood whole-genome sequencing (WGS) was available for 41 patients with RNA-seq profiles. We assessed PR/SET domain 9 (PRDM9) in patient tissues, perturbed prdm9 in zebrafish and mice, and performed mechanistic profiling in PRDM9-knockout human-induced pluripotent stem cell-derived enteric neural crest cells. Mosaic promoter deletions (MPDs) were called from colon WGS (n = 30; matched blood, n = 27) and summarized as a blood MPD score in blood WGS cohorts (discovery: 89 HSCR and 43 controls; validation: 165 HSCR and 42 controls), alone and combined with the polygenic risk score. A predominant subgroup (79.6% [82 of 103]) showed coordinated repression of neurogenesis programs. Promoter motif enrichment implicated PRDM9, which we localized to normal enteric nervous system but found down-regulated in aganglionic tissue with promoter hypermethylation. Prdm9 perturbation reduced HuC/D-positive differentiated enteric neurons and impaired motility in zebrafish and mice. In enteric neural crest cells, PRDM9 loss redistributed DNA double-strand breaks toward promoters and enhancers, generated MPDs at neurogenesis loci, and correlated with transcriptional repression and impaired neuronal differentiation. Blood MPD score discriminated HSCR from controls in a discovery cohort (area under the receiver operating characteristic curve [AUROC], 0.78) and an independent validation cohort (AUROC, 0.82), and improved with polygenic risk score (AUROC, 0.89 and 0.91, respectively). PRDM9 deficiency links ectopic DNA breaks to MPDs and impaired enteric neuronal differentiation in a predominant molecular subgroup within sporadic HSCR and enables complementary, noninvasive molecular stratification.

## Increased Incidence and Improved Survival of Ewing and Ewing-Like Sarcomas in Children and Young Adolescents in the Netherlands Since the 1990s: A Population-Based Study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 12:00
- 期刊: Cancer Med · 中科院医学3区(肿瘤学3区) · IF 3.5 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 荷兰 · Princess Máxima Center for Pediatric Oncology
- 通讯作者: Karim-Kos HE
- DOI: 10.1002/cam4.72429
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/cam4.72429) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42967889/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估1990—2018年荷兰0—17岁儿童及年轻青少年Ewing及Ewing样肉瘤的发病率和生存趋势。
- 核心发现/数据：1990—2018年年均诊断16例，年龄标化发病率每年增加2.1%，从3.7/百万增至5.2/百万；局部治疗联合化疗比例由75%升至94%。5年总生存率从56%升至70%，10年总生存率从51%升至65%；2010—2018年死亡风险较1990—1999年降低（HR 0.4，95% CI 0.3—0.6）。转移性疾病和较大儿童仍有较高死亡风险。
- 临床启示：诊断、治疗、支持治疗及集中化管理的进步可能共同改善了患儿生存；仍需针对转移性疾病和较大儿童加强研究及靶向治疗以进一步改善预后。

**原文摘要**：Against the background of therapeutic improvements and centralization of care, we evaluated epidemiological trends in Ewing and Ewing-like sarcomas (ES) among children and young adolescents in the Netherlands over three decades. Data on 464 pediatric patients (0-17 years) diagnosed with ES in the Netherlands between 1990 and 2018 were obtained from the Netherlands Cancer Registry, with follow-up until February 2024. Incidence trends were evaluated by calculating the average annual percentage change (AAPC). A Cox regression model was used to assess the effect of sex, age, stage at diagnosis, and tissue-specific tumor site on changes in 5-year observed overall survival (OS) over time. Between 1990 and 2018, on average 16 pediatric patients were diagnosed with ES yearly. The age-standardized incidence rate increased by 2.1% annually, rising from 3.7 per million person-years in 1990-1999 to 5.2 in 2010-2018. Over time, the use of local therapy combined with chemotherapy increased from 75% in 1990-1999 to 94% in 2010-2018. Five-year OS improved from 56% (1990-1999) to 70% (2010-2018), and the 10-year OS increased from 51% to 65% in these periods. Multivariable analyzes confirmed a lower risk of mortality from ES in 2010-2018 (HR 0.4, 95% CI 0.3-0.6) compared to 1990-1999. ES incidence increased alongside improved survival, likely influenced by various factors, including advancements in diagnostics, treatment, supportive care, and follow-up. Nevertheless, patients with metastatic disease and older children continue to experience higher mortality risks, highlighting the importance of ongoing research and targeted treatments to further improve pediatric ES outcomes.

## Eosinophilic Myenteric Ganglionitis in a Newborn: Case Report.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 12:00
- 期刊: Pediatr Dev Pathol
- 国家/单位: 哥伦比亚 · Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine
- 通讯作者: Fernández-Trujillo L
- DOI: 10.1177/10935266261446768
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1177/10935266261446768) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42154483/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告首例新生儿肠旋转不良合并嗜酸性肌间神经节炎的病例，并强调全面肠壁活检对准确诊断的重要性。
- 核心发现/数据：患儿为男性新生儿，因肠梗阻就诊，术中发现肠旋转不良，组织学显示结肠壁神经节细胞周围中度嗜酸性粒细胞浸润。行手术矫正并留置保护性回肠造口后，临床症状逐步改善。18月龄随访造影灌肠发现功能不良的乙状结肠直肠内持续存在陈旧造影剂，提示可能存在直肠动力障碍。
- 临床启示：对疑似病例应行全面肠壁活检以明确诊断，早期诊断和规范处理有助于改善管理并预防并发症；该罕见病仍需进一步研究。

**原文摘要**：Eosinophilic myenteric ganglionitis is a rare cause of pseudo-obstructive intestinal syndrome, predominantly reported in children. It is characterized by symptoms that mimic mechanical intestinal obstruction, despite the absence of a demonstrable lesion, and is identified histologically by an eosinophilic myenteric inflammatory process. We report the case of a male newborn presenting with intestinal obstruction found to have intestinal malrotation and histological evidence of colonic wall ganglion cells surrounded by a moderate eosinophilic infiltrate. The patient underwent surgical correction with a protective diverting ileostomy, resulting in progressive clinical improvement. At 18 months of age, follow-up with a contrast enema revealed persistent old contrast medium in the dysfunctional sigmoid rectum, suggesting possible rectal motility impairment. This is the first reported case in the literature of a newborn with intestinal malrotation in the presence of eosinophilic myenteric ganglionitis. The need for comprehensive intestinal wall biopsy for accurate diagnosis is emphasized, highlighting the importance of early diagnosis for proper management and complication prevention. This case underscores the necessity for further research to enhance understanding, diagnosis, and treatment of eosinophilic myenteric ganglionitis.

## Dinoprostone Versus Balloon Catheter for Cervical Ripening in Multiparous Women Undergoing Induction of Labor: A Retrospective Cohort Study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 21:18
- 期刊: J Obstet Gynaecol Res · 中科院医学4区(妇产科学4区) · IF 1.7 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 以色列 · Galilee Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Frank Wolf M
- DOI: 10.1111/jog.70552
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1111/jog.70552) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42969109/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：比较经产妇足月宫颈不成熟引产中，慢释阴道地诺前列酮（10 mg）与球囊导管对母儿结局的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：回顾性队列纳入802例经产妇，地诺前列酮组宫颈成熟至分娩间隔短于球囊导管组（11.6 vs. 19.4 h，p<0.001），12 h内分娩率更高（51.4% vs. 26.1%，p<0.001），但24 h内分娩率无显著差异（75.7% vs. 71.1%，p=0.183）。地诺前列酮组自然阴道分娩率更高（95.0% vs. 90.0%，p=0.015）、剖宫产率更低（3.2% vs. 8.2%，p=0.006）、产时发热更少（1.4% vs. 4.2%，p=0.036），新生儿结局包括NICU入住无显著差异。
- 临床启示：经产妇足月宫颈不成熟引产时可考虑优先选择地诺前列酮，以缩短分娩间隔、提高自然阴道分娩率并降低剖宫产率；但仍需结合个体情况评估，24 h内分娩率及新生儿结局与球囊导管相似。

**原文摘要**：To compare maternal and neonatal outcomes of induction strategies initiated with slow-release vaginal dinoprostone (10 mg) or balloon catheter in multiparous women undergoing term induction of labor with an unfavorable cervix. This retrospective cohort study included 802 multiparous women with singleton pregnancies at ≥ 37 weeks' gestation, Bishop score < 8, and a medical indication for induction of labor at a tertiary university-affiliated center (2020-2025). Women underwent cervical ripening with either a balloon catheter (n = 522) or a slow-release vaginal dinoprostone insert (n = 280). The co-primary outcomes were the cervical ripening-to-delivery interval and delivery within 24 h. Multivariable analyses identified independent predictors of delivery interval and cesarean birth. Compared with balloon catheter, dinoprostone was associated with a shorter cervical ripening-to-delivery interval (11.6 vs. 19.4 h; p < 0.001). Delivery within 12 h was more frequent with dinoprostone (51.4% vs. 26.1%; p < 0.001), whereas delivery within 24 h did not differ significantly between groups (75.7% vs. 71.1%; p = 0.183). Dinoprostone was also associated with a higher spontaneous vaginal delivery rate (95.0% vs. 90.0%; p = 0.015) and a lower cesarean delivery rate (3.2% vs. 8.2%; p = 0.006). Intrapartum fever occurred less frequently with dinoprostone (1.4% vs. 4.2%; p = 0.036), while no statistically significant differences were observed in measured neonatal outcomes, including NICU admission. Among multiparous women undergoing term induction of labor, dinoprostone was associated with a shorter cervical ripening-to-delivery interval, higher spontaneous vaginal delivery rates, and lower cesarean rates than balloon catheter. Delivery within 24 h was similar between groups, with no significant differences in measured neonatal outcomes.
