## Concurrent biliary atresia and intestinal atresia: a systematic review of clinical characteristics, pathogenesis, management, and outcomes.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:19
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 印度 · Indira Gandhi Institute of Medical Sciences
- 通讯作者: Ali MM (mohdmokarramali1990@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06665-3
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06665-3) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42853333/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统评估胆道闭锁（BA）合并肠闭锁（IA）的临床表现、发病机制、治疗策略及结局。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入15项研究、98例患者；BA患者中合并IA约0.5–5%，十二指肠闭锁最常见（0–1.5%），其次为空回肠闭锁（0.5–5%）。联合病例死亡率超过50%；60天前行Kasai门肠吻合可改善结局，但合并短肠会限制Roux-en-Y支长度并增加胆管炎风险。
- 临床启示：IA手术时应仔细评估胆道，对持续胆汁淤积及时排查BA，并尽早行Kasai门肠吻合以改善预后。

**原文摘要**：The coexistence of biliary atresia (BA) and intestinal atresia (IA) is exceptionally rare. Delayed recognition of BA in neonates undergoing surgery for IA may adversely affect outcomes. This systematic review aimed to evaluate the available evidence regarding the clinical presentation, pathogenesis, management strategies, and outcomes of patients with concurrent BA and IA. A systematic literature search of PubMed, MEDLINE, and Google Scholar was conducted for studies published up to December 2025. Relevant studies encompassing case reports, case series, and retrospective reviews were identified. Data regarding demographics, type of intestinal atresia, associated anomalies, diagnostic methods, surgical management, and outcomes were extracted. Fifteen studies comprising 98 patients met inclusion criteria. The association of BA with IA is rare, affecting 0.5-5% of BA patients. Duodenal atresia (DA) represents the most common intestinal association (0-1.5% of BA), followed by jejunoileal atresia (JIA) (0.5-5%). Among 98 identified cases, mortality exceeds 50% in combined presentations. Early Kasai portoenterostomy (KPE) before 60 days improves outcomes, but concomitant short bowel limits Roux-en-Y limb length, increasing cholangitis risk. Concurrent BA and IA represent a rare but severe neonatal condition associated with substantial morbidity and mortality. Careful biliary assessment during IA surgery, prompt investigation of persistent cholestasis, and timely Kasai portoenterostomy are essential for improving outcomes.

## Incidence and clinical outcomes of postoperative small bowel obstruction after neonatal abdominal surgery: a single-center retrospective study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:19
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 870-8511 · Oita Prefectural Hospital
- 通讯作者: Fukuhara M (fukuhara_masao@yahoo.co.jp)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06676-0
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06676-0) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42853310/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估新生儿腹部手术后术后小肠梗阻（SBO）的发生率、管理及临床结局。
- 核心发现/数据：在312例出生30天内接受腹部手术的新生儿中，19例（6.1%）发生术后SBO，共29次事件；首次SBO距初次手术中位间隔10.9个月，57.9%在1年内发生。首次SBO发作中52.6%需手术干预，21.1%需肠切除，36.8%复发，SBO治疗期间无死亡。1、5、10、20年累积发生率分别为4.6%、7.3%、8.1%、13.2%。
- 临床启示：新生儿腹部手术后SBO多在1年内发生，但长期风险持续存在，应重视长期随访；较高的手术干预率和复发率提示其是新生儿腹部手术后重要的长期并发症。

**原文摘要**：The long-term incidence and clinical course of neonatal abdominal surgery remain unclear. We evaluated the incidence, management, and outcomes of postoperative SBO following neonatal abdominal surgery. We retrospectively reviewed the records of neonates who underwent abdominal surgery within 30 days of birth in 2000-2025. Patients with postoperative SBO were identified, and their clinical characteristics, treatment, and outcomes were analyzed. The cumulative incidence was estimated using a competing risk analysis, with death before SBO treated as a competing event. Among 312 neonates, 19 (6.1%) developed postoperative SBO, with 29 SBO events. The median interval from the initial surgery to the first SBO was 10.9 months, and 11 patients (57.9%) developed SBO within 1 year. During the first SBO episode, surgical intervention was required in 10 patients (52.6%). Bowel resection was required in 4 (21.1%). Seven patients developed recurrence (36.8%). No deaths occurred during SBO treatment. The cumulative incidence at 1, 5, 10, and 20 years was 4.6%, 7.3%, 8.1%, and 13.2%, respectively. SBO after neonatal abdominal surgery frequently occurs within 1 year, but the risk persists for a long time. The high surgical intervention and recurrence rates support its importance as a long-term complication of after neonatal abdominal surgery.

## Endotracheal balloon bursting: modified technique for percutaneous ultrasound-guided balloon puncture to reverse tracheal occlusion in congenital diaphragmatic hernia.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:19
- 期刊: Fetal Diagn Ther · 中科院医学3区(妇产科学3区) · IF 1.7 · JCR Q3
- 通讯作者: Del Pozo-Orozco M
- DOI: 10.1159/fdt/acuag009
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1159/fdt/acuag009) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42853731/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估改良经皮超声引导球囊穿刺/破裂作为先天性膈疝胎儿FETO术后逆转气管封堵首选方法的可行性及围产结局。
- 核心发现/数据：2012年1月至2025年12月纳入88例孤立性CDH FETO病例（左侧74例、右侧14例），FETO平均孕周29.6周，球囊移除平均孕周33.8周，40.7%为急诊操作。经皮超声引导球囊穿刺可行率为80%，在选定病例中技术成功并使球囊破裂的比例高达98.5%；球囊移除后无胎儿出血或宫内胎儿死亡，出生后均确认气道通畅。
- 临床启示：该改良技术安全、可行，可作为FETO术后逆转气管封堵的首选尝试，从而避免再次胎儿镜手术。

**原文摘要**：Fetuses with congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH) treated with fetal endoscopic tracheal occlusion (FETO) require a second procedure to reverse tracheal occlusion before birth. In this study, we aimed to report the feasibility and perinatal outcomes of a modified technique for ultrasound-guided balloon bursting used as a primary attempt. From January 2012 to December 2025 a prospective cohort of isolated CDH cases treated with FETO was included in a management protocol for balloon removal by a percutaneous balloon bursting using a 22-gauge needle in a transversal view of the fetal trachea under simultaneous 2D and color Doppler ultrasound guidance (dual imaging) to avoid vessels. The feasibility of this technique, procedure-related characteristics, complications and outcomes were evaluated. During the study period, 88 (74 left-sided, and 14 right-sided) CDH were treated with FETO at a mean GA of 29.6 weeks. The balloon was removed at a mean GA of 33.8 weeks, and as an emergency procedure in up to 40.7% of the cases. Percutaneous ultrasound-guided balloon punction was feasible in 80% of the cases. Our technique was technically successful and allowed balloon rupture in up to 98.5% of the selected cases. No cases of fetal bleeding or intrauterine fetal demise after balloon removal were observed and, in all cases, a patent airway was confirmed after birth. Implementing a modified technique for percutaneous ultrasound-guided balloon bursting is safe and feasible and allows balloon bursting avoiding the need for a second fetoscopic procedure for the reversal of FETO.

## Comparative of Robotic-Assisted versus Laparoscopic-Assisted Modified Soave Pull-Through with Short Muscular Cuff Anastomosis in Infants Under 3 Months with Classical Hirschsprung Disease.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 10:25
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Children Hospital of Guizhou province
- 通讯作者: Zheng Z (zebing1988@sina.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163453
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163453) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42854893/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：比较机器人辅助与腹腔镜辅助改良Soave拖出术联合短肌鞘吻合术治疗3个月以下经典Hirschsprung病婴儿的临床疗效

**原文摘要**：To compare the clinical efficacy of robotic-assisted versus laparoscopic-assisted modified Soave pull-through with short muscular cuff anastomosis in infants under 3 months of age with classical Hirschsprung's disease (HSCR). A retrospective analysis was conducted on 35 infants under 3 months with classical HSCR who underwent radical surgery at our hospital from January 2020 to December 2024. Among them, 19 underwent robotic-assisted surgery (robotic group), and 16 underwent laparoscopic-assisted surgery (laparoscopic group). Baseline characteristics, perioperative outcomes, postoperative complications, and defecation function were compared between the two groups. Baseline characteristics were comparable between groups (P > 0.05). Compared with the laparoscopic group, the robotic group had significantly shorter total operative time [(142.5±16.8) min vs. (171.3±24.6) min; P < 0.05], shorter anal dissection time [(20.8±5.2) min vs. (36.7±9.8) min; P < 0.05], less intraoperative blood loss [(6.5±2.4) ml vs. (11.0±5.6) ml; P < 0.05], and shorter postoperative hospital stays [(7.2±1.6) d vs. (8.9±2.4) d; P < 0.05]. The incidence of postoperative enterocolitis was significantly lower in the robotic group [0(0%) vs. 4(25.0%); P < 0.05]. Regarding defecation function, the robotic group showed fewer daily soiling episodes [(0.8±0.6) vs. (1.6±0.9); P < 0.05] and higher Kelly scores [(2.9±0.3) points vs. (2.4±0.7) points; P < 0.05]. No significant differences were observed between the two groups in terms of anastomotic complications, need for constipation intervention, daily bowel movement frequency, or excellent rate of defecation function (P > 0.05). In this non-randomized, retrospective comparison, robotic-assisted modified Soave pull-through with short muscular cuff anastomosis was found to be safe and feasible for infants under 3 months with classical HSCR. The robotic approach was associated with shorter operative time, reduced intraoperative blood loss, shorter hospital stays, and may be associated with improved mid-term defecation function compared with laparoscopic assistance.

## A massively parallel reporter assay of MECP2cis-regulatory elements reveals genetic candidates for male-biased autism.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:25
- 期刊: Am J Hum Genet · 中科院生物学1区(遗传学1区) · IF 7.7 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Baylor College of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Zoghbi H (hzoghbi@bcm.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.ajhg.2026.09.011
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2026.09.011) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42854690/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过大规模并行报告分析解析MECP2顺式调控元件及自闭症相关变异的功能，探索男性偏倚自闭症的遗传基础。
- 核心发现/数据：在人神经元中绘制了MECP2顺式调控元件内的转录因子结合位点，并鉴定出两个改变调控元件活性的非编码变异，与家系中男性偏倚自闭症表型相关。其中一个启动子变异破坏NFY结合，使MECP2表达降低约30%，该降低幅度在小鼠中可导致社交缺陷、多动和焦虑样表型。
- 临床启示：提示男性自闭症或神经发育障碍患者可考虑评估MECP2非编码调控变异，尤其是启动子区NFY结合位点。该研究为解释X染色体剂量敏感基因相关“缺失遗传力”及遗传咨询提供了新线索。

**原文摘要**：Autism affects male individuals four times more often than female individuals, yet the basis of this sex bias remains unclear. One hypothesis is that this sex bias is due to genetic variants in X chromosome genes where haploinsufficiency causes neurodevelopmental disorders (NDDs). In heterozygous XX female probands, a complete loss-of-function variant in one of these genes often causes syndromic NDD, but the same variant is too deleterious for survival in hemizygous XY males. A mild hypomorphic variant, however, may be tolerated in a female carrier but may produce milder neurological phenotypes in a male proband. We tested this by investigating cis-regulatory elements (CREs) of MECP2, a dosage-sensitive gene on the X chromosome where total loss-of-function variants cause Rett syndrome in female children and severe, usually fatal neonatal encephalopathies in male infants. Using a massively parallel reporter assay in human neurons, we mapped transcription factor binding sites within MECP2 CREs and tested autism-associated variants for functional impact. We identified two noncoding variants that change CRE activity and are associated with male-biased autism phenotypes in the proband pedigrees. One of these, a promoter variant, disrupts NFY binding and reduces MECP2 expression by ∼30%, a magnitude that produces social deficits, hyperactivity, and anxiety-like phenotypes in mice. These findings suggest noncoding MECP2 variants can cause idiopathic, male-biased autism and provide a framework for uncovering regulatory variants in other X chromosome neurodevelopmental genes that may contribute to autism's missing heritability.

## Hepatic manifestations of ciliopathies: genetic causes and clinical update.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:25
- 期刊: Eur J Pediatr · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区) · IF 2.9 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: SE5 9RS · GI and Nutrition Centre
- 通讯作者: Hegarty R (roberthegarty@nhs.net)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00431-026-07468-3
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-026-07468-3) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42855640/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统综述纤毛病累及肝脏的遗传病因、纤毛在胆道发育中的作用及儿童与成人肝胆临床表型。
- 核心发现/数据：初级纤毛功能障碍破坏胆管细胞信号和胆板重塑，导致先天性肝纤维化、门脉高压、肝囊肿、脂肪性肝病、转氨酶升高及新生儿胆汁淤积/胆道闭锁等谱系表现。经典基因PKHD1、PKD1、PKD2仍为核心，GANAB、ALG8、PRKCSH、SEC63、DNAJB11、TULP3、WDR家族及DCDC2、PKD1L1等新兴基因拓展了遗传景观；ARPKD、Bardet-Biedl、Joubert和WDR相关病多在儿童至青少年期起病，ADPKD/ADPLD多成年期起病。
- 临床启示：对不明原因儿童肝胆病，尤其新生儿胆汁淤积/胆道闭

**原文摘要**：Ciliopathies are genetically heterogeneous disorders of primary cilia that frequently involve the liver in childhood, producing a spectrum of hepatobiliary pathology. Primary cilia on cholangiocytes and hepatic progenitors are mechano-, chemo-, and osmo-sensors essential for remodelling of the ductal plate, the embryologic precursor of the liver's intrahepatic bile duct system. Disruption of the components of primary cilia (basal body, intraflagellar transport (IFT), transition-zone, trafficking, and endoplasmic reticulum (ER)/Golgi protein homeostasis components) leads to ductal plate malformation, aberrant biliary branching, and progressive periductal fibrosis. Classical loci (PKHD1, PKD1, PKD2) more commonly associated with cystic kidney disease remain central to aetiology, while ER/glycosylation protein homeostasis genes (GANAB, ALG8, PRKCSH, SEC63, DNAJB11), trafficking/IFT-A and WDR/dynein-2 components (TULP3, WDR family) are increasingly recognised. Liver disease most often appears from childhood to adolescence in autosomal recessive polycystic kidney disease (ARPKD), Bardet-Biedl syndrome, Joubert syndrome, and WDR-related disorders, while autosomal dominant polycystic kidney and liver disease (ADPKD and ADPLD) usually presents in adulthood. The main disease patterns also vary: congenital hepatic fibrosis and portal hypertension in ARPKD, hepatic cysts in ADPKD, steatotic liver disease in Bardet-Biedl syndrome, and transaminitis or fibrocystic liver disease in Joubert and WDR-related disorders. Emerging contributors such as DCDC2 and PKD1L1 implicate microtubule-cilium interactions and mechanosensory dysfunction in neonatal cholestasis and biliary atresia. Diagnosis increasingly relies on broad multigene panels or exome/genome sequencing, with functional assays guiding interpretation of novel alleles.  This narrative review synthesises contemporary knowledge on ciliary roles in biliary development, the clinical hepatic phenotypes observed in children and adults, and the evolving genetic landscape with emphasis on discoveries from recent sequencing cohorts and functional studies. • Ciliopathies are genetically heterogeneous disorders in which primary cilia dysfunction disrupts cholangiocyte signalling and ductal plate remodelling, leading to a spectrum of paediatric hepatobiliary disease ranging from congenital hepatic fibrosis and portal hypertension to cystic liver disease, steatotic liver disease, and neonatal cholestasis. • Recent genomic and functional studies have broadened the spectrum of ciliopathy-associated genes implicated in paediatric hepatic disease. Clinicians caring for these children should maintain a high index of suspicion as the range of reported phenotypes continues to expand.
