## Health-related quality of life following thoracoscopic repair in children with long-gap esophageal atresia.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 01:53
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 50-367 Wroclaw · Wroclaw Medical University
- 通讯作者: Rozensztrauch A (anna.rozensztrauch@umw.edu.pl)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163258
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163258) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42349546/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估经纯胸腔镜手术修复的长段型食管闭锁（LGEA）儿童的健康相关生活质量（HRQoL），并与远端气管食管瘘（DTEF）患儿进行比较。
- 核心发现/数据：LGEA与DTEF患儿在EA-QoL各领域评分无显著差异（p>0.05），如进食领域78.6 vs 75（p=0.375）、躯体健康与治疗领域75 vs 66.7（p=0.638）、社交隔离与压力领域75 vs 81.3（p=0.476）。在2-7岁LGEA患儿中，食管扩张次数与进食领域（r=-0.62，p=0.014）及躯体健康与治疗领域（r=-0.53，p=0.044）评分呈负相关。
- 临床启示：LGEA患儿经胸腔镜分期牵引修复后的生活质量可与DTEF患儿相当，但早产和多次食管扩张可能增加HRQoL降低的风险，提示需关注此类高暴露患儿的长期随访与支持。

**原文摘要**：By retaining the native esophagus and minimizing postoperative morbidity, the thoracoscopic approach contributes to improved functional outcomes for patients with esophageal atresia (EA). The aim of this study was to evaluate HRQoL-including digestive, respiratory, and feeding outcomes in children treated exclusively by a thoracoscopic approach, with a specific focus on comparing outcomes between patients with LGEA (Gross type A and B) and those with DTEF (Gross type C or D). LGEA was treated using the internal traction technique with staged repair, 25 children remained (16 aged 2-7, 9 aged 8-17), the comparison group included 69 children with DTEF (34 aged 2-7, 35 aged 8-17) who underwent thoracoscopic primary anastomosis. Final EA-QOL scores, based on clinical data and validated instruments (the EA-QOL questionnaires), were evaluated predefined hypotheses, with statistical significance set at p < 0.05. No significant differences were observed between LGEA and DTEF across EA-QoL domains (p>0.05). In LGEA children aged 2-7 years, the number of esophageal dilatations was negatively correlated with HRQoL in the Eating domain (r=-0.62, p=0.014) and the Physical Health and Treatment domain (r=-0.53, p=0.044). In the Eating domain, median EA-QoL scores were 78.6 for LGEA and 75 for DTEF (p=0.375). Physical Health and Treatment scores were 75 vs. 66.7 (p=0.638), and Social Isolation and Stress scores were 75 vs. 81.3 (p=0.476). HRQoL in children with LGEA is comparable to DTEF, although prematurity and multiple dilatations may increase the risk of reduced HRQoL.

## Child Physical Abuse and Cervical Spine Injury: A Prospective Observational Study of over 1,200 Children in the Western Pediatric Cervical Spine Study.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 08:23
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University of Utah
- 通讯作者: Western Pediatric Cervical Spine Study Group
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163445
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163445) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42700814/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：描述疑似儿童身体虐待患儿中颈椎损伤的发生率与损伤模式，并评估相关危险因素及影像学检查价值。
- 核心发现/数据：在1,251名疑似受虐儿童中，6%（75例）确诊颈椎损伤，以韧带损伤最常见（68%），其次为脊髓损伤（19%）；61%合并创伤性脑损伤，GCS 3-13（OR 3.29）和气管插管（OR 3.07）与颈椎损伤显著相关。不稳定性韧带损伤仅7例，CT可识别所有行CT检查者，无一例神经系统完好且影像正常患儿出现漏诊的不稳定损伤。
- 临床启示：对疑似受虐且意识障碍（GCS<14）的患儿，CT有助于颈椎损伤评估；若CT正常，即使意识状态异常也可考虑移除颈椎固定，但需警惕个别MRI才能发现的损伤。

**原文摘要**：The incidence and patterns of cervical spine injury (CSI) have not been well described in children who present after suspected child physical abuse (CPA). The purpose of this study was to describe the pattern of CSI in patients evaluated for suspected CPA in a large, prospectively collected dataset. This is a secondary analysis of a prospectively collected, multicenter, pediatric CSI dataset which included 72 trauma centers across the United States. All children with suspected CPA were included, with concerns for CPA determined by each participating center. All children had cervical spine imaging within 24 hours of injury. Among 19,995 pediatric blunt trauma patients, 1,251 children with suspected CPA were identified. Median age was 6 months (IQR 2-12), 42% female, 19% presented with a GCS less than 14, and 11% required intubation. Concomitant traumatic brain injury (TBI) was present in 61% of patients. Overall, 75 children (6%) were diagnosed with CSI. Ligamentous injury was most common (68%), followed by spinal cord injury (19%). No patient underwent operative cervical spine stabilization. Unstable ligamentous injury occurred in seven patients. Four were identified on CT, three were detected on MRI. One patient had a normal initial XR prior to MRI diagnosis with a presenting GCS of 10. On multivariable regression, GCS 3-13 (OR 3.29, 95% CI 1.61-6.47), and intubation (OR 3.07, 95% CI 1.57-6.27) were associated with CSI. In this large prospective multicenter cohort, CSI occurred in 6% of children evaluated for suspected CPA and consisted primarily of ligamentous injuries. Low GCS and intubation were strongly associated with CSI. Unstable injuries were rare, CT identified all injuries in which it was performed, and no missed unstable injuries were observed in neurologically intact children with normal screening radiographs. These findings suggest that CT may be a useful adjunct for CSI evaluation in obtunded patients with suspected CPA (GCS < 14) and support a low threshold for advanced imaging in this population. In children with suspected physical abuse who undergo CT imaging, our findings support that a normal CT may be sufficient to allow removal of cervical immobilization regardless of mental status.

## Rectal bleeding in early infancy: a warning sign of necrotizing enterocolitis that should not be overlooked in high-risk infants.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 08:23
- 期刊: Dig Liver Dis
- 国家/单位: 意大利 · "Bambino Gesù" Children's Hospital IRCCS
- 通讯作者: Ronci S (sara.ronci@opbg.net)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.dld.2026.08.008
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dld.2026.08.008) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42701088/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估3月龄以下婴儿直肠出血的病因、管理及结局，重点关注坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）的风险。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入91例婴儿，中位发病年龄20天；NEC为最常见病因（35.1%），其次为孤立性直肠出血（22.0%）和感染性胃肠炎（16.5%）。NEC患儿中25.0%需手术，其中先天性心脏病（占78.1%）和早产儿（占21.9%）占比高；住院死亡率2.2%。
- 临床启示：婴儿早期直肠出血即使看似良性，也需警惕NEC，尤其对先天性心脏病及早产等高危儿应尽早识别并密切监测，以改善预后。

**原文摘要**：Rectal bleeding in infants younger than 3 months may arise from benign conditions but can also represent an early sign of necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC). We aimed to describe etiology, management, and outcomes of rectal bleeding in early infancy. We retrospectively analysed data from patients presenting with rectal bleeding between 2015 and 2024, collecting demographics, comorbidities, diagnostic work-up, treatments, recurrence, and outcomes. Ninety-one infants were included, with a median age at onset of 20 days; 34 (37.4%) were preterm and 43 (47.3%) had congenital heart disease (CHD). NEC was the most frequent diagnosis (35.1%), followed by isolated rectal bleeding (IRB, 22.0%) and infectious gastroenteritis (16.5%). Among infants diagnosed with NEC, 25.0% required surgery; it occurred predominantly in those with CHD (25/32, 78.1%) and preterm infants (7/32, 21.9%). Stool pathogens were detected in 25.3% of cases. Bleeding recurrence occurred in 16.5% of infants. In-hospital mortality was 2.2%. NEC is the leading cause of rectal bleeding in early infancy, followed by IRB. Although IRB is often benign, rectal bleeding should always prompt careful evaluation, particularly in high-risk infants such as those with CHD and those born preterm, in whom rectal bleeding may represent an early manifestation of NEC. Early recognition and close monitoring are essential to optimize outcomes.

## A rare combination of gastroschisis, jejunal atresia, colonic atresia, and midgut malrotation in a newborn: a case report and literature review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 08:23
- 期刊: Int J Surg Case Rep · 中科院医学4区(外科4区) · IF 0.6
- 国家/单位: Palestine · Al-Mezan Speciality Hospital
- 通讯作者: Saada J
- DOI: 10.1097/RC9.0000000000000755
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/RC9.0000000000000755) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42699884/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告一例同时合并腹裂、空肠闭锁、结肠闭锁及中肠旋转不良的新生儿罕见病例，并回顾相关文献。
- 核心发现/数据：男婴于36周产前诊断腹裂，生后即出现明显腹胀和胆汁性胃液。术中证实为IIIb型空肠闭锁、I型右结肠闭锁、中肠旋转不良及未使用的小结肠；仅在一次完整远端肠管探查后才确诊结肠闭锁。行一期肠吻合、Ladd术及腹壁关闭，术后第2周实现全口喂养，但第6周发生新生儿坏死性小肠结肠炎、产ESBL肺炎克雷伯菌感染性休克及多器官衰竭，于生后第48天死亡。
- 临床启示：复杂型腹裂合并多发消化道畸形时，术中必须仔细检查远端肠管以免漏诊闭锁；术后营养支持与感染防控极为困难，死亡率高。

**原文摘要**：Gastroschisis is a congenital full-thickness anterior abdominal wall defect that allows bowel evisceration without a covering sac. While most cases are isolated and have good outcomes, complex gastroschisis involves associated intestinal pathology and carries significantly higher morbidity and mortality. The simultaneous occurrence of jejunal atresia, colonic atresia, and midgut malrotation in the setting of gastroschisis is extraordinarily rare. A male infant was delivered at 36 weeks' gestation following an antenatal diagnosis of gastroschisis. At birth, marked abdominal distension and bilious nasogastric aspirate raised immediate concern for an associated anomaly. Intraoperative assessment revealed type IIIb jejunal atresia, type I right colonic atresia, midgut malrotation, and an unused microcolon. The colonic atresia was only identified after further inspection to ensure distal bowel patency. Single-stage repair was performed, consisting of primary jejunoileal and colocolic anastomoses, Ladd's procedure, and abdominal wall closure. The early postoperative course was encouraging, with full oral feeding achieved by the second postoperative week. However, by week 6, the infant developed medical necrotizing enterocolitis, septic shock due to an ESBL-producing Klebsiella, and multiorgan failure, and died on day 48 of life. Complex gastroschisis with multiple gastrointestinal anomalies severely limits intestinal function, increases susceptibility to infection, and complicates operative and postoperative recovery. Even following a technically successful repair, optimizing nutrition and preventing sepsis remain difficult. Careful intraoperative distal bowel evaluation is essential to avoid missed atresias. Mortality remains notable in this exceedingly rare combination due to the cumulative burden of underlying anomalies and infectious complications.

## Adaptability of minimally invasive thoracoscopic repair for congenital diaphragmatic hernia: a neonatal case report from a low-income country.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 08:23
- 期刊: Int J Surg Case Rep · 中科院医学4区(外科4区) · IF 0.6
- 国家/单位: Afghanistan · French Medical Institute for Mothers and Children
- 通讯作者: Mohammadi MH
- DOI: 10.1097/RC9.0000000000000868
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/RC9.0000000000000868) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42699850/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估在低收入国家资源有限条件下，新生儿先天性膈疝（CDH）经胸腔镜微创修复的可行性与结局。
- 核心发现/数据：一名2日龄、体重2.5kg的女性新生儿因左侧Bochdalek疝（含胃、脾、左肾及肠管）伴严重呼吸窘迫接受胸腔镜修复，采用三个5mm穿刺孔、CO₂气压5mmHg、流量1L/min，直接间断缝合5×5cm膈肌缺损（未用补片），手术时间35分钟。术后第1天拔管、第4天出院，随访1年无复发。
- 临床启示：在资源受限地区，对经选择的合适新生儿实施胸腔镜膈疝一期修复是可行的，可获得良好的短期和长期预后。

**原文摘要**：Congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH) is a rare but potentially life-threatening congenital anomaly characterized by herniation of abdominal organs into the thoracic cavity, resulting in respiratory compromise and pulmonary hypoplasia. A 2-day-old female neonate weighing 2.5 kg presented with severe respiratory distress, cyanosis, and a scaphoid abdomen. Chest radiography and computed tomography revealed a large left-sided Bochdalek hernia containing the stomach, spleen, left kidney, and bowel loops, causing compression of the left lung and mediastinal shift. After stabilization with mechanical ventilation, the patient underwent thoracoscopic repair. The patient was positioned in the right lateral decubitus position, and three 5-mm ports were inserted. Carbon dioxide insufflation was maintained at 5 mmHg with a flow rate of 1 L/min. The herniated viscera were reduced, and a 5 × 5 cm diaphragmatic defect was closed primarily with interrupted 3-0 polypropylene sutures, without mesh reinforcement. The operative time from skin incision to wound closure was 35 minutes. The postoperative course was uneventful, with extubation on the first postoperative day and discharge on the fourth postoperative day. No recurrence was observed over 1 year of follow-up. This case demonstrates the feasibility of thoracoscopic primary repair of CDH in a carefully selected neonate within a resource-limited setting, resulting in favorable short- and long-term outcomes.

## Ileal duplication cyst linked to type 3A ileal atresia: a rare case report and review of the literature.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 08:23
- 期刊: Int J Surg Case Rep · 中科院医学4区(外科4区) · IF 0.6
- 国家/单位: Sudan · University of Khartoum
- 通讯作者: Yasin SA
- DOI: 10.1097/RC9.0000000000000761
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/RC9.0000000000000761) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42699543/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告一例罕见的新生儿回肠重复囊肿合并3A型末端回肠闭锁，并探讨其可能的共同病因。  
- 核心发现/数据：足月男婴（2900 g）出生后第3天出现低位肠梗阻，第5天剖腹探查证实3A型回肠闭锁合并4×4 cm交通性重复囊肿，与末端回肠共享肌壁和血供，位于回盲瓣近端12 cm处；切除15 cm扩张近端肠管、囊肿及远端段后行一期吻合。组织病理及文献支持产前肠系膜血管意外是两者并存的可能病因。  
- 临床启示：对于新生儿期罕见的回肠闭锁合并重复囊肿，早期手术切除并一期吻合有效；产前血管意外机制有助于指导诊断，并在资源有限环境下改善预后。

**原文摘要**：Jejuno-ileal atresias represent a significant cause of neonatal intestinal obstruction, occurring in approximately 1 in 3000-5000 live births. Terminal ileal atresia, classified as type 3A according to the Louw and Barnard system, specifically involves a mesenteric defect. Ileal duplication cysts, typically cystic or tubular structures sharing a common muscular wall and blood supply with the native ileum, can be communicating or non-communicating and pose risks of obstruction, volvulus, bleeding, or perforation. This case highlights terminal ileal atresia (type 3A) concurrent with an ileal duplication cyst. A term male neonate (2900 g), born via a normal delivery, presented on day 3 with low intestinal obstruction: failure to pass meconium, abdominal distension, bilious aspirates, and air-fluid levels on X-ray. Laparotomy on day 5 confirmed type 3A atresia with a 4 × 4 cm communicating duplication cyst that shared a wall and blood supply with the terminal ileum, 12 cm proximal to the ileocecal valve; 15-cm dilated proximal bowel was resected, the cyst was excised, the distal segment was flushed, and a primary anastomosis was performed. Antenatal mesenteric vascular accidents likely explain this co-occurrence, causing differential healing in which the spared segments form duplications. Mechanisms include volvulus or intussusception induced by the cyst. This case underscores antenatal mesenteric vascular accidents as a likely etiology of concurrent ileal atresia and duplication cysts, supported by histopathological evidence and prior theories. Surgical resection with anastomosis remains effective for such non-separable cysts. Early diagnosis and intervention optimize neonatal outcomes in such uncommon associations in resource-limited settings.

## Successful surgical intervention and management of biliary atresia in a 4-month-old infant: a case report.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 08:23
- 期刊: Int J Surg Case Rep · 中科院医学4区(外科4区) · IF 0.6
- 国家/单位: Afghanistan · Afghan Arya Specialty Hospital Herat
- 通讯作者: Yama M
- DOI: 10.1097/RC9.0000000000000888
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/RC9.0000000000000888) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42699514/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告一例4月龄婴儿在资源有限环境中晚期就诊的胆道闭锁成功手术治疗的罕见案例。
- 核心发现/数据：患儿总胆红素18.8 mg/dL、直接胆红素17.2 mg/dL，超声示胆囊闭锁及三角索征；行扩大肝门分离和Roux-en-Y肝门空肠吻合术后，辅以糖皮质激素和熊去氧胆酸治疗，肝功能及临床状况持续改善，随访时黄疸完全消退。
- 临床启示：即使超过传统60-90天手术窗口，经谨慎选择的晚期胆道闭锁患儿仍可能实现成功胆汁引流；延迟就诊不应自动排除手术治疗。

**原文摘要**：Biliary atresia (BA) is a progressive fibro-obliterative disorder of the biliary tract that causes cholestasis, cirrhosis, and liver failure if left untreated. Kasai portoenterostomy (KP) is the standard initial treatment, with better outcomes when performed early. We report a rare case of successful surgical management of late-presenting BA at 4 months of age in a resource-limited setting. A 4-month-old girl presented with persistent jaundice, pale stools, dark urine, and hepatomegaly. She had previously been treated for neonatal hepatitis without improvement. Laboratory evaluation showed conjugated hyperbilirubinemia (total bilirubin 18.8 mg/dL, direct bilirubin 17.2 mg/dL). Ultrasonography demonstrated an atretic gallbladder and a triangular cord sign suggestive of BA. Due to limited access to advanced diagnostic modalities, the diagnosis was established based on clinical, laboratory, ultra-sonographic, and intraoperative findings. Extended hilar dissection and a wide Roux-en-Y hepatic portoenterostomy were performed. Postoperative management included corticosteroids and ursodeoxycholic acid. Progressive improvement in liver function tests and clinical status was observed, with complete resolution of jaundice during follow-up. Although KP performed within 60-90 days provides the best outcomes, selected late-presenting patients may still achieve successful bile drainage. This case emphasizes the importance of careful patient selection, meticulous surgical technique, and optimized postoperative management, even beyond the traditional therapeutic window. Delayed presentation should not automatically exclude infants with BA from surgical treatment. Early recognition, referral, and appropriate surgical management remain essential to improve outcomes in late-presenting cases.

## Clinical outcomes and longitudinal growth trajectories in preterm infants fed donor human milk versus formula: A retrospective cohort study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 08:23
- 期刊: Medicine (Baltimore) · 中科院医学4区(医学：内科4区) · IF 2.0 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 韩国 · Kyung Hee University College of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Chung SH
- DOI: 10.1097/MD.0000000000050588
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000050588) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42700076/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估极低出生体重儿接受机构性3周捐献母乳策略与早产配方奶相比的临床结局及纵向生长轨迹差异。
- 核心发现/数据：配方奶组坏死性小肠结肠炎（≥2期）发生率高于捐献母乳组（14.0% vs 4.4%），调整后风险显著增加（aOR=6.27，95%CI 1.37-42.71，P=0.016），且24小时禁食 episodes 更多（0.3±0.5 vs 0.1±0.3，P=0.010）。出生后3周时捐献母乳组体重、身长、头围z评分均显著更低，但在出院时两组z评分差异消失，且在预设±0.5 z范围内达到等效。
- 临床启示：短期捐献母乳喂养可降低NEC和喂养不耐受风险，早期生长迟缓可通过后续强化喂养策略代偿；但维持捐献母乳项目需国家层面支持，否则系统资源不足可导致项目中断。

**原文摘要**：This study evaluated the clinical efficacy and growth impacts of an institutional 3-week donor human milk (DHM) strategy in very low birth weight infants before and after its discontinuation due to staffing shortages and financial deficits. This retrospective study analyzed 125 consecutive very low birth weight infants from 2 eras: DHM group (n = 68; January 2020-February 2023) and Formula group (fed preterm formula; n = 57; March 2023-December 2025). The primary outcomes were necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC ≥ stage 2), cumulative 24-hour nil per os episodes, and anthropometric z-scores at birth, 3 weeks, and discharge. NEC was analyzed using Firth penalized-likelihood regression, and serial z-scores using linear mixed-effects models with formal equivalence testing at discharge. NEC occurred in 3 of 68 infants (4.4%) in the DHM group and 8 of 57 (14.0%) in the Formula group (absolute risk difference 9.6%, 95% confidence interval: -2.0 to 19.9). The preterm formula era was associated with a higher adjusted risk of NEC ≥ stage 2 (adjusted odds ratio [OR] = 6.27, 95% confidence interval: 1.37-42.71, P = .016) and with more 24-hour nil per os episodes (0.3 ± 0.5 vs 0.1 ± 0.3, P = .010). It is one of the most common causes of neonatal intestinal obstruction. A dermoid cyst arises from the trapping of ectodermal elements during embryonic development. A 6-day-old female neonate was brought to the hospital with a history of bilious emesis and failure to pass stool. The antenatal history was uneventful. She was diagnosed with intestinal obstruction. The patient was treated initially as a case of clinical sepsis and then treated surgically. Through a transverse incision, the abdomen was explored, and midgut malrotation with Ladd's bands was found under an adhering ovarian cyst, which was diagnosed as a dermoid cyst by histopathology. The simultaneous dual-cause obstruction in this case has prompted us to report it. Malrotation results when the midgut fails to complete its 270° counterclockwise rotation in utero, leading to anomalous positioning and often to fibrous Ladd's bands that cross and compress the duodenum. Meanwhile, congenital ovarian dermoid cysts can contribute to obstruction through adhesive bands or direct mass effect. The concurrent occurrence highlights the importance of considering multiple simultaneous etiologies in diagnosis and surgical planning. Considering multiple congenital defects, such as malrotation and dermoid ovarian cysts, as a dual cause of intestinal obstruction is important in a condition that has a large differential diagnosis.

## Adult intestinal malrotation with Ladd's bands causing acute small-bowel obstruction in a virgin abdomen: a case report.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 08:23
- 期刊: Int J Surg Case Rep · 中科院医学4区(外科4区) · IF 0.6
- 国家/单位: 瑞士 · Hirslanden Clinic St. Anna
- 通讯作者: Delko T
- DOI: 10.1097/RC9.0000000000000780
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/RC9.0000000000000780) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42699889/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告一例成人肠旋转不良伴Ladd索带导致无手术史腹部急性小肠梗阻的罕见病例，强调其诊断与治疗要点。
- 核心发现/数据：该病例为三十余岁男性，表现为急性绞窄性腹痛、恶心呕吐及腹胀，无既往腹部手术史；增强CT显示I型肠旋转不良（非旋转）的典型三联征，术中证实Ladd索带引起完全性小肠梗阻，行Ladd手术联合阑尾切除术及预防性结肠固定术，术后第6天出院，12个月随访症状完全消失。成人肠旋转不良仅占全部病例的0.2%-0.5%，本例在“处女腹”中由先天性腹膜索带致梗阻极为罕见。
- 临床启示：对无手术史的成人急性小肠梗阻，应警惕先天性肠旋转不良可能；增强CT具诊断价值，急性期采用开放Ladd手术可获得良好预后。

**原文摘要**：Intestinal malrotation is a rare congenital anomaly that results from incomplete midgut rotation. Although 90% of cases present in infancy, adult presentations account for only 0.2%-0.5% of cases. Congenital peritoneal bands causing obstruction in a virgin abdomen are rarely considered, making this a clinically important and easily overlooked diagnosis. A man in his early thirties presented to the emergency department with a 2-day history of severe colicky abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, and abdominal distension, and without prior abdominal surgery. Contrast-enhanced CT demonstrated pathognomonic features of Type I intestinal malrotation (non-rotation). After resuscitation and nasogastric decompression, urgent open laparotomy confirmed Ladd's bands causing a complete small-bowel obstruction. Ladd's procedure, with appendectomy and prophylactic colopexy, was performed. The patient was discharged on postoperative day 6 and had complete symptom resolution at the 12-month follow-up. CT achieved a prompt diagnosis via the characteristic CT triad of abnormal superior mesenteric vein-artery orientation, malpositioned duodenojejunal junction, and right-sided small-bowel clustering. The open operative approach was selected due to high-risk physiology, massively dilated bowel, and anticipated difficulty with laparoscopic visualization, consistent with published guidance favoring primary open laparotomy in this setting. Congenital intestinal malrotation must be considered in adults with acute small-bowel obstruction, even in a virgin abdomen. Contrast-enhanced CT is diagnostic, and the open Ladd's procedure in the acute setting achieves excellent outcomes.
