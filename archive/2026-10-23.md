## Ruxolitinib for the treatment of pediatric acute and chronic steroid-refractory graft-versus-host disease: Efficacy, safety, and outcomes.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:32
- 期刊: Hematol Oncol Stem Cell Ther
- 国家/单位: Russian Federation · R.M. Gorbacheva Memorial Institute of Hematology
- 通讯作者: Zubarovskaya LS
- DOI: 10.4103/hemoncstem.hemoncstem-D-25-00044
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.4103/hemoncstem.hemoncstem-D-25-00044) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42904489/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估芦可替尼治疗18岁以下儿童急性和慢性激素难治性移植物抗宿主病（SR-GVHD）的疗效、安全性及结局。
- 核心发现/数据：单中心回顾性研究纳入77例急性（51%为III-IV级）和45例慢性（82%为重度）SR-GVHD患儿，急性和慢性总缓解率分别为83%和87%。慢性GVHD器官特异性缓解率中皮肤71%、胃肠道83%、肝脏88%、口腔黏膜56%、眼52%、肺37%、关节42%、生殖器40%；3年总生存率急性70%、慢性85%，3年内停用全部免疫抑制者急性60%、慢性43%。年龄<12岁不影响结局，血液学毒性为主要不良事件，慢性GVHD中芦可替尼初始剂量与血液学毒性及肝功能指标升高相关。
- 临床启示：结果支持芦可替尼在儿童各年龄段SR-GVHD中具有较好的疗效和安全性，但临床仍需通过前瞻性研究优化剂量并加强长期监测。

**原文摘要**：Steroid-refractory graft-versus-host disease (SR-GVHD) remains a major cause of morbidity and mortality after allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation. Ruxolitinib is food and drug administration (FDA) approved for patients ≥12 years, but data remain limited in other pediatric cohorts. This single-center retrospective study evaluated 77 acute (51% grades III-IV) and 45 chronic (82% severe) SR-GVHD patients under 18 years of age (range 0-17). The overall response rate (ORR) was 83% and 87% in patients with acute and chronic SR-GVHD, respectively. The organ-specific ORRs in chronic SR-GVHD for the skin, gastrointestinal (GI) tract, liver, oral mucosa, eyes, lungs, joints, and genitalia were 71%, 83%, 88%, 56%, 52%, 37%, 42%, and 40%, respectively. Three-year overall survival was 70% (acute) and 85% (chronic). By 3 years, 43% of chronic and 60% of acute patients discontinued all immunosuppression. Age < 12 years did not affect outcomes (acute ORR: 78% vs. 76%, P = 0.96; chronic ORR: 89% vs. 79%, P = 0.14). Hematological toxicity was the main adverse event. The initial ruxolitinib dose correlated with hematologic toxicity and liver function test elevations in chronic GVHD. These results support the efficacy and safety of ruxolitinib across pediatric age groups, though further prospective studies are needed to optimize dosing and long-term monitoring.

## Incidence and risk factors of parenteral nutrition-associated cholestasis in surgical very low birth weight infants.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:32
- 期刊: J Pediatr Gastroenterol Nutr · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/胃肠肝病学4区/营养学4区) · IF 3.1 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 韩国 · CHA Bundang Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Seo JM
- DOI: 10.1002/jpn3.70607
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/jpn3.70607) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42904576/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：确定接受胃肠道手术的极低出生体重儿中肠外营养相关胆汁淤积（PNAC）的发生率并识别其危险因素。
- 核心发现/数据：61例因坏死性小肠结肠炎、自发性肠穿孔或胎粪相关肠梗阻手术的极低出生体重儿中，30例（49%）发生PNAC；PN至PNAC发生的中位时间为6周，70%在PN开始后8周内发生。79%的患儿存在肠衰竭，且PNAC组更常见；6周内未能达到全肠内喂养与PNAC独立相关。尽管常规使用SMOFlipid，PNAC发生率仍较高，严重胆汁淤积（直接胆红素≥6 mg/dL）改用鱼油单药治疗很少逆转。
- 临床启示：早期肠内喂养推进情况可反映整体疾病严重程度，并可作为PNAC风险的重要临床标志。对术后极低出生体重儿应密切监测胆汁淤积并积极优化肠内喂养和PN管理。

**原文摘要**：Surgical intervention for necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC), spontaneous intestinal perforation (SIP), and meconium-related ileus (MRI) is frequently complicated by parenteral nutrition-associated cholestasis (PNAC). This study aimed to determine the incidence and identify risk factors of PNAC in very low birth weight (VLBW) infants following gastrointestinal surgery. We conducted a retrospective review of medical records for VLBW infants who underwent gastrointestinal surgery between January 2012 and December 2020. PNAC was defined as direct bilirubin ≥2.0 mg/dL persisting for two postoperative weeks while on PN. Among 61 VLBW patients who underwent surgery for NEC, SIP, or MRI, 30 (49%) developed PNAC. The median duration of PN until PNAC onset was 6 weeks, and 70% developed cholestasis within 8 weeks of PN initiation. Intestinal failure was present in 79% of the cohort and was significantly more frequent in infants with PNAC. Inability to achieve full enteral feeding within 6 weeks was independently associated with PNAC. Despite routine use of SMOFlipid, PNAC incidence remained substantial. Among infants with severe cholestasis (direct bilirubin ≥6 mg/dL), switching to fish oil monotherapy rarely resulted in reversal. PNAC developed early and frequently in surgical VLBW infants despite SMOFlipid use and was strongly associated with intestinal failure and inability to achieve full enteral feeding. Early feeding progression appears to reflect overall disease severity and may represent an important clinical marker for PNAC risk.
