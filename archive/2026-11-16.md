## The impact of parity on postpartum recovery and perinatal outcomes among obese women.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 12:26
- 期刊: Afr J Reprod Health · 中科院医学4区(公共卫生、环境卫生与职业卫生4区) · IF 1.0

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估肥胖女性中产次与母婴结局及产后恢复质量之间的关系。
- 核心发现/数据：研究纳入128名BMI≥30 kg/m²女性（初产妇64名、经产妇64名），两组产后恢复总分无显著差异（p>0.05），但经产妇的当前恢复感知得分更高（p<0.05）。分娩方式、新生儿重症监护室入住及需氧情况与恢复结局相关；规律产检是显著预测因素，但回归模型解释的方差比例有限。产次并非整体恢复的独立决定因素，母婴因素似乎更重要。
- 临床启示：肥胖产妇的产后恢复评估应重点关注分娩方式及新生儿并发症等因素，而不应仅依据产次判断恢复风险。加强规律产检可能有助于改善相关结局。

**原文摘要**：Maternal obesity is associated with adverse maternal and neonatal outcomes and may affect postpartum recovery; however, the role of parity remains unclear. This study evaluated the relationship between maternal and neonatal outcomes and postpartum recovery quality by parity in obese women. This descriptive and correlational study included 128 women (64 primiparous, 64 multiparous) with body mass index ≥30 kg/m². Data were collected using standardized forms and the Obstetric Quality of Recovery Scale. No significant difference was found between groups in total recovery scores (p>0.05), although multiparous women had higher scores in current recovery perception (p<0.05). Mode of delivery, neonatal intensive care unit admission, and oxygen requirement were associated with recovery outcomes. Regular antenatal care was identified as a significant predictor, although the regression model explained only a limited proportion of the variance. Parity was not an independent determinant of overall recovery; maternal and neonatal factors appear to play a more important role. L'obésité maternelle est associée à des issues maternelles et néonatales défavorables et peut affecter la récupération post-partum ; toutefois, le rôle de la parité reste incertain. Cette étude a évalué la relation entre les issues maternelles et néonatales et la qualité de la récupération post-partum en fonction de la parité chez les femmes obèses. Cette étude descriptive et corrélationnelle a inclus 128 femmes (64 primipares, 64 multipares) présentant un indice de masse corporelle ≥ 30 kg/m². Les données ont été recueillies à l'aide de formulaires standardisés et de l'échelle de qualité de la récupération obstétricale. Aucune différence significative n'a été observée entre les groupes concernant les scores totaux de récupération (p > 0,05), bien que les femmes multipares aient obtenu des scores plus élevés concernant leur perception de la récupération actuelle (p < 0,05). Le mode d'accouchement, l'admission en unité de soins intensifs néonatals et les besoins en oxygène étaient associés aux issues de récupération. Un suivi prénatal régulier a été identifié comme un facteur prédictif significatif, bien que le modèle de régression n'explique qu'une faible proportion de la variance. La parité n'était pas un déterminant indépendant de la récupération globale; les facteurs maternels et néonataux semblent jouer un rôle plus important.

## Genetic Diagnoses and Postnatal Outcomes in a Prenatal Cohort of Arthrogryposis Multiplex Congenita.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 12:26
- 期刊: Prenat Diagn · 中科院医学2区(遗传学2区/妇产科学3区) · IF 3.1 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Elizabeth J. Ferrell Fetal Health Center
- 通讯作者: Sparks TN
- DOI: 10.1002/pd.70310
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/pd.70310) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43035101/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：描述产前诊断的AMC队列的遗传诊断及出生后结局。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入94例妊娠，按Hall分类分为孤立性AMC 15例、伴非中枢神经系统系统异常32例、伴中枢神经系统受累或FADS 47例；遗传学发现包括18例非整倍体、3例拷贝数变异和25例单基因病，非诊断性细胞遗传学结果后行NGS者中50%（24/48）获得分子诊断。FADS新生儿结局极差，死亡率86%（6/7）、插管失败率50%（3/6）；孤立性AMC新生儿77%（10/13）存活，但常需呼吸/喂养支持、骨科手术和行走辅助，并存在神经发育迟缓。
- 临床启示：AMC遗传异质性高且单基因病常见，产前诊断应积极考虑NGS；FADS围产期死亡和插管失败风险极高，需加强预后咨询及多学科围产管理。

**原文摘要**：Describe genetic diagnoses and characterize postnatal outcomes in a prenatal cohort of arthrogryposis multiplex congenita (AMC) evaluated at a quaternary fetal center. Retrospective cohort study of pregnancies referred between 2014 and 2026 for contractures involving ≥ 2 body areas. We categorized cases into three groups using the Hall classification. Demographics, prenatal imaging, genetic results, autopsy findings, and postnatal outcomes were collected. Ninety-four pregnancies were included: 15 with isolated arthrogryposis, 32 with arthrogryposis and systemic anomalies excluding the central nervous system (CNS), and 47 with arthrogryposis and CNS involvement or fetal akinesia deformation sequence (FADS). Genetic findings in our cohort included 18 aneuploidies, 3 copy number variants and 25 single gene disorders. Of pregnancies with non-diagnostic cytogenetic findings that underwent next-generation sequencing (NGS), 50% (24/48) had a molecular diagnosis. Neonates with FADS had notably poor outcomes, including high mortality (86%, 6/7) and failed intubation (50%, 3/6) rates. Of neonates with isolated arthrogryposis, 77% (10/13) survived but frequently required respiratory or feeding support, orthopedic surgery, ambulation assistance and had neurodevelopmental delays. AMC is genetically heterogeneous with frequent underlying single gene disorders, supporting the use of NGS in prenatal diagnosis. Neonatal morbidity and mortality remain high, particularly with FADS.

## Status epilepticus in mTORopathies: a systematic review of clinical features, management, and outcomes.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 12:26
- 期刊: Neurol Sci
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Uniformed Services University of the Health Sciences
- 通讯作者: Ma Y (mayt21054@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s10072-026-09534-y
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10072-026-09534-y) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43035524/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统综述mTORopathies中癫痫持续状态（SE）的临床特征、治疗策略及结局，并探讨mTOR信号在SE中的作用。
- 核心发现/数据：共检索897条记录，最终纳入31项研究；SE可见于不同mTORopathies，常早发、频繁复发且可呈灾难性，难治性SE（RSE）主要影响新生儿和婴儿，并常伴皮质发育畸形（MCD）。SE可通过多种抗癫痫发作药物单用或联合mTOR抑制剂终止，但RSE管理仍困难；现有策略包括新型抗癫痫发作药物、麻醉药、mTOR抑制剂、生酮饮食、免疫治疗、癫痫手术和神经调控，多模式治疗有助于控制发作并改善发育结局。
- 临床启示：对mTORopathies患者尤其新生儿和婴儿RSE，应早期识别MCD并采取多模式个体化治疗；mTOR信号可能是潜在治疗靶点，但mTOR抑制剂和免疫治疗对SE/RSE结局的影响仍需大队列研究验证。

**原文摘要**：The mechanistic target of rapamycin (mTOR) pathway plays a critical role in epileptogenesis; however, its involvement in human status epilepticus (SE) remains poorly understood. mTORopathies, genetic disorders defined by mutations that disrupt mTOR signaling, frequently manifest severe neurological complications, among which SE is particularly consequential. A systematic search of PubMed, Embase, and Web of Science was conducted for studies on mTORopathies and SE. We analyzed the clinical presentation, treatment strategies, and outcomes of all cases. Eight hundred ninety-seven records were initially identified, of which 31 were included in this review. SE may manifest in different mTORopathies. SE often presents early, recurs frequently, and can be catastrophic. Refractory SE (RSE) predominantly affects neonates and infants and frequently occurs in the context of MCD. SE can be terminated with multiple anti-seizure medications (ASMs), alone or in combination with mTOR inhibitors. However, managing RSE is challenging. Current treatment strategies include novel ASMs, anesthetics, mTOR inhibitors, the ketogenic diet, immunotherapy, epilepsy surgery, and neuromodulation. A multifaceted approach is preferred to achieve seizure control and to improve developmental outcomes. SE remains a significant clinical challenge in patients with mTORopathies. mTOR signaling may contribute to the pathogenesis of SE and could represent a potential therapeutic target. Further investigation in large cohort studies is needed to clarify the role of mTOR signaling in SE and to evaluate the effects of mTOR inhibitors and immunotherapies on outcomes in patients with SE/RSE.
