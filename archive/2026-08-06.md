## A temporal differentiation program after birth delineates γδ T cell immunity.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 01:30
- 期刊: Sci Adv · 中科院综合性期刊1区(综合性期刊1区) · IF 13.9 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 丹麦 · Technical University of Denmark
- 通讯作者: Bekiaris V
- DOI: 10.1126/sciadv.aee6790
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aee6790) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42555706/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探究出生后肠道γδ T细胞是否在离开胸腺后继续分化为功能性亚群。
- 核心发现/数据：出生后第一周，肠道γδ T细胞上调多种T细胞激活及3型免疫相关基因；断奶后阶段则以效应功能、1型免疫和细胞毒性基因诱导为主，此期间γδ T细胞保护肠道免受真菌感染。成年肠道中，IL-17产生的γδT17细胞呈现Tbet依赖的1型免疫表型及增强的TCR信号；新生儿人类肠道中亦存在γδT17样细胞群。
- 临床启示：提示肠道γδ T细胞出生后存在动态时序分化程序，可能为新生儿期真菌感染防治及早期免疫干预提供新靶点。

**原文摘要**：Type 1 and type 3 γδ T cells develop in the embryonic thymus, yet it is not known whether they continue to differentiate after birth in tissues. Here, we show that in the first week of life, intestinal γδ T cells up-regulated many T cell activation and type 3 genes, while the postweaning period was dominated by induction of genes related to effector function, type 1 immunity, and cytotoxicity. During this period, γδ T cells protected the gut from fungal infection. In the adult intestine, type 3 interleukin-17-producing γδ T cells (γδT17 cells) had a distinct phenotype, a Tbet-dependent type 1 immune profile and enhanced T cell receptor (TCR) signaling. Last, we found a γδT17 cell-like population in the neonatal human gut. Collectively, our data suggest that γδ T cells continue to mature after they leave the thymus and, upon localization to the gut, a dynamic temporal differentiation program reshapes type 3 cells into Tbet

## Mortality amongst European children with congenital anomalies: Associations with socio-economic status in the EUROLINKCAT cohort.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 01:30
- 期刊: PLoS One · 中科院综合性期刊3区(综合性期刊3区) · IF 2.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 英国 · Swansea University
- 通讯作者: Evans HT
- DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0352025
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0352025) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42555545/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估欧洲先天性畸形儿童中社会经济状况对1岁及10岁前死亡率的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：在47,134名先天性畸形活产儿中，最贫困组相比最不贫困组，1岁内死亡风险HR=1.47（95%CI 1.19-1.83），1-10岁死亡风险HR=2.00（95%CI 1.32-3.02）；贫困对1-10岁死亡率的影响大于婴儿期，且仅在乌克兰和威尔士所有分析中差异显著；非欧盟国籍儿童死亡率更高，单亲母亲未显著增加风险。
- 临床启示：对最脆弱的先天性畸形儿童，需优先提供更多医疗与社会资源，以缩小健康不平等并改善长期生存。

**原文摘要**：Childhood mortality is affected by major congenital anomalies and socio-economic status (SES). To our knowledge, their combined impact has not been explored to age 10. We analysed the population-based EUROlinkCAT cohort to ascertain the impact of SES on mortality by ages 1 and 10 amongst children with congenital anomalies in ten congenital anomaly registers in seven European countries. Four countries defined SES according to maternal education, and two used their national index of multiple deprivation. The ten registers used a common script to generate data on survival by ages 1 and 1-10 (365-3651 days). Eight registers analysed deaths according to their SES criteria, categorised as low, intermediate and high SES. Linked data were combined in random effects meta-analyses. Finland and Emilia-Romagna held data on single motherhood and EU-nationality. We analysed mortality in relation to SES using data on 47,134 live-born children with major congenital anomalies classified and recorded 1996-2014. Mortality by age 1 and ages 1-10 was higher amongst the most than the least deprived, hazard ratios (HR) and 95% confidence intervals (CI) 1.47, 1.19-1.83 and 2.00, 1.32-3.02. Differences between intermediate and least deprived groups were smaller. Differences were statistically significant for all four analyses only in Ukraine and Wales. Mortality rates were higher for children of non-EU nationals, but not single mothers. Deprivation was more strongly associated with death at ages 1-10 than in infancy. These analyses of the most ill children in Europe indicate that, to achieve sustainable development goals, more resources are needed for the most vulnerable children.

## Asymmetric Epiglottic Development as a Cause of Difficult Laryngoscopy in a Neonate with Suspected Branchial Arch Hypoplasia: A Case Report.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 01:30
- 期刊: A A Pract · 中科院医学4区(麻醉学4区) · IF 0.7
- 国家/单位: 日本 · Niigata University Graduate School of Medical and Dental Sciences
- 通讯作者: Harada M
- DOI: 10.1213/XAA.0000000000002258
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1213/XAA.0000000000002258) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42554617/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨综合征新生儿中因声门上结构畸形（尤其是会厌不对称发育）导致的困难喉镜检视及其安全气道管理策略。
- 核心发现/数据：报道3例综合征儿科病例，均存在会厌形态异常相关的困难喉镜检视；其中索引新生儿因疑似左侧鳃弓发育不全，支气管镜显示同侧会厌及杓状区发育不良，且会厌自舌根异常深位发出。
- 临床启示：面对综合征新生儿困难气道时，应警惕声门上畸形可能；优先使用适合年龄的视频喉镜，必要时早期联合软式支气管镜引导，可提升气道管理安全性。

**原文摘要**：Difficult laryngoscopy in syndromic children is usually attributed to mandibular or cervical abnormalities; however, supraglottic malformations may also impair laryngeal exposure. We describe three syndromic pediatric cases in which difficult laryngoscopy was associated with abnormal epiglottic morphology. In the index neonate with suspected left-sided branchial arch hypoplasia, bronchoscopy demonstrated ipsilateral hypoplasia of the epiglottis and arytenoid region, with the epiglottis arising unusually deep from the tongue base. These findings suggest that supraglottic malformation should be considered and that primary use of an age-appropriate video laryngoscope, with early flexible bronchoscopic guidance when needed, may facilitate safe airway management.

## Utility of Amniotic Fluid Biomarkers to Predict Preterm Delivery for Clinical Chorioamnionitis in Women With Intact Membranes.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 01:30
- 期刊: Aust N Z J Obstet Gynaecol
- 国家/单位: 澳大利亚 · Royal Prince Alfred Hospital
- 通讯作者: Narayan R
- DOI: 10.1111/ajo.70168
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1111/ajo.70168) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42554080/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估羊水与血清生物标志物预测胎膜完整孕妇在穿刺后14天内因临床绒毛膜羊膜炎分娩的诊断价值。
- 核心发现/数据：31例可评估病例中，11例（35%）发生临床绒毛膜羊膜炎分娩。羊水基质金属蛋白酶-8、乳酸脱氢酶和葡萄糖预测能力最强（AUC分别为0.93、0.93和0.92），均优于血清标志物（CRP AUC 0.70，白细胞计数AUC 0.67）。新型羊水细胞因子未显示优于传统羊水标志物。
- 临床启示：羊水生物标志物（尤其乳酸脱氢酶和葡萄糖）有助于识别亚临床绒毛膜羊膜炎高危孕妇，可能支持早期干预以改善围产期结局，但需进一步验证。

**原文摘要**：Clinical chorioamnionitis is a major contributor to preterm birth and neonatal morbidity. Diagnosis in women with intact membranes is challenging, particularly in the subclinical phase where maternal signs are subtle and conventional serum markers are limited in predictive value. To evaluate the diagnostic performance of selected novel amniotic fluid biomarkers (interleukin-6, interleukin-10, tumour necrosis factor-ɑ, matrix metalloproteinase-8), and conventional amniotic and serum biomarkers in predicting delivery for clinical chorioamnionitis within 14 days of amniocentesis in women with suspected subclinical infection and intact membranes. A prospective cohort study was conducted at a single Australian tertiary hospital. Thirty-two women with singleton pregnancies and suspected subclinical chorioamnionitis underwent amniocentesis. Amniotic fluid and serum were analysed for selected biomarkers. The primary outcome was delivery for clinical chorioamnionitis within 14 days of amniocentesis. ROC curves were constructed to assess biomarker performance. Delivery for clinical chorioamnionitis occurred in 11 of 31 evaluable cases (35%). Matrix metalloproteinase-8, lactate dehydrogenase, and glucose were the strongest predictors (area under the curve (AUC) of 0.93 (95% CI 0.85-1.0), 0.93 (95% CI 0.85-1.0), and 0.92 (95% CI 0.8-1.0) respectively). Amniotic fluid biomarkers outperformed serum markers (C-reactive protein AUC 0.70 (95% CI 0.49-0.91) and white cell count AUC 0.67 (95% CI 0.46-0.86)). Novel amniotic fluid biomarkers did not demonstrate superior diagnostic performance over conventional amniotic fluid biomarkers including lactate dehydrogenase and glucose. Amniotic fluid biomarkers show promise in identifying women with subclinical chorioamnionitis. These findings support further validation of scalable biomarker-based testing to guide early intervention and improve perinatal outcomes.
