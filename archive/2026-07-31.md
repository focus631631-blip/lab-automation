## Overexpression of CD97 in intestinal epithelial cells attenuates LPS-induced pro-inflammatory cytokine induction via stabilization of β-catenin early in life.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 01:51
- 期刊: PLoS One · 中科院综合性期刊3区(综合性期刊3区) · IF 2.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 德国 · University Hospital Leipzig
- 通讯作者: Riedel J
- DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0354507
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0354507) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42531273/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨CD97在肠上皮细胞中过表达是否通过稳定β-catenin调节未成熟肠道对LPS诱导的炎症反应。  
- 核心发现/数据：CD97过表达在早期发育阶段及回肠组织中显著抑制LPS诱导的TNF-α表达，而对成年小鼠肠上皮细胞无影响；该抑制效应依赖于β-catenin稳定化，阻断β-catenin可逆转抗炎表型并恢复促炎基因表达。  
- 临床启示：CD97-β-catenin-NF-κB轴可能成为保护早产儿未成熟肠道免受坏死性小肠结肠炎等炎症损伤的新型治疗靶点。

**原文摘要**：Acute inflammatory conditions in the intestine of preterm infants are linked to increased susceptibility due to the immature degree of the gut's epithelial barrier, microbiota, and pattern recognition receptors. Toll-like receptor 4 (TLR4) plays a pivotal role in recognizing lipopolysaccharides (LPS) from gram-negative bacteria, triggering pro-inflammatory cytokine responses (TNF-α, IL-1β, CXCL1) via nuclear factor kappa B (NF-κB) signaling. These processes are implicated in necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC), a severe gastrointestinal condition in premature infants. CD97, an adhesion G-protein coupled receptor (aGPCR), has emerged as a modulator of immune responses influencing inflammatory signaling pathways. CD97 expression is typically low in intestinal epithelial cells (IECs); however, protective effects of increased CD97 levels have been described in experimentally induced colitis. In this study, we examined the role of CD97 in modulating the inflammatory response in the immature gut, utilizing wild-type (WT) and transgenic CD97-overexpressing mice (TgCD97) with epithelial-specific expression in the intestinal epithelium. LPS was administered to IECs and organ segments isolated from the small intestine of mice of specific ages, modeling certain stages of human perinatal/postnatal gut development. CD97 overexpression attenuated LPS-induced TNF-α expression in IECs during early intestinal development while IECs from adult mice remained unaffected. This effect was attributed specifically to ileal tissue. Attenuation was mediated by β-catenin stabilization, leading to suppressed LPS/NF-κB signaling. Inhibition of β-catenin in TgCD97 IECs reversed the anti-inflammatory phenotype restoring pro-inflammatory gene expression. These findings suggest that CD97 overexpression modulates the inflammatory response in the developing gut by stabilizing β-catenin and interacting with the LPS/NF-κB axis. This effect was restricted to early developmental stages and ileal tissue, highlighting a previously unrecognized role of CD97 in age-dependent endotoxin tolerance. These findings identify a novel CD97-β-catenin-NF-κB regulatory axis in the immature small intestine and highlight its potential as a therapeutic target to protect the immature neonatal gut from inflammatory damage.

## Value of contrast-enhanced colosonography in the assessment of anorectal malformations.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 01:51
- 期刊: Pediatr Radiol · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区/核医学3区) · IF 2.5 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Shenzhen Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Lin Z (linzhou_szet@163.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00247-026-06715-x
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00247-026-06715-x) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42530580/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估对比增强结肠超声在肛门直肠畸形诊断中的准确性及其对手术方式选择的指导价值。
- 核心发现/数据：对比增强结肠超声对瘘管检出的敏感性显著高于透视下结肠造影（98.6% vs. 83.3%，P<0.001），与手术解剖的符合率为98.6%（透视下结肠造影为73.6%）。对于直肠-球部尿道瘘患者，P-P距离截断值23.5 mm时，AUC为0.747（敏感性66.7%，特异性76.0%）。
- 临床启示：对比增强结肠超声是一种安全、有效且多用途的术前评估方法，可替代传统影像学检查，并有助于区分瘘管类型，在特定病例中通过P-P测量辅助选择恰当的手术入路。

**原文摘要**：Anorectal malformations are common congenital anomalies. Accurate preoperative imaging of fistulous connections is crucial for surgical planning. Traditional imaging uses ionizing radiation and often requires sedation. Contrast-enhanced colosonography, based on the application of ultrasound contrast agents, has been developed to overcome these limitations while still providing high-resolution structural information for the evaluation of anorectal malformations. The indications of laparoscopically assisted anorectoplasty for recto-bulbar urethral fistulas remain debated. This study aimed to evaluate the diagnostic accuracy of contrast-enhanced colosonography in detecting anorectal malformations and its value in guiding surgical approach selection. The diagnostic performance of preoperative contrast-enhanced colosonography and fluoroscopic colostography was compared in 72 cases, with surgical findings serving as the reference standard. Additionally, the relationship between surgical approach and the data from recto-urethral fistula was evaluated. Contrast-enhanced colosonography showed higher sensitivity for fistula detection rate than fluoroscopic colostography (98.6% vs. 83.3%, P<0.001). Concordance with surgical anatomy was 98.6% for contrast-enhanced colosonography, while 73.6% for fluoroscopic colostography. Among patients with recto-bulbar urethral fistula, a pouch-perineum (P-P) cutoff value of 23.5 mm (sensitivity 66.7%, specificity 76.0%) yielded an area under the curve of 0.747 (P=0.01, 95% CI 0.589-0.904). Contrast-enhanced colosonography appears to be a safe, effective, and versatile method for obtaining critical preoperative structural information in patients with anorectal malformations. For patients with recto-urethral fistulas, contrast-enhanced colosonography enables further differentiation between recto-membranous and recto-bulbar urethral fistulas. In specified cases, the P-P measurement may be helpful in selecting the appropriate surgical approach.

## Imaging Features to Distinguish Intralobar Versus Extralobar Bronchopulmonary Sequestration.

- 期刊: J Comput Assist Tomogr · 中科院医学4区(核医学4区) · IF 1.4 · JCR Q3

- 通讯作者: Hammer MM
- DOI: 10.1097/RCT.0000000000001917
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/RCT.0000000000001917) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42530165/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估支气管肺隔离症（BPS）的CT特征，并确定用于预测杂交病变及区分叶内型（ILS）与叶外型（ELS）隔离症的影像学表现。
- 核心发现/数据：静脉引流模式在ILS与ELS间差异显著，11%的ILS引流至体循环静脉系统，而ELS为57%（P=0.013）；98%的ILS与周围肺组织不可分离，而57%的ELS与正常肺组织分离（P<0.001）。CT上出现囊性变的12例杂交病变中有7例，而无杂交病变的40例中有17例；影像上囊性变比例高于病理报告，提示病理分析可能低估杂交病变。
- 临床启示：仅凭静脉引流和与肺实质的可分离性不能完美区分ILS与ELS，且CT囊性变与病理杂交病变存在不一致，临床应警惕杂交病变的漏诊。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate the CT characteristics of bronchopulmonary sequestration (BPS) and determine the imaging features in predicted hybrid lesions and sequestration subtypes (intralobar vs. extralobar sequestration). This was a multicenter retrospective study in which all surgical pathology specimens of lung sequestration performed in a tertiary hospital system were reviewed from January 2008 to January 2026. In total, 52 pathologic samples of lung sequestrations were analyzed and compared with preoperative imaging features. Imaging features including anatomic and vascular anatomy, parenchymal changes and relationship to the pleural were noted. BPS with coexistingcongenital pulmonary airway malformation, also called a hybrid lesion, on pathology was correlated with imaging findings. Venous drainage patterns differed significantly between the intralobar sequestration (ILS) and extralobar sequestration (ELS) groups, with 11% of the ILS group draining to the systemic venous system versus 57% of the ELS group (P=0.013). In addition, 98% of ILS were inseparable from the surrounding lung, whereas 57% were separate from the normal lung (P<0.001). Cystic changes were common; 7/12 cases of hybrid lesions had cystic abnormalities on CT, whereas 17 out of the 40 cases without pathologic evidence of hybrid lesions had cystic changes on CT. Distinguishing intralobar from extralobar sequestration is imperfect based on venous drainage and separability from the normal lung parenchyma. Our data also showed a discrepancy between cystic abnormalities on CT and pathologically proven hybrid lesions, with a higher proportion of sequestrations with cystic changes on CT than were reported on pathologic analysis. Thus, hybrid lesions may be underdiagnosed on pathologic analysis.

## To Wrap or Not to Wrap: Does Concomitant Fundoplication at the Time of Gastrostomy Tube Placement Remain a Question?

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 06:43
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: San Diego · University of California San Diego
- 通讯作者: Hyman SC (sihyman@health.ucsd.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163339
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163339) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42532174/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估婴儿在放置胃造口管时是否同时行胃底折叠术，对术后肺部并发症及后续需要幽门后喂养的影响。

- 核心发现/数据：在649例接受胃造口管置入的婴儿中，56%接受了同期胃底折叠术；与单纯胃造口管置入相比，联合胃底折叠术的肺部并发症发生率更高（18% vs. 11%，p=0.01），尤其是在神经功能障碍（26% vs. 12%）和颅面畸形（26% vs. 14%）患儿中。多因素分析显示，同期胃底折叠术（OR 1.76）、颅面畸形（OR 1.74）和神经功能障碍（OR 1.88）是术后肺部并发症的独立危险因素；两组术后因难治性反流转为胃空肠喂养的比例无显著差异（7% vs. 5%，p=0.29），且仅1%的单纯胃造口患儿后续需要补做胃底折叠术。

- 临床启示：对于无医学难治性胃食管反流的婴儿，放置胃造口管时不应常规联合胃底折叠术，因其反而增加肺部并发症风险，且不能减少术后转幽门后喂养的需求，未来需要补做胃底折叠术的比例也很低。

**原文摘要**：Gastrostomy tube (GT) placement is common in infants with oral aversion and/or oropharyngeal dysphagia. Historically, GT placement was combined with fundoplication to mitigate gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) and aspiration. This study aimed to evaluate the impact of concomitant fundoplication on pulmonary complications and the need for post-pyloric feeds following GT placement. A single institution review of infants <6 months-old undergoing GT placement for oral aversion and/or oropharyngeal dysphagia was performed (January 2011-June 2025). Patients with medically-refractory GERD were excluded. Pulmonary complications were defined as postoperative admissions for aspiration or bacterial pneumonia. Univariate and multivariate analyses were performed. Among 649 infants undergoing GT placement, 56% had a concomitant fundoplication. Comorbidities were similar between groups. Fundoplication was associated with increased pulmonary complications compared to GT alone (18% vs. 11%, p=0.01), particularly among infants with neurologic (26% vs. 12%, p<0.01) or craniofacial disorders (26% vs. 14%, p=0.04). On multivariate analysis, concomitant fundoplication (OR 1.76, 95%CI 1.11-2.79), craniofacial disorders (OR 1.74, 95%CI 1.09-2.75) and neurologic disorders (OR 1.88, 95%CI 1.21-2.93) were independently associated with postoperative pulmonary complications. Three infants (1%) underwent metachronous postoperative fundoplication. Rates of postoperative conversion to a gastrojejunostomy for refractory GERD were similar between groups (7% GT alone vs. 5% concomitant fundoplication, p=0.29). Concomitant fundoplication is independently associated with increased risk of pulmonary complications in infants undergoing GT placement and does not reduce the need for postoperative conversion to post-pyloric feeds. Further, the need for future fundoplication in those undergoing GT alone is rare. III.

## At the Limit of Viability: Outcomes of Necrotising Enterocolitis in Neonates Born Before 25 Weeks' Gestation.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 06:43
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: London · The Royal London Hospital
- 通讯作者: Abdullah ME (mohamad.abdullah1@nhs.net)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163345
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163345) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42532173/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估≤25周超早产儿坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）的治疗结局与预后指标。
- 核心发现/数据：51例≤25周NEC新生儿中，手术组68.6%，药物组31.4%，总体存活率76.5%，手术组与药物组存活率相似（77.1% vs 75.0%，p=1.000）。22-23周出生儿存活率达76.5%；存活者中慢性肺病（CLD）占83.8%，管饲依赖占29.7%，重度脑室内出血（3/4级IVH）占13.5%，22-23周亚组管饲依赖高达69.2%。
- 临床启示：极早产本身不应成为NEC手术治疗的排除标准，但存活者面临显著慢性呼吸和营养并发症，需长期神经发育随访以支持家庭咨询和后续照护。

**原文摘要**：To determine management outcomes and prognostic indicators for necrotising enterocolitis (NEC) in extremely premature neonates born at the threshold of viability (≤25 weeks). A retrospective review of a prospectively maintained, consecutively recorded database of neonates born ≤25 weeks' gestation admitted with NEC to a tertiary neonatal unit between January 2020 and December 2025. Outcomes measured included survival to discharge, overall survival, and medium-term morbidity RESULTS: 51 neonates with ≤25 weeks' gestation developed NEC. Mean GA was 24 weeks and BW 667 g, with male predominance (60.8%). 35 (68.6%) underwent surgical intervention, and 16 (31.4%) were managed medically. At the time of diagnosis, CRP was normal in 54.9%, and thrombocytopenia occurred in 56.8%. Overall survival was 76.5% and was similar between the surgical and medical cohorts: 77.1% (27/35) and 75.0% (12/16), respectively (p=1.000). Among survivors, CLD affected 83.8%, tube feeding dependency 29.7%, and Grade 3/4 IVH 13.5%. Infants born at 22-23 weeks achieved a survival rate of 76.5%. Among survivors, rates of CLD, Grade 3/4 IVH, and feeding-tube dependence were 84.3%, 15.4%, and 69.2%, respectively. NEC in neonates born at the threshold of viability show promising survival, implying that extreme prematurity shouldn't exclude surgical treatment. Survivors experience significant comorbidities, especially chronic respiratory and nutritional morbidity. Collecting long-term neurodevelopmental data is crucial for family counselling and future care.

## Prospective Comparison of Near-Infrared I and II Fluorescence Imaging for Intraoperative Navigation in Pediatric Hepatoblastoma Resection.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 06:43
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Capital Center for Children's Health
- 通讯作者: Li X (lixu622@163.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163340
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163340) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42532172/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：前瞻性比较基于吲哚菁绿（ICG）的近红外II区（NIR-II）与近红外I区（NIR-I）荧光成像在小儿肝母细胞瘤切除术中导航的可行性与性能。
- 核心发现/数据：NIR-II成像的术中肿瘤-正常组织比（TNR）显著高于NIR-I（10.11±3.48 vs 6.49±3.48，P<0.05），离体肿瘤-背景比（TBR）亦更高（16.05±8.43 vs 9.88±5.35，P<0.05）。基于标本分析，NIR-II的敏感性（88.9% vs 77.8%）和准确性（84.4% vs 80.0%）均优于NIR-I，并能额外识别NIR-I未清晰显示的卫星灶或转移灶，改善深部肿瘤定位。
- 临床启示：NIR-II荧光成像在小儿肝母细胞瘤手术中可行，尤其对小型、深部或弱荧光病灶可提供优于NIR-I的术中可视化，有望辅助术中决策，但需更大样本研究验证。

**原文摘要**：Complete resection is critical for cure in hepatoblastoma (HB), but small, superficial, or deep lesions may be missed by conventional intraoperative assessment or near-infrared window I (NIR-I) fluorescence imaging. We prospectively evaluated the feasibility and comparative performance of indocyanine green (ICG)-based near-infrared window II (NIR-II) fluorescence imaging during pediatric hepatoblastoma resection. Fourteen children with HB were prospectively enrolled. All patients received preoperative ICG administration and underwent intraoperative paired NIR-I and NIR-II fluorescence imaging using a self-developed multispectral imaging system. In vivo and ex vivo fluorescence findings were correlated with histopathology. In vivo tumor-to-normal ratio (TNR), ex vivo tumor-to-background ratio (TBR), and lesion detection performance were compared between the two modalities. NIR-II imaging showed higher intraoperative TNR than NIR-I imaging (10.11 ± 3.48 vs 6.49 ± 3.48, P < 0.05) and higher ex vivo TBR (16.05 ± 8.43 vs 9.88 ± 5.35, P < 0.05). In specimen-based analysis, NIR-II showed higher sensitivity (88.9% vs 77.8%) and accuracy (84.4% vs 80.0%) than NIR-I imaging. NIR-II also identified additional satellite or metastatic lesions not clearly visualized by NIR-I imaging in representative cases and improved localization of deeply located tumors. ICG-based NIR-II fluorescence imaging is feasible in pediatric HB surgery and may provide incremental intraoperative visualization compared with NIR-I imaging, particularly for selected small, deep, or weakly fluorescent lesions. These preliminary findings support the complementary role of NIR-II imaging in intraoperative decision-making but require validation in larger studies with standardized follow-up. Level III.

## Cultured Thymus Tissue Implantation for Congenital Athymia: Surgical Site Complications and Dose-Effect Analysis.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 06:43
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2

- 通讯作者: Antiel RM
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163341
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163341) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42532171/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估先天性胸腺缺失患儿行培养胸腺组织植入术的局部手术并发症，并分析植入剂量与体积对T细胞重建的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：107例患儿中9例（8.4%）发生手术相关不良事件（均为Clavien-Dindo I或II级），包括伤口裂开5例、缝合脓肿2例、手术部位出血1例、手术部位水肿1例。在64例可评估患儿中，12个月时CD3、CD4、CD8及初始CD4 T细胞数量与植入剂量或体积均无显著相关性（如CD3 vs 剂量 r=0.0472, p=0.7112；初始CD4 vs 体积 r=0.0097, p=0.9525）。

**原文摘要**：Allogeneic processed thymus tissue-agdc (RETHYMIC®) is the only FDA-approved therapy for immune reconstitution in pediatric patients with congenital athymia. Early mortality due to immune dysregulation and overwhelming infections occurs in the majority of untreated children. Our aims were to report local surgical morbidity of the implantation procedure and evaluate the dose- and volume-effect relationship. We analyzed children in prospective, single-arm open-label studies (IRB Pro00104164), undergoing allogeneic cultured thymic tissue implantation (CTTI) in the quadriceps muscle, between 1993 and 2021. Adverse events, CTT (cultured thymus tissue) doses and naïve T-cell reconstitution at 12 months were reviewed to analyze dose- and volume-effect relationships. 107 children underwent CTTI. Nine (8.4%) surgery-related adverse events (Clavien-Dindo grade I or II) were observed: five cases of wound dehiscence, two stitch abscesses, one surgical site bleed and one surgical site edema managed with compression. Ninety-seven children had treatment-naïve congenital athymia, of which 64 had both a dose reported and T-cell flow cytometry performed 12 months post-implantation. The number of T-cells (CD3, CD4, CD8 and naïve CD4) did not correlate with the administered dose (CD3: r=0.0472, p=0.7112, CD4: r=0.2149, p=0.0882, CD8: r=-0.048, p=0.7111, naïve CD4: r=0.0847, p=0.5504) or volume (CD3: r=0.01979, p=0.1728, CD4: r=0.139, p=0.3358, CD8: r=0.0954, p=0.5143, naïve CD4: r=0.0097, p=0.9525). CTTI was associated with a low rate of procedure-site morbidity in an immunocompromised population. Whereas naïve CD4 T-cells are observed 12 months post-implantation, the dose and volume of CTT does not correlate with the magnitude of the T-cell reconstitution.

## IMPACT OF 2-SITE CRYOABLATION ON PAIN CONTROL AFTER THE NUSS PROCEDURE.

- 通讯作者: DiFiore JW (difiorj@ccf.org)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163342
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163342) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42532170/)
