## Qualitative Exploration of Fertility-Related Attitudes and Perspectives Among Young Adult Women Diagnosed With Breast Cancer at Periurban Cancer Center in Uganda.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:33
- 期刊: JCO Glob Oncol · 中科院医学3区(肿瘤学3区) · IF 3.8
- 国家/单位: Uganda · Mother Kevin Postgraduate Medical School
- 通讯作者: Kayiira A
- DOI: 10.1200/GO-25-00441
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1200/GO-25-00441) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42842860/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：在乌干达城郊癌症中心探索年轻成年女性乳腺癌患者对生育相关决策的态度、观点及系统层面障碍。
- 核心发现/数据：对20名年轻成年女性乳腺癌患者进行半结构化深度访谈并作主题分析，提炼出4个主题：母亲身份的文化社会意义、综合生育咨询需求、对未来生育的不确定与焦虑，以及生育力保存服务可及性障碍。高费用、缺乏医保、服务有限等因素显著限制了患者追求生育力保存，且肿瘤生育咨询未充分整合入常规肿瘤照护。
- 临床启示：应将及时、共情且详细的肿瘤生育咨询纳入常规乳腺癌照护，并着力解决费用、保险、服务可及性与转诊/基础设施障碍，以支持以患者为中心的生存照护。

**原文摘要**：Understanding how women navigate fertility-related decisions after a breast cancer diagnosis is critical for patient-centered survivorship care in low-resource settings. This study explored young women's fertility-related perspectives and, importantly, how their experiences point to system-level gaps in oncofertility counseling and access to fertility preservation at a periurban cancer center in Uganda. A qualitative phenomenologic study design was used. Twenty young adult women diagnosed with breast cancer were purposively selected from St Francis Hospital Nsambya, in Kampala, Uganda. Data were collected through face-to-face, semistructured, in-depth interviews guided by an interview schedule. Interviews were audio-recorded, transcribed verbatim, and analyzed using thematic analysis to systematically identify themes, subthemes, and illustrative quotes from participants' narratives. Four major themes emerged from the analysis: (1) Cultural and Societal Significance of Motherhood-childbearing held significant personal and cultural importance, affecting participants' identity and relationship decisions; (2) Need for Comprehensive Fertility Counseling-participants expressed the necessity for timely, empathetic, and detailed fertility information from health care providers and self-esteem; (3) Uncertainty about ika Fertility-considerable anxiety was expressed regarding potential infertility and adverse treatment effects on future offspring, resulting in difficult prioritization between survival and reproduction; and (4) Beyond reach-significant barriers, including high costs, lack of health insurance, and limited service availability, restricted participants' ability to pursue fertility preservation. Young women with breast cancer in Uganda experience profound fertility-related distress shaped by sociocultural expectations and by health system constraints. Limited integration of oncofertility counseling into routine oncology care, gaps in provider communication, and restricted access to affordable fertility preservation (infrastructure constraints, high out-of-pocket costs, insurance exclusions, and geographic centralization) complicate decision making and highlight priorities for equitable service delivery.

## Safety and cost-effectiveness of a pure hypochlorous acid cleansing solution in wounds and non-intact skin management: a narrative review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:33
- 期刊: J Wound Care · 中科院医学4区(皮肤病学4区) · IF 2.0 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 美国
- 通讯作者: Kirsner RS
- DOI: 10.12968/jowc.2026.0428
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.12968/jowc.2026.0428) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42842699/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过叙述性综述评估纯次氯酸（pHA）清洗液（330 ppm、pH 3.5–5.5）在伤口及非完整皮肤管理中的安全性、有效性、耐受性、可接受性和成本效果。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入56篇文献，分析2682例患者、2968处伤口，其中包括140名早产儿、新生儿和儿童。该溶液无论应用方法、适应证和人群如何均显示良好获益/风险，较生理盐水、肥皂水或标准护理显著减少细菌生长及伤口相关并发症，并促进清创、愈合且具成本效果；安全性“非常好”，未报告相关并发症，包括早产儿。
- 临床启示：pHA溶液是伤口和非完整皮肤管理的有用选择，尤其适用于感染控制和组织耐受性要求较高的情况。仍值得开展更多高质量比较研究以强化证据。

**原文摘要**：Wound cleansing is an essential part of wound bed preparation and its importance in wound management is supported by numerous international guidelines and consensus documents. Cleansing solutions are used by healthcare professionals for wounds and non-intact skin. However, some of these solutions may not be effective in addressing the condition of the wound or safe enough to be used in certain patients due to the solution's potential for irritation or toxicity. According to guidelines, solution of pure hypochlorous acid (pHA; free of hypochlorite derivatives) provides a good balance between safety and efficacy. This narrative review discusses the clinical evidence available on the use of a pHA solution (Vashe Wound Solution; Urgo Medical North America, US) formulated at 330ppm with a tightly controlled, slightly acidic pH (pH 3.5-5.5). A literature search of the Embase, MEDLINE, Google Scholar and TRIP databases was conducted (firstly in December 2025 and updated in June 2026), for original clinical studies reporting the use of the pHA solution, including randomised controlled trials, comparative studies, observational studies, case series/reports and health economic evaluations. Outcomes relating to efficacy, safety, tolerability, acceptability and costs were synthesised narratively. A total of 56 publications were identified and included in the analysis. These clinical evaluations analysed the data of 2968 wounds in 2682 patients, including 140 preterm infants, neonates and children. The treated indications were wounds presenting with risk or clinical signs of local infection, infected wounds, skin tissue or mucosa, including wounds at debridement stage or requiring thorough cleansing, and patients with fragile, immature or non-intact skin. Across the different studies, consistent and concordant outcomes were noted, with a favourable benefit/risk profile of the solution, regardless of the method of application, treated indications and patient populations. Cleansing with the pHA solution, using soaked gauze or different irrigation techniques, to remove debris, slough and foreign bodies from the wound and skin, was found to significantly reduce bacterial growth compared to saline, soap and water, or standard of care, resulting in fewer wound-related complications. The cleanser solution also appeared to facilitate wound debridement, promote wound healing and be cost-effective in numerous indications. The safety profile of the pHA solution was 'very good', and no complication related to its use was reported in any of the clinical evaluations, including in preterm infants. The clinical evidence identified in this review consistently supported the pHA solution with a slightly acidic pH as a useful option in the management of wounds and non-intact skin, aligned with its intended use, particularly in situations where infection control and tissue tolerability are important considerations. Conducting additional high-quality comparative studies would be worthwhile to strengthen the available evidence.

## Dynamic landmark models predicting native liver survival after kasai portoenterostomy: a bicentric study.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:33
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Tianjin Children's Hospital (Children's Hospital of Tianjin University)
- 通讯作者: Zhan J (zhanjianghuatj@163.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06661-7
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06661-7) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42841979/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：开发并验证可在Kasai门肠吻合术（KPE）后不同预设随访时点动态更新胆道闭锁（BA）患儿自体肝生存（NLS）预测的landmark模型。
- 核心发现/数据：在训练168例、验证80例的双中心队列中，术前及术后2周模型

**原文摘要**：Native liver survival (NLS) after Kasai portoenterostomy (KPE) for biliary atresia (BA) is highly heterogeneous, and clinically practical approaches that update prognostic estimates across prespecified postoperative follow-up time points remain limited. In this retrospective bicentric cohort study (training, n = 168; validation, n = 80), Cox proportional hazards landmark models were developed at five prespecified time points (preoperative and 2 weeks, 1, 3, and 6 months post-KPE) using a LASSO/Boruta dual-algorithm intersection approach with variance inflation factor (VIF)-based collinearity pruning. Internal validation used 500-replicate bootstrap resampling; external validation was performed in an independent cohort. Preoperative and 2-week models showed limited discrimination (C-index ≤0.576). The 3-month two-variable model (TBA + TBil clearance ratio [TBil-CR]) achieved a training C-index of 0.812 (optimism 0.003) and an external C-index of 0.778 (95% CI 0.679-0.878). Risk stratification yielded a 35-percentage-point difference in 2-year cumulative event rates (41.7% vs. 7.0%; log-rank P < 0.001), consistently reproduced in the validation cohort. TBil-CR HR declined progressively across postoperative landmarks, and its single-variable C-index exceeded the jaundice clearance criterion by 0.103 at 3 months. A parsimonious two-variable landmark model incorporating TBil-CR and TBA at 3 months post-KPE accurately predicts long-term NLS in children with BA, outperforming traditional binary bilirubin thresholds.

## An international multicenter analysis of the outcome of pediatric first variceal hemorrhage.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:33
- 期刊: Hepatology · 中科院医学1区(胃肠肝病学1区) · IF 18.0 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: Baylor College of Medicine and Texas Children's Hospital · Baylor College of Medicine and Texas Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Grammatikopoulos T
- DOI: 10.1097/HEP.0000000000001881
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/HEP.0000000000001881) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42842335/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过国际多中心研究，明确儿童首次静脉曲张出血（1VH）后的结局及相关影响因素。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入44个国际中心、345例患儿；门静脉血栓和非肝硬化疾病的总体生存（OS）及自体肝生存（SNL）显著高于胆道闭锁和肝硬化（p<.001）。失代偿（腹水需利尿剂或1VH时住院）、胆道闭锁胆汁引流差、肝硬化、硬化治疗或未行内镜干预与SNL降低相关，肝硬化或需硬化治疗与OS降低相关。总体6周死亡率为4.1%，非住院患儿为1.4%，且中心聚类2较聚类1显著更高（P=.010）。
- 临床启示：儿童1VH后即时死亡率低于成人，但结局具有诊断特异性，失代偿状态和硬化治疗提示更差预后；中心特异性因素可能影响短期结局，临床应据此进行风险分层和资源/策略优化。

**原文摘要**：Management of portal hypertension in children including primary prophylaxis of variceal bleeding is controversial due to limited understanding of mortality risk after first variceal hemorrhage (1VH). This international multicenter study was designed to advance understanding of outcomes after pediatric 1VH. Center-specific characteristics and approaches to portal hypertension were determined at 44 international sites. K-means clustered the centers based upon volume and complexity of approaches [cluster 1 high (n=5), cluster 2 low (n=21), cluster 3 intermediate (n=18]). Clinical features and outcomes after 1VH were available from 345 subjects. Factors associated with overall survival (OS) and survival with native liver (SNL) were estimated by Kaplan-Meier survival curves, log rank tests, univariable and multivariable Cox regression models. OS and SNL were high in portal vein thrombosis and noncirrhotic diseases and significantly higher than biliary atresia and cirrhotic diseases (p<.001). Diminished SNL was observed in decompensated disease [diuretics (i.e. ascites) or hospitalization at 1VH], biliary atresia with poor bile flow, cirrhotic disease, after sclerotherapy or no endoscopic intervention. Diminished OS was noted in those with cirrhosis or requiring sclerotherapy. 6-week mortality was determined and compared among clusters using average treatment effect under the Rubin causal model. Overall 6-week mortality was 4.1%, 1.4% in nonhospitalized subjects and was significantly higher in cluster 2 versus 1 centers (P=.010). Immediate mortality after 1VH in children is low compared to adults. Outcomes after 1VH are diagnosis-specific and worse in decompensated disease and after sclerotherapy. Center-specific factors may influence immediate outcomes after 1VH.
