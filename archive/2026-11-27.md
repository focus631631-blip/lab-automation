## Preoperative Sepsis Severity and Outcomes After Appendectomy for Complicated Appendicitis in Children: A NSQIP-P Analysis.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 05:51
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University of Florida
- 通讯作者: Raymond SL (Steven.Raymond@surgery.ufl.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163607
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163607) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43098348/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估儿童复杂性阑尾炎行阑尾切除术前脓毒症严重程度是否与术后结局相关。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入34,436例患儿，术前无脓毒症、SIRS、脓

**原文摘要**：Complicated appendicitis in children is associated with substantial morbidity, but outcomes remain heterogeneous. We evaluated whether preoperative sepsis severity is associated with postoperative outcomes after appendectomy for complicated appendicitis. The 2016-2022 American College of Surgeons National Surgical Quality Improvement Program-Pediatric database was queried for patients younger than 18 years undergoing appendectomy for complicated appendicitis. Patients were stratified as no sepsis, systemic inflammatory response syndrome (SIRS), sepsis, or septic shock within 72 hours of surgery. The primary outcome was 30-day composite postoperative morbidity. Multivariable logistic regression assessed associations between sepsis severity and morbidity. Among 34,436 children, 27.6% had no sepsis, 8.7% had SIRS, 63.4% had sepsis, and 0.3% had septic shock. Composite morbidity occurred in 11.1%, 11%, 17.5%, and 43.1%, respectively (P<.001). Organ-space surgical site infection, unplanned reoperation, postoperative percutaneous drainage, and hospital length of stay were greatest with sepsis and septic shock. Three patients with septic shock died (2.9%; P<.001). On multivariable analysis, sepsis (aOR 1.64, 95% CI 1.53-1.77; P<.001) and septic shock (aOR 2.78, 95% CI 1.67-4.62; P<.001) were associated with increased postoperative morbidity after adjustment for demographic and clinical covariates. Increasing preoperative sepsis severity is associated with worse outcomes after appendectomy for complicated appendicitis in children and may inform risk stratification, perioperative planning, and postoperative surveillance before operative findings are available to treating clinicians. Retrospective cohort study.

## Management of Infant Perianal Abscesses: A Pediatric Surgery Research Collaborative Prospective Quality Improvement Study.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 05:51
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Oregon Health & Science University
- 通讯作者: Selesner L (selesner@ohsu.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163606
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163606) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43098346/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估婴儿肛周脓肿初始非手术治疗路径与初始手术治疗相比，对脓肿消退、复发及手术/麻醉暴露的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入277例婴儿（非手术方案组200例，初始手术对照组77例）；两组完全消退中位时间无差异（2.4 vs. 1.4个月，p=0.44），3个月和6个月累积消退率亦无差异（53% vs. 60%；86% vs. 85%；log-rank p=0.58），复发率无差异（41% vs. 39%，p=0.81）。非手术方案组接受手术干预（16% vs. 99%，p<0.001）和全身麻醉（14% vs. 53%，p<0.001）的比例显著更低；校正分析显示其手术干预 odds 显著降低（aOR 0.003，95% CI 0.0004-0.022，p<0.001）。
- 临床启示：对婴儿肛周脓肿可优先考虑初始非手术治疗，以减少手术操作和麻醉暴露，且未观察到脓肿消退时间或复发率增加。

**原文摘要**：Management of perianal abscesses (IPAs) in infants remains controversial, with high recurrence rates and limited prospective data. We performed the first multi-institutional prospective quality improvement (QI) study utilizing a non-operative pathway, with comparison to initial operative IPA management. Through the Pediatric Surgery Research Collaborative, 14 institutions enrolled infants younger than 12 months with a perianal abscess within 2 cm of the anal verge (2022-2025). Management was determined by the treating surgeon: upfront operative intervention (off-protocol, control group) or initial non-operative care per standardized QI protocol (on-protocol, protocol group). The primary outcome was time to complete abscess resolution. Secondary outcomes included the need for surgical intervention and abscess recurrence. Of 277 infants (77 control, 200 protocol), baseline characteristics were similar. Median time to complete resolution did not differ (2.4 vs. 1.4 months; p=0.44), nor did Kaplan-Meier estimated cumulative resolution at 3 months (53% vs. 60%) or 6 months (86% vs. 85%; log-rank p=0.58). Recurrence also did not differ between groups (41% vs. 39%; p=0.81). Protocol patients were less likely to undergo operative intervention (16% vs. 99%; p<0.001) or require general anesthesia (14% vs. 53%; p<0.001). On adjusted analysis, protocol patients had lower odds of operative intervention (adjusted odds ratio 0.003, 95% CI 0.0004-0.022; p<0.001) without differences in time to resolution or recurrence. Initial non-operative management was associated with fewer operative procedures and less anesthesia exposure, with no detectable difference in time to resolution or recurrence, supporting non-operative management as a reasonable first-line strategy for IPA. Level III - Treatment Study.

## The Congenital Heart Technical Skill Study: Can Peer Surgeon Assessment of Technical Skill Predict Outcomes?

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:51
- 期刊: J Thorac Cardiovasc Surg · 中科院医学2区(外科1区/心脏和心血管系统2区/呼吸系统2区) · IF 4.7 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons
- 通讯作者: Anderson BR (Brett.Anderson@mssm.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jtcvs.2026.11.006
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtcvs.2026.11.006) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43098455/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：验证利用志愿者先天性心脏病外科医生自选的单段代表性手术视频及同行评审机制评估技术技能，并预测患者结局的有效性。
- 核心发现/数据：29名同行外科医生对来自14个中心的17名外科医生提交的Norwood手术（n=12）、CAVC修复（n=11）和ASO（n=14）视频按改良OSATS（1-5分）评分，平均总技能分为3.2（SD约1.0），各亚手术间相关性高（ρ=0.8-0.9）。将评分与STS-CHSD中5年病例链接后，Norwood和CAVC视频评分与Norwood结局显著相关；技能评分每增加1个标准差，5年Norwood病例风险校正后主要并发症或死亡预测概率由43%降至25%。在全部新生儿及全部儿科指标病例中，技能评分与结局的关联较弱。
- 临床启示：同行对单段代表性自选视频的评估可测量先心病外科医生技术技能，CHTSS与5年Norwood病例风险校正无并发症生存强相关，可作为技术精进和改善患者结局的评估工具。

**原文摘要**：Motivated to improve patient outcomes, we launched The Congenital Heart Technical Skill Study in 2015. In this proof-of-concept study, we sought to test the validity of leveraging single, representative, self-selected, video recordings of volunteer, operating congenital heart surgeons and a peer-review mechanism to assess surgeon technical skill. In this national collaboration, volunteer surgeons submitted self-selected, "representative" videos of themselves performing: 1) Norwood procedures, 2) complete atrioventricular canal (CAVC) repairs, and/or 3) arterial switch operations (ASOs). Twenty-nine peer-surgeons from 21 centers rated pre-designated video segments, using a modified Objective Structured Assessment of Technical Skills Score (OSATS; scale 1-5). Operations were graded on five specific domains (gentleness, tissue exposure, instrument handling, time/motion, and flow of operation) and given summary scores. Scores were linked via NPI to all cases performed by participating surgeons over five contemporaneous years in The Society of Thoracic Surgeons-Congenital Heart Surgery Database. Logistic regression quantified risk-adjusted associations between skill scores and a composite outcome (major morbidity or mortality) among three, concentric, cohorts: 1) All Norwood, 2) all neonatal, and 3) all pediatric index cases. Models adjusted for clinical characteristics and clustered standard errors by surgeon. Seventeen surgeons from 14 centers submitted videos of themselves performing Norwood procedures (n=12), CAVC repairs (n=11), and ASOs (n=14). Over 5 years, these surgeons performed 234 Norwood, 1,755 index neonatal, and 8,559 index pediatric cases. Mean overall skill scores were 3.2 (SD ∼1.0) on Norwood, CAVC, and ASO videos. Scores correlated highly across sub-procedures (ρ's 0.8-0.9). Norwood and CAVC video scores were significantly associated with Norwood outcomes. One standard deviation increase in skill scores was associated with reduction in the risk-adjusted predicted probability of major morbidity or mortality among 5-years of surgeons' Norwood cases from 43% to 25%. Associations between skill scores and outcomes were weaker among all index neonatal and all index pediatric cases. Congenital heart surgeons' technical skills can be measured directly via peer assessment of single, representative, self-selected, video recordings. These Congenital Heart Technical Skill Scores (CHTSS) are strongly associated with risk-adjusted morbidity-free survival across five years of surgeons' Norwood cases. This could serve as a valuable tool in surgeons' quest for technical mastery and-more importantly-in the quest to improve patient outcomes.

## Validation of Pre-Operative Proteinuria for Post Cardiac Surgery Acute Kidney Injury Prediction in Infants.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 05:51
- 期刊: Pediatr Cardiol · 中科院医学4区(儿科3区/心脏和心血管系统4区) · IF 1.3 · JCR Q3

- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00246-026-04426-6) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43097109/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：验证将术前及术后尿白蛋白-肌酐比（ACR）与年龄、性别、既往心脏手术、手术类型、RACHS-1评分、体外循环时间及基线肾功能等临床变量整合后，对婴儿心脏手术后急性肾损伤（AKI）的预测模型。
- 核心发现/数据：66例婴儿中AKI发生率为45.5%（30例）。术前临床模型AUC为0.838（95%CI 0.732-0.944），加入术前ACR后AUC提高至0.925（95%CI 0.864-0.986），敏感度80.0%、特异度94.4%、阳性预测值92.1%、阴性预测值85.0%；加入术后首次ACR未进一步改善模型判别能力。最终8变量logistic回归模型以列线图形式呈现，用于估计个体婴儿术后AKI概率。
- 临床启示：将白蛋白尿与临床参数整合可提高婴儿心脏手术后AKI的预测能力，该联合模型及列线图有助于早期风险分层，尤其在资源有限环境中具有实用价值。

**原文摘要**：To validate a clinical prediction model for acute kidney injury (AKI) in infants undergoing cardiac surgery by integrating pre- and post-operative urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio (ACR) with established clinical variables, including age, sex, prior cardiac surgeries, surgical type, RACHS-1 score, cardiopulmonary bypass time, and baseline kidney function. It was a prospective observational study done in a cardiothoracic unit of a tertiary care hospital in India. Infants aged 1 month to 1 year under cardiopulmonary bypass. Exclusion criteria included neonates, children > 1 year, pre-existing kidney dysfunction, structural renal abnormalities, prior kidney transplant, and incomplete data. Patients were followed through hospital discharge (up to 11 days). AKI was defined using KDIGO criteria. Urine ACR was measured at 6 h post-operatively and daily for two days. Among 66 infants, AKI occurred in 45.5% (n = 30). The pre-operative clinical model demonstrated an AUC of 0.838 (95% CI, 0.732-0.944). Adding pre-operative ACR improved the AUC to 0.925 (95% CI, 0.864-0.986), with a sensitivity of 80.0%, specificity of 94.4%, positive predictive value of 92.1%, and negative predictive value of 85.0%. Adding first post-operative ACR did not further improve model discrimination. The final eight-variable logistic regression model was presented as a nomogram to estimate an individual infant's probability of postoperative AKI. Integration of albuminuria with clinical parameters improves prediction of cardiac surgery-associated AKI in infants. This combined model and accompanying nomogram provide a practical approach for early risk stratification and may be particularly valuable in resource-limited settings.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：批判性评估孕期传统及下一代益生菌预防早产（PTB）的证据，明确其疗效、安全性及研究缺口。
- 核心发现/数据：证据明显不对称：在极低出生体重早产儿中，益生菌可降低严重坏死性小肠结肠炎和全因死亡，但这属于治疗早产后果，不能外推至产前预防。孕期证据不支持预防PTB：唯一以PTB为主要终点的meta分析为阴性，唯一报告产科结局的RCT未达主要终点，GBS定植的合并估计基于偏倚风险多不清或高的试验且后续安慰剂对照试验未重现，最有利结果来自单臂历史对照研究。因此疗效未证实，临床试验外不能推荐任何菌株或制剂；胎盘微生物组报告未获证实，益生菌作用具菌株特异性

**原文摘要**：Preterm birth (PTB) is a leading cause of neonatal morbidity and mortality. Probiotic modulation of the maternal microbiota has been proposed as prevention. We find a marked asymmetry in the evidence. Probiotic effects are strain-specific, yet several studies with obstetric endpoints do not report the organism to strain level. Safety differs fundamentally between pregnant adults and preterm infants, in whom invasive infection, product contamination and the absence of licensed products are documented concerns. Next-generation approaches remain preclinical. Progress requires adequately powered randomised trials that report full strain designations, confirm engraftment at the intended site and use clinical obstetric endpoints as primary outcomes.

**原文摘要**：Gastric outlet obstruction (GOO) in pancreatic cancer involving the first and second parts of the duodenum is typically palliated via endoscopic stenting or surgical gastrojejunostomy, often combined with biliary drainage. Infra-ampullary obstruction of the third and fourth parts of the duodenum is a distinct and poorly described problem. When biliary stenting has been performed, distal duodenal obstruction can drive reflux of enteric content into the bile duct and subsequent cholangitis, whilst food stasis and distension of the proximal duodenum promote nausea. A conventional gastrojejunostomy may not relieve these issues and endoscopic duodenal stenting may be technically challenging and often not durable. We report five consecutive patients. Five patients (three pancreatic adenocarcinoma, one pancreatic neuroendocrine tumour, one benign annular pancreas with choledochal cyst) underwent Nikfarjam bypass. One patient was subsequently converted to pancreaticoduodenectomy. In this initial experience, symptom relief was achieved and no patient required re-intervention. In this preliminary case series, this double bypass with decompressing duodenojejunostomy provided symptom relief for infra-ampullary duodenal obstruction. The core principle of the reconstruction is to prevent biliary-reflux cholangitis and duodenal distension whilst preserving a straightforward path to resection.

**原文摘要**：Cri du chat syndrome (CdCs) is a rare genetic disorder caused by deletions of various sizes in the short arm of chromosome 5. The clinical course of CdCs involves neurodevelopmental and communication features, including feeding and swallowing difficulties. Dysphagia is common in CdCs, with reported deficits meeting criteria for pediatric feeding and swallowing disorder, yet characteristics and symptom progression from birth to adulthood have not been studied in depth. The aim of this article is to describe the prevalence and evolution of feeding and swallowing difficulties from birth to adult age. Adult patients with a confirmed cytogenetic and/or molecular diagnosis of CdCs were recruited from the Italian Cri du chat Association (ABC Associazione Bambini Cri du chat Onlus, Italy) over a 1-year period. Pathogenic variants, along with key information regarding oral-motor features, were collected. Twenty-three patients were included in this study (10 M; mean age 34.3 years; SD 8.3 years; median age 34 years; age range 18-50 years). None of them were siblings to other participants. All patients were of Italian origin. While the whole cohort underwent typical karyotyping, Array CGH was performed in 13 patients. In individuals with CdCs, feeding and swallowing difficulties begin in the neonatal period (96%) and persist throughout life. Adulthood is characterized by a marked dependence on carers and has a significant impact on the family's care burden. In the present study, we reported their prevalence and evolution from birth to adulthood. Our study may improve the patients' management and support the introduction of preventive supporting habits in this rare disease.
