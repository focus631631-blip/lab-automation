## A Review of 87 Patients with Perineal, Scrotal, and Penoscrotal Hypospadias: More than 10 Years of Follow-up.

- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 日本 · Juntendo University School of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Miyake Y (ymiyake@juntendo.ac.jp)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163416
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163416) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42648437/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估会阴型、阴囊型及阴茎阴囊型（“严重”）尿道下裂术后至少10年的长期并发症发生情况。
- 核心发现/数据：87例患者中，20例（23.0%）在术后12个月内出现早期并发症，其中18/20（90.0%）无后遗症；另有7例（8.0%）出现晚期并发症（尿道狭窄1例、弯曲复发2例、腔内毛发生长1例、射精功能障碍3例），其中5/7（71.4%）无早期并发症史，发病时间多在术后6-9年甚至10年后。
- 临床启示：严重尿道下裂术后应延长随访时间，至少应终身随访，因为晚期并发症可能延迟出现且缺乏早期预警信号。

**原文摘要**：Data for long-term postoperative follow-up of perineal, scrotal, or penoscrotal subtype ("severe") hypospadias patients is scarce. Timing/types of complications and outcomes over at least 10 years' follow-up were reviewed. A retrospective review of all severe hypospadias patients (n=87) who underwent surgical repair by a single surgeon between 2000 and 2015 and followed-up for at least 10 years was performed. Severe hypospadias was perineal (n=26), scrotal (n=21), or penoscrotal (n=40). All had Thiersch-Duplay urethroplasties. Clinical variables analyzed included age at surgery, surgical repair procedures, follow-up duration, and incidence of specific problematic postoperative complications (urethral fistula or stenosis, recurrence of curvature, intraluminal hair growth, or ejaculatory dysfunction after puberty). Overall, 20/87 developed some type of postoperative complication within 12 months; 18/20 (90.0%) recovered without sequelae and 7/87 (8.0%) developed urethral stenosis (n=1), recurrence of curvature (n=2), intraluminal hair growth (n=1), or ejaculatory dysfunction (n=3) later. Onset was 6-9 years for the stenosis, curvature, and 1 ejaculation case and after 10 years for the hair and 2 ejaculation cases. Of these, 2/7 (28.6%) had previous early complications, but 5/7 (71.4%) had no previous history of complications. Early postoperative complications in severe hypospadias patients recovered in 90% without sequelae. Late specific problematic complications took at least 5 years to develop in 8% with 71.4% having no previous history of postoperative complications. Consequently, follow-up should be extended at minimum and possibly life-long because of the potential for specific problematic postoperative complications to develop late, as demonstrated by this review.

## Vaginal Reconstruction in Anorectal Malformations: A 17-Year Single-Center Experience.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 05:45
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2

- 通讯作者: Tilev Oztan SM (stilev@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163403
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163403) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42648436/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：总结17年单中心肛门直肠畸形（ARM）合并阴道异常患儿行阴道重建的经验，探讨手术决策、技术选择及结局。
- 核心发现/数据：共18例患者，其中持续性泄殖腔占61%，直肠前庭瘘伴肛门闭锁占33%；61%为双阴道，33%为远端阴道缺如。重建方法包括阴道口成形术（28%）、阴道隔切除术（33%）和肠段/瘘管组织阴道替代术（33%）；并发症发生率44%，其中伤口裂开33%、瘘管形成28%，且瘘管形成与既往伤口裂开显著相关（p<0.001）。
- 临床启示：ARM相关阴道异常需个体化重建，优先利用自体阴道组织，复杂病例应行保护性结肠造口，并随访至青春期。

**原文摘要**：/Purpose: Vaginal anomalies frequently accompany anorectal malformations (ARMs) in females, yet management strategies remain poorly standardized. This study presents our 17-year single-center experience with vaginal reconstruction in ARM patients with documented Müllerian and/or vaginal anomalies, focusing on surgical decision-making, technique selection, and outcomes. This study was conducted retrospectively on all children with ARMs who had documented Müllerian and/or vaginal anomalies who underwent vaginal reconstruction at our tertiary referral center between January 2004 and January 2021. Patient demographics, ARM types, vaginal anomaly types, associated anomalies, surgical approaches, vaginal reconstruction techniques, complications, and long-term outcomes were analyzed. Categorical variables were compared using Fisher's exact test. Eighteen patients underwent vaginal reconstruction: 11 (61%) had persistent cloaca (PC), 6 (33%) had rectovestibular fistula with anal atresia (RVFAA), and 1 (6%) had cloacal exstrophy. Eleven patients (61%) had duplicated vaginas; distal vaginal agenesis was present in 6 (33%). Associated anomalies were highly prevalent: vertebral (72%), gastrointestinal (39%), urinary (39%), cardiac (27%), and VATER/VACTERL association (22%). Mean age at definitive surgery was 2.50±1.53 years. Vaginal reconstruction methods included introitoplasty (28%), vaginal septum excision (33%), and vaginal replacement using bowel segments and/or rectovestibular fistula tissue (33%). Mean achieved vaginal length was 60.56±13.16 mm. Complications occurred in 44% of patients, predominantly wound dehiscence (33%) and fistula formation (28%). Fistula formation was significantly associated with prior wound dehiscence (p<0.001). Mean follow-up was 7.18±3.66 years (range 2.3-14.3 years). Vaginal anomalies in ARMs require individualized reconstruction approaches. No single technique is universally applicable. The surgical algorithm should prioritize maximal native vaginal tissue use, appropriate replacement tissue selection when needed, protective colostomy for complex cases, and long-term follow-up through puberty.

**原文摘要**：Growth differentiation factor 15 (GDF15), a stress-responsive cytokine, is a diagnostic biomarker of mitochondrial diseases. Given our previous finding that hepatocellular mitochondrial viability is crucial for native liver survival (NLS) in patients with biliary atresia (BA), this study investigated whether GDF15 could function as a biomarker for liver injury, diagnosis, and prognosis in BA. Its correlation with hepatic fibrosis severity, jaundice clearance (JC) achievement, and NLS was evaluated in BA. The GDF15-positive hepatocyte proportion was higher in BA (n = 54) than in IC (n = 22) or NL (n = 16) (p<0.001). This proportion was also higher in BA without JC (p<0.01) or NLS (p<0.05) and correlated with fibrosis severity (p<0.001). Serum GDF15 levels were higher in BA (n = 11) than in IC (n = 9) (cutoff = 680.4 pg/mL; sensitivity = 80%, specificity = 82%) and controls (n = 40) (p<0.001), and significantly correlated with GDF15-positive hepatocyte proportion (p<0.001) and with total and direct bilirubin, aspartate aminotransferase, alanine aminotransferase, and alkaline phosphatase (all p<0.05), while showing inverse correlations with platelet count (p<0.01). GDF15 is a reliable indicator of hepatic dysfunction and a diagnostic and prognostic biomarker of BA. Ⅱ.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估维生素D补充对短肠综合征/肠衰竭（SBS/IF）患者血清25(OH)D水平的影响，并探讨25(OH)D水平与感染风险的关系。
- 核心发现/数据：补充维生素D显著提高血清25(OH)D（平均变化+15.61 vs -0.40 ng/mL；ANCOVA F=18.33, p=0.001）。基线25(OH)D普遍不足，78.3%缺乏（<20 ng/mL）。随访期间共发生26次感染，个体内25(OH)D每升高1 ng/mL，感染风险降低8%（HR 0.92，95%CI 0.869-0.976，p=0.005）。
- 临床启示：维生素D补充可有效改善SBS/IF患者的维生素D状态，且较高的25(OH)D水平与更低的感染风险相关，提示维生素D可能是该人群可干预的感染风险因素。

**原文摘要**：Patients with short bowel syndrome (SBS) and intestinal failure (IF) are highly susceptible to micronutrient deficiencies and recurrent infections. Vitamin D plays an important role in immune modulation and has been linked to infection outcomes in other high-risk populations, yet its relevance in SBS/IF remains unclear. This study evaluated the effectiveness of vitamin D supplementation and the association between serum 25-hydroxyvitamin D [25(OH)D] levels and infection risk in SBS/IF patients. In this single-center retrospective cohort study, 23 SBS/IF patients (8 pediatric, 34.8%) followed between November 2022 and November 2025 were included. Baseline 25(OH)D levels showed universal insufficiency, with 78.3% classified as deficient (<20 ng/mL). Changes in 25(OH)D levels at 6-12 months were compared between patients with and without supplementation using ANCOVA. The association between serial 25(OH)D levels and recurrent infection events was analyzed using a time-dependent Cox model with person-mean centering. Vitamin D supplementation resulted in significantly greater increases in serum 25(OH)D compared with non-supplementation (mean change +15.61 vs. -0.40 ng/mL; ANCOVA F = 18.33, p = 0.001). During follow-up, 26 infection events occurred. Higher within-individual 25(OH)D levels were associated with a reduced hazard of infection (HR 0.92 per 1 ng/mL increase; 95% CI 0.869-0.976; p = 0.005). Vitamin D supplementation effectively improves vitamin D levels in SBS/IF patients. Higher 25(OH)D levels were associated with lower infection hazard, suggesting vitamin D status may represent a modifiable factor related to infection risk in this population.
