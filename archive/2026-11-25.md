## Hemolysis in neonatal whole-blood gas testing: prevalence and association with potassium.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 06:03
- 期刊: Eur J Pediatr · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区) · IF 2.9 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: Madrid · Hospital General Universitario Gregorio Marañón
- 通讯作者: Merino-Hernández A (amaia.merino1@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00431-026-07658-z
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-026-07658-z) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43077136/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估新生儿全血血气检测中溶血的发生率及其与血钾升高的关联，并分析采集方式和样本来源的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：在334例患者的2520份样本中，溶血阳性率为10.2%；毛细血管血溶血率高于注射器采集（12.6% vs 6.1%），且按来源以毛细血管（14.0%）、静脉（7.1%）、动脉（2.0%）递减。溶血样本高钾发生率为44.0%，非溶血样本为14.2%（RR 3.09，95%CI 2.60-3.67），且随溶血指数升高呈分级关系；HI≥3时高钾占63.1%（RR 3.93，95%CI 3.20-4.83），校正后溶血仍独立关联高钾（aOR 4.62，95%CI 3.39-6.29）。pH与血钾的关联因溶血状态不同而不同（交互p=0.015）。
- 临床启示：新生儿血气检测中应重视溶血对血钾假性升高的影响，结合溶血指数、血钾、pH及样本来源综合判读，并加强毛细血采集等前分析质量控制。

**原文摘要**：Hemolysis is a frequent preanalytical problem in neonatal blood sampling and may lead to falsely elevated whole-blood potassium measurements. Integrated hemolysis detection may identify affected samples at the point of care. We retrospectively analyzed neonatal whole-blood samples from 18 May to 9 September 2026 using the GEM Premier 7000 with integrated hemolysis index (HI) measurement. Significant hemolysis was defined as HI ≥ 2 (> 50 mg/dL plasma-free hemoglobin) and elevated potassium as K⁺ > 5.2 mmol/L. We assessed hemolysis prevalence, collection container, sample source, and associations with potassium and pH. Repeated measurements were accounted for using patient-clustered generalized estimating equations. Among 2520 samples from 334 patients, 257 (10.2%) were hemolysis-positive. Hemolysis was more frequent in capillary- than syringe-collected specimens (12.6 vs 6.1%; p < 0.001) and differed by source (capillary 14.0%, venous 7.1%, arterial 2.0%; p < 0.001). Elevated potassium occurred in 44.0% of hemolyzed versus 14.2% of non-hemolyzed samples (RR 3.09, 95% CI 2.60-3.67; p < 0.001) and increased with higher HI (ρ = 0.23; p < 0.001). At HI ≥ 3, 63.1% had elevated potassium versus 16.0% below this threshold (RR 3.93, 95% CI 3.20-4.83). Hemolysis remained independently associated with elevated potassium (adjusted OR 4.62, 95% CI 3.39-6.29; p < 0.001). The pH-potassium association differed by hemolysis status (interaction p = 0.015).  Hemolysis was strongly associated with elevated potassium, with a graded relationship across HI categories. Hemolysis also varied by collection characteristics and sample source. Integrating HI with potassium, pH, and sample source may improve neonatal potassium interpretation and preanalytical quality monitoring. • Hemolysis is a common preanalytical problem in neonatal blood sampling and can cause falsely elevated potassium concentrations. • Integrated hemolysis detection in blood-gas analyzers may improve recognition of hemolysis-related potassium interference. • In neonatal whole-blood samples, hemolysis was independently associated with elevated potassium and showed a graded relationship with increasing hemolysis severity. • Hemolysis prevalence differed according to collection characteristics and sample source, with the highest prevalence in capillary specimens; the relationship between pH and potassium also differed according to hemolysis status.

## Stillbirth Risk by Gestational Age for Fetuses With Selected Birth Defects.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 06:03
- 期刊: Obstet Gynecol · 中科院医学2区(妇产科学2区) · IF 5.1 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: Georgia · Medical Genetics
- 通讯作者: National Birth Defects Prevention Study
- DOI: 10.1097/AOG.0000000000006487
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/AOG.0000000000006487) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43077661/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：估计美国人群中特定出生缺陷胎儿按孕龄的前瞻性死胎风险。
- 核心发现/数据：研究纳入18,739例，其中活产91.9%（17,217例）、死胎4.5%（850例）、≥20周因出生缺陷终止妊娠3.6%（672例），53%（9,940例）为孤立性缺陷。多数缺陷的前瞻性死胎风险在20周最高、35或37周最低；无脑畸形、双侧肾缺如及羊膜带伴肢体体壁复合体在所有孕龄时间点均超过100/1,000例风险胎儿。羊膜带序列（仅肢体）和脐膨出早期风险亦高且持续高于一般人群；多数胃肠道、骨骼肌肉和神经缺陷风险中等但高于人群，食管闭锁、脑积水和单纯唇裂/腭裂最低（各时间点≤20/1,000）。
- 临床启示：这些按缺陷类型和孕龄分层的死胎风险数据可用于父母咨询，并辅助规划产前胎儿监测的时机和强度。

**原文摘要**：To estimate the prospective risk of stillbirth by gestational age among fetuses with specific birth defects in a U.S. population-based cohort. We conducted a retrospective cohort study using data from the National Birth Defects Prevention Study, a large, population-based, multistate, collaborative case-control study of selected major birth defects in the United States. The database includes pregnancies ending in live birth, stillbirth, or termination with selected major birth defects (excluding those with genetic disorders) from active surveillance programs in nine U.S. states during 1997-2011. Clinical geneticists confirmed and classified all defects using abstracted medical record information. Prospective stillbirth (fetal death at or after 20 weeks of gestation) risk through 44 weeks was estimated at 20, 29, 35, and 37 weeks as the conditional probability of stillbirth from a given gestational age until the end of pregnancy. Of the 18,739 cases included in the analytic population, 91.9% (n=17,217) were liveborn, 4.5% (n=850) were stillborn, and 3.6% (n=672) underwent termination for birth defects at or after 20 weeks of gestation. Fifty-three percent (n=9,940) had an isolated birth defect. Prospective stillbirth risk was highest at 20 weeks of gestation and lowest at 35 or 37 weeks for most defects. Estimates exceeded 100 in 1,000 fetuses at risk across all gestational age timepoints for fetuses with anencephaly, bilateral renal agenesis, and amniotic bands with limb body wall complex. Estimates for amniotic band sequence (limbs only) and omphalocele were similarly high early in gestation and remained markedly elevated relative to the general population. Estimates for most gastrointestinal, musculoskeletal, and neurologic defects were more moderate in magnitude but consistently higher than population risks. Estimates were lowest (at or below 20/1,000 fetuses at risk) at each timepoint for esophageal atresia, hydrocephaly, and cleft lip or palate alone. Stillbirth risk varied by birth defect and across gestation. These estimates may be beneficial for parental counseling and for planning the timing and intensity of antepartum fetal surveillance in affected pregnancies.
