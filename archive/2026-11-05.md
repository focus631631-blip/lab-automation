## Domino Liver Transplantation Using the Explanted Native Liver of a Multivisceral Transplant Recipient: A Pediatric Case Report and Systematic Review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 19:24
- 期刊: Pediatr Transplant · 中科院医学4区(儿科4区/移植4区) · IF 1.4 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 美国 · MedStar Georgetown Transplant Institute
- 通讯作者: Gondolesi GE
- DOI: 10.1111/petr.70518
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1111/petr.70518) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42985780/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告一例婴儿多脏器移植受者切除的结构正常自体肝用于儿童多米诺肝移植的病例，并系统回顾相关文献以评估其可行性、结局及临床意义。
- 核心发现/数据：本病例为4个月男婴因近全肠切除合并胃、十二指肠及膀胱瘘接受多脏器移植，其结构正常自体肝跨ABO血型移植给11个月胆道闭锁女婴（预测生存率约1%）；多脏器移植受者末次随访良好，多米诺受者术后3周死于颅内出血但肝功能正常。系统综述仅发现1997—2023年3篇既往报告共4例（1例成人受者、1例儿童摘要、1组2例儿童接受联合肝肠移植受者左外叶供肝）。原受者移植物功能稳定，多米诺受者易发生胆道和血管并发症；该策略可行且可由一个供者创造两名受者。
- 临床启示：多脏器移植来源的自体肝可作为扩大儿童供肝池的潜在多米诺供肝，甚至可跨ABO血型使用；但推广前需建立标准化流程、分阶段知情同意和登记随访，且受者术前危重程度可能比供肝质量更影响结局。

**原文摘要**：Children awaiting liver transplantation face donor scarcity and waitlist mortality. Domino liver transplantation (DLT), or re-use of a structurally normal explanted liver, has expanded the donor pool in metabolic disease, but the multivisceral transplant (MVT) recipient, whose normal native liver is removed when a liver-inclusive visceral allograft is implanted, is rarely used. MEDLINE, Embase, and PubMed were used from inception to August 21, 2025, with conference-proceedings and citation searching for any report of DLT using the explanted native liver from a MVT recipient or combined liver-intestine transplant recipient, without age or language restriction. Two reviewers screened and appraised the evidence. Our case was a 4-month-old boy who underwent a MVT for near-total enterectomy complicated with a gastric, duodenal, and bladder fistula. His structurally normal native liver was transplanted across ABO incompatibility into an 11-month-old girl with biliary atresia and ~1% predicted survival. The MVT recipient remains well at last follow-up; the domino recipient died of intracranial hemorrhage three weeks after transplantation, with normal liver function. Three prior reports including 4 cases exist worldwide (1997-2023): one adult-recipient case, one pediatric abstract, and one series of two children given domino left-lateral-lobe grafts from combined liver-intestine recipients. Index recipients consistently retained stable graft function; domino recipients were prone to biliary and vascular complications. DLT from an MVT explant is feasible, including across ABO incompatibility, and can create two recipients from one donor. In our case the domino graft functioned immediately, but the recipient died from complications of her pretransplant critical illness; consistent with the literature, domino outcomes appeared to be driven more by recipient pretransplant severity than by graft quality. Standardized protocols, staged consent, and registry tracking are needed before wider adoption.

- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gassur.2026.102666) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42982335/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：识别儿童结肠代食管术后吻合口漏的围手术期预测因素，以指导风险分层和管理。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入51例接受结肠代食管的儿童，术后30天内吻合口漏发生12例（23.5%）。多因素分析显示，较大年龄与漏风险降低独立相关（每增加1个月aOR 0.95，95%CI 0.91-0.99，p=0.017），而术前喂养造口与漏风险增加独立相关（aOR 15.00，95%CI 2.64-85.19，p=0.002）。在腐蚀性狭窄亚组中，严重初始食管损伤和复杂扩张史亦与漏相关。
- 临床启示：对低龄及术前存在喂养造口的患儿应加强个体化术前风险评估和围手术期管理。上述发现尚需更大规模多中心研究验证。

**原文摘要**：Esophageal replacement (ER) in children, primarily for caustic stricture or long-gap atresia, remains a high-morbidity procedure. Anastomotic leak is a critical postoperative complication. This study aimed to identify perioperative predictors of anastomotic leak following colonic interposition, thereby guiding risk stratification and management. A retrospective single-center cohort study included 51 pediatric patients (median age: 48 months) who underwent colonic interposition for ER between 1998 and 2025. Cervical anastomotic leak within 30 days was the primary outcome. Patients with and without leak were compared using univariate analysis of demographic, preoperative, intraoperative, and postoperative variables, followed by multivariate logistic regression. A secondary analysis was performed in the caustic stenosis subgroup (n = 43). Anastomotic leak occurred in 12 patients (23.5%). On univariate analysis, patients with leak were younger, more often had a preoperative feeding stoma, less frequently received nasogastric tube placement, and had higher rates of postoperative respiratory complications with longer ventilation duration. In multivariate analysis, older age at ER was independently associated with a decreased risk of anastomotic leak (a OR 0.95 per month; 95% CI 0.91-0.99; p = 0.017). Conversely, a preoperative feeding stoma independently increased the odds of leak formation (a OR of 15.00; 95% CI 2.64-85.19; p = 0.002). In this cohort, younger age at ER and a preoperative feeding stoma were independently associated with anastomotic leak. Among children with caustic stricture, severe initial esophageal injury and a complicated dilatation history were also associated with leak. These findings support individualized preoperative risk assessment and should be validated in larger multicenter studies.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：总结先天性膈疝合并脊髓脊膜膨出、Chiari II畸形及肾发育不良的产前与产后超声表现。
- 核心发现/数据：患儿为35周出生的女性新生儿，体重2550 g，Apgar评分2/4/5，存在严重左侧Bochdalek膈疝、腰骶部脊髓脊膜膨出、Chiari II畸形及单侧肾发育不良；母亲无叶酸补充史。尽管接受重症监护及吸入一氧化氮治疗持续性肺动脉高压，患儿仍于72小时死亡。

**原文摘要**：Congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH) associated with neural tube defects is exceedingly rare. Maternal history revealed no folic acid supplementation. Despite intensive care and inhaled nitric oxide for persistent pulmonary hypertension, the patient died at 72 h. This case highlights the value of prenatal ultrasound in detecting multisystem anomalies and supports the concept of early embryologic mesodermal disruption, potentially exacerbated by nutritional deficiencies.
