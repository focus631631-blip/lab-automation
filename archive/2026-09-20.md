## National medical expenditures associated with pediatric surgical care in the United States.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 09:04
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Stanford University School of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Groenewald CB (groenewald@stanford.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163492
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163492) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42763012/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：基于2017—2022年美国医疗支出面板调查，估算0—18岁儿童外科相关医疗支出及其全国经济负担。
- 核心发现/数据：儿童手术加权患病率为4.1%（95% CI：3.9%—4.4%）。调整社会经济因素和合并症后，手术儿童人均总医疗支出为13,271美元，未手术儿童为2,198美元，增量支出为11,072美元（95% CI：7,677—14,467美元）；全国儿科外科支出年均约354亿美元（95% CI：245亿—463亿美元）。增量支出最高为住院服务（4,854美元）和门诊就诊（2,030美元），主要支付方为私人保险（5,417美元）和Medicaid/CHIP（4,867美元），自付支出增加522美元。
- 临床启示：儿童外科治疗带来显著经济负担，临床实践需重视成本效益、围术期资源优化及家庭自付压力。该数据可为医疗政策制定和资源配置提供依据。

**原文摘要**：Surgical care accounts for a large proportion of U.S. healthcare costs, yet comprehensive data on pediatric surgical expenditures remain limited. This study provides nationally representative estimates of medical expenditures associated with pediatric surgery in the United States. We performed cross-sectional analysis of children ages 0-18 years participating in the 2017-2022 Medical Expenditure Panel Survey (n=39,926). Children were considered to have undergone surgery if they had at least one medical encounter with the primary reason being an "operation or surgical procedure." Multivariable regression models were used to estimate incremental medical expenditures associated with surgery, adjusting for sociodemographic characteristics and comorbidity burden. The overall weighted prevalence of surgery was 4.1% (95% CI: 3.9-4.4). After adjustment for sociodemographic and comorbid conditions, children who underwent surgery had significantly higher total per-person medical expenditures compared to those who did not ($13,271 vs $2,198, respectively; p<0.0001). This resulted in an incremental expenditure associated with surgery of $11,072 (95% CI: $7,677-$14,467). Weighted to the national population, pediatric surgical expenditures averaged 35.4 billion dollars per year (95% CI: $24.5 billion- $46.3 billion). When examined by service category, the highest incremental expenditures were for inpatient services ($4,854) and office-based visits ($2,030). When categorized by payment source, the largest incremental expenditures were borne by private insurance ($5,417) and Medicaid/CHIP ($4,867), with significant increases also noted for out-of-pocket expenditures ($522). Pediatric surgery is associated with substantial economic burden of over $11,000 per child and over $35 billion dollars nationally. These findings underscore the significant financial impact of surgical care and can inform healthcare policy and resource allocation decisions.

## Exposure to Polystyrene Nanoplastics Induces Ferroptosis-Associated Cardiac Injury: Cell-Based Evidence for Lysosome-Associated NLRX1 Loss and an NLRX1-STING-NCOA4 Response.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:04
- 期刊: Cardiovasc Toxicol · 中科院医学4区(毒理学3区/心脏和心血管系统4区) · IF 3.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Shandong Provincial Hospital Affiliated to Shandong First Medical University
- 通讯作者: Zhao G (zg000507@163.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s12012-026-10193-w
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12012-026-10193-w) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42762285/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探究聚苯乙烯纳米塑料（PS-NPs）是否通过降低NLRX1、激活STING并上调NCOA4，导致心肌细胞铁稳态失衡及铁死亡相关心脏损伤。
- 核心发现/数据：在原代新生小鼠心室心肌细胞及8周口服暴露小鼠模型中，PS-NPs降低NLRX1和GPX4、升高STING磷酸化和ACSL4，促进铁蓄积和脂质过氧化，导致细胞活力下降及小鼠心功能障碍和纤维化。机制上，NLRX1 mRNA不变而蛋白经溶酶体途径丢失；NLRX1过表达减轻STING磷酸化、铁蓄积和脂质过氧化，STING沉默降低NCOA4并升高FTH1，NCOA4沉默逆转GPX4下调和ACSL4

**原文摘要**：Nanoplastics are widespread environmental contaminants encountered through ingestion and inhalation, raising concerns about their cardiovascular toxicity. How polystyrene nanoplastics (PS-NPs) disrupt cardiomyocyte iron homeostasis remains unclear. We hypothesized that reduced NLR family member X1 (NLRX1) links PS-NP exposure to stimulator of interferon genes (STING) signaling and nuclear receptor coactivator 4 (NCOA4)-associated iron dysregulation. We examined primary neonatal mouse ventricular cardiomyocytes and an 8-week oral-exposure mouse model. In both models, PS-NPs reduced NLRX1 and glutathione peroxidase 4 (GPX4) protein levels, increased STING phosphorylation and acyl-CoA synthetase long-chain family member 4 (ACSL4) expression, and promoted iron accumulation and lipid peroxidation. Cell viability declined, and mice developed cardiac dysfunction and fibrosis. In cardiomyocytes, NLRX1 mRNA remained unchanged. Bafilomycin A1 attenuated NLRX1 protein loss, while NLRX1 showed greater spatial association with lysosome-associated membrane protein 1 (LAMP1)-positive compartments. NLRX1 overexpression reduced STING phosphorylation, iron accumulation, and lipid peroxidation. STING silencing decreased NCOA4 and increased ferritin heavy chain 1, whereas NCOA4 silencing attenuated GPX4 downregulation and ACSL4 upregulation. Re-expression experiments supported the functional ordering of NLRX1, STING, and NCOA4. Ferrostatin-1 (Fer-1) co-treatment attenuated iron accumulation, lipid peroxidation, and GPX4/ACSL4 changes in both models. It improved cardiomyocyte viability and attenuated cardiac dysfunction and fibrosis, although the myocardial phosphorylated-to-total STING ratio remained above control levels. These findings support ferroptosis-associated cardiac injury following PS-NP exposure, with cellular evidence implicating lysosome-associated NLRX1 protein loss and a downstream STING-NCOA4 response.
