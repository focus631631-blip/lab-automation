## Retrospective cohort analysis of spontaneous regression in pediatric lymphatic malformations and its role in modern multidisciplinary approach.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 12:16
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 日本 · Kyoto Prefectural University of Medicine
- 通讯作者: Fumino S (fumin@koto.kpu-m.ac.jp)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163602
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163602) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43067521/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估儿童淋巴管畸形（LMs）自发消退的发生率、时间及预测因素。
- 核心发现/数据：118例患儿中43例（36.4%）发生自发消退；巨囊型病变消退率为55.9%（33/59），显著高于非巨囊型的16.9%（10/59），多变量校正OR为5.11（95%CI 1.94-13.45，p<0.001）。1年、2年、5年累积自发消退率分别为26.6%、31.1%、35.9%，中位消退时间为193天（范围5-2869天）。
- 临床启示：儿童LMs自发消退并不少见，且与巨囊型形态密切相关；在多学科诊疗框架下，对经选择的临床稳定患者可考虑谨慎的初始观察策略。

**原文摘要**：Lymphatic malformations (LMs) often require active treatment, including sclerotherapy, surgery, or sirolimus. However, some lesions regress spontaneously, and factors associated with spontaneous regression remain incompletely undefined. In this study, we aimed to investigate the incidence, timing, and predictors of spontaneous regression in pediatric LMs. We retrospectively reviewed 118 patients diagnosed with LMs before 20 years of age and by 2025 who had documented outpatient follow-up and/or treatment at our institution between January 2007 and April 2026. Spontaneous regression was defined as a >50% reduction in lesion volume without standard LMs-directed treatment, with no clinically significant symptoms, only minimal residual cosmetic concern, and no active treatment considered necessary; regression was confirmed by MRI in all cases. Logistic regression was performed to identify associated factors. Cumulative incidence of spontaneous regression was estimated using a competing-risks approach, with initiation of LMs-directed treatment before regression as a competing event. Spontaneous regression occurred in 43 of 118 patients (36.4%), including 33 of 59 macrocystic lesions (55.9%) versus 10 of 59 non-macrocystic lesions (16.9%). Macrocystic morphology remained significantly associated with regression after multivariable adjustment (odds ratio, 5.11; 95% confidence interval, 1.94-13.45; p < 0.001). The cumulative incidence of spontaneous regression was 26.6% (95% CI, 18.5-34.7%) at 1 year, 31.1% (22.6-39.6%) at 2 years, and 35.9% (27.0-44.8%) at 5 years. Among regressors, the median time to documented regression was 193 days (range, 5-2869 days). Spontaneous regression occurred in a substantial proportion of pediatric LMs and was strongly associated with macrocystic morphology. Careful initial observation may be a reasonable management strategy for selected clinically stable patients within a multidisciplinary approach. Level II Retrospective Study.

## Distinct Treg-Mediated Cardiac Remodeling in HFpEF Mice Treated With Neonatal Mesenchymal Stem Cells and Their Secretome.

- 国家/单位: 美国 · Stanley Manne Children's Research Institute
- 通讯作者: Davis ME (michael.davis@bme.gatech.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jacbts.2026.101751
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacbts.2026.101751) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43067120/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估新生儿间充质干细胞及其分泌组能否通过调节性T细胞（Treg）改善射血分数保留型心衰（HFpEF）小鼠的心脏重构与舒张功能。
- 核心发现/数据：新生儿间充质干细胞及其分泌组可扩增Treg、减轻炎症和纤维化，从而改善HFpEF小鼠舒张功能。研究构建的因果机器学习框架识别出驱动心功能改善的因果免疫通路，并将Treg确定为治疗靶点。摘要未提供具体数值数据。
- 临床启示：提示新生儿间充质干细胞及其分泌组有望成为HFpEF的疾病修饰治疗策略。靶向Treg介导的免疫通路可能为HFpEF提供新的临床干预方向。

**原文摘要**：Heart failure with preserved ejection fraction lacks effective disease-modifying therapies. We show that neonatal mesenchymal stem cells and their secretome improve diastolic function by expanding regulatory T cells, reducing inflammation and fibrosis. Our new causal machine learning framework, variable importance prediction for cellular function, identified causal immune pathways driving cardiac functional improvement and revealed regulatory T cells as a therapeutic target.

## Role of remote ischemic conditioning in necrotizing enterocolitis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 12:16
- 期刊: World J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学4区(儿科4区/外科4区) · IF 2.1
- 国家/单位: 加拿大 · The Hospital for Sick Children
- 通讯作者: Pierro A
- DOI: 10.1136/wjps-2026-001251
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1136/wjps-2026-001251) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43065813/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：总结远程缺血调理（RIC）治疗坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）的科学依据与临床研究进展，并评估其作为NEC治疗策略的可行性。
- 核心发现/数据：临床前模型中，NEC早期给予RIC可通过一氧化氮和硫化氢信号改善肠道微循环障碍，减少肠损伤、降低促炎细胞因子并延长生存，但保护作用仅发生在不可逆黏膜损伤出现之前。一项非随机I期安全性研究首次证实RIC在NEC婴儿中安全且无不良并发症。目前RIC-NEC II期可行性随机对照试验正在8个国家15个国际中心开展，评估在NEC诊断后24小时内随机分组并实施掩蔽RIC的可行性。
- 临床启示：RIC是一种非侵入性、早期干预有潜力的NEC辅助治疗策略，但现有证据尚限于临床前及I期安全性研究，需等待II/III期试验验证其能否阻止疾病进展和改善结局。临床暂不应将其作为常规治疗。

**原文摘要**：Necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) is a life-threatening inflammatory intestinal disorder predominantly affecting preterm infants, with a high mortality and morbidity rate. The pathophysiology of NEC is intimately linked to immaturity of the intestinal vasculature and derangements in microcirculatory perfusion, rendering the developing bowel susceptible to ischemic injury following enteral feeding. Remote ischemic conditioning (RIC) is a non-invasive maneuver involving brief cycles of limb ischemia and reperfusion, which engages endogenous cytoprotective mechanisms that can be transmitted to distant organs, including the intestine. This review examines the scientific basis and clinical trajectory of RIC as a therapeutic strategy in NEC. In preclinical models, RIC administered during the early stages of NEC counteracted characteristic derangements in intestinal microcirculation, mediated through nitric oxide and hydrogen sulfide signaling, and thereby led to reduced intestinal injury, attenuation of pro-inflammatory cytokines and prolonged survival. These protective effects occurred only before the onset of irreversible mucosal damage. A non-randomized phase I safety study subsequently confirmed, for the first time in infants with NEC, that RIC was safe and produced no adverse complications. To address the knowledge gaps necessary for an efficacy trial, the RIC-NEC phase II feasibility randomized controlled trial is currently ongoing at 15 international centers across 8 countries. This trial evaluates the feasibility of randomizing and delivering masked RIC to eligible neonates within 24 hours of NEC diagnosis. If successful, a future phase III multicenter randomized controlled trial will assess whether RIC can prevent disease progression and improve clinical outcomes in this vulnerable population.
