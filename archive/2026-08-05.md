## From Barriers to Solutions: Establishing an Academic Pediatric Surgical Hub in an Underserved Region of Tunisia.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 01:42

- DOI: 10.1002/wjs.70514
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/wjs.70514) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42549714/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：在突尼斯欠发达地区建立学术儿科手术中心，解决资源受限环境下儿科手术可及性不足的问题。
- 核心发现/数据：通过“无墙服务”模式将儿科手术嵌入成人外科，年手术量增加84%（2025年达361例），住院人次增至三倍；无围手术期死亡，术后并发症率仅3%且均为轻微伤口问题。
- 临床启示：资源受限地区无需等待完善基础设施，依托现有成人外科体系并实施分诊风险管理，即可安全推进儿科手术服务的 decentralization。

**原文摘要**：In low- and middle-income countries (LMIC), pediatric surgical care remains concentrated in urban tertiary centers, resulting in delayed access for rural populations. To address this inequity, we established an academic pediatric surgery department within a secondary-level regional hospital in Zaghouan, Tunisia, serving a broad underserved catchment area. Operating without dedicated pediatric infrastructure or pediatric anesthesiology services, we implemented a "service without walls" model by embedding pediatric surgery within an existing adult surgical department. Adult wards and perioperative systems were adapted through close collaboration with anesthesia teams, and a strict triage system was implemented to ensure safe case selection. Since its establishment, the department has demonstrated a significant increase in activity, with an 84% rise in annual surgical volume (361 procedures in 2025) and a tripling of inpatient admissions. The service now manages a wide spectrum of pediatric surgical conditions locally, including emergencies and selected complex cases, while high-risk neonates are stabilized and referred. No perioperative mortality occurred, and postoperative morbidity was limited to 3% consisting of minor wound-related complications. Our experience demonstrates that establishing a regional surgical hub does not require waiting for flawless infrastructure. By embedding pediatric surgery within existing adult surgical infrastructure, implementing collaborative perioperative protocols, and using triage-based risk management, safe pediatric surgical care can be successfully decentralized in resource-constrained settings. This model provides a scalable framework for improving access to pediatric surgical care in similar LMIC settings.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告放射学疑似先天性节段性过度充气（CSO）患儿的手术指征及术后组织学分类。
- 核心发现/数据：143例因先天性肺畸形切除的患儿中，16例为先天性大叶性过度充气（CLO），7例为CSO；4例CSO保守治疗后因节段内感染需手术，1例术前CT发现新增小囊肿；6例CSO组织学为先天性肺气道畸形（CPAM）特征，而CLO中15例为过度充气、仅1例为CPAM（p=0.0005）。

**原文摘要**：Congenital segmental overinflation (CSO) is frequently managed non-operatively. We observed some children with CSO developing symptoms and requiring resection. Here we aim to report indications for surgery and subsequent histological classification of children undergoing resection for radiologically suspected CSO. Retrospective data collected included patient characteristics, radiological findings, symptom status and histological findings. **原文摘要**：Up to 30% of in-utero selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor (SSRI)-exposed newborns experience withdrawal and up to 60% experience behavioural impacts. Breastfeeding may offer benefits from skin-to-skin contact and transfer of SSRIs through breastmilk. Limited data are available on healthcare utilisation of these newborns beyond neonatal intensive care unit admission. ## Perioperative Mammillary Body Injury on MRI in Neonates with Severe Congenital Heart Disease.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 06:26
- 期刊: Can J Cardiol · 中科院医学2区(心脏和心血管系统3区) · IF 5.7 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: Utrecht · Wilhelmina Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Nijman M (M.Nijman@umcutrecht.nl)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.cjca.2026.07.371
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cjca.2026.07.371) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42551788/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估新生儿危重先天性心脏病（CCHD）患者围手术期乳头体（MB）损伤的发生率、时机及其与两岁时认知结局的关联。  
- 核心发现/数据：术前MB损伤发生率5%，术后升至61%（p < 0.001）。术前及术后新发MB损伤均与较低出生后月经龄（p = 0.002）和围手术期低心排综合征（p = 0.030/0.033）显著相关；但新生儿期MB损伤与两岁时认知指数评分无显著差异。  
- 临床启示：CCHD新生儿围手术期MB损伤常见，尤其术后高发，且与低心排综合征和早产/低孕周相关，提示需关注记忆相关脑结构的保护，其远期认知影响仍需进一步研究。

**原文摘要**：Neonates with critical congenital heart disease (CCHD) are at risk for long-term cognitive impairments, including memory deficits. We aimed to assess the incidence and timing of mammillary body (MB) injury, critical memory structures, in neonates with CCHD and explore its association with cognitive outcome at two years of age. We included neonates who underwent surgery with cardiopulmonary bypass in the first six weeks of life. Pre- and postoperative cerebral magnetic-resonance-imaging were reviewed for MB injury, defined as signal change on T1-, T2-, or diffusion-weighted imaging or atrophy. Cognitive outcomes at two years were measured using the Bayley Scales of Infant and Toddler Development, Third Edition. A total of 96 neonates were included. MB injury was present in 5% preoperatively and 61% postoperatively (p < 0.001). It was not related to other acquired brain lesions. Preoperative MB injury occurred more frequently in neonates with lower postmenstrual age at birth (p = 0.002) and those who developed low cardiac output syndrome prior to surgery (p = 0.030). Similarly, new postoperative MB injury was associated with lower postmenstrual age at surgery (p = 0.002) as well as perioperative low cardiac output syndrome (p = 0.033). No significant differences in cognitive index scores emerged between children with neonatal MB injury and those without. MB injury is frequent in neonatal CCHD, predominantly postoperatively, and is associated with low cardiac output syndrome and lower postmenstrual age. The relationship between neonatal MB injury and cognitive and memory outcomes later in life warrants further study.
