## Durability of Right Ventricle to Pulmonary Artery Conduits Implanted in Patients Less Than One Year of Age.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 01:42
- 期刊: World J Pediatr Congenit Heart Surg · 中科院医学4区(心脏和心血管系统4区) · IF 0.9
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Heart Institute
- 通讯作者: Wiggins L
- DOI: 10.1177/21501351261458314
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1177/21501351261458314) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42550826/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估一岁以下婴幼儿右心室-肺动脉带瓣管道植入后的功能及再干预情况。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入91例婴儿，中位年龄15天、体重3.6kg，中位管道直径10mm，最常见为肺动脉同种移植物（45%）；无手术死亡，中位随访5.8年，所有患者均至少经历1次管道置换，中位间隔27.2个月，其中24%在首次置换时小于1岁；首次再手术后中位随访14.2个月内有7例死亡，其中86%在置换时小于1岁。
- 临床启示：一岁以下婴儿因管道衰败需再次手术置换时，死亡风险显著增高，临床应优化初次管道选择与随访策略，尽量延迟再手术年龄。

**原文摘要**：ObjectivesNeonates and infants with conotruncal heart defects often require placement of valved conduits to establish continuity between the right ventricle (RV) and pulmonary arteries (PA). The objective of this study was to assess the function and reintervention profile of RV-PA conduits implanted in patients who are less than 1 year of age.MethodsNinety-one infants underwent placement of an RV-PA conduit at a single institution between 2012 and 2023. Patient records and imaging were reviewed for demographics, operative characteristics, and postoperative outcomes.ResultsThe median age and weight at the time of index RV-PA conduit were 15 (IQR 6-174) days and 3.6 (IQR 3.0-6.5) kg. The median conduit diameter was 10 (IQR 9-12) mm, and the most common conduit was a pulmonary homograft (45%, 41/91). There were no mortalities at the index operation. Median follow-up was 5.8 (IQR 2.8-9) years.All patients in the cohort have undergone at least 1 conduit replacement at a median interval of 27.2 (IQR 10.1-53.2) months, with 24% of those patients (22/91) being less than 1 year of age at first replacement. There were 7 deaths following the first reoperation over a median follow-up of 14.2 (0.6-59.5) months, and 86% (6/7) of those who died were less than 1 year of age at the time of conduit replacement.ConclusionsThese results benchmark contemporary outcomes of right ventricle to pulmonary artery conduit durability in neonates and infants. There is significant morbidity and mortality associated with reoperation for conduit replacement at less than 1 year of age.

## From Barriers to Solutions: Establishing an Academic Pediatric Surgical Hub in an Underserved Region of Tunisia.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 01:42
- 期刊: World J Surg · 中科院医学3区(外科3区) · IF 2.2 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Tunisia · Zaghouan Hospital
- 通讯作者: Ammar SB
- DOI: 10.1002/wjs.70514
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/wjs.70514) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42549714/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：在突尼斯欠发达地区建立学术儿科手术中心，解决资源受限环境下儿科手术可及性不足的问题。
- 核心发现/数据：通过“无墙服务”模式将儿科手术嵌入成人外科，年手术量增加84%（2025年达361例），住院人次增至三倍；无围手术期死亡，术后并发症率仅3%且均为轻微伤口问题。
- 临床启示：资源受限地区无需等待完善基础设施，依托现有成人外科体系并实施分诊风险管理，即可安全推进儿科手术服务的 decentralization。

**原文摘要**：In low- and middle-income countries (LMIC), pediatric surgical care remains concentrated in urban tertiary centers, resulting in delayed access for rural populations. To address this inequity, we established an academic pediatric surgery department within a secondary-level regional hospital in Zaghouan, Tunisia, serving a broad underserved catchment area. Operating without dedicated pediatric infrastructure or pediatric anesthesiology services, we implemented a "service without walls" model by embedding pediatric surgery within an existing adult surgical department. Adult wards and perioperative systems were adapted through close collaboration with anesthesia teams, and a strict triage system was implemented to ensure safe case selection. Since its establishment, the department has demonstrated a significant increase in activity, with an 84% rise in annual surgical volume (361 procedures in 2025) and a tripling of inpatient admissions. The service now manages a wide spectrum of pediatric surgical conditions locally, including emergencies and selected complex cases, while high-risk neonates are stabilized and referred. No perioperative mortality occurred, and postoperative morbidity was limited to 3% consisting of minor wound-related complications. Our experience demonstrates that establishing a regional surgical hub does not require waiting for flawless infrastructure. By embedding pediatric surgery within existing adult surgical infrastructure, implementing collaborative perioperative protocols, and using triage-based risk management, safe pediatric surgical care can be successfully decentralized in resource-constrained settings. This model provides a scalable framework for improving access to pediatric surgical care in similar LMIC settings.

## Thoracoabdominal Cysts in the First Trimester as an Early Sonographic Sign of Congenital Diaphragmatic Hernia: A Case Series.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 01:42
- 期刊: J Ultrasound Med
- 国家/单位: 越南 · Hung Vuong Hospital
- 通讯作者: Le Anh T
- DOI: 10.1002/jum.70407
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1002/jum.70407) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42550919/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨孕早期右侧胸腹腔交界处囊肿作为先天性膈疝（CDH）早期超声征象的临床意义。
- 核心发现/数据：报告3例孕早期右侧胸腹腔交界处囊肿，初判为肝脏或肺部来源，后均进展为右侧CDH。囊肿在孕中期消退的时间点与胸腔内肝脏疝出的发生相重合，而非先于疝出出现。
- 临床启示：识别这一超声模式有助于更早诊断CDH，并为围产期咨询提供重要依据。

**原文摘要**：First-trimester fetal abdominal cysts at the right thoracoabdominal border are typically regarded as benign findings. We report three cases in which such cysts, initially attributed to hepatic or pulmonary origin, subsequently evolved into right-sided congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH), providing additional sonographic evidence for a previously under-recognized pattern. Notably, cyst regression in the second trimester coincided with-rather than preceded-the development of intrathoracic hepatic herniation. Recognition of this pattern may facilitate earlier CDH diagnosis and improve perinatal counseling.
