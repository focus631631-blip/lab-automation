## Identification and validation of a robust inflammation-related three-gene signature for the diagnosis of neonatal necrotizing enterocolitis via machine learning integration.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:49
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · The University of Hong Kong Shenzhen Hospital
- 通讯作者: Wong KKY (kkywong@hku.hk)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06659-1
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06659-1) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42799867/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：整合转录组数据与机器学习算法，识别并验证用于新生儿坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）诊断的稳健炎症相关三基因标志物。
- 核心发现/数据：在训练集GSE64801中识别出452个差异表达基因，与935个炎症相关基因交集后得到20个候选基因，并经LASSO回归和Boruta随机森林筛选出REG3A、CXCL9和KNG1三个核心生物标志物，且在训练集和独立验证集GSE46619的NEC组织中均上调。基于三基因构建的列线图诊断效能较高，训练队列AUC达0.956；机制分析显示趋化因子信号、肠道免疫网络及移植物抗宿主反应等免疫炎症过程参与，并预测avoralstat等潜在治疗药物。
- 临床启示：该三基因炎症签名对NEC具有较高诊断准确性，有望作为早期分子诊断工具，并为精准治疗提供科学依据。

**原文摘要**：Neonatal necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) is a severe gastroesophageal emergency in neonates, especially premature infants, with acute inflammation and necrosis. Due to the nonspecific nature of early clinical manifestations, delayed diagnosis remains an issue often associated with high mortality and morbidity, hence finding molecular biomarkers with high diagnostic specificity is critical for better clinical outcomes. This work combines transcriptomic data with machine learning algorithms to identify a diagnostic signature that strongly correlates to NEC. The GSE64801 transcriptome dataset from GEO was trained and the independent GSE46619 dataset was validated externally. A bioinformatics pipeline was developed to detect differentially expressed genes (DEG) between NEC and control tissues intersected with 935 known inflammation-related genes (IRGs) to identify inflammation-specific candidates. For feature robustness, the combinatorial screening strategy involving least absolute shrinkage and selection operator regression and Boruta random forest algorithm was used. Diagnostic performance of identified core biomarkers was validated across cohorts. A diagnostic nomogram was constructed and performance was evaluated using Receiver Operating Characteristics (ROC) curves, calibration plots, Decision Curve Analysis (DCA). Various downstream analyses including GSEA, regulatory network construction, drug target prediction were performed. In the training cohort 452 DEGs were identified, down to 20 candidates after intersecting with IRGs. The two machine learning strategies identified three key biomarkers: REG3A, CXCL9 and Kininogen 1 (KNG1). Validation assays showed that these genes are upregulated in NEC tissues in both cohorts. The nomogram constructed from this three genes was more diagnostically effective and achieved Area Under the Curve (AUC) of 0.956 in the training cohort. Mechanistically GSEA plays a significant role in key immune-inflammatory processes such as chemokine signaling, intestinal immune networks and graft-versus-host responses. Further upstream transcriptional regulation network was constructed and potential therapeutic agents such as avoralstat were predicted. We identified and validated a novel three gene inflammatory signature (REG3A, CXCL9, KNG1) high diagnostic accuracy for NEC. These biomarkers are involved in the basic pathophysiology of the disease, providing a scientific basis for early diagnostic tools and precise treatments.

## Alobar Holoprosencephaly with Severe Craniofacial Anomalies and Congenital Diaphragmatic Hernia: First Reported Case from Somalia.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:49
- 期刊: Pediatric Health Med Ther · 中科院医学4区(儿科3区) · IF 1.8
- 国家/单位: Somalia · Mogadishu Somali Turkish Training and Research Hospital
- 通讯作者: Mohamed NM
- DOI: 10.2147/PHMT.S625135
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.2147/PHMT.S625135) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42799234/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告索马里首例无叶全前脑畸形合并严重颅面畸形及先天性膈疝的新生儿病例，并强调早期产前筛查与遗传咨询的重要性。
- 核心发现/数据：患儿为足月新生儿，母亲为来自农村地区的多产妇且未行产前筛查；出生时表现为无鼻、无眼等严重中线颅面畸形。经颅超声提示单一单脑室及关键中线结构缺失，符合无叶全前脑，并合并先天性膈疝和无发绀型膜周部室间隔缺损；因多系统畸形仅予支持治疗，于生后第3天死于多系统衰竭。
- 临床启示：无叶全前脑预后极差，临床管理以呼吸稳定、喂养支持和舒适护理等支持治疗为主；在资源有限地区尤应重视早期产前筛查、详细超声和遗传咨询，以促进及时诊断、家长知情咨询及新生儿救治规划。

**原文摘要**：Alobar holoprosencephaly (HPE) is a severe malformation where the embryonic forebrain fails to divide, characterized by profound midline facial defects and high mortality. We report a case of a term neonate born to a grand multigravida mother from a rural area with no prior antenatal screening. At birth, the neonate presented with severe midline craniofacial anomalies, including arrhinia and anophthalmia. Transcranial ultrasonography demonstrated findings highly suggestive of alobar HPE, including a single monoventricle and absence of key midline structures, while additional findings included a congenital diaphragmatic hernia and an Acyanotic perimembranous ventricular septal defect. Due to the severity of these multisystem malformations, the patient was managed with supportive care and succumbed to multisystem failure on the third day of life. Alobar holoprosencephaly has an extremely poor prognosis, with most affected neonates not surviving long after birth; therefore, management focuses on supportive care, such as respiratory stabilization, feeding, and comfort. This case highlights the crucial role of early prenatal screening, detailed ultrasound, and genetic counseling especially in resource-limited settings to allow for timely diagnosis, informed parental counseling, and better planning for neonatal care.

## Growth and dietary effects on gastrointestinal symptoms in children with Hirschsprung's disease.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 09:49
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: S-222 42 · Skåne University Hospital
- 通讯作者: Telborn L (lovisa.telborn@med.lu.se)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06657-3
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06657-3) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42799814/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：本研究旨在调查先天性巨结肠患儿自我报告的生长情况，以及饮食对胃肠道症状的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：共71例患儿参与（84%，中位年龄6岁），9%（6/71）报告生长迟缓，直肠乙状结肠型与长段型无显著差异（7% vs 15%，p=0.328）。77%（55/71）报告饮食诱发的胃肠道症状，两组亦无显著差异（74% vs 92%，p=0.227）。在评估的90种食物中，最常触发症状的是高FODMAPs、游离糖和脂肪食物。
- 临床启示：先天性巨结肠患儿随访中应常规评估生长和饮食因素，并关注饮食相关胃肠道症状。未来需进一步开展饮食策略干预研究以优化临床管理。

**原文摘要**：Growth and diet are major concerns for patients with Hirschsprung's disease yet are rarely described. This study aimed to investigate self-reported growth and the dietary impact on gastrointestinal symptoms in children with Hirschsprung's disease. This observational cross-sectional study used a validated self-report questionnaire. All children aged 1-18 years old (n = 85) with Hirschsprung's disease treated at a national center were invited to participate. In total, 71 children (84%) participated (median age 6 years [range 1-17]). Stunting was reported in 6 of 71 children (9%), with no significant difference between those with rectosigmoid and long-segment aganglionosis (4/58 [7%] vs. 2/13 [15%], p = 0.328). The majority (55/71; 77%) reported diet-induced gastrointestinal symptoms, again with no significant difference between the groups (43/58 [74%] vs. 12/13 [92%] p = 0.227). Of the 90 food items assessed, the most frequently reported to trigger symptoms were high in fermentable oligosaccharides, disaccharides, monosaccharides, and polyols (FODMAPs), free sugar and fat. Diet-related gastrointestinal complaints are reported frequently in children with Hirschsprung's disease, regardless of aganglionic extent. The findings highlight the need to assess growth and dietary factors in follow-up care and support further interventional studies on dietary strategies.
