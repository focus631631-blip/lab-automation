## Reduced apelin expression and atrial fibrosis in postoperative atrial fibrillation following coronary artery bypass grafting.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:44
- 期刊: Biomol Biomed · 中科院医学4区(医学：研究与实验4区) · IF 2.8 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Shanxi Cardiovascular Hospital (Institute)
- 通讯作者: Wang H
- DOI: 10.17305/bb.2026.14946
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.17305/bb.2026.14946) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42993807/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨apelin与冠状动脉旁路移植术（CABG）后心房颤动（POAF）相关心房纤维化的关联及其对促纤维化信号的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：研究纳入18例（CABG伴POAF 6例、CABG无POAF 6例、先天性心脏病窦律6例作为次级参考）；POAF组心房apelin表达及术后第2天血清apelin低于无POAF组，伴胶原沉积、TGF-β1表达和Smad2磷酸化增加，而总Smad2不变。体外实验中，apelin可减轻TGF-β1诱导的新生大鼠心房成纤维细胞活化、胶原表达和Smad2磷酸化，沉默apelin则增加纤维化相关蛋白表达。
- 临床启示：提示apelin降低可能与CABG后心房纤维化及POAF相关，并可能作为潜在抗纤维化干预靶点；但该研究为小样本探索性研究，临床意义尚需更大队列和补充实验验证。

**原文摘要**：The role of apelin in atrial fibrosis linked to postoperative atrial fibrillation (POAF) following coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG) is not fully understood. This exploratory study investigated apelin's association with POAF-related atrial fibrosis and its impact on profibrotic signaling. The study included 18 patients: six CABG patients with POAF, six CABG patients without POAF, and six patients with congenital heart disease in sinus rhythm (serving as a secondary reference). Left atrial appendage tissue was analyzed histologically and molecularly, and serum apelin levels were measured on postoperative day 2 in CABG patients. Primary neonatal rat atrial fibroblasts were treated with transforming growth factor-β1 (TGF-β1) both with and without apelin, or subjected to apelin silencing. Compared with CABG patients without POAF, the POAF group exhibited lower atrial apelin expression and serum concentrations, increased collagen deposition, elevated TGF-β1 expression, and enhanced phosphorylation of mothers against decapentaplegic homolog 2 (Smad2), while total Smad2 remained unchanged. Experimentally, apelin attenuated TGF-β1-induced fibroblast activation, collagen expression, and Smad2 phosphorylation. Conversely, apelin silencing increased the expression of fibrosis-associated proteins. These preliminary findings suggest a link between reduced apelin and atrial fibrosis, as well as POAF after CABG. They also indicate antifibrotic effects of apelin, potentially mediated through TGF-β1/Smad2 signaling. Further research with larger clinical cohorts and complementary experimental models is necessary to fully elucidate the pathway mediation and clinical significance of these findings.

## Intestinal perforations in extremely and very low birth weight infants: prognostic factors and differentiation between necrotizing enterocolitis and spontaneous intestinal perforation.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:44
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 日本 · Kumamoto City Hospital
- 通讯作者: Okumura K (okuehon@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06713-y
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06713-y) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42993324/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨极低和超低出生体重儿肠穿孔的生存预后因素，并寻找术前鉴别坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）与自发性肠穿孔（SIP）的预测指标。
- 核心发现/数据：回顾性纳入2005—2025年40例因肠穿孔手术的极低/超低出生体重儿，幸存者与非幸存者的基础疾病差异显著；非幸存者穿孔时血小板计数更低、C反应蛋白更高。NEC患儿较SIP更可能在穿孔前接受>0.5 mL肠内喂养，且穿孔时血小板计数更低。基础疾病和炎症严重程度与死亡相关，耐受肠内喂养量和血小板计数可能有助于术前鉴别NEC与SIP。
- 临床启示：基础疾病及炎症严重程度可用于评估肠穿孔患儿预后；结合穿孔前耐受肠内喂养量和血小板计数，有助于术前区分NEC与SIP并指导手术决策。

**原文摘要**：Extremely low birth weight infants (ELBWIs) and very low birth weight infants (VLBWIs) are at high risk of developing intestinal perforation in the early postnatal period, and improvements in diagnosis and management are required. Since the optimal first surgical intervention may differ between necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) and spontaneous intestinal perforation (SIP), reliable preoperative differentiation is needed. This study aimed to identify factors associated with survival and determine predictors useful for differentiating NEC from SIP. We retrospectively reviewed ELBWIs and VLBWIs who underwent surgical treatment for intestinal perforations at our institution between 2005 and 2025. Forty patients were included in the study. The underlying diseases differed significantly between survivors and non-survivors. Non-survivors had lower platelet counts and higher C-reactive protein levels during perforation. NEC patients were more likely than SIP patients to receive enteral feeding (> 0.5 mL) before perforation and had lower platelet counts at the time of perforation. Underlying diseases and severity of inflammation are associated with mortality. Tolerated enteral feeding volume and platelet count may aid in preoperative differentiation between NEC and SIP and in surgical decision-making.

## Outcomes with indocyanine green fluorescence angiography use in pediatric colorectal surgery.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:44
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Texas Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Hickner BT (brian.hickner@bcm.edu)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06711-0
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06711-0) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42993328/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估术中吲哚菁绿荧光血管造影（ICG-FA）在小儿结直肠手术中与术后临床结局的关联。
- 核心发现/数据：184例患儿中37例（20.1%）使用ICG-FA；ICG组更年长且复杂手术比例更高（59.5% vs. 15.7%，p<0.001），ICG-FA在5例（13.5%）中改变了术中决策。两组吻合口破裂和再手术率相似，但ICG组术后狭窄率显著更低（2.7% vs. 17.0%，p=0.032）；复杂病例中ICG组无狭窄，而非ICG组为7例（30.4%）（p=0.009）。多因素logistic回归显示ICG使用仍与较低狭窄率相关（OR 0.12，95% CI 0.01-0.61）。
- 临床启示：ICG-FA可影响术中决策，尤其可能适用于复杂小儿结直肠重建，并与较低术后狭窄率相关；临床可考虑在复杂病例中辅助评估肠管灌注，但仍需前瞻性研究确认。

**原文摘要**：Adequate intestinal perfusion is essential in pediatric colorectal surgery. Indocyanine green fluorescence angiography (ICG-FA) is increasingly used to assess perfusion, yet its impact on postoperative outcomes remains unclear. We evaluated the association between ICG-FA use and clinical outcomes in children undergoing colorectal procedures. We performed a retrospective cohort study of pediatric patients undergoing posterior sagittal anorectoplasty (PSARP) for anorectal malformations, pull-through for Hirschsprung disease, or cloacal repair between 2016 and 2025. Primary outcomes included anastomotic disruption, stricture, and reoperation. Among 184 patients, 37 (20.1%) underwent intraoperative ICG-FA. Patients receiving ICG-FA were older and more likely to undergo complex procedures (59.5% vs. 15.7%, p < 0.001). ICG-FA altered intraoperative decision-making in five cases (13.5%). Rates of anastomotic disruption and reoperation were similar between groups. Stricture rates were significantly lower in the ICG group (2.7% vs. 17.0%, p = 0.032). Among complex cases, no strictures occurred in the ICG group compared with 7 (30.4%) in the non-ICG group (p = 0.009). On multivariable logistic regression, ICG use remained associated with lower observed stricture rates (OR 0.12, 95% CI 0.01-0.61). ICG can influence surgical decision-making and may be particularly useful in complex colorectal reconstructions. Postoperative stricture rates were lower among patients who underwent ICG-FA, particularly those undergoing more complex operations.
