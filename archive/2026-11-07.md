## Reduced apelin expression and atrial fibrosis in postoperative atrial fibrillation following coronary artery bypass grafting.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:44
- 期刊: Biomol Biomed · 中科院医学4区(医学：研究与实验4区) · IF 2.8 · JCR Q3
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Shanxi Cardiovascular Hospital (Institute)
- 通讯作者: Wang H
- DOI: 10.17305/bb.2026.14946
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.17305/bb.2026.14946) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42993807/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨apelin与冠状动脉旁路移植术（CABG）后心房颤动（POAF）相关心房纤维化的关联及其对促纤维化信号的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：研究纳入18例（CABG伴POAF 6例、CABG无POAF 6例、先天性心脏病窦律6例作为次级参考）；POAF组心房apelin表达及术后第2天血清apelin低于无POAF组，伴胶原沉积、TGF-β1表达和Smad2磷酸化增加，而总Smad2不变。体外实验中，apelin可减轻TGF-β1诱导的新生大鼠心房成纤维细胞活化、胶原表达和Smad2磷酸化，沉默apelin则增加纤维化相关蛋白表达。
- 临床启示：提示apelin降低可能与CABG后心房纤维化及POAF相关，并可能作为潜在抗纤维化干预靶点；但该研究为小样本探索性研究，临床意义尚需更大队列和补充实验验证。

**原文摘要**：The role of apelin in atrial fibrosis linked to postoperative atrial fibrillation (POAF) following coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG) is not fully understood. This exploratory study investigated apelin's association with POAF-related atrial fibrosis and its impact on profibrotic signaling. The study included 18 patients: six CABG patients with POAF, six CABG patients without POAF, and six patients with congenital heart disease in sinus rhythm (serving as a secondary reference). Left atrial appendage tissue was analyzed histologically and molecularly, and serum apelin levels were measured on postoperative day 2 in CABG patients. Primary neonatal rat atrial fibroblasts were treated with transforming growth factor-β1 (TGF-β1) both with and without apelin, or subjected to apelin silencing. Compared with CABG patients without POAF, the POAF group exhibited lower atrial apelin expression and serum concentrations, increased collagen deposition, elevated TGF-β1 expression, and enhanced phosphorylation of mothers against decapentaplegic homolog 2 (Smad2), while total Smad2 remained unchanged. Experimentally, apelin attenuated TGF-β1-induced fibroblast activation, collagen expression, and Smad2 phosphorylation. Conversely, apelin silencing increased the expression of fibrosis-associated proteins. These preliminary findings suggest a link between reduced apelin and atrial fibrosis, as well as POAF after CABG. They also indicate antifibrotic effects of apelin, potentially mediated through TGF-β1/Smad2 signaling. Further research with larger clinical cohorts and complementary experimental models is necessary to fully elucidate the pathway mediation and clinical significance of these findings.

## Intestinal perforations in extremely and very low birth weight infants: prognostic factors and differentiation between necrotizing enterocolitis and spontaneous intestinal perforation.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:44
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 日本 · Kumamoto City Hospital
- 通讯作者: Okumura K (okuehon@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06713-y
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06713-y) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42993324/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨极低和超低出生体重儿肠穿孔的生存预后因素，并寻找术前鉴别坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）与自发性肠穿孔（SIP）的预测指标。
- 核心发现/数据：回顾性纳入2005—2025年40例因肠穿孔手术的极低/超低出生体重儿，幸存者与非幸存者的基础疾病差异显著；非幸存者穿孔时血小板计数更低、C反应蛋白更高。NEC患儿较SIP更可能在穿孔前接受>0.5 mL肠内喂养，且穿孔时血小板计数更低。基础疾病和炎症严重程度与死亡相关，耐受肠内喂养量和血小板计数可能有助于术前鉴别NEC与SIP。
- 临床启示：基础疾病及炎症严重程度可用于评估肠穿孔患儿预后；结合穿孔前耐受肠内喂养量和血小板计数，有助于术前区分NEC与SIP并指导手术决策。

**原文摘要**：Extremely low birth weight infants (ELBWIs) and very low birth weight infants (VLBWIs) are at high risk of developing intestinal perforation in the early postnatal period, and improvements in diagnosis and management are required. Since the optimal first surgical intervention may differ between necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) and spontaneous intestinal perforation (SIP), reliable preoperative differentiation is needed. This study aimed to identify factors associated with survival and determine predictors useful for differentiating NEC from SIP. We retrospectively reviewed ELBWIs and VLBWIs who underwent surgical treatment for intestinal perforations at our institution between 2005 and 2025. Forty patients were included in the study. The underlying diseases differed significantly between survivors and non-survivors. Non-survivors had lower platelet counts and higher C-reactive protein levels during perforation. NEC patients were more likely than SIP patients to receive enteral feeding (> 0.5 mL) before perforation and had lower platelet counts at the time of perforation. Underlying diseases and severity of inflammation are associated with mortality. Tolerated enteral feeding volume and platelet count may aid in preoperative differentiation between NEC and SIP and in surgical decision-making.

## Outcomes with indocyanine green fluorescence angiography use in pediatric colorectal surgery.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:44
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Texas Children's Hospital
- 通讯作者: Hickner BT (brian.hickner@bcm.edu)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06711-0
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06711-0) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42993328/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估术中吲哚菁绿荧光血管造影（ICG-FA）在小儿结直肠手术中与术后临床结局的关联。
- 核心发现/数据：184例患儿中37例（20.1%）使用ICG-FA；ICG组更年长且复杂手术比例更高（59.5% vs. 15.7%，p<0.001），ICG-FA在5例（13.5%）中改变了术中决策。两组吻合口破裂和再手术率相似，但ICG组术后狭窄率显著更低（2.7% vs. 17.0%，p=0.032）；复杂病例中ICG组无狭窄，而非ICG组为7例（30.4%）（p=0.009）。多因素logistic回归显示ICG使用仍与较低狭窄率相关（OR 0.12，95% CI 0.01-0.61）。
- 临床启示：ICG-FA可影响术中决策，尤其可能适用于复杂小儿结直肠重建，并与较低术后狭窄率相关；临床可考虑在复杂病例中辅助评估肠管灌注，但仍需前瞻性研究确认。

**原文摘要**：Adequate intestinal perfusion is essential in pediatric colorectal surgery. Indocyanine green fluorescence angiography (ICG-FA) is increasingly used to assess perfusion, yet its impact on postoperative outcomes remains unclear. We evaluated the association between ICG-FA use and clinical outcomes in children undergoing colorectal procedures. We performed a retrospective cohort study of pediatric patients undergoing posterior sagittal anorectoplasty (PSARP) for anorectal malformations, pull-through for Hirschsprung disease, or cloacal repair between 2016 and 2025. Primary outcomes included anastomotic disruption, stricture, and reoperation. Among 184 patients, 37 (20.1%) underwent intraoperative ICG-FA. Patients receiving ICG-FA were older and more likely to undergo complex procedures (59.5% vs. 15.7%, p < 0.001). ICG-FA altered intraoperative decision-making in five cases (13.5%). Rates of anastomotic disruption and reoperation were similar between groups. Stricture rates were significantly lower in the ICG group (2.7% vs. 17.0%, p = 0.032). Among complex cases, no strictures occurred in the ICG group compared with 7 (30.4%) in the non-ICG group (p = 0.009). On multivariable logistic regression, ICG use remained associated with lower observed stricture rates (OR 0.12, 95% CI 0.01-0.61). ICG can influence surgical decision-making and may be particularly useful in complex colorectal reconstructions. Postoperative stricture rates were lower among patients who underwent ICG-FA, particularly those undergoing more complex operations.

## Causal association between gestational diabetes mellitus and peripartum mental disorders: Evidence from two-sample Mendelian randomization.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:53
- 期刊: Medicine (Baltimore) · 中科院医学4区(医学：内科4区) · IF 2.0 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Hebei Medical University
- 通讯作者: Wu X
- DOI: 10.1097/MD.0000000000050818
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000050818) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42994878/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估遗传预测的妊娠期糖尿病是否因果性增加围产期精神障碍（抑郁、焦虑和自杀倾向）风险。
- 核心发现/数据：共识别19个独立单核苷酸多态性；逆方差加权分析显示遗传预测的妊娠期糖尿病与围产期精神障碍呈正向因果关联（β=0.048，SE=0.014，P=8.3×10⁻⁴；OR=1.049，95%CI=1.020–1.0779）。敏感性分析结果一致，未发现异质性或多效性证据。
- 临床启示：研究提示妊娠期糖尿病可能因果性增加围产期精神障碍风险，临床应整合代谢控制与心理健康管理。

**原文摘要**：Gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM) is associated with multiple maternal and neonatal complications, but its direct causal impact on peripartum mental health remains uncertain. This study used a two-sample Mendelian randomization (MR) approach to assess whether genetically predicted GDM increases the risk of peripartum mental disorders, including depression, anxiety, and suicidality. Genetic variants associated with GDM were obtained from the FinnGen consortium, and summary data for peripartum mental disorders were derived from genome-wide association studies. Independent single-nucleotide polymorphisms (P < 5 × 10-6, r2 < 0.001) were analyzed using inverse-variance weighted, MR-Egger, and weighted median methods. Heterogeneity, pleiotropy, and causal direction were evaluated using Cochran's Q, the MR-Egger intercept, and Steiger filtering. Nineteen independent single-nucleotide polymorphisms were identified. Inverse-variance weighted analysis indicated a positive causal association between genetically predicted GDM and peripartum mental disorders. The estimated effect was β = 0.048, with a standard error (SE) of 0.014 and P = 8.3 × 10⁻⁴; the odds ratio was 1.049 (95% confidence interval = 1.020-1.0779). The results were consistent in all the sensitivity analyses, with no evidence of heterogeneity or pleiotropy. The MR findings provide genetic evidence supporting a causal link between GDM and peripartum mental disorders. These results underscore the need for integrated clinical management that simultaneously addresses metabolic control and psychological well-being in women diagnosed with GDM.

## Concurrent prenatal diagnosis of hereditary neuropathy with liability to pressure palsies and congenital pulmonary airway malformation: A case report.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:53
- 期刊: Medicine (Baltimore) · 中科院医学4区(医学：内科4区) · IF 2.0 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Changsha Hospital for Maternal and Child Health Care
- 通讯作者: Li C
- DOI: 10.1097/MD.0000000000051479
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000051479) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42994863/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过一例胎儿同时产前诊断遗传性压力易感性周围神经病（HNPP）和先天性肺气道畸形（CPAM）的病例，强调产前发现结构异常时进行详细家族史采集和遗传评估的重要性。
- 核心发现/数据：孕妇38岁，孕24周超声发现胎儿右胸腔约49 mm×27 mm×39 mm高回声病灶，血供来自右肺动脉；羊膜腔穿刺染色体微阵列分析检出17p12区1.38 Mb杂合缺失，涉及PMP22基因，确诊HNPP，胸部病灶依据超声特征诊断CPAM。多学科咨询后继续妊娠，于38+2周择期剖宫产分娩健康女婴（3150 g，Apgar 9/10），无神经或呼吸功能缺陷，随访超声示CPAM逐渐消退。
- 临床启示：产前超声发现结构异常时应详细询问家族史并考虑靶向遗传检测。对HNPP胎儿可实施个体化围产计划，包括择期剖宫产以避免器械助产和减少产程神经压迫，并建立长期神经随访。

**原文摘要**：Hereditary neuropathy with liability to pressure palsies (HNPP) is a rare autosomal dominant peripheral neuropathy typically diagnosed in adolescence or adulthood. Prenatal diagnosis is exceedingly rare. Congenital pulmonary airway malformation (CPAM) is a common congenital lung lesion. We report a unique case of concurrent prenatal diagnoses of HNPP and CPAM, highlighting the importance of genetic evaluation when structural anomalies are detected prenatally. A 38-year-old pregnant woman (gravida 3, para 1) underwent a systematic prenatal ultrasound at 24 weeks of gestation, which revealed a hyperechoic lesion measuring approximately 49 mm × 27 mm × 39 mm in the fetal right thoracic cavity with blood supply from the right pulmonary artery. The pregnancy was otherwise uncomplicated. The fetus's father and paternal aunt had recurrent limb numbness and weakness, with a known genetic diagnosis of HNPP in the aunt. Amniocentesis and chromosomal microarray analysis identified a 1.38 Mb heterozygous deletion at 17p12 encompassing the peripheral myelin protein 22 gene in the fetus, confirming the diagnosis of HNPP. The right-sided thoracic lesion was diagnosed as CPAM based on characteristic ultrasound features. Following multidisciplinary counseling, the parents opted to continue the pregnancy. Individualized perinatal planning was implemented, including elective cesarean delivery at 38 weeks to avoid instrumental extraction and minimize intrapartum nerve compression. Serial ultrasound follow-up was performed every 2 to 3 weeks, and a long-term neurological follow-up plan was established for the newborn. The patient delivered a healthy female neonate (3150 g, Apgar 9/10) via elective cesarean at 38 + 2 weeks. No neurological or respiratory deficits were observed. CPAM regressed gradually on follow‑up ultrasound. The infant is under regular pediatric and neurological surveillance, with chest imaging scheduled at 6 to 12 months. This case underscores the need for detailed family history and targeted genetic testing when prenatal ultrasound detects structural anomalies. For fetuses with HNPP, individualized perinatal planning - including elective cesarean and long‑term neurological follow‑up - is feasible and recommended. The co‑occurrence of HNPP and CPAM appears coincidental, with no causal link established.

## Surgical outcomes of congenital cleft palate repair in dogs: a scoping review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:53
- 期刊: J Am Vet Med Assoc
- 通讯作者: Wolfs E
- DOI: 10.2460/javma.26.07.0551
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.2460/javma.26.07.0551) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42994099/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统梳理犬先天性腭裂手术修复的手术方式、结局、并发症及证据缺口。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入19项研究、122只犬，均为非前瞻性研究，手术方式包括von Langenbeck型修复、重叠瓣腭成形术等；口鼻瘘是最常见并发症，发生率为0%～100%，翻修手术率最高达50%。所有研究证据等级为III～V级，手术技术、结局定义和随访时间（0.25～127个月）异质性较大，尚无某一种手术技术显示出一致优越性。多种技术可获得可接受的功能结局，但术后并发症仍较常见。
- 临床启示：犬先天性腭裂修复应重视口鼻瘘等术后并发症的监测与管理，并结合个体情况选择合适术式。现有证据级别偏低且异质性大，临床决策需谨慎，未来需开展前瞻性研究并标准化结局报告。

**原文摘要**：Congenital cleft palate (CFP) is a common orofacial malformation in dogs associated with substantial morbidity and neonatal mortality. The objective of this scoping review was to map the current veterinary literature describing surgical approaches, reported outcomes, complications, and evidence gaps following surgical repair of canine CFPs. A scoping review was conducted and reported in accordance with the Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses extension for Scoping Reviews (ie, PRISMA-ScR) framework. PubMed, Web of Science, and Google Scholar were searched through January 21, 2026. Two reviewers independently screened records and charted outcomes. Eligible studies reported clinical data on surgical management of canine CFP, including oronasal fistula formation, revision surgery, or postoperative complications. "Experimental Design Grade" and "Evidence Grade" were used as descriptive critical appraisal tools. These grades were not considered formal risk-of-bias assessments. 19 studies involving 122 dogs met inclusion criteria. No prospective studies were identified. Surgical approaches included von Langenbeck-type repairs, overlapping flap palatoplasty, and other mucoperiosteal or adjunctive reconstructive techniques. Oronasal fistula formation was the most commonly reported complication, ranging from 0% to 100%. Revision surgery was reported in up to 50% of cases. All included sources were classified as Experimental Design Grade III-V, and substantial heterogeneity existed in surgical techniques, outcome definitions, and follow-up duration (0.25 to 127 months). No surgical technique demonstrated consistent superiority. Multiple surgical techniques can achieve acceptable functional outcomes in dogs with CFPs; however, postoperative complications, particularly oronasal fistula formation, remain common. The current literature is limited by predominantly lower-level study designs and heterogeneity, highlighting the need for prospective studies with standardized outcome reporting.

## Motor patterns and neuromuscular transmission in different colonic segments of patients with Hirschsprung's disease studied ex vivo.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:53
- 期刊: Eur J Pharmacol · 中科院医学3区(药学2区) · IF 5.7 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 西班牙 · Hospital de Valle Hebron
- 通讯作者: Jimenez M
- DOI: 10.1016/j.ejphar.2026.179528
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejphar.2026.179528) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42994550/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估先天性巨结肠症患儿无神经节、移行及有神经节结肠段的体外肌源性运动模式和神经肌肉传递功能。
- 核心发现/数据：对7例患儿手术获取的结肠肌条研究显示，所有节段均存在自发性肌源性慢相收缩；抑制性神经传递在有神经节段最强、移行区减弱、无神经节段基本缺失，但无神经节组织仍对一氧化氮供体及部分嘌呤能激动剂有反应。兴奋性胆碱能传递在有神经节段保留且可被阿托品阻断，移行区减弱，无神经节段对电刺激无功能性兴奋反应但对直接毒蕈碱激动剂有反应，乙酰胆碱酯酶抑制可增强收缩。
- 临床启示：无神经节段神经介导反应缺失，但肌肉及受体水平反应部分保留，提示一氧化氮供体或抗毒蕈碱药等可能作为改善巨结肠症结肠动力的药物靶点。

**原文摘要**：Hirschsprung's disease is a congenital disorder characterized by the absence of ganglion cells in the distal bowel, leading to functional obstruction. This study aimed to assess the myogenic motor patterns and neuromuscular transmission in ex vivo colonic segments-ganglionic, transitional, and aganglionic-obtained from pediatric patients with Hirschsprung's disease. Colonic segments from 7 patients were collected during surgery and colonic strips were studied in vitro. Myogenic activity, as well as inhibitory and excitatory neuromuscular responses, were evaluated using isometric force transducers and microelectrodes. All segments exhibited spontaneous myogenic slow phasic contractions. Inhibitory neuromuscular transmission, characterized by inhibitory junction potentials or muscle relaxation induced by electrical field stimulation (EFS)-, was robust in ganglionic segments, reduced in transitional zones, and largely absent in aganglionic segments. Despite this, aganglionic tissue responded to nitric oxide donors and partially to purinergic agonists, indicating the presence of post-junctional relaxation mechanisms. Excitatory transmission was preserved in ganglionic segments and mediated via cholinergic pathways, as confirmed by atropine sensitivity. The response was reduced in transitional zones, and aganglionic segments lacked functional excitatory responses to EFS but responded to direct muscarinic agonists. Acetylcholinesterase inhibition enhanced contractions, suggesting the presence of excitatory nerves. This study provides functional evidence correlating neuromuscular activity with histopathological findings in Hirschsprung's disease . While neural-mediated responses are absent in aganglionic zones, muscle and receptor-level responses are partially preserved, highlighting potential pharmacological targets, such as nitric oxide donors or antimuscarinic agents, for managing colonic motility in Hirschsprung's disease.
