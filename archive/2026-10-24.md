**原文摘要**：To analyze the factors associated with the occurrence of childbirth complications in Brazil. A cross-sectional study was conducted using data from the 2019 National Health Survey (Pesquisa Nacional de Saúde - PNS). Data from 2,875 women were analyzed. Chi-square tests and logistic regression were used to investigate the association between sociodemographic characteristics, childbirth care indicators, and the occurrence of childbirth complications (outcome). Clinical factors and characteristics of childbirth care were associated with the occurrence of childbirth complications in Brazil. Furthermore, women who experienced childbirth complications had higher odds of adverse neonatal outcomes.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：确定巴西萨尔瓦多先天白内障患儿首次手术年龄及诊断至治疗间隔，并量化治疗延迟、描述其临床流行病学特征。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入187例（274眼），以男性（60.4%）和双侧（54.5%）为主；首次手术中位年龄为4岁（IQR 2.0-6.0），无患儿在推荐时间窗内手术，仅7.0%（13例）在1岁前手术。诊断至治疗间隔合适者占48.1%、延迟者占51.9%；探索性校正模型中无变量与手术年龄独立相关（校正R²=0.029），即使绝对手术指征亚组中位年龄仍为4.0岁。
- 临床启示：手术明显晚于推荐时间窗，而院内间隔相对高效，提示延迟主要发生在转诊前；应加强新生儿筛查和基层早期识别，以缩短诊断至手术时间。

**原文摘要**：To determine the age at first congenital cataract surgery and the diagnosis-to-treatment interval at a specialized service in Salvador, Brazil, quantifying treatment delay and characterizing the clinical-epidemiological profile of the population. Retrospective, observational case series with an exploratory analytical component, including pediatric patients who underwent congenital cataract surgery through the Brazilian Unified Health System between 2015 and 2024. The diagnosis-to-treatment interval was adequate in 48.1% and delayed in 51.9%. In the exploratory adjusted model, no variable was independently associated with surgical age (adjusted R2=0.029). The median remained 4.0 years even in the absolute-surgical-indication subgroup, indicating that greater clinical severity did not translate into earlier surgery. Surgery occurred well beyond the recommended window. As the in-hospital interval was relatively efficient, the delay appears to lie upstream of referral, reinforcing the need to strengthen neonatal screening and early detection in primary care.

## Transfer timing is not independently associated with ECMO utilization in outborn congenital diaphragmatic hernia: a severity-adjusted analysis.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:31
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Children's Hospital of Michigan
- 通讯作者: Davis RT (davis3rt@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06671-5
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06671-5) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42911227/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估院外出生先天性膈疝（CDH）新生儿从出生至到达具备ECMO能力的三级中心的时间是否与ECMO使用独立相关。
- 核心发现/数据：46例患儿中21例（45.7%）需要ECMO，25例（54.3%）不需要；需要ECMO者CDHSG出生后评分更高、胸内肝疝更常见。调整严重度和解剖特征后，转运时间与ECMO使用无独立关联（aOR 0.79，95%CI 0.50-1.23），而胸内肝疝仍独立相关（aOR 8.65，95%CI 1.52-49.38），CDHSG评分未达统计学显著。
- 临床启示：在所观察的转运间期内，转运时长本身对预测ECMO使用的价值可能不如基础疾病特征，尤其胸内肝疝。临床不应仅依据转运时间判断ECMO需求。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate whether time from birth to arrival at a tertiary extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO)-capable center is independently associated with ECMO utilization among outborn neonates with congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH). We hypothesized that longer transfer intervals would be associated with greater ECMO utilization after adjustment for disease severity and anatomic characteristics. We performed a retrospective cohort study of outborn neonates with CDH transferred to a tertiary neonatal intensive care unit with ECMO capability. Transfer interval was analyzed using multivariable logistic regression adjusting for CDHSG postnatal score and anatomic characteristics. Among 46 neonates, 21 (45.7%) required ECMO and 25 (54.3%) did not. Infants requiring ECMO had greater CDHSG postnatal scores and more frequent intrathoracic liver herniation. After adjustment, transfer time was not independently associated with ECMO utilization (aOR 0.79, 95% CI 0.50-1.23). Intrathoracic liver herniation remained independently associated (aOR 8.65, 95% CI 1.52-49.38), whereas CDHSG score did not reach statistical significance. Within the transfer intervals observed, transfer time was not independently associated with ECMO utilization. These findings suggest that transport duration alone may be less informative than underlying disease characteristics in this cohort.

- 核心发现/数据：患儿体重3.5 kg，Rastelli A型平衡型cAVSD伴重度CAVVR和CoA，9日龄行主动脉弓修复+肺动脉环缩术，但因严重低血压无法脱离体外循环，TEE示CAVVR加重，调整/移除PAB无效；术中见瓣膜严重发育不良、不适合持久修复，ECMO支持心肌恢复后转为单心室路径，行共同房室瓣置换、Damus-Kaye-Stansel吻合及右心室-肺动脉分流，术后心室功能保留且无残余反流。

**原文摘要**：Balanced complete atrioventricular septal defect (cAVSD) with aortic coarctation (CoA) is generally considered amenable to staged biventricular repair (BVR). However, anatomic suitability does not necessarily guarantee functional stability. We report a 3.5-kg neonate with balanced cAVSD (Rastelli type A), severe common atrioventricular valve regurgitation (CAVVR), and CoA. Preoperative echocardiography indicated suitability for BVR. However, weaning the patient from cardiopulmonary bypass was impossible because of profound hypotension, and transesophageal echocardiography demonstrated worsening of CAVVR. PAB adjustment and removal were ineffective. Intraoperative inspection revealed a severely dysplastic valve morphology incompatible with durable repair. After myocardial recovery with extracorporeal membrane oxygenation support, the patient was converted to a univentricular pathway. The patient subsequently underwent common atrioventricular valve replacement, Damus-Kaye-Stansel anastomosis, and right ventricle-to-pulmonary artery shunt placement. Postoperative ventricular function was preserved without residual regurgitation. This case underscores the importance of flexible intraoperative decision-making guided by direct valve assessment and timely strategic conversion in high-risk neonates with cAVSD and severe CAVVR.

## Gut microbiota-derived indole-3-acetic acid ameliorates necrotizing enterocolitis-induced adolescent-stage cognitive impairment via regulating AHR-mediated neuroinflammation and synaptic structural integrity.

- 期刊: Int Immunopharmacol · 中科院医学2区(药学2区/免疫学3区) · IF 5.6 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 中国 · Children's Hospital of Chongqing Medical University
- 通讯作者: He Y (heyu@hospital.cqmu.edu.cn)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.intimp.2026.117561
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intimp.2026.117561) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42913200/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨肠道菌群来源的吲哚-3-乙酸（IAA）及其下游AHR信号对坏死性小肠结肠炎（NEC）诱导的神经炎症、突触损伤和青春期认知功能障碍的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：新生期NEC暴露导致青春期小鼠持续认知缺陷和焦虑样行为，海马IL-1β、IL-6、TNF-α升高而IL-22降低，疾病相关小胶质细胞扩增并高表达Trem2、Cd68，促进过度突触修剪及突触间隙增宽、PSD降低、树突棘和PSD-95减少。临床和动物模型均显示色氨酸代谢紊乱及循环IAA降低，且分别与全身炎症和认知表现相关；NEC小鼠产IAA菌（Lactobacillus johnsonii和Akkermansia muciniphila）耗竭，菌群耗竭加重损伤，而外源IAA通过激活AHR缓解神经炎症和突触损伤，CH223191抑制AHR则完全逆转保护作用。
- 临床启示：恢复IAA-AHR轴可作为预防早产儿NEC相关神经发育并发症的微生物-代谢物靶向策略。临床可关注产IAA菌、IAA水平及AHR信号，用于风险分层和早期干预。

**原文摘要**：Necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) is the most severe acute gastrointestinal disease in preterm infants and is an independent risk factor for adolescent-stage cognitive deficits, especially persistent cognitive impairment. Accumulating evidence indicates that gut-brain axis dysfunction mediates NEC-related brain injury. However, the precise metabolic mechanism linking gut microbiota to sustained cognitive deficits remains unclear. This study aimed to explore the effects of gut microbiota-derived indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) and its downstream AHR signaling on NEC-induced neuroinflammation, synaptic damage and cognitive dysfunction. A well-established neonatal mouse NEC model was used in this study. All mice received behavioral tests at postnatal week 4 to evaluate adolescent-stage cognitive. We applied 16S rRNA sequencing and targeted metabolomics to characterize gut microbial dysbiosis and altered tryptophan metabolism in clinical NEC patients and animal models. Hippocampal neuroinflammation was assessed by inflammatory cytokine ELISA and Iba-1 immunofluorescence staining. Single-nucleus RNA sequencing (snRNA-seq) was used to analyze microglial phenotypic changes. Synaptic structural integrity was verified by transmission electron microscopy (TEM), Golgi staining and PSD-95immunofluorescence staining. We further conducted microbiota depletion, exogenous IAA supplementation and pharmacological AHR inhibition with CH223191 to confirm the causal role of the IAA-AHR regulatory axis. Neonatal NEC exposure induced persistent cognitive deficits and anxiety-like behaviors in adolescent mice. The mouse hippocampus exhibited a severe pro-inflammatory microenvironment with increased IL-1β, IL-6 and TNF-α and decreased anti-inflammatory IL-22. snRNA-seq identified significant expansion of disease-associated microglia (DA_ Micro), which highly expressed phagocytic genes (Trem2, Cd68) and promoted excessive synaptic pruning. We further confirmed that NEC also induced obvious synaptic damage, including widened synaptic clefts, reduced postsynaptic density, and decreased dendritic spine and PSD-95 levels. Both clinical and animal results showed that disrupted tryptophan metabolism and reduced circulating IAA levels were correlated with systemic inflammation and cognitive performance, respectively. 16S rRNA sequencing confirmed the depletion of IAA-producing bacteria (Lactobacillus johnsonii and Akkermansia muciniphila) in NEC mice. Antibiotic-mediated microbiota depletion further aggravated neuroinflammation, synaptic loss and cognitive dysfunction. In contrast, exogenous IAA treatment relieved neuroinflammation and restored synaptic integrity by activating AHR, whereas AHR inhibition with CH223191 completely reversed these protective effects. NEC-induced gut dysbiosis reduces microbiota-derived IAA and inhibits protective hippocampal AHR signaling. This metabolic deficiency triggers sustained microglial overactivation, persistent neuroinflammation and synaptic damage, ultimately leading to adolescent-stage cognitive deficits. Restoring the IAA-AHR axis provides a novel microbiota-metabolite-targeted strategy to prevent NEC-related neurodevelopmental complications in preterm infants.

## AhR ligands in necrotizing enterocolitis: a provisional evidence map of ligand-specific biology and translational considerations.

**原文摘要**：Necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) is a major cause of morbidity and mortality in preterm infants. Early diagnosis remains challenging because clinical signs are nonspecific. We performed an ancillary analysis within the multicenter ClosNEC cohort of infants born before 32 weeks' gestation. Stool samples were collected at diagnosis in infants with confirmed NEC (Bell stage II or higher) and at comparable postnatal ages in preterm infants without NEC. **原文摘要**：Heme-oxygenase-1 (HO-1) is a key regulatory factor in organ development, organ function, cellular metabolism, heme processing, and bilirubin production. Dysregulation of HO-1 adversely affects placentation, yolk sac development, cardiovascular development, immune function, angiogenesis, microcirculation, alters the oxidoreductive balance, and is associated with adverse respiratory outcomes and necrotizing enterocolitis. The impact of HO-1 on neonatal neurodevelopment is unknown and is currently being investigated by our laboratory. In this review, we will discuss the morbidities associated with HO-1 dysregulation and importantly, we will navigate the unknown. We will highlight how infection or inflammation affect HO-1 homeostasis, its implications on neonatal morbidities at the limits of viability, summarize the relevant basic, translational, clinical and epidemiologic studies. We will conclude with proposing new approaches for the future, filling the gap between what is currently known and unknown. IMPACT: What does this article add to the existing literature? HO-1 pathway is relevant to pregnancy, fetus, neonatal, and later life outcomes. Provides comprehensive organ systems-based evaluation of Heme-oxygenase-1 in pregnancy, fetus, extreme preterm, and periviable neonates. Highlights how environmental factors, including infection, inflammation resulting in systemic dysregulation of HO-1 affects major neonatal morbidities. Underscores that novel investigations to close the knowledge gap are forthcoming to transform the HO-1 field.
