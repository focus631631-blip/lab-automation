## Early mitophagy defects and impaired mitochondrial energy metabolism drive target organ damage progression: Lessons from the Fabry heart.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:01
- 期刊: Cell Rep · 中科院生物学1区(细胞生物学2区) · IF 7.7 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 意大利 · Department of Clinical Medicine and Surgery
- 通讯作者: Iaccarino G (guiaccar@unina.it)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.celrep.2026.118183
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2026.118183) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42945215/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨Fabry病心脏中线粒体自噬缺陷和线粒体能量代谢障碍是否驱动靶器官损害进展，并评估其治疗靶向性。
- 核心发现/数据：在人源化FD小鼠模型（R301Q-Tg/GLA敲除）、胚胎成纤维细胞及新生和成年心肌细胞中，发现线粒体自噬和线粒体呼吸受损、活性氧增加，并与溶酶体功能障碍形成恶性循环。上述缺陷随年龄进展，导致功能失调线粒体堆积、能量衰竭、凋亡、心肌肥厚和舒张功能障碍。L-精氨酸通过促进NO/PGC-1α依赖性线粒体修复，可预防心脏异常。
- 临床启示：线粒体功能障碍是Fabry病心肌病的关键驱动因素，溶酶体-线粒体轴是可干预的治疗靶点。L-精氨酸等线粒体救援策略可能用于Fabry病心肌病的早期防治。

**原文摘要**：Increasing evidence supports a pathogenic role for impaired energy metabolism in organ damage. Genetic diseases provide opportunities to investigate these mechanisms, as exemplified by Fabry disease (FD), caused by an α-galactosidase A (GLA) deficiency and globotriaosylceramide (Gb3) accumulation, with cardiomyopathy as the leading cause of death. Beyond Gb3 storage, impaired energy metabolism may contribute to cardiomyopathy. Here, we demonstrate mitochondrial dysfunction in FD heart and its therapeutic targeting. Using a humanized FD mouse model (R301Q-Tg/GLA knockout), embryonic fibroblasts, and neonatal and adult cardiomyocytes, we found impaired mitophagy and mitochondrial respiration, increased reactive oxygen species, and a vicious cycle aggravating lysosomal dysfunction. These defects progressed with age, causing accumulation of dysfunctional mitochondria, energetic failure, apoptosis, cardiac hypertrophy, and diastolic dysfunction. L-arginine, promoting NO/PGC-1α-dependent mitochondrial rescue, prevented cardiac abnormalities. Our findings identify mitochondrial dysfunction as a key driver of FD cardiomyopathy and reveal the lysosome-mitochondria axis as a targetable pathway in FD and beyond.

## Intrinsic epithelial defects and NF-κB-dependent signaling heighten viral injury in biliary atresia.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:01
- 期刊: JCI Insight · 中科院医学1区(医学：研究与实验1区) · IF 6.8 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center and Children's Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Bezerra JA
- DOI: 10.1172/jci.insight.205019
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1172/jci.insight.205019) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42944767/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨胆道闭锁（BA）胆管上皮内在缺陷及NF-κB依赖信号在病毒性损伤易感性中的作用。
- 核心发现/数据：与正常供体胆管类器官相比，BA诊断期和移植期类器官存在紧密连接结构和屏障功能基线缺陷，RRV感染后上皮破坏和凋亡更明显。转录组显示诊断期BA以干扰素相关抗病毒反应为主，而移植期BA基线即富集NF-κB（RELA/NFKB1）调控网络并被感染放大；GYY4137抑制NF-κB信号可恢复类器官生长、改善紧密连接、降低通透性并减轻RRV诱导的凋亡。小鼠BA模型中GYY4137改善疾病严重度、保护胆管结构和上皮连接完整性，并减少胆管细胞凋亡。
- 临床启示：胆管细胞NF-κB优势程序可能是BA病毒损伤易感性的关键决定因素，靶向胆管细胞NF-κB信号有望成为抑制BA疾病进展的潜在治疗策略。

**原文摘要**：Targeted injury to the biliary epithelium and impaired bile flow are central mechanisms underlying childhood cholangiopathies. In biliary atresia (BA), immune-mediated cholangiocyte injury drives progressive bile duct obstruction, yet epithelial-intrinsic programs regulating injury susceptibility remain incompletely defined. Here, we modeled virus-epithelium interactions using rhesus rotavirus (RRV) infection of human liver-derived cholangiocyte organoids from normal donors (NCO) and infants with BA at diagnosis (BACO-Dx) or transplantation (BACO-Tx). Compared with NCO, BA organoids displayed baseline defects in tight junction architecture and barrier function, with prominent epithelial disruption and apoptosis following RRV exposure. Transcriptomic profiling revealed that while NCO and BACO-Dx mounted predominantly interferon-associated antiviral responses, BACO-Tx exhibited an expanded inflammatory program characterized by enrichment of NF-κB (RELA/NFKB1) regulatory network at baseline and amplified by infection. Inhibition of NF-κB signaling with the hydrogen sulfide donor GYY4137 restored organoid growth, improved tight junction organization, reduced permeability, and attenuated RRV-induced apoptosis. In the murine BA model, GYY4137 improved disease severity, preserved bile duct architecture, and protected epithelial junctional integrity while limiting cholangiocyte apoptosis. These findings identify an epithelial NF-κB-predominant program as a key determinant of intrinsic susceptibility to viral injury in BA and support targeting cholangiocyte NF-κB signaling to suppress disease progression.

## Trauma Center Drug Screening Practices and Equity in Screening for Injured Adolescents.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 10:22
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2

- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163558
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163558) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42946156/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估医院层面生化药物筛查频率与受伤青少年按性别、种族、族裔和保险状态存在的筛查差异之间的关联。
- 核心发现/数据：纳入2017—2022年TQIP数据库中237,802名12—17岁受伤青少年和474个创伤中心，医院层面筛查频率中位数为23.6%（IQR=11.5%—40.8%）；低筛查中心较高筛查中心更多为ACS认证儿童创伤中心（36.1% vs 24.4%，p=0.002）。在高筛查中心，美洲印第安（AOR=0.29）、西班牙裔（AOR=0.73）、太平洋岛民（AOR=0.71）和医疗补助参保青少年（AOR=0.76）的筛查差异缩小，但女性青少年的筛查差异扩大（AOR=1.19，p=0.009）。
- 临床启示：提高儿科创伤中心药物筛查率并推行普遍筛查方案，可能缩小部分人群的筛查差异、增加物质使用障碍干预机会并促进公平筛查，同时需关注女性青少年筛查差异可能扩大的问题。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate the association between hospital-level screening frequency and disparities in screening by sex, race, ethnicity, and insurance. It is unknown how institutional screening practices contribute to known disparities in biochemical drug screening at pediatric trauma centers. We included injured adolescents (12-17 years) in the 2017-2022 Trauma Quality Improvement Program dataset. We assessed how hospital-level drug screening frequency (proportion of adolescents biochemically drug screened by trauma center) was associated with disparities in screening (increased likelihood of screening for American Indian, Black, Hispanic, female, Medicaid-insured, and uninsured adolescents). We compared high-screening (fourth-quartile) versus low-screening (first-quartile) centers. We used multivariable logistic regression with interaction terms between screening frequency and sociodemographic characteristics to evaluate for disparities in screening. For 237,802 adolescents at 474 trauma centers, median hospital-level drug screening frequency was 23.6% (IQR=11.5%-40.8%). Compared to high-screening centers, low-screening centers were more often ACS-verified pediatric trauma centers (36.1% vs 24.4%; p=0.002). Among groups with disparately greater odds of undergoing biochemical drug screening, treatment at trauma centers with higher hospital-level screening frequencies was associated with narrowed disparities in screening for American Indian adolescents (AOR=0.29; 95%CI=0.1-0.6; p<0.001), Hispanic adolescents (AOR=0.73; 95%CI=0.6-0.9; p<0.001), Pacific Islander adolescents (AOR=0.71; 95%CI=0.51-1.00) and Medicaid-insured adolescents (AOR 0.76; 95%CI=0.7-0.9; p<0.001) and more disparate screening for female adolescents (AOR=1.19; 95%CI=1.0-1.4; p=0.009). Increasing drug screening rates at pediatric trauma centers may narrow some disparities in screening. Universal screening protocols may increase opportunities to intervene on substance use disorders and encourage equitable screening for injured adolescents.

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过数值方法为矢状缝早闭新生儿制定术前规划，确定牵引弹簧的力量、放置位置及颅骨切开部位，并评估其对头形的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：基于患者特异性有限元模拟、术后3D光学表面扫描和临床验证，研究在1例4月龄患儿中观察到头颅外形显著改变、颅顶外扩，以及头颅指数和整体颅骨几何形态的可测量改善。所提出的替代弹簧分配方案在数值上显示可降低应力并改善塑形，但本研究为单例概念验证，尚需更大规模临床试验验证。

**原文摘要**：This study presents the application of numerical methods, for preoperative planning and determining the force and placement of distraction springs, as well as the cranial incision sites, in a newborn with a congenital disability involving cranial suture fusion. This study employs a multifaceted methodology based on the work of an interdisciplinary team. Based on this single-patient numerical case study, it is hypothesized that the design of craniosynostosis treatment planning can be configured to introduce personalized force into critical areas. The proposed alternative spring allocation suggests potential biomechanical advantages, including stress reduction and improved shape optimization. However, these findings serve as a proof-of-concept and require validation in larger clinical trials. Achieving a clinical effect that is sustained over time by reducing stress values can also be significant, because the bone remodeling process can be designed so that bones multiply along the direction of principal stresses, thereby allowing for the optimization of the skull shape envisioned by the neurosurgeon.

## Reconstructive management of an extensive myelomeningocele defect using allogeneic fascia lata as temporary biological coverage: a case-based mini-review.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:22
- 期刊: Childs Nerv Syst
- 国家/单位: Buenos Aires · Hospital de Pediatría J.P. Garrahan
- 通讯作者: Medina JA (juanmed2090@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00381-026-07507-2
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00381-026-07507-2) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42946782/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨同种异体阔筋膜作为临时生物覆盖物，用于广泛脊髓脊膜膨出缺损重建的可行性。
- 核心发现/数据：报告1例足月男婴（3.5 kg）伴16×10 cm胸腰骶部脊髓脊膜膨出，因周围皮肤不足无法实现无张力一期闭合；在神经修复及水密硬膜闭合后，采用组织库同种异体阔筋膜临时覆盖。随访见肉芽组织形成及皮肤边缘向心性上皮化，4个月达完全上皮覆盖，1年时重建区稳定、无局部感染且无需额外重建手术。该技术仅为单病例经验，尚不能确定其比较疗效、安全性或可重复性。
- 临床启示：当常规重建方案不可行时，库存同种异体阔筋膜可作为广泛脊髓脊膜膨出缺损的挽救性临时生物覆盖选择；其潜在优势包括机械强度、避免供区并发症及机构组织库即时可用，但仍需更多研究验证。

**原文摘要**：Extensive myelomeningocele defects may be difficult to reconstruct when tension-free primary skin closure cannot be achieved, and an appropriate local flap is not available. In such cases, various biological materials and dermal substitutes have been used. Allogeneic fascia lata is a mechanically robust tissue with established use in reconstructive surgery, but its use as temporary superficial biological coverage for extensive myelomeningocele defects has not been specifically described. We report a term male newborn weighing 3.5 kg with a 16 × 10 cm thoracolumbosacral myelomeningocele and insufficient surrounding skin to achieve tension-free primary closure. After neural repair and watertight dural closure, the defect was covered with allogeneic fascia lata obtained from the Tissue Bank of Hospital de Pediatría "Prof. Dr. Juan P. Garrahan" and used as temporary biological coverage. During follow-up, progressive granulation tissue formation and centripetal epithelialization from the skin margins were observed, with complete epithelial coverage achieved at four months. At one-year follow-up, the reconstructed area remained stable, with no local infection or need for additional reconstructive procedures. Banked allogeneic fascia lata may be a salvage option for temporary biological coverage of extensive myelomeningocele defects when conventional reconstructive options are not feasible. Potential advantages include mechanical strength, avoidance of donor-site morbidity, and immediate availability in centers with institutional tissue banks, with potential logistical and economic benefits compared with commercially manufactured substitutes. These findings are based on a single case and do not establish comparative efficacy, safety, or reproducibility of the technique.

## Days of unblended oxygen as a predictor of severe retinopathy of prematurity in Uganda.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:22
- 期刊: J AAPOS
- 国家/单位: Uganda · King Ceasor University
- 通讯作者: Rodriguez SH (srodriguez5@bsd.uchicago.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jaapos.2026.105326
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jaapos.2026.105326) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42946154/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：在氧气管理设备有限的乌干达新生儿病房中，探讨未混合补充氧天数（DUSO）是否与严重早产儿视网膜病变（sROP）风险增加相关。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入331名胎龄<37周的早产儿，其中12例发生sROP。未调整Firth模型中，每增加1天DUSO与sROP风险增加18%相关（OR=1.18；95%CI 1.09-1.27；P<0.001）；调整出生体重后风险增加13%（aOR=1.13；95%CI 1.02-1.25；P=0.017）。
- 临床启示：在资源有限环境中，监测并限制未混合氧暴露可能有助于降低sROP风险；仍需进一步研究以指导筛查和氧管理方案。

**原文摘要**：In high-income countries, a higher number of days of supplemental oxygen has been associated with higher risk for type 1 retinopathy of prematurity (ROP). However, this association was studied in settings where oxygen saturation targeting is supported by pulse oximetry and oxygen blenders. The purpose of this study was to investigate whether a higher number of days of unblended supplemental oxygen (DUSO) was also associated with increased risk of severe ROP in Ugandan neonatal units where oxygen management equipment was limited. This analysis was part of a larger cross sectional prevalence study of preterm infants born at <37 weeks' gestational age at two major referral hospitals in Kampala between August and October 2022. We evaluated the association between DUSO and severe ROP (sROP), defined as aggressive ROP, any stage with plus disease, stage 4 or stage 5 ROP. Given the small number of infants with sROP, Firth penalized logistic regression was used to reduce small-sample bias. A total of 331 infants were included. Of these, 12 developed sROP. Each additional DUSO was associated with 18% higher odds of sROP in the unadjusted Firth model (OR = 1.18; 95% CI, 1.09-1.27; P < 0.001) and 13% higher odds after adjustment for birth weight (adjusted OR 1.13; 95% CI, 1.02-1.25; P = 0.017). Our findings support existing evidence that monitoring and limiting unblended oxygen exposure may help reduce sROP in resource-limited settings. Further research is warranted to guide screening and oxygen management protocols.

## Intranasal insulin improves long-term cognitive deficits and protects hippocampal damage following neonatal hypoxia-ischemia in juvenile rats.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 10:22
- 期刊: Neuroscience · 中科院医学4区(神经科学4区) · IF 3.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University of Mississippi Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Bhatt AJ (abhatt@umc.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.neuroscience.2026.10.039
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroscience.2026.10.039) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42946117/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨鼻内胰岛素能否对新生大鼠缺氧缺血（HI）所致海马损伤和认知障碍提供长期神经保护。
- 核心发现/数据：在P10大鼠右侧颈总动脉结扎后给予90分钟8%氧缺氧，并即刻及24、48小时鼻内给予25 μg胰岛素，可显著减轻急性ATP耗竭、降低IL-1β和皮质酮水平并改善生长。行为学上，鼻内胰岛素改善雌雄幼鼠（P21–P25）的感觉运动缺陷、工作记忆和学习缺陷，并完全逆转雄性幼鼠的长期记忆缺陷。组织学上，胰岛素减轻HI诱导的海马神经元丢失、CA3成熟NeuN+神经元减少、齿状回神经发生抑制及小胶质细胞激活和脑炎症，两性均获益。
- 临床启示：鼻内胰岛素是一种非侵入性干预，对新生儿HI具有长期神经保护潜力，支持进一步研究其用于新生儿缺氧缺血性脑病（HIE）的临床转化价值。

**原文摘要**：Hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy remains a significant cause of neonatal morbidity and mortality in neonates. Our study tested whether intranasal insulin provides long-term neuroprotection against neonatal hypoxia-ischemia (HI)-induced hippocampal damage and cognitive disturbances in juvenile rats. Postnatal day 10 (P10) Sprague-Dawley rats underwent right common carotid artery ligation or sham surgery followed by 90 min of hypoxia (8% oxygen) or room air, respectively. Immediately after, insulin (25 μg) or vehicle was administered in each naris, followed by repeated doses 24 and 48 h post-insult. HI caused acute cerebral ATP depletion, an acute inflammatory response characterized by increased IL-1β, and corticosterone elevation. Intranasal insulin significantly attenuated ATP loss, reduced interleukin-1β and corticosterone levels, and improved somatic growth. Behaviorally, intranasal insulin protected against neonatal HI-induced sensorimotor deficits, working memory impairments, and learning deficits in both sexes of juvenile rats (P21-P25). Male pups exhibited selective long-term memory deficits following HI, which were fully rescued by intranasal insulin. Histologically, neonatal HI induced hippocampal neuronal loss and increased Fluoro-Jade C + degenerating cells, together with loss of mature NeuN + neurons in hippocampal CA3, and suppression of dentate gyrus neurogenesis, with greater loss of immature DCX + neurons in P25 male rats. HI insult also resulted in brain inflammation, as indicated by microglia activation (Iba1 + ) and serum interleukin-1β elevation. Intranasal insulin significantly reduced HI-induced brain inflammation and protected against hippocampal neuronal injury in both sexes. These findings demonstrate long-lasting neuroprotective effects of intranasal insulin following neonatal HI and support further investigation of this non-invasive approach for hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy in neonates.

**原文摘要**：Current trends in reconstructive surgery increasingly advocate delaying vaginal reconstruction until patients can participate in decision-making. While this approach may be appropriate for conditions such as Mayer-Rokitansky-Küster-Hauser syndrome and isolated vaginal agenesis, its application to patients with complex anorectal malformations (ARM), requires careful consideration. This narrative review discusses the anatomical, surgical, and psychosocial factors influencing the timing of vaginal reconstruction in patients with complex anorectal malformations. Drawing on contemporary surgical principles and extensive clinical experience with reconstruction, differences between ARM and other causes of vaginal atresia are examined, together with reconstructive options and emerging strategies to preserve future reproductive potential. Patients with complex ARM differ substantially from patients with isolated vaginal agenesis. They frequently present with associated urinary tract anomalies, neurogenic bladder, fecal incontinence as well as previous abdominal and perineal operations. In selected patients, vaginal reconstruction performed during the initial repair offers important technical advantages, including optimal pelvic exposure before fibrosis develops, preservation of surgical options, and avoidance of later operations in a hostile pelvis. The choice of reconstructive tissue should be individualized according to anatomy and anticipated bowel function. Native vaginal tissue remains the preferred option whenever available; however, when replacement is necessary, the rectum provides the most favorable intestinal substitute in appropriately selected patients, followed by colon, whereas small bowel should generally be reserved for situations in which other options are unavailable. Delayed vaginal reconstruction should not be considered the universal standard for patients with anorectal malformations. Surgical timing should be individualized according to anatomical complexity, predicted bowel function, future urological procedures, patient circumstances, and long-term quality-of-life considerations. Management within specialized multidisciplinary centers remains essential for optimizing both functional and reproductive outcomes.

**原文摘要**：Congenital biliary dilatation (formerly referred to as choledochal cyst) is a congenital anomaly characterized by cystic dilatation of the bile ducts. Historically, an artificial intestinal valve (AIV) was sometimes added to Roux-en-Y reconstruction to prevent postoperative reflux cholangitis, but its clinical significance remains controversial. We report the case of an 18-year-old man who underwent excision of congenital biliary dilatation and Roux-en-Y hepaticojejunostomy with an AIV during infancy. In this case, the AIV became a critical obstacle to endoscopic access approximately 20 years postoperatively. With advancements in double-balloon enteroscopy-assisted endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography, securing future endoscopic access is increasingly important. An AIV can hinder endoscopic biliary access after congenital biliary dilatation surgery, and our institution has since discontinued its use.
