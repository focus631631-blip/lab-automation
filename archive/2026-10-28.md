## Early mitophagy defects and impaired mitochondrial energy metabolism drive target organ damage progression: Lessons from the Fabry heart.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:01
- 期刊: Cell Rep · 中科院生物学1区(细胞生物学2区) · IF 7.7 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 意大利 · Department of Clinical Medicine and Surgery
- 通讯作者: Iaccarino G (guiaccar@unina.it)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.celrep.2026.118183
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2026.118183) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42945215/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨Fabry病心脏中线粒体自噬缺陷和线粒体能量代谢障碍是否驱动靶器官损害进展，并评估其治疗靶向性。
- 核心发现/数据：在人源化FD小鼠模型（R301Q-Tg/GLA敲除）、胚胎成纤维细胞及新生和成年心肌细胞中，发现线粒体自噬和线粒体呼吸受损、活性氧增加，并与溶酶体功能障碍形成恶性循环。上述缺陷随年龄进展，导致功能失调线粒体堆积、能量衰竭、凋亡、心肌肥厚和舒张功能障碍。L-精氨酸通过促进NO/PGC-1α依赖性线粒体修复，可预防心脏异常。
- 临床启示：线粒体功能障碍是Fabry病心肌病的关键驱动因素，溶酶体-线粒体轴是可干预的治疗靶点。L-精氨酸等线粒体救援策略可能用于Fabry病心肌病的早期防治。

**原文摘要**：Increasing evidence supports a pathogenic role for impaired energy metabolism in organ damage. Genetic diseases provide opportunities to investigate these mechanisms, as exemplified by Fabry disease (FD), caused by an α-galactosidase A (GLA) deficiency and globotriaosylceramide (Gb3) accumulation, with cardiomyopathy as the leading cause of death. Beyond Gb3 storage, impaired energy metabolism may contribute to cardiomyopathy. Here, we demonstrate mitochondrial dysfunction in FD heart and its therapeutic targeting. Using a humanized FD mouse model (R301Q-Tg/GLA knockout), embryonic fibroblasts, and neonatal and adult cardiomyocytes, we found impaired mitophagy and mitochondrial respiration, increased reactive oxygen species, and a vicious cycle aggravating lysosomal dysfunction. These defects progressed with age, causing accumulation of dysfunctional mitochondria, energetic failure, apoptosis, cardiac hypertrophy, and diastolic dysfunction. L-arginine, promoting NO/PGC-1α-dependent mitochondrial rescue, prevented cardiac abnormalities. Our findings identify mitochondrial dysfunction as a key driver of FD cardiomyopathy and reveal the lysosome-mitochondria axis as a targetable pathway in FD and beyond.

## Intrinsic epithelial defects and NF-κB-dependent signaling heighten viral injury in biliary atresia.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 03:01
- 期刊: JCI Insight · 中科院医学1区(医学：研究与实验1区) · IF 6.8 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center and Children's Medical Center
- 通讯作者: Bezerra JA
- DOI: 10.1172/jci.insight.205019
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1172/jci.insight.205019) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42944767/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探讨胆道闭锁（BA）胆管上皮内在缺陷及NF-κB依赖信号在病毒性损伤易感性中的作用。
- 核心发现/数据：与正常供体胆管类器官相比，BA诊断期和移植期类器官存在紧密连接结构和屏障功能基线缺陷，RRV感染后上皮破坏和凋亡更明显。转录组显示诊断期BA以干扰素相关抗病毒反应为主，而移植期BA基线即富集NF-κB（RELA/NFKB1）调控网络并被感染放大；GYY4137抑制NF-κB信号可恢复类器官生长、改善紧密连接、降低通透性并减轻RRV诱导的凋亡。小鼠BA模型中GYY4137改善疾病严重度、保护胆管结构和上皮连接完整性，并减少胆管细胞凋亡。
- 临床启示：胆管细胞NF-κB优势程序可能是BA病毒损伤易感性的关键决定因素，靶向胆管细胞NF-κB信号有望成为抑制BA疾病进展的潜在治疗策略。

**原文摘要**：Targeted injury to the biliary epithelium and impaired bile flow are central mechanisms underlying childhood cholangiopathies. In biliary atresia (BA), immune-mediated cholangiocyte injury drives progressive bile duct obstruction, yet epithelial-intrinsic programs regulating injury susceptibility remain incompletely defined. Here, we modeled virus-epithelium interactions using rhesus rotavirus (RRV) infection of human liver-derived cholangiocyte organoids from normal donors (NCO) and infants with BA at diagnosis (BACO-Dx) or transplantation (BACO-Tx). Compared with NCO, BA organoids displayed baseline defects in tight junction architecture and barrier function, with prominent epithelial disruption and apoptosis following RRV exposure. Transcriptomic profiling revealed that while NCO and BACO-Dx mounted predominantly interferon-associated antiviral responses, BACO-Tx exhibited an expanded inflammatory program characterized by enrichment of NF-κB (RELA/NFKB1) regulatory network at baseline and amplified by infection. Inhibition of NF-κB signaling with the hydrogen sulfide donor GYY4137 restored organoid growth, improved tight junction organization, reduced permeability, and attenuated RRV-induced apoptosis. In the murine BA model, GYY4137 improved disease severity, preserved bile duct architecture, and protected epithelial junctional integrity while limiting cholangiocyte apoptosis. These findings identify an epithelial NF-κB-predominant program as a key determinant of intrinsic susceptibility to viral injury in BA and support targeting cholangiocyte NF-κB signaling to suppress disease progression.
