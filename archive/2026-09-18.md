## The long-term follow-up outcomes and transition rates across seven representative pediatric congenital surgical conditions: disease severity drives follow-up retention.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:10
- 期刊: Pediatr Surg Int · 中科院医学3区(儿科3区/外科3区) · IF 1.8 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 日本 · Research Field in Medical and Health Sciences
- 通讯作者: Harumatsu T (t-haru@m.kufm.kagoshima-u.ac.jp)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00383-026-06640-y
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00383-026-06640-y) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42752690/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估七种代表性小儿先天性外科疾病的长期随访结局及儿科向成人医疗过渡率。
- 核心发现/数据：单中心337例患者中，43%（146例）失访，仅3%（10例）完成正式过渡；高/中间型肛门直肠畸形（61%）、先天性巨结肠（55%）和低位肛门直肠畸形（54%）失访率最高，末次随访中位年龄分别为15.0、5.5和7.6岁。胆道闭锁（21%）和泄殖腔畸形（0%）失访率较低，提示疾病严重程度驱动随访保留；一例泄殖腔畸形患者失访后35岁死于尿路感染，且术后结局良好者的随访流失反而最高。
- 临床启示：应优先为肛门直肠畸形和先天性巨结肠等高风险人群建立系统化儿科-成人过渡计划，同时不能放松胆道闭锁、泄殖腔畸形等重症患者的终身随访和远期并发症管理。

**原文摘要**：Patients with congenital surgical conditions require lifelong follow-up. However, the transition from pediatric to adult care remains poorly established. This study aimed to evaluate the long-term follow-up outcomes and transition rates of seven pediatric congenital surgical conditions. This single-institution retrospective study included 337 patients born before 2008 who underwent surgery for esophageal atresia/tracheoesophageal fistula (TEF-EA, n = 30), choledochal cyst (CC, n = 53), biliary atresia (BA, n = 62), Hirschsprung's disease (HD, n = 60), high/intermediate anorectal malformation (ARM, n = 56), low ARM (n = 69), and cloacal malformation (Cloaca, n = 7). Follow-up outcomes and transition status were assessed. Overall, 146 patients (43%) were lost to the follow-up, and only 10 (3%) achieved formal transition. The lost to follow-up rates were highest in High/Int ARM (61%), HD (55%), and Low ARM (54%), with median last visit ages of 15.0, 5.5, and 7.6 years. Conversely, BA (21%) and cloacal (0%) anomalies demonstrated low attrition despite early surgical ages, suggesting that disease severity drives follow-up retention. One patient with Cloaca who disengaged from follow-up died from a urinary tract infection at 35 years old. Follow-up attrition was paradoxically the highest among patients with favorable postoperative outcomes. Targeted transition programs focusing on ARM and HD populations are urgently needed.

## Direct Innominate Artery Cannulation for Antegrade Cerebral Perfusion Across the Spectrum of Congenital Aortic Arch Reconstruction.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:10
- 期刊: World J Pediatr Congenit Heart Surg · 中科院医学4区(心脏和心血管系统4区) · IF 0.9
- 国家/单位: 墨西哥 · Instituto Nacional de Pediatría
- 通讯作者: Palacios-Macedo Quenot A
- DOI: 10.1177/21501351261477057
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1177/21501351261477057) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42752264/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估直接无名动脉插管在先天性主动脉弓重建中建立顺行性脑灌注（ACP）的安全性、可行性与可重复性。
- 核心发现/数据：25例患儿接受该策略，年龄6–971天、体重2.0–12.7 kg；平均体外循环时间153分钟、主动脉阻断时间67分钟、ACP时间24分钟。无插管相关血管损伤或转流方式转换，无结构性神经损伤，仅1例短暂神经症状且无后遗症；2例住院死亡与插管技术无关。该策略在多种复杂先天性主动脉弓病变中均可一致应用。
- 临床启示：直接无名动脉插管可作为先天性主动脉弓重建中ACP的安全、简便且可重复的插管选择。其稳定的围术期结果支持在广泛弓畸形谱系中推广应用。

**原文摘要**：BackgroundAntegrade cerebral perfusion (ACP) is widely used during congenital aortic arch reconstruction; however, variability persists regarding cannulation strategies. Direct innominate artery cannulation may simplify arterial access while maintaining reliable cerebral perfusion, but reports describing its systematic use remain limited.MethodsA retrospective single-center study was performed including consecutive patients undergoing congenital aortic arch reconstruction with ACP established through direct innominate artery cannulation between December 2022 and February 2026. All procedures were performed using the same cannulation strategy by the same attending congenital cardiac surgeon. Demographic, operative, perfusion, and postoperative variables were analyzed.ResultsTwenty-five patients underwent aortic arch reconstruction using direct innominate artery cannulation for ACP. Age ranged from 6 to 971 days and weight from 2.0 to 12.7 kg. Diagnoses included coarctation complex with transverse arch hypoplasia, interrupted aortic arch, Taussig-Bing anomaly, transposition of the great arteries, recoarctation with arch hypoplasia, complete atrioventricular septal defect, and borderline left ventricle. Associated procedures included ventricular septal defect closure and arterial switch operation. Mean cardiopulmonary bypass time was 153 min, mean cross-clamp time was 67 min, and mean ACP duration was 24 min. No cannulation-related vascular injuries or conversions occurred. No structural neurologic injury was identified. One patient developed transient neurologic symptoms without sequelae. Two hospital deaths occurred, neither attributable to the cannulation technique.ConclusionsDirect innominate artery cannulation represents a safe, reproducible, and technically straightforward strategy for ACP during congenital aortic arch reconstruction. Consistent use without major modification supports its reliability across a heterogeneous spectrum of congenital arch procedures.

## Albumin uptake through FcRn promotes tumor-associated macrophage maturation and albumin-bound immunotherapy response.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:10
- 期刊: Cell Rep · 中科院生物学1区(细胞生物学2区) · IF 7.7 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 美国 · Center for Systems Biology
- 通讯作者: Miller MA (miles.miller@mgh.harvard.edu)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.celrep.2026.118010
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2026.118010) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42752090/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：探究血清白蛋白在肿瘤相关巨噬细胞（TAM）中的摄取机制及其对TAM成熟和以白蛋白为载体的免疫治疗响应的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：TAM是小鼠肿瘤中主要的非恶性白蛋白累积细胞且单细胞摄取最高；髓系FcRn介导巨噬细胞白蛋白摄取和单核细胞向TAM成熟，FcRn缺失使瘤内TAM/单核细胞比降低约3倍，白蛋白缺乏部分模拟该缺陷，回顾性临床数据也显示低白蛋白与TAM减少相关。机制上白蛋白通过p62-Nrf2信号促进巨噬细胞成熟，白蛋白偶联可延长STING激动剂循环并增强抗肿瘤疗效，而清除巨噬细胞或宿主FcRn缺陷则削弱该疗效。
- 临床启示：该研究表明白蛋白-FcRn-p62-Nrf2轴是TAM成熟的调节通路，可作为髓系靶向免疫治疗的新靶点；白蛋白偶联STING激动剂策略及患者白蛋白水平/TAM状态可能指导肿瘤免疫治疗。

**原文摘要**：Serum albumin accumulates in tumors and is widely exploited for drug delivery, but its accumulation and function in nonmalignant cells are less understood. Here, we show that tumor-associated macrophages (TAMs) are the dominant nonmalignant albumin-accumulating population in mouse tumors and exhibit the highest per-cell uptake. Bone marrow chimeras and adoptive transfer established a myeloid-intrinsic role for the neonatal Fc receptor (FcRn) in macrophage albumin uptake and monocyte-to-TAM maturation. FcRn loss reduced the intratumoral TAM-to-monocyte ratio approximately 3-fold. Albumin deficiency partially phenocopied this defect in mice while retrospective clinical data linked lower albumin to fewer TAMs. Mechanistically, albumin engaged p62-Nrf2 signaling, and Nrf2 inhibition impaired albumin-driven macrophage maturation. Leveraging this, albumin conjugation prolonged STING-agonist circulation and improved antitumor efficacy versus free agonist in syngeneic tumors, with diminished activity after macrophage depletion or host FcRn deficiency. These findings identify albumin as an endogenous regulator of TAM maturation and provide a mechanistic basis for myeloid-directed immunotherapies.

## International consensus statements and clinical support tool on the timing and considerations of vaginal replacement in patients with anorectal malformations, vaginal agenesis, and identifiable Müllerian structures.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 08:47
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 西班牙 · University Hospital La Paz
- 通讯作者: Stenström P (pernilla.stenstrom@med.lu.se)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163481
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163481) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42754007/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：通过国际多学科共识，明确肛门直肠畸形、阴道发育不全且可识别苗勒管结构患者行阴道替代的时机、随访及以患者为中心的照护建议。
- 核心发现/数据：27项研究仅提供低级别证据，多数报告支持早期阴道替代；71名全球多学科专家调查显示对延迟重建的接受度增加，并强调与父母共同决策。面对面会议中22名专家和患者未就最佳时机达成共识，但就全面咨询与多学科照护、结构化随访（含影像学和性心理支持）、患者及父母教育以支持自我管理达成共识。
- 临床启示：目前无法推荐统一的早期或延迟阴道替代时机，临床应个体化决策、加强共享决策，并提供多学科长期随访与性心理支持；未来需更多长期结局研究指导实践。

**原文摘要**：Traditionally, vaginal replacement in patients with anorectal malformations (ARM) has been performed concomitantly with anorectal reconstruction during childhood when the native vagina does not reach the perineum. Concerns regarding long-term outcomes have led to increasing debate about whether reconstruction should instead be deferred until adolescence or adulthood. This consensus report aimed to establish best-practice recommendations regarding the timing of vaginal replacement, follow-up, and patient-centered aspects of care in patients with ARM, vaginal agenesis, and identifiable Müllerian structures for whom vaginal replacement is required. An international multidisciplinary panel used the Nominal Group Technique to gather evidence from a literature review, digital expert case discussions, a global survey, and an in-person meeting involving 22 specialists and patients. Evidence from 27 studies revealed only low-level evidence studies. Most reports favored early vaginal replacement. Responses from 71 multidisciplinary experts worldwide indicated growing acceptance of delayed reconstruction and highlighted the importance of shared decision-making with the parents. In the in-person meeting no consensus on the optimal timing of vaginal replacement was achieved, while consensus was reached regarding: (1) comprehensive counseling and multidisciplinary care; (2) structured follow-up, including imaging, and psychosexual support; and (3) patient and parent education to support self-management. In ARM patients with vaginal agenesis and identifiable Müllerian structures, no consensus currently exists regarding early versus deferred vaginal replacement. This report is the first to summarize expert perspectives on the advantages and disadvantages of both approaches and highlights the need for further research on long-term outcomes to inform future practice.
