## Effect of intact cord resuscitation versus conventional cord clamping on clinical outcomes in very preterm neonates: a randomized controlled trial from Eastern India.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 01:05
- 期刊: Eur J Pediatr · 中科院医学3区(儿科2区) · IF 2.9 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: 印度 · Medical College and Hospital
- 通讯作者: Mandal K (kushalastrophys@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s00431-026-07323-5
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-026-07323-5) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42584505/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估完整脐带复苏（ICR）与传统脐带钳夹（CCC）对极早产儿临床结局的影响。
- 核心发现/数据：ICR组复合不良结局（院内死亡、严重脑室内出血或支气管肺发育不良）发生率低于CCC组（35.9% vs 60.3%，绝对风险降低24.4%）；死亡率（26.6% vs 41.3%）和严重IVH（9.4% vs 20.6%）亦更低。ICR还改善了Apgar评分、氧合，减少正压通气需求，提高72小时血红蛋白，且母体安全性相当。
- 临床启示：ICR可能作为极早产儿复苏的优化策略，在维持胎盘循环的同时完成稳定，有助于降低死亡和严重并发症风险，临床可考虑推广。

**原文摘要**：Early umbilical cord clamping during delivery-room resuscitation may interrupt placental transfusion during cardiopulmonary transition, increasing neonatal morbidity and mortality. This study aimed to evaluate whether intact cord resuscitation (ICR) improves delivery-room stabilization and clinical outcomes in very preterm neonates. In this single-centre, open-label randomized controlled trial at a tertiary-care hospital in Eastern India, neonates born at 26⁺⁰-31⁺⁶ weeks' gestation requiring delivery-room resuscitation were randomized (1:1) to ICR until physiological placental expulsion or conventional cord clamping (CCC) within 30-60 s of birth. The primary outcome was a composite of in-hospital mortality, severe intraventricular haemorrhage (IVH), or bronchopulmonary dysplasia. Secondary outcomes included delivery-room stabilization, maternal safety, and other neonatal morbidities. Analyses followed a per-protocol approach. A total of 127 neonates were analysed (ICR, n = 64; CCC, n = 63). Median (IQR) cord attachment duration was 330 (240-480) seconds in the ICR group and 60 (45-60) seconds in the CCC group. The composite adverse outcome was less frequent with ICR than CCC (35.9% vs 60.3%), corresponding to an absolute risk reduction of 24.4%. Mortality (26.6% vs 41.3%) and severe IVH (9.4% vs 20.6%) were also lower with ICR. ICR was associated with higher Apgar scores, improved oxygenation, reduced positive-pressure ventilation needs, earlier spontaneous respiration, lower sepsis and retinopathy of prematurity rates, higher haemoglobin at 72 h, earlier full enteral feeds, and shorter oxygen therapy. Maternal safety outcomes were comparable between groups. ICR may be a promising strategy to improve early cardiopulmonary stabilization and reduce adverse neonatal outcomes in very preterm infants. CTRI/2025/10/096001; Registration date October 13, 2025. • Cord clamping before completion of cardiopulmonary transition may adversely affect haemodynamic stability and clinical outcomes in very preterm infants. • Intact cord resuscitation (ICR) permits stabilisation while maintaining placental circulation, but evidence regarding its efficacy and safety in very preterm neonates remains limited. • ICR reduced the composite adverse outcome including mortality and, severe intraventricular haemorrhage. • ICR improved delivery-room stabilisation, reduced positive-pressure ventilation requirements, and increased haemoglobin concentration at 72 h.

## Chronic gastrointestinal symptoms in adulthood revealing congenital intestinal malrotation with midgut volvulus: a case report.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 01:05
- 期刊: J Surg Case Rep · 中科院医学4区(外科4区) · IF 0.5
- 国家/单位: Palestine · Alia Governmental Hospital
- 通讯作者: Jabari AY
- DOI: 10.1093/jscr/rjag693
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1093/jscr/rjag693) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42583473/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：报告一例成年期因先天性肠旋转不良伴中肠扭转而表现为慢性胃肠道症状的病例，以提高临床认识。
- 核心发现/数据：48岁男性，有4年复发性绞痛和非胆汁性呕吐史，近3个月加重至每日呕吐近10次；增强CT示近端十二指肠明显扩张、中段十二指肠移行带提示部分梗阻，上消化道造影确诊中肠旋转不良伴不全扭转；行Ladd手术后好转。成人肠旋转不良约占所有病例的0.2%-0.5%。
- 临床启示：对慢性不明原因腹痛和呕吐的成人患者，应考虑中肠旋转不良的可能，早期诊断可预防扭转和肠缺血等危及生命的并发症。

**原文摘要**：Intestinal malrotation is a congenital anomaly of midgut rotation and fixation that typically manifests in infancy, with most cases diagnosed within the first year of life. Although rare, accounting for ~0.2%-0.5% of all malrotation cases, intestinal malrotation can present in adults, with nonspecific symptoms that may delay diagnosis. We report the case of a 48-year-old male with a four-year history of recurrent colicky abdominal pain and non-bilious vomiting that progressively worsened over the preceding three months, with vomiting increasing to nearly ten episodes daily. A contrast-enhanced abdominal computed tomography demonstrated marked dilatation of the proximal duodenum with a transition point at the mid-duodenum suggestive of a partial obstruction. Upper gastrointestinal fluoroscopy confirmed midgut malrotation with an incomplete volvulus. The patient subsequently underwent a Ladd procedure. Adult midgut malrotation should be considered in patients with chronic, unexplained abdominal pain and vomiting. Early diagnosis is essential to prevent life-threatening complications such as a volvulus and bowel ischemia.
