## ECMO cannulation techniques in children: Heterogeneous and often complicated.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 02:02
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: Madrid · Hospital Universitario La Paz
- 通讯作者: Moratilla-Lapeña L (lucas.moratilla@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163259
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163259) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42349545/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：描述儿童颈部ECMO置管技术的并发症及管理变异性。
- 核心发现/数据：74%的患者发生并发症，以机械性并发症为主（52%）；使用6 Fr动脉插管的患者全部因溶血或泵衰竭死亡，提示应尽可能使用8 Fr插管或考虑中央ECMO。小儿外科与心胸外科在固定方式、血管修复及抗凝策略上存在显著异质性；动脉修复后通畅率非结扎组为5/5（100%），结扎组为4/5（80%）。
- 临床启示：儿童颈部ECMO置管并发症率高且管理缺乏标准化，需规范插管尺寸选择和血管处理策略，以降低可避免的死亡与并发症风险。

**原文摘要**：Indications for ECMO are increasing, but standardized cannulation guidelines are lacking. This study aimed to describe complications and variability in management. A single-center retrospective review of children undergoing cervical ECMO (2016-2023). Data included demographics, ECMO technique, cannula size, complications (ELSO classification), mortality and follow-up. Surgeon specialty [pediatric (PS) or cardiothoracic (CS)] and cannula fixation method (ligation vs. non-ligation) were recorded. Twenty-seven patients underwent ECMO, mainly for congenital diaphragmatic hernia (37%). All CS used non-ligation fixation (8/27), while PS used ligation fixation (19/27). Complications occurred in 74% of patients, predominantly mechanical (52%). PS cases had more cannula malposition and surgical site bleeding. Bleeding requiring intervention occurred in two patients per group. All patients with a 6 Fr arterial cannula died from hemolysis or pump failure. At decannulation, CS repaired both vessels, whereas PS repaired the artery and ligated the vein, with heterogeneous anticoagulation strategies. Six-month follow-up ultrasound was available in 10 of 13 patients who underwent arterial repair, showing patency rates of 5/5 (100%) for repaired arteries in the non-ligation group, and 4/5 (80%) in the ligation group. Venous patency was confirmed in 2/5 (40%) of repaired veins. Cervical ECMO cannulation carries high complication rates and heterogeneous management. All patients cannulated with a 6 Fr arterial cannula died, suggesting that an 8 Fr cannula should be used whenever technically feasible and that central ECMO should be considered when it is not.

## Pediatric robotic thyroid surgery and scar-related quality of life: A retrospective cohort of 40 patients.

- 来源: JPS 圣刊 · 推送 02:02
- 期刊: J Pediatr Surg · 中科院医学2区(儿科1区/外科2区) · IF 2.3 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 94800 Villejuif · Paris Hospitals Public Assistance
- 通讯作者: Airaudo O (olivier.airaudo@gustaveroussy.fr)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163262
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2026.163262) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42349544/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估儿童机器人甲状腺手术的可行性及术后疤痕相关生活质量。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入40例患者，中位年龄15岁；手术方式包括经腋窝25例、经耳后15例，中位手术时间217分钟。术后并发症均为暂时性，随访中位31个月时全部缓解。31例完成SCAR-Q问卷，外观、症状及心理社会影响中位评分分别为80、82、87，总体满意度高，经腋窝入路评分呈更高趋势。
- 临床启示：远程入路机器人甲状腺切除术在经选择的儿童患者中可行且美观满意度高；经腋窝入路可能更具优势，但需更大样本验证。

**原文摘要**：To retrospectively evaluate pediatric robotic thyroid surgery and assess postoperative satisfaction using the SCAR-Q scale. Patients undergoing robotic thyroid surgery between January 2017 and December 2023 were included. Eu-TIRADS and Bethesda classification were assessed. Surgeries were performed either a transaxillary or retroauricular approach. Clinical variables and complications were recorded. Postoperative scar-related satisfaction was assessed using the validated SCAR-Q questionnaire. Forty patients were included, with a median age of 15 years (range: 7-18). Histology revealed 23 benign nodules, 6 multinodular goiters, 4 cases of Graves' disease and 7 differentiated carcinomas. A transaxillary approach was performed in 25 cases and a retroauricular approach in 15. Median operative time was 217 min (range: 119-347 min). Postoperative complications included seven temporary recurrent laryngeal nerve palsies, two temporary hypocalcemias, one wound dehiscence, and one temporary impairment of upper limb extension; all resolved at the last follow-up (median follow-up 31 months, range: 3-77). Thirty-one patients completed the SCAR-Q questionnaire from July 1, 2023 to September 1, 2023. Median scores were 80 for appearance (range: 17-100), 82 for symptoms (range: 47-100), and 87 for psychosocial impact (range: 0-100). Satisfaction was high overall, with a trend toward better outcomes after the transaxillary approach. Remote-access robotic thyroidectomy appears to be a feasible option in selected pediatric patients, with high cosmetic satisfaction. The transaxillary approach was associated with a trend toward higher SCAR-Q scores, although selection bias and limited sample size preclude firm conclusions regarding superiority.

## Beyond da Vinci: a systematic review of next-generation multiport robotic platforms in pediatric surgery.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 02:02
- 期刊: J Robot Surg · 中科院医学3区(外科3区) · IF 4.2 · JCR Q1
- 国家/单位: Ancona · Department of Specialized Clinical and Odontostomatological Sciences
- 通讯作者: Di Fabrizio D (dona.difabrizio@gmail.com)
- DOI: 10.1007/s11701-026-03879-4
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11701-026-03879-4) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42704500/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：系统评估达芬奇之外的新一代多孔机器人平台在儿童手术中的技术特点、应用及围手术期结局。
- 核心发现/数据：共纳入6项研究、166例患儿、27种手术类型，其中Senhance平台占164例，Hugo RAS和Hinotori各仅1例；患儿年龄15天至17岁，体重3.8至>100 kg。中转或计划性升级手术19例（11.4%），术中并发症4例（2.4%），术后并发症20例（12.0%），再干预7例（4.6%），再入院7例（4.6%），无死亡及比较性研究。
- 临床启示：目前新一代多孔机器人平台在儿科的证据极少且几乎限于Senhance，仅提示早期临床应用的可行性，尚不能支持其安全性、有效性或平台优越性的结论，需开展标准化报告的前瞻性多中心研究。

**原文摘要**：As robotic surgery expands beyond the da Vinci platform, the relevance of new-generation systems to pediatric patients remains uncertain. This review examined the technical characteristics, applications, and perioperative outcomes of alternative multiport robotic platforms in children. PubMed/MEDLINE, Web of Science, Scopus, and the Cochrane Library were searched through 28 February 2026 in accordance with PRISMA 2020. Owing to clinical heterogeneity, findings were synthesized narratively, with Wilson 95% confidence intervals for key binary outcomes. Six studies reported 166 patients across 27 procedure types. Senhance accounted for 164 patients, while Hugo RAS and Hinotori were each represented by one patient. Ages ranged from 15 days to 17 years and weights from 3.8 to more than 100 kg. Senhance was the only platform used with 3-mm robotic instruments. Conversion or planned escalation occurred in 19 patients (11.4%; 95% CI, 7.5-17.2%). Four intraoperative complications were reported (2.4%; 95% CI, 0.9-6.0%). Across all reports, 20 patients experienced postoperative complications (12.0%; 95% CI, 7.9-17.9%). In the largest cohort, seven patients required reintervention (4.6%; 95% CI, 2.2-9.2%) and seven were readmitted (4.6%; 95% CI, 2.2-9.2%). No deaths or comparative pediatric studies were reported. Published experience remains sparse and is almost entirely limited to Senhance. Current evidence describes early clinical use without establishing comparative safety, effectiveness, or platform superiority. Prospective multicenter studies with standardized reporting are needed.

## Association between changes in the lung-to-head ratio and postnatal survival in fetuses with congenital diaphragmatic hernia.

- 来源: PubMed · 推送 08:36
- 期刊: Clinics (Sao Paulo) · 中科院医学4区(医学：内科4区) · IF 2.4 · JCR Q2
- 国家/单位: 中国 · National Clinical Research Center for Women's Health and Obstetric and Gynecologic Diseases
- 通讯作者: Lü Y (lvyan@pumch.cn)
- DOI: 10.1016/j.clinsp.2026.101073
- 获取全文: [DOI 全文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clinsp.2026.101073) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42705012/)

**中文总结**：- 研究目的：评估先天性膈疝胎儿超声测量的肺头比观察值与期望值之比（O/E LHR）的纵向变化与生后存活率之间的关联。
- 核心发现/数据：在74例以轻型CDH为主的单中心队列中，总存活率为73.0%；存活者与死亡者的ΔO/E LHR差异显著（13.32±22.09 vs. -13.93±17.59，p<0.001）。多因素分析中，ΔO/E LHR（OR=1.089，95%CI 1.024-1.158；p=0.007）、诊断时孕周（OR=1.462，95%CI 1.041-2.053；p=0.029）及疝侧与肝脏疝出联合变量（p=0.011）与存活相关；ΔO/E LHR预测存活的AUC为0.836（95%CI 0.725-0.948）。
- 临床启示：在轻型CDH胎儿中，纵向监测O/E LHR变化可能比单次测量更具预后价值，但个体解剖亚组的估计不精确，需更大队列验证后方可用于个体化风险预测。

**原文摘要**：To evaluate the association between longitudinal changes in Observed-to-Expected Lung-to-Head Ratio (O/E LHR) measured by ultrasound and postnatal survival in fetuses with Congenital Diaphragmatic Hernia (CDH). A total of 74 pregnant women carrying fetuses with CDH were retrospectively analyzed. The change in O/E LHR (ΔO/E LHR) was assessed as a continuous variable using univariate and multivariate logistic regression analyses. Hernia lateralization and liver herniation were combined into a single categorical variable to account for their clinical and statistical correlation. Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) analysis was performed to evaluate the discriminative ability of ΔO/E LHR for survival. The majority of patients (86% and 74% according to the first and last ultrasound examinations, respectively) in this cohort had mild CDH, with an overall survival rate of 73.0%. ΔO/E LHR differed significantly between survivors and non-survivors (13.32±22.09 vs. -13.93±17.59, p < 0.001). In multivariate analysis, ΔO/E LHR (OR = 1.089, 95% CI 1.024-1.158; p = 0.007), gestational age at diagnosis (OR = 1.462, 95% CI 1.041-2.053; p = 0.029), and the combined variable of hernia lateralization and liver herniation (p = 0.011) were associated with survival. ROC analysis of ΔO/E LHR yielded an AUC of 0.836 (95% CI 0.725-0.948). In this single-center cohort of fetuses with predominantly mild CDH, ΔO/E LHR was associated with postnatal survival and showed good discriminative performance. Gestational age at diagnosis and the combined variable of hernia lateralization and liver herniation were also associated with outcomes. However, estimates for individual anatomical subgroups were imprecise because of the limited number of cases and should not be used for individual risk prediction. These findings suggest that longitudinal assessment of O/E LHR may be more informative than a single measurement in this specific population. Further validation in larger cohorts is warranted.
